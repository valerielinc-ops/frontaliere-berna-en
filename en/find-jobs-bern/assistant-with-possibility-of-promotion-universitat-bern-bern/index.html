About us

Our research focus in international economic law is on the law of the WTO and the bilateral and regional trade agreements, especially Switzerland. In the area of European law, we focus on the law of the European Union’s foreign relations, in particular foreign economic relations and relations between Switzerland and the EU, as well as constitutional and internal market law. We also work closely with the interdisciplinary World Trade Institute (WTI). Due to our internationally shaped research topics, we also welcome applications from people with a foreign degree.
