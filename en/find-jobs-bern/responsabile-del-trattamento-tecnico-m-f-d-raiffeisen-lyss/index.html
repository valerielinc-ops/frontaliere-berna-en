08.09.2026 Lyss

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Seeland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Aarberg, Biel, Büren, Lyss, Ins and Studen, we are looking for a motivated personality as a manager: in specialist processing.

What awaits you?

  • Leadership and further development of currently 9 employees of the specialist processing team. Nicole, Colin and other colleagues are looking forward to you.
  • Shared responsibility for the compliant and timely settlement of all credit transactions in the retail and corporate segment
  • Check for completeness and collection of documents, preparation of loan templates and contract documents, timely disbursement and redemption, netting and repatriation of credit positions
  • Processing of periodic new permits, construction loan processing, management of electronic customer dossier and debt letter management (Terravis)
  • Support of credit advice in administrative matters
  • Telephone and written communications with offices, insurance companies and other banks
  • Participation in various projects

What do you bring?

  • Completed training in the financial sector with experience in credit
  • You feel comfortable in dealing with and applying regulatory requirements
  • You already have profound leadership experience and are characterized by high social skills
  • You are a communicative personality with a convincing appearance and work independently and accurately
  • You have a high sense of quality and responsibility

You love variety, organize with foresight and always keep the overview. Then we should get to know each other! Andrea Bosshard Head of Credit Office & Processing Why Raiffeisen?
