Full description

Portrait The Salome Brunner Foundation works for around 300 children and adolescents with language development disorders and learning difficulties. With our various schools, we see ourselves as an important part of the educational landscape of the Canton of Bern.

The Language Healing School Wabern is a special elementary school for children and adolescents with a complex language development disorder as well as a reduction in intelligence and comprises 21 classes from the basic level to the vocational preparation school year (BVS). Small classes make it possible to respond to the individual needs of the students: inside. Speech therapy, occupational and physiotherapy take place as individual therapy. Social pedagogical support offers the supplementary school day school and boarding school.
