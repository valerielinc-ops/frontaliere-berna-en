Full description

In a nutshell

From ammunition and communications equipment to provisions, you will be responsible, together with your team colleagues, for the warehouse management processes required to fulfill the mission of the Swiss Armed Forces. We offer you a varied specialist role with many interesting challenges in army logistics.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20160
  • Work location: Guisanplatz 1, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Be responsible for second-level support for the SuperUsers of the logistics centers
  • Lead the monitoring of warehouse processes, carry out corrections and initiate measures
  • Create training materials and user documentation and pass on the content as a trainer
  • Represent warehouse processes in projects such as ERP logistics and in various working groups
  • Review and coordinate specifications for settings, changes and further development in the SAP system

This is what makes you unique

  • Higher vocational education in a technical or business administration field
  • In-depth knowledge of warehouse logistics
  • Experience with SAP and good MS Office skills
  • Customer-oriented and networked thinking, as well as enjoyment of working in a team
  • Good knowledge of a second official language

Your commitment to safety and freedom The Logistics Base of the Swiss Armed Forces (LBA) serves the troops. It provides all logistics services for the Swiss Armed Forces and, with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12'000 militia personnel, manages the equipment, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss Armed Forces. Whether in the workshop or office, indoors or outdoors: the LBA offers a wide range of jobs throughout Switzerland. There is something for everyone here.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – and do so at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: the Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.
