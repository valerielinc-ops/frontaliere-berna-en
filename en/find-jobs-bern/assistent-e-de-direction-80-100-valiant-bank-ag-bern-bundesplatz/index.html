About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial provider. It is exclusively present in Switzerland and offers private customers and SMEs a complete, simple and understandable offer in all financial fields. Valiant is located locally in fifteen cantons: Aargau, Basel-Campaign, Basel-City, Bern, Fribourg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Saint-Gall, Schaffhouse, Solothurn, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. In addition, it is present throughout Switzerland thanks to its innovative and digital services. Valiant has a total balance sheet of 38.2 billion francs and employs approximately 1200 collaborators, including over 70 trainees.
