Full description

Portrait The OSZ Ins is an association school, which is attended by 265 cycle 3 students from seven communities. Ins is a village in the exceptionally beautiful Zealand with about 3800 inhabitants and perfect public transport connections to Bern, Biel, Neuchâtel and Freiburg. At the school, 13 classes are currently taught according to a transparent model (3b/4).

Tasks

As of 12.10.2026, you will take on a temporary position as class teacher at a 7th grade (model 4), Math 5L, ERG 2L, RZG 3L, NT 3L. You also teach at an 8th grade 5L Math and 3L NT. Deputy may take longer, until the end of the semester or the end of the school year. The amount can also be divided between 2 people. They actively shape everyday school life and work cooperatively with the various stakeholders. They work closely with the teachers of the year team.

Requirements

  • You are a motivated, reliable and creative personality who likes to work in a team.
  • You bring a completed degree program that qualifies you for teaching at secondary level I or are currently in training.

We offer

A motivated and helpful team, an appreciative, pleasant working atmosphere in a future-oriented school, a supportive and present school management and a functioning school culture.
