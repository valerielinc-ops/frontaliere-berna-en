University Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of the UPZ, as one of the leading institutions in Switzerland, provides outpatient, semi-inpatient and inpatient psychiatric care as well as emergency care for children and adolescents in the Canton of Bern as well as in parts of the Cantons of Solothurn and Freiburg. As a university hospital, it also has an extended mandate in the supraregional specialized care as well as in teaching and research. In the departmental management of child and youth forensics, you can expect a versatile, paid internship of 20%, in which you participate in the establishment and organization of a training curriculum for employees of Jugendhilfe. You will gain exciting insights into children’s and youth forensics as well as the organization of further education and training. In addition, you support our secretariat once a week for general administrative tasks. You can expect a committed team, innovative approaches and a supportive working atmosphere. The internship offers you the opportunity to combine your studies with practical experience and to take on responsible tasks.

Your tasks

  • You are involved in setting up, organizing and implementing a training curriculum for youth welfare employees
  • You coordinate the communication with the registered participants and take over the preparation and follow-up of the seminars
  • You support the administration around the curriculum and the twelve seminar days, especially with registrations, scheduling and the preparation of documents
  • You support the secretariat once a week for general administrative tasks
  • You help ensure that the training offers are implemented organizationally smoothly

Your profile

  • You are studying psychology or another relevant subject
  • You have good IT knowledge and are safe in dealing with Word, Excel and Outlook
  • You work in a structured, careful and independent way and also keep an overview of several tasks
  • You like to communicate, perform reliably and professionally and enjoy contact with different people
  • Work discreetly and responsibly
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
