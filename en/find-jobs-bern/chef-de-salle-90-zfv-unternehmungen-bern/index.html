Full description

This workplace includes a restaurant. The meeting place in Bern creates an inviting meeting point for seminar, event and celebration guests. From Monday to Friday, our team delights an average of six guests here with a varied offering – from freshly prepared menus and seasonal offerings to regional specialities. Are you our new team member who will support us as Chef: de Service (90%) immediately or by agreement?

You work at 90% capacity, spread over 5 days per week between Monday and Sunday in early, middle and late shifts. Depending on events and the season, shifts between 07:00 and 02:00 are possible

Your vacation is spread over 3 weeks during the summer holidays and 2 weeks over Christmas and New Year.

Your task menu

  • Responsibility for a visible, attentive and service-oriented presence in the dining room during peak hours
  • Looking after and advising guests in the seminar and à la carte areas
  • Wine recommendations including wine service
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Setting up & dismantling seminar rooms
  • Support with various administrative tasks (daily closing procedures, DCC, website, checking delivery notes, event quotations, email enquiries, table reservations using Aleno)
  • Assistance with ordering and inventory
  • Support with the planning, training and qualification of employees
  • Delegation of staff, including briefings & debriefings, as well as independently conducting smaller to medium-sized events
  • Participation in regular management meetings as well as mandatory training sessions and company events
  • Responsibility for and ensuring compliance with the quality standards and ZFV guidelines (hygiene and occupational safety, service, etc.)

Your recipe for success

  • Completed vocational training as a restaurant specialist EFZ
  • 5 years of professional experience in the hospitality industry; initial management experience preferred
  • Good German, Swiss German and English skills; any additional foreign language is an advantage
  • Experience working with MS Office programs
  • Leadership skills
  • Empathetic hospitality professional
  • Enthusiastic
  • Charismatic sales talent
  • Passion for enjoyment

What we offer you

We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged to develop. We work together and focus on everything that does us good. Among other things, we place great importance on pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, at a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary between CHF 60'000 and CHF 68'900 (including a 13th-month salary).

If you would like to share your passion for the hospitality industry with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
