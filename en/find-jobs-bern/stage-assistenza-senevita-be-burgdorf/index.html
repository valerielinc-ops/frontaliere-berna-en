Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita in Burgdorf we are looking for an intern / intern care 100 % immediately or by agreement The duration of the internship can be determined individually (between 6 and 12 months).The internship can be completed as preparation for an apprenticeship or as an opportunity for graduates of SRC nursing training. Your tasks

  • In everyday life you take over the competent basic care according to individual needs
  • You help with activating and supervising tasks (menu selection, daily structure, etc.)
  • You report your observations to the responsible nurse.
  • You ensure order and cleanliness in the care area taking into account internal company regulations.

This is what you bring with you

  • You like working with older people and respect their dignity and self-determination.
  • You show interest and appreciation in working with our residents, customers and employees.
  • You are helpful, reliable and enjoy working in a team.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing.

We offer you

  • Contemporary employment conditions at a leading provider in the long-term care sector
  • Extensive training offer of the Senevita Academy, can be visited free of charge
  • Discounted food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Miscellaneous benefits (half tax subscription, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 or from 60 years 7 weeks)
