Full description

For the Psychiatric Centre for Adolescents and Young Adults (PZJE) of the Private Clinic Meiringen in Thun, we are looking for a committed senior physician. At the PZJE, we treat adolescents and young adults with diseases from the entire spectrum of psychiatry with extensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment. The centre has 30 inpatient treatment places. The treatment is carried out according to the latest evidence-based medical, therapeutic and nursing knowledge. One focus is the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of adolescents and young adults, in addition, the center also offers admission in crises and is primarily conducted openly and, if necessary, protected in a ward.

  • Supervision and professional support of employees in training
  • Co-design and further development of the inpatient setting
  • Implementation of the professional, organizational and economic objectives Our expectations
  • Recognized specialist title as a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Joy in innovation
  • High social competence, teamwork and sense of responsibility
  • Structured and independent way of working
