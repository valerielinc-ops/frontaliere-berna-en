You can look forward to this. SBB Insurance AG is a 100% subsidiary of the Swiss Federal Railways SBB. As a primary insurance company, it insures the risks of the parent company and its subsidiaries

  • but we are also pioneers, risk managers and guardian angels for one of the most diverse companies in Switzerland. As Chief Underwriting Officer (m/f/d), you will take on an important key position and work closely with colleagues at SBB Insurance AG, based in Liechtenstein, as well as the SBB and external bodies. With your colleagues from the insurance sector, who combine expertise and passion, you will navigate through the challenges of everyday life and also overcome unexpected events and claims. Ensuring insurance coverage for SBB's various business areas such as mobility, energy, real estate and infrastructure makes this position demanding
  • but extremely exciting and unique! You can move that.
  • As Chief Underwriting Officer (m/f/d), you are responsible for the underwriting strategy, guidelines and risk policy as well as their implementation.
  • You are responsible for risk selection, underwriting policy and pricing.
  • Your tasks include analyzing market trends, emerging risks and competitive developments as well as deriving strategic measures from them.
  • You represent the interests of SBB Insurance towards various internal and external stakeholders such as reinsurance companies and service providers and ensure stable and trusting relationships.
  • Sustainability is a real concern for you
  • that's why you're pushing the topic forward with us in the insurance market and setting new standards in the process.
  • You consistently implement the strategic goals of SBB Insurance and further develop them. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are characterized by your solution-oriented thinking, decision-making power, sense of responsibility, clear communication and a can-do mentality.
  • You have broad and in-depth knowledge of the insurance and reinsurance business, particularly in the area of ​​property, business interruption and liability insurance.
  • Your many years of experience in risk assessment and evaluation and support of risk audits form the basis for the derivation of wording and pricing.
  • You are both a technical risk specialist and a strategic manager and have already celebrated successes as a specialist manager and know how to overcome complex challenges with various stakeholders. You also know the requirements regarding regulation and compliance.
  • We require a university degree in business administration (FH or university) with a focus on finance/insurance or equivalent further training in the insurance sector.
  • You are fluent in German (level C2) and you have a good knowledge of English (level B2), another national language is a plus.
