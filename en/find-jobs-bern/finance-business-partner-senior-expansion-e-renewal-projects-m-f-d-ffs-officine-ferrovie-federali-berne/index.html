  • Make Switzerland move with us. Finance Business Partner Senior Expansion and renewal projects (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.09.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here ## What awaits you. Shape the future of railway infrastructure as a Finance Business Partner (m/f/d) for expansion and renewal projects. You put your expertise in business management at the service of financial management. You develop performance, guarantee compliance and support the business on commercial issues in the area of ​​extension and renewal projects (AEP). What you can make move.
  • You assume overall responsibility for the planning process with budget, medium and long-term planning, updated forecasts as well as the performance cockpit of the Expansion and Renewal Projects area.
  • You manage and assume responsibility for Finance Business Partnering with a focus on the income statement / financial performance for the management of extension and renewal projects (AEP) as well as for its specialized areas. In this position, your knowledge of French is also required.
  • As a Finance Business Partner for cross-functional programs (notably SAP S/4HANA, cost reduction program, track construction equipment) you proactively provide financial perspectives.
  • You identify gaps at an early stage, deduce well-founded recommendations for action and contribute to overall performance as well as the achievement of ambition and productivity objectives.
  • Compliance with financial directives, standards and compliance is guaranteed thanks to your experience which you put at the service of demanding decisions.
  • You actively optimize financial management instruments, support operational unit managers as equal sparring partners and encourage ambitious and autonomous definition of objectives.
  • What you bring.
  • You have several years of solid experience in controlling and convince with your great communication skills.
  • Through your commitment to work and your proactive taking of responsibility, you motivate and convince your stakeholders.
  • You work very autonomously, think analytically and in scenarios and have a strong affinity for systems and tools.
  • As a business driver (Value Creation), you identify complex cause-and-effect relationships and actively contribute to managing them.
  • You have completed university or HES studies, preferably in business management, finance & controlling, or technical training supplemented by continuing management training.
  • Knowledge of German and French at least at level C1 is required and ideally knowledge of Italian at level A2. ## Your salary and benefits. We are actively committed to equal pay.
