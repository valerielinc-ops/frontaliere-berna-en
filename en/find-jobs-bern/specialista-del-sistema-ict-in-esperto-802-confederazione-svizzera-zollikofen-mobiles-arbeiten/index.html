Put to the Point

Are you an e SharePoint/M365 engineer with an affinity for processes, solution development and M365 products? Your goal is to analyze challenges and requirements with our customers and to find efficient solutions together with them, to build bridges between customer requirements, technical possibilities and developers and to assume different roles in an agile environment in order to support nationwide digitalization? Then you are exactly right at the BIT - thanks to you we guarantee our partners a stable and modern environment that is up-to-date.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 609-15503
  • Place of work: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Automate processes using PowerShell
  • Data migration and deployment from SharePoint OnPrem to M365
  • Development of SP OnPrem and M365 backend services
  • Provide new or existing solutions in the M365 environment as well as migration and transformation of solutions from SharePoint Classic
  • Generating automatic reports via Power BI; Analyze and troubleshoot and automate LifeCycle issues in the operation of SharePoint OnPrem and M365 services
  • Customer care in migration issues of M365 and consulting in solution finding with M365 services (especially in approaches with Microsoft Search and VIVA Suite)
  • Lead in projects with agile working methods and proactive participation in their work processes (SCRUM, SAFe, Kanban)

This makes you unique

  • Computer Science Education (EFZ) with flair for software development and automation
  • Many years of customer experience in comparable projects as well as know-how in SharePoint OnPrem and M365 and knowledge in the required technologies (PowerShell, HTML5 and REST service, Graph API, PnP); Experience in LowCode Solutions (Power Platform)
  • Experience working in agile teams (SAFe/SCRUM)
  • Motivated and committed personality; customer- and team-oriented and networked way of working; communicative and able to understand demanding relationships
  • Good knowledge of a second official language
