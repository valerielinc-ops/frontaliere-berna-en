This is what awaits you: In this position as team leader: you lead a team in the Under Papers, which is responsible for the active processing and further development of the demanding and exciting SME business in the market region of Western Switzerland. You set clear goals, regularly give feedback, support your employees in a targeted manner and accompany them in everyday life. In addition, you pre-empt and promote an open, trusting and performance-oriented team culture. In the day-to-day operational business, you advise and support our general agencies in this responsible role in the implementation of a risk- and market-oriented under-paper. You accompany our insurance consultants:inside during customer visits and carry out sound risk assessments and risk analyses. You also coordinate and realize the professional training and further training of the general agencies. You also take over the management of selected projects and work in interdisciplinary teams on exciting projects.
