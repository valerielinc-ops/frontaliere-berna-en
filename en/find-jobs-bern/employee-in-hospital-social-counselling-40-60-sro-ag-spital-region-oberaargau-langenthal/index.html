Main responsibilities

  • They advise our patients independently and accompany them and their reference system in difficult life situations in connection with illness and hospital stay
  • They arrange and coordinate external offers, advise patients and their relatives on inpatient follow-up solutions (age and nursing home, rehabilitation and recovery) as well as outpatient care and support offers and organize them
  • They perform administrative tasks and keep patient records, history records and statistics
  • They actively participate in the interdisciplinary exchange at the acute-geiatric rehabilitation station
  • They organize and lead interdisciplinary family meetings.
  • You are well versed in social security issues related to illness and accident and access financial resources
  • They coordinate the networking of patients with the Pro Senectute, the community social services, the KESB and other third parties. Requirements
  • You have completed a bachelor's degree in social work FH BSc/B.A., a bachelor's degree in social education HF or an equivalent education and/or bring a CAS in case management and ideally experience in clinical social work
  • They encounter people in challenging health situations with empathy and appreciation and show skill in talking to predominantly older patients.
  • You have good knowledge of social law issues
