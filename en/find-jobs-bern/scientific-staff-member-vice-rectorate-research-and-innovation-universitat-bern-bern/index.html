Full description

Tasks: They support all areas of responsibility of the Vice Rector Research and Innovation. These include:

  • Preparation and follow-up of the documents for the meetings of the university management
  • Preparation of proposals of the Vice Rectorate of Research and Innovation for the benefit of the university management
  • Creation of a basis for decision-making
  • Project management of strategically important, complex projects
  • Collaboration in projects of the Central Division and the Vice Rectorate for Research and Innovation
  • Participation in financial, accounting and financial controlling tasks You act as an important link and coordinator: between the Vice Rector Research and Innovation and the different departments of the Vice Rectorate. In particular, they shall:
  • Maintaining the exchange of information between the areas of the Vice Rectorate and with the Vice Rector
  • Organisation of meetings, retraits, conferences
  • Preparation of specific orders for the areas of the Vice Rectorate
  • Lateral management or co-management of individual areas or projects together with the vice-rector Requirements
  • Higher education
  • Affinity to finance and controlling topics in the university environment
  • Leadership experience, possibly a CAS in academic leadership and management and/or Master in Public Administration/Managment
  • Affinity for research topics, ideally research experience, e.g. as part of a PhD
  • Rapid comprehension and conceptual and networked thinking skills
  • Very good communication and expression ability
  • Independent working methods, impeccable reputation and discretion
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss higher education landscape as well as the University of Bern and its structures or the willingness to acquire this knowledge
  • Language skills: excellent in German, English and French We offer: Working in an attractive professional, academic environment with strong social relevance
