Main responsibilities

  • You develop processes, guidelines and structures further, ensure clear responsibilities and anchor a uniform approach in dealing with third parties within the furniture.
  • In this way, you make an important contribution to transparency, efficiency and quality in the area of third parties.
  • This is what you do with us: As a TPM Owner, you are responsible for the further development and operational management of the end-to-end processes in third-party management: from the onboarding of new third parties to the termination of cooperation.
  • In doing so, you will further develop the operating model and the governance structure and implement it in a practical and implementation-oriented manner together with the participating areas.
  • With your conscientious work, you ensure clear, efficient and sustainable processes for the entire company.
  • You’ll work closely with procurement, various departments, as well as legal, compliance, finance and IT, ensuring that third-party management is seamlessly integrated into existing processes.
  • You will understand different requirements, define guidelines, roles and responsibilities and create viable and consistent solutions together with those involved.
  • You will continue to develop the central third-party management solution and together with the specialist departments ensure an up-to-date and qualitatively reliable third-party register.
  • This ensures the quality of relevant master data, responsibilities and process information.
