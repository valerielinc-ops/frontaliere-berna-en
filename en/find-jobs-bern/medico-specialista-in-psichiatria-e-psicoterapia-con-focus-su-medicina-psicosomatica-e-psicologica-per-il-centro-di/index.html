Full description

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of internistic-oncological, psychosomatic and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. With the Center for Pain and Stress Medicine in Bern, we run a specialized outpatient competence center for the integrative treatment of chronic pain and stress secondary diseases – an offer with a consistent biopsychosocial approach that is unique in the region.

Their tasks

  • They assume a central medical function in an established and at the same time dynamic competence center.
  • They treat complex pain and stress secondary diseases as well as psychosomatic diseases according to an evidence-based, biopsychosocial approach.
  • You contribute your clinical experience, actively shape the further development of our treatment concept and shape the profile of the center with your own specialist focus.
  • They participate in the optimization of processes, therapy offers and interdisciplinary cooperation and thus provide new impetus for the care of our patients.
  • They work closely with our rehabilitation facility, clinical partners and external referees – for high-quality care.

Our expectations

  • Completed specialist title
  • Focus title SAPPM or interest in acquiring it - the focus title can be acquired as part of the activity at ZSSM Bern
  • Experience in the treatment of pain and/or stress secondary diseases is advantageous
  • Interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Responsibility, empathy and pleasure in conceptual participation

Our offer

  • Working in an experienced interdisciplinary team
  • Versatile patient spectrum
  • High degree of professional autonomy and scope for design
  • Collegial support, appreciative cooperation and professional training
  • Central workplace in attractive premises in the old town of Bern

Requirements

  • Completed specialist medical qualification

  • Focus title SAPPM or interest in obtaining it

  • Experience in treating pain and/or stress-related disorders

  • Interest in interdisciplinary collaboration

  • Responsibility, empathy, and enthusiasm for conceptual development

  • Excellent communication skills
