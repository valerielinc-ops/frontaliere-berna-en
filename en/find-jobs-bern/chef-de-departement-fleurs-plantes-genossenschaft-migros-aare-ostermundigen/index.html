Full description

Your professional experience in the field of flowers distinguishes you as well as your competent advice to our customers? Then you fit us perfectly. In the role of head of department, you take over the professional management of the team. Do you feel called? We look forward to meeting you.

Mansioni

  • You advise and serve our customers with pleasure and your expertise
  • An attractive presentation of goods, efficient goods management and ensuring availability are your focus
  • You ensure that the defined processes in your department are adhered to
  • With creativity and skill, you design floral arrangements and take care of flowers and plants
  • In this role, the focus is on the professional leadership of the team
  • You support actively and engaged in other departments

Requirements

  • Ideally, several years of professional experience: in the retail trade specialization Flowers / Plants or as a florist
  • Completed basic or further education: in the retail trade specialization Flowers / Plants or as a florist
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales, you make shopping a positive experience
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • You have leadership experience
  • You have a flair for creative flower arrangements
  • in German (negotiation certain)
