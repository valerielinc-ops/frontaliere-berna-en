Share your passion for books You like to read for your life? You like to dive into incredible worlds of books? And you love to share your passion for books with other readers? Then apply now for an apprenticeship as a book dealer at Orell Füssli. Because we love books as much as you do. Orell Füssli is also a place where employees feel comfortable, where they like to work together and achieve more together. During your training, you will learn about the book trade from scratch. We are looking forward to seeing you as a 1st person.

August 2027 in one of our branching regions Bern (Thun, Bern, Schönbühl) / Central Switzerland (Emmenbrücke). Your main tasks:

  • Competent advice for customers
  • Recommendation of books and new publications
  • Acceptance of orders and their processing
  • Various work on the computer and in our goods management system
  • Presentation of books and non-books You bring with you:
  • Good secondary education
  • High service orientation, contact, attention and curiosity
  • Non-introduce German skills and a joy in foreign languages
  • Passion for books and reading
  • Passion for media and current events This is what awaits you:
  • Varied training in different departments
  • Collaborative and appreciative corporate culture
  • Generous holiday arrangements
  • Versatile discount offer
  • Central work place with good public transport connections We are looking forward to your online application. Please send us this including motivational letters, CV, complete school certificates and with a short text to your favorite book. See you soon.
