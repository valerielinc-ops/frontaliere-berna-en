Full description

The Human Resources domain, managed at the national level, is responsible for, and manages, the personnel files of the entire SSR. It accompanies SSR employees in all personnel matters, offers a modern offer of initial and continuing training, and responds, in its field, to the overall implementation of modern key processes.

The Department of Personnel & Culture Development strengthens culture and collaboration, supports change and promotes an inclusive and healthy work environment. It contributes to people and leadership development, supports learning and creates clear perspectives for succession and talent.

Your Mission

We are looking for a change management specialist to join our Culture & Organisation team at an appropriate date. You support responsible people, teams and organizational units in their development and change processes, and strengthen a culture of collaboration based on the vision, mission and values of the CSR. Particular emphasis is placed on supporting cultural and organisational development as part of the transformation of the SSR. In this context, you reconcile a common national framework with regional needs and organize implementation in collaboration with the relevant services.

Within the Culture & Organisation team, you focus on the following topics:

  • Support for change projects in the context of transformation and implementation of appropriate change measures
  • Advice and support to managers, project leaders and HR partners in change management
  • Design and animation of cultural, organizational and team development programs and strengthening leadership and collaboration
  • Analysis of change needs and risks, assessment of the impact of measures and identification of avenues for improvement
  • Promoting networking across linguistic, regional and sectoral boundaries and sharing and disseminating successful approaches and experiences

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in the field of cultural and organisational development, as well as a solid experience in designing and accompanying complex change projects
  • University degree in organizational psychology, psychology, business economy or equivalent, supplemented by continuing training in organizational development, systemic advice or change management
  • Systemic thinking and ability to analyze complex systems and translate them into effective solutions
  • Great initiative, excellent counselling and communication skills, a taste for interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Very good command of German and French; having good knowledge of another national language would be an advantage

What we offer you

  • An exciting, living and dynamic environment
  • A great freedom of action and a high degree of autonomy
  • A position in a national environment comprising four linguistic regions and offering multiple perspectives
  • A motivated, interdisciplinary and open team with a high level of professional competence
