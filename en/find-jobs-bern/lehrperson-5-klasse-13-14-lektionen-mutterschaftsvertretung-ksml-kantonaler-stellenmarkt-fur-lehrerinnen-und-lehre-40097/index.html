Full description

Portrait The primary school Boll belongs to the municipality Vechigen. It has 4 kindergartens and 12 rule classes. About 340 students are taught at the school. Teachers and parents work together constructively. Boll is very easy to reach with public transport.

Tasks

  • Subjects: Mathematics, French, Media and Computer Science, Music, NMG, Visual Design
  • Lesson days: Tuesday to Thursday

You will be supported at work by an experienced job partner and two curative pedagogue.

Requirements

  • Diploma corresponding to the level.
  • Commitment and joy for your own class, level and school as a whole.
  • You are an uncomplicated, dutiful and team capable teacher and have a flair in dealing with a heterogeneous class.
  • Experience in teaching.

We offer

  • An exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in a very well-rehearsed class team and college.
  • A resource-oriented cooperation with the job partner, the curative teacher and the teachers of the level.
  • The school provides all teachers with their own laptop and a modern IT infrastructure with Google workspace.
  • The modern school rooms are all multimedia equipped and the classes in Cycle 2 work with their own Chromebook.
  • Option: The position can also be filled until 31.07.2027. The job partner attends the semester course and an extension until 31.01.2028 would be desirable, but not an exclusion criterion.
