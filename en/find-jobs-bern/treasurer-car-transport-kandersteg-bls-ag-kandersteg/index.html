Full description

Escape from everyday life and immerse yourself in another world through the Lötschberg – thanks to you, the anticipation of our numerous customers begins when loading your vehicle in Kandersteg, because: you are the friendly face of car loading.

Here’s what’s waiting for you

  • Sell tickets to customers, advise and inform them
  • Collect missing customer data
  • Ensure proper handling of money and payment cards
  • Operate / monitor height limit and act correctly during alarm

Requirements

  • Completed professional training
  • Experience in sales and customer service and a high level of customer orientation
  • Good PC user skills
  • Very good written and spoken German skills, good oral language skills in Italian, English and French are an advantage
  • Irregular working hours (evening, weekend)

Your benefits – you can look forward to them

  • Get a GA 2. Class, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • Divide your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to purchase an additional five to ten vacation days per year
  • Financial support for external training courses as well as an exciting internal range of learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.
  • 18 days of paternity leave and 18 weeks of maternity leave with the option to extend this for another 6 months as unpaid leave.

About the BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
