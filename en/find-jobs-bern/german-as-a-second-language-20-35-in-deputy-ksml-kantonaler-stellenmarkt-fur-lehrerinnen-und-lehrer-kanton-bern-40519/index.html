Full description

Portrait The Lengnau School is a lively place for learning and personal development. Our school includes around 650 students from kindergarten to 9th grade, who are accompanied by a dedicated team of 85 teachers.

The continuous development of our school is particularly important to us – both to our students and to the quorum. The municipality of Lengnau supports us with the necessary resources to enable young people to enjoy optimal learning conditions in our modern learning and working spaces.

We are also happy to present our school in more detail at the following link: https://youtu.be/9EKn3A0rN2Y?si=NtkdkoyVqmRoZMrf

Assignments Your responsibilities will include

  • the planning, preparation, implementation and follow-up of DaZ lessons.
  • the promotion of pupils of the 1. to 4. Class in the acquisition of the German language.
  • teaching in small groups with individual and needs-based support.
  • close cooperation and regular exchange with classroom teachers.
  • the observation and documentation of the children's learning progress.

Requirements

You have pedagogical training as a teacher or ongoing training in the field of education and, at best, experience in DAZ teaching. Working with children from different linguistic and cultural backgrounds gives them pleasure and you are interested in the field of language support and integrative education. You work independently, reliably and like to work in a team.

What we offer

You can expect a committed, open-minded and helpful staff at an innovative school.

What we offer

  • a varied and meaningful activity in an appreciative school environment.
  • the possibility of specifically supporting children in acquiring the German language.
  • working together in a dedicated team.
  • compact working days Mon, Tue, Wed in the morning.
  • the prospect of an extension of the proxy beyond 6 November.

Tolerance, respect and appreciation characterise our school culture. We look forward to welcoming you to our team. Have we piqued your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your application.
