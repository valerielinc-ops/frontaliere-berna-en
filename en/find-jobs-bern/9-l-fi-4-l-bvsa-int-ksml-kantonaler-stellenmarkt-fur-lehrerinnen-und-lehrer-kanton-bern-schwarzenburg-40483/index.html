At Thunstrasse Primary School in Schwarzenburg, two 1./2. and 3./4. classes each, and four 5./6. classes, are taught. Further information can be found at www.schuleschwarzenburg.ch .

9 IF lessons and 4 L bVSA int. for the three 5./6. classes at Thunstrasse Primary School

Requirements

We offer
