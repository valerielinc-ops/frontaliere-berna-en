Full description

It develops concepts, studies and preliminary projects for a transport system that takes all forms of urban mobility into account. The aim is to improve the quality of life and the environment, to increase safety and to preserve the quality of the city. Employees of <a href="https://www.bern.ch/politik-und-verwaltung/stadtverwaltung/tvs/verkehrplanung/fachstelle-fuss-und-veloverkehr?searchterm=Fachstelle+Fuss-">Fachstelle Fuss- und Veloverkehr</a> are committed to all concerns and needs of cyclists and pedestrians. We need your support for this.

Tasks

  • Carrying out traffic measurements including assembly and disassembly of the measuring devices as well as evaluation and preparation of the measured data
  • Support for the operation of the Bernese bike stations including rapid solving of minor problems as well as participation in major adjustments and further developments
  • Answering inquiries from the population and dealing with corresponding concerns
  • Developing smaller measures such as B. Improvement of visibility ranges, removal of trottoir parking

Requirements

  • (Current) studies with a focus on geography, transport/space planning or similar
  • Great interest in traffic planning issues (especially foot and bicycle traffic)
  • Enjoyment of working in a team
  • Independent and structured working method
  • Experienced working with the usual MS Office programs (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)
