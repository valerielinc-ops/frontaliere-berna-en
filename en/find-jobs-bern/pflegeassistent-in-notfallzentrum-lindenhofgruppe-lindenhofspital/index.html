Full description

What you do

You like to get involved, keep an overview even in lively situations and appreciate the cooperation in the team. In the emergency center you bring your strengths and support the nurses where your help is needed.

  • You carry out domestic tasks and take over internal transport services.
  • You are jointly responsible for ordering and managing the care material.
  • In consultation with the specialists, you support in nursing interventions and in the care of patients.
  • In exchange with various professional groups, you contribute to a good cooperation.

What sets you apart

  • You have an education as a nursing assistant with FA, as an assistant in Health and Social EBA or are a nursing assistant / in SRK with professional experience.
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in the acute field.
  • You feel comfortable in a dynamic environment and react flexibly to changing patient situations.
  • With your reliable and quality-conscious nature, you meet people respectfully and appreciatively.
  • You communicate very well in German and understand Swiss German.
