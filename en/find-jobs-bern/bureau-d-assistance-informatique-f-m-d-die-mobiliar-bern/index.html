Full description

This is what awaits you

As an IT Service Desk employee: you are part of the support team, which provides first-class services and represents the business card of IT. It forms the link between internal customers and the IT organization and contributes significantly to a smooth operation with its professionalism.

This is what you do with us

In connection with the Mobiliar IT landscape, you will take care of all support requests (MS Office applications, workplace and insurance applications) from Mobiliar employees.

You and your team are responsible for the satisfactory completion of support requests. The concerns of German and French-speaking employees (Italian is a plus) you answer efficiently. If necessary, you bring in IT specialists:innen.

In addition, you ensure compliance with the service level and document your knowledge in our knowledgebase.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic technical or commercial education
  • Professional experience in 1st-Level Support
  • Knowledge of common technologies and IT applications
  • Networked thinking, a high sense of responsibility and communication skills
  • Team-oriented, empathetic and resilient personality, distinguished by an independent and reliable working method
  • Very good oral French skills as well as good German skills in word and writing (Italian is a plus)

Working with us

A team is waiting for you to give you the space to contribute your experiences and implement your ideas. Together they maintain an uncomplicated approach and attach importance to equal opportunities and a working environment in which everyone feels valued in their diversity.

Your assignment starts on 01.08.2026 or by agreement and is limited to one year. Your workload is negotiable from 40% and home office is open to you.
