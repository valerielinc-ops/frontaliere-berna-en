Your mission In synergy with national campaigns, you will design and implement the most effective communication strategies to guarantee three pillars: raising awareness, attracting visitors, and developing brand preference.   • Field presence: You are not just behind a desk. You regularly visit stores in your perimeter to understand local challenges and adapt your plans.
