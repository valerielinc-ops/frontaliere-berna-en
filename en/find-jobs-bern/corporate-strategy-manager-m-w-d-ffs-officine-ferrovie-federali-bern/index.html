Corporate Strategy Manager (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Corporate Strategy Manager (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You will help shape the long-term orientation of SBB and work on strategic topics with an impact for the entire company at Group level. In interdisciplinary teams, you work on strategic issues with different professional and entrepreneurial perspectives. A professional environment with high visibility, direct access to decision-makers and space for initiative enables you to quickly translate strategic impulses into effective results. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • Challenging strategy projects take you on your own responsibility from analysis to decision.
  • In interdisciplinary teams, you combine different perspectives on viable solutions and accompany their implementation in the organization to its effect.
  • You prepare decision documents for the strategic management level appropriately and represent your topics convincingly.
  • You combine strategic and business analysis with long-term, data- and scenario-based perspectives.
  • From this, you will develop well-founded options and recommendations for SBB.
  • You assess processes, economic relationships and interactions from an overall company perspective and use them for robust strategies.

You bring that with you.

  • You bring a sound understanding of strategy and strong strategic competence as well as a high seniority in professionalism, methodology and appearance.
  • With your entrepreneurial thinking and your solid business understanding, you evaluate opportunities, risks and effects holistically. You will quickly familiarize yourself with new topics and bring a high level of initiative and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Your convincing communication strength, moderation security and cooperation skills support you in stakeholder management at all levels.
  • You enjoy effective teamwork and the ability to integrate different professional perspectives and lead to a clear recommendation.
  • In this role, a master’s level university degree and deep experience in managing complex strategy projects are required. Experience in the environment of a large corporation and railway know-how are advantageous.
  • Negotiable oral and written knowledge of German (C2) is required. Further language skills in French or Italian are welcome.

Your rewards and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This employment takes place in accordance with bond law, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
