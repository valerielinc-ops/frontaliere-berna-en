Full description

Portrait Welcome to the MR-region Längenberg-Gürbetal. This includes the school communities Kaufdorf, Riggisberg, Thurnen and Toffen. We are a team of around 40 teachers who are engaged and competent in the areas of integrative promotion, speech therapy, psychomotor skills, German as a second language and gifted education. www.mr-gl.ch We are looking for a psychomotor substitute for 15-20 weekly lessons from the end of November 2026 to the end of January 2027. If you would like to work independently as a psychomotor therapist and still want to be involved in a specialist team, then you are exactly right with us. We are a small team that is moving.

Tasks

As a psychomotor therapist you work in prevention and diagnostics. Your work includes psychomotor support and promotion of perceptual and movement development by addressing child movement needs, by strengthening emotional-social skills and by promoting orientation and action in space. Technical assessments will be necessary. However, you will receive appropriate support for this. You primarily care for children from the municipalities of Kaufdorf, Toffen and Thurnen, who are transported to Riggisberg for the therapy sessions.

Requirements

You have psychomotor training. You enjoy working in a team and maintain an open approach to rule teachers, parents and guardians. You are an established personality and like to actively design a supportive and constructive learning environment.

We offer

  • motivated and committed MR team, regular professional exchange
  • helpful teachers, support from the school management MR
  • own equipped psychomotoric room
  • good connection to public transport or parking at the school house
