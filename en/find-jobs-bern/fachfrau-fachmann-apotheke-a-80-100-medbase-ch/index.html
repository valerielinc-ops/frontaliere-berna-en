Welcome to Medbase Zweisimmen

Our modern, over 400m2 practice with its own pharmacy is located in the village center of Zweisimmen. The homely village in the Bernese Oberland with direct connection to the hiking and winter sports region Gstaad offers a high leisure and recreational value. Our interdisciplinary team takes care of the basic medical care of the local population as well as the initial care of tourists. Our specialists from the fields of general internal medicine and pediatrics offer family consultation hours and special medical examinations. The MPA team ensures excellent practice organisation. In cooperation with Spital STS AG, Medbase Zweisimmen is actively committed to an integral and forward-looking care concept in the region.

You can move that

A varied role for everyone who can do pharmaceuticals – and wants to experience outpatient medicine. With us you combine your passion for medicines and pharmaceutical products with personal patient contact and a versatile everyday practice. You take responsibility for the practice pharmacy, work closely with the doctor responsible for the pharmacy and at the same time support our team at the reception, triage and in administrative tasks.

  • Responsibility for the practice pharmacy in cooperation with the doctor responsible for the pharmacy
  • Administration of medicines, administration of goods and narcotics controls
  • Maintenance of the pharmacy manual and participation in the assortment design
  • Support for reception, triage and appointment management
  • Administrative tasks such as patient data maintenance and documentation of ECG reports
  • Active teamwork and participation in team meetings and quality circles

What you bring

  • Completed training as a specialist pharmacy EFZ
  • Enjoyment of medicines and pharmaceutical products as well as interest in the outpatient medical environment
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Organizational talent, team spirit and willingness to actively contribute to everyday practice
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2)

What we offer

  • Scheduled working hours: Monday to Friday within the opening hours from 07:30 – 17:00
  • Working at eye level: Close and appreciative cooperation with doctors and MPA’s
  • Varied everyday practice: You combine your pharmaceutical expertise with patient contact, reception, triage and administrative tasks
  • More responsibility: Take on a central role in the organisation of the practice pharmacy
  • Co-design desired: Bring your pharmaceutical expertise in product range design, quality processes and the further development of the practice pharmacy
  • Interprofessional team: Bring your knowledge, actively shape processes and work together with different professional groups on high-quality patient care
