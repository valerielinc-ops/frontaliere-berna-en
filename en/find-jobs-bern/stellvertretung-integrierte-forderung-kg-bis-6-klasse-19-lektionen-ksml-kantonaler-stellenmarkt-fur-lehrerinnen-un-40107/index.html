Full description

Profile

We are looking for a substitute special education teacher for cycles 1 and 2 from January 11 to February 5, 2027. We are a rural school with 115 students (kindergarten - 9th grade). They are currently taught in five multi-grade classes. The school building is in the middle of the village, has good infrastructure, and can be reached by public transport via Signau. However, a car is an advantage.

Tasks

  • Substitute for integrated support, 13 lessons (kindergarten: 2 lessons; 1. Grade: 5 lessons; 2nd/3rd grade: 4 lessons; 4th-6th grade: 2 lessons)
  • Support for BVSA student, 6th grade: 6 lessons.

Requirements

Diploma as a special education teacher Diploma as a primary school teacher, kindergarten - 6th grade

We offer

  • School operations with manageable structures and a professional, motivated staff.
  • motivated students from a rural background
  • Modern infrastructure
  • Collaboration and support within the team
  • fixed-term employment

Requirements

  • Diploma in School Special Needs Education

  • Diploma as a Primary School Teacher KG - 6th grade

  • Experience in integrated support and accompanying pupils

  • Ability to work in multi-grade classes

  • Availability from 11 January to 5 February 2027

  • Strong teamwork skills

  • Understanding of the Swiss rural school context

  • Willingness to take on fixed-term assignments
