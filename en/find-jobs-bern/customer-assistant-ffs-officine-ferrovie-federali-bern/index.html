Customer Assistant

Bern, 01.03.2027, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

We offer our customers an excellent service before, during and after the trip. Our specialists in the field of services support travellers with and without reduced mobility along the travel chain and support the sales organization. This includes certain logistics tasks as well as ensuring the availability of lockers and SBB advertising materials in the station. In a dedicated team, you contribute visibly to customer satisfaction and shape the mobility of the future.Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • You individually assist travelers when boarding, disembarking and transferring, even if they have limited mobility.
  • Customer concerns such as luggage storage and the use of lockers are handled competently and reliably.
  • You support travelers in operating the ticket machines, provide timetable information and offer further assistance.
  • Logistical tasks such as the management of prospectuses are handled carefully.
  • If necessary, you can help out at other service locations and get involved flexibly.

You bring that with you.

  • You enjoy direct contact with people and you show a pronounced customer and service orientation.
  • You like to tackle a variety of tasks in the train station everyday life.
  • With Office applications you are safe and interested in new digital tools such as apps and mobile devices.
  • You have completed a basic vocational training EFZ.
  • In German you are fluent in word and writing (at least level C1). You have a good knowledge of a second national language or English at at least level B1 or are ready to reach this level within two years.
