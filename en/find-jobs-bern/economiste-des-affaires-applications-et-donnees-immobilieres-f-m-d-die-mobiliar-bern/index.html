Full description

This is what awaits you: You take on the professional responsibility for the IT system landscape of real estate management and ensure that our applications and data support the employees of the real estate sector optimally. This is what you do with us: In this central role, you ensure the smooth operation of the purchased specialist applications and coordinate first-level support as well as maintenance and releases. You actively promote the continuous development of the systems and implement technical requirements in close cooperation with the software providers. You are responsible for data management in the real estate sector and ensure the quality, consistency and availability of the data. Together with the specialist departments, you will further develop data structures and interfaces and thus create the basis for meaningful evaluations and well-founded decisions. In addition, you will help shape the digital transformation and future viability of the area by taking over (sub)projects and supporting the departments in the conception and implementation of innovative solutions. This is what you bring with you:

  • Completed higher education or training in business administration, business informatics or similar specialisation
  • Experience in real estate management or in the support of specialist applications, ideally in the financial sector
  • Good technical understanding and high affinity for data, digitalization and new technologies
  • Experience in managing/collaborating projects with strong analytical skills and communication skills
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method with pleasure in teamwork
  • Knowledge of the applications Immopac, Wüest Dimensions or GARAIO REM is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German and French Working with us: There is an established and motivated team waiting for you. Together we live a communicative, solution-oriented and independent way of working. Your workload is 60-80% and the place of work is Bern. More about our commitment as an employer can be found below. This is what we offer: Compatibility We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund. Training and development We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously. Advantages Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
