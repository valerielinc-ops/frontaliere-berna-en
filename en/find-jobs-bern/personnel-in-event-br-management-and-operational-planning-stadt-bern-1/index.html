Full description

Do you keep track when many threads come together at the same time? Do you think ahead, coordinate different people involved and have a strong sense of security, organization and good collaboration? Then you will help shape protection and rescue in Bern when it comes to planning events safely and optimally preparing the deployment of the fire brigade and rescue service.

Tasks

  • You accompany the planning of events, demonstrations and sporting events from a safety perspective and coordinate dates, responsibilities and requirements with those involved
  • You check security concepts and assess them, particularly with regard to fire protection, intervention and rescue services
  • You take part in coordination meetings, inspections and collaudations, represent the interests of Bern Protection and Rescue and ensure the follow-up of the agreed measures
  • You create and maintain operational plans for the rescue service and ensure that mission-relevant information is passed on in a timely manner
  • You work on projects and in the further development of fundamentals and use feedback from events to continually improve planning and communication processes

Requirements

  • You have completed basic commercial training or comparable training
  • Further training in the field of fire protection is an advantage and you ideally have experience in the blue light sector, in event management or in operational or security planning
  • You have good knowledge of assessing security concepts as well as operational and event planning
  • You communicate confidently and convincingly, can bring different interests together and present yourself competently to event organizers, authorities and partner organizations
  • You have very good PC user knowledge
