Full description

Meiringen Private Hospital is located in the impressive landscape of the Haslital, in the Bernese Oberland. Here people with all forms of mental suffering can find reception, treatment and care. The clinic with its own beds of 18 is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient basic care of the eastern Bernese Oberland. Care and care are based on current medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge.

The team of the Des Alpes 3, focusing on affective diseases and psychotherapy, provides care for patients who require extensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment. This task is based on the latest scientific findings in an individually defined treatment and care process. Psychosocial reintegration is considered a priority treatment goal.

Your tasks

  • They are mainly responsible for the implementation of patient-oriented professional care in the context of the care process and the personnel work.
  • You are committed to quality care in the sense of the Best Practice.
  • You actively shape the treatment process in interprof-essional cooperation.
  • You are jointly responsible for the organisational, personnel and professional management in cooperation with the focus manager.
  • You represent the Head of Focus in her absence.
  • You will participate in interdisciplinary treatment meetings.
  • You accompany and guide the students/learning staff in everyday care.
  • They contribute to improving quality and process processes.

Our expectations

  • You have completed a degree at the level of HF or equivalent SRC recognition in foreign diplomas, professional experience in psychiatry is advantageous.
  • You practice your profession with a lot of professionalism and commitment.
  • They are highly service oriented towards patients.
  • You have the ability to communicate in a way that is appropriate to your mind and enjoy the guidance.
  • You are an independent, responsible and resilient personality.
  • They are characterised by an exact and goal-oriented approach.
  • They attach great importance to a cooperative and social team behaviour.
  • They value a varied field of work and activity.

Our offer

  • You work in a future-oriented and innovative private clinic in interprof-essional treatment teams, where the high level of commitment for the care and recovery of patients is the first priority.
  • They attach great importance to the management system (care/medicine), in which the management team meets at eye level and sees the joint patient care as a core task.
  • An exciting, diverse and demanding working environment awaits you, in which you can contribute your full potential to the task and count on competent support.
  • We offer you generous opportunities for further training and the opportunity for career development.
  • Timely working and employment conditions with very good social benefits and attractive benefits such as holidays on your own birthday, free parking, various free sports activities in the sense of health promotion and various staff events
  • Own day care centre on the clinic area
