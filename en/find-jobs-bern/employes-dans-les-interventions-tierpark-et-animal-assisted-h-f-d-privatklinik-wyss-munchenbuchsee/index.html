Full description

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy. Special areas of expertise include the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders. The clinic offers its outpatient, daily clinical and inpatient services in the region of Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel and employs around 340 employees at all three locations. December 2026 or by appointment #naturnah, #sustainable, #respectful, #supportive, #flexible: Diversity in a family and benevolent environment awaits you! As part of our team Park/Tierpark (7 people, 5.5 full-time positions), you will take care of all the animals in our zoo – from alpacas, chickens and dwarf goats to emus, wallabies and turtles to pigs, rabbits and sheep. In addition to animal care, you accompany patients individually or in groups in the sense of animal-assisted interventions, which take place up to twice a day. Our beautiful and certified outdoor facility is a meeting and retreat for our patients and employees and is also well visited by the local population. An exciting mix of tasks in a diverse environment awaits you, which challenges you both in the respectful treatment of humans and animals and at the same time offers you a lot of space for your own development. This is what your professional day looks like with us

  • For the most part, you look after our animals and take care of their well-being and health by ensuring their welfare.
  • You carry out the work in the animal care independently – within the framework of the specifications of our specialist animal care. You actively contribute your own suggestions and expertise and help shape animal care.
  • In addition, you carry out regular animal training and bring ideas and suggestions for improvement to the further development of the zoo.
  • You perform independently and reliably animal-assisted interventions (up to a maximum of two hours per day) and support the therapeutic goal according to case management
  • You work in the interprofessional treatment team. You have a federal certificate of ability in animal care or several years of professional experience in the care of different animal species. Training in Animal Assisted Intervention (TGI) or interest in completing it completes your profile. You enjoy animal care and interaction with people. Patience and flexibility are your strengths?
