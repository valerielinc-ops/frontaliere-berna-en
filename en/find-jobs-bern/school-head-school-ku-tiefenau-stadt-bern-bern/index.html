Role overview

In the collective accommodation (KU) Tiefenau in Bern, the school office of the city runs, on behalf of the canton, a school with six "reception classes" (various cycles, multi-year classes) for refugee children and adolescents from kindergarten to the 9th.

Class who live in the KU and are prepared for transition to the regular school system.

The classes consist of 8 to max. 12 students, although there may be regular departures or new entries.
