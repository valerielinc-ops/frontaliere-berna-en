Full description

Portrait Our country school is located on the Langenberg, about 15 km south of Bern. In the Ziegelacker School House, the students of the kindergarten are taught in 6 classes up to the 6th grade. Our schoolhouse was reopened at the beginning of the 2021/2022 school year after a major renovation and offers excellent infrastructure and a wonderful view.

Tasks

They cover the lessons of our TTG teacher, who goes on maternity leave. The workload is between 10 and 12 lessons: TTG 10 lessons (5./6. grade 6L, 3./4.) Class 2L, 1./2. Class 2L) and M&I 2 lessons.

  • The workload is expandable with day school support on Monday and Tuesday noon as well as Tuesday afternoon from school end until 6 pm.
  • Working days: Monday and Tuesday
  • The start of the deputy is open and can be between the beginning of November and mid-December. The employment is expected to last until the end of the school year.

Requirements

  • They have an educational education.
  • They can be used flexibly and can start the deputy at any time from November.
  • You can work with several different classes and colleagues.

We offer

  • An employment according to the cantonal specifications.
  • Only half-class lessons with small groups.
  • Well-equipped TTG rooms and optimal framework conditions in the school building.
  • A free parking option, hourly public transport connections.
