Full description

Portrait The Bern and Biel School of Design is the canton of Bern's center of excellence for design, communication, media and art. Over 900 learners are trained in 17 professions at the vocational school. Compulsory physical education lessons strengthen movement, health, self-responsibility and the ability to work in a team.

We are looking for a personality as of August 1st, 2027

Tasks

  • You take over the technical management of the sports department, coordinate the team, plan teaching activities and sports facility occupancy and further develop sports lessons.
  • You plan, teach and evaluate 16 to 18 weekly lessons of compulsory physical education in EBA and EFZ basic education.
  • You design safe, motivating and differentiated learning settings for different learning requirements, assess the learners in a comprehensible manner and promote exercise, health, self-responsibility and the ability to work in a team. You will also be involved in sports events, school sports projects and quality development.

Requirements

  • You have a high school teaching qualification for sports, a teaching qualification for compulsory school with the required additional specialist qualification in sports or a qualification recognized as equivalent.
  • You have several years of teaching experience as well as experience in professional leadership or the willingness to take on this, ideally at secondary level II.
  • You lead cooperatively, communicate clearly and appreciatively and work independently and reliably. Enjoyment of working with young people and openness to different sports and performance levels round off your profile.

We offer

  • A versatile leadership and teaching role with scope for creativity and the opportunity to actively develop physical education lessons at our school
  • A committed staff, diverse groups of learners and a modern sports infrastructure
  • Attractive employment conditions and further training opportunities
  • Employment and remuneration are based on the cantonal law on the employment of teachers (LAG)
