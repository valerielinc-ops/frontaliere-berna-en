These tasks await you

  • Creating daily media monitoring
  • Cooperation in media releases, media inquiries and media conferences
  • Insights into strategic communication work from objective to evaluation

About us

With more than 19,000 students and around 8,000 employees, the University of Bern is one of the largest universities in Switzerland. It is excellently networked nationally and internationally and at the same time anchored in the region. With her research she sets a worldwide accents – especially in medicine, space and climate research she is at the forefront.
