Full description

Portrait The Salome Brunner Foundation works for around 300 children and young people with language and learning disabilities. With our various schools, we see ourselves as an important part of the educational landscape of the Canton of Bern. Langenthal Language Therapy School is a special primary school for children with complex language disorders. It comprises four classes in Cycle 1. The children are accompanied and supported in their development by specially qualified professionals. Small classes enable students to respond to their individual needs. Speech therapy and psychomotor skills take place mainly as individual therapy.
