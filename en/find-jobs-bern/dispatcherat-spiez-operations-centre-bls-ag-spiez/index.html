Full description

The rail network needs you! Our operating processes are planned down to the last detail – and this is exactly where you come in. With your vision, your organizational skills and a sense of subtleties, you make the decisions that make rail transport a little better every day. You ensure smooth processes, even better connections and that our partners and customers are always clear, timely and reliably informed.

This is waiting for you

  • You will ensure that rail operations run efficiently, non-discriminatory and on time – in all operating situations
  • You make optimal use of the available routes and intervals and get the most out of our network
  • You inform partners and customers correctly, purposefully and in a timely manner
  • You will analyze operational processes and help to continuously improve them

What you bring to the table

  • Training as a train traffic controller or equivalent qualification
  • French language skills A2
  • Italian language skills desired (can be learned)
  • Assertiveness and high security awareness
  • High affinity for the application of modern control systems
  • Enjoyment of analysing procedures and processes

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • We promote the most flexible planning of your tours and shifts
  • Get a GA travelcard 2. Class, a GA travelcard 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are surcharges for workloads at night and on weekends.
  • It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external training and an exciting internal learning and course offer are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
