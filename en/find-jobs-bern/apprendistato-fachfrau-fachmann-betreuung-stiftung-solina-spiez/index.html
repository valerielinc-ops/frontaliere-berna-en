Apprenticeship specialist / specialist supervision

More than theory Your future starts here

Apprenticeship specialist / specialist care 100% Spiez from August 2027 At Solina you learn the profession where it counts: in the middle of everyday life. You get involved, ask questions, make mistakes, get better and take responsibility step by step. We accompany you professionally and humanly. In teaching, you learn not only for your profession, but for life. In our resilience program “Gmeinsam starch” you strengthen your personality, learn to deal with stress and stay cool even when it gets difficult. Join the project Zackstark: Stay nicotine-free during training and refrain from smoking, snusing or vaping. We reward you with CHF 500.- francs per apprenticeship year. Your body is happy, your wallet too. In our regular workshops you train what counts in the profession: from communication to nutrition to personal care. Always varied and tailored to your work. 👉 By the way: At Solina you benefit not only during the teaching of many extras.
