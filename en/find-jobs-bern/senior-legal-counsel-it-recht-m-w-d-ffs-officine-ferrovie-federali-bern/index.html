(Senior) Legal Counsel IT Law (m/f/d) 60-80% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. (Senior) Legal Counsel IT Law (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or by agreement, 60-80 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You form the bridge between law and technology and manage the IT of SBB with noticeable effect. In an interdisciplinary environment, you work closely with IT purchasing, data protection, information security, IT architecture and AI governance and contribute to making digital solutions legally compliant, secure and pragmatic. You will analyze opportunities and risks, define scope for action and support the procurement of IT services and applications. Attractive working conditions and a culture of cooperation, trust and development await you. With your legal expertise, you make a valuable contribution to public transport. You can move that.

  • You advise the IT organization of SBB on legal issues, in particular on IT procurement, digitalization, artificial intelligence, cloud, software, data and outsourcing.
  • You negotiate, review and design contracts with technology providers, including framework contracts, SaaS, licenses, and development, maintenance and operation agreements.
  • You will develop and implement contract patterns, guidelines, processes and assessments for the use of new technologies, especially artificial intelligence.
  • You contribute to risk assessments and governance processes and provide clear basis for decision-making.
  • You develop and carry out trainings so that specifications are understood and applied in everyday life.

You bring that with you.

  • Conceptual and networked thinking as well as organizational and communication skills set you apart.
  • You develop simple and business-oriented solutions. In addition, you take responsibility and use the creative leeway for pragmatic solutions in a complex environment.
  • You bring a law degree. A Swiss attorney’s patent or further additional training is an advantage. In addition, you have several years of experience in a company or in a law firm.
  • Negotiating confident you are in German (C2) and have a very good knowledge of English (C1) as well as a knowledge of another national language.
  • You are IT-savvy and familiar with AI, especially GenAI.

Your reward and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This employment takes place in accordance with bond law, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
