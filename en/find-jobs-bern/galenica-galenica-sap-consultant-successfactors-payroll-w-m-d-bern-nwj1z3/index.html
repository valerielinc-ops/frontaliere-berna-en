Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

Do you want to actively shape the future of the HR system landscape? In this multifaceted role, you take responsibility for the operation and further development of SAP SuccessFactors Payroll and other HR applications. You will work at the interface between IT and HR, advise specialist departments and advance digitalization and optimization projects within the Galenica Group.

You can do that.

  • You ensure the stable operation, support and further development of SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll
  • You edit incidents and service requests and coordinate changes and releases
  • You will advise our HR departments, analyze their requirements and develop suitable solutions together with them
  • You can manage other SuccessFactors modules and selected non-SAP applications in the HR environment
  • You support integrations and interfaces and actively shape our HR system landscape
  • You participate in HR and digitalization projects – from introduction to tests and cutover to the hypercare phase

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience with SAP HCM Payroll or SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll as well as in application management and support
  • You know HR processes well and bring experience in requirements analysis, testing and change management
  • You communicate very well in German and well in English
  • Knowledge of other SuccessFactors modules, integration solutions, non-SAP HR systems or SAP HCM are a plus
  • You work independently, structured and analytically and convince with high customer, service and consulting competence
  • You enjoy working in a team, communicating clearly and enjoying learning new things, discovering technologies and collaborating across disciplines

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-shaping healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
