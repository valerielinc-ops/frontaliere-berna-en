Full description

The train is one of the safest means of transport in the world, according to statistics. But behind the numbers are people – people like you! 60 million riders a year trust in your skill and ambition to maintain the vehicles reliably and thus ensure their safety. This makes you proud as a professional.

Here’s what’s waiting for you

  • Carry out a wide range of maintenance work as well as demanding repairs on multiple units of the passenger vehicle fleet and their systems
  • Carry out fault analyses on vehicles and components
  • Support and representation of the vocational trainer in the Spiez workshop in the field of automatic learners
  • Cleanly record maintenance and fault data
  • Schedule, edit, and finalize vehicle stays

Requirements

  • Successfully completed apprenticeship as an automatic technician or equivalent training
  • Enthusiasm for guiding, motivating and developing young people
  • Computer skills – user
  • Very good command of German
  • Enjoy irregular working hours (night and weekend work)

Your benefits – you can look forward to them

  • We promote the most flexible possible planning of your tours and shifts
  • Get a GA 2. Class, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare pass.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are surcharges for work at night and on weekends.
  • It is possible to purchase an additional five to ten vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external further education and an exciting internal range of learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.

Requirements

  • Erfolgreich abgeschlossene Lehre als Automatiker:in oder gleichwertige Ausbildung

  • Begeisterung am Anleiten, Motivieren und Weiterentwickeln junger Menschen

  • EDV Anwenderkenntnisse

  • Sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse

  • Freude an unregelmässigen Arbeitszeiten (Nacht- und Wochenendarbeit)
