Full description

Introduction For our Movements sector, we are looking for a Response Methods (H/F) whose main mission will be to define, industrialize and optimize the methods of assembly of new products in project phase, as well as products in series life. Responsibilities

  • Create and support line assembly methods
  • Define assembly processes to achieve production objectives
  • Ensure continuous improvement of the flows produced during serial life
  • Create and maintain data from the assembly range in the information system
  • Define tooling and packaging requirements
  • Identify and support the need for and development of technical documentation
  • Define the need and evolution of database data
  • Realize and be the guarantor of the accuracy of the operating times of the assembly ranges
  • Participate in the industrialisation of the new products of the line by proposing the strategy of assembly
  • Conduct projects within the sector Profile
  • Diploma of Method Technician or Bachelor in Industry
  • Experience in industrial methods and monitoring of new products
  • Tool Knowledge Method: VSM, Takt-Time, operating time, indicator analysis
  • At ease with the usual computer tools, knowledge of SAP software would be an asset
  • Proactive and autonomous, with a very good spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Listening, good relationship and customer-oriented skills Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
