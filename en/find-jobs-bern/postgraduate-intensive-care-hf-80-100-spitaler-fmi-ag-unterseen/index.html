Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Two-year practice-oriented further training for/to the dipl. Expert/Expert Intensive Care NDS HF with close theory-practice reference.
  • Acquisition and deepening of expertise in the care, care and monitoring of intensive care patients.
  • Share responsibility for professional action in acute, complex and rapidly changing situations.
  • Continuous competence building through the change between theoretical teaching at the Pflege Bern Education Centre and practical work at the Interlaken Hospital.
  • Intensification of intensive care skills during one semester in the intensive care unit of the Hirslanden Klinik Beau Site with focus on operational areas, in particular cardiac surgery.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation as well as linking nursing expertise with medical therapy concepts.
  • Active participation in clinical decision-making processes and patient-oriented care.
  • Holistic care also for severely ill patients taking into account their individual needs and quality of life.
  • Accompaniment, advice and support of relatives in demanding treatment situations. Your skills
  • Completed training as a dipl. nurse HF or Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH.
  • at least 6 months of professional experience after completion of the diploma in an acute care unit
  • Strong ability for interprofessional and multiprofessional cooperation.
  • High sense of responsibility as well as strong observation and decision-making ability.
  • Quiet, calm and solution-oriented action
  • High resilience, flexibility and organisational capacity.
  • Empathetic and patient-centered care of patients and relatives.
  • Great cooperation of all professional groups: Everyone talks about interdisciplinary and cross-professional cooperation - we do it too! Projects and working groups are deliberately put together in an interdisciplinary and professional manner. And we also consciously promote the informal exchange between professional groups through various activities.
  • Family, leisure and work (you): We offer you attractive working (time) models, the purchase of unpaid leave or the opportunity to work part-time in all areas, including in the squad. We are happy to respond to individual needs.
  • Working with quality of life (you): Working with a view to a unique mountain world? A bath in the lake at work? Unlimited outdoor activities on days off? Is it your desire to live and work in a great tourist and recreational region? We fulfill it to you!
  • Friendly working atmosphere (you): New employees already feel it on the first working day: With us you will be welcomed and supported. Even in everyday life you openly approach each other, greet each other and like to help. We are proud of the way employees treat each other and take care of it. For questions about the job, please contact Michael Heyn, Head of Care Intensive.
