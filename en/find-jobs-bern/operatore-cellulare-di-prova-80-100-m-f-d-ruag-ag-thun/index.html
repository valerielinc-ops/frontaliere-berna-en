Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • You perform test runs on engines, transmissions and drive groups, document the results and prepare measurement and test reports.
  • You diagnose errors, analyze faults and perform initial troubleshooting on the test pieces.
  • You service and maintain the testing infrastructure, ensure its availability and perform minor repairs.
  • You document your work in the appropriate systems and support the engine and transmission workshop in assembly, detection and repair work.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic training EFZ as a commercial vehicle, agricultural machine or automotive mechatronics engineer
  • You bring good knowledge of mechanics and electronics. Experience in the testing of motors and transmissions as well as in the operation and maintenance of test facilities is advantageous.
  • You work independently, accurately and reliably and convince through team spirit as well as a strong sense of quality and responsibility.
  • You have good PC user knowledge and good German skills.

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

You can expect a technically versatile task in which you can put your diagnostic knowledge to practical use and deepen it. You work with sophisticated drive systems, take responsibility for the quality of your tests and are part of a team that together ensures reliable results.
