Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking for meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

As a competent sparring partner, you ensure the proactive operational and partly strategic consulting and support of superiors across departments and taking into account the corporate and HR strategy. As a member of the HR management team, you contribute to the further development of the HR of Spital STS AG, work in projects, accompany e.g. peer coaching, take on speaker activities within the framework of internal seminars and take care of a wide range of HR topics.

Admission immediately or by arrangement.

  • Initiative, flexible and proactive personality with entrepreneurial thinking
  • High willingness to learn and change
  • Strong implementation strength and willingness to shape
  • Affinity for personnel development, HR controlling and project management

  • Healthcare experience is beneficial
