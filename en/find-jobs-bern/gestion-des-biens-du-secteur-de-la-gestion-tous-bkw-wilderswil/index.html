Full description

Your tasks

  • You take over the deputy head of the Asset Service Management area, are responsible for investment responsibility for defined network assets and take over defined tasks in the network area
  • Together with internal and external partners, you will coordinate and plan network connections, maintenance, investment and operational contracts
  • The resources in the team as well as the use of external suppliers are coordinated as needed and accompany their execution
  • As a contact person for authorities, customers, landowners and internal departments, you ensure solution-oriented cooperation
  • You take necessary measures to ensure reliable network operation and coordinate disruptions and planned customer disconnections
  • Operational management in the 0.4 kV network and various roles in switchgear and plant management are part of your versatile area of responsibility. You bring that with you
  • You have a higher education as a master grid electrician:in or through a vocational examination as a grid electrician:in with a willingness to further education
  • Several years of professional experience in network or plant operation form a solid basis for this demanding function
  • With SAP, common office applications and mobile work equipment, you can handle things safely and efficiently
  • Clear verbal and written communication supports you in cooperation with different stakeholders
  • You work in a structured way, take responsibility and find suitable solutions even in demanding situations
