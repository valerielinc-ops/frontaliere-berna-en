Put to the Point

As a Teradata DWH operational and architecture resource, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that drives the strategic development of our data analytics platform. Your focus is on the stable operation and the architectural development of our Teradata Data Warehouse, supplemented by tasks in the field of data engineering and analytics architecture.

  • Date of entry: 01.10.2026
  • By agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 609-15984
  • Place of work: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the stable operation of the DWH platform with Teradata
  • Designing and further developing data architecture and integration processes
  • Responsible for the stable operation of the data warehouse platform with Teradata
  • Implemented data flows and models for analytics and AI applications
  • Analyze system malfunctions and initiate measures
  • Examine new technologies and ensure coordination of architecture with committees

This makes you unique

  • Completed degree in (economic) informatics or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in the operation and architecture of data warehouse systems, ideally with a focus on Teradata
  • In-depth knowledge in DWH architecture principles, ETL design, data modeling and analytics architectures
  • Experience with complex data structures, big data environments, on-prem and cloud technologies and modern data platforms
  • Knowledge of architectural documentation, feasibility analyses and experience with architectural boards or committee work
  • Strong analytical skills, structured working methods and strong communication and consulting skills
  • Good knowledge of a second official language
