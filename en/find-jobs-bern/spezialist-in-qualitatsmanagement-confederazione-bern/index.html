Put to the Point

Do you work exactly, service-oriented and like to work in a team? Then become part of our HR Service Center of the Swiss Army. Together with HR Administration, HR Infoline and Quality Management, we ensure the correct wage and personnel administration as well as the advice of around 6,500 civilian and 3,500 military employees. In quality management, you take on responsibility: You ensure compliance with our HR standards, monitor the process quality and consistently push for improvements. Did we get your interest? We look forward to welcoming you to the team soon.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Army Staff (A Staff) supports the Chief of the Army in the leadership of the Departmental Department of Defence and is responsible for the overall military planning and development of the Swiss Army. Here, in accordance with the political guidelines, the strategic foundations, the armed forces development and the army and armaments planning are developed and tasks in the international security context are performed. The command, control and coordination of all cross-cutting areas (personnel, finance, real estate) also fall under the responsibility of the Army Staff.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
