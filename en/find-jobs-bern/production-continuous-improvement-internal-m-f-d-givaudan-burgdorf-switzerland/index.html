Naturex AG, a company of the Givaudan Group, is a world-leading supplier of high-quality fruit and vegetable powders for the food and baby food industry. At our production sites Bischofszell (TG) and Burgdorf (BE), we combine the highest quality standards with sustainable and innovative solutions – for customers all over the world.

For our production site in Burgdorf, we are looking for a committed person as Production & Continuous Improvement Intern for the duration of 12 months (60-100%). In this varied role, you will work closely with the production manager, support the day-to-day business as well as various continuous improvement projects and get a comprehensive insight into our production processes. You also take responsibility for an exciting AI project – from planning and implementation to employee training.

Your main tasks

  • Analysis and documentation of workflows
  • Cooperation in continuous improvement projects (e.g. Pitstop)
  • Accompaniment and support of ongoing productions
  • Support for production planning
  • General support of the production manager in day-to-day business
  • Independent setup, planning, implementation and training of an AI/AI project according to the project mandate

Your profile

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in food technology or completed training as a food technologist
  • Very good PC knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as English skills are advantageous
  • Enjoy spending a lot of time directly in production and getting to know processes on site
  • Initial experience with AI/AI projects is an advantage (e.g. digital business or AI engineer), but no requirement
