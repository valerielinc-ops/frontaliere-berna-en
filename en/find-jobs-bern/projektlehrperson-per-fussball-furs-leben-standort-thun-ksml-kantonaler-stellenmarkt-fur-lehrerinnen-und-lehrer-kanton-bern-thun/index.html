Role overview

Portrait About the project "Football for Life

“Football for Life” is an integrative sport and education project that sustainably improves the chances of children in the 3rd-6th grade (cycle 2) – especially girls.

Through a combination of weekly language- and values-oriented football training and semi-annual cultural excursions, the project strengthens the movement behavior, the joy and self-confidence in speaking and the personal development of the participating children.
