Full description

The UniBE is ambitious. With us, they perform excellence and compete with the best.

Tasks

  • Academic research in finance with the aim of writing a dissertation
  • Collaboration in research projects of the institute
  • Participation in lectures and seminars at bachelor and master level
  • Supervision of bachelor and master theses

Requirements

  • Very successfully completed studies (university or ETH/TU) in business administration or economics with a focus on finance
  • In-depth knowledge of finance and interest in financial science issues
  • Joy of analytical thinking, scientific work and academic teaching
  • Commitment to high-quality research and interest in exchange with students
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

International reputation Application and contact Please send your complete application documents (motivation letter, CV and copies of university certificates and grade sheets) by e-mail to Prof. Dr. Philip Valta, Director of the Institute of Financial Management (philip.valta@unibe.ch). Applications shall be examined on an ongoing basis until the post is filled. With us, you create value. For themselves and society.

About Us

The Institute of Financial Management (IFM) is responsible for the teaching of financial topics within the framework of the Bachelor and Master courses at the Department of Business Administration of the University of Bern. The Institute’s employees also actively contribute to academic research in the areas of corporate finance, corporate governance and asset pricing.
