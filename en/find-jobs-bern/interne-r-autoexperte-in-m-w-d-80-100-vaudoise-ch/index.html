Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and forward-looking employer. The Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop. To strengthen our team we are looking for an internal car expert. The following exciting tasks await you:

  • the processing of expert opinions from a distance, including the examination of photographs and invoices
  • the organisation of the allocation of opinions to the independent experts
  • the creation and verification of cost proposals and invoices for vehicle repairs
  • the processing of damages according to the applicable guidelines
  • the determination of vehicle value before the conclusion of insurance contracts
  • the verification of plausibility in exceptional cases This distinguishes you:
  • You have a professional identity card as a body builder or mechanic
  • You have several years of professional experience in the vehicle sector or in the car expert
  • Your native language is German, good French skills are beneficial
  • They work independently, can set priorities, keep deadlines and have good stress resistance
  • They are motivated and committed, have strong social competencies, are happy to work in the team and with various partners
  • We offer you:
  • A pleasant working environment in a cooperatively shaped company that has been chosen to be the best employer several times
  • Numerous social benefits and employee benefits, including flexibility in the organisation of work
  • support in your further development and further training
  • The well-being of our employees as first priority We look forward to your complete application documents electronically (with CV, letter of motivation, copy of the certificates and diplomas).
