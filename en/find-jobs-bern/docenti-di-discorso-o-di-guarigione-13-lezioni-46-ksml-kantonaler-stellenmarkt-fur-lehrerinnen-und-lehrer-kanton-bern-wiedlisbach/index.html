Full description

Portrait We are looking for you! If you are motivated to participate in a team and work out new things, then you are exactly right with us!

Who are we? We are an association consisting of 5 municipalities (Farnern, Rumisberg, Wiedlisbach, Oberstufenzentrum Wiedlisbach and Attiswil).

Tasks

  • Diagnosis, therapy and promotion of children with speech, voice and swallowing disorders
  • Cooperation with teachers, curative school teachers: inside, parents and specialists
  • Documentation, funding planning and progress assessment
  • Participation in school location discussions and interdisciplinary clarifications

Requirements

  • Completed training as a speech therapist (recognized diploma) or the willingness to deal with the topic of language promotion.
  • Enjoy working with children in a school environment
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Teamwork and good communication skills

We offer

  • Varied and meaningful activity in the school environment
  • Current employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Collegial cooperation in a supportive school network
  • Good organisational framework conditions and professional exchange
  • a fully-equipped logo room in Wiedlisbach (no back-and-forth men's iron required)
