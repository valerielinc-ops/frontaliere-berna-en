Full description

University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The University Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy combines care, teaching and research and stands for high-quality psychiatric primary care as well as the treatment of complex, severe and rare clinical pictures. You can expect versatile tasks in a demanding and interdisciplinary environment. You work independently and independently, are at the same time part of a committed multi-professional team and move in both the outpatient and clinical setting. Your day-to-day work takes place during office hours and requires a high degree of independence, organizational talent and logistical thinking. In contact with adult psychiatric patients, you bring empathy and a sense of responsibility and take an active role in treatment and care within the context of the case management concept. In addition, you are ready to assume responsibility for medical and pharmaceutical measures and to continuously develop yourself – for example through internal or external training and the acquisition of a vocational training certificate.

Your tasks

  • You take responsibility for individual treatment planning and ensure continuous and needs-based care
  • You will independently conduct discussions and appointments with patients, relatives and other involved persons and specialist centres
  • You take on an active role in case management and coordinate the treatment together with the specialists and institutions involved
  • You work closely with a multi-professional team and actively contribute your nursing expertise to the treatment
  • You will maintain cooperation and networking within the UPZ and external regional partners
  • You document your work carefully and ensure a professional and traceable performance recording
  • You work independently, responsibly and with a view to efficient and economical service provision
  • You accompany learners and students in practice and get involved in vocational training

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF or FH
  • You bring at least two years of professional experience in psychiatry, ideally in the outpatient setting
  • You are interested in working according to the case management model (a CAS in case management is advantageous, but not a requirement)
  • You have a certificate as a vocational trainer or are ready to complete this further education
  • You bring a high level of personal responsibility as well as an economical and solution-oriented working method
  • You can basically stick to a fixed service planning and at the same time bring the necessary flexibility as a team player
  • You are ready to make appointments outside the office
  • You have a category B driving licence and are ready to use a car for official appointments

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
