Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey AG Biel is an official sales and / or service partner of the brands Subaru, Jaguar / Land Rover, Toyota, Kia, and Suzuki. We are looking for reinforcement for our team in Biel immediately or by agreement. Their tasks

  • They convincingly take care of the success-oriented and active sale of our wide model palettes
  • From the first contact, you support our customers, advise them on vehicle selection as well as on the various financing and insurance options
  • They ensure successful vehicle delivery as well as subsequent contact (especially customer satisfaction)
  • The daily support of your existing customer base as well as the acquisition of new customers is just as much a part of your task as the maintenance and management of the car park.
  • Maintenance of the online presence of our vehicles (tendering, photos, etc.)
  • Assistance at events/exhibitions and close cooperation with our departments (workshop, spare parts warehouse and customer service) is required Your profile
  • Advice and sales make you fun
  • You are a sales-oriented and negotiating personality and already have experience in sales and in the automotive industry (advantageous)
  • Your commercial flair, your customer and service orientation and your communicative strength characterize you
  • A high level of self-motivation, assertiveness and passion for cars round off your profile
  • They are fascinated by new technologies, trends and computers and show a high degree of flexibility and willingness to learn
  • They speak at least two languages (DE and FR) and hold the Cat's driving license. B

  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • At least 25 days of vacation As you can contact us Stefan Lanz, Head of Administration is looking forward to the complete application (e.g. CV, employment certificates, etc.) via the link "Apply now!".
