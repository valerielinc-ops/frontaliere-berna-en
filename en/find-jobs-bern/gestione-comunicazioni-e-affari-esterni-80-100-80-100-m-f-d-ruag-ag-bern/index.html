Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • You support the media work of RUAG and process media inquiries. For suitable topics, you take on the speaker function.
  • You research and prepare complex topics from companies, politics and security in a comprehensible and addressable manner and develop Q&As, statements, language regulations, briefings and other communication documents.
  • As a business partner, you contribute to communication on selected topics and projects and support specialist areas in the preparation and implementation of external communication measures.
  • You observe and analyze political, parliamentary and regulatory developments with relevance for RUAG.
  • You support the preparation and follow-up of sessions, political deals and selected dossiers.
  • You will research and maintain the basics of political stakeholders, create stakeholder profiles and support the preparation and follow-up of discussions, visits and political events.
  • Together with the External Affairs team, you ensure that relevant information from media, politics and the corporate environment is recognized at an early stage, processed in a structured manner and used for the external positioning of RUAG.
  • You work closely with the Director External Affairs and the Lead Media Work.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed university studies or a comparable education, ideally in communication, journalism/publicity, political science, economics or a related field.
  • You have several years of experience in journalism, media work or corporate communication and have a good understanding of the Swiss media landscape.
  • Experience in a media position or as a media spokesperson is an advantage.
  • You enjoy research and basic work, can quickly capture complex issues, identify relevant information and prepare them precisely, comprehensibly and appropriately.
  • You are familiar with Switzerland’s political system, its institutions and decision-making processes and are interested in public affairs as well as political and regulatory issues.
  • You will have an understanding of the special framework conditions of companies close to Germany and are interested in Swiss security and defence policy
  • Experience is beneficial.
  • You write stylishly in German, have good French and English skills.
  • You convince through a structured, reliable and independent working method, discretion and pleasure in working together in the team.

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
