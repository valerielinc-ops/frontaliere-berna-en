Full description

Portrait The Oberstufe Erlach is a regional school of the municipalities Erlach, Gals, Lüscherz, Tschugg and Vinelz. It is a rural area with a high quality of life, to which a good public transport network contributes. The small school offers some advantages, such as close cooperation, the family atmosphere and the good framework conditions.

Tasks

As a specialist teacher, you teach two mathematics classes (8th Kl. Sek and 9th Kl. Real), a mixed class in the NT (8th Kl.) and two IVE lessons (one 8th Kl. and one 9th Kl. each). The workload consists of 13 lessons, spread over three days of the week (from 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. on Mondays to Wednesdays, no lessons on Tuesday afternoons).

Requirements

They have a teaching qualification for upper secondary school (preferably in mathematics and possibly NT) or are in the appropriate training.

We offer

We offer temporary employment (at least until the end of the month, probably longer) at our school. A personal workplace is available, as well as a contemporary infrastructure. We enjoy a good working atmosphere
