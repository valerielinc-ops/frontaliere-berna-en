fast - exactly - practical , that is Medics; an initiative and flexible medical diagnostic laboratory based in Bern and Mels. In this position, you take on a central task in the operational finance area: Together with the Head of Finance, you ensure a smooth process and constantly develop processes and systems. Their structured, accurate and technically sound approach makes a decisive contribution to the financial stability and development of our Group.

  • Preparation and preparation of quarterly and annual financial statements according to OR for our Group companies
  • Co-responsibility for asset accounts
  • Cooperation in VAT and withholding tax accounts
  • Cooperation in the creation of budgets incl. Quarterly Controlling
  • Accounting for debtors and creditors incl. Payment and dunning services
  • Creating reports, evaluations and statistics
  • Basic commercial training with a federal certificate for finance and accounting or on the way to it
  • Several years of professional experience in finance and accounting incl. Final work
  • Experience with Abacus and the Microsoft Office range, especially Excel
  • Analytical thinking and quick comprehension combined with an independent working method
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as the ability to communicate complex issues appropriate to the addressee
