Shipments

For this challenging transformation project, we look for a figure with entrepreneurial spirit and strong management skills for the role of Responsible Real Estate SSR .

In this key function, you will assume the overall strategic and operational responsibility of the Real Estate of SSR portfolio across the Swiss territory. You will account directly to the Finance Manager and be part of the Finance Management team. Together with your management team, you will help define the future panorama of the SSR offices, providing a decisive contribution to the long-term safeguard of its public service mandate. In this context, consider buildings not only as infrastructure, but also as elements of connection between people, working environments and corporate culture. You will define and realize the real estate strategy of SSR with the aim of using spaces more efficiently, optimize costs in a sustainable way and ensure long-term sustainability of production and media locations.

  • Development and implementation of the SSR real estate strategy
  • Conduction and development of an active real estate organization across the Swiss territory composed of over one hundred collaborators and collaborators
  • Responsibility for a portfolio Properties of great strategic, technological and social importance
  • Implementation of headquarters strategy, continuous increase in operational efficiency and sustainable optimization of space and infrastructure costs
  • Development of the main SSR locations, in particular the Geneva/Ecublens and Comano production centres, as well as the Leutschenbach SRF site
  • Responsibility for masterplan, investment planning and long-term development of the Real Estate portfolio
  • Management of complex construction, renovation and transformation programs
  • Representation of the SSR's real estate interests towards the Management Committee, the Board of Directors, the authorities, the partners and other interest groups
