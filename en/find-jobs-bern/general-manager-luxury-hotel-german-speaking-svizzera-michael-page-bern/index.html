About Our Client Our client is a renowned luxury player located in the German-speaking region of Switzerland and part of a growing international group with ambitious expansion plans. The group is actively pursuing strategic growth opportunities, including future acquisitions, and is seeking an exceptional General Manager to lead this flagship property while contributing to the broader development of the organization. This is an outstanding opportunity for an entrepreneurial luxury hospitality leader who combines commercial acumen, strategic thinking, and operational excellence with a passion for delivering world-class guest experiences. The successful candidate will be a highly visible leader, equally comfortable engaging with guests, motivating teams, driving financial performance, and participating in strategic business decisions. Beyond leading the hotel, this individual will have the opportunity to grow within the organization and play an increasingly significant role as the group expands.
