Full description

Portrait Where others go on holiday! The school Diemtigtal is located in a natural, agricultural community with about 2200 inhabitants. Three kindergartens, six primary classes and two real classes are run at two locations. The approximately 200 children and young people from the eight villages of the valley are taught by 24 teachers. Our classrooms and specialist rooms are equipped with the most modern teaching and presentation aids. A very well-developed transport system and innovative day school offers enable a student- and family-friendly school and class organization.

Tasks

You teach a total of 4 L. Of these are 2L ERG, 1L Franz, 1L English. Classes will take place on Wednesday morning from 08.20-11.50.

Requirements

  • You are trained in the subject or have an adequate education. You are creative and like to try new things.
  • With your repertoire of methods, you pick up the children where they stand and enjoy their progress with them.
  • You are ready to work with the LehrerOffice and Klapp.

The cooperation at school events (Christmas, school closure, project days, sports days) we would be very happy.

We offer

  • Excellently equipped classrooms, Clevertouch wall panels, visualizers, iPads and laptops for all students.
  • Lots of freedom and enough financial resources
  • A wonderful place to work in the most beautiful nature, only 20 minutes from Thun.
  • An exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility and diverse design possibilities

A free parking option directly at the schoolhouse.
