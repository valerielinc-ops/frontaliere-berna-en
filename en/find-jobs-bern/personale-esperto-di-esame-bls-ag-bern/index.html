Full description

You climb into the Mutz cab from Biel to Berne and create decisive moments in everyday train life. With your calm, appreciative manner, you guide train drivers safely and comprehensibly through their examination – clearly in the assessment, engaged in the accompaniment. So you make sure that our trains roll reliably and our customers arrive safely.

This is waiting for you

  • Responsibility for fair checks in real railway operations
  • Co-design of modern and economic auditing processes
  • Close cooperation with specialists in the railway and training environment
  • Cooperation in event analyses with a direct impact on safety
  • You actively contribute your know-how to projects relating to railway operations

This is what you bring with you

  • Training as a train driver: in passenger or freight transport and the willingness to acquire further necessary knowledge
  • Experience as an examination expert:in or speaker:in training an advantage
  • Further education in adult education or coaching – or the willingness to develop in it
  • Quiet, appreciative communication to radiate security and trust in exam situations
  • Very good knowledge of German and French – you are sure to move in both languages, Italian orally advantageous

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year
  • Financial support for external further education as well as an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
