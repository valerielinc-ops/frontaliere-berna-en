Specialist in professional retraining 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Make Switzerland move with us. Professional retraining maneuver specialist Biel/Bienne, 01.01.2027, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. At SBB Cargo, you are at the heart of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 170,000 tonnes of goods every day, removing 15,000 heavy goods vehicle journeys from the road and reducing the CO2 footprint by around 460,000 tonnes per year. An innovative work environment awaits you, where you make SBB Cargo move with your colleagues and where you follow varied and paid training in sorting. You benefit from real development prospects as a shunting locomotive driver or Cargo locomotive driver. Training start date January 1, 2026 and May 1, 2026. We make a point of offering attractive working conditions, even in the event of irregular hours. With us, night and weekend work entitles you to additional pay (e.g. 16 francs per hour for Sunday work). Time increases of between 10 and 30% are also granted for night work. This means that you have more free time than employees who do not work nights or weekends. What you can make move.

  • As a maneuvering specialist, you contribute to reliable and punctual service for our customers. You move, form and break down freight trains in marshalling yards and training yards.
  • You prepare trains for departure, conduct complex maneuvering movements with radio communication and secure vehicles parked within the perimeter of the tracks.
  • Handling simple infrastructure installations such as points, barriers and access gates is part of your daily life.
  • Carry out the coupling and uncoupling of locomotives and freight wagons, check the technical condition of vehicles and loads and assess their suitability for operation.
  • Ensure the delivery and collection of wagons from customers and maintain direct and regular exchanges with customers. Maneuver collaborator | CFF for more information. What you bring.
  • You are attentive, you demonstrate concentration and good physical resistance to carry out this demanding task as part of team work involving 24-hour activity. You are also willing to work outside in all weathers.
  • As all instructions and documentation are available in electronic form, you will quickly become familiar with the digital tools.
  • You have successfully completed initial training (at least a CFC or equivalent diploma) and you have, ideally, professional experience in the field of crafts, logistics or a similar sector.
  • You have a good command of French, both spoken and written (level B2 at least).
  • Your color vision is perfect (neither protanomaly nor deuteranomaly) and there is no entry in your criminal record or the prosecution register.
