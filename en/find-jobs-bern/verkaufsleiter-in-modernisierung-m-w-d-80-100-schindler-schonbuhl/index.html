We are AS Elevators

AS Elevators is a Swiss partner for the assembly, modernization and maintenance of elevators. The diversity and individualization of our products characterizes us. Be as unique as we are!

Experience the AS Spirit as

Sales manager* in modernization (m/f/d) 80-100 %

Through your start at AS Elevators you benefit from an appreciative and family environment. In this way, we promote cohesion among employees through our du-culture and various events. Find out more about us here: Video

What to expect

  • Management of the business unit modernization in the office as well as responsibility for achieving the budget specifications through optimal use of resources
  • Technical and disciplinary management of subordinate sales staff
  • Development of strategies and action plans for processing the respective market/customer segments on the basis of portfolio analyses
  • Coaching of sales employees with the aim of improving their skills (in the areas of lead management, project management, negotiation,...)

What you bring

  • Basic technical or commercial training completed
  • Additional training in sales
  • Several years of sales and management experience
  • Experience in the elevator industry is an advantage
  • Project management knowledge and basic technical knowledge and understanding
  • Good knowledge of the local language, other languages beneficial

Your many advantages

  • Excellent development opportunities: Personnel development is lived with us. We provide our employees with various national and international talent programs and support them temporarily and financially with training and further education.
  • Work-life balance: 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, part-time work and unpaid leave.
  • Healthy working environment: High health & safety standards and own occupational health management including social advice and health promotion.
  • Own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • Compensation for your lunchtime.
  • Employee benefits: generous participation in the public transport and fitness subscription, fleet discounts, reduced purchase of Reka money, reduced insurance premiums and various benefits.
  • Cultural diversity and inclusion & diversity within the company.
