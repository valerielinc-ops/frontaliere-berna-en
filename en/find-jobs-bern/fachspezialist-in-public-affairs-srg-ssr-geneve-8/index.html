Full description

The policy determines the framework conditions of the SRG. That is why dialogue with political actors and mutual understanding is central. The Public Affairs Department of SRG is responsible for the strategic management of decision-making processes at the interface between politics, business and society, is in ongoing exchange with political stakeholders and accompanies legislative processes in the Federal House.

This is your contribution.

As a specialist: in Public Affairs, you independently conduct political dossiers, contribute your political know-how to specialist groups and accompany parliamentary business from A to Z.

  • Responsibility for public affairs projects and representation of public affairs in internal project and working groups
  • Research & analysis, development of scenarios and development of concepts and strategies (including positions and measures) in the area of public affairs
  • Creating fact papers, political arguments, presentations and texts for various internal and external channels
  • Monitoring of the political and regulatory environment, strategic assessment of relevance for SRG, regular management reporting
  • Support for political stakeholder management, situational contacts and advocacy towards political stakeholders

They inspire us.

  • Proven several years of experience in public affairs in a company, association or agency
  • In-depth knowledge of political processes and institutions, familiarity with public affairs tools and methods
  • Political or media science background, great understanding of media policy issues and public service
  • Style confident in German with very good knowledge of the French language. Knowledge of other national languages is beneficial
  • Great interest and understanding of the challenges of the media service public, solution-oriented approach

So we inspire you.

  • They contribute to the social added value of a public media house
  • You will work on diverse content and exciting challenges in a dynamic environment and have the chance to work in a multilingual working environment and with different cultures.
  • You have a lot of personal responsibility for your area of activity and the opportunity to help shape the transformation of the company
  • Your place of work is Bern, home office is possible

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion-forming and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
