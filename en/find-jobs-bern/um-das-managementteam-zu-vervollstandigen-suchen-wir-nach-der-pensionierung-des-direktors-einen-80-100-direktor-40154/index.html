Full description

Portrait Tramelan Elementary School consists of 21 1H-8H classes, two support groups and a host group. These classes are divided into 4 school buildings. The teaching team consists of more than 60 teachers, including 18 specialised teachers. Our school is headed by a management team consisting of a director, a special education director, a vice-principle 1 and a vice-principle 2.

Tasks

  • Organisation of the teaching and guidance of the school
  • Implementation and adaptation of a strong settlement project
  • Management of relations and communication with teachers, parents and school-related services
  • Collaboration with political bodies
  • Overall supervision of the school and the teaching teams in cycles 1 and 2
  • Periodic evaluation of teachers
  • Administrative management of the school in close collaboration with the secretariat
  • Replacement management
  • Participation in school and local life

Profile required

  • Recognized pedagogical training with at least 5 years of experience in public school
  • FORDIF or training deemed equivalent
  • Management experience and management
  • Good knowledge of primary schools
  • Extended skills in team coordination, planning, piloting and administrative monitoring
  • Very good sense of communication and analysis
  • Clear and collaborative conduct
  • To the benefit of a strong and extensive network would be an advantage
  • Ensure compliance with the budgetary framework and appropriations

We offer

  • An attractive workplace, a stimulating educational environment led by a dynamic and committed team with a strong inclusive policy. We maintain close links with the school's partner services.
  • An 80% position with the possibility of completing with teaching lessons and/or administrative pool.

This position is part of the Executive Board (155 per cent).
