Full description

Portrait Welcome to the Cycling School Belpberg. The Cycle School Belpberg is a rural school and consists of a basic level and a 3rd - 6th grade. The school is based on the pedagogical approach of multi-year classes with mixed learning. The cycle school is part of the Belp folk school and represents a complementary school model for it.

Tasks

They shall provide information from 11. January 2027 16-20 lessons at the base level. They complement the workload to an existing and experienced teacher. Participation in hour planning and working days possible. Possible assumption of the class teaching office together with the job partner.

Requirements

  • A big heart for children
  • Joy at work in multi-year classes
  • Teamwork for cooperation and agreement with teachers
  • Ideally, training as a teacher or equivalent experience

We look forward to a dedicated teacher who likes to work in age-mixed classes.

We offer

  • A manageable, family-run schoolhouse with a beautiful view of the mountain range
  • Temporary employment with option to take over
  • 15 Lessons in Teamteaching
  • Good support from the step team, the curative teacher and the school management
  • Possibility to actively shape processes within the stage and contribute ideas
  • Many group rooms
  • Motivated, committed and mixed-age college
  • School social work
  • School garden and forest days
  • Parking directly at the school building, no public transport connection
  • Working with Escola (communication with parents)
