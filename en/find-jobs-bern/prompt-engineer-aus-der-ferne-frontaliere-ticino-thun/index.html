Full description

We are looking for a remote Prompt Engineer to design, test, and optimize conversational prompts and workflows for artificial intelligence systems. The resource will work closely with product, development and internal stakeholders to transform business needs into clear, measurable and reliable instructions for language models.

Liability

  • Design, rewrite and optimize prompt for specific use cases.
  • Test the quality of the generated responses and improve accuracy, consistency and robustness.
  • Define evaluation criteria and quality metrics.

Analyze errors, edge houses and abnormal behaviors of the model.

  • Document versions, experiments and operational guidelines.
  • Collaborate with technical and product teams to integrate prompts into application flows.

Requirements

Excellent writing, analysis and problem solving.

Experience with LLM, chatbot or AI tools.

Ability to translate complex requirements into structured instructions.

Iterative testing and validation of results.

Good knowledge of technical English.

Experienced with JavaScript, API, workflow automation or data analysis.

Preferential skills Prompt chaining knowledge, structured output and model evaluation.

Familiarity with RAG, agents, tool use or automation systems.

Experience in technical documentation and cross-functional collaboration.

Sensitivity to quality, safety, bias and reliability of AI responses.

We offer

Dynamic and innovation-oriented environment.

Possibility of working on high impact AI products.

Organizational flexibility and international collaboration.

Insertion into a technical team with strong attention to quality and experimentation.
