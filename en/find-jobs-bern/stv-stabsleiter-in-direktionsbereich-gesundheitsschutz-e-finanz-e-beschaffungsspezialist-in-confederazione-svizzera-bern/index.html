Put to the Point

With your networked thinking and your sense of business and political connections, you contribute to an effective planning and control of our management department.

  • Entry Date: Immediately
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20672

You can make this contribution

  • Represent the staff management in technical, personnel, organizational and strategic matters
  • Together with the management team, perform planning and control tasks of the management department and manage the financial controlling
  • Coordinate parliamentary affairs, oversee their contents, dates and processing status and keep the management department continuously informed
  • Ensure procurement and contract management, process procurement and subsidies economically and legally compliantly and advise contract managers on procurement law issues
  • Support and relieve the management team in business tasks and issues as well as in cross-divisional tasks and activities of the staff

This makes them unique.

  • Completion of a technical college or higher technical school, preferably in business administration or a comparable field of specialisation
  • Knowledge and experience in public procurement
  • Strong capacity for business, analytical and networked thinking and action
  • Great interest in political processes and health issues
  • Experience with political or parliamentary affairs is advantageous
  • High personal responsibility, commitment and willingness to cooperate as well as an independent and structured working method
  • Very good knowledge of the Microsoft Office range, in particular Excel, as well as SAP knowledge is advantageous
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and passive knowledge of the third official language
