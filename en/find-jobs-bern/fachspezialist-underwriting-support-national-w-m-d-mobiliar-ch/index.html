Full description

This is what awaits you

In this role, you are part of the support team, which together supports the underwriting teams for our corporate customers.

This is what you do with us

The core of your job is supporting corporate insurance underwriters in the property, tech and liability industries. You act as a link between underwriting and general agencies.

In your role, you are responsible for recording and managing the contracts as well as for processing final billings.

With your IT affinity, you serve supporting IT systems and handle administrative business processes.

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic training in insurance, preferably further training at the level of specialist card
  • Professional experience in the tech and substantive industries, preferably liability
  • Team-capable, customer-oriented and learning personality
  • Precise, forwarding operation
  • Self-employment and service orientation
  • Fluent German skills, French and Italian are a plus

Working with us

A team of motivated colleagues in the whole area is waiting for you. Together you live a busy and solution-oriented way of working, in which the team spirit is promoted in addition to the independent work culture.

Here you can choose your place of work between Bern and Zurich.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
