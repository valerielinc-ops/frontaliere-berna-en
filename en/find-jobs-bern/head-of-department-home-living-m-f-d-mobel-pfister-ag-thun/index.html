We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon? In Thun, we are opening our newest pfister branch – and for this we are looking for a dedicated team that is there from the beginning. Take the opportunity to help shape the new location, build something together and make the lives of our customers in the Bernese Oberland even more beautiful.

Your new tasks

  • Responsibility for one or more product groups
  • Ensuring the product range appearance at the POS with an inspiring product presentation
  • Participation in assortment meetings and implementation of the corresponding concepts
  • Customer service with own sales responsibility
  • Proper processing of customer orders
  • Responsibility for achieving the KPIs assigned to you
  • Planning and leadership of a team
  • Leadership experience in the management of a small team, as well as the motivation to start professionally as a lateral entrant in the interior segment
  • A strong passion for sales and pleasure in competing and achieving success together
  • Experience in trade or sales as well as a good feeling for the needs of the clientele
  • Teamwork, openness and a strong customer orientation distinguish you
  • An existing qualification as a vocational trainer or the willingness to acquire it. Delight in accompanying and promoting learners as well as supporting their personal and professional development

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
