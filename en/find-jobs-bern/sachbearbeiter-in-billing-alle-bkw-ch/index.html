Full description

Your tasks

  • You ensure the completeness and correctness of the master and billing data and plan necessary adjustments in advance with
  • You carry out the billing processes from data delivery to billing and monitor the set dates
  • You analyze and correct errors in billing independently and take care of investments that have not yet been billed.
  • For the creation of evaluations, you maintain the relevant databases and data bases
  • For tests, release changes and projects, you actively participate and document change requests and incidents
  • The billing of the billed assets as well as deputy tasks within the billing team round off your area of activity

You bring that with you

  • You have a basic commercial training and several years of professional experience, ideally in the energy industry or a comparable environment
  • Experience in customer support as well as in project and quality management strengthen your profile
  • You have in-depth knowledge of billing processes and use SAP IS-U as well as other accounting and process systems established in the energy industry
  • You approach tasks independently and with commitment and contribute with your constructive nature to a good cooperation in the team
  • Structured, reliable and motivated work as well as the joy of discovering and implementing new solutions set you apart
  • You can certainly use fluent German skills in customer dialogue and consulting, knowledge of French gives you an advantage

About us

BKW offers its customers internationally complete solutions in the fields of energy, buildings and infrastructure. We employ around 11,500 people. Together we build networks, produce energy, plan and realise energy-efficient buildings and design sustainable living spaces of tomorrow. For this we need you: people with ideas and commitment. Make a valuable contribution to society and our environment with us!
