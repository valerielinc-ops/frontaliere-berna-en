Kubectl apply -f «myOutstandingCV.yaml» - if you share this humor, then you are at the right place with us. You do not want to simply run IT, but actively shape modern cloud platforms based on Kubernetes? Then become part of our young cloud platform team and develop the Secure Private Cloud Bund with us, a powerful and secure platform for Switzerland’s internal security.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 485-20990
  • Location: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Operation, monitoring and technical development of complex, highly available and secure Kubernetes platforms, services and infrastructures
  • Developing cloud-native platform architecture and delivering conceptual work
  • Planning and implementing maintenance, releases, migrations and technical changes
  • Optimize Kubernetes-based cloud infrastructures and introduce new technologies and integrations, taking into account architecture and security requirements
  • Engineering of cloud-native infrastructure services (Prometheus, Grafana, ArgoCD, HELM etc.)
  • accompany technical projects and further developments, document expertise and support DevSecOps teams

This makes you unique

  • Completed computer science training or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional experience as an ICT system specialist or in a comparable function
  • Very good knowledge of Kubernetes infrastructures and the operation of complex ICT systems
  • In-depth knowledge in the analysis, correction and optimization of complex technical faults as well as in the further development of platforms and services
  • Experience and skill in the development of platform architectures and conceptual activities
  • Analytical and conceptual skills as well as active knowledge of two official languages and English skills
