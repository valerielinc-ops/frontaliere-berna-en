Full description

Do you want to take responsibility, look after varied objects and make a real difference? Then you are exactly right with us!

Tasks

In this versatile function, you manage several objects on site and are the contact person for our customers.

Your tasks include

  • Operation and maintenance of several technical and infrastructural properties.
  • Small repair work on house equipment and facilities.
  • Coordination and control of external craftsmen in maintenance and renovation work.
  • Creating offers for our customers.
  • Assist in organizing and implementing projects.
  • Support for technical and organizational issues.
  • Contact person for owners, administration, tenants, authorities, neighbours and internal bodies on technical issues.
  • Maintenance and maintenance of building and outdoor facilities.
  • carrying out cleaning and environmental work.
  • Ensure the proper appearance of the property.
  • Picetting service missions.

Requirements

You live service and work with conviction for quality and reliability. You bring with you:

  • Completed training as a professional:woman maintenance/housekeeper (or similar training).
  • Several years of professional experience in the infrastructural/housekeepers sector.
  • Technical know-how is mandatory.
  • Knowledge of fire detection/sprinkler systems and building control system.
  • Independent, structured working methods and a sense of responsibility.
  • Strong service mentality and organisational talent.
  • Solution-oriented thinking and openness to administrative tasks.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as English skills are advantageous
  • Affinity and joy in digital work
  • Readiness for Picetting Operations
  • Valid driving licence Cat B and residence in the Bern region

We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational safety and improve comfort in buildings. In this way, we minimize costs and create optimal conditions so that our customers can concentrate on their core business.

As a company of Equans Switzerland, the leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition.
