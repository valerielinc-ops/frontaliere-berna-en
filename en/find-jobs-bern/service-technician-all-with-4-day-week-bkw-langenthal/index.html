Full description

We make living spaces worth living in.

100 % in a 4-day week Become part of a committed and motivated team in a dynamic company with a 4-day week.

Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for servicing refrigeration and air-conditioning systems (VRF and split systems, no commercial refrigeration) and ventilation systems.
  • You identify sources of faults and rectify malfunctions.
  • You carry out air volume measurements and commission systems.
  • You carry out maintenance on our systems.
  • You advise customers on site.

What you bring

  • A completed vocational apprenticeship as a refrigeration technician or several years of experience in servicing HVAC systems.
  • You are flexible and work independently and accurately.
  • You keep an overview even during hectic periods.
  • Enjoyment of a high level of personal responsibility.
  • Customer-oriented actions and thinking.
  • You have a Category B driver's license.
  • You are between 25 and 50 years old.
