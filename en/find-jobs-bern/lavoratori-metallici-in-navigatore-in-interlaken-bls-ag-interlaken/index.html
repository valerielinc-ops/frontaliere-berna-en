Full description

We like lakes, we like nature, we like our guests – and we like people who want to actively shape the future of BLS shipping together with us. We offer our employees a unique, varied and stable environment. With us, you can discover new horizons by giving us the opportunity to develop your organizational talent in a dynamic environment and to shape the development of operational processes decisively.

This is waiting for you

  • Our national and international guests provide convincing and competent advice and care
  • Checks and sales of tickets
  • Preparation and follow-up of ships
  • Collaboration in nautical service (we train you)
  • Maintenance and renewal of our fleet in winter

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training metalworkers: in EFZ, experience in welding and construction as well as several years of professional experience
  • Category B driving licence
  • Enjoyment of dealing with guests from different cultures
  • Language skills: Very good German, oral English & French skills (or willingness to learn them)
  • Strong sense of security and responsibility
  • Readiness for irregular work assignments (weekend / evening)

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2. Class or a half-tax subscription as well as attractive driving benefits for the family and free international free tickets.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work.
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year.
  • We contribute 60 percent of the pension fund contributions.
  • Generous gifts of seniority from five years of service.
  • A dreamlike place of work awaits you in the heart of the Bernese Oberland.

About BLS shipping

White ships. Blue water. Fresh air. The mountains of the Bernese Oberland. What almost sounds like cliché is nothing more than the fascination of shipping. For around 120 employees on and on Lake Brienzer and Thunersee, this is part of daily reality.
