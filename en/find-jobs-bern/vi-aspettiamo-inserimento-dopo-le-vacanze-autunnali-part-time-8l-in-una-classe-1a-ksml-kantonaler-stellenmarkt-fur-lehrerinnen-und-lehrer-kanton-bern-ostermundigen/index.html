Full description

Portrait

We are one of four semi-autonomous neighborhood schools in Ostermundigen. At our school, 70 teachers teach about 350 children and young people from kindergarten to 6th grade. The school is centrally located in Ostermundigen at Bernstrasse 60. Our school has different class types: We run a KbF, an intensive German course and classes of the special elementary school. We also have a day school. We attach great importance to integration. I am careful!

Responsibilities

  • From the 2nd quarter onwards, you will take on a part-time job at one of our 1st classes and teach 8 lessons.
  • Subjects: German, NMG, Musical Primary School, Sports.
  • Working days: Tuesday morning 3L and afternoon 1L until 14.30h, Thursday morning 4L.
  • You will guide and accompany the students in their learning and development.
  • You cultivate the exchange in the class team.
  • You will actively participate in projects and special events of the level and help shape our school.

Requirements

  • You have a teaching qualification of cycle 1 or are in training for it.
  • You enjoy and are interested in schoolchildren from different cultures.
  • You are creative, patient and resilient.

We offer

  • We offer permanent employment (alternating 1st/2nd class).
  • We are a competent, committed and helpful team.
  • We maintain good cooperation within the level.
  • We have an attractive place to learn and work with good infrastructure.
  • We are easy to reach by public transport.
