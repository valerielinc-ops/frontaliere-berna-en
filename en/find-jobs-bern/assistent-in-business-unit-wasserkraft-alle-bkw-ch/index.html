Full description

You keep the overview, like to organize, think a step ahead and bring order to many different topics? Then you are exactly right as an assistant hydropower: You support the management and team in a wide variety of topics and ensure that everything runs smoothly in the background.

Your field of activity

  • You organize and lead the secretarial and assistance tasks for the management of the business unit Hydro Power and ensure a smooth administrative process
  • As a proactive support, you coordinate appointments, correspondences, sessions and presentations and carefully prepare the basis for decision-making.
  • VR mandates and meetings are administratively supervised by you, documents prepared and the corresponding secretariats managed
  • Travel at home and abroad as well as internal and external meetings, workshops and events you plan and organize independently
  • Also for topics, projects, personnel administration, expenses, invoices and other organizational concerns you tackle
  • Support the management team and employees of the Hydro Power business unit and ensure that important information and documents are in the right place at the right time

You bring that with you

  • A commercial education or a comparable qualification forms the basis for your activity
  • You bring experience in an assistant function and are used to keeping an eye on several topics at the same time
  • Very good oral and written German skills distinguish you
  • With MS Office, in particular Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and SharePoint, you are safe and routine
  • Self-initiative and independence are part of your way of working, reliability and trustworthiness are a matter of course for you, at the same time you are flexible and service-oriented
  • As a team player with an open personality, you always keep track, set the right priorities and work reliably and quality-consciously.
