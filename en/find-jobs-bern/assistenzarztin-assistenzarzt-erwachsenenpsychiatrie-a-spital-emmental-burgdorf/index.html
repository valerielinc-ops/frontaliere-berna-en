Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital offers demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and takes into account their own resources. In our manageable company, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. We regard individuality as enrichment. We offer jobs at the two locations Burgdorf and Langnau. We are a SIWF-approved training institution.

Do you want to work psychiatrically, be increasingly self-employed and at the same time benefit from a close specialist supervision? Then you can expect a varied activity at the interface between outpatient psychiatry, psychiatric emergency care and somatic medicine. The position combines approximately equal parts of the work in our psychiatric ambulatory with the work on the interdisciplinary emergency at the Burgdorf location. Your main tasks

  • You diagnose and treat the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases under specialist supervision
  • You are looking after your own patients independently in the ambulatory setting
  • You conduct psychiatric assessments and crisis interventions on the Interdisciplinary Emergency
  • You work in the consiliary and liaison psychiatry within the somatic hospital
  • You work interprofessionally with the medical disciplines, psychology, care and other professional groups
  • You contribute to the preparation of psychiatric reports

Your profile

  • You have completed your medical studies with a Swiss diploma or with a degree recognised by MEBEKO
  • Psychiatric prior experience, especially from the stationary area, is advantageous, but not a requirement
  • You are interested in the full breadth of adult psychiatry and psychiatric emergency situations
  • You work independently, structured and responsible
  • You also perform sovereignly in demanding clinical situations
  • Interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation makes you happy
  • You are willing to increasingly take responsibility for your own outpatient treatment activity
