Full description

The "Basics & Safety" area creates the prerequisites for safe operation of the railway infrastructure that complies with standards and regulations. Together with your colleagues, you will analyse events and determine improvement measures. You will also work on topics relating to emergency infrastructure management and operating regulations.

This is waiting for you

  • Implementation of event analyses including determination of improvement measures
  • Recording and documenting events
  • Collaboration in claims management for operational events (insurance processing) incl. billing
  • Audits and video evaluations to ensure safety on railway sites
  • Development of security concepts for events at railway stations

What you bring to the table

  • KVöV with a degree as a train traffic controller or equivalent training
  • Strong process understanding and good analytical skills
  • Methodological competence for process modelling and for the implementation of CIP moderation
  • Very good knowledge of driving and operating regulations as well as operational management systems
  • Winning appearance and cultivated manners in contact with customers and authorities
  • Training RTE 20100 (safety manager) and RTE 20600 (switching authorization)

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA travelcard 2. Class, a GA travelcard 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Organize your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year
  • Financial support for external training courses as well as an exciting range of internal learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
