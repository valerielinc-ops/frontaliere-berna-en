Full description

Your field of activity

  • Management and coordination of risk management within BKW Power Grid as well as ensuring reliable and transparent risk reporting
  • Analysis, evaluation and monitoring of material risks and opportunities to ensure sound decision-making as well as advice and support of risk owners and managers in the risk assessment and definition of measures
  • Further development of methods, processes and tools for risk assessment, early warning, action control and reporting
  • Analysis and optimization of end-to-end processes in close cooperation with the team and the departments for the active and continuous development of the process landscape.
  • Moderation of risk reviews and process workshops as well as support for strategic projects for the successful implementation of company goals

Your expertise

  • University degree in business administration, industrial engineering, a technical or scientific discipline or comparable education
  • Several years of experience in risk and process management as well as profound knowledge in risk assessment, risk management and process optimization
  • Experience in cross-divisional projects as well as security in the moderation of workshops and in the preparation of demanding basis for decision-making
  • Knowledge in the energy industry or in a technical environment is a plus
  • Strong analytical and quantitative skills as well as a structured and solution-oriented approach
  • Holistic thinking skills with the ability to link risks, processes and strategic objectives
  • Very good knowledge of German and French
