A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. The handling of the care car, the electronic care documentation (Phoenix), the drug distribution system (Pyxis) and the electronic patient safety system (IDEFIS) accompanies you in the daily work process.
