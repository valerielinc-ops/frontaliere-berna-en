Sales advisor Textile (m/f/d)

Coop Genossenschaft — Bienne, Bern, Svizzera

Mission

Your versatile function will be exercised mainly in the textile sector. Thanks to your friendliness, you welcome and advise our customers with competence, make the store attractive and carefully manage the assortment of shelves. You ensure the control and installation of the merchandise according to the Coop presentation concepts. You collect, order, track and rotate inventory. You are actively involved in sales promotion.

Qualifications

You benefit from a recognized training in sales or have already assumed a similar function. You have a sense of responsibility and are effective on the ground. You are a smiling person, endowed with great ease in contacts and seek a varied and versatile job. Knowing how to work in an organized and autonomous way, you are dynamic and you can offer flexibility in working hours. There are no problems with handling and you have very good knowledge of German and French, both oral and written.
