Full description

Introduction

For our Supplies sector, within our de-lamination business, we are looking for a (M/F) Quality Controller whose main mission will be to ensure that components comply with quality requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Control parts on dimensional and aesthetic aspects, according to defined standards
  • Sort parts where necessary
  • Control lots or samples with one or more control equipment
  • Communicate clearly and accurately the test results to the production conductors
  • Ensure proper use and maintenance of control equipment
  • Participate in projects and approaches for continuous improvement
  • Informing production information in dedicated systems

Profile

  • Experience in aesthetic and dimensional quality control, ideally in the watchmaking or peeling sector
  • Mastering the reading of technical plans
  • Knowledge in Statistical Process Control (SPC)
  • Use of IT tools
  • Good dexterity and excellent visual acuity
  • Good communication skills, organizational sense and autonomy

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
