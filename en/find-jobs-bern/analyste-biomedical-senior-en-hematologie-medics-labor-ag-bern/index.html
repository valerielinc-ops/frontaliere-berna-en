Full description

Always on the pulse of time and committed to customers as well as employees: this is Medics Pathologie AG - an active and carefully growing company. To strengthen our team in Bern we are looking for an agreement:

  • Processing tissue samples from laboratory input to stained histology preparation
  • Independent processing of biopsies and preparations of small surgery, assistance in macro-cutting
  • Pouring of paraffin blocks and production of paraffin cuts on the rotary microtome
  • Cutting on the cryostat for quick cuts and immunofluorescence
  • Coloring of overview and special colors with the machine and by hand
  • Performing immunohistochemical stainings
  • Processing of gynecological and clinical cytology submission material
  • Completed training for biomedical:n analyst:in (BMA) HF
  • Experience in the field of histopathology incl. Immunohistochemistry and technical cytology are advantageous
  • Engaged and team capable personality
  • Responsible, self-employed and efficient functioning
  • High social skills and a strong sense of service

We offer a versatile and exciting working environment, fair employment conditions and a family culture in a manageable team. With us, you are an employee: not a number, but an independent and respected personality.
