Full description

Data Platform and Engineering

Make a decisive contribution to our central data hub: You develop robust data solutions, connect internal and external data sources and thus create a reliable foundation that enables the departments to use data and information easily and independently.

Your field of activity

  • Design, develop and operate robust, scalable data pipelines and data solutions together with AI/Data Scientists and Platform Engineers
  • You play a key role in designing the central data hub and bring together internal and external data sources for different applications.
  • Model and transform data to be reliable, consistent and usable for different use cases
  • You provide data and data products in such a way that specialist areas can use them easily and as independently as possible for their applications.
  • For data quality, monitoring, testing and traceable documentation along the data value creation you take responsibility
  • Cross-organisational, you develop digital solutions with specialist areas, AI/Data Scientists and Platform Engineers and support Kafka-based streaming solutions

You bring that with you

  • Several years of practical experience in data engineering and a good understanding of modern data and platform architectures
  • With Python, SQL, ETL/ELT and data pipelines you work well

You also know as many of our technologies as possible: Snowflake, Databricks, dbt, Git and CI/CD

  • Experience in the development and use of interfaces and APIs is also part of your technical foundation
  • You translate requirements from the specialist areas with product thinking into sustainable technical solutions and work on their implementation yourself
  • Interdisciplinary, cross-organisational cooperation and agile working methods are familiar to you from practice
  • Good knowledge of German and English as well as the willingness to build up a comprehensive understanding of the power and distribution grid industry round off your profile
