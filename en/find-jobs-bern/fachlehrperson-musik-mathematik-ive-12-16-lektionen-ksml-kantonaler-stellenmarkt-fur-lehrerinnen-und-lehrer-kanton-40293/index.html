Full description

Portrait The OSZ Ins is an association school, which is attended by 265 cycle 3 students from seven communities. Ins is a village in the exceptionally beautiful Zealand with about 3800 inhabitants and perfect public transport connections to Bern, Biel, Neuchâtel and Freiburg. The school currently teaches 13 classes according to a transparent model (3b/4).

Tasks

As of 12.10.2026, you will take on a temporary position as a specialist teacher in various classes (model 4). They teach music (6L), mathematics (4L), IVE (2L) and possibly advanced support (2L) and/or 2L team teaching (MRDV Art. 3b). The exact allocation of the amount and the definitive division of the compartments are negotiable and can be agreed together. They actively shape everyday school life and work cooperatively with the various stakeholders. They work closely with the teachers of the year team.

Requirements

  • You are a motivated, reliable and creative personality who enjoys working in a team.
  • You bring a completed degree program that qualifies you for teaching at secondary level I or are currently in training.

We offer

A motivated and helpful team, an appreciative, pleasant working atmosphere in a future-oriented school, a supportive and present school management. From 2027 a new, modern school house with a unique school garden.

The employment conditions depend on the cantonal specifications.
