What you do

Where every minute counts, expertise and humanity make the difference.

  • Together with your team, you will ensure a structured and professional process at our emergency department.
  • You care for, monitor and accompany patients with acute, serious or life-threatening injuries or diseases.
  • You support diagnostic and therapeutic measures and actively contribute your expertise.
  • You use medical devices and aids safely.
  • You meet patients and relatives with empathy and professionalism.
  • You work in a three-shift operation and make an important contribution to emergency care.

What distinguishes you

  • You have completed NDS HF emergency care or a comparable European degree.
  • You bring several years of professional experience in emergency care.
  • You convince with team spirit, commitment and a sense of responsibility.
  • You communicate safely in German (minimum level C1).
  • You work independently, reliably and keep an overview even in demanding situations.
  • You like to develop professionally and personally.
