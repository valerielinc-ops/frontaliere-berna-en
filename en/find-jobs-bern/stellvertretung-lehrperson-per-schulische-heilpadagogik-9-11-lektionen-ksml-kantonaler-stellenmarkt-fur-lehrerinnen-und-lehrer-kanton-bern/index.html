Full description

Portrait The school location Marzili/Sulgenbach consists of the two school facilities Marzili and Sulgenbach as well as external kindergartens. In total, the school location comprises eight kindergarten classes and 21 primary classes in the 2026 school year, in which 600 learners are supervised and taught by around 70 teachers.

Tasks

They accompany learners with special needs and advise teachers in their work. Their tasks include:

  • Work on two classes 3/4 and 5/6 in Marzili
  • Early lesson every Wednesday and Friday 07.30 a.m. Learning Group German (funding topic LRS)
  • Implementation of internal school concepts
  • Recognize and accompany SuS with funding needs within the framework of MR lessons
  • Preparation of technical reports, communicate with specialist bodies and authorities
  • Collaboration in a team
  • Parental work

Requirements

Willingness to take on the entire task Completed training as a curriculum pedagogue:in or in training Experience Cycle 2 High level of teamwork and flexibility Joy of working with children and adults You address issues, work in an interdisciplinary network and document your work in our document system

We offer

We assume a longer-term deputy until spring or summer. Due to the sick leave, however, we can not advertise longer than until and with December. Presence days are negotiable, at least on two days.

A dedicated and supportive team awaits you. The two school buildings Marzili and Sulgenbach as well as the kindergartens are centrally located and easily accessible by bicycle.

We offer a supportive mentorship as well as accompaniment by teamwork: inside, school management, college and school social work.

We look forward to meeting you.
