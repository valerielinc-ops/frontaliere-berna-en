Full description

Portrait We are one of four semi-autonomous quarter schools in Ostermundigen. At our school, 70 teachers teach about 350 children and young people from kindergarten to 6th grade. The school is centrally located in Ostermundigen on Bernstrasse 60. Our school has different class types: for example, we run a KbF, an intensive German course and classes of the special elementary school. We also have a day school. We attach great importance to integration. Sorry!
