Full description

This is what awaits you

With a view to retirement at the end of 2027, early succession planning with a structured transfer of know-how is planned at the Competence Center Personal Injury. During your time in the specialist network Schaden (FNS), you will develop into a specialist in personal injury. During your assignment, you will be fully integrated into the Personal Injury Competence Center team and build up your expertise in a practical and step-by-step manner.

This is what you do with us

You systematically work your way into complex case constellations and increasingly take responsibility for personal injury cases. You will learn about relevant topics such as sickness allowance, accident insurance and withholding tax and apply your knowledge in everyday work.

In addition to the core tasks, you are involved in withholding tax billing as well as in projects and change initiatives. You are actively involved in shaping your role and supporting the team in broadening its expertise and ensuring the long-term handling of sensitive personnel cases.

This is what you bring with you

  • Strong interest in personal injury and motivation to systematically build up in-depth expertise
  • Insurance training, experience in claims
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method
  • High sense of responsibility as well as sensitivity and discretion in dealing with personnel cases
  • Openness to additional responsibilities, projects and changes as well as enjoyment of actively developing your role
  • Very good knowledge of German and French
  • Knowledge of Italian is an advantage

Your development path in the FNS

This position is part of our specialist network Schaden, FNS for short. With this development program, we specifically build specialists for important and specialized tasks in the damage area. In this way, we secure valuable knowledge, create professional prospects and prepare talent for future specialist functions.

You are fully integrated into the Personal Injury Competence Center from day one and actively participate in day-to-day business. Experienced specialists will accompany you in expanding your knowledge in a practical manner and gradually taking on more responsibility. Your development is based on an individual development plan with clear goals. This is determined together with you, your manager in the competence center and the management of the FNS.

The goal is that you qualify for the intended specialist function within about one to two years and then work permanently in a suitable function in the damage area. Your employment is unlimited from the beginning.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programmes abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
