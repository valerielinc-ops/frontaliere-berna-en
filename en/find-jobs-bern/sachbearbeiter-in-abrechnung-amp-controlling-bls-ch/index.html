Full description

What happens behind the scenes of a reliable railway operation? A lot and some of it has to do with numbers.

As a clerk: in billing & controlling, you ensure that services are billed correctly, key figures are prepared in an understandable way and deviations are detected. You connect numbers with operational events and thus create transparency on the basis of which decisions can be made.

This is waiting for you

  • Responsible for billing and operational controlling
  • Development of production budget positions and preparation of basics for pre- and post-calculations
  • Create and analyze the performance figures of the product results statement
  • Preparation of analyses and evaluations
  • Leading smaller projects and advancing independently
  • Support the responsible energy manager in the calculation of the Europe-wide energy consumption of our locomotives

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic commercial training, ideally with further training in accounting and controlling
  • Joy of thinking in contexts and recognizing solutions in numbers
  • Experience managing small projects is your plus
  • Fit in Excel, Power BI and SAP-Fi/Co
  • Strong in German – other languages such as English or Italian are welcome

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year
  • Financial support for external further education as well as an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.

About the BLS Cargo

We can't move mountains, of course. It doesn't matter. Because as experienced mountaineers: In rail freight transport, we see mountains as a challenge and not as an obstacle. We transport heavy goods from the North Sea through the Alps to the Mediterranean, and that is a tricky task. We master them in a team with our approximately 450 employees in Switzerland, Belgium, Germany and Italy. Our customers are international, our roots local. This is another reason why it is exceptionally exciting to work for BLS Cargo.
