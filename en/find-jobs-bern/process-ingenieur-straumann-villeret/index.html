Full description

Ready to reveal your potential? At Straumann Group, we live an exciting adventure, driven by growth, innovation and our desire to have a positive impact. Our mission is to improve oral health and transform the lives of millions of people around the world. United by this ambition, we give every day the best of ourselves, in a culture of high performance that values learning, collaboration, curiosity and the desire to progress. You will have the opportunity to take charge of your career, develop your skills and continue to evolve with your expertise, commitment and desire to learn. Together, we not only help create new smiles: we reveal the potential of everyone, including yours. Introduction What if your expertise in process engineering contributes directly to the quality and performance of our medical devices? For our Villeret site, we are looking for a process engineer who wants to contribute to the optimization, reliability and evolution of our manufacturing processes. Attached to the Team Leader Process Project & Engineering, you will play a key role in developing robust, reproducible industrial solutions that meet quality requirements. At the intersection of engineering, production and innovation, you will participate in various technical projects, from continuous improvement to the integration of new technologies. Your role You will contribute to:

  • Optimize our manufacturing processes by identifying opportunities to improve quality, productivity and reliability.
  • Pilot technical activities related to the evolution of processes and equipment, from the definition of requirements to their introduction into production.
  • Explore new technologies, including automation, robotics, industrial vision and data collection solutions.
  • Characterize and validate processes by defining critical parameters, conducting industrial tests and participating in IQ/OQ/PQ qualifications.
  • Solve technical problems in collaboration with production, quality and maintenance teams, through structured and data-based analyses.
  • Ensure risk control and compliance by contributing to risk analysis, change management and corrective and preventive actions (CAPA).
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, other Straumann sites and external partners to share best practices and develop sustainable solutions.
  • Share your expertise by documenting results, standardizing solutions and supporting their deployment to production teams.
  • Your profile Essential skills to succeed in this role:
  • Advanced training in process engineering, industrial engineering, mechanics, materials, chemistry, automation or equivalent.
  • Professional experience in process engineering, industrialization or process improvement in an industrial environment.
  • Practical skills in characterization and process control, problem solving, risk analysis and validation.
  • Ability to understand and apply quality and regulatory requirements related to your activity.
  • Mastery of French and ability to communicate in English in a professional context (minimum B2 level).
  • Good control of Microsoft Office tools. Additional skills that would be appreciated:
  • Experience in medical devices, pharmaceutical industry or other regulated environment.
  • Knowledge of ISO 13485, ISO 14971 or other applicable regulatory requirements.
  • Practice of statistical and continuous improvement tools (DOE, SPC, MSA, Lean/Six Sigma).
  • Familiarity with SAP, ERP system or production and project monitoring tools. All applications meeting the required qualifications will be examined without discrimination on grounds of race, skin colour, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin or disability.
