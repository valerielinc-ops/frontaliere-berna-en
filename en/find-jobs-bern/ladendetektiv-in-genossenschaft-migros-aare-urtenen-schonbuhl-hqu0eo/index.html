Full description

To increase the team, we are looking for your support. As a shop detective, you are on the road in our branches in the Canton of Bern and Solothurn and actively ensure the protection of our assets and the sense of security of our customers and employees. At the same time, you support us in all security topics - apply now!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You monitor the sales areas and actively prevent shoplifting in the economic area, mainly in the regions of Bern, Berner Oberland and Solothurn as well as occasionally in the canton of Aargau
  • You carry out personnel checks and issue house bans
  • You process customer crimes, clarify identities, conduct interviews and report to the police
  • You create theft reports, evaluations and reporting as well as secure and edit evidence, such as video recordings
  • You support the operational security department in day-to-day business and special topics

Requirements

  • Some years of professional experience: in the security sector, ideally in the order service and / or as a shop detective*
  • Completed initial or further training: EFZ and further training as a shop detective or training in the public order service
  • You act confidently, represent your concerns with the necessary assertiveness and find suitable solutions even in demanding situations.
  • You have experience in store theft detection and video surveillance
  • You have a good reputation
  • You have good user experience in MS Office
  • You know the relevant legal requirements
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates of newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance funds
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions of over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
