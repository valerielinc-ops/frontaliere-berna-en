Full description

Portrait The school Madretsch belongs to the school unit Linde – Madretsch. Children of different cultures attend our school in several locations. We attach great importance to cooperation and live the motto "We take care" actively in our school day. This not only promotes cooperation, but also the children’s sense of responsibility for each other and for their surroundings. Our schoolhouse is also very easy to reach – in just 5 minutes by bus or bike from Biel station.

Tasks

You work in a dedicated class team. They support the teachers during the lessons. For example, you accompany individual students: inside or small groups, support the children in the everyday routines and accompany the children in the break.

Requirements

  • We are looking for a dedicated person who can accompany children in challenging situations, implement the assignments of teachers and have experience in caring for children.
  • They work every morning from 8-12 o’clock and on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons.
