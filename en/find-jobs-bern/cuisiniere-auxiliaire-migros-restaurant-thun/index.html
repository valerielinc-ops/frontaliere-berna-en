Full description

Do you want to be part of our innovative and lively gastronomy business? With your commitment, you will ensure that our customers feel at home and meet their diverse culinary wishes. If quality, freshness and hospitality are of course for you, we should definitely get to know each other!

Mansioni

  • Fresh, warm and seasonal dishes are prepared by you
  • Customers are actively serviced and advised by you
  • It is your responsibility to present the goods responsively and ensure their constant availability
  • You are responsible for quality and process assurance
  • Compliance with and enforcement of the HACCP concept falls within your task

Requisiti

  • Some years of professional experience: as a cook*Köchin in the field of (system)gastronomy
  • You show a high sense of quality and responsibility
  • Selling and consulting are your passions and customer satisfaction your motivation
  • You convince by your resilience, flexibility and high performance
  • PC skills and a high digital fitness
  • No training required
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Professional pension: The Migros-Pensionkasse guarantees the financial security of Migros employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Additional cumulus points in all Migros-Filials and various subsidiaries
  • Migros Club School: Generous financial participation in courses of the Migros Club School
  • Sports and fitness services: Benefits for fitness subscriptions
  • Mobile phone subscription: various discounts on mobile subscriptions
  • Mobility: Participation of an annual subscription to public transport, discount on fuel (Migrol), reduced annual contribution to mobility carsharing and entitlement to fleet rebate
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates of newly completed fixed mortgages
  • Staff offers: Professional home with one of the most successful and financially sound Swiss companies.
