Full description

Profile

BFF Kompetenz Bildung Bern is the leading intercultural education center for the following areas: bridging programs, care, health, hotel and housekeeping, early childhood education, social pedagogy, facility management and continuing education. 5500 people from more than 80 different nations work and study at the school.

At the Vocational Education and Training Department, young people and adults in 2- and 3-year basic vocational training programs in the occupational fields of care, health and hotel and housekeeping are taught. In addition, the one-year general education course for adults is offered. The learners attend practical and competency-oriented classes with us. Around 100 teachers support more than 2400 learners on their way to successfully completing their vocational qualification. Become one of them!

For teaching in our youth training programs in a total of 7 classes; in the Hotel and Housekeeping Specialist EFZ and Care Specialist EFZ programs, we are looking for two for the period from February 1 to July 31, 2027

Responsibilities

  • You teach general education in accordance with the school curriculum.
  • Your teaching is based on BFF's ABU concept, with an action- and competency-oriented approach as well as situational didactics in the two learning areas «Language and Communication» and «Society».
  • As a class teacher, you are responsible for ensuring good cooperation with the other teachers and the training companies.
  • You are part of a committed team and actively contribute to subject-area and school development.
  • The new ABU 2030 curriculum also requires expert involvement in the development of new teaching materials.
  • Working days: Monday, Thursday and Friday.
  • The position is fixed-term. There is a possibility of a permanent position and an increase in the workload from August 1, 2027.

Requirements

  • As a qualified or aspiring vocational school teacher for general education, you have teaching experience and sound subject expertise.
  • You demonstrate a high level of initiative, can work with learners with different starting points, and enjoy working with learners.
  • Differentiated instruction, language-sensitive teaching, individualized learning support and proficient use of blended learning are strengths of your teaching.
  • You deal professionally with behavioral issues in adolescence and intergenerational conflicts.
  • You bring a willingness to engage in proactive, interdisciplinary collaboration within the specialist group and the department as a whole.
  • You spend a large proportion of your working time on site, exchanging with your team colleagues.

We offer

  • You will be introduced to the field and to collaboration through a mentorship.
  • You will work in an interesting environment and be welcomed by a collegial and motivated team.
  • Employment and remuneration are governed by the cantonal Teacher Employment Act (LAG).
