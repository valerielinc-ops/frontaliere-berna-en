ICT-Security Operations Manager*in — Stadt Bern

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality as of 1 September 2026 or after agreement. In a small team, you are responsible for analyzing security-relevant events and assessing their potential impact on urban and school infrastructure. If you enjoy working independently and want to work in a dynamic IT environment, we look forward to your application.

Tasks

  • Operation, control, development and monitoring of enterprise-wide ICT security systems
  • Assess the effectiveness of the security measures taken and initiate further measures to improve security
  • Analyze security-related events and their potential impact on ICT systems in terms of hardware, software, communications networks and services
  • Initiation and coordination of immediate measures such as protection, transition, evasion and escalation measures in the event of security-threatening events
  • Assist in evaluating and procuring security systems and tools

Requirements

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship in the computer science environment and further training such as HF, FH (ideally in the cyber security sector)
  • Practical experience in the field of operational IT security (log analysis, SIEM, vulnerability management), especially in the Microsoft environment (MS Defender)
  • Communicative and team capable personality
  • High degree of independence, personal commitment and flexibility
  • Sociability and the willingness to quickly familiarize yourself with new areas of work
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
