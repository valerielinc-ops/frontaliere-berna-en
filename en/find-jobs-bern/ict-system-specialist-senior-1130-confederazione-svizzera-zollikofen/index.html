Put to the Point

You are a Microsoft specialist with an affinity for Unified Communication and Collaboration (UCC/Skype for Business/Microsoft Teams) services and like to design innovative communication services within and outside the federal administration? Then you are exactly right at the BIT - thanks to you we guarantee our partners a stable and modern environment that is up-to-date.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • By agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 609-17798
  • Place of work: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Operate, configure, support and develop Microsoft Teams and UCC platforms in a DevOps team
  • Technical cooperation and support of customers in agile UCC projects as well as their concerns
  • Automate processes using PowerShell and manage and optimize interfaces to other services (e.g. in and around M365)
  • Provide new or existing UCC solutions in the M365 environment as well as customer support in the introduction
  • Collaboration in Team Picettdienst

This makes you unique

  • Computer Science Education (EFZ) with flair for UCC services and automation or equivalent in practice acquired expertise in the operation of UCC Services and Microsoft Teams solutions
  • Several years of experience in administering and operating (monitoring, securing, testing) M365 services
  • Powershell knowledge is advantageous
  • Experience working in agile teams (SAFe/SCRUM)
  • Motivated and committed personality; customer- and team-oriented and networked way of working; communicative and able to understand demanding relationships
  • Good knowledge of a second official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

How much (B)IT is in you?

We – the Federal Office for Informatics and Telecommunications (BIT) – are the future-oriented and agile office. Here, curious minds, ambitious makers and innovative thought leaders develop a wide range of digital solutions for Switzerland. We support and promote mobile and location-independent work. In this way, our team players can design their work flexibly and individually where possible - for the compatibility of family and work. In addition, we maintain an open you culture at BIT and promote you and your future with a broad range of training and further education.
