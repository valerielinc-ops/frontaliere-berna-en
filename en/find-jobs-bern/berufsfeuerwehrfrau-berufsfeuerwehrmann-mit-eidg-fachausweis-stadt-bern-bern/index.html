Full description

You are not looking for an ordinary job, but a task with responsibility, team spirit and real meaning? Protection and Rescue Bern is looking for committed, resilient and ready-to-learn personalities who are willing to prepare intensively for the versatile profession of professional firefighter during the 18-month training. You can expect an exciting and instructive training period, exciting assignments, modern technology and a strong team that can rely on each other.

Tasks

  • You will complete the 18-month training, which is divided into course and internship blocks
  • You will learn how to use the latest technical material to provide targeted help in emergencies of people and animals
  • You close the course to the professional firefighter or professional firefighter with a federal law. Specialised card
  • After passing the exam, we offer you an interesting and varied activity in the 24-hour shift service for the welfare and protection of our population.
  • You have the opportunity to develop personally and professionally

Requirements

  • Completed basic vocational training EFZ, Matura or higher education
  • You are between 24 and 32 years old when you start your job
  • Upon entering the job, you will be in possession of driving licence categories B and C
  • You have very good health, normal visual and hearing acuity and are physically and psychologically resilient
  • Your strong teamwork and above-average willingness to perform distinguish you
  • You bring very good German skills in word and writing, good computer science user knowledge and craft skills
  • An impeccable reputation is essential
  • Your application documents contain at least a CV with references, a letter of motivation, employment certificates and diplomas, a copy of your driving licence, a criminal record and an IVZ record
