Full description

For a long-term and technologically demanding transformation program, we are looking for an experienced PMO Quality & Risk Manager for our customer from Bern.

In this role, you ensure that quality standards are adhered to, risks are identified at an early stage and appropriate control measures are implemented across the entire program organization.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for quality management within a complex program and project environment
  • Creation, maintenance and further development of quality management plans and QA concepts
  • Organization, coordination and implementation of quality gates and quality checks
  • Preparation of quality, risk and status reports for project and programme management
  • Support for acceptance, phase releases and ensuring defined quality standards
  • Development and development of an ICT quality management system in close cooperation with relevant stakeholders
  • Management and maintenance of risk management within a multi-project organization
  • Identification, evaluation, monitoring and tracking of risks and definition of appropriate countermeasures
  • Advising project and sub-project management on all aspects of risk and quality management
  • Harmonisation of risks and ensuring transparent risk reporting across different projects

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in quality management, risk management or PMO environment
  • Experience in complex project, program or transformation projects
  • Sound knowledge of quality management methods and governance processes
  • Experience in planning and executing audits, reviews or quality gates
  • Knowledge in the development and further development of quality management systems
  • Structured experience in risk, issue and action management
  • Very good analytical and conceptual skills
  • Secure handling of MS Office as well as common project management tools
  • Experience in agile project environments is an advantage
  • Negotiable knowledge of German mandatory
  • Proper reputation is mandatory
