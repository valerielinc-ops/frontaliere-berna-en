About Us

The Institute of Financial Management (IFM) is responsible for the teaching of financial topics within the framework of the Bachelor and Master courses at the Department of Business Administration of the University of Bern. The Institute’s employees also actively contribute to academic research in the areas of corporate finance, corporate governance and asset pricing.
