Full description

More than 1000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: The CSD Group is a dynamic, constantly growing engineering company. Inventiveness, competence and multidisciplinarity characterize our teams, optimal solutions for our customers with a plus for the quality of life and the environment are our trademark. Do you enjoy working in a professional and agile environment and attach great importance to quality and efficiency? Become part of our success! We are looking for an expert in hydrogeology 60-100% (m/f/d) for our field of geology in Bern.

Your responsibilities

  • Management and management of complex hydrogeological projects
  • Coordination and communication between different disciplines and project stakeholders
  • Thematic area Groundwater protection, use of groundwater, contaminated sites, geoengineering and/or tunnelling
  • Acquisition of projects and contact person for our customers

Your profile

  • Degree ETH/FH in Geology or Hydrogeology
  • At least 7 years of professional experience as a project manager in the field of hydrogeology in an engineering office
  • Experience in the areas of contaminated sites, natural hazards or beneficial raw materials
  • Customer orientation and acquisition are in your blood
  • Appropriate expression in German, oral and written
  • Team-oriented, committed and efficient way of working
  • Communicative personality with strong personal responsibility

We offer

  • Development prospects
  • Exciting projects
  • A dynamic team and broad training opportunities
  • A pleasant working environment and a modern and centre-oriented workplace awaits you at our office location at Eigerplatz in Bern

We look forward to your application! Offers from recruitment agencies are not considered in this recruitment. CSD will therefore in no way negotiate the payment of claims.
