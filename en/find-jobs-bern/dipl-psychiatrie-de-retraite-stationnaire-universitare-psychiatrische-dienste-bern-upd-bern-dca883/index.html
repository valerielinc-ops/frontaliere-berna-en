University Hospital for Old Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The University Hospital for Old-Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Bern offers versatile and responsible tasks in acute psychiatry. In a dynamic environment, you accompany patients with different medical conditions through challenging crisis situations. The work is based on a person-centred recovery-oriented mission statement and combines a comprehensive range of non-drug interventions to support patients’ self-efficacy. In addition to direct care, you take on responsibility in the daily accompaniment and promotion of learners FaGe and students HF and actively shape the training of young nurses.

Their tasks

  • They design a person-centered, recovery-oriented care and accompany psychiatric patients in their self-determination and in the pursuit of their individual life goals.
  • They integrate non-drug interventions into patient support and everyday life.
  • You are part of the interprofessional team
  • You support the learners FaGe and students HF on a daily basis
  • They actively contribute to improving the quality of care

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH
  • They communicate clearly, are empathetic and have a high level of social competence
  • They work independently, professionally and responsibly
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
