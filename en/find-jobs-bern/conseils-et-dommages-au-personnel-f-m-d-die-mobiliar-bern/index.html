Full description

This is what awaits you

Want to take a job with meaning instead of just a phone? At Mobi24 AG, a company of the Mobiliar Group, you are the first point of contact in exceptional situations and emergencies. Together with your team, you help ensure that assistance and services are provided quickly and reliably.

This is what you do with us

You take calls and thus directly support the general agencies. You collect damage reports from all industries and coordinate help in the event of breakdowns in Switzerland.

You also handle administrative activities around our customers and thus take on an important hub function.

You build up know-how in the insurance world and do your training as an insurance intermediary: in VBV. After successful completion, you will conduct initial discussions, advise our customers and prepare offers and proof of insurance.

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic commercial education completed
  • Enjoyment of customer contact and strong communication skills
  • Teamwork and independent, customer-oriented working methods
  • Flexibility for shift operation (maximum until 8.30 pm, sometimes on weekends – with corresponding surcharges)
  • Fluent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of French and/or Italian

Working with us

We are a diverse, dynamic team with great team spirit and humour. We offer you flexible workloads of 60-100% with home office options in shift operation with 40-hour week. In addition to training as an insurance intermediary: in VBV we offer a large internal training offer and clear development opportunities. In addition, there are the attractive benefits of the Mobiliar Group.

Your start is possible by agreement.

More about our commitment as an employer can be found below.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
