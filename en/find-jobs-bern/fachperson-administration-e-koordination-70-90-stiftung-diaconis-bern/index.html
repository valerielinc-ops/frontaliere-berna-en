Role overview

On behalf of the Canton of Bern, Diaconis’ labour integration offers various labour market measures for the sustainable professional integration of job seekers.

One of them is the measure EAR (determination of labour market relevant resources), a location determination and potential assessment for German and French-speaking job seekers.

It is aimed at people with an unclear reintegration strategy, if there is a need for clarification on labour market-relevant skills and an assessment of the integration potential for the labour market.
