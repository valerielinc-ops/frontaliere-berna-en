Regional education managers 80-100 %

Your tasks

  • Ensure, accompany and further develop the quality of training in the assigned holdings
  • Conducting site visits as well as advising and accompaniing vocational trainees, education managers and leaders on training issues (care and other professional fields)
  • Training and, if necessary, intensive support for vocational training managers and vocational training staff at the locations
  • Support in challenging situations with learners and students as well as cooperation with internal key offices, schools and educational offices
  • Planning and implementation of training and exchange of experience in the field of vocational training, participation in learning events and participation in regional trade fairs
  • Participation in projects to further develop the training of learners and students within Tertianum

Your profile

  • Completed training for/for dipl. Nursing professional HF or equivalent and in-depth experience in vocational training
  • Ongoing or completed qualification at level SVEB 2 or equivalent
  • Strong consulting, coaching and communication skills as well as pleasure in accompanying people in their development
  • High social skills, resilience and the ability to quickly integrate into new teams and structures
  • Enjoy a varied activity with regular travel within the assigned region (GA is provided)
  • Very good knowledge of German; ideally good to very good French skills in word and writing
