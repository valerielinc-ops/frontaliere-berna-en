You have acquired your specialist title Dermatology in Germany and would like to build your professional future in Switzerland? Are you consciously looking for a job where you can gain Swiss experience and at the same time create the conditions for your further outpatient work? This position offers you a well-accompanied introduction to Swiss dermatology with a wide range of patients, modern infrastructure and an interesting long-term perspective. At two established locations in the Bern/Solothurn region, a modern dermatological environment with a broad spectrum of classical and aesthetic dermatology awaits you. Especially for specialists from Germany, the job is interesting: You can use your first years in Switzerland specifically to get to know the Swiss health system, to network professionally and regionally and to build the conditions for your further work in the outpatient sector. You should enjoy a down-to-earth, medically versatile patient care. Not big city and lifestyle medicine are in the foreground, but a broad dermatology for the population of the region. Complemented by aesthetic treatments and modern procedures.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of own patients
  • Broad spectrum of classical dermatology and venerology
  • Possibility to work in aesthetic dermatology
  • Further development of dermatological offers together with your colleagues
  • Networking with local doctors in the region
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with phlebology and plastic surgery

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Dermatology and Venerology, gladly acquired in Germany
  • Interested in a long-term career entry into Switzerland
  • Joy of a broad, patient-oriented dermatology
  • Willingness to engage in a regionally shaped and versatile patient care system
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Communicative and independent working methods

Their advantages

  • Attractive entry opportunity for specialists from Germany
  • Working at a recognised Swiss training institution
  • Opportunity to build up relevant Swiss professional experience for later OKP approval
  • Modern treatment rooms and contemporary equipment for classical and aesthetic dermatology
  • No night services and regular working hours
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Close cooperation with phlebology and plastic surgery
  • Good social benefits and attractive earning opportunities
  • Possibility to build up a Swiss network during the first years
  • A position that opens up further professional opportunities in Switzerland after entering
