Company description

OMEGA is a prestigious global brand that has been striving for the highest quality, highest precision and ever-increasing innovation for more than 170 years. Our watches are among the best in the world, they are the result of technologies that are leaders in the watch industry and are characterized by a timeless design.

Over the course of our long history, we have set precision records, played a decisive role in sports timekeeping and continue to play a pioneering role in the art of watchmaking.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues who are ready to support our teams on virtually every continent through their talents, ambitions and inspiration.
