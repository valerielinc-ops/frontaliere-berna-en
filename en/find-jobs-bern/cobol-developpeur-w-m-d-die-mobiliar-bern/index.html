Full description

This is what awaits you: In this role, you are responsible for the conception, implementation, further development and operation of Mobiliar’s content management system. The MCM team ensures that all business-relevant documents are securely, fully and efficiently available at all times. As a central document hub, the content management system integrates over 50 systems, supports around 4,000 active users daily and processes around 100,000 new documents per day. This is what you do with us: You actively shape the content management system from planning to operation. Conscientious and implementation-oriented, you develop user-friendly solutions for your document management together with internal customers and ensure that they are implemented in a practical, sustainable and audit-proof manner. In the role as a fullstack developer: in your focus is mainly on the backend. You will use your know-how to develop complex backend solutions and ensure that the content management system can be operated reliably and efficiently. Together, you use modern AI tools that support the development processes. This not only increases team productivity, but also helps to create innovative and future-oriented solutions for furniture document management. With your open-minded personality and as a supportive specialist, you share your knowledge, promote motivation within the team and develop practice-oriented guidelines for successful cooperation. This is what you bring with you:

  • Completed computer science studies or similar specialist training
  • Experience in agile software development (Java, Angular, Kotlin of advantage)
  • Knowledge of development-supporting tools (IntelliJ, Git, Jira, GitLab, CI Pipelines)
  • Experience with cloud services (Microsoft Azure) and DevOps tools of advantage
  • Implementation-oriented, communicative and customer-oriented personality with pleasure in working in interdisciplinary teams
  • Curiosity about complex questions and new technologies such as AI, Spec-Driven Development etc.
  • Fluent German and English skills Work with us: The agile team MCM, as part of ART Communication Management, awaits you. Together they live an authentic, solution-oriented and committed way of working with a high level of motivation, in which private exchange is not neglected.
