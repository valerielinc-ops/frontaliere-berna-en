Portrait The Curative Education School Lyss is a day school for children and adolescents with a mental or multiple impairment. We teach around 115 students in 18 classes (basic to upper and orientation level) between the ages of 4 and 18. For our immigration class with 6 SuS we are looking for:

Tasks

  • They teach at the immigration class
  • You recognize the possibilities of your students
  • They promote with eye measure, according to the basics of the ICF and work in a heterogeneous class

Individualized with their students

  • They shape the parental contacts and lead the discussions on support planning
  • They network in the funding process with therapy persons and specialists
  • You work together with a:r educational staff member:in and a:r intern:in at the class
  • They are in close contact with the department management and the teaching team

Requirements

  • Enjoying the challenges of everyday school life
  • Overview in hectic situations and talent in class organization
  • Diploma in curative education or primary teacher with professional experience in curative education

Response requested

  • High level of teamwork and willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Humor and clear appearance
