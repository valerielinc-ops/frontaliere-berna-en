Full description

Portrait

In rural Frauenkappelen with 1,300 residents, around 120 children are taught at the school. A family team of 16 teachers accompanies the children in mixed-age classes from kindergarten to 6th grade.

Our school has newly renovated, bright classrooms, a large auditorium, a gymnasium with a large sports field and various special rooms. The cozy library with enough storage space is available for speech therapy. We belong to the MR region Mühleberg-Frauenkappelen.

Tasks

  • Clarification of support and therapy needs
  • Speech therapy support and therapy in individual or group settings
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with the MR team, teachers, departments, school management and parents
  • Quality assurance through documentation

Requirements

As a trained speech therapist or in training, you have a well-organized and structured way of working. You have good communication skills and observation skills and show a lot of empathy and patience. You are flexible, get involved and are open to new things. You also enjoy working with various stakeholders.

We offer

  • permanent employment
  • Good cooperation in the MR region Mühleberg-Frauenkappelen
  • Great, committed, helpful team
  • Very cooperative environment (parents, authorities...)
  • Cozy library with plenty of storage space as a place to work
  • Good public transport connections (Postbus 7 minutes from Bern-Westside) / Free parking space
