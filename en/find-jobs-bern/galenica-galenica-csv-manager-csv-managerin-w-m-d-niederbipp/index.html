About us

Health Grossist No. 1 Galexis is a company in the Galenica network and belongs to the Wholesale & Logistics Service Unit. Galexis is the leading healthcare grocer in Switzerland and supplies pharmacies, medical practices, drugstores, nursing homes and hospitals. From the two distribution centres in Niederbipp and Lausanne-Ecublens, Galexis distributes pharmaceuticals, parapharmaceuticals and OTC medicines, medical technology and cosmetics. Galexis can handle 150,000 order lines per day. If the care of patients is important to you and you not only have professional knowledge, but also a sense of the whole, you are in the right place with us.
