Overall project manager: in operating properties Real Estate Development 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Overall Project Manager: in Operating Objects Real Estate Development Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You will become part of an experienced team responsible for the construction projects for company buildings such as workshops, offices and logistics halls. This will make an important contribution to reliable rail operations. You expect a lot of creative leeway, fast decision-making paths and an inspiring working environment by living cooperation, openness, respect and trust every day. In the area of real estate development, you will take over the overall project management of challenging new and renovation projects with investment volumes of CHF 5 to 100 million in a technically diverse context. We support your further development with suitable education and training and offer flexible forms of work thanks to Work Smart. You can move that.

  • As the overall project manager, you are responsible for achieving the goals (quality, costs, deadlines, etc.) of the various real estate projects from the study to commissioning for efficient operational infrastructures that make railway operations more efficient and reliable.
  • You represent the interests of SBB and lead the project team from internal areas as well as external planners and specialists: inside, conduct tenders, award services and are the contractual contact person until the transfer to the company.
  • Complex procedures as well as inter- and multidisciplinary coordination with numerous partners are in your hands.
  • In close cooperation with other divisions, future users: you ensure clear and addressable communication.
  • You plan and optimize costs, quality and deadlines, manage risks to meet budget and achieve project goals.

You bring that with you.

  • Proactive, dynamic and reliable. You work independently and solution-oriented and keep an overview even in demanding, complex situations.
  • With your communication and negotiation strength, you win stakeholders for common goals and lead teams through challenging project phases.
  • We require a technical education or a university degree ETH/FH/uni in the construction or real estate sector.
  • Several years of experience in managing complex construction projects for company buildings including contract and procurement make it easier for you to get started.
  • Confident knowledge of German (minimum C1) and ideally French (B2).

Your rewards and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This employment takes place in accordance with bond law, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.

This is the starting point.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the greatest motivation for us. Therefore, we refrain from a letter of motivation at this point. If you still want to tell us your motivation, you still have the opportunity to do so in our application tool. Making big is easier with you. This starts with your application.

That's how it goes.
