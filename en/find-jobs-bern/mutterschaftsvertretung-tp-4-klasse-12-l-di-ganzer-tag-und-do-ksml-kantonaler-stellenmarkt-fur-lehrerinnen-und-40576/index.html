Full description

Portrait The school unit ADN comprises the three locations Allmendingen, Dürrenast and Neufeld in the city of Thun. In 10 kindergarten classes, 27 primary classes, an EC and a KbF (1st - 3rd grade), around 770 students are taught by about 110 teachers.

Tasks

We are looking for a committed and motivated teacher for the 4th year class in the Schulhaus Neufeld.

Pensum: 12 lessons Di. 7:30 - 11:50 (Sport 2L / D / M / NMG) and 14:00 - 15:35 (BG 2L) Do. 7:30 - 11:50 (NMG / Textiles Gestalten 2L / D / M)

Job: 10 November or by appointment Duration: Temporary employment as maternity representation or by arrangement.

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a teacher for cycle 2.
  • A good relationship with the students and a real interest in their everyday life are important to you.
  • Rituals in class and a clearly structured class leadership are part of your pedagogical self-image.
  • They enjoy working in the class team and appreciate the exchange with a committed college.

We offer

  • Temporary employment as a deputy
  • A school with a dedicated, motivated team
  • Support by specialised teachers, school social work and school management
  • Good accessibility with public transport

Applications are processed on an ongoing basis. interviews by arrangement.
