Full description

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by 1 January 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73441

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients as well as for highly qualified medical doctors.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional, human and organisational management of the care units in cooperation with the responsible managers
  • Ensuring and continuously developing a high, patient-oriented quality of care
  • Responsibility for the economic and needs-based use of human and material resources including job and service planning
  • Responsibility for education, training and further education in the care sector as well as promotion and development of employees and managers
  • Close, solution-oriented cooperation with the medical profession, medical areas and other internal and external interfaces
  • Active participation in the management team and in the MES committee, participation in the strategic and organizational development of the clinic as well as completion of the management picket service

Your profile

  • Completed higher education in nursing, ideally at tertiary level, supplemented by further management and/or management training
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in care, preferably in an acute hospital
  • Strong leadership skills as well as experience in the development of employees, teams and managers
  • In-depth understanding of care quality, clinical processes, personnel planning and business relationships
  • Strategic, structured and solution-oriented working method with high decision-making and implementation competence
  • Very good communication skills as well as good German and French skills

Why does it work for us?

  • We work appreciatively and respectfully at eye level with each other
  • Possibility of working with flex and leisure plus models
  • Up to 4 additional free days per year as compensation for changing time (pension-dependent)
  • Above-average night and weekend allowance of CHF 10.- per hour
  • Many discounts and perks in the fitness center, over 100 service providers, leisure events and mobile subscriptions

Benefit as a Hirslanden employee: in many more benefits!
