Full description

The Michel Group AG comprises the psychiatric institution Privatklinik Meiringen, the rehabilitation clinic Hasliberg, the Helsenberg Foundation with dormitories and protected workplaces as well as the Haslital laundry and the Haslital daycare centre as well as services in other areas. It employs a total of around 800 people at its locations in Willigen, Meiringen, Hasliberg, Ringgenberg, Interlaken, Thun and Bern. The Michel Group has over 90 properties.
