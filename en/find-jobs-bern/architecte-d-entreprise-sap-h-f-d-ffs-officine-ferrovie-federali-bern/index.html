SAP Enterprise Architect (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. SAP Enterprise Architect (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In order to continue to “keep the railway under control” for our customers in the future, we need efficient, standardized and future-proof system processes and applications. Our existing SAP system landscape plays a key role in this. With the S/4 SBB program, SBB is replacing the current ERP system with a new SAP solution. We use this transformation as an opportunity to harmonize processes and standardize systems. Cross-divisional and consistent. We are looking for an experienced enterprise architect (m/f/d) to sustainably develop the SAP architecture in the areas of enterprise asset management (EAM) as well as project and portfolio management (PPM/PS). You will strategically develop the SAP system landscape in the SAP Integration Hub together with other specialists and organizations within SBB. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • As an SAP Enterprise Architect, you will develop target images for EAM and PPM/PS along with the corporate strategy and ensure effective SAP governance through coordinated documentation and communication.
  • You identify standardization potential across company areas, business capabilities and processes in the EAM and PPM/PS environment and support them in implementation.
  • You bring together the SBB business strategy and the SAP strategy from a technical and technological perspective, derive architecture enablers, show potential and make decisions.
  • In stakeholder management, you work closely with business owners, business development and architecture roles and ensure transparency about content, priorities and impacts. This is what you bring with you.
  • Strong leadership qualities, networked thinking and very quick comprehension, combined with an independent, solution-oriented way of working and high quality standards.
  • Deep SAP expertise with a focus on ideally EAM and PPM/PS, complemented by a solid technical foundation for the use of AI in the SAP environment.
  • Sound methodological expertise in the architectural discipline as well as practical experience in defining SAP architecture target images and anchoring them in the organization.
  • Confident action in complex technical and organizational environments as well as appropriate communication in order to convey complex issues convincingly.
  • You have completed a degree/university degree in computer science, business informatics or comparable and have very good German (at least C1) and good English (at least B2) skills.
