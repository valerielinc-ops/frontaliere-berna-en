Short overview position

550 employees, who are responsible for the production of high-quality infant food for Germany and abroad, shape the dynamic, highly automated working environment of the Konolfingen factory. This places high demands on our team every day in terms of compliance with hygiene regulations and occupational safety. Would you also like to be part of our team?

To strengthen our Special Products (SP) department, we are looking for a communicative and responsible personality as a plant manager.

A day in the life of a plant manager

  • Processing of milk and children’s foods with the help of modern production facilities
  • Implementation of the feed of raw materials, spray drying and mixing, provision and filling of powder
  • Implementation of the cleaning process (CIP cleaning) as well as general cleaning work according to the existing quality specifications
  • Documentation of production and cleaning processes
  • Activities in the line laboratory and carrying out analyses according to QMS
  • Responsibility for product manufacturing in compliance with safety, hygiene and environmental regulations as well as NGMP and SOP's
  • Ensuring careful handling of raw and packaging materials as well as energies, plants, laboratory equipment and cleaning agents
  • Readiness for shift and weekend work

This makes them successful.

  • Successfully completed EFZ training as a dairy technologist or food technologist or many years of experience as a plant manager in the food sector
  • Good technical understanding and proactive action
  • Customer-oriented behavior in all work situations
  • Strong safety, environmental and hygiene awareness
  • Team-capable and independent personality who shows initiative and commitment
  • Practical handling in SAP, MES and MS Office
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
