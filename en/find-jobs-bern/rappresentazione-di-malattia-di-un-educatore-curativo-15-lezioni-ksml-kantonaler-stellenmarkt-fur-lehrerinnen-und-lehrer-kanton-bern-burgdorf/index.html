Full description

Portrait The Lerchenbühl Foundation is a socio-economic, politically and confessionally neutral foundation based in Burgdorf. With a comprehensive coaching, education and living offer, we accompany and support children, young people and young adults with special needs when they start into the future. The aim is to make life and work as self-determined and sustainable as possible. Our work focuses on school, professional and social inclusion.

We are looking for support for 2 students in the autism spectrum in the 1:1 setting in the high school of our special elementary school. Total 15 lessons with option to extend the bet.

Tasks

For the individual accompaniment of 2 students in the autism spectrum in the 1:1 setting in high school, we are looking for a committed and empathetic personality. The setting initially takes place in the home environment and is oriented towards the needs and resources of the children. The focus of the task is on building a sustainable relationship and jointly shaping a reliable daily structure. They plan and accompany activities, create security and enable the student to participate and experience positive success in everyday life. You will also support students in building self-employment and learning stress reduction strategies and communicating their own needs.

Requirements

They have an education or experience in the socio-educational, curative or educational field and have an interest or experience in working with children on the autism spectrum. They approach children with patience, empathy and appreciation and understand how to build sustainable relationships and provide security. A structured, independent and reflective working method distinguishes you as well as resilience and reliability in challenging situations. They like to work interdisciplinary, communicate openly and appreciate the cooperation with teachers, specialist bodies and parents. In addition, you have good observation and documentation skills, act resource-oriented and flexible and are ready to work in the home environment. Commitment to an inclusive attitude and joy in shaping everyday school life
