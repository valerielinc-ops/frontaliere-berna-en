Full description

Portrait Our school – small, family-oriented, engaged with us children learn in a kindergarten class as well as in two primary school classes in a lively and manageable community. The small size of the school creates a family atmosphere in which relationships can be consciously maintained. The College works with a lot of passion: it is mixed with age and experience and enjoys joint projects. We see ourselves as a dedicated, supportive team that strengthens each other, learns from each other and shares challenges. Our school is located in a rural environment that we experience as a real advantage: a quiet environment, short (communication) paths and a strong village life that connects school and community directly. We value close cooperation, open exchange and mutual respect. Appreciation shapes our culture – in dealing with the children as well as in working together in a team. The needs of children are at the centre. Further information and pictures can be found on our website: www.schulefreimettigen.ch

Tasks
