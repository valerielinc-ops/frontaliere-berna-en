Full description

Portrait Would you like to design a school together with us?

For cycle 3 at the Sumiswald location and the MR management of the entire Sumiswald plus Regio School, we are looking for 1. February 2027 a dedicated personality as school principal (50%).

Perhaps you are looking for a leadership task in which you can take responsibility, contribute your own ideas and at the same time count on reliable support? Then we could fit well together.

At the Sumiswald location, you can expect six classes of Cycle 3, organized in Model 3b "Spiegel", and a motivated team that is committed to shaping its school. In addition, you take over the MR management for the entire Regioschule Sumiswald plus. This job offers variety, responsibility and does not guarantee a boring working day.

Tasks

You lead cycle 3 in terms of personnel, education and organisation. Together with the team, you will shape a successful school day, provide development impulses and create reliable framework conditions for teachers and students.

As an MR management, you coordinate and design this area throughout the entire Regioschule. They think beyond their own location, bring together different perspectives and further develop existing structures.

You are not alone: a well-organized school secretariat, the head of education, the other members of the school management and the education commission are at your side as reliable partners.

Requirements

They suit us if you...

  • They like to lead, accompany and strengthen people,
  • be able to communicate clearly and keep an overview even in demanding situations,
  • making decisions without forgetting to listen,
  • combine pedagogical understanding with organisational skill,
  • want to take responsibility and use creative leeway,
  • bring teaching experience,
  • have completed or started school management training
  • and school with heart, mind and a good dose of pragmatism.

We offer

What awaits you with us are...

  • a motivated and committed team,
  • careful introduction and reliable support,
  • a well-organized school secretariat,
  • close cooperation with the Head of Education and other members of school management;
  • its own well-equipped office,
  • versatile tasks and real scope for design
  • and an attractive place to work in the middle of the beautiful Emmental, which is easily accessible by public transport.
