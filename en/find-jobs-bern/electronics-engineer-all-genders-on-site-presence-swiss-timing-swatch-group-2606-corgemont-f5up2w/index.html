Full description

As an Electronic Engineer, you will play a key role in the development of innovative timing technologies and wearable sensors used in international sports events. Working within a multidisciplinary environment, you will contribute to the full product lifecycle, from concept and design through validation, industrialization, and operational support.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and develop electronic timing devices and wearable sensor solutions.
  • Support the industrialization and production transfer of new products and systems.
  • Provide technical support during product validation activities and sports competitions.
  • Collaborate closely with testing and operational teams to ensure product performance and reliability.
  • Contribute to innovation initiatives in sports timekeeping technologies.
  • Ensure compliance with development methodologies, internal guidelines, and quality standards.
  • Support prototype manufacturing, testing, and continuous product improvement.
  • Monitor emerging technologies and contribute to technology scouting and end-of-life (EOL) management.
  • Travel internationally as required (approximately 10% of the time).

Profile

  • Passionate electronics professional with a hands-on mindset and focus on reliable products.
  • Strong technical expertise combined with innovation and quality-driven execution.
  • Solution-oriented with a proactive and positive attitude.
  • Self-organized with excellent prioritization and time-management skills.
  • Resilient and able to perform effectively in fast-paced and demanding environments.
  • Strong team player with excellent communication and collaboration skills.
  • Passionate about technology, innovation, and continuous improvement.

Professional requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electronic Engineering or a related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of professional experience in electronic product development.
  • Strong experience with Altium Designer for electronic design and PCB development.
  • Solid knowledge of analog electronics.
  • Good understanding of Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) principles and standards.
  • Experience with prototyping, testing, validation, and product industrialization.
  • Familiarity with quality assurance processes and continuous improvement methodologies.
  • Experience working in multidisciplinary development environments is considered an asset.

Languages

  • Fluent English, both written and spoken.
  • Fluent French, both written and spoken.
  • German is an advantage.
