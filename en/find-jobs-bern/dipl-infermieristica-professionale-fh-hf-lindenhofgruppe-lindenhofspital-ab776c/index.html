Full description

What you do

  • You will care for and care for our patients in shifts with a high level of personal responsibility.
  • You will carry out the nursing process according to the latest findings, supported by the clinic information system KISIM.
  • You take joint responsibility for achieving the divisional and departmental goals and are committed to the ongoing optimization of nursing processes.
  • You will also support the apprentices and students in their training.
  • You will work collegially in a large interprofessional team and attend further training, case discussions and supervision with us.

What sets you apart

  • You are a qualified nurse (AKP/DN II/HF/FH) and ideally have experience in the acute sector.
  • Experience in the field of palliative care as well as further training in PC B 1 or CAS Palliative Care are an advantage.
  • The well-being of patients is the focus of your work and you treat people with respect and appreciation and are empathetic and resilient.
  • You are characterized by the ability to work in a team as well as independent and quality-conscious work.
  • Good knowledge of German rounds off your profile.
  • Permanent night watch possible after introduction.

  • A time credit of 30% for night duty (optional)
