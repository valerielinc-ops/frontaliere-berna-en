Key responsibilities Are you an initiative, sociable personality and are you at your best when you advise customers with the help of the latest technologies?

Please contact us. We are looking for a colleague for our team in Gstaad who has a flair for the concerns of international customers in our tourist place and feels comfortable in the digital world.

And these are your core tasks

  • After a comprehensive training period, you support and process your own customer portfolio with investment and mortgage customers.
  • They capture the versatile needs of our customers precisely and advise them competently.
  • They have a strong sense of optimal solutions. This ensures long-term customer relationships and scores with new customers.
  • You actively approach your customers and create a customer proximity that allows you to exploit further potential.
  • In your consulting, you also rely on innovative tools and apps. This is how you take your customers on our journey into the future: you encourage them to use our digital products and support them in doing so.

The team You work at the Gstaad office. Support us and discover the opportunities at one of the world’s leading financial institutions with strong Swiss roots. We are a team of specialists, summit strikers and experienced people and look forward to new ideas that inspire us.

Diversity helps us to grow together. This is why we are committed to promoting and nurturing diversity, equal opportunities and inclusion. This strengthens our business and adds value to our customers.

Your skills and experience

  • Commercial education, ideally with further training in banking or finance
  • In-depth understanding of investments and mortgages
  • Knowledge of the local market and ability to manage and expand a customer portfolio
  • Joy in dealing with international, upscale customers
  • Ability to deal with cultural and linguistic diversity as well as curiosity for technical progress and great openness to digitalization
  • Sales passion and high service orientation
  • Dedicated commitment to the success of the entire team
  • Flowing German and good knowledge of French and English are advantageous
