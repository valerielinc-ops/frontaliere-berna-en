Full description

On behalf of the Canton of Bern, Diaconis’ labour integration offers various labour market measures for the sustainable professional integration of job seekers. One of them is the measure EAR (determination of labour market relevant resources), a location determination and potential assessment for German and French-speaking job seekers. It is aimed at people with an unclear reintegration strategy, if there is a need for clarification on labour market-relevant skills and an assessment of the integration potential for the labour market. The results form an important basis for further professional integration. The assigning authority is the cantonal office for unemployment insurance (RAV). You combine psychological expertise with leadership experience and would like to build up a new labour market measure right from the start and shape it professionally? You know the professional integration and bring experience in case management, discussion and diagnostics? For the establishment and management of our new labour market measure EAR we are looking for 1.
