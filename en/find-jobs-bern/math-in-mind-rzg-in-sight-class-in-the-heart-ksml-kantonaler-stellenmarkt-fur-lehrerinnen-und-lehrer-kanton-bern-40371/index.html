Full description

Portrait We are an upper stage association near Bern. Around 25 teachers and curative teachers teach our young people in nine classes and together we work for the same goal: to accompany and promote our young people on their way to the professional world in the best possible way and to shape an exciting and versatile school day together with them.

Tasks

We are looking for a deputy for 8 to 23 lessons, who would like to work in a wonderful place near the city and want to be part of a fantastic team. Unfortunately, the job owner is out for several weeks. We accompany, challenge and promote our young people with an attitude that gives them some confidence and at the same time orientation. We focus on relationship and clarity and we are committed to this with a lot of passion.

  • A multifaceted task awaits you
  • 9 lessons in mathematics
  • 6 lessons RZG
  • 2 Lessons in Media & Computer Science
  • 4 lessons IVE
  • 2 lessons ERG
  • Class teacher function

Requirements

You lead clearly. You stay close. You're moving something. We are looking for a dedicated teacher with a focus on mathematics and RZG who takes young people seriously, builds relationships and leads them reliably. Someone who likes to take responsibility and demand quality. We expect commitment and not perfectionism. We sometimes make mistakes and they help to get better. Humor provides a pleasant ease that helps us tackle challenges.

We offer

An established and very committed team and a supportive school management welcomes you. We work with a very good (IT) infrastructure. We count on the participation of young people in everyday school life and the respectful treatment of each other is lived by all participants. Our school is beautiful and in the middle of the green. You can count on a careful delivery of the teaching content.

The location can be divided sensibly, for example by compartment. What is important to us is the willingness to be at our school several days a week.
