Introduction of society

OMEGA is a prestigious watch brand worldwide known for its quality, precision and innovation requirements for over 170 years. Our watches are among the most beautiful in the world and are characterized by their state-of-the-art technology and timeless style.

Over the course of our long history, we have broken records of precision, redefined sporting timing and maintained our position at the cutting edge of Haute Horlogerie.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams on almost every continent with their talents, ambitions and inspirations.

Description of post

  • You are responsible for all aspects of fire protection.
  • After evaluating existing buildings and systems, you develop fire protection concepts, implement the recommended measures and ensure that they are applied and respected.
  • You are responsible for raising awareness and informing employees about evacuation procedures and preventive measures.

Profile

  • You have a building training (CFC, ES, HES, or equivalent)
  • Training supplemented by an AEAI degree in fire safety.
  • To benefit from a minimum experience of 5 years at a similar position, you have a very good technical knowledge of the buildings.
  • Proven experience in project management

Qualifications

  • You demonstrate good organizational and communication skills, have a good team spirit and know how to take initiatives.
  • Flexible personality with high capacity to adapt to local cultures and environments

Languages

  • You speak French and German (oral and written), English is a plus.

Requirements

  • Vous êtes titulaire d’une formation dans le domaine du bâtiment (CFC, ES, HES, ou titre jugé équivalent)

  • Formation complétée par un diplôme AEAI de chargé de sécurité en protection incendie.

  • Au bénéfice d’une expérience de minimum 5 ans à un poste similaire, vous avez une très bonne connaissance technique des bâtiments.

  • Expérience avérée en gestion de projets

  • Vous faites preuve de bonnes compétences organisationnelles et de communication, avez un bon esprit d’équipe et savez prendre des initiatives.

  • Personnalité flexible et dotée d’une grande capacité d’adaptation aux cultures et environnements locaux

  • Vous maîtrisez les langues française et allemande (oral et écrit), l’anglais est un plus.
