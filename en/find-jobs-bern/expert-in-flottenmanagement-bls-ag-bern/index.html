Full description

When a locomotive rolls in Italy and moves on reliably at Lötschberg, your work is behind it. You accompany our fleet throughout its entire life cycle, make decisions for tomorrow’s missions today and balance technology, efficiency and international requirements. This ensures that goods are safe, sustainable and on time – day after day, across borders. This is waiting for you:

  • You are responsible for fleet management throughout the entire life cycle, ensuring reliability
  • You strategically align the assigned fleet and develop the fleet strategy in technical and economic aspects
  • You are responsible for the further development of the locomotive monitoring system with a focus on optimized maintenance
  • Refit and Up-Grade projects lead you from planning to successful implementation
  • You are responsible for the international suitability of the locomotives as well as for regulations and specifications, coordinating new requirements
  • You represent the interests of BLS Cargo in specialist committees and actively participate in the purchase of services This is what you bring with you:
  • A higher technical education FH or university in electrical engineering or mechanical engineering forms your basis
  • A business education supplements your technical understanding
  • Experience in fleet management or in interdisciplinary projects in the railway sector distinguishes you
  • Know-how in fleet technology, desirable in the area of ETCS backup systems and life-cycle costs you bring with you
  • Sovereign appearance in German and English and willingness to travel round off your profile Your advantages – you can look forward to:
  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year
  • Financial support for external further education as well as an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health. About the BLS Cargo We can't move mountains, of course. It doesn't matter. Because as experienced mountaineers: In rail freight transport, we see mountains as a challenge and not as an obstacle. We transport heavy goods from the North Sea through the Alps to the Mediterranean, and that is a tricky task. We master them in a team with our approximately 450 employees in Switzerland, Belgium, Germany and Italy. Our customers are international, our roots local. This is another reason why it is exceptionally exciting to work for BLS Cargo.
