Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment and maintenance of installations.

Join a position where your expertise really makes a difference: as an installer-electrician, you intervene on sites of all sizes that value your know-how and your ability to manage complex installations. Here, autonomy and responsibility are not mere promises, but the heart of your daily life. You are evolving in a dynamic environment with the confidence to drive your interventions, take initiatives and actively contribute to the success of ambitious projects.

Installer or installer 80-100%

Mission

  • Performing work related to multi-technical electrical installations, you master the reading of plans and diagrams of electrical installations
  • Install, connect and modify electrical panels, various electrical appliances, communication, multimedia, security, control, etc...
  • Participate in various implementations
  • Also install channels, draw wires and cables
  • Making films and governance bills
  • Participation in the training of our apprentices

Profile

  • Holder of CFC installer
  • Confirmed experience in general electrical installations
  • You recognize yourself as a person with a sense of organization and responsibility
  • Relationship and sense of service
  • Essential driving licence

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
