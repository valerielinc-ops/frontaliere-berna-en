Full description

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • You are responsible in close coordination with your PPM setup and development of your assortments. You have the specific needs and requirements of Swiss consumers in mind and offer the best price / performance ratio of your articles
  • Due to your supplier relationships at home and abroad and negotiating skills, you always have the latest range at the best conditions.
  • You develop content, market comparisons and sales arguments in the field of outdoor, fitness and electricity
  • You assume business responsibility for your product ranges as well as for the optimization of the relevant KPIs (net proceeds, contribution margin, storage level). You also carry end-to-end responsibility for the operational procurement process – from the selection of the delivery source to the invoice audit.
  • You assess the key figures with appropriate initiation of measures
  • You conduct trend and competition analysis
  • You plan and implement activities as well as new product launches taking into account the pricing strategy

Requirements

  • High affinity for the topics outdoor, fitness and electricity, knowledge about the market and new products as well as technologies and various brands
  • Completed basic commercial education with business education or a business degree
  • min. 2 years of experience in a comparable position
  • Confident knowledge of German in words and writing is mandatory, solid knowledge of English as well as French is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of dealing with MS Office
  • First experience in SAP is an advantage
  • Customer and solution-oriented thinking and acting
  • High commitment and willingness to learn
  • Self-responsible, reliable and analytical working method
  • Team-oriented work

We offer

We offer you a modern, dynamic working environment in which you can contribute your skills and your personality every day and make a difference. For joint success. For a common future.
