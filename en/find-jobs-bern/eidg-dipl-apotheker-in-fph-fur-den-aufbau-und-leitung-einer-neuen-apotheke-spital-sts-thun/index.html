Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking for meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

You will be responsible for the conception and construction of the new Offizin Pharmacy and will contribute to the operational processes, the range of products and the positioning of the new pharmacy. You also take an active role in the recruitment process of your future team, which you will lead. In this role, you build long-term relationships with our customers, patients and the medical community in the region and in the hospital.

Job opening by arrangement.

This is waiting for you

  • The opportunity to design and participate from the ground up a public pharmacy that will be part of Spital STS AG
  • A completely new workplace with the latest technology and contemporary tools
  • The opportunity to leave your own traces in a project from the beginning
  • A well-established and motivated team in the hospital pharmacy, which supports and cooperates in a motivated way
  • Close, interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Flexibly selectable holiday model
  • Attractive leisure opportunities in the sea and mountain regions

Become part of our team

  • Completed study of pharmacy with a federal diploma or a corresponding MEBEKO recognition
  • Professional Permit (BAB) in the Canton of Bern incl. FPH title officin pharmacy
  • Initial leadership experience and/or project experience are beneficial
  • Personality with balanced social and professional competence and a high understanding of interdisciplinary cooperation
