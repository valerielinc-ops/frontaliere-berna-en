Full description

As a firmly rooted company in Zealand, we offer comprehensive transport and storage solutions and ensure cleanliness in more than 30 communities with our waste transports. Our success is based on motivated employees and modern infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • You distribute general cargo shipments throughout Switzerland in accordance with scheduled tour planning and ensure reliable and timely delivery
  • As a die-hard chauffeur, you enjoy passing on your expertise to apprentices, lateral entrants and new drivers
  • With pleasure you accompany our newcomers on their first trips with motor cars and trailers and stand by them with advice and action
  • You teach practical everything about cargo securing, trailer, vehicle body, lifting platform, blank top as well as a safe and efficient driving style
  • Together with you, your vehicle is the most important and always visible business card of our company – so you take care of its care and maintenance

Why do you succeed?

  • You can drive trailer train CE like a professional
  • You have already successfully completed the VZV training or are ready to complete it
  • Do you have an ADR ID? Perfect!
  • Thanks to your patient and appreciative nature, you take time to understand processes.
  • Your criminal record is unobjectionable, you are not complaining (please send along)
  • Orally you can safely express yourself in German or French

  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
