Full description

Portrait

The Arch-Leuzigen school association includes over 300 students from cycle 1 to cycle 3 at three locations. We work in a committed team with an appreciative and solution-oriented school culture. Collaboration, trust and humor characterize our everyday school life.

Tasks

  • Carrying out speech therapy assessments and therapies (individual and group settings)
  • Advice for teachers and parents
  • interdisciplinary collaboration with teachers and specialist departments
  • Participation in funding planning and location discussions

Requirements

You are a trained speech therapist or are currently undergoing appropriate training. You enjoy working with children and young people and supporting them in their language development. Constructive collaboration with teachers and parents is important to you. You are reliable, humorous and keep an overview even in lively situations.

We offer

  • 16 lessons of speech therapy in school
  • a committed and collegial team
  • a supportive and appreciative school culture
  • good cooperation between teachers and departments
  • a school management who supports you in everyday life
