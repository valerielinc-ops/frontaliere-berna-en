Full description

Portrait The primary school Mühlefeld comprises 17 classes, of which 5 are kindergarten classes. The Kindergarten Erlacherweg is beautifully situated in the Mühlefeld quarter. It was newly renovated and re-appointed in summer 26. In the same building is also a French-speaking kindergarten class. The garden and parts of the kindergarten are used together. As a German speaking team we are well connected and work closely together at the level. We teach a variety of subjects with a focus on the Churer model. Non-violent communication (according to Rosenberg) is important to us.

We're supported by a school social worker.

Our students attend a bilingual day school.

Tasks You teach in the Kindergarten Erlacherweg. Mutual support and cooperation are the main focus. The working days are Monday and Tuesday morning.

The class is accompanied by a subject-teacher (1Nami lessons, 5L DAZ and 4L teamteach) and an IF-teacher (2L / week),

Requirements You have an apprenticeship as a teacher or are in training. You are motivated to work in a heterogeneous environment. We look forward to an established and committed teacher.

We offer good support from an ingenious college with established forms of cooperation. We work with nonviolent communication, the Churer model and cooperative learning forms. Good accessibility: 7 min by bus from Biel station, 5 min by bike or 20 min by foot.

We offer variety and challenge at a lively school. The cooperation is spontaneous and close. Individual further education as well as an interdisciplinary exchange are part of this.
