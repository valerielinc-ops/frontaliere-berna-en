Full description

You like to work independently in a professional team and individual care at a high level is important to you? Then we look for them as dipl. Care professionals HF 40 - 100% As a care coordinator (=case management), you accompany, advise and care for people at home and are responsible for the care process and the organization of a holistic care. You have the appropriate professional qualification and have already gained initial experience, so that you can take on your tasks independently and responsibly after a thorough introduction. We offer a varied and very independent position in a dynamic service operation. Their tasks correspond to the current training content at tertiary level. They benefit from advanced employment conditions as well as an extensive range of further education and training. When re-entering the profession or individual development, we are happy to support you.

Portrait From Langnau and Eggiwil we provide the people in the upper Emmental with Spitex services. It is important to us to be close to the people, but also to have a good quality of care and to work together with relatives, doctors and institutions.
