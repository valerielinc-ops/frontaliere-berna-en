Full description

On behalf of the Canton of Bern, Diaconis’ labour integration offers various labour market measures for the sustainable professional integration of job seekers. One of them is the measure EAR (determination of labour market relevant resources), a location determination and potential assessment for German and French-speaking job seekers. It is aimed at people with an unclear reintegration strategy, if there is a need for clarification on labour market-relevant skills and an assessment of the integration potential for the labour market. The results form an important basis for further professional integration. The assigning authority is the cantonal office for unemployment insurance (RAV). Do you want to use your work-oriented expertise in resource assessment and accompany people in their professional reorientation? For our labour market measure EAR we are looking for 1. Employee / Employee Resource Assessment in Labour Integration 60 - 100%

Your tasks with us

  • You develop and update practical work assignments to clarify labour market-relevant resources
  • You accompany participants in specific work situations and observe their professional, methodological and personal skills

  • You work closely with case management and career guidance
