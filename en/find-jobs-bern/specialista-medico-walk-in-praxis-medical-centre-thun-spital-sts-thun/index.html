Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

Our modern walk-in practice is part of the hospital Thun and combines the dynamics of emergency medicine with the continuity of outpatient care. As a specialist in our Walkin practice, you look after patients from a general medical spectrum (internal medicine, traumatology, small surgery, paediatrics, etc.). With your reliable and motivated work, you help to ensure highly qualified care and support. In your demanding job, you will be supported by a young, motivated and quality-oriented team. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality.

Job as of 1 January 2027 or by arrangement.

This is waiting for you

  • Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday within our office hours (08.00-19.00)
  • A varied spectrum of patients from general internal medicine, traumatology, small surgery, pediatrics and other specialist areas
  • Direct connection to Spital Thun with uncomplicated access to specialists from various disciplines
  • Modern practice with fully electronic medical history, digital X-ray, laboratory connection, ultrasound, ECG and spirometry
  • Possibility to acquire POCUS baseline emergency onography
  • Collegial, young and committed team with appreciative cooperation
  • Central workplace directly at Thun station
  • Short decision-making paths and an open corporate culture
  • Attractive employment conditions and good opportunities for professional development

Become part of our team

  • Medical diploma (Switzerland or EU)
  • Specialist title or advanced training, preferably General Internal Medicine, Surgery/Orthopedics or Emergency Medicine
  • Enjoying a broad medical spectrum and working independently
  • Reliable, motivated and team capable personality
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
