Portrait The Oberstufenzentrum Kreuzfeld is next to the kindergarten one of the four school centers of the Langenthal elementary school. It is located in the center of the city (about 16'000 inhabitants) and is easily accessible by public transport. The 460 students are taught by around 70 teachers in 24 classes. The children and young people receive additional support from competent specialist teachers and school social workers. In addition to separateive training (2 EC classes, 5 KbF classes), many children are promoted integratively.

Further details can be found at www.volksschule-langenthal.ch
