Full description

Portrait Therapeutic School of the Thun Region We run a school for children and adolescents aged 4 - 18 years with curative education needs. HPS has the task of providing students with an individual and holistic education, education and promotion. We have three kindergartens, four sub-levels, four intermediate levels, four upper levels and three final levels.

Tasks

Are you looking for a job where you can work with heart and effect – but no weekends, no night shifts and enjoy long school holidays? Then you fit perfectly with us!

Your opportunity As a class assistant at the upper school level of the HPS Region Thun, you accompanied children aged 13 - 16 in everyday school life. You will become part of a dedicated team that together creates an appreciative, structured and supportive learning environment. Their tasks

  • Active support of the curative teacher during teaching
  • Individual support for children with special needs
  • Cooperation in support offers, care and support tasks
  • Accompanying activities in the schoolhouse and outside
  • Participation in everyday and class structures

Requirements

Your profile

  • Joy and passion for working with children
  • Social skills, patience and reliability
  • Teamwork and appreciative communication
  • Openness to interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Experience in the field of healing education is beneficial
  • Completed training as FaBe

  • Clear processes, modern infrastructure and an appreciative cooperation
  • 40 working weeks per year, pleasant annual working hours
  • Opportunities for integrated training and personal development
  • Resource-oriented pedagogical work
