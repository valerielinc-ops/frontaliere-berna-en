Full description

Portrait The Aefligen school is located in a quiet and natural location between Solothurn and Bern, directly on the Emme. Whether by car via the Kirchberg/Alchenflüh motorway junction, which is only three minutes away, or by train via the Burgdorf–Solothurn line, the Aefligen school is easily accessible! From summer 2026, we will teach two kindergarten classes at our small, family-run school, one 1st/2nd grade, one 3rd/4th grade and one 5th/6th grade. Our motivated team of twelve teachers is looking forward to reinforcement - maybe you!

Responsibilities

From November 1, 2026 to July 2, 2027, we are looking for an IF teacher to support and encourage our students in mathematics during 5 lessons per week.

  • The operation takes place on Monday and Thursday mornings:
  • Monday:
  • 09.05 – 09.50 a.m.: 5. Class
  • 10.20 – 11.05 a.m.: 6. Class
  • 11.05 – 11.50 a.m.: 3. Class
  • Thursday:
  • 09.05 – 09.50 a.m.: 5. Class
  • 10.20 – 11.05 a.m.: 6. Class

The employment is for 4 lessons through the municipal association in Kirchberg and for 1 lesson directly through our school. The four scheduled meetings for IF teachers will take place in Kirchberg and are to be attended accordingly.

Additional possibility

From 1 November 2026 to 31 July 2027, there is the possibility to take over an additional 2 lessons of sports on Monday afternoons in the 5th/6th grade.

Requirements

  • You are mathematically adept and can explain mathematical content in an understandable way and in different ways.
  • You enjoy supporting children individually and responding to their different learning requirements.
  • You can get children excited about mathematics and make them enjoy learning.
  • You enjoy working in a team and cultivate an appreciative cooperation with the class teachers.
  • You are reliable, committed and enjoy getting involved in our everyday school life.

We offer

  • Motivated classes and supportive parents
  • A great, helpful and appreciative staff
  • A family school atmosphere
  • A varied and meaningful task
