Full description

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for all activities related to business allocation and execution for command training (triage, business management, quality assurance, deadline compliance)
  • Participate in the further development and design of the business processes (especially workflows) of the Training Command and the Defense Group as well as in the promotion of business process automation including programming of specialist data
  • Ensure the training and further education of employees of the Command Training staff in the application of the ACTA NOVA system and GEVER processes as well as the federal training of employees in the area of configurations of workflows and technical data in the ACTA NOVA system
  • Support the Head of Management in establishing, continuing and cleaning digital document storage as well as in expanding and maintaining knowledge management in day-to-day business
  • Advise staff on the selection of the most compliant and efficient document storage

This makes them unique.

  • Higher vocational training in the commercial sector or equivalent education and 2-3 years of functional experience
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language
  • Very good knowledge of the GEVER application (technology and system environment, GEVER expert 2, organizational regulations, GEVER regulations, registry plan)
  • Very good IT knowledge as well as a high level of initiative and interest in the further development and improvement of existing processes and systems
  • Strong customer and service orientation, combined with personal responsibility and pleasure in working with different contact persons
  • Professional experience in military administration beneficial

Your commitment to security and freedom The training command (Kdo Ausb) is responsible for training within the Swiss army – from recruitment to general basic training to cadre training for the militia and the professional military. The Kdo Ausb includes the higher cadre training, the personnel of the army, five teaching associations, the training center as well as the Women in the Army and Diversity Department. One third of the approximately 2,350 employees work in civilian professions in the scientific, technical, medical, psychological, administrative or sports sector and contribute to success together with the professional military.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
