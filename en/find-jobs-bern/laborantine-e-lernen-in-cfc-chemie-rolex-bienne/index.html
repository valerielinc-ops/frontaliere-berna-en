Full description

Motivated by an apprenticeship in chemistry? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The chemical laboratory is active in the field of technical support for production, quality control and research and development (R&D), where it assists chemists. It is responsible for the implementation of environmental protocols and is responsible for planning, carrying out, interpreting and presenting the results of chemical analyses of all kinds. In addition, he/she maintains laboratory equipment and manages the consumables required for analysis.

This learning is suitable for young people who are passionate about working in a world apart from a research and development laboratory.

The first year of training takes place full-time at the Technical Division (DIVTEC) in Porrentruy.

Profile

  • Interest and aptitudes for scientific branches, in particular chemistry
  • Logical and methodical approach and sense of observation
  • Rigor and accuracy
  • Access with IT tools
  • Good editorial skills
  • Sense of initiative and good team spirit
