Full description

This is what awaits you

You enjoy contact with customers, burn for varied help and stay calm and solution-oriented on the phone? Then you fit perfectly to Mobi24!

Become part of a team that supports people around the clock – at home and on the road, at home and abroad – and gives them security in challenging situations.

This is what you do with us

As an organizational and language talent, you are the first contact person on the phone and take over assistance cases independently from A to Z. You organize immediate measures in case of breakdowns, accidents or other concerns and thus provide fast and concrete help.

You coordinate assignments with our service providers and partners and ensure a smooth process.

With your customer-oriented approach, you can handle the calls of our customers outside the opening hours of the general agencies – and ensure that they feel in good hands at all times.

This is what you bring with you

  • Several years of professional experience in direct customer contact, e.g. a completed basic training in the gastro, tourism, medical and insurance industry, in customer dialogue or in the commercial sector
  • Safe handling of computers and common digital work equipment
  • High willingness to provide services as well as reliability, punctuality and flexibility for 24/7 shift operation (including weekends)
  • Flowing Swiss German and French skills, good Italian and English skills are advantageous

Working with us

You can expect a motivated, collaborative team that really lives the value of “Proud to help”. We focus on people and we maintain an open, employee-oriented culture. We offer you shift planning with consideration: Even in 24/7 operation, we respond to personal requests when planning operations. In addition, a well-founded familiarization with us is a matter of course: You are carefully introduced and then gradually take on more responsibility. Independent work and thinking is expressly desired.

In order to preserve our special culture, there is a mandatory presence on site during a basic training course of about six months. After this basic training, you work at least 50% of your work in the office. The rest of the time you can flexibly divide. A workload above 80% is negotiable. We offer you a reduced working week of 40 hours in shifts, a half-tax subscription, a free mobile subscription and much more.

With us, you can develop professionally and personally – in an environment where support, team spirit and genuine willingness to help are lived.

Advantages
