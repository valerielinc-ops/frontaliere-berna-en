Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The restoration of the hospital center Biel includes a cafeteria and a restaurant. Our cafeteria is open for 365 days and can accommodate 90 guests. During the summer months, an additional terrace is operated. In the restaurant, around 200 employees and guests of the hospital are catered for at noon. We also hold special events such as rich aperitifs and banquets. They are happy to host and look after the internal and external guests in the cafeteria or in the restaurant with joy and warmth.
