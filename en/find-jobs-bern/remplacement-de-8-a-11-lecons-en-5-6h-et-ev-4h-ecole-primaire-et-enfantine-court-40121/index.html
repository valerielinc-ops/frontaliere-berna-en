Full description

The school of Court is a small-sized institution, located in a small village in the Bernese Jura. Here, we live and work in a family atmosphere, with parents and students carrying positive values. Our dynamic and warm pedagogical team willingly accompanies each new arrival in a benevolent atmosphere. Cohesion and solidarity are at the heart of our functioning. The school commission and the municipality support this dynamic by ensuring that teachers have a conducive environment for professional development. We look forward to discovering your application and, perhaps, meeting you during an interview.

Tasks

Teach two mornings, on Tuesday and Friday, in a 5H class sometimes accompanied by 6H students and possibly three ACM lessons in 4H, on Tuesday afternoon every other week and on Thursday afternoon every week. Required Profile

  • Hold a teaching diploma for primary level or a title deemed equivalent by CDIP.
  • Possession of a pool plus certificate would be an asset.
  • Have the ability to collaborate within a small team of teachers and have already experienced various pedagogical experiences.

Diligent, communicative, and persevering, you are used to working with students aged 7-10 and are ready to actively collaborate with parents, teachers, and management. Listening, a sense of responsibility, and assertive authority are expected skills. You demonstrate initiative and have the necessary availability to engage in school projects and extracurricular activities.

We offer

8-11 lessons distributed according to this schedule:

Tuesday morning from 08:15 to 11:50

  • 2 French lessons in 5H
  • 2 environment lessons in 5H

Friday morning from 08:15 to 11:50

  • 1 history of religions lesson in 5-6H
  • 2 French lessons in 5H
  • 1 physical education lesson in 5-6H (During some Friday mornings, it is planned to go to the pool for swimming lessons.)

And possibly

Tuesday afternoon from 13:25 to 15:05

  • 1/2 week: 2 ACM lessons in 4H

Thursday afternoon from 13:25 to 15:05

Every week: 2 ACM lessons in 4H The position can be split into two between mornings and afternoons.
