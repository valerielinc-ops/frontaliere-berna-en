Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

You are an organizational talent who can keep track of eight calendars, five mailboxes, seventeen priorities and twelve streams of information at the same time – without losing your nerves and professionalism? Then you are exactly right with us! We are looking for our HRM in the area of HRM services as of September 2026 or by agreement:n

Your mission

HRM Services is the engine of a functioning human resource management. As a central administrative hub, the department ensures that our HRM processes function efficiently, reliably and on schedule. With your organizational talent, your care and your service concept, you can also keep track of hectic situations and create structure.

  • You and your colleagues are the control center of our HRM. Executives, HR Business Partners and the HRM leadership team count on you to keep track, prioritize and get things rolling. Organization, coordination and information management are in your blood.
  • Routine tasks? You can. But it will also be exciting for you when new challenges arise. You take responsibility for HRM projects – from the first idea to implementation to successful anchoring in everyday life.
  • You love the mix of day-to-day operations and innovative topics. While you master a complex personnel administration in the morning, you drive a project forward in the afternoon, coordinate different stakeholders and make independent decisions.
  • When others see problems, you see solutions. You tackle, think along, think ahead and keep a cool head even when things get hectic.
  • In short, you are the organizational backbone of our HRM, combine professional HR skills with project management and ensure that good ideas become successful solutions.

You are fit for us if

  • Thanks to your quick comprehension, you can keep track of complex situations, identify priorities and act accordingly – even if everyone is in sprint mode.
  • you like to be responsible and always do your work cleanly, completely and reliably thanks to your high quality standards
  • your “inner monk” reports if a shelf is not labeled clean, you encounter unsightly formatting in the document or there are spelling errors (ps. have you already discovered the errors in the advertisement?)
  • Confidentiality and integrity are not buzzwords for you, but internalized attitude
  • you know when AI is your friend and when your own grip is in demand
  • You appear sovereign and also have a smile on your lips at full speed
  • stylish German language for editing texts flows through your veins
  • Quicksteps in Outlook and the slide master in PowerPoint are not foreign words for you, but everyday life – because you blindly master MS-Office and you count yourself among the digital natives
  • You enjoy tackling where your power is needed – without drama and without allures
  • you bring a healthy level of humor – we work focused, but laugh a lot and regularly!
  • you bring a further training in the field of HR (for example, HR professional ID)
