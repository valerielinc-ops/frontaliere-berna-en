Place of work: Hirslanden BernOccupation by: From now on or by agreementOccupation type: Indefinite reference number: 71982

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards at the Hirslanden Group with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Do you want to make a difference, help shape and actively develop your education? Then you are exactly right with us! You have the chance to further develop vocational training. Actively shape processes, contribute your ideas and become part of a dedicated team that creates the future together.

In your role as a training manager, you are part of the central vocational training team, consisting of the management and two colleagues. Together with the network of decentralized training managers and vocational trainers, you will also design the learning process for various EFZ and tertiary level professions within and outside the medical sector.

Your Advantages in Us

  • Family atmosphere with du-culture and cooperation at eye level
  • Modern workplaces in the heart of the city of Bern with the possibility for home office
  • Extensive benefits offer (e.g. publishing, public transport coupons, fitness subscriptions, etc.)
  • Discounted food options in restaurants in clinics

YOUR TASKS

  • You take responsibility for the learning process of the assigned learners and students and ensure a high quality of education with the necessary coordination and administration
  • You are actively involved in the further development of our educational concepts
  • You coach the trainers, lead them professionally and ensure their vocational pedagogical competence
  • You lead the vocational training group and control its further development
  • You implement educational marketing measures and actively participate in network meetings
  • You develop, plan and design learning units in theory and practice

Your profile

  • You have completed a tertiary education of advantage in health care
  • Complemented with the Federal certificate as trainer (SVEB 2) or equivalent
  • In a comparable role, you have already gained several years of professional experience
  • You bring high social skills, especially in the areas of communication, cooperation and conflict resolution
  • You have an independent, conscientious and reliable way of working with a high quality awareness
  • You are an organizational talent and can easily set the right priorities in day-to-day business
  • You are experienced in the daily application of the common MS-Office programs
  • Your knowledge of German is very good both verbally and in writing (min. level C1)
