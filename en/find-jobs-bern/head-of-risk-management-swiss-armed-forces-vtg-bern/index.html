Do you want to take responsibility and shape the future of cyber risk management? You can make this contribution

  • Developing decision bases for the Command Cyber management team and the supervisor
  • Manage and control the integral risk portfolio (operation) in close coordination with the audit management in the Cyber command
  • Planning and coordinating risk management assessments, assessing and prioritizing risks and pursuing the implementation of risk reduction measures together with line managers

  • Further develop the risk management process with the GRC approach (Governance, Risk and Compliance) and promote a sustainable risk culture in the Cyber Command
  • The Cyber Command is actively represented in expert committees and working groups of the higher authority as well as with relevant partners
