Full description

The Meiringen Private Clinic is located in the Haslital in the Bernese Oberland. Here people with all forms of psychological suffering find reception, treatment and care. The clinic with 187 beds is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient psychiatric primary care of the eastern Bernese Oberland. Care and care take place according to current medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge. As a member of Swiss Leading Hospitals, Privatklinik Meiringen is one of the leading private clinics in Switzerland.

In Bern, the private clinic Meringen operates an ambulatorium that serves the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic aftercare of former inpatients, as well as the evaluation and treatment of assigned patients. All psychiatric disorders are treated; due to the previous inpatient stay, however, mainly patients with depression and anxiety disorders, anxiety and trauma disorders, ADHD, as well as stress disorders and addictions
