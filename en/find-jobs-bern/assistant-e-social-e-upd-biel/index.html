Full description

Psychiatry Bienne UPZ Münsingen

Do you want to accompany people with mental disorders in a comprehensive approach, taking into account the different dimensions of their social, professional and personal life? Within Biel's outpatient psychiatry, you are working in a varied and bilingual work environment where you can fully use your social work skills. Our interprofessional team offers competent, rapid and individualized psychiatric care to people with a wide range of mental disorders. As a member of the UPZ outpatient team, you work closely with specialists in the medical, psychological and nursing fields, as well as with the professionals and partners of the regional network. You accompany our patients in social, financial, legal and professional matters and develop with them concrete solutions adapted to their daily lives. We offer you a variety of activities, a great autonomy and the possibility to contribute sustainably to strengthening the resources of our patients, including in complex living situations.

Your tasks

  • You plan, coordinate and implement support measures in the fields of employment, housing, budget, law and migration
  • You provide psychosocial support in acute crisis situations, in close collaboration with the outpatient team
  • You advise and accompany our patients, especially in matters relating to social insurance law
  • In your daily work, you work closely with the authorities, including the Authority for the Protection of Children and Adults (APEA) and social services, as well as with legal services, consultation centres and other specialised partners.
  • You use your experience in psychosocial support and actively contribute to maintaining and developing our high quality requirements
  • You accompany our patients individually, according to their needs, resources and circumstances
  • You develop and interview the network with social bodies, institutions and partner organisations, especially in the areas of shelter and protected employment
  • You work in an interprofessional team of doctors, psychologists and nursing professionals.

Your profile

  • You have a Bachelor's degree in social work or an equivalent degree.
  • You have strong knowledge of social law as well as good knowledge in the medical-psychiatric field
  • You have strong advisory skills and proven experience in this area
  • A complementary qualification in social insurance law and/or continuing training in a systemic approach is an asset.
  • You're an open, reliable, stress-resistant person with a good dose of humor.
  • You show an excellent sense of organization and appreciate self-employment
  • Prior professional experience in the field of psychiatry is an asset
  • You speak French or German and have good knowledge of the other language

UPZ

The University Psychiatric Centre in Bern (UPZ) is the main centre of competence in psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region as a university clinic. It provides psychiatric care in the canton of Bern throughout the care chain, from early identification to outpatient treatment, day care and stationary treatment, to rehabilitation and forensic psychiatry. With more than 30 sites, UPZ offers a healthcare network covering the entire territory. UPZ also makes an important contribution in the fields of education, training and research. More information
