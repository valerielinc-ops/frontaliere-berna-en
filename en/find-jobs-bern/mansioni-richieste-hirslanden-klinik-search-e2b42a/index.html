Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 62182

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

To strengthen our dedicated team in the field of infection prevention and hospital hygiene, we are looking for a Dipl. nurse HF/FH (a).

The advertised position is explicitly aimed at nurses who are enthusiastic about this special field and would like to complete the further training as a specialist or specialist for infection prevention. We actively support you on your development path. You benefit from a structured familiarization and will be supported in the development of your expertise in the field of infection prevention and hospital hygiene.

YOUR TASKS

  • You are actively involved in the implementation of measures to prevent, detect and combat nosocomial infections
  • You support employees from different professional groups in applying hygiene standards safely and sustainably in everyday life
  • In the collection, processing and evaluation of epidemiological data, you also take on a supporting function.
  • You will be involved in the further development and implementation of infection prevention measures
  • You will also be involved in the optimization of processes and processes in the area of infection prevention and hospital hygiene
  • In exciting projects and special orders, you are committed to developing your expertise continuously.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH (for foreign degrees: with SRK recognition), including professional experience in an acute hospital
  • Advantageously, you already have further training as a nursing expert, a completed LINK Nurse course or experience in the areas of hygiene or clinical development.
  • You are interested in infection prevention and hospital hygiene and would like to specialize in this field
  • You are ready to complete the training as a specialist or specialist for infection prevention or are already in the corresponding training, the willingness to complete this training is a mandatory prerequisite for this position
  • You are a communicative and team capable personality with a positive attitude and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You bring your own initiative, are open to new things and work independently and structured
  • Good IT skills (Microsoft Office) as well as very good oral and written German skills (at least level C1) round off your profile

Your benefits to us

  • Family atmosphere with du-culture and cooperation at eye level
  • Modern jobs in the heart of the city of Bern
  • Extensive benefits offer (e.g. publishing, public transport coupons, discount fitness subscriptions, etc.)
  • Discounted food options in restaurants in clinics
  • Additional free time through holiday purchase of up to two weeks

An overview of our employment conditions and other benefits can be found under: Team diagnosis - What we lack is you.

For additional information, Nicole Burri, Head of Staff and Business Development, is available at nicole.burri@hirslanden.ch or Raphael Brand, Head of Recruitment, at raphael.brand@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact and application.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
