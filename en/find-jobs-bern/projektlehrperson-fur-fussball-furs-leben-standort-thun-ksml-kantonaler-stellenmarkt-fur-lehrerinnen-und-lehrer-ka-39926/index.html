Full description

Portrait About the project "Football for Life" “Football for Life” is an integrative sport and education project that sustainably improves the chances of children in the 3rd-6th grade (cycle 2) – especially girls. Through a combination of weekly language- and values-oriented football training and semi-annual cultural excursions, the project strengthens the movement behavior, the joy and self-confidence in speaking and the personal development of the participating children. The enthusiasm for football serves as a vehicle to promote language and personality playfully. The weekly units, which take place at the participating schools, are led by an interdisciplinary team of prospective teachers and trainers: local associations.

Tasks

For our project “Football for Life” we are looking for a dedicated project teacher for the upcoming school year 2026/2027 who would like to strengthen children in their linguistic and personal development through football and who would like to get involved in a meaningful sport and education project at the project location Thun.

Your responsibilities

  • Joint planning and implementation of the language- and value-oriented football units in an interdisciplinary team during the school year 26/27: once a week for 2 hours Wednesdays, 13:00-15:00

  • Supervision of the project group at the annual football final tournament
