Enrich our team! For our new VOI Migros Partner branch in Aarwangen, we are looking for an open-minded sales assistant with a passion for creating an outstanding shopping experience through competent customer advice and friendly service. Are flexibility and teamwork among your strengths? Then we should get to know each other.

Tasks

  • You advise and serve our customers with joy and your expertise
  • An appealing product presentation, ensuring availability, and inventory management are important to you
  • You ensure smooth operations at the checkout
  • Quality and date control are part of your duties
  • You are responsible for order and cleanliness in the workspace
  • You work actively in all specialist areas

Requirements

  • First professional experience: in retail
  • Completed basic or further training: in retail is an advantage
  • As a customer-oriented personality with a joy for sales, you make shopping a positive experience
  • You are independent and your way of working is fast and precise
  • You are willing to work flexible shifts (excluding Sundays/holidays) and Saturdays
  • German (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Modern infrastructure and work equipment
  • Fair wages: Contemporary salary & social benefits
  • Working climate: Family atmosphere, motivated and cheerful team
