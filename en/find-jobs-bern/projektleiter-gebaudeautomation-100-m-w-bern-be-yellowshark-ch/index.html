Future employer

For our client, an established company in the field of technical building services and automation, we are looking for an experienced and solution-oriented professional as a Building Automation Project Manager (m/f).

The company implements modern automation solutions for demanding building services projects and combines control, regulation and automation technology in the process. In this position, you will play a central role in the planning, implementation and commissioning of projects, while working closely with customers, specialist planners and other trades involved.

02

Your responsibilities

  • Independent planning and execution of projects in the field of building automation.
  • Development and implementation of automation solutions for heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and refrigeration systems, as well as for room automation.
  • Project planning and programming of control and regulation systems.
  • Carrying out commissioning and functional tests of the completed systems.
  • Coordination of the various specialist disciplines involved and close cooperation with the heating, ventilation, refrigeration, plumbing and electrical trades.
  • Responsibility for the technical, scheduling and organizational implementation of the projects.
  • Monitoring the financial project objectives and assuming commercial responsibility.
  • Direct support and advice for customers throughout the entire project execution.
  • Close cooperation with Sales in supporting existing customers and developing new projects.
  • Developing practical solutions to technical and project-specific issues.

03

The Commission

  • Flat hierarchies

  • Career and advancement opportunities
  • Free parking spaces

04

Your profile

  • Completed technical vocational training, for example in electrical engineering, building services engineering or automation.
  • Further training at HF, FH level or an equivalent qualification is an advantage.
  • Several years of practical experience in building automation.
  • Good knowledge in at least several areas of technical building services, particularly heating, ventilation, air conditioning, plumbing and electrical systems.
  • Alternatively, solid experience in industrial or process automation.
  • Experience in programming and commissioning automated systems.
  • Good technical understanding and a structured, solution-oriented way of working.
  • Strong organizational skills and enthusiasm for independently managing projects.
  • Resilience and a sense of responsibility.
  • Customer-oriented manner and enjoyment of working with different specialist departments.
  • Team-oriented personality with excellent communication skills.

05

  • At least five weeks of vacation and the option to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Attractive internal and external training and development opportunities.
  • A professional and collegial working environment.
  • Long-term career prospects in an established company.
