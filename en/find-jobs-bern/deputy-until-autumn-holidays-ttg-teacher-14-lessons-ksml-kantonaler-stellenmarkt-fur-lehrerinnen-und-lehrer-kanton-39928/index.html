Original description

Portrait Around 500 pupils and kindergarten children attend the primary/kindergarten level of the Wabern School. We lead six kindergarten, six basic level and five multi-year classes in the 1st-2rd school year and seven in the 3rd-4th school year. The College consists of 73 teachers, specialised teachers and school social workers. More information can be found at www.schulewabern.ch. Tasks As a class teacher, you teach the 15 children with a total of 20 lessons (+5% compensation class teacher). Lesson days: Monday 8.20 - 15.05, Tuesday 8.20 - 16.05, Wednesday 8.20 - 11.50, Friday 9.10 - 11.50 Their responsibilities include: - Planning, preparation, implementation and evaluation of own teaching - Promotion of learners in social, technical and self-competence - Collaboration with the team partner and IF teachers, the college, the guardians, the school management and specialist positions A handover of the prepared teaching units and planning takes place by the job owner. Requirements They are in possession of the diploma of lower/intermediate level and already have initial professional experience. You are a reliable, motivated and resilient personality with a healthy pinch of humor. They like to work independently and in a team. You prepare your lessons carefully. Relationship work with the students entrusted to you is an important condition for successful teaching. We offer An exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility, diverse options for teaching, a pleasant working atmosphere in a well-coordinated and helpful college, support from school management and school secretariat. The schoolhouse, which is ideally located in the center of Wabern, has a good infrastructure and is easily accessible by car and public transport (tram, bus, S-Bahn, PubliBike).
