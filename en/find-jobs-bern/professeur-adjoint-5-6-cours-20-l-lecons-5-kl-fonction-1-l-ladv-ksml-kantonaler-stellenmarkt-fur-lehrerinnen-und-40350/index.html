Full description

Portrait The municipality of Seftigen is located in the upper Gürbetal. The village lies on the end moraine hill of the Belpberg, nestled between the mountain forest in the north and the Widerhub in the south. Seftigen ranges from the Wyssbach to the area of Allmend. The municipality has an area of about 4 km2 and lies at 578 meters above sea level. Neighboring communities are Uetendorf, Burgistein, Gurzelen and Noflen. Politically, Seftigen belongs to the administrative district of Thun.

The municipality is located on the railway lines S4 and S44 with direct connection to Thun and Bern. The distance to Thun is about 10 km and to Bern about 25 km. The Thun-Nord motorway access can be reached in about 5 minutes by car.
