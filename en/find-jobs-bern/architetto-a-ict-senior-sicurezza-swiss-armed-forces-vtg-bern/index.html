Full description

Put to the Point

Become part of the Cyber Command and actively shape the digitalization and in particular the digital networking of the Swiss Army. Get involved in this unique task – the implementation of safety aspects – in a dynamic working environment.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20784
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Define, maintain and maintain the safety architecture and safety case for military aviation and air traffic control systems over the entire life cycle in accordance with applicable industry standards
  • Perform hazard, risk and security analyses for hardware and software, networks and processes, set safety goals (SIL/DAL) and contribute them to procurement or change projects
  • Ensure resilience, operational reliability and controlled degradation behavior, architecturally anchor corresponding concepts for safety-critical services and establish architecture decision records, design principles and technical standards
  • Control change, configuration and fault management on the security side and check each change (change, release, HW exchange, process adjustment) for security effects
  • Coordinate the cooperation between safety and security as well as the regulatory demonstration, compile technical approval dossiers for regulatory approvals (MAA/FOCA/BAZL), prepare and accompany safety audits
  • Act as a central point of contact between different stakeholders such as operations, security and customers and drive the continuous development of the security culture

This makes you unique

  • Higher or higher education degrees in aerospace engineering, computer science or telecommunications (or similar disciplines) as well as experience and competences in the field of security, data protection, compliance and governance requirements
  • Good knowledge and operational experience in the aviation environment
  • Several years of experience in ICT architecture, as well as integration of complex ICT systems with in-depth knowledge in System Safety
  • In-depth understanding of modern security concepts such as zero trust, defense-in-depth and cross-domain and zone concepts
  • Ability to consolidate technical requirements of different stakeholders within agile frameworks (e.g. SAFe) as well as to lead interdisciplinary teams professionally and align them to common goals
  • Good knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, passive knowledge of a third official language

We offer this

  • We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
