Full description

You can contribute to this role

  • Ensure the security of the F-35A program at the military airfield in all security-related matters (structural, technical, organizational)
  • Share responsibility for the implementation, compliance, and verification of all security requirements, as well as for the certification of infrastructure and personnel in connection with the F-35A program in the SAPF (Special Access Program Facility) area
  • Develop and maintain comprehensive security-related documentation of construction projects and phases, as well as subsequent operations, including construction site security; establish a visitor control program
  • Ensure collaboration and coordination with partner organizations at the airfield regarding the F-35A program
  • Represent the Government SAP Security Officer (GSSO) of the military airfield, jointly perform protection tasks related to the F-35A program, and contribute to their successful implementation

What makes you unique

  • Basic training in a technical or commercial profession, as well as advanced training with a federal diploma of vocational education and training, preferably in the security field (e.g., police officer, customs and border security specialist, or equivalent); several years of relevant professional experience
  • High affinity for safeguarding the interests of the air force and for working in a classified security environment; practical experience in roles related to the security of airfields, buildings, or critical infrastructures
  • Strong analytical thinking, experience in project management, and strong implementation skills
  • Swiss citizen with an impeccable reputation
  • Very good active knowledge of a second national language and English, both spoken and written (at least B2 level)

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Operations Command (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and missions of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes, among others, the air force, the army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT competence center, the special forces command, and the military police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are employed as civilians – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs over 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – and this at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. There is also provision for young people: the Swiss Army trains apprentices in over 30 professions.
