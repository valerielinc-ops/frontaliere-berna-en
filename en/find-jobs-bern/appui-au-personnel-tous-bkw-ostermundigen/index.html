Full description

Your field of activity

  • You independently handle tasks in easement management, from forwarding documents and search queries to the correct and timely payment of compensation
  • In the area of general administration, you open orders, prepare project dossiers and create information letters, correspondence and document templates.
  • As support for the management team, you will organize meetings, appointments and other organizational issues
  • Monthly reports, organizational charts and employee overviews keep you up-to-date and coordinate their connection in the intranet
  • The planning and organization of internal team meetings, conferences and training courses as well as the coordination of translations are also part of your versatile area of responsibility.
  • When training our CV learners, you are supportive and give them a good start in the professional world.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed commercial vocational training or several years of experience in a similar function
  • Organizational talent, a structured working method and enjoyment of versatile administrative tasks distinguish you
  • With MS Office and common computer programs you are safe; SAP ERP knowledge is an advantage
  • Your good imagination and affinity for technical topics allow you to quickly familiarize yourself with new tasks and areas of expertise.
  • Your language skills allow you to communicate safely in German and French
  • Industry knowledge is a plus and makes it easier for you to enter your new environment
