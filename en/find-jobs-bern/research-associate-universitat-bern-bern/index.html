As a full university, the University of Bern trains more than 19,000 students at eight faculties and employs more than 8,000 employees in around 150 institutes and the central department. The Rectorate of the University ensures that the tasks of the Senate and the university management are implemented in a timely, appropriate and coordinated manner. It supports the Rector in her management tasks and includes the staff of the university management as well as the Communication & Marketing department. As a research assistant, you actively support the Rector in strategic, university policy and institutional issues.
