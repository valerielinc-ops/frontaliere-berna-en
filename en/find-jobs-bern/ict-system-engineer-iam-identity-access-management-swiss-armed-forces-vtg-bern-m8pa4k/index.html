Full description

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure planning, design, development and implementation of the IAM platform: specify, evaluate, configure and implement the infrastructure components for the IAM platform
  • Implement lifecycle management: plan and implement maintenance, updates and release management as well as ensure stakeholder communication
  • Integrate applications: Advise application teams, implement role and authorization models, and configure interfaces
  • Handling malfunctions: analysing malfunctions, taking appropriate action and contributing to the rapid restoration of operations
  • Ensuring quality, safety and operational continuity: Test releases, accompany acceptances, ensure system monitoring and identify and implement potential for improvement
  • Collaboration with interdisciplinary teams and stakeholders: take up specialist requirements, develop solution concepts and operational documentation, accompany pentests and help shape further development in DevSecOps mode

This makes you unique

  • Completed degree (Bachelor/Master) in Computer Science or an equivalent education in the IT environment
  • Several years of practical experience in the operation, support or engineering of an IAM solution in a complex environment
  • In-depth technical understanding of IAM topics such as identity management, authentication, authorization, role and authorization models and interfaces to target systems
  • Practical experience with agile process models (preferably SAFe) as well as tools and processes in a DevSecOps environment
  • Structured, independent and reliable working methods as well as strong coordination and communication skills

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
