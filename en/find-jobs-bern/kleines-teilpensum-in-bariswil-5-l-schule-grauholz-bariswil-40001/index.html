Portrait The Grauholz school is attended by around 1,000 students from the three communities of Urtenen-Schönbühl, Bäriswil and Mattstetten. Cycles 1 and 2 are taught at different locations in the three communities, cycle 3 in the central Lee school complex in Urtenen-Schönbühl. The three agglomeration communities with rural influence are located in the north of Bern. The school facilities are very easy to reach by public transport. You can find out more at: www.schule-grauholz.ch
