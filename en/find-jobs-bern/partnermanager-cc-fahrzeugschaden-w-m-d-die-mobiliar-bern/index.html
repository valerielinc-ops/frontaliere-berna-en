Full description

: As a partner manager, you support the development and development of a professional network of regional repair partners. With your sensitivity for cooperation, your communicative strength and your structured way of working, you contribute to ensuring that our customers can always count on high-quality services.

In this role, you combine analytical thinking with a sovereign, appreciative attitude and build sustainable relationships – both with partner companies and with internal contacts.

This is what awaits you

A versatile role at the interface between claims management, regional repair companies, general agencies and internal departments.

You actively shape the further development of a professional partner network and contribute your ideas for quality and process improvement.

You can expect an environment with a high level of personal responsibility, short paths and a culture in which cooperation, reliability and pragmatic solutions count.

This is what you do with us

In this function, you actively participate in the cooperation with our repair partners and make an important contribution to efficient and customer-oriented claims processing. You are responsible for the management of a defined partner portfolio and ensure that quality, cost and service goals are achieved sustainably.

By analyzing relevant key figures and partner data, you identify development opportunities at an early stage and implement targeted improvement measures. You will move safely between business issues, operational requirements and the needs of our customers.

As a central interface between partner companies, departments and regions, you initiate and accompany changes, develop standards further and strengthen partnership-based cooperation in the entire vehicle damage process.

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic training in the automotive industry with Swiss certificate
  • Experience in partner management or property or insurance environment is a plus
  • Good understanding of business relationships, cost/benefit issues and quality indicators
  • Enjoyment in dealing with data, digital tools and structured decision-making basis
  • Communication strength, negotiation skills and the ability to combine different interests in a solution-oriented manner
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method as well as a sovereign appearance at eye level
  • High service and quality orientation as well as pleasure in shaping relationships in the long term

Working with us

You will become part of the CC Vehicle Damage and work closely with motivated colleagues from the general agencies, the management and XpertCenter.

We maintain an open, uncomplicated and appreciative cooperation. Responsibility, mutual support and a clear customer orientation are our focus.

You will gain creative leeway to visibly develop an important topic in claims management.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
