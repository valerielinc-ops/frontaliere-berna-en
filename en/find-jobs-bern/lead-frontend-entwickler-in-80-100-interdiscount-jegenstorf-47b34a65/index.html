Interdis Code — Jegenstorf, Berne, Sizzera

We at Interdis Mark offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Monitoring and software-engineering best practices consistently - you actively drive the use of AI tools in software development

  • Appreciable passion for frontend engineering: You like to dive into different topics and keep your knowledge up to date in areas such as the following areas: Sieby, Core Web Vitals, AI-Engineering, Performance and User Experience
  • Strong interest in AI-Engineering:

You're introducing ideas on how to profitably use AI technologies for our online shop - CI/CD-Pipelines (ideally based on GitHub Actions) are a plus - Stylish German in word and writing as well as good English skills
