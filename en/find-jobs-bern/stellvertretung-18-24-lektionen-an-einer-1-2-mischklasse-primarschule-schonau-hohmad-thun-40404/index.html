Full description

Portrait To strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated, committed class teacher at the Schönau school location. We are a diverse, decentralized primary school with 7 kindergartens, 20 rule classes and 2 classes for special promotion (KbF). In a total of 29 classes, around 530 students are taught by 65 teachers.

Tasks

  • Responsibility as a class teacher for a 1st/2nd MK with 18 SuS (1st grade, 12 SuS / 2nd grade, 6 SuS)
  • As a class teacher, lead and teach the majority of lessons
  • Collaboration with the class and school house team, sporadically in the overall college
  • a handover will be possible by the current teachers
  • on Wednesday morning 4 lessons half-class lessons (in parallel with this, TTG takes place)

Requirements

You have a Bachelor of Arts in Primary Education, a primary teaching patent or equivalent degree. You are an independent, responsible, communicative, resilient and team capable personality. In addition, working with children of cycle 1 corresponds to you and you know how to motivate and inspire them. As a class teacher, a good contact with the parents, the relationship with the students and an appreciation-based class leadership is important to you.

We offer

  • a deputy with the prospect of permanent employment
  • Well-equipped school infrastructure with modern ICT environment
  • a part-time teacher who teaches the half-classes in the TTG
  • a school-house-internal specialist teacher in the class for the promotion and accompaniment of SuS
  • An open and helpful team
  • Support from school social work, school secretariat and school management
  • Accompaniment by a mentor, a mentor
