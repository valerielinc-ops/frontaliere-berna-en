Portrait The Burgdorf Speech Pedagogy is a central offer, which provides logopaedic support from the elementary school from kindergarten to level 1 from all over the city. The team already consists of six speech pedagogues who work closely together and share well-equipped therapy rooms including the kitchen and movement room.

Tasks

  • Clarification and therapy of communication, speech and speech disorders in the oral and written field
  • Advice for parents and teachers
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Collaboration with the College
  • Personal training
