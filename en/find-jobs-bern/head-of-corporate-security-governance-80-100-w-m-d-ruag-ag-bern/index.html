Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Leadership, organization and further development of the Corporate Security Governance team and ensuring efficient delivery of services
  • Representation of the Chief Security Officer in the further development, management and monitoring of the Group-wide corporate security management system as well as ensuring effective governance in the areas of safety, security, environment, sustainability, emergency and crisis management as well as business continuity management
  • Defining and governance of all integral security requirements for the RUAG MRO Holding AG Group in the area of security such as the development, maintenance and further development of guidelines, standards and specifications including supporting documents in the areas of physical security, operational security, emergency and crisis management as well as business continuity management
  • Ensuring an effective control system (2nd line of defense) through audits, assessments, risk and vulnerability analysis and follow-up of measures
  • Monitoring compliance with legal, regulatory, normative and internal requirements in subsidiaries as well as assessing security risks, threats and vulnerabilities
  • Professional leadership, coordination and control of the Corporate Security Experts as well as ensuring a coordinated approach between the departments
  • Professional advice to the operating business areas and subsidiaries as well as preparation of reports and decision-making principles for the chief security officer, management, board of directors and specialist committees
  • Management and support of improvement and further development projects as well as implementation of training, awareness-raising measures and management briefings
  • Steering cooperation with internal and external stakeholders such as authorities, auditors, customers and control bodies and supporting the business areas in external audits and customer reviews

You bring that with you

  • University degree (FH/uni) in security or risk management, military or police sciences, engineering, business administration or similar discipline
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience (minimum 5 years) in the area of corporate security, security management or a comparable governance, risk or assurance function
  • In-depth knowledge in at least one of the fields of security, crisis management, business continuity management or protection of critical infrastructures
  • Very good knowledge of security management, risk assessment, auditing and governance, as well as the development, implementation and monitoring of policies, standards and control mechanisms
  • Experience in managing change and improvement projects as well as working with decentralized organizational units
  • Strong leadership, communication and coordination skills as well as analytical, structured and solution-oriented working methods
  • Sovereign appearance and experience in cooperation with authorities, customers, auditors and other relevant stakeholders
  • High integrity, initiative, responsibility and assertiveness
  • Very good German and English skills, other languages such as French or Italian are advantageous

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
