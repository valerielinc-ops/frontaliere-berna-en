Full description

Are you our new, motivated team member? Actively shape the shopping experience at the checkout by welcoming and saying goodbye to our customers in a friendly and professional manner. As a reliable employee at the checkout, you are also responsible for the correct and smooth money traffic. We look forward to getting to know you!

Mansioni

  • You advise and serve our customers with pleasure and your expertise
  • An attractive product presentation, efficient goods management and the guarantee of availability are in your focus
  • You ensure smooth processes at the checkouts and subito stations
  • You actively and committedly support in other departments

Requisiti

  • Ideally professional experience: in the supermarket area
  • Completed basic or further education: in retail trade of advantage
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure of sale, you make the purchase a positive experience
  • PC skills and a high digital fitness distinguish you
  • You convince by a structured, reliable and accurate working method
  • English (very good knowledge)
