Full description

Portrait

The Brunnmatt/Steigerhubel school location, with two school buildings and several neighborhood kindergartens, is located in the heart of Bern. Our school accompanies and supports students from kindergarten to 9th grade. We are diverse and lively and in the coming years we will focus on the New Authority and on a school with almost no grades. The school is led by three headmistresses.

Tasks

From January 11, 2027 until the summer holidays 2027 We are looking for a speech therapist as part of a maternity replacement You will primarily work at the Brunnmatt location.

Your tasks include

  • Speech therapy support and support for students from kindergarten to 9th grade
  • Clarification of the speech therapy needs of individual students
  • Advice and cooperation with parents and teachers
  • Collaboration in the school team and in the MR area

Requirements

  • You have a Bachelor of Arts in Speech Therapy, a diploma in speech therapy. that is recognized in the canton of Bern or by the EDK or are training to become a speech therapist
  • You are an open, communicative and committed personality
  • You are independent, flexible and a team player and greatly appreciate the close collaboration with the speech therapist at our second location (Steigerhubel) and in the MR team.

We offer

  • A well-equipped therapy room.
  • An open-minded MR team and appreciative support from the school management.

Before a possible job interview, we would like to invite you to a trial visit so that you can get an idea of ​​your future workplace and the requirements. The current speech therapist will offer the trial visit.
