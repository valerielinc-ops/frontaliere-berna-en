Your tasks

  • Development and implementation of new backend functionalities with Java EE/JEE, Spring Boot, Microservices and REST Webservices

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science (FH, ETH, University) or a comparable education

  • In-depth knowledge of Java EE/JEE, Spring Boot, Microservices and REST APIs
  • Practical experience with container technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes, ideally OpenShift
  • Very good knowledge of relational databases and SQL, preferably Oracle and PL/SQL
  • Experience in the development and implementation of automated software tests
  • Knowledge of agile development methods, ideally Scrum or SAFe
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method with high quality awareness
  • Pronounced initiative, quick comprehension and pleasure in working together in a team
  • Correct repute (no enforcement or criminal records)
  • Very good German and English skills
