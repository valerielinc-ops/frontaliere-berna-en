Full description

This is what awaits you: As team leader: in and member of the management team Process Damage you actively shape the future damage processes in the non-life of the furniture. Together with your team of product owners, business analysts and quality engineers, you drive the modernization of our claims world. This is what you do with us: You will lead and empower an interdisciplinary team of product owners, business analysts and quality engineers so that the roles in the agile implementation organization are effectively perceived. At the same time, you take an active role in the agile network organization (e.g. as a business analyst) and participate in the implementation of initiatives – if necessary also in cooperation with offshore teams. In close cooperation with stakeholders from business, IT and the SAFe organization, you ensure that requirements are coordinated, prioritized and efficiently implemented, promoting a collaborative, learning-oriented culture and the further development of agile working methods. This is what you bring with you:

  • Completed higher education or training in business informatics, business administration or a similar specialisation
  • Several years of experience as a business analyst or product owner, ideally in the insurance industry, with a professional focus in non-life
  • Experience as a leader (e.g. in team management, professional leadership or project management in agile environments) is an advantage
  • Strong entrepreneurial thinking, high level of initiative and an agile mindset
  • Sovereign, professional approach to business and IT stakeholders
  • Fluent knowledge of German and good English Working with us: In the “Process damage” department, you can expect committed colleagues, interdisciplinary teams in a modern network organization and a working environment in which you can make a difference. We maintain a communicative, independent and solution-oriented working method and meet at eye level. This is what we offer: Compatibility We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund. Training and development We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously. Advantages Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
