Portrait Burgdorf - Educational city in the green Burgdorf with its more than 17'000 inhabitants is the lively center of the Emmental and an attractive educational city. The Education Directorate of the City of Burgdorf designs a future-oriented educational landscape and creates optimal conditions for children, young people and adults. It develops concepts, promotes innovative projects and ensures close networking between education, care, sport and child and youth promotion.

The education directorate includes elementary and day schools, early childhood education, sport, youth and leisure. A dedicated and competent team works together to ensure high-quality offers for the population. At the municipal elementary school, around 1,900 pupils are taught at five primary school locations, two high school centres and several kindergartens.
