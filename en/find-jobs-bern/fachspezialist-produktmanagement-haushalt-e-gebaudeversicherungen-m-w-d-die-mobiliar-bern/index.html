Full description

This is what awaits you

You will become part of our product management for household and building insurance and work in interdisciplinary teams based on agile working methods and actively contribute your expertise to the further development of our insurance products. Together, we design customer-oriented solutions and continuously develop our products and processes according to the needs of our customers and market requirements.

This is what you do with us

  • You develop, guide and manage our household and building insurance products throughout their entire lifecycle.
  • You actively shape a central insurance portfolio with high strategic importance and contribute to the future-oriented development of our products.
  • Together with the team, you actively shape the strategy as well as the product and customer experience vision for the household and building insurance products and implement them step by step.
  • You accompany product ideas from the first conception to the successful market launch and ensure a high-quality implementation.
  • You will analyze customer needs, market and competition developments as well as our portfolio and derive sound recommendations for action.
  • You develop technical product concepts and business cases as the basis for innovative product developments and optimizations.
  • You support the planning, implementation and testing of product launches and further developments. You work interdisciplinary with the areas of product development, sales, claims, underwriting, pricing, law and IT and together ensure a successful implementation of new solutions.

This is what you bring with you

  • You have a higher education in the insurance sector, for example as an insurance specialist or insurance economist HF.
  • You will bring several years of professional experience in the insurance industry, ideally in the area of household and building insurance.
  • You have strong analytical and conceptual skills, network thinking and enjoy developing innovative solutions.
  • Customer orientation and interest in technically demanding questions distinguish you.
  • You are an open, proactive personality and convince with your independent and structured way of working.
  • You have very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of French and/or English.

Working with us

You can expect a motivated team of specialists, insurance technicians and pricing specialists who live innovation, personal responsibility and strategic thinking. Together, you actively shape the future of our household and building insurance and develop innovative solutions in an interdisciplinary exchange that meet the needs of our customers of tomorrow.

Flexible working time models and home office are of course part of our business. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions, diverse training offers and numerous additional benefits.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
