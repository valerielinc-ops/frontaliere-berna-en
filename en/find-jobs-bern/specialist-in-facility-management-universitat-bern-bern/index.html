Tasks: As a specialist facility management with a focus on provider management Building technology and digitalization They play a key role in the management of external service partners and the further development of facility management. • They shape effective partner and stakeholder management, further develop external service partners and ensure sustainable, high-quality service provision. • They define quality standards, KPIs and technical requirements and ensure their implementation. • They optimize the technical operation of buildings together with internal departments and external partners. • They represent facility management in construction, conversion and renovation projects and ensure the early consideration of operational requirements in facility management (pbFM) accompanying planning and construction. • They accompany projects until commissioning and ensure an operator-friendly transfer into operation. • They drive the digitalization of facility management with CAFM, BIM and data management and ensure the quality of operating and plant data. • They continuously develop FM processes, standards and operator concepts. • They ensure compliance with operator responsibility and legal requirements in the areas of technical safety and fire protection. • You are actively involved in projects in the areas of sustainability, energy efficiency and smart building. Requirements • They have in-depth training in facility management, building technology or a comparable discipline – be it basic technical training with higher education or university studies. • You have several years of experience in technical facility management, provider management and in the management of external service partners. • They have knowledge in the operation of technical facilities as well as in operator responsibility and the relevant legal requirements. • You have experience in facility management (pbFM) for construction, conversion or renovation projects. • You are familiar with digital systems (ideally Planon, SAP) and digital FM processes and are interested in BIM and data management. • They communicate convincingly, network stakeholders and promote cooperative partnership. • They bring an analytical mindset, solution-oriented action and a strong understanding of quality. • They take responsibility, bring their own initiative and actively shape changes.
