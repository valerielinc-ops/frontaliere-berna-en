Your tasks

  • Your passion is the manufacturing and welding of steel structures, metal structures and assemblies
  • You manufacture, process and weld a wide variety of components and constructions made of steel, chrome-nickel steel and aluminum according to technical drawings
  • You independently carry out general locksmith and workshop work in the field of steel and metal construction as well as maintenance and repair work on machines and systems
  • You plan the work preparation (AVOR) for your orders independently, paying attention to quality, safety and clean execution
  • You will carry out the general operational work as well as any administrative tasks reliably and with an overview
  • You will work exclusively in the workshop - no assembly work is planned. This is what you bring with you
  • Completed training as a locksmith, metal worker, plant and apparatus engineer or in a comparable skilled trade
  • Ideally, you have valid welding tests in procedures 111, 131, 135 and 141 as well as experience in metal processing and in dealing with conventional machines
  • You read and understand technical drawings confidently and you work independently, precisely and quality-conscious
  • Good knowledge of German and user knowledge of MS Office and SAP make your everyday work easier
  • You have a category B driving license
