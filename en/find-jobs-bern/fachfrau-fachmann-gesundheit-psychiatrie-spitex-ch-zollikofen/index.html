You are a Professional / Health Psychiatry Your area of responsibility The varied position in the SPITEX environment guarantees diversity and an exciting task area. In the care you include relatives according to their possibilities. What you bring with you You have an education in psychiatry as well as sound professional experience. In hectic situations, it is easy for you to keep track. The professional field requires a high sense of responsibility and a fine sense of dealing with people. If you are also a discreet, reliable, self-employed and team capable person, then we should get to know each other. Driving licences are required for this job. What to expect We offer you a careful introduction as well as a collegial cooperation in a professional and motivated team. Attractive employment conditions with at least five weeks of holidays per year, training opportunities, regular fallsupervisions, Intervision and customer reports are a matter of course for us.
