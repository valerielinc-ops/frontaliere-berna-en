Full description

Business Analyst: in Facility Management 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Business Analyst: in Facility Management Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You design the digital basis for the facility management of SBB Immobilien and you make a tangible contribution to the availability and safety of our buildings and facilities. In an interdisciplinary team of business analysts, BizDevOps and developers, you work closely with the Facility Management department and drive central solutions such as SAP PM, SSAM, Fiori and Campos. We have several exciting projects ahead of us that start with the analysis and create real added value for users. The role offers responsibility, the opportunity to lead topics independently, and professional and methodological further development in an environment with servant leadership and short decision-making paths. You can move that.

  • As a Business Analyst, you capture, prioritize and sharpen requirements for maintenance solutions such as SAP PM and SSAM, contributing to the stability and development of our applications.
  • You organize and moderate workshops with the area of facility management and lead participants structured from the idea to the viable requirement.
  • With a clear view of conflicting goals, you moderate between new business wishes, system standards and the current state and find solutions that are acceptable to all sides.
  • As an interface between business and IT, you translate professional requirements into realizable IT requirements, coordinate with internal and external partners and coordinate as a contact person: for owners of larger projects (epics) the progress together with the business project manager
  • You document requirements up to technical specification, create test cases, carry out tests and accompany user tests until acceptance.
  • You are happy to take responsibility for data quality and individual topics are independently and proactively managed by agreement.

You bring that with you.

  • With team and communication strength, you enjoy working with business and IT and promoting a clear exchange.
  • High initiative, a pragmatic working method and the willingness to actively collect missing information characterize the working method. Connected and conceptually thinking, you take on responsibility in the SBB environment and keep an eye on the whole thing.
  • An education in facility management or computer science (or equivalent) gives you a good foundation. You bring several years of experience in business analysis or a corresponding qualification.
  • With IT applications for facility management you work safely, especially you know SAP EAM or PM incl. SSAM and possibly CAFM systems.
  • You have project management experience as well as knowledge of agile methods such as Scrum and SAFe.
  • In German you are confident in negotiations, ideally you have good knowledge of English and/or French (minimum B2).

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This position is in requirement level J, regional allowance level 1.

If you still want to tell us your motivation, you still have the opportunity to do so in our application tool. Making big is easier for you. This starts with your application.

That's how it goes.
