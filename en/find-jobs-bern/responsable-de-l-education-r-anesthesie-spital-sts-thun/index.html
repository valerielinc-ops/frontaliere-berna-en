Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking for meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

As an educational manager: r you take on the professional and organizational responsibility for the training of our students in anesthesia care. Your core tasks include recruiting, introducing and accompanying students as well as planning and coordinating their training, including internships. In addition, you will continuously develop our training concept and ensure its implementation. The share of education is about 40-60%.

In addition, you work 40-50% as a dipl. Expert: in anesthesia care NDS HF in clinical everyday life. In our modern operating room with eight operating rooms and clearly structured processes, you contribute to a safe and high-quality anesthesiological care. You work interdisciplinary and maintain a close cooperation with the anesthesiologists as well as the teams of storage care, the operating area and the recovery room.

We also give committed personalities the opportunity to complete the training as an educationally responsible anesthesia care specialist and thus develop themselves specifically for this responsible task.

Admission immediately or by arrangement.
