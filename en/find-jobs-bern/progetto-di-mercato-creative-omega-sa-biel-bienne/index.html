Full description

Company introduction OMEGA is a prestigious watchmaking brand known worldwide for its high standards of quality, precision and innovation for more than 170 years. Our watches are among the finest in the world and are characterized by their cutting-edge technology and timeless style.

Throughout our long history, we have broken precision records, redefined sports timekeeping and maintained our position at the forefront of Haute Horlogerie.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams on almost every continent through their talents, ambitions and inspiration.

Job description Dedicated to creative activities within the Creative Marketing team, you will assist the head of the creative team. Your role will be key, your challenges will be numerous, and you will be energized by the excitement of requests and the rhythmic pace of creation.

Your tasks will be

  • Design/creation and production of advertising materials in all formats, following a holistic approach
  • Coordination with the various departments
  • Management of the flow of information with external agencies, freelancers and others
  • Identification and development of relationships with new service providers (photographers, directors, agencies, …)
  • Provide the necessary support for various internal requests

Profile

  • Degree in visual communication and/or digital/online design or Graphic Arts
  • Minimum 5 years of professional experience, including at least 1 year in a communications agency and/or luxury company
  • Excellent command of Adobe tools (Photoshop, Indesign, Illustrator, …)
  • Excellent command of graphic and typographic composition
  • Strong knowledge of ergonomic principles (mobile, web, app, …)
  • Motion design skills are a plus
  • Great attention to detail and organizational skills with excellent time and priority management
  • Strong experience in managing, interpreting and executing creative projects within tight deadlines
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills
