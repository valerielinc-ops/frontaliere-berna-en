Technical supporter for the Rail Control System (RCS) 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Technical supporter for the Rail Control System (RCS) Bern / Work Smart, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. The Agile Release Train Capacity Control develops, operates and trains products for the automation of operational rail operations and you make a noticeable contribution to the reliable control of over 10,000 trains in Switzerland every day. In an interdisciplinary environment with ten agile teams, you will work with the business to design the product range from conception to operation and advance highly available and rail-critical systems in a future-oriented manner without affecting ongoing operations. You can expect a work-related environment with direct connection to the operations centers as well as a team that ensures the efficient and economical use of the applications, especially the Rail Control System RCS-D. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • As a technical user support in second level support, you ensure the stable and effective operation of RCS-D and represent the concerns of users in IT.
  • You analyze disruptions and data errors in a structured manner and derive appropriate measures so that operations remain reliable.
  • You will process reported incidents from the initial assessment through effective workarounds to long-term resolution.
  • You maintain master data carefully and ensure quality and consistency for the operation.
  • You will build suitable tools for efficient support services and transparently track error messages and incidents.
  • You will actively participate in tests and the preparation of new releases as well as innovations in the RCS-D environment and contribute to continuous improvement.

This is what you bring with you.

  • You are characterized by the ability to work in a team and strong communication skills, and you act calmly and service-oriented even under pressure.
  • You combine a high sense of responsibility and quality with a strong customer and service orientation.
  • Analytical and solution-oriented thinking as well as independent action help you to quickly understand complex issues and approach effective solutions.
  • As a user of RCS Dispo, for example as a dispatcher or ZVL, you have practical experience or the willingness to acquire the necessary specialist knowledge
  • Experience in SQL database queries and the willingness to familiarize yourself with the complex world of railway IT systems. - You have a very good command of spoken and written German, ideally at level C1 and knowledge of another national language is desirable and you are interested in working together for several years.
