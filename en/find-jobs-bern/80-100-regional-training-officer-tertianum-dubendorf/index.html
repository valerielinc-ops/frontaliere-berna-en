Full description

Are you passionate about personal development and health training? Do you want to use your skills and experience constructively to sustainably strengthen the quality of training for our apprentices and students? Then you're the person we need!

For our Corporate Service Center site in Dübendorf or Bern, we are looking for a solution now or to agree on a

80-100% Regional Training Officer

Your missions

  • Preparation, implementation and follow-up of structured accompanying visits in the

TERTIANUM companies to ensure the quality of training of apprentices and students (nursery and other professions)

  • Training and, if necessary, intensive support for in-company training managers (BBV/BB)
  • Support and communication in complex situations with key in-house services, schools and education services
  • Active participation in TERTIANUM training and development initiatives
  • Collaboration in the recruitment of apprentices and students

Your profile

  • Completed training in care (at least tertiary level) and strong experience in vocational training (BV/BB function and experience in care management)
  • SVEB 2 or equivalent training
  • Strong skills in counselling, stress resistance and ability to quickly integrate into new teams/structures and initiate change in a pragmatic way.
  • High availability to travel within assigned regions, flexibility and willingness to carry out short- and medium-term on-site attendance phases in institutions. (A general subscription is available)
  • Very good command of German and French in oral and written terms (minimum level C1), bilingualism being an asset

  • Various fleet reductions
