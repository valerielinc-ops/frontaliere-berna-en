Clinic for Depression and Anxiety

At UZP, the focus is on people. On our treatment unit for depression and anxiety diagnoses, we accompany and support patients on their individual path through treatment and back to a better quality of life. For this we are looking for a committed and competent nurse who enjoys psychiatric care and is actively involved in an interprofessional team. With us, you can expect a varied and demanding working environment in which relationship design, professional competence and a professional attitude are central. Your experience in the field of psychiatry and your perspective are expressly in demand.

Your tasks

  • You accompany and monitor patients: women in depression treatment
  • You work in a committed and motivated interprofessional team, where you pursue defined goals together and search for solutions together
  • You contribute your valuable experience and actively participate in the compliance and optimization of our high quality standards
  • You take on the role as the nursing caregiver of the patient assigned to you:
  • You contribute to a high patient through competence and relationship formation: inner satisfaction

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH
  • You have experience in psychiatry or somatics with an interest in psychiatry
  • You are resilient and appreciate the challenges on a depression station
  • Through professional attitude and critical (co-)thinking, you can contribute to the optimization of structures and processes

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
