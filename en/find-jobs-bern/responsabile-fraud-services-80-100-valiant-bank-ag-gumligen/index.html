Full description

Fraud forms are constantly evolving and thus the importance of effective fraud prevention is growing. Are you ready to build a new Fraud Services team right from the start and actively shape the future of fraud management at Valiant?

They shall

  • Lead the Fraud Services team professionally and personnel and ensure the achievement of quality and quantity goals
  • actively shape and shape the development of the new Fraud Services Team from the beginning
  • responsible for day-to-day operations in fraud management and participate
  • Ensure compliance with regulations, uniform processes, standards and risk mitigation measures as well as their optimization in the area of fraud management
  • Steer collaboration with relevant stakeholders such as digital banking, product management, legal and CISO to ensure a smooth flow of information
  • Working closely with the risk owners, preparing and presenting status and risk reporting, and representing Valiant Bank in fraud-related projects and committees

They bring

  • adequate education at university/technical level
  • proven leadership experience in a similar function
  • comprehensive know-how in fraud management, preferably from the financial services environment
  • Expertise in regulatory requirements, in particular FINMA requirements
  • Pronounced analytical-conceptual thinking, result orientation and strong communication skills
  • Negotiation-proof French and conversation-proof English skills
  • High motivation to actively shape and further develop the area of fraud management
  • Safe and convincing appearance and assertiveness

About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial services provider operating exclusively in Switzerland. It offers private customers and SMEs a comprehensive, easy-to-understand offer in all financial matters. Valiant is locally anchored in the following 15 cantons: Aargau, Basel Landscape, Basel City, Bern, Freiburg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. It is also present throughout Switzerland thanks to innovative, digital services. Valiant has a balance sheet total of 38.2 billion francs and employs around 1200 people – of which more than 70 are apprentices.
