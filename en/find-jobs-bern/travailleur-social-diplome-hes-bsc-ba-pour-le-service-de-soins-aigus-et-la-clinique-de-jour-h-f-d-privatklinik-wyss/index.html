Full description

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy. Special areas of expertise include the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders. The clinic offers its outpatient, daily clinical and inpatient services in the region of Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel and employs around 340 employees at all three locations. Dipl. You are empathetic, professional and keep track even in complex situations? Then we look for you. As a social worker in the acute care department, you accompany patients individually and personally – where social challenges, problems or crises are part of the psychiatric treatment task. Your work thus makes a decisive contribution to holistic care. In our acute care department, our interprofessional team treats people with psychiatric conditions who need more intensive support and this is where you come into play: with expertise, sensitivity and serenity. This is what your professional day looks like with us

  • You work directly on our acute department, in the middle of the interprofessional team and very close to the patients. In addition, together with another social worker, you are responsible for patients at the day clinic.
  • You advise on social issues, help with day-to-day management and plan the next steps after the hospital stay together with the patients.
  • You will raise the resources and challenges of our acute inpatient and day-to-day clinical patients from a social work perspective, prioritize the topics displayed and accompany them in finding solutions and implementing them.
  • This includes close interprofessional cooperation and networking both internally and externally with institutions, employers and people from the patient’s social environment.
  • You plan, organize and conduct network conversations independently or with the treatment team.
  • The course documentation and correspondence are also part of your tasks.
  • You also take on other tasks for the clinic social service and help to represent colleagues in their absence. You bring that with you
  • Graduate degree in social work (or equivalent) and experience in clinical social work in psychiatry, health care or social insurance.
  • Even in hectic times, you can count on your independence, flexibility and personal responsibility.
  • For dealing with people in crisis situations, your empathy and friendliness will help you.
  • At the same time, you enjoy tackling challenging goals and achieving them together with the team and the department.
  • You consider the administration as a natural part of social work and do it efficiently and quickly.
  • Standing still is not yours – You have fun developing new ideas and putting them into practice in order to further develop the clinic social service. As an SME with a considerable size, the working days are very varied and offer variety and design possibilities. The contact with the colleagues is personal and the decision paths are short. We operate with flexible working time models wherever possible and use technology to support our flexibility.
