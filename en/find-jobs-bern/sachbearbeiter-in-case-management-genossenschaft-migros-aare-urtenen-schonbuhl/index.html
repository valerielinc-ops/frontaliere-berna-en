Full description

Our presence and case management support employees in health challenges and successful return to work. As a staff member, you are the team's organizational hub. With your organizational talent, you ensure smooth processes and help ensure that employees receive the best possible support.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the entire process from damage reporting to administrative case management
  • Your support includes all administrative tasks in cooperation with the social security partners (KV, UV, IV)
  • You are responsible for monthly controlling tasks, including absence evaluations and control
  • You are actively involved in projects such as process optimization of the company case management, including its implementation and documentation
  • In addition to the specific tasks you take over general

administrative activities - As a central contact person for external and internal inquiries, you ensure efficient communication in the company

Requisiti - First professional experience: in a comparable turntable function in a large company - Completed basic or further training: as a saleswoman/business man or in a comparable administrative course (medical secretary, assistant)

Ä.)

  • you have solid knowledge in MS Office applications to create professional documents and communicate effectively -

Teamwork is important to you and you bring a positive dynamic into the team - German (negotiable)
