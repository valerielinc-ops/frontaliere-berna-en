Full description

Schleuniger in Thun is a leading technology company and has been part of the Komax Group since 2010. For decades, the company and its approximately 200 employees have set standards in semi-automatic cable processing – with innovative solutions and future-oriented technology. With its products, Schleuniger serves a wide range of customers from the automotive industry, transport, medical technology, aerospace, information and communication technology as well as industrial and consumer electronics. Your tasks In this role, you actively shape and work closely with the interface between development and production. They not only bring Lean into workshops, but also implement improvements directly in production – visible, measurable and effective.

  • Conducting transfer projects from development to series production
  • Planning, design and implementation of assembly workstations taking into account lean philosophy
  • Design of processes and continuous improvement over all value creation levels; active cooperation and management in optimization projects Your profile You are a practical personality who is happy to assume responsibility, works structured and appreciates the dialogue between technology and production.
  • Basic technical training in the field of mechanics/assembly technology/electromechanics or similar.
  • Further education FS/HF or FH (machinery, process engineering, mechatronics, electrical engineering etc.) or many years of practical experience in the manufacturing process
  • Well-founded experience in production logistics, AVOR, psp
  • proven experience in project work and lean methods
  • very good knowledge in MS Office and SAP
  • German negotiation-proof, English desired Our offer A practical role with a real influence on processes, workstations and series production.
  • Exciting challenge in a global technology company
  • Modern infrastructure and working conditions
  • Flexible working hours and models
  • Dynamic and open team We look forward to receiving your application! Do you have any questions? We are at your disposal for further information: Reto Feller jobs@schleuniger.ch
