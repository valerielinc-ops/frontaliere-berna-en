About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial services provider and operates exclusively in Switzerland. It offers private customers and SMEs a comprehensive, easy-to-understand offering on all financial issues. Valiant is locally anchored in the following 15 cantons: Aargau, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Freiburg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. It is also present throughout Switzerland thanks to innovative, digital services. Valiant has total assets of 38.2 billion francs and employs around 1,200 people - including over 70 trainees.
