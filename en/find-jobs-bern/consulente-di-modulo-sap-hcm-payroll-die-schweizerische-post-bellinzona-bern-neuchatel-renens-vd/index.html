Full description

You are passionate about contract processing in a corporate environment and would like to work in an attractive HR IT environment that actively contributes to the support of internal postal partners with innovative solutions? Then apply to us today – because with you we focus on diversity and creativity in finding solutions.

You can do that.

  • As an SAP HR module consultant: in Payroll you are jointly responsible for the wage processing of the approximately 47'000 employees of Swiss Post.
  • In the Payroll team, you carry out the technical wage runs reliably and on time, where you are responsible for data processing, billing, FICO booking and the sending of payment documents, among other things.
  • You are responsible for technical development, work independently in projects and optimize existing processes in your areas of expertise – for example, payroll, wage types or family allowances.
  • You support the team in various issues relating to the Payroll module and take over the 3rd-level support for the ongoing operation in the SAP HCM Payroll.
  • Existing solutions are optimized by you through support, customization and targeted further developments, where you have the opportunity to gain development experience directly in everyday work.
  • With your know-how and experience, you develop customized solutions for our internal partners and implement them independently.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic commercial training and further education in the field of payroll/social insurance form the basis of your professional experience.
  • Your knowledge as a payroll specialist: in resp. Specialist: in SAP HCM and in the field of social insurance, you have been able to apply successfully for several years.
  • Experience with SuccesFactors EC is welcome.
  • You work precisely, quality-consciously and reliably, bring a healthy self-motivation and convince through your solution orientation and resilience – even in hectic situations you keep a clear head.
  • With your networked thinking and open nature, you build a bridge between computer science and the department of personnel.
  • Very good knowledge of German round off your profile and every other language is an advantage.

Independent work is possible, 2 days of attendance per week in Bern are required.
