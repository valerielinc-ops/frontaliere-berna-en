Full description

One of these is the labour market measure EAR (determination of labour market relevant resources), a location determination and potential assessment for German and French-speaking job seekers. It is aimed at people with an unclear reintegration strategy, if there is a need for clarification on labour market-relevant skills and an assessment of the integration potential for the labour market. The results form an important basis for further professional integration. The assigning authority is the cantonal office for unemployment insurance (RAV). You recognize professional potential and can derive realistic prospects for the first job market? You combine career advice with in-depth knowledge of the labour market, have experience in professional integration and would like to help shape a new labour market measure right from the start? For our labour market measure EAR we are looking for 1. December 2026 dedicated personalities for our location Ostermundigen as

Professional and career counselling 60 - 100%

Your tasks with us

  • Together with the participants, you will analyze their professional experiences, skills and development opportunities with regard to the current job market.
  • You will determine realistic job search areas and develop concrete career prospects
  • Together with the participants, you define suitable job search areas and create the technical basis for the further reintegration strategy
  • You bring your expertise to the job market, job profiles and career development in the resource assessment
  • You create together with the expert case management well-founded resource profiles
  • You work closely with the other specialists of the AMM EAR and bring your professional perspective to the interdisciplinary clarification

Here's what you bring with you

  • You have a CAS in Career Diagnostics and Career Advice for Adults or an MAS in Career Advice
  • You have at least three years of proven professional experience in professional integration
  • You know the Swiss labour market and have in-depth knowledge of various professional and activity fields
  • You have experience in career consulting, resource assessment, potential analysis or professional location determination
  • You can assess professional resources and competencies in a differentiated way and derive realistic perspectives for the first job market.
  • You have very good knowledge of German or French in word and writing. Knowledge of the other language is an advantage
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented
  • You are confident in using digital tools

Your advantages with us

  • Possibility to help shape a new labour market measure right from the start
  • Meaningful activity with direct contribution to professional integration
  • Technically demanding consulting work with high personal responsibility
  • Collaboration in a committed and interdisciplinary team
  • Attractive and progressive employment conditions
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Modern workplace in Ostermundigen
  • At least six weeks of vacation per year
