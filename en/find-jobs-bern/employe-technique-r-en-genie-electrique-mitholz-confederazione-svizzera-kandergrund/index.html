Full description

Carry out maintenance, repair and overhaul services of electrical installations and equipment in the fields of high and low current, safety systems or telecommunications

  • Manage and take responsibility for the support and maintenance of complex security, alarm, lighting, audio and video systems
  • Participation in the supervision of maintenance, commissioning, inspections and other activities by third parties

  • Independent, team-capable, resilient, communicative personality who wants to work together with the team for positive success
  • Require readiness for coercive and weapon training
  • Member of the company fire brigade
