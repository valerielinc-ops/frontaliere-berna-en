Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

At People & Culture, we accompany our employees in everyday work and take care of their concerns in order to contribute to a smooth working environment. If you are enthusiastic about Human Resources and are happy to take on administrative tasks, we look forward to meeting you! In order to strengthen our dynamic team, we are looking for a socially competent personality as:

Personnel/in Hu­man Resources 100%

You do

  • You take on a wide range of tasks in personnel administration and ensure professional handling of all administrative processes from entry to exit
  • You collect and maintain all data along the entire employee lifecycle in SAP HCM, from entry to exit
  • You create employment certificates and other personnel-relevant documents
  • Together with your team, you are the contact person for personnel administrative concerns of our employees
  • You process special cases and individual requests and are also in exchange with external bodies
  • You manage the staff administration counter and are a competent point of contact for our employees.

This sets you apart

  • You have completed a commercial apprenticeship as well as further training as a clerk / staff
  • You bring 2–3 years of experience in personnel administration and wage processing
  • You have very good MS Office knowledge and experience with SuccessFactors and SAP
  • You work independently, carefully and quality-consciously and convince with your organizational talent
  • Your mother tongue is French or German and you have very good oral and written knowledge of the other language (level B2)

You will benefit from this

A bilingual and versatile working environment awaits you at the SZB. We work together with people from a wide variety of professional groups and value appreciative cooperation, mutual support and good cooperation. With us you can contribute your experience, take on responsibility and develop professionally. In addition to the employment conditions according to our GAV, you benefit from various advantages and discounts, including discounts in our pharmacy, public transport, for your leisure time and in our staff restaurant. In addition, we offer a good connection to public transport as well as a weekly offer with yoga, meditation and various group courses.
