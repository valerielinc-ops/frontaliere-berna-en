Full description

Portrait The Educational Center for Hearing and Speech HSM Münchenbuchsee is a service center with a wide range of support and advice for children and adolescents with severe hearing and speech impairments and their environment. We train normally gifted pupils with hearing and/or language impairments from basic to secondary level I.

Do you like to contribute to developments and are you ready to build and implement structures? Do you see solutions and resources? Then you are exactly right with us.

For our school area we search from 1 February 2027 or by agreement

Tasks

The object is composed as follows

Cycle 3 in Münchenbuchsee (3 classes) Cycle 1 at the locations Burgdorf, Worb, Rüderswil (4 classes)

  • They guide and support all professionals in their everyday professional life
  • They value education quality
  • They focus on teaching and school development topics
  • Work closely with other leaders
  • You work with all areas of the HSM

Requirements

  • They have school management training and bring managerial and professional experience
  • They put people at the center and like challenges
  • You are a flexible and resilient personality
  • You are capable of teamwork and work solution-oriented
  • You have knowledge of the Bern school system

We offer

  • diverse activity with scope for design
