Full description

Portrait

At the Untere Emme school, over 1,000 students are taught by 125 teachers at the three locations in Bätterkinden, Utzenstorf and Wiler.

Around 400 children, from kindergarten to 9th grade, attend classes at the Bätterkinden location.

Tasks

  • You accompany and support a bVSA international student in a 7th grade with 6 lessons.
  • You support the two 8th graders with 6 IF and 2 MRDV lessons.
  • The classes are taught in Model 4.
  • You work together in a motivated, committed and collegial team.
  • You benefit from mentorship and school social work.
  • We also focus on learning support/coaching.
  • We are also on the move with “School Acts” and are working on company health management for our employees.

Requirements

  • You have a diploma in school-based curative education or are in training for it.
  • If you are a teacher who is passionate about teaching and has an open and positive attitude, then we look forward to meeting you.

We offer

  • As part of our team, you have the opportunity to do a varied and interesting job.
  • The Bätterkinden location is easily accessible by public transport.
  • We also offer you free parking spaces directly on the school premises.
  • You will receive a laptop.
  • We work with Microsoft products as well as teacher office and Klapp.
