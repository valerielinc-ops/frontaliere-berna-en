For Raiffeisenbank Seeland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Aarberg, Biel/Bienne, Büren, Ins, Lyss and Studen, we are looking for a motivated and customer-oriented person as a back-office all-rounder: in Lyss. Tasks:
