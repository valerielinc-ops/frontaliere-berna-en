Full description

Portrait Our school - located in beautiful Allenlüften in the municipality of Mühleberg - has around 330 students, 49 teachers, a school social worker, an MR team, a school management team and a caretaker team surrounded by two school buildings, a modern triple sports hall including a climbing wall, a large sports field, a day school, a library and a youth club. The school is located in a beautiful, rural setting, about 15km from Bern and Murten and can be reached in 17 minutes by train/bus from Bern.

Responsibilities

  • We are looking for a teacher > substitute for 4 weeks who would like to teach at the 3./4.C.
  • Part-time with 15 lessons.
  • They teach at the 3rd/4th grade C (17 children) - German, Mathematics, French 3. Class, NMG, Sport and TTG.
  • They accompany, encourage and support our learners in everyday school life.
  • They take responsibility for the class and show flexibility.
  • They maintain an open and constructive exchange with parents, team and other stakeholders.

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have a pedagogical education, for example a Bachelor's degree in Primary Education or a seminar teaching certificate.
  • You already have initial professional experience as a teacher and have a positive attitude towards children, parents and the team.
  • As a responsible, communicative and flexible personality, you keep track of things even in challenging situations and are resilient.

We offer

  • You can expect a committed and helpful team as well as a supportive school management.
  • You will benefit from a supportive cooperation with our MR team and the school social worker.
  • Our school has a modern and attractive infrastructure.
  • Each teacher receives a personal work device (MacBook).
  • Free parking is available directly at the school building.
