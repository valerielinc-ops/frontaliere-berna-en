About us

We are actively researching the genetic causes of hereditary diseases and other hereditary characteristics in pets. The results of our research are the basis of genetic tests that can prevent the unintentional breeding of sick animals and promote animal health. In addition, the results of our research are also relevant for human patients and human medicine. Their tasks
