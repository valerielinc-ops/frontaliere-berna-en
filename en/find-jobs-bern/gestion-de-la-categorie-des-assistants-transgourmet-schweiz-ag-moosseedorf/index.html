Assistant Category Management Non-Food

Transgourmet Schweiz AG — Moosseedorf, Bern, Svizzera

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional consulting and innovative services.Transgourmet Schweiz AG belongs to the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • Together with the Category Manager, you will form a well-established team, further develop your product range and be the central point of contact for suppliers and internal stakeholders.
  • You support the Category Manager in the range design
  • You work with price calculations and price adjustments
  • You create evaluations, analyses and decision bases
  • You observe the market, recognize trends early and bring new impetus to the range
  • You are actively involved in projects and help to continuously develop processes
  • You open, maintain and optimize your article master data

Requirements

  • You have a degree as a merchant/merchant or a comparable qualification
  • You will benefit from professional experience in category management, product management or procurement
  • You are well-versed in MS-Office applications, especially Excel
  • You distinguish your highly structured, exact and independent working method
  • You are a team capable, responsible and reliable personality
  • In addition to your fluent German skills, you will ideally bring French skills with you.
