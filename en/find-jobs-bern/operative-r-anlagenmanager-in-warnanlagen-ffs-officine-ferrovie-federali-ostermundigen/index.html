You can look forward to this. In this role, you will make a direct contribution to safety at work sites in the track area, because reliable warning systems protect employees and ensure smooth construction and maintenance processes. You work closely with different departments, contribute ideas for product improvements and develop yourself further both professionally and methodically. A collegial environment and exchange with specialists support you in your everyday life. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • As an operational system manager: in warning systems, you look after the Swiss-wide warning systems over the entire life cycle from project planning to decommissioning, thereby contributing to rail safety.
  • You manage the Swiss-wide warning systems throughout their entire life cycle and ensure their reliable operation. You support the local superusers and the security management in technical issues. If necessary, you will ensure the transfer of knowledge through training.
  • You bring technical expertise into new projects and ensure compliance with relevant guidelines and regulations for safe workplaces.
  • In contractual and commercial contexts, you act as the primary contact for warning system providers and other stakeholders.
  • You manage security system projects in the area on behalf of project and system management as well as life cycle management.
  • In collaboration with strategic asset management, you will be involved in product evaluations and procurements in accordance with public procurement law. This is what you bring with you.
  • Safety, quality and responsibility are your top priority and you clearly represent this attitude in our collaboration.
  • You like teamwork, you organize yourself efficiently, negotiate skillfully and think in a networked manner across specialist boundaries.
  • With personal responsibility, you proactively seek sustainable solutions, show perseverance and are willing to work across departments and visit workplaces throughout Switzerland.
  • You are interested in safety on railway construction sites and are open to new technologies. Professional experience in the area of ​​railway warning systems is an advantage.
  • You ideally have higher technical training at HF ​​or FH level and experience in security management functions is desirable.
  • You speak German or French at least at level C1 and another national language, ideally at level B2. You have at least level B1 technical English.
