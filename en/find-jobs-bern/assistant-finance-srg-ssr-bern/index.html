Role overview

In the context of the SSR Finance sector, as a member of the assistance pool, you will provide administrative and organizational support to the Finance Management Team, including:

  • management of the agenda of selected components of the management team Finances;
  • coordination of appointments and organization of internal and external meetings, business trips and events.
  • management of department-specific communication channels (e.g.

MS Teams channels, newsletters and intranet);

  • organization and verification of translations, with particular attention to German and Italian (e.g.

presentations, documents and communication content);

  • coordination of the supply of continuing training and support in the measures of change and transformation;
  • interface management with other SSR assistance pools and replacement, during holiday absences, of other people with assistance in the Finance sector.

Requirements

  • Multiannual experience in a service function, ideally supporting leadership roles.
  • Service-oriented, structured and proactive working methods.
  • Discretion in managing sensitive information and ability to establish priorities reliably.
  • Good familiarity with digital tools and MS 365 environment.
  • Passion for interdisciplinary collaboration in a dynamic context, with strong rooting in different language regions.
  • Excellent knowledge of German and Italian, including stylistic mastery in oral and written communication.
  • Esperienza pluriennale in una funzione di assistenza, idealmente a supporto di ruoli dirigenziali.
  • Modalità di lavoro orientata al servizio, strutturata e proattiva.
