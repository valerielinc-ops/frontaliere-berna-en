Full description

Portrait Niederbipp is a municipality with about 5500 inhabitants on the Jurassic foot. The municipality has a motorway access and is also easily accessible by public transport, ASM and SBB. The municipality is constantly evolving and offers a large recreation area in addition to many jobs. The Niederbipp school, with around 660 pupils in 34 classes, forms a lively and attractive school landscape. At the upper level nine classes with about 165 students are taught.

Tasks

  • As a Heilpä-Pä-dagogin/heilpä-pä-agoge or social pedagogue you take over in close cooperation with other special teachers and class teachers the integrative promotion of different classes in cycle 3.
  • We maintain a close cooperation with school social work and other specialist offices and a regular exchange in the MR team is of course.
  • In addition to accompanying students, their tasks include the counselling of teachers.

Requirements

  • You are a school-based healing päedagogue or in training or social pädagogic.
  • You bring joy to work in the interdisciplinary team and are ready to contribute and develop new contemporary vessels.
  • The integrative promotion and support of learners is at your heart and you focus on the development potential of the learners.

We offer

  • permanent employment
  • a good public transport connection
  • a dedicated team that supports and exchanges each other.
