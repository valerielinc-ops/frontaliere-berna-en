Full description

Reference code: JR130111

Since MONTBLANC revolutionized writing culture in 1906 with the first spill-proof fountain pen, we have been known for outstanding craftmanship, highest aesthetics, and innovative solutions. And we keep pushing the boundaries. We combine the best of tradition, technology and design in writing instruments, watches, leather goods, new technologies and selected accessories. With our name and in our emlem, we embrace this mission: to implement bold ideas with outstanding craftsmanship and create refined products for people with highest expectations. In doing so, we are at the forefront of technology and design, drawing on the passion of more than 3,000 employees and living a culture that - like our products - inspired people to unleash their potential, to three and to achieve.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Design and Development:
  • Design and develop mechanical clock movements using 2D CAD and 3D software PTC Creo and Tellwatch
  • Conduct technical feasibility studies and functional analyses of new movements or changes in existing movements.
  • Define the mechanical architectures, kinematics and dimensional tolerances of components.
  • Integrate aesthetic, technical, cost and production constraints from the design phase
  1. Collaboration with Industrialization and Prototyping:
  • Complete technical records (manufacture plans, nomenclatures, technical specifications) for prototyping and production.
  • Collaborate with the prototyping workshop and project manager for the realization of the first parts and assembly of the movements.
  • Participate in prototype testing and validation phases, analyze results and propose improvements.
  • Technical monitoring during the industrialization and mass production phase.
  1. Optimization and Continuous Improvement:
  • Find and propose innovative solutions to improve the performance, reliability, accuracy and ease of assembly of movements.
  • Optimize manufacturing costs without compromising quality.
  1. Collaboration and Communication:
  • Work closely with designers, watchmakers, methods, project managers and production teams.
  • Present technical advances and solutions to internal teams and management.
  • Participate in project reviews and technical meetings.

Qualifications Required

  • Training: Diploma of engineer in microtechnics watchmaking orientation or title deemed equivalent
  • Experience: Minimum 5 years of significant experience in the design and development of complex watch movements, ideally within a high watchmaking factory.

Technical Skills

  • In-depth mastery of watch mechanics and complications.
  • Expertise in PTC Creo and Windchill software
  • Knowledge of watchmaking materials and manufacturing processes (machining, cutting, surface processing, etc.).
  • Ability to read and interpret complex technical plans.
  • Knowledge of watch industry standards and standards.
  • Languages: Mastery of French

Personal qualities

  • Rigor, precision and attention to detail.
  • Creativity and innovation.
  • Ability to analyze and solve complex problems.
  • Autonomy and proactivity.
  • Excellent team spirit and communication skills.
  • Passion for watchmaking and precision mechanics.
