Full description

With a keen eye for detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury destination in Switzerland. It's people like you who make the difference and bring to life the «place we love to be» together with us. YOU MAKE THE DIFFERENCE HERE

  • Together with the sales management, you ensure that our customers don't just shop, but take away a beauty experience that inspires and delights.
  • In the absence of the sales manager, you take over the personnel and organizational management of the department and create an environment where advisory culture, style awareness, and team spirit are lived.
  • You advise the team in day-to-day business, promote type-specific advice, and bring your strengths in visual merchandising and staging brand and style worlds to the table.
  • Performance tracking and KPI analysis. You keep an eye on the team's performance to make strategic decisions that make us successful together.
  • You manage processes efficiently, support events and administrative tasks – and ensure that digital tools are used smartly.
  • You accompany new employees and trainees, showing leadership with heart and intelligence.
  • Requirements
  • You bring several years of professional experience and initial leadership experience in high-end retail – ideally as deputy sales manager or team leader.
  • Your foundation is a completed training in retail EFZ – supplemented by further education.
  • You have a feel for trends, brands, and styles – and know how to advise people in a type-specific, authentic, and enthusiastic manner.
  • You work in a structured way, think entrepreneurially, and actively promote the team.
  • You communicate confidently in German, additional language skills are a plus.
  • You use digital applications with ease.
  • What we offer
  • A workplace in the heart of Bern – in a house that stands for culture, premium brands, and excellent service.
  • A team with experience, beauty expertise, and a keen sense, which looks forward to an inspiring leadership personality.
  • Attractive social benefits and at least 5 weeks of vacation – plus the possibility of up to 180 days of unpaid leave.
  • Up to 50% discount on the entire GLOBUS range.
  • Targeted further training, so you can advance professionally and personally. Contact We look forward to receiving your online application. It's very straightforward.
