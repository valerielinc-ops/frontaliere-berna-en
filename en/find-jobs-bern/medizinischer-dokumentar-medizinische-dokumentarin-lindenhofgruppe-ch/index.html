Full description

Tasks

Become part of our team at the Oncology Center and bring your expertise in tumor documentation and medical data collection.

  • Collection and maintenance of oncological case data as well as aftercare data in tumor documentation software
  • Participation in the weekly tumor boards including presentation of cases and support in protocol management
  • Control and plausibility check of key figures with those responsible as well as creation of relevant documents for certification and audits
  • Cooperation in internal and external audits
  • Collection and collection of highly specialised medicine (HSM) data and medical registries from patient dossiers and medical reports
  • Financial statement and data preparation support

Requirements

  • Completed training and experience in the medical field, ideally in nursing or surgical technique
  • Oncology professional experience is beneficial
  • Very good IT user knowledge and a good understanding of data management and database systems
  • Structured, careful and quality-conscious working method
  • Team-oriented, flexible and solution-oriented personality with a positive attitude
  • Motivation to make an important contribution to the continuous improvement of treatment quality
