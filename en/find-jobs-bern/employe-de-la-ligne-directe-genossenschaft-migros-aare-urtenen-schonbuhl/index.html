Full description

Do you have experience in social insurance, payroll and a flair for numbers as well as HR IT systems such as SAP HR? Then you are exactly right with us! Support our team in day-to-day business, advise employees and managers competently and actively shape the digitalization of our HR processes. Take the opportunity to leverage your expertise in an exciting environment.

Mansioni

  • Working in day-to-day operations in various social insurances, time recording and payroll processing
  • Implementation of daily allowance processing (KTG/UTG) in UKA and SAP for the monthly wage runs
  • Advising and supporting managers and employees on social security issues
  • Active participation in the optimization and digitalization of processes
  • Support in the preparation of evaluations and lists

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in wage and/or social security
  • Completed basic or further education: as a businesswoman and further education in HR/Payroll
  • Team-capable personality with exact, networked and efficient working methods
  • A pronounced flair for numbers and an affinity for HR IT systems (SAP HR, UKA)
  • High degree of independence and flexibility
  • Customer focus and self-responsible work
  • German (very good knowledge)
