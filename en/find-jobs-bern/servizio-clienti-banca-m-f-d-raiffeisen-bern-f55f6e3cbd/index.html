Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Bern, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices at the Orphanage Square and in Muri b. Bern, we are looking for a committed, sales- and service-oriented personality as customer advisor (m/f/d) 60-100%.

What do you expect?

  • Comprehensive advice and support of customers in the basic products "Pay, save, invest, provide"
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous support of the team customer portfolio as well as acquisition of new customers
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the individual wishes, goals and life situations of the customers
  • Cooperation in market development measures such as contact campaigns and/or events
  • Management of money input and output equipment
  • A great working environment

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • Experience in the financial services sector
  • Salesmanlike flair and likeable, winning appearance
  • Enjoying proactive customer contact with a strong customer orientation
  • Very good knowledge of German, knowledge of French advantageous

Our customers with their needs and goals are the focus. We advise them competently, comprehensively and with passion. Jasmine Fuhrer Team leader private client consultancy Why Raiffeisen?
