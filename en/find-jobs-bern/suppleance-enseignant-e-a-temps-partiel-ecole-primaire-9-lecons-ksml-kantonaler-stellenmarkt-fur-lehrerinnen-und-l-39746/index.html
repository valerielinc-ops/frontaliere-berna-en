Are you interested in a city school with a village character? At the Lerchenfeld school location we are looking for a replacement for a part-time teacher at one of our 1st/2nd classes. Multi-year class. Do you feel addressed or do you have any questions? Then contact us immediately!

Tasks

  • As a substitute for a part-time teacher, you will teach a 1st/2nd grade. Multi-year class with a total of 9 lessons.
  • Working days are Thursday morning and Friday morning.
  • You teach the following subjects: German (2 lessons), mathematics (2 lessons), NMG (4 lessons), exercise and sport (1 lesson).
  • You work in a motivated team at the Lerchenfeld primary school and work with several colleagues.

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have a seminary primary teacher certificate or a bachelor's degree for preschool and primary school.
  • You are motivated to teach in a multi-year class.

We offer

  • Temporary employment according to cantonal employment conditions (LAG)
  • On-site support from the class teacher, IF teacher, team of teachers and school management
  • Easily accessible by public transport
  • Parking space available at the school building (chargeable, Fr. 2/day)
  • Good infrastructure
  • Applications are processed continuously
