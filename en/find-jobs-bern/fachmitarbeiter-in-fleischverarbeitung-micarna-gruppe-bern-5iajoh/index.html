Full description

Mérat AG has been serving the Swiss gastronomy for over 70 years. From eight regional locations, restaurants, hotels, and communal catering facilities are supplied daily with first-class meat and delicatessen specialties.

Responsibilities

  • Goods receipt & warehouse management (freezers and cold storage)
  • Portioning, cutting, and processing according to customer requirements
  • Unpacking frozen goods, piece goods, meat, and charcuterie products
  • Implementing hygiene regulations
  • Order picking

Requirements

  • 1-3 years of experience: In meat processing
  • Completed basic or further training: Food industry
  • Efficient and precise working method
  • Cheerful, dynamic personality
  • Willingness to work early hours
  • German (very good knowledge)

  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on private and investment accounts for its employees
