More about this search for cross-border applicants

Browse 111 work opportunities in Aarau, the capital of Canton Aargau and a regional employment centre between Zurich and Basel. The local market combines public administration and professional services with healthcare, education, manufacturing, engineering, logistics, retail and hospitality. Vacancies range from entry-level and part-time roles to skilled technical, office and supervisory positions. Aarau’s central railway station and connections to surrounding municipalities make the area accessible to commuters, while many employers expect German, particularly for customer-facing and administrative work.
