Full description

This is what awaits you

Together with our collaboration and communication team, you actively shape the future of work at Mobiliar. As a Senior Engineer, together with Team Horizon, you will be responsible for the technical development, governance and secure operation of Microsoft 365 incl. Copilot within the Microsoft 365 platform.

As an experienced engineer, you take responsibility across team boundaries, drive innovation and ensure that modern AI technologies are used safely, sustainably and with added value for our employees.

This is what you do with us

You take responsibility for the development and safe operation of Microsoft 365 services. You will analyze new functions, in particular M365 Copilot, evaluate their benefits and risks and derive standards, governance requirements and technical guidelines from them. You work implementation-oriented, keep an eye on dependencies and ensure stability, quality and sustainable lifecycle management. You set priorities and provide a clear basis for decision-making.

Product owners, product managers, departments and executives advise on the implementation and use of Microsoft 365 solutions. In addition, you will recognize technical debts, risks and optimization potential, initiate appropriate measures and further develop operational processes, monitoring and automation. Together with security, architecture and platform teams, you will ensure secure, scalable and efficient operations and bring together different perspectives.

You actively contribute your expertise to the team and the organization. In exchange with internal stakeholders as well as external partners and manufacturers, you represent your specialist area and support you in architectural and technology decisions. You coach teammates and help the team learn from each other and develop viable solutions for the furniture together.

This is what you bring with you

  • University degree in computer science, business informatics or comparable education and training
  • Several years of experience as Engineer:in, DevSecOps Engineer:in or Solution Architect:in in the Microsoft cloud environment, especially with Microsoft 365, Azure and Copilot
  • Experience in operating mission-critical platforms and services
  • In-depth knowledge of Microsoft 365 services including co-pilot, large language models (LLMs), AI governance and prompt engineering
  • Experience with Microsoft tenant management, cloud platforms, automation and software as a service with a focus on security and compliance
  • Knowledge of Copilot Studio, Power Platform, Azure AI Services or related technologies is a plus
  • High initiative, networked thinking and a solution- and implementation-oriented working method
  • Delight in taking responsibility, driving change and empowering others
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English

Working with us

A motivated team is waiting for you to actively shape the future of the digital workplace. You will have the opportunity to play a decisive role in the use of artificial intelligence at Mobiliar, to establish new technologies and to create sustainable added value for our employees. We offer you modern forms of work, plenty of room for manoeuvre and an environment in which personal responsibility, cooperation and continuous learning are given priority.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
