You can look forward to this. You lead a team that ensures the availability of track construction machines and means of transport for maintenance and renewal, thereby strengthening punctuality and safety in the railway network. In the area of ​​infrastructure, you work closely with internal and external partners and actively shape processes and digital solutions. Work Smart allows you to have flexible working days and you can develop your leadership and specialist topics in a targeted manner.   • As head of planning and scheduling for track construction machines, you lead the team and ensure the economical, punctual and safe availability of machines and means of transport, thereby directly contributing to customer punctuality and customer satisfaction.
  • You will be responsible for the overall planning and programming of all machine resources and means of transport according to clear economic principles and in coordination with those who need them.
  • You assume technical responsibility for contracts, represent user interests in tenders and procurement and monitor the budget and service provision of external partners (SBB, BLS and TPF).
  • You will continuously develop processes and systems for planning and scheduling and drive digitalization forward together with the team.
  • You will work across departments with internal and external stakeholders and ensure transparent communication and clear priorities. This is what you bring with you.
  • Thanks to your persuasive and assertive skills, you communicate in a targeted and level-appropriate manner with the various internal and external contacts.
  • Ideally, you have experience in the railway environment and have several years of management experience in a comparable role.
  • You have basic technical or railway training as well as a technical degree at HF, FH or university level or equivalent further training.
  • We require good user knowledge of SAP systems and common Office applications. You are also IT savvy and willing to familiarize yourself with area-specific applications.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (at least C1) and can communicate in French (at least B1).
