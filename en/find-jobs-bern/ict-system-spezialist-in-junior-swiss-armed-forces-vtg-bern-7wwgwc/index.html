Put to the Point

Here you combine classical computer science with practical craftsmanship – and ensure that technology works reliably at several locations.

You can make this contribution

  • Participate in the commissioning, installation and maintenance of IT systems (servers, databases, sensor systems)
  • Carry out maintenance or installation work on antennas, wiring and signal processing hardware and cooperate with external bodies as required
  • Contribute to system availability and data integrity in the team to enable unrestricted further processing
  • Accompany the project management in procurement or development projects as a representative of the system operation and participate in the future of sensor systems
  • Perform measures for quality assurance, tracking of system documentation and overview and maintenance of inventory (hardware & software)

This makes you unique

  • Completed basic technical training with further training in telecommunications or computer science (systems technician/in) required
  • Basic computer science knowledge (e.g.: Linux, server, data transmission, programming) as well as experience in the field of radio communication and/or network technologies desirable
  • Openness to learn new things and develop subject-specifically if necessary
  • Independent, flexible working method and high quality awareness
  • Active knowledge of an official language and good knowledge of English

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
