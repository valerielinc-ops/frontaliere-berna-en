Full description

Tasks

For our 35 residents in the Belp family residence and for our 80 residents with moderate to severe dementia in the Oberried Dementia Competence Center, we are looking for reinforcement in the care teams.

We are looking for nursing professionals (tertiary and secondary level) in the Belp dormitory and the Oberried Dementia Competence Centre for the Night’s Watch who want to work in beautiful rural surroundings and accompany our residents.

Our focus is on cooperation and collegial cooperation. Therefore, we are curious to know you, your career, your skills and your goals. Send us your application or contact us by e-mail or telephone if you would like to find out more. Either way, we look forward to meeting you.

Requirements

  • tertiary education (FH, HF, DNII, AKP, PsyKP, KWS), or
  • Secondary education (FaGe, FaBe, DNI, FASRK, caregivers:in, home caregivers:in and others)
  • Joy of caring in a family atmosphere and fun together

We offer

  • Lively and exciting workday with a lot of variety (entrance and exits in the Belp dormitory and focus on dementia in the Oberried Dementia Competence Center)
  • 5 weeks holiday, from 45 years 6 weeks, from 55 years 7 weeks
  • Great opportunities to organize the breaks, e.g. on our beautiful restaurant terrace, in the rest room or in the garden
  • Development opportunities and self-responsible action in a motivated and supportive working environment

Domicil offers senior citizens stationary services in care and care. With more than 20 locations and around 1,600 employees, Domicil is the number 1 in long-term care in the canton of Bern. Under the umbrella of Concara, we are working together with Spitex Bern for integrated all-round care.

Domicil dormitory Belp is located in the most beautiful location above the roofs of Belp. In a family environment, we care for and look after holistically and individually. Domicil Competence Center Dementia Oberried is surrounded by a large park with different garden worlds that allow plenty of space for movement. Together we have to be careful.
