Full description

For the Raiffeisenbank Gürbe Genossenschaft, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Belp, Thierachern and Wattenwil, we are looking for a personality as a customer advisor:

Tasks

  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous support of our customers in the segment "Basic"
  • Ensuring simple, standardised advice on numbers, savings, investing and provisioning
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the customer’s living situation as well as within the segment-specific / customer-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular in contact campaigns
  • Opportunity to help train learners in basic counselling

Requirements

  • Enjoying daily customer contact and working in a team
  • Completed banking training or similar training
  • Good communication and social skills
