Full description

Portrait Lengnau School is a living place of learning and personal development. Our school covers from kindergarten to 9. Around 650 students, accompanied by a dedicated team of 85 teachers.

The continuous development of our school is particularly important to us – both for our students and in the college. The municipality of Lengnau supports us with the necessary resources to provide optimal learning conditions for young people in our modern learning and working spaces.

We are also happy to introduce our school in more detail under the following link: https://youtu.be/9EKn3A0rN2Y?si=NtkdkoyVqmRoZMrf

Tasks

From February 1, we are looking for one or two new curative teachers (IF teachers) who would like to strengthen our team in our 2nd classes.

In the function of an IF teacher, you accompany individual children or groups as well as our students in the special education sector and assume responsibility for their promotion and the corresponding administrative

  • Tasks and parental work.

In each case, we are looking for the best possible solutions, so that stable class teams (class, subject and IF teachers) are created, who want to advance a class together in the sense of a multi-professional team. If you want, you can also take a small part of the team teaching lessons and be even more in class. The two parallel classes also work closely together.

Requirements

Ideally, you have a diploma as a curriculum teacher: in, teacher or are in training for it (healing education or primary teacher). They work closely with a motivated and supportive college and actively contribute to the further development of the class teams and our school. They assume responsibility for the teaching in the sense of IF teaching on ideally at least three different half days.

We offer

An open-minded, creative and active team at an innovative school. Support by a helpful college and reliable school management. A modern infrastructure with contemporary tools for a versatile teaching. Complementary offers such as school social work and a day school that enrich the school day.

Tolerance and appreciation have a high priority at the Lengnau School. A dedicated and warm college and an open school culture look forward to welcoming you!

Application interviews are ongoing.
