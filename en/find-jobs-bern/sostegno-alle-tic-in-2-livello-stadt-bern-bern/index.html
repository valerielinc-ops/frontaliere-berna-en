Full description

To strengthen our Support Center Team (2nd level) we are looking for a dedicated and service-oriented personality by immediately or by agreement. If you enjoy customer support and want to work in a dynamic IT environment, we look forward to your application. The position is limited to 1 year, with the possibility of a permanent job.

Tasks

  • Co-responsible for operation, maintenance and further development of the Support Center 2nd Level
  • proactively optimize customer support and work processes
  • Regular field service assignments according to operational plan
  • Support 1st level support for complex malfunctions and support requests
  • Secure the interfaces and communication to other departments, collaborate in projects and participate in committees

Requirements

  • Completed training as an ICT specialist EFZ or comparable training
  • Perennial experience as an ICT supporter/in 2nd Level & Field Support
  • High level of commitment and flexibility as well as communicative, resilient, reliable person
  • Team of the operator with an independent, solution- and customer-oriented approach
  • Guide card category B
