Full description

In the area of Service Monitoring, you will engage in the following topics:

  • Central monitoring of national ICT and infrastructure services as well as all SRG SSR programs with various distribution channels in early, mid, and late shifts.
  • Overall coordination and assurance of the service support processes with the support units within SRG and external suppliers.
  • Incident, change, access, and event handling including their documentation in the service management system.
  • Initiation of initial continuity measures for broadcast and broadband services and escalations to internal SRG teams or our external partners.
  • Coordination and communication of troubleshooting measures in the event of "Major Incidents" with national impact as well as their follow-up.
  • Quality assurance in the incident process. Requirements
  • Completed apprenticeship in computer science, electronics, or electrical engineering.
  • Broad ICT and/or broadcast knowledge and interest desirable.
  • Strong communication and coordination skills in dealing with business partners, suppliers, and customers.
  • Language skills in German and English, as well as ideally in French or Italian.
  • Willingness to work shifts and weekends as well as on-call duty.
  • Practical and theoretical ITIL knowledge in the area of service support and service operation. What we offer
  • Versatile and interesting role in a dynamic environment of electronic media and public service.
  • Extremely exciting and collegial environment.
