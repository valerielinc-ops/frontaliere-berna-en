Full description

  • Planning, controlling and successfully managing the preliminary project of the Mobile Security Communication System (MSK) by 2029 as well as laying the foundations for subsequent implementation
  • Designing, coordinating and successfully implementing the proof of concept for future mobile security communication together with internal and external partners
  • Coordinate the cooperation between the Confederation, the cantons and other partners of the Swiss Security Association and combine different requirements into viable solutions
  • Develop portfolio visions, roadmaps and implementation strategies as well as coordinate requirements with specialist areas, product management and agile development teams
  • Actively manage project progress, risks, changes, costs and deadlines, support the financing, procurement and embassy processes for the later main project as well as prepare the decision-making basis for program management, steering committees and clients
  • Represent the interests of the stakeholder groups and actively shape the development of a resilient, future-oriented security communication for Switzerland
  • University or university degree in computer science, business informatics, engineering or a comparable discipline as well as in-depth training in project management
  • Several years of experience in managing complex digitalization or ICT projects, preferably in a security-critical or public environment
  • Strong knowledge of agile process models such as SAFe and DevSecOps as well as the project management method HERMES and the ability to combine the different methods.
  • Convincing personality with a high level of social competence, reliability, negotiating skills and confident appearance at all hierarchical levels
  • Ability to act even under time pressure and in a complex and volatile environment and to set priorities
  • Networked, analytical and solution-oriented thinking as well as enjoyment of mastering complex challenges together with interdisciplinary teams
  • Good knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, passive knowledge of a third official language, or good knowledge in English
