Full description

This is what awaits you

Would you like to further develop your specialist knowledge in the claims area and gain a deeper insight into how to deal with complex building damage cases? In the specialist network for damage (FNS), you have the opportunity to gradually expand your skills in the field of building physics and building technology, deepen your knowledge in practice and prepare yourself for a future specialist role as a building specialist in property damage.

During your development in the FNS, you will be part of the East Property Damage team and will work as a damage inspector and as a contact person for structural issues. You will have the opportunity to further develop your construction know-how, analyze complex building damage and gain practical experience during on-site visits. Together with experienced colleagues, you will support the general agencies and management with complex building damage and continually expand your professional expertise.

This is what you do for us

The focus of your work is the processing of complex building damage cases for residential and commercial buildings. Step by step, you will take on more responsibility during on-site visits, assessing the causes of damage and dealing with complex issues relating to building construction, building physics and building technology.

As you gain experience, you will develop well-founded solution suggestions for repairs and damage repairs and support you in estimating costs and effort.

You will also advise colleagues on construction-related issues and contribute your knowledge to internal training and further education. In doing so, you will build a valuable network of craftsmen, planners and other specialists that will support you in your everyday work.

You can contribute your practical know-how and experience to projects, plans and the further development of claims processes. At the same time, you will have the opportunity to continually expand your specialist knowledge and develop both professionally and personally.

This is what you bring with you

  • Experience in insurance or an interest in acquiring relevant knowledge
  • A higher education in the field of building physics (HF/FH) or a comparable qualification with practical experience in the construction or building sector
  • Knowledge of building construction, building physics and renovation. Experience with relevant SIA standards is an advantage
  • The ability or motivation to analyze the causes of complex damage, to assess them in depth and to develop appropriate solutions
  • Enjoy communication and interest in passing on knowledge and conducting training courses
  • Resilience and willingness to carry out on-site visits and handle complex claims
  • An independent, structured, solution- and customer-oriented way of working
  • Initiative, commitment and negotiation skills as well as a confident demeanor
  • Very good knowledge of German. Additional language skills are welcome

Don't you meet all the criteria yet? Feel free to contact us anyway!

Your development path in the FNS

This position is part of our specialist damage network, or FNS for short. With this development program, we specifically build specialists for important and specialized tasks in the claims area. In this way, we secure valuable knowledge, create career prospects and prepare talented people for future specialist functions.

You are fully integrated into the East Property Damage team from day one and actively participate in day-to-day business. Experienced specialists will accompany you in expanding your knowledge in a practical way and gradually taking on more responsibility. Your development is based on an individual development plan with clear goals. This will be determined together with you, your manager in East Property Damage and the management of the FNS.

The goal is for you to qualify for the intended specialist role within around one to two years and then work permanently in this role in the claims area. Your employment is permanent from the start.
