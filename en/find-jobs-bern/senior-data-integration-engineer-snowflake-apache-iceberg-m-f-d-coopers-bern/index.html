Full description

For our client in Bern (Switzerland) , we are looking for a Senior Data Engineer – Snowflake & Apache Iceberg (Fully Remote within Europe possible) .

The objective of the project is to build a complete, reliable and production-ready CDC solution to synchronise data from TigerData to Snowflake based on Apache Iceberg.

The solution should achieve a level of reliability, transparency and operational stability comparable to the existing CDC processes based on Fivetran HVR.

Your tasks

  • Analyse and evaluate the CDC approach provided by TigerData based on Apache Iceberg
  • Define and design a suitable target architecture for continuous data synchronisation from TigerData to Snowflake
  • Configure and optimise Snowflake for processing data provided through Apache Iceberg
  • Design and implement the required Apache Iceberg components and data structures
  • Ensure reliable processing of inserts, updates and deletes
  • Address schema evolution, data consistency, data latency, recovery and reconciliation
  • Establish monitoring, logging and error-handling mechanisms to ensure stable production operations
  • Coordinate technical activities with TigerData and, where required, directly with Snowflake
  • Support the implementation, validation and production rollout of the end-to-end solution
  • Document the technical architecture and facilitate knowledge transfer to the internal team

Your profile

  • Several years of hands-on experience in designing and implementing data integration and replication solutions
  • Strong knowledge of Snowflake, supported by practical project experience
  • Proven hands-on project experience with Apache Iceberg
  • Solid understanding of Change Data Capture (CDC), data replication, and streaming or synchronisation methods
  • Experience in building and operating production-ready CDC and data integration platforms
  • Knowledge of schema evolution, data consistency, recovery, reconciliation, monitoring and error handling
  • Experience integrating different data platforms and building robust end-to-end data pipelines
  • Ability to analyse and drive technical solutions in collaboration with software vendors and internal stakeholders
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working approach
  • Experience with Fivetran HVR or comparable CDC and data replication solutions is an advantage
  • Experience integrating time-series databases or related data platforms is an advantage
  • Fluent in Englisch or German
