Introduction For our Straumann Villeret SA site (Villeret, Corgémont and Sonceboz), we are looking for an Infrastructure Service Collaborator. In this key role, you actively contribute to the continuity of site activities by guaranteeing the security, availability and performance of infrastructure (buildings, technical installations and energy). You operate in a demanding industrial environment where the reliability of installations is essential to the smooth running of production. You will work within a committed team and work closely with maintenance, external service providers and the site's various departments. Your role As an Infrastructure Service Collaborator, you: Security & compliance

  • Actively contribute to maintaining a safe working environment for all employees and external partners.
  • Ensure the proper functioning of control and safety devices.
  • Integrate risk management into all your interventions (maintenance, projects, continuous improvements).
  • Ensure compliance with EHS (Environment, Health, Safety) rules. Maintenance & operation of infrastructure
  • Supervise and maintain the technical installations of the building (heating, ventilation, water treatment, pure water/DI, etc.).
  • React to alarms and carry out troubleshooting interventions to ensure equipment availability.
  • Participate in preventive, corrective and improvement maintenance.
  • Analyze technical malfunctions and propose structured improvements.
  • Coordinate and monitor the interventions of external companies. Operational & logistics support
  • Manage stocks of consumables and spare parts in collaboration with the relevant departments.
  • Supply the workshops with technical consumables.
  • Carry out handling, transport and preparation of premises as needed.
  • Create the necessary maintenance orders and purchase requests. Organization & collaboration
  • Share useful information with the team and ensure regular reporting.
  • Work closely with Maintenance and other departments to coordinate interventions.
  • Contribute to a culture of collaboration, continuous improvement and shared responsibility. This position involves flexible hours, 2x8 work as well as on-call interventions (nights/weekends) depending on operational needs.

  • Professional training (CFC or equivalent qualification) in a technical field, ideally linked to building infrastructures.
  • Experience in technical maintenance or operation of industrial installations.
  • Driving license (light vehicle).

  • Availability for flexible hours (2x8) and on-call service.
  • Residence allowing rapid intervention on site (response time compatible with operational requirements). Assets
  • Knowledge of heating, ventilation, water treatment or industrial installations.

  • Knowledge of English or German.
