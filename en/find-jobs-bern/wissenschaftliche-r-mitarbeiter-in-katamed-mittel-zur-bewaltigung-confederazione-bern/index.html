Full description

Implementation and management of scientific projects in the area of means of coping. -In the case of events, the partners of the civil protection organization KATAMED advise and support in the crisis management in the area of KATAMED.

  • Representing the business and department in cantonal, national and international committees.
  • Perform coordination tasks with the competent professional and political authorities and associations, authorities and organizations for rescue and security (Bors), health care and army and especially with the corresponding offices of the cantons.
  • Completed studies at the level of master or equivalent training with several years of function of the relevant experience in a comparable function.
  • Very good knowledge of the Swiss health care system with its federalist challenges as well as good networking nationally and internationally.
  • Experience in the field of disaster medicine and in this field of expertise.
  • Several years of experience in the field of means of response (staff, logistics, strategic patient distribution or medical infrastructure).
  • Experience in the development of structures for disaster management, as well as preclinical or clinical work experience in the health sector, at best already with regard to disaster medicine or disaster health.
  • Active knowledge of two official languages, passive knowledge of a third official language and English.
