Full description

Portrait In the agglomeration municipality of Münchenbuchsee with just over 10,000 inhabitants - 10 km from Bern - around 1,150 children and young people are taught in 11 kindergarten, 33 primary school, 14 upper school classes, two classes with special needs (KbF) and a 6-class comprehensive school (Diemerswil) at six different locations. In the atmospheric and listed Paul Klee school building, 8 grade classes from 1st to 4th grade are taught in double rows. The school's educational offer is supplemented by a growing day school. Münchenbuchsee is an important residential and business location with very good transport connections, various well-known educational institutions (Pedagogical Centre for Hearing and Language Münchenbuchsee (HSM), special school home Mätteli, Gymnasium Hofwil), diverse shopping facilities and cultural offerings (Restaurant Bären with 80 concerts per year).

Responsibilities

  • As a subject teacher, you teach 14 lessons Mon (4 L.), Tue Mon/Na (5 L.), Wed Mon (5 L.) in various subjects (M, D, NMG, BG)
  • You will be the job partner of a long-time class teacher.
  • This is a temporary employment (01.02.2027-31.07.2027)

Requirements

  • The interplay of teaching and learning between teacher and student is a matter of course for you.
  • Your enjoyment of the profession is reflected in your committed, empathetic and creative way of working.
  • Cooperative and professional cooperation between you, your team colleagues, students and parents is a central concern for you.
  • You bring a pinch of humor and a positive attitude to life into the classroom and the teacher's room.
  • You have a diploma as a teacher, are in training and/or have several years of professional experience in your working environment.

We offer

  • Fixed-term employment in accordance with cantonal guidelines
  • Inspiring schoolhouse atmosphere in the birthplace of Paul Klee with contemporary infrastructure
  • mixed-generational, committed, open and solidary staff
  • Opportunity to actively shape content within the level/school building and to contribute ideas - school-supplementary offers with expanded school social work and professional day school
  • Excellent public transport connection to Bern and Biel (7 min. walk from/to the train station)
