Full description

Tasks

  • Team management: You lead your 5-member payroll team professionally, personnel and promote it specifically.
  • Wages processing: You and your team ensure the correct and punctual processing of wages for 2,200 people (including annual accounts).
  • Operational activities: You are actively involved in day-to-day business at Payroll and Zeitwirtschaft (system RTM / Perigon).
  • Process expansion: You help with the further development of our HR service center and optimize the processes.
  • Interfaces: You connect the locations and make cooperation with the decentralized HR administration even better.

Requirements

  • Education: Completed basic education plus further education in the field of payroll or social insurance.
  • Leadership: You have already gained experience in leading teams.
  • Numbers & IT: You love numbers, have strong analytical skills and are familiar with HR tools, especially Abacus.
  • Hands-on mentality: You enjoy the operative work in everyday life and want to make things happen.
  • Language: Very good knowledge of German.

We offer

  • A future-proof healthcare environment.
  • A motivated team and an experienced predecessor as direct support.
  • Space for your own ideas in a newly built role.

The Concara Group unites the forces of Domicil Bern AG and Spitex Bern in a common vision: As soon as people are dependent on support, they should receive it from a single source. With around 2,200 employees, the Concara Group is a leading healthcare company in the Canton of Bern.

At Concara Services AG, we provide various services for all operating units of the Concara Group, implement support processes and provide professional support.
