Full description

With us you experience the magical Christmas season up close! For this year’s Christmas tree sale at Shoppyland Schönbühl, we are looking for dedicated employees who want to become part of this festive atmosphere. Do you feel called? We look forward to your application.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, MMM Shoppyland, Industriestrasse 10, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

The sale of the Christmas trees takes place outside the shopping center We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You will advise our customers on the selection of suitable Christmas trees and support them with questions
  • You work actively in external sales and support the team in day-to-day business
  • You provide the Christmas trees and pack them for safe transport
  • You ensure cleanliness and a well-kept overall impression of the sales place

Requirements

  • Experience in sales: an advantage
  • You enjoy selling, respond to the needs of the customer and convince with a customer-oriented personality
  • Good physical condition for working in outdoor sales as well as enjoyment of an outdoor task
  • You show flexibility regarding working hours
  • No education/training required
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Fair wages: functional and market-oriented remuneration
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility

Contatto

Application and contact Melanie Knuchel Recruitment partners +41 58 565 85 85

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %
