Klipp & Klar School is looking for social educators, school special educators and teachers with experience in the field of autism

Description

Klipp & Klar Schule GmbH provides individual schooling for normally and highly gifted neurodivergent children and young people at two locations. We offer transitional schooling at a third location. As a special elementary school bVSA approved by the Canton of Bern, we offer individualized instruction in individual and/or small group settings (max. 5) from grades 1 to 10.

At the heart of individualized schooling is the question of how the existing framework conditions can be brought together with the individual resources of the affected child or young person in order to open up the best possible development opportunities and promote reintegration.

We trust in the development of potential

Requirements

  • Ideally experience in the school system with children and young people with special educational needs
  • Experience in designing individual and holistic educational offers for children and young people
  • Openness to working in an innovative team and contributing (social) educational knowledge
  • Motivation to seek creative solutions
  • Interest in supporting children and young people in challenging and stressful situations and crises
  • Willingness to work in a team and at the same time ability to act independently

We offer

  • A setting that creates framework conditions for needs-based, individualized learning with a focus on resources and intrinsic motivation
  • Very motivated and competent school team
  • Collaboration with a qualified, interdisciplinary network
  • Specialist advice in the area of autism and trauma
  • Regular supervision and case intervention
  • Internal training (trauma, ASS, de-escalation, etc.)
  • Good infrastructure
  • Workplace in a central location in the city of Biel
  • Salary according to cantonal salary classification
  • Advanced working conditions
  • Regulated working hours
