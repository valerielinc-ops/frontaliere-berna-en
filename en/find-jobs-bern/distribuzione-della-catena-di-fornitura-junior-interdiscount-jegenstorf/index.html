  • You are responsible for the E2E optimization and the management of partner performance in drop shipping (fulfilment of the customer promise price, quantity & deadline, application, implementation & optimization of the SLAs) as well as a standardized and highly automated design of the order-to-cash process.

  • You act actively and offensively in various projects in your area of responsibility and are ready to process short-term requests quickly.
