Full description

Portrait For our school with good transport connections to Bern and yet in a rural location, we are looking for a school curative teacher for cycles 1+2. Our MR team is looking forward to a committed and interested new member. The position may also be divided between two persons. The current workload is limited until the end of the school year. The commute talks for the next school year will take place in March.

Tasks

clarifications / counselling of families / teaching of children of primary school age. Close cooperation with a class teacher. Exchange with the entire MR team on site and professional exchange with the colleagues:in our association communities (Vechigen - Bolligen).

Requirements

Master of Arts in curative education (or equivalent diploma); started training for / SHP. Commitment and sensitivity in the field of curative education. They accompany and support regular students: inside and bVSA int. Children in cycle 1+2 as a specialist in MR.

  • In cooperation with the regular teachers, they promote the students: inside the class according to their individual needs.
  • They regularly exchange views with the class team, specialist positions and school social work.

We offer

Pensum of 24L on three classes (cycles 1+2). The 24 lessons are distributed throughout the school week until Friday noon (Wednesday afternoon free). The timetable for the current school year is set, as far as possible adjustment wishes are implemented. morning: Mo-Fr 8:10 - 11:55 / afternoon: Mo+Do 13:45-15:20 / Di 13:45 - 16:20 In parallel HPS studies, the best possible division among the study-free days is discussed and opportunities are sought together. The existing, motivated MR team is looking forward to an established, communicative and active new member. MR - Office with workplaces in the main school building Overall manageable school with primary, real and secondary classes (around 330 SuS, 45LP). Good accessibility of the place of work with public transport (Libero basic subscription), if necessary free parking at the school house. School homepage: https://www.stettlen.ch/bildungfs
