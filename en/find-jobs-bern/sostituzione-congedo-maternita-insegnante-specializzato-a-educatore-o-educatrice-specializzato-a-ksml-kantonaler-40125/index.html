Full description

The Salome Brunner Foundation is committed to over 300 children and adolescents with severe language disorders. With our various schools, we are an important part of the educational landscape of the canton of Bern. The Bienne Language Classes consist of ten classes, four of which are French-speaking from 1H to 8H. The students who attend them have severe language disorders and are accompanied and supported in their school journey by qualified teachers and therapists. Small groups allow working with each student according to their needs. Speech therapy and psychomotricity are part of the therapeutic offer of our school.
