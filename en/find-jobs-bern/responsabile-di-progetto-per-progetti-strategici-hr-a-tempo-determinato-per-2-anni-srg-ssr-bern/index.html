Full description

The national competence center HR SRG manages the strategic human resources business of SRG and coordinates the HR departments of the company units. The Competence Center sets the framework conditions and ensures equal treatment throughout the company.

This is your contribution.

As a public media company, we are undergoing profound change. Our corporate culture is a key lever for implementing our strategy. You are directly subordinate to the Head of Human Resources and Cultural Development and are responsible for the overall coordination and management of a portfolio of strategic initiatives and projects within the framework of cultural development. This cross-cutting function without leadership responsibility builds on your ability to influence, mobilize people and coordinate activities across language, regional and professional boundaries.

  • Managing a strategic project portfolio: planning, coordinating and managing several cultural transformation projects independently
  • Ensure cooperation between departments, regions and language regions as well as promote the implementation of agreed measures and milestones
  • Diagnosis and data-based control: conduct stakeholder and impact analyses, evaluate cultural diagnoses and employee surveys to derive recommendations and measure progress

They inspire us.

  • Master's degree in social sciences, organizational psychology, business administration or a similar field
  • Additional training in change management or organizational development is an advantage
  • Several years of experience in change management and cultural transformation, ideally both in consulting and in a company, including comprehensive reorganizations
  • Proven experience in the management and control of cross-sectoral cultural initiatives and projects as well as in cooperation with leaders
  • Safe use of diagnostic methods (effect analyses, surveys, cultural diagnoses) and a data-based approach
  • Demonstrated ability to bring people together and advance projects without hierarchical authority: diplomacy, political flair and persistence
  • Excellent knowledge of German and French in words and writing
  • Knowledge of Italian is an advantage
  • Sensitivity to the challenges of a multilingual environment and a public institution

So we inspire you.

  • A strategic role at the heart of a transformation that affects the entire company
  • Great autonomy and direct access to the relevant stakeholders
  • Progressive employment conditions and training opportunities
  • A multilingual environment and a meaningful task for Swiss society

Application deadline: 25.10.2026

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
