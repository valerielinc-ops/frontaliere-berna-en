Full description

Technically responsible for a BESS project, from detailed planning to construction and commissioning to handover to operation: In this role, you control technical decisions, ensure quality and grid compliance, and bring your expertise where it directly affects the course of the project.

Your field of activity

  • You are responsible for the technical compliance of the BESS delivery scope for European projects from detailed planning to construction and commissioning up to handover to operation
  • As a technical interface, you control the engineering topics and lead the assigned internal and external specialists functionally.
  • Planning and design documents are checked and approved based on the recommendations of the owner's engineer and decide on technical deviations within the approved parameters
  • On the client side, you ensure QA/QC and are responsible for ITP, FAT, SAT, technical acceptance as well as NCR and defect management
  • For grid compliance and certification, you coordinate protection concept, models, PPC/EMS parameters and the required conformity documentation.
  • You control the commissioning until the technical handover and evaluate changes, claims and deviations from a technical point of view

You bring that with you

  • Your basis is a higher technical training in energy or electrical engineering at the level of HF/FH/ETH or a comparable training
  • Several years of experience you bring from planning, construction, commissioning and handover of large energy or infrastructure projects
  • In electrical/energy engineering and BESS systems you have in-depth knowledge, especially in medium/high voltage, mains connection and SCADA/EMS/OT
  • You are familiar with QA/QC, ITP, FAT, SAT, NCR and Punch Lists as well as technical change/claim management.
  • You know grid compliance, plant certification and grid connection processes as well as cooperation with external engineering and monitoring partners.
  • Confident German and good knowledge of English complement your ability to identify technical risks early and make informed decisions
  • Willingness to travel to a closer European country
