Introduction For our Procurement, Support & Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a Control Methods (H/F) Officer whose primary mission will be to ensure the reliability of the measurements and ensure that the control methods are rigorously applied throughout the parts control process. Responsibilities

  • Process requests for specific measures

  • Deal with measures for correlations with suppliers
  • Create and modify control DXFs
  • Create dimensional QICs
  • Create and modify other documents related to the use of control means / control procedures
  • Ensure that control methods are applied in accordance with protocols established on the ground
  • Support respondents in programming standard equipment
  • Address challenges related to equipment
  • Experience in the field of quality and measurement
  • Knowledge of the different measurement methods
  • Customer and supplier guidance, good team spirit
  • Strong autonomy and initiative
  • Very good spirit of analysis, synthesis and good writing skills Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, d, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social services.
