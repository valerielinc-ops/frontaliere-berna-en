Full description

Portrait Healing School of the Thun Region In our curative school we accompany around 115 children and adolescents aged 4-18 years on their individual development path. Together with a dedicated team of 125 employees, we create a place where learning, promotion and personal development are the focus.

To strengthen our team, from 01.02.2027 or already from 12.10.2026 we are looking for a committed personality for 12-16-year-old adolescents in upper school (Monday / Tuesday).

Tasks

  • Teaching a high school class with 6-7 students with highly individualized needs
  • Responsibility for individual funding goals
  • Support by a trained/experienced teaching assistant and an intern
  • Participation in school development and active participation in the school community

Requirements

  • Completed studies in curative curative education or the willingness to achieve this
  • Intrinsic motivation for the promotion of pupils with a healing need
  • Enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team
  • Reliable, flexible and resilient to new situations
  • A large portion of serenity, humor and endurance

Please do not apply if you have not completed at least one educational Bachelor. Thank you.

We offer

  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Modern infrastructure
  • Support from a dedicated and well-established school team
  • Attendance of individual and collective training within and outside HPS
  • Meaningful work in the environment of education and promotion
  • A culture of appreciative cooperation, mutual trust and a very positive team climate. This culture is just as important to us as the professional quality of our work.
