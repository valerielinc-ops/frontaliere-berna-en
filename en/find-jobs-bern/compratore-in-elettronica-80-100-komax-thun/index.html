Full description

Schleuniger in Thun is a leading technology company and part of the Komax Group since 2022. For decades, the company and its approximately 200 employees have been setting standards in semi-automatic cable processing – with innovative solutions and forward-looking technology.

With its products Schleuniger serves a large number of customers from the automotive industry, transport, medical technology, aerospace, information and communication technology as well as industrial and consumer electronics. More information on komaxgroup.com/Accelerator.

Their tasks

  • Operational (about 90%) and strategic procurement of electrical or electromechanical components
  • Distribution of shopping parts and appointment monitoring
  • Ensuring material availability and implementing quality assurance measures
  • Evaluating and developing new sources of procurement at home and abroad
  • Pricing and contract negotiations with suppliers
  • Conducting supplier audits and implementing appropriate measures
  • Maintenance of material master data

Your profile

  • Technical apprenticeship in electronics or electrics
  • Completed commercial school, further education as technical merchant, technical clerk or comparable training
  • Several years of purchasing experience, ideally in a technical or industrial environment
  • Business and accounting understanding
  • Negotiating, communicative and responsible personality
  • Good English skills and safe handling of MS Office
  • SAP experience is beneficial

Our offer

  • Dynamic and open team
