Full description

Does your heart beat for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully contribute your skills and experience to us in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are a perfect fit for us!

We are looking for someone for the Tertianum “Résidence” in Bern on August 1st, 2026 or by appointment

Health specialist EFZ 60-80%

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and support for guests in accordance with our nursing principles and the nursing process
  • Taking on tasks as a caregiver
  • Participation in the care process and in the care needs assessment with RAI LTCF for the assigned guests
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Planning and accompanying doctor's visits
  • Assuming day-to-day responsibility
  • Taking on tasks as a subject person on a specialist topic and thus participating in the group-wide network on care quality

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health specialist EFZ
  • Professional experience in long-term care an advantage
  • RAI knowledge desired or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy a variety of challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German
