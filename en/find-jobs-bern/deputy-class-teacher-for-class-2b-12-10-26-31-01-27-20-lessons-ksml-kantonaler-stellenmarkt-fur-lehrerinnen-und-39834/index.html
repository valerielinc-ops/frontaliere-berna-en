Tasks

Class teacher 4.-6. Category The three intermediate classes work closely together. In some subjects, the classes are taught in the year class, which is why the teachers work in all three middle school classes. The preparatory work is jointly developed and divided in the step team. The cooperation is accordingly close, supportive and well-established. Ideas, attitudes and projects are defined and supported together. The administrative effort is manageable and meetings are organized efficiently.

Working days: Tuesday, Wednesday morning, Thursday morning (= Compact timetable) Subjects: NMG, Math, German, Visual Design, Media & Computer Science. (Subject agreement or change requests are partly possible).
