Full description

Real estate is your passion? With us, you take responsibility for a diverse portfolio, conduct contract negotiations with business and housing tenants and maintain direct exchange with various stakeholders. You also represent Migros Aare in tenant associations and floor owners’ meetings.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You independently supervise a real estate portfolio assigned to you and ensure professional management
  • You support the rental, conduct lease contract negotiations, draw up lease contracts and supplements and process sales rental interest reports
  • You manage leases of leased properties and coordinate the cooperation between landlords, partner tenants and owners
  • You prepare information and decision bases and thus support informed decisions
  • You create ancillary cost statements, ensure their quality and keep an eye on appointments.You represent Migros Aare as owner, landlady or tenant in tenant associations and floor owners meetings
  • You are responsible for the annual budget planning and carry out the monthly final controlling
  • You participate in conversion and renovation projects and accompany their implementation from the point of view of real estate management

Requirements

  • Professional experience: property management
  • Completed basic or further education: as a businesswoman / businessman with further education in real estate management
  • Thanks to your calm and thoughtful nature, you can also keep track of complex and hectic situations.
  • With your strong communication and negotiation skills, you convince different stakeholders and maintain a cooperative partnership.
  • You convince through your talent in building and maintaining sustainable relationships
  • Even with a high workload, you work in a structured, reliable and solution-oriented way.
  • Team spirit, commitment and cooperative cooperation distinguish you
  • You have a category B driving licence
  • in German (negotiation certain)
