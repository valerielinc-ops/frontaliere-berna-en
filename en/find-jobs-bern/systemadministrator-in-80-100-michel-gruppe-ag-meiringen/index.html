Full description

The Michel Group AG comprises the psychiatric institution Privatklinik Meiringen, the rehabilitation clinic Hasliberg, the Helsenberg Foundation with dormitories and protected workplaces as well as the Haslital laundry and the Haslital daycare centre as well as services in other areas. It employs a total of around 800 people at its locations in Willigen, Meiringen, Hasliberg, Ringgenberg, Interlaken, Thun and Bern.

Due to the ever-growing demands on a modern IT infrastructure, we are looking for a system administrator 80 – 100% to support the team.

Tasks

  • Co-responsibility in the administration and further development of the entire IT infrastructure of the Michel Group, its institutions and external customers.
  • They monitor and maintain clients, servers and networks as well as their interfaces.
  • They work on various computer science projects in close cooperation with the team leader and the specialist departments.
  • The smooth and highly available operation of the IT infrastructure is ensured together with the IT team.
  • They actively support the computer science helpdesk and receive requests, solve problems together in the team or forward them to the responsible office.
  • You take over the installation, monitoring, support and maintenance of clients, servers and peripheral devices.
  • You are jointly responsible for the support, administration and further development of the telephony solutions and their interfaces.

Expectations

  • Studies or training in computer science with corresponding professional experience (EFZ, TS, FH)
  • Relevant professional experience from Microsoft system and network engineering, virtualization solutions and SQL, with a generalist orientation coupled with computer science project experience
  • Team player, with a high customer and service orientation as well as a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Motivated and committed personality who also likes to contribute their own ideas
  • Good methodological knowledge in the areas of project work and 1st and 2nd level support round off your profile
  • High sensitivity for computer security
  • Basic knowledge of interfaces for medical data (HL7/FHIR) is an advantage
  • Open to new technologies and ready to question processes and optimize them if necessary

Offer

  • Versatile work in a dynamic and appreciative company
  • Attractive employment conditions as well as a wide range of further education and training opportunities
  • Sound introduction to the work area
  • Working in a small team
  • Own day care center in the Michel Group
