Full description

Portrait The Grauholz School is attended by around 1,000 students from the three municipalities of Urtenen-Schönbühl, Bäriswil and Mattstetten. Cycles 1 and 2 are taught at different locations in the three communities, cycle 3 with 13 classes in the central school complex Lee in Urtenen-Schönbühl. The partially rural agglomeration communities are located north of Bern. The school location is very easily accessible by public transport (13 min. from Bern).

Tasks

As a class teacher alongside a super cool specialist teacher, you will take over class 5b with 20 students at the Urtenen- Schönbühl location. The subjects include NMG, English, German, mathematics, media and computer science, BG, technical design and a rotation lesson (one more week, for 3 weeks spring break).
