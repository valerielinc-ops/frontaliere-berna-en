Full description

You are helping to shape the digital transformation at BKW Power Grid. In 18 months, you will get to know three different areas of the network business and develop your knowledge of data, processes and systems. Start: 1 March 2027.

Your tasks

In these three business areas, you work on current projects, apply your skills and develop them further. Asset Finance and Information

  • You will work on data models for our network infrastructure and learn to create, model and optimize them
  • You use your theoretical knowledge of Python to write code in our development environment

Network customer and grid connection management

  • You support the teams in digitizing processes and analyzing data relating to our network customers and connection applications
  • You create evaluations and statistics as a basis for business decisions

Asset Service Management

  • You work in process and IT management and develop digital solutions for planning, control and order processing
  • You analyze processes, take on requirements and accompany new digital solutions from conception to introduction

You bring that with you

  • You have studied business informatics, business administration, industrial engineering or a related discipline and are interested in data and business analytics as well as process and system development
  • You have initial practical knowledge of Python and/or SQL
  • You meet others openly and uncomplicatedly, communicate appreciatively and work constructively in a team
  • You think along, bring your own ideas and enjoy working interdisciplinary
  • You communicate negotiable in German
