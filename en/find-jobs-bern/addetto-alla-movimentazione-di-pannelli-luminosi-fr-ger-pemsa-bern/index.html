Your assignment

  • Install and operate lighting and signage devices, including fixing and electrical connection operations.
  • Operate at altitude in the yards using safety equipment (air platforms, scaffolding, platforms).
  • Organize the cable path, create simple electrical connections and ensure the reliability of the systems.
  • Check the correct operation of the equipment and participate in preventive maintenance activities.
  • Strictly respect the safety standards, technical standards and electrical regulations in force.
  • Ensure disassembly, diagnosis and adaptation to existing plant standards.
  • Work in collaboration with operational teams and specialists to ensure lasting and quality achievements.

And now we're talking about you?

You must possess

  • An excellent physical form and the habit of working outdoors and at altitude.
  • Driving license.
  • A good command of the German (minimum level B1) to ensure the communication in the yard.

You are

  • Motivated, dynamic and animated by the desire to learn.
  • Organized and able to work effectively on the team.
  • Respectful of safety standards and attentive to the quality of the work done.
  • Flexible and available according to the needs of the yards.
