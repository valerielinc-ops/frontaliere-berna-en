Full description

In your capacity as chief audit officer, under the supervision of a judge, you conduct performance audits in accordance with the methodology of the Court of Auditors based on the standards of the International Organisation of Supreme Institutions of Public Finance (INTOSAI). You digest all or part of public reports and participate in the development of recommendations. You support the judges of the Court of Auditors with proposals for audit, audit and audit strategies.

  • Within the framework of the guidelines given by the responsible magistrate, work on proposals for topics, audit proposals and audit strategies.
  • Carry out audit tasks falling within the scope of the Court's control.
  • Draft reports in accordance with the general methodology.
  • Participate in the publication of the final report.
  • Participate in specific activities related to the functioning of the Court. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Valérie Schwaar, President of the Court of Auditors at 021 316 58 14.
  • Master's degree in the humanities, degree of expert, or equivalent qualifications.
  • Advanced skills in finance and management, preferably public.
  • Experience of 4 to 6 years in conducting audits/analysis in the fields of public administration, business management, finance or management.
  • Experience and ability to write complex economic reports.
  • Knowledge of German and English.
  • Spirit of synthesis, faculty of analysis.
  • Ability to work independently and within a team.
  • Integrity, intellectual rigor, stress resistance.
  • Open mind and listening.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
