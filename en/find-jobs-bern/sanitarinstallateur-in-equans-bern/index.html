Full description

To strengthen our team for the Berne region, we are looking for a sanitary installer immediately or by agreement.

You can move that with us.

  • Assistance and independent processing of construction sites (e.g. in the areas of pharmaceuticals, industry, shop conversions, etc.)
  • Execution of sanitary installations (water / sewage / compressed air / process lines)
  • Assembly work (from raw assembly to finished assembly)

That's what makes you.

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in the field of sanitary and several years of professional experience in this field
  • Professional treatment of customers
  • Initiative, teamwork, accuracy and personal commitment
  • Good knowledge of German and possession of category B driving licence
  • Readiness for operations throughout the Canton of Bern

That's what we offer you.

  • 40 hours week at 100 %
  • Above-average social benefits
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits
  • Modern tools
  • Diverse internal and external training opportunities
