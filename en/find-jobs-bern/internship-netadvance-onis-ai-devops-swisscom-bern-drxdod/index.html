# Internship NETAdvance - ONIS AI DevOps

Il tuo futuro inizia

In this 12-month, practice-oriented student programme, you will develop and operate software for Swisscom’s optical transport and time synchronisation networks. Your home team ONIS (Optical Network, IT and Synchronization) combines network technologies, operates the associated IT systems and develops automation solutions for laboratory and production environments. In addition to working in the day-to-day business, you will participate in a practical Data & AI innovation project with experienced engineers. You develop your software and operational knowledge, build a network within NET and participate in cross-programme activities as well as an international visit. The programme starts on 1 February 2027 and consists of 60% teamwork and 40% innovation work.

  • Together with the ONIS Engineers and the users of our network tools, you will develop Python automation and API integrations.
  • You’ll use approved AI engineering tools like GitHub Copilot or Kiro throughout the development process, from design and implementation to testing and documentation to error analysis.
  • You will deliver minor, reviewed changes via GitLab CI/CD and GitOps using Swisscom’s security, identity and data management standards.
  • Together with a mentor, you will run a pilot project, complement meaningful monitoring and gain experience in incident diagnosis, rollback and recovery processes in a safe environment.
  • You will share evaluated AI practices with your team, develop reusable tests and ensure a sustainable transfer of knowledge.

Your skills

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree (Uni/FH) in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering or related disciplines (degree not more than one year ago)
  • Initial knowledge in the areas of AI, cloud technologies or data-oriented engineering as well as good programming and scripting basics, ideally in Python.
  • Basic understanding of network concepts, experience with Git, and knowledge of APIs and structured data formats such as REST and JSON.
  • Interest in AI-based software development. First experience with Copilot, Kiro or similar tools is an advantage. MCP, ArgoCD/GitOps and operational best practices you learn together with the team and are not required as expert knowledge.
  • Basic understanding of MCP servers and best practices in using AI tools for engineers and developers.
  • Enthusiasm for new technologies and agile working methods, independent and structured working methods, proactive team orientation, analytical thinking and interest in the business context.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, French is an advantage.

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
