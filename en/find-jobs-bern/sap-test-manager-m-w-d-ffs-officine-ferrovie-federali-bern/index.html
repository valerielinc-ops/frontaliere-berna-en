SAP Test Manager (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. SAP Test Manager (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In the Delivery Pipeline team in the SAP Integration Hub, you will design the integrated release control and organization-wide test management of SBB’s SAP platforms. In doing so, you introduce changes in a controlled, high-quality and transparent manner and make a significant contribution to the stability and further development of the system landscape. In the interface to specialist areas, development, release management and management, you actively develop the test processes and shape the future of SAP test management at SBB. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a test manager, you will continue to develop organization-wide test management including test automation and ensure the necessary governance.
  • You will actively shape the future test management landscape and lead the replacement of SAP Solution Manager 7.2 in cooperation with the product architect into viable target images.
  • Integration and acceptance tests are planned, coordinated and accompanied in complex change and release projects.
  • Faculties, projects and test managers advise you on methodological and procedural issues relating to test management.
  • In close cooperation with Release Management, Change Management and Transport Management, you ensure smooth coordination with all relevant stakeholders.
  • Standards, training materials, quality indicators and documentation are continuously developed.

You bring that with you.

  • Strong communication skills as well as high customer and service orientation.
  • Networked work with a willingness to take responsibility and promote collaboration across team boundaries.
  • Openness to change and motivation to actively shape new topics and technologies.
  • Several years of experience in test management with SAP Solution Manager and Focused Build as well as in the control of test activities in complex organizations.
  • Good German and English skills at least B2.
  • Knowledge of SAP Cloud ALM, Jira and Confluence or experience in an agile environment is an advantage.

Your reward and benefits.
