Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Would you like to be part of our technical team? Then a varied working environment awaits you, in which you are challenged daily and actively contribute to the smooth operation of our plants. Shape your future with us – and strengthen our team in Burgdorf from now on or by agreement as

Your tasks You keep our mechanical and building systems in operation and develop them further with us. To do this, you assess the state of the infrastructure, identify the need for action at an early stage and plan maintenance and repair work. You approach disorders systematically and repair them independently. When working on buildings, technical equipment and medical devices, you bring your expertise to bear. External suppliers and craftsmen accompany you on site, check their work and ensure a clean handover. You document your interventions and investment data in our software in such a way that your colleagues can continue working with it.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic technical training, for example as a specialist in operation maintenance, maintenance mechanic:in, polymechanic:in, plant and apparatus manufacturer:in, industrial mechanic:in or metal manufacturer:in. An equivalent education also fits
  • You are very familiar with the maintenance and maintenance of building technology systems. You can assess technical problems, justify decisions and reliably complete work. You think beyond your own task and coordinate well with the team and the external experts
  • For piquettes, you are flexible in terms of time and location. You have a category B driver’s license. Experience with medical technology is welcome – but not a must
