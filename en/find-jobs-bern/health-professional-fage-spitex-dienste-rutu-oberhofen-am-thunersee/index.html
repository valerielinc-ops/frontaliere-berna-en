Full description

As a public SPITEX organization of the municipalities of Hilterfingen/Hünibach, Oberhofen and Heiligenschwendi, we care for and accompany people at home – with heart and expertise. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement Health Professional (FaGe)

Your activities

  • You take over the basic and treatment care at our clients at home
  • In this case, keep You always keep an eye on their well-being, recognize changes early on, take the appropriate measures and act beyond standard care.
  • You accept medical prescriptions and implement them in cooperation with the care team
  • You work interdisciplinary and professionally implement the care process

Your profile

  • You have an education as a specialist health EFZ, FA SRK with treatment care course or an equivalent education
  • You bring professional experience – ideally in the SPITEX
  • You maintain a benevolent, respectful treatment of clients and third parties
  • You enjoy the care and contact with (aged) people and let others participate in it
  • You work independently, flexibly, reliably and responsibly
  • You have the driver's license Kat. B and are traveling with your own car
  • Ideally, you will live in the extended catchment area of our SPITEX organization

What we offer you

  • An exciting, meaningful task with a lot of independence and variety
  • A careful, individually adapted training and a modern office
  • A committed, open and appreciative company culture with a healthy dose of humor
  • Progressive employment conditions with at least five weeks of holidays, individually adapted service planning and further benefits.
