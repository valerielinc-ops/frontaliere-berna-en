Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital: small but fine – We offer demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and consideration of own resources. In our company, which is manageable in terms of size, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. In this sense, we regard individuality as enrichment. We actively promote cooperation among professional groups. The Clinic for Old-Age Psychiatry is a SIWF-recognized training institution for psychiatry and psychotherapy and has the full qualification for the focus on old-age psychiatry and psychotherapy (A2 inpatient and outpatient) and consiliary and liaison psychiatry (A2). We are a successful training facility of the BZ Pflege For our openly guided old psychiatric station in dual guidance with the senior doctor we are looking for 1. August 2026 or by agreement a committed, professionally experienced and managerial personality who takes over the nursing management. Their tasks In this responsible role, together with the interprofessional team, you will design a person-centered, appreciative and professional treatment of life-experienced people in psychiatric crisis situations.

  • Nursing, organizational and personnel management of the station in dual management with the senior physician
  • Ensuring high-quality, person-centered care (reference care concept)
  • Leadership, promotion and further development of the station team
  • Responsibility for the implementation of treatment concepts, care standards and quality requirements
  • Implementation and promotion of interprofessional cooperation
  • Initiation and support of development and change processes
  • Responsibility for resource and budget planning in the nursing sector
  • Promoting a structured, activating and secure station environment Your profile
  • Certified nurse HF/FH
  • Several years of professional experience in psychiatry, ideally in old-age psychiatry
  • Training in leadership/management (e.g. station management, leadership) of advantage or readiness
  • Experience in HR management, team management and conflict management
  • Organizational skills, decision-making skills and communication skills
  • Empathetic, reflected and appreciative attitude in dealing with life-experienced people and their relatives
  • Ability to create a motivating, supportive working environment for an interprofessional team
  • Openness to innovation and further development of nursing practice
