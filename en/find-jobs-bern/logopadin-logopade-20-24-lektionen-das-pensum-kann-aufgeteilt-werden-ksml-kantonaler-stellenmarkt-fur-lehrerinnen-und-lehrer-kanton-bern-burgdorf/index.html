Burgdorf speech therapy is a central service that provides speech therapy for elementary school pupils from kindergarten to secondary school level 1 from all over the city. The team already consists of six speech therapists who work closely together and share well-equipped therapy rooms including a kitchen and exercise room.

Tasks

  • Clarifications and therapy for communication, language and speech disorders in the oral and written areas
  • Advice for parents and teachers
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Participation in the college
  • Personal training

Requirements

Completed training as a speech therapist or in training

  • supporting MR school management
  • Opportunity for mentorship and/or internship support

  • very good equipment and lots of therapy material available
  • the workload can be designed individually
  • easily accessible by public transport
