Full description

Do you not only want to accompany vocational training, but actively participate? This is precisely why we are creating new opportunities: We want to make education in nursing more central in the future – and to further develop this path together with you. In this role, you can contribute your experience and ideas and play a decisive role in shaping the structures, processes and training quality of tomorrow.

Your tasks

  • Co-development and development of a centrally oriented education
  • Further development of the educational concept and training strategy
  • Planning and coordination of practical training of learners and students
  • Planning of training places and coordination of practical missions
  • Leadership, coaching and further development of the two main responsible vocational trainers: women and all learners in care (around 15 learners)
  • Organisation and management of vocational training groups
  • Ensuring and further developing a high quality of training
  • Cooperation and networking with internal and external educational partners
  • Accompanying learners and students in special training situations
  • Main responsibility in the selection, introduction and support of new learners and students
  • Evaluation of relevant training indicators and derivation of development measures

Your profile

  • Completed nursing training (HF, FH or DN2) or equivalent qualification
  • Further training in the field of vocational training (e.g.: trainer:in with federal certificate or MAS Adult Education)
  • At least 5 years of professional experience and high professional security in the care sector
  • Experience in vocational training and enjoyment of the development of learners and students
  • Strong conceptual, organisational and strategic skills
  • Proven leadership experience and pleasure in the professional support and development of educational persons

Our offer

  • Much room for manoeuvre in setting up and further developing central education
  • Possibility of actively shaping the future orientation of vocational training
  • A forward-looking and flexible service planning that supports your personal lifestyle – with annual working time models
  • Plannable work and leisure – overtime usually does not apply to us
  • Relaxing rest (fitness classes, free wellness offers in our partner companies and discounts on fitness subscriptions)
  • Contemporary employment conditions with progressive non-wage benefits

Interested? Then we look forward to your online application. Klinik Schönberg AG, Estelle Fuchs, Human Resources, Schönbergstrasse 40, 3654 Gunten
