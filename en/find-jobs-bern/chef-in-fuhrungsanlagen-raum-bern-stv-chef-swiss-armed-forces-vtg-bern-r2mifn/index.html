Your commitment to security and freedom The Swiss Army's logistics base (LBA) is there for the troops. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and, with its approximately 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia members, manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army. Whether in the workshop or in the office, indoors or outdoors, the LBA offers a wide range of jobs throughout Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs over 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – and this is done at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Young people are also provided for: The Swiss Army trains students in over 30 professions.
