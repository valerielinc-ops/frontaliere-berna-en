Full description

This is what awaits you: As a Deep Dive Trainee Data Science, you will immerse yourself in three different teams – each for six months in:

  • Analytics, Reporting & Project Management (Asset Management)
  • Document, work management and robotics (insurance)
  • Artificial Intelligence (Market Management) You gain practical experience in data science, analytics and AI projects, take on responsibility in agile teams at an early stage and build up a cross-departmental network with experts: women, data scientists, developers and product owners. This is what you do with us: In asset management, you revise existing applications and scripts, transfer them to a modern data environment (e.g. Databricks) and support the development of the data platform: You define requirements with the teams, contribute to the data strategy and identify possible AI solutions. In the area of document, work management and robotics, you will work in requirements engineering and technical specifications in agile teams, support the product owner, help with AI in data extraction from documents and e-mails, take on issues management tasks and optionally develop robotics automation or Java/Python applications. In the Artificial Intelligence team, you analyze data and technical requirements, evaluate machine learning models and approaches, check their quality, compare baselines and variants and participate in the development and implementation of productive AI solutions. This is what you bring with you:
  • Master's degree in STEM (e.g. computer science, mathematics, physics or other quantitative fields of study)
  • Programming skills in Python
  • Knowledge of statistics and machine learning
  • Committed, responsible and independent working methods
  • Communicative personality
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English Working with us: You work in various motivated teams with established colleagues on data-driven topics. In a positive, collegial work culture and with a broad internal training offer, you shape your entry into the world of data science and AI. The Deep Dive Trainee program runs until the end of February 2027 and covers a workload of 80-100%. Professional and dynamic teams are waiting for you in a motivating and innovative working atmosphere with the latest infrastructure. Program start is as of 1 September 2026 or by agreement. More information about the recruitment process can be found below. This is what we offer: Remuneration During the university program, you will receive an annual salary of CHF 79’300 with a 100% fee. Social benefits We contribute to your health insurance premium. You are also insured first-class worldwide and receive pay in case of illness or accident from the first day onwards. Advantages Among other things, we offer you a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
