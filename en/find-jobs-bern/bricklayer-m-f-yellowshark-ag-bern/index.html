01 Future employer

Perhaps we are looking for exactly you! For our clients in the canton of Bern (BE), we are looking for physically strong masons for temporary or permanent employment.

02 Your duties

  • As a mason, you work according to construction plans and are responsible for expertly building walls from various materials as well as working on unfinished and exposed masonry.
  • You will also carry out plastering work and apply cement screeds as part of your daily duties.
  • You always implement safety and environmental protection measures conscientiously.
  • The work takes place in the region in the areas of new construction and renovation.
  • Our clients offer you good prospects and attractive working conditions.

04 Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a mason in Switzerland or an EU country
  • Professional experience is an advantage
  • Knowledge of occupational safety
  • Expert knowledge of masonry
  • Good physical condition
  • Customer-oriented thinking and action
  • Good knowledge of German, Italian or Portuguese

05 Important information

Only electronic applications can be considered. Applications sent by post will not be returned.
