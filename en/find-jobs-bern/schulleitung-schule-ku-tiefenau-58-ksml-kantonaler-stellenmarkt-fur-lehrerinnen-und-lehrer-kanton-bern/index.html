Full description

Portrait Your work has an impact – for Bern and for the people who live and work here. Shape the community and the future of this city with us: visible, noticeable, sustainable.

At the Directorate for Education, Social Affairs and Sport, people are the focus. The school authority is the competence center of the public kindergarten and school system.

Tasks

  • Leadership and further development of a committed, culturally diverse team of teachers, class assistants and school secretariat
  • Holistic operational management of the school in organizational, personnel, administrative and pedagogical matters as well as strategic development in coordination with the school authority and the AKVB
  • Designing the work with parents and supporting the students in their successful integration into the Bern school system, in close cooperation with the teachers and the schools taking over
  • Active networking and coordination with the operation of the collective accommodation, municipal and cantonal authorities and other involved authorities
  • Co-design and further development of teaching and school quality together with the staff and the pedagogical management

Requirements

  • Completed school management training or willingness to complete it
  • Professional experience as a teacher or in the educational field as well as knowledge of the asylum and elementary school system in the canton of Bern
  • Strong flexibility, resilience and leadership skills as well as enjoyment of an extremely dynamic working environment
  • Structured and forward-looking way of working, combined with pragmatism, creativity and intuition
  • Communicative personality with empathy, humor and ability to work in a team
  • Enjoy working in a small, familiar team as well as direct contact with students and guardians from different backgrounds
  • Sound knowledge of English is an advantage

We offer

In the Tiefenau collective accommodation (KU) in Bern, the city's education authority runs a school on behalf of the canton with six "reception classes" (different cycles, multi-year classes) for refugee children and young people from kindergarten to 9th grade, who live in the KU and are being prepared for the transition to the regular school system. The classes consist of 8 to a maximum of 12 students, although there may be regular departures or new entries. It is a dynamic environment in which the children who have recently arrived - in their life situation, which is fraught with great uncertainty - are provided with structure and support through a good teaching atmosphere.

The current school management workload is limited until July 31, 2027 and is approved annually based on the number of students.
