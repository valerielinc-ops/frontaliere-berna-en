Full description

For Renewables and BESS

Do you not only want to analyze finance, but also actively shape it? In this role, you combine financial perspectives with business and the energy industry and support important decisions regarding new renewables and large-scale batteries.

Your field of activity

  • You are responsible for the financial management of the areas of new renewables and large batteries and create transparency about their development
  • Analyze developments, deviations and trends and derive concrete recommendations for management
  • As a Finance Business Partner, you are an important contact for the business unit management and support them in financial and strategic issues.
  • With investments, new business ideas and the further development of the portfolio, you bring in your financial perspective
  • Ensure a consistent financial view of asset development, investment, operation and marketing
  • Together with the responsible finance and controlling functions, you will further develop financial management and strengthen business orientation
  • You work on strategic projects and transformation initiatives at the interface of finance, business and energy industry

You bring that with you

  • Master’s degree in Business Economics or Business Administration with several years of experience in finance or in the energy industry
  • You combine your profound financial knowledge with a good understanding of energy economic relationships
  • Experience in financial management, management reporting and the creation and assessment of business cases
  • You are familiar with IFRS and common ERP and financial systems and have very good knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint
  • You can quickly grasp strategic questions and complex relationships, analyze them in a structured and understandable way
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as enjoyment of working with different stakeholders round off your profile
