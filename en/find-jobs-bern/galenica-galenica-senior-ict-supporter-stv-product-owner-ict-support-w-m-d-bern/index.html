Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

Do you want to combine your experience in ICT support with additional responsibility? In this senior function, you will further develop the quality of ICT support and support the team with your know-how. A central part of your role is the deputy of the product owner ICT Support, where you take responsibility and actively shape the further development of the services.

You can do that.

  • Analyse ICT incidents and service requests, coordinate suitable solutions and thus ensure a high quality of service for internal customers
  • In the event of disruptions, outages, releases and updates, you can help with escalations and ensure the flow of information between ICT support and other teams.
  • Create and develop assistance, knowledge articles, checklists and work instructions so that the team can work efficiently and according to uniform standards
  • With training and professional support, you strengthen the team in daily business and ensure that defined ICT service management processes are lived.
  • Maintaining the quality of service support, evaluating insights and working with the team to drive the continuous improvement of ICT services
  • You take over the deputy of the product owner ICT Support and ensure that its tasks are performed reliably even in absences

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a computer scientist or EFZ or many years of well-founded experience in the corresponding area of responsibility
  • Several years of experience in ICT support and ICT service management as well as very good knowledge of ICT infrastructure, client management or software applications
  • You have experience in a deputy, coordinating or comparable responsible role and are able to independently assume the tasks of the product owner ICT Support in his absence
  • You approach complex support cases in a structured and solution-oriented way and also keep track of escalations
  • You convince with customer and service orientation, team spirit and networked thinking and enjoy sharing your knowledge and openly shaping changes
  • You communicate in German and also have good knowledge of English or French.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
