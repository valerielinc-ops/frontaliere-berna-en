Full description

Portrait The Sigriswil Schools, in the most beautiful location above Lake Thun, is visited by around 300 students. For cycle 3 at Sigriswil we are looking for an MR teacher.

Tasks

Together with the class and specialist teachers, you plan and design the integrative teaching. They teach, accompany and support the pupils in the age- and level-mixed classes (IF lessons, eU lessons and accompaniment pupils in the special Voksschule offer bVSA int.). They work closely with the second MR-LP and the rule instructors.

Requirements

  • They are as trained as possible in school curative education or in training for it. An educational background is mandatory.
  • They work in the class team and in the MR specialist group (teaching development, projects, further education).
  • You like to think out of the box and get involved with your ideas.
  • They are flexible in attendance and can spread their lessons over several days of lessons at best. Thursday as a working day.

We offer

Rural environment and good infrastructure. Motivated and supportive college with present school management. Active school development and current development of our age-mixed and level-mixed teaching.
