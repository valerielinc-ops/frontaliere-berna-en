Full description

Your field of activity

  • You support corporate housekeeping and help ensure that corporate law processes within the BKW Group are implemented efficiently and legally.
  • Prepare shareholder and board decisions, support commercial register mutations and carefully maintain company law documentation
  • You support the corporate compliance team in the operational implementation of central compliance processes, in particular in training, trade bans, business partner audits and instruction management
  • The management, versioning and legally secure filing of legal documents you reliably ensure
  • You will be involved in the digitalization and standardization of corporate governance processes and support the further development of LegalTech and AI solutions
  • With reporting, working documents and service-oriented cooperation, you support internal stakeholder groups throughout the Group

You bring that with you

  • You bring professional experience as a paralegal or in a comparable function in the area of legal, compliance or corporate governance
  • You master German stylishly and use your language skills in everyday work, other languages such as English and French are an advantage
  • A structured, reliable and independent way of working distinguishes you as well as a careful handling of confidential information
  • Ideally, you have experience in investment management or have received further training in the area of Legal & Compliance
  • You are digitally affine, enjoy working with tools, data, workflows and reports and have experience with LegalTech and AI applications
  • You work solution-oriented, take responsibility for your tasks and bring new ideas pragmatically into the implementation

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
