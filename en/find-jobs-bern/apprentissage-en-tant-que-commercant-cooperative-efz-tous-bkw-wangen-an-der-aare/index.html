Cheeks at the Aare (BE)

You can expect a versatile commercial education in an exciting environment. During your apprenticeship, you will gain insight into various departments of Arnold AG and will be accompanied by experienced trainers. You can make contacts and get the tools for your future.

Your field of activity

As a merchant or merchant, you independently perform a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks. You work at the reception, in marketing, in the secretariat, in customer service, in accounting and in personnel administration. Together with your team, you take care of the concerns of our employees, applicants, customers and suppliers. So no day is like the other and you are always challenged. Arnold offers an apprenticeship with the two compulsory elective areas second foreign language (with or without vocational training) or individual project work. In the 3rd year you have the opportunity to deepen different competences (communication in German or French, finance, technologies).

You bring that with you

  • Good secondary education or the 10. School year completed
  • Interest in commercial expertise, administration and modern technologies
  • Sociability and language skills in German and French
  • networked thinking and a quick understanding
  • acting independently and willing to take responsibility
