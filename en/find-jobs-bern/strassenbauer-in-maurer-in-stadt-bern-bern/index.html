Do you want to contribute with passion to ensure that Bern – the beautiful capital with its unique cityscape and UNESCO World Heritage – remains cared for, safe and livable every day?
Then you are exactly right with us! In Infra Street you work where the city lives: on streets, squares, paths and facilities. In the sun, rain or snow, you and your team ensure that Bern works – visibly, reliably and with pride. To complement our team, we are looking for you immediately or by agreement as Tasks: • Carry out maintenance and construction work on the urban road network • Complete flooring and concrete work and work on paving, squares and natural paths • Carry out work outside the infrastructure (events, impact protection) and carry out maintenance work in the area of traffic management and special maintenance • Performing assignments in the winter service pikett Requirements • Completed vocational training in the building industry, ideally as a road builder or mason EFZ • Professional experience of several years • Category B driving licence • Independent working method, interested, proactive, reliable and capable of teamwork • Readiness to perform piquet and special operations We offer: For sense and city life With us you change something. You help shape city life, carry responsibility and make Bern more liveable. In the city of Bern, more than 4,500 people work together across five directorates and more than 80 job profiles. We are committed with passion and conviction to the people who live here, who work here. Together we shape the community and the future of this city – visible, tangible, sustainable. for our city. For Bern.
