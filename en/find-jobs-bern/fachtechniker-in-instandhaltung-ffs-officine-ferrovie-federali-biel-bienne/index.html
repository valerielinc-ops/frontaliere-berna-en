Diagnostician 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Diagnostician Biel/Bienne, 01.01.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can implement major changes and thus make a decisive contribution to the digitisation of SBBs. In collaboration with your team's colleagues, you develop and implement innovative solutions to meet existing challenges. We offer a wide range of continuing training to support your professional development. What you can move.

  • As a diagnostician, you make diagnoses of disturbances affecting passenger rolling stock during operation or during vehicle immobilization times.
  • Your skills allow you to organize the quick and lasting elimination of problems or to proceed with the removal of troubles.
  • Through your analysis, you help increase the availability of trains and reduce the time involved in disruptions.
  • You perform specific tasks related to the site.

What you bring.

  • Your autonomy and analytical capacity allow you to make decisions and find solutions. Your sense of communication and flexibility allow you to work well as a team.
  • You have completed technical training with CFC or equivalent (in the field of electricity and mechanics) and have several years of professional experience in the maintenance of rail vehicles.
  • You are willing to work at night and have a Class B driver's licence that allows you to do so.
  • You can communicate in French (level C2).
  • You want to invest in your professional development, successfully complete in-house training and refresher courses and obtain the corresponding certification in the relevant fields.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the F level and is eligible for a level 0 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the greatest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
