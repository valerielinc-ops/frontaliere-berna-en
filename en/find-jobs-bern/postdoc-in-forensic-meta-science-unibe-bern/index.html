Full description

UniBE is ambitious. With us, you deliver top performance and test your skills against the best.

Your role / profile

  • Conducting cognitive task analyses with members of the science 'sleuth' community.
  • Co-organising and running workshops with sleuths.
  • Analysing these qualitative data to develop a taxonomy of sleuthing practices.
  • Creating a roadmap for such checks that can be formalised into methods and tools.
  • Teaching opportunities are available in English and/or German, but there are no teaching requirements.

Your profile

Essential

  • A PhD in Psychology, Human-Computer Interaction, Epidemiology, Research Methods, or a related field.
  • Demonstrated interest in (forensic) meta-science, scientific error detection, trustworthiness assessment, or quality assessment within research synthesis.
  • Comfortable working closely with people, building rapport with expert practitioners and eliciting knowledge from them.
  • Strong written and spoken English.
  • Willingness to commute or relocate to Bern, Switzerland, and begin by between February 1st and April 1st. This is an in-person or hybrid commuting position; remote work is not available.

Desirable

  • Familiarity with cognitive task analysis, process tracing, think-aloud protocols, or similar methods for eliciting how experts work and why.
  • Experience designing, conducting, and analysing qualitative research (e.g., in-depth interviews, observation, thematic or framework analysis).
  • Familiarity or prior experience with science sleuthing (e.g., PubPeer, forensic meta-science checks).

With us, you create value. For yourself and for society.

Projekt Description

This postdoc position is funded by the UKRI under its Metascience funding scheme as part of the project "After the Flood: Near-Future Scenarios and Countermeasures of AI-Accelerated Research Fabrication" (PI: Prof Sebastian Deterding, Imperial College London). You will work under Dr. Ian Hussey, whose research group is embedded in the broader department of Psychology of Digitalisation chaired by Prof. Malte Elson. This project aims to design a roadmap for the current and future development methods and tools to help formalise 'science sleuthing' into structured Trustworthiness Assessments. Your role / profile

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
