Full description

Portrait In our special education school we accompany around 115 children and young people aged 4-18 on their individual development path. Together with a dedicated team of 125 employees, we create a place where learning, support and personal development are the focus.

At the HPS Region Thun, supported communication (UK) is particularly important as an attitude. All teachers and employees share this basic attitude, with the aim of enabling the students to be as independent and active as possible.

As a result of retirement, we are looking for a strong leader and committed personality to co-lead our school from February 1st, 2027.

Tasks

  • Shared responsibility for pedagogical, personnel, organizational and administrative leadership in partnership in the 3-person management team
  • Further development of school and pedagogical quality, as well as support of employees
  • Designing common pedagogical attitudes and concepts
  • Active participation in strategic development in close cooperation with the club board and management
  • Represent our school externally by maintaining constructive cooperation with parents, specialist departments, authorities and external partners

Requirements

  • Ideally pedagogical training in the field of special education, special education or social education with several years of teaching experience
  • Further training in the area of leadership and practical experience
  • Experience in co-management desired
  • Leadership style that lives and supports our culture
  • Strong communication and social skills
  • Enjoyment of educational issues and openness to change and development processes

We offer

  • A school with a well-established and stable organization
  • A responsible management position with a lot of shared creative freedom
  • Collaboration in an equal partnership co-management with mutual representation
  • High flexibility in the organization of working hours (annual working hours)
  • Meaningful work in the area of education and support
  • A culture of appreciative cooperation, mutual trust and an extremely positive team atmosphere. This culture is just as important to us as the professional quality of our work.
