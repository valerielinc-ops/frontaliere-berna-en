Full description

Your field of activity

  • You will lead your team professionally and in terms of personnel and assume overall responsibility for an international portfolio of wind and solar parks across the entire asset lifecycle.
  • As a member of the management team, you shape the strategic direction of the business unit and actively participate in investments, business development initiatives and M&A projects.
  • Responsibility for budget, forecast, performance indicators as well as technical and commercial reporting to Group management and investors is part of your versatile area of responsibility.
  • Continuously develop existing assets, identify potential for optimization and ensure the successful implementation of repowering, development and contracting projects
  • Demanding contract and claim negotiations as well as cooperation with authorities, municipalities, partners and internal specialist areas, you will lead sovereignly and on an equal footing

Your expertise

  • A completed master's degree in technology or economics forms the basis of your professional expertise, supplemented by several years of relevant professional experience.
  • In-depth experience in the asset management of wind turbines or comparable infrastructure facilities enables you to safely control complex technical and economic relationships
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, analytical strength and a consistent focus on results characterize your working method as well as your decision-making and assertiveness
  • Dealing with demanding stakeholders at management level is easy for you and you convince through a solution-oriented, cooperative cooperation across specialist and national borders
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as good user knowledge in Microsoft Office and SAP you bring
  • Knowledge of French and willingness to travel round off your profile
