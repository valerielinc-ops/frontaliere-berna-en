Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The Biel Hospital Center is expanding its ENT offering and thus creating new opportunities for modern and well-organized outpatient care. In the ENT-Ambulatorium you can expect a versatile and self-responsible role with plenty of room for your own ideas and design. You are the first contact person for the ENT, are responsible for central administrative processes and support the medical team in day-to-day business. At the same time, you analyze and optimize processes, accompany projects and actively participate in the further development of the ENT offer. You like to work independently, think analytically and enjoy optimizing processes and helping to shape something new? Then strengthen our team by 1. January 2027 as

Medical/practical assistant/in 40%

You do

  • You are the first contact person for the ENT – inside and outside the hospital
  • You support the ENT doctors and are responsible for the administration including secretarial work, reports, cost language and dunning procedures
  • You manage the material and ensure the maintenance of the office rooms
  • You analyze and optimize the ENT processes and bring your own suggestions for further development
  • You accompany ongoing projects, support project management and help with further ENT expansion
  • Once a month, you support the ENT consultation hours at the SZB location as well as your colleagues for holiday absences and work as a user representative for the new building in Bruges.

This sets you apart

  • Completed training as medical/practice assistant/EFZ
  • High degree of independence, initiative and strong organisational talent
  • Analytical thinking, pleasure in process optimization and number affinity
  • A sense of duty, reliability and team spirit
  • Good knowledge of German and French
  • Digital affinity and safe handling of digital systems

You will benefit from this

  • An exceptionally free role with a lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to actively shape a new offer
  • Plenty of space to independently design and develop administrative processes
  • Flexible work design with a workload of 40%; at least one day per week on site, one day home office possible by appointment
  • The opportunity to participate in the new building in Bruges as a user representative
