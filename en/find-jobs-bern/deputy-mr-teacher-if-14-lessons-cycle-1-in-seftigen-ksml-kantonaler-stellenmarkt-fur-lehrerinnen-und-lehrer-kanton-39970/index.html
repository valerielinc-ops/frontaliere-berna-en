Original description

Portrait The municipality of Seftigen is located in the upper Gürbetal. The village is located on the end moraine hill of the Belpberg, nestled between the mountain forest in the north and the Widerhub in the south. Seftigen ranges from the Wyssbach to the area of Allmend. The municipality has an area of about 4 km2 and lies at 578 meters above sea level. Neighboring communities are Uetendorf, Burgistein, Gurzelen and Noflen. Politically, Seftigen belongs to the administrative district of Thun. The municipality is located on the railway lines S4 and S44 with direct connection to Thun and Bern. The distance to Thun is about 10 km and to Bern about 25 km. The Thun-Nord motorway access can be reached in about 5 minutes by car. At the Seftigen School, around 180 students are taught in 10 classes. They are divided into 2 kindergarten, 6 primary and 2 mixed-year real classes. Tasks IF lessons in cycle 1 (1st to 3rd grade) Accompaniment of a child with bVSA int. The individual promotion of students takes place in team teaching, group setting or individually. Goals are defined in close cooperation with the class team and implemented together. The MR teacher prepares funding plans, carries out learning status recordings and is responsible for the documentation. The exchange with parents and external specialists takes place regularly. Requirements Education in curative curative education / as a teacher or experience in the field of IF Great interest and pleasure in inclusive promotion. Working with the class team. Cooperation with team, parents and specialist bodies. Participation in conferences and training.
