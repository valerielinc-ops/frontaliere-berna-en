Role overview

We are looking for a motivated Associate Scientist / Senior Laboratory Specialist who will join our Pilot Plant Bern team.

In this role, you take on a central, practice-oriented function in the preparation, implementation and follow-up of pilot plant activities.

The focus is on the independent operation, cleaning and support of process plants as well as the reliable documentation and support of development, transfer and lifecycle management activities.
