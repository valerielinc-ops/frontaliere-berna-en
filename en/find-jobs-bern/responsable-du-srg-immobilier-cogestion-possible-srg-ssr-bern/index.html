We act as ONE team for the success of SRG. Together with other areas, finance is responsible for strategic management, targeted allocation of funds and creates transparency for sustainable decisions.

This is your contribution.

For this challenging transformation task, we are looking for an entrepreneurially thinking and leadership-strong personality as a leader: in Immobilien SRG.

In this key role, you will assume overall strategic and operational responsibility for SRG’s Swiss real estate portfolio. They report directly to the Director of Finance and are a member of the management team of the Finance Directorate. Together with your management team, you will shape the future location of SRG and make a significant contribution to securing its public service mission in the long term. You understand real estate not only as an infrastructure, but also as a connecting element between people, working environments and corporate culture. They develop and implement the SRG location strategy with the aim of using space more efficiently, optimizing costs sustainably and aligning the production and media locations in a sustainable manner in the long term.

  • Further development and implementation of SRG’s real estate strategy
  • Management and development of a Swiss-wide real estate organization with more than 100 employees
  • Responsibility for a real estate portfolio with high strategic, technological and social significance
  • Implementation of the location strategy, continuous increase in operational efficiency and sustainable optimization of space and infrastructure costs
  • Further development of central SRG sites, in particular the Geneva/Ecublens and Comano production sites and the Leutschenbach SRF site
  • Responsibility for master plans, investment planning and long-term portfolio development
  • Control of complex construction, renovation and transformation programs
  • Representation of the real estate interests of SRG vis-à-vis the management, board of directors, authorities, partners and other stakeholders
