Full description

In the private clinic Meiringen people with all forms of psychological suffering find admission, treatment and care. The third-largest psychiatric clinic in the Canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient primary psychiatric care of the eastern Bernese upper country. Care and care take place according to current medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge. As a member of Swiss Leading Hospitals, Privatklinik Meiringen is one of the leading private clinics in Switzerland.

At PZJE, we treat young adults aged 16 to 25 with diseases from the entire spectrum of psychiatry with extensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment. The center comprises 30 inpatient treatment stations. The treatment is based on the latest evidence-based medical, therapeutic and nursing knowledge.

The center offers a 24-hour emergency admission requirement and is primarily run open and protected in an area if necessary.
