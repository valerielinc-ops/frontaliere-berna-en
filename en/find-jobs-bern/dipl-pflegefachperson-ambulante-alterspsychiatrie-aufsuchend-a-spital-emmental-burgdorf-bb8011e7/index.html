Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital: small but fine – we offer demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and consideration of our own resources. In our company, which is manageable in terms of size, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. In this sense, we regard individuality as enrichment. We actively promote cooperation among professional groups. We are a successful training facility of the BZ Pflege.

For the visiting care of life-experienced people with psychiatric diseases in the outpatient setting, we are looking for an immediate or by agreement to strengthen our team.

Tasks

  • Resource- and person-centered care and support of life-experienced people with mental illness
  • Stabilisation and maintenance of independence and quality of life in domestic setting
  • Crisis intervention and support in challenging situations
  • Independent case management and treatment planning
  • Collaboration with the interprofessional team of the Clinic for Old Age Psychiatry
  • Advice and networking with relatives, authorities and referrals

Profile

  • Completion as a dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • Experience in age psychiatry/Spitex is beneficial
  • Joy and interest in dealing with older people and their individual life situations
  • Empathy, patience and appreciative attitude towards life-experienced people
  • Safe occurrence also in cognitive changes and/or impairments
  • Self-employed, responsible work
  • Driving licence Cat. B
