Full description

Tasks

You take responsibility for the operation of the Perigon and easyDOK maintenance software and ensure first- and second-level support. You coordinate and implement technical enhancements of the maintenance software, plan and carry out test work according to service patches and updates and ensure smooth operation. You regularly check the IT processes and interfaces and develop them continuously. In addition, you actively shape the interfaces between the various IT applications, train employees in innovations of the care software and support internal working groups and superusers in the area of care informatics. In addition, you will take over the first and second-level support for the lifestage software, take part in its technical development and the IVF-Hartmann app and support the ICT team in projects for the further development of the entire ICT infrastructure.

Requirements

  • Completed nursing training
  • Further education or in-depth experience in the field of computer science or Application management
  • Experience in Spitex or Healthcare
  • Knowledge of needs assessment (RAI) is an advantage
  • Very good technical understanding and enjoyment of digital solutions
  • Analytical, networked and solution-oriented thinking
  • Pedagogical skill as well as enjoyment of schooling and supporting employees
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • High service orientation and strong communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

We offer

  • Responsible and varied activity with plenty of room for manoeuvre
  • Participation in innovative digitalization projects in the healthcare sector
  • Close exchange with care, ICT and external partners
  • Modern work equipment and contemporary IT infrastructure
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Appreciative corporate culture with short decision paths
  • Possibility to actively shape the future of digital care

Spitex Bern and Domicil have merged under the umbrella of the non-profit Concara Group. With more than 2000 employees, we are committed to integrated all-round care, both inpatient and at home. At Concara Services AG, we provide various services for all operating units of the Concara Group, implement support processes and provide professional support.
