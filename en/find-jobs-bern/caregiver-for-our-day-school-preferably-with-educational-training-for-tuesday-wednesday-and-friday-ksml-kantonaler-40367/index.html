Full description

Portrait The Lengnau School is a living place of learning and personal development. Our school covers from kindergarten to 9. Around 650 students, accompanied by a dedicated team of over 90 teachers. In addition to the school, we run a modern day school, which welcomes around 70 children per week in various modules from kindergarten to 9th grade.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a warm, committed and reliable Personality who enjoys working with children and adolescents Appreciative and competently accompanied.

We are happy to present our school facilities in more detail under the following link: https://youtu.be/9EKn3A0rN2Y?si=NtkdkoyVqmRoZMrf

Tasks

Caring for children and young people in day-school life.

  • Tuesday: 11.30–18.00
  • Wednesday: 11.30 a.m. to 4.55 p.m.
  • Friday: 11.00 – 18.00

Its tasks shall include

  • Support and implementation of age-appropriate activities up to planning
  • Individual support and promotion of children and young people
  • Support for conflicts and challenging situations
  • Promoting self-employment and social cohesion
  • Accompanying the children on the school and kindergarten path
  • Participation in the work in day school everyday life
  • Cooperation and exchange in the day-school team

Requirements

  • Experience in pedagogical or socio-pedagogical work or related profession
  • Experience and joy in working with children and adolescents of different ages in the accompaniment up to the promotion
  • An empathetic, appreciative and patient attitude
  • A calm, balanced and resilient personality
  • Responsibility and reliability
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Willingness to participate in team meetings and training
  • Swiss German is an advantage

We offer

An open-minded and committed team, with an experienced and supportive day school management.

A supportive community that is particularly concerned about the continuous development of our school, providing the necessary resources to provide optimal care and learning conditions for young people in our modern learning and working spaces.

We look forward to an open, heartfelt and committed personality who helps ensure that the children and young people in our day school feel comfortable, safe and secure.
