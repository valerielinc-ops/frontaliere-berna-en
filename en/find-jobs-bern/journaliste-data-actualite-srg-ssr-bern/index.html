Full description

Your Mission Within the data team at Ecublens, you will be required to conduct research and publish surveys fed by the tools of datajournalism. Linked to the Survey Pole, you will propose original editorial contributions, in collaboration with journalists from the various poles of expertise. You will also be asked to put your expertise at the service of the various vectors of RTS (audio, video, digital), daily and on projects in the longest duration. You will be responsible for disseminating your work on RTS channels. Your profile

  • CFJM training or deemed equivalent;
  • Excellent knowledge of datajournalism practices and tools (especially spreadsheets, visualization tools);
  • Mastering artificial intelligence (AI) tools;
  • Aisance with data analysis and editorial exploitation;
  • Knowledge of desired programming language(s) (Python, SQL,...);
  • Mastery of basic mathematical and statistical concepts;
  • Attracted for news in general, datajournalism and investigation in particular;
  • Good current knowledge;
  • Ability to write synthetic journalistic texts and format them for audiovisual purposes;
  • Enhanced sense of initiative, precision and rigour;
  • Team spirit;
  • Acute sense of public service;
  • French mother tongue, with good knowledge of German and English (level B1). What we offer you
  • A key role in the unified drafting of the RTS
  • The possibility of conducting data surveys on different vectors and of touching on various subjects
  • Active participation in the transformation of a public service media We are looking for an 80% data journalist to complete the data cell of the transmedia editor of the RTS, within the information department in Ecublens.
