Put to the Point

We offer you a varied and interesting task in the field of personnel management. With your contribution, contribute to a positive image of Pers A and the entire army! You work in an exciting and versatile environment and are responsible for controlling the formations and courses assigned to you.

You can make this contribution

  • Tasks related to the performance of the training and military duty of assigned troops such as day management, command, mutation and promotion
  • carry out clarifications of applicants, employers and authorities and make decisions on requests for postponement and relocation
  • Using the Army Personnel Information System (PISA) to carry out business, monitor appointments, arrange and administer orders for individual services (courses, reports, etc.)
  • Ensure quality control of duty day reports before entering PISA and the correct application of the relevant rules on eligibility of duty days
  • Initiate investigations and assess the results of investigations and review and open requests for provisional evidence for non-appeals for non-compliance

Your commitment to security and freedom The training command (Kdo Ausb) is responsible for training within the Swiss army – from recruitment to general basic training to cadre training for the militia and the professional military. The Kdo Ausb includes the higher cadre training, the personnel of the army, five teaching associations, the training center as well as the Women in the Army and Diversity Department. One third of the approximately 2,350 employees work in civilian professions in the scientific, technical, medical, psychological, administrative or sports sector and contribute to success together with the professional military.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
