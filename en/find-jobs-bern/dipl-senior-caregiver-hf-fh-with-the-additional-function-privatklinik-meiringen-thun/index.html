Full description

Who we are In the private hospital of Meiringen, people with all forms of mental suffering find accommodation, treatment and care. The third largest psychiatric clinic in the canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the basic stationary psychiatric care of the eastern Bernese Oberland. Care and care are carried out according to current state of medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge. As a member of the "Swiss Leading Hospitals", the private clinic Meiringen is one of Switzerland's leading private clinics. In the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic centre for young adults in Thun (PZJE), we treat young adults aged 16 to 25 with diseases from the entire spectrum of psychiatrics with an extensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment. The center includes 30 stationary beds, has a 24-hour emergency take-up obligation and is run primarily open and if necessary protected on a station. The treatment is based on the latest evidence-based medical, therapeutic and nursing knowledge. Your tasks
