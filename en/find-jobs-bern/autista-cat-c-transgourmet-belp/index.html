Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You do your tours independently.
  • You load your vehicle at temperature and secure the load professionally.
  • You check the deliveries on the basis of the delivery notes – everything has to be right.
  • You deliver professionally and take back empty goods and any returns.
  • You recognize customer needs on the go and forward them internally.
  • You take care of your vehicle and take on further tasks after consultation.

Requirements

  • You have the driving license Cat. C and have several years of experience as a chauffeur or chauffeur
  • You have experience in general cargo distribution – ideally with food
  • You know geography well and navigate safely
  • You are physically fit and resilient
  • You speak and write good German
  • You work structured, reliable and enjoy customer contact
