Portrait The NMS Bern is a Switzerland-wide unique private law organized, not profit-oriented Educational institution with 175 years of tradition, which works closely with cantonal institutions. At currently three school departments (Volksschule, Fachmittelschule and Gymnasium) and an accredited university institute (Pedagogical University Institute NMS), about 850 children, adolescents and adults learn and study under the NMS umbrella at three centrally located, modern locations in the heart of Bern and at an external location in Guttannen – supported by around 200 employees. More information can be found on our homepage www.nmsbern.ch.
