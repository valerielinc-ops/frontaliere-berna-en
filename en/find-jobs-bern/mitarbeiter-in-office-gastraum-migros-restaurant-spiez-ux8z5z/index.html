Full description

Do you want to become part of our innovative and lively catering business? With your commitment, you ensure that our customers feel at home and that their versatile culinary wishes are fulfilled. If quality, freshness and hospitality are a matter of course for you, then we should definitely get to know each other!

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Migros Restaurant Spiez, Centre Terminus, 3700 Spiez

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • In the areas of the guest room, office and kitchen, you will carry out cleaning work
  • You are responsible for cleaning and washing the dishes
  • Preparation tasks in the kitchen will be done by you
  • You are actively working in the team

Requirements

  • Ideally, a few years of professional experience: in the field of (system) gastronomy / retail trade
  • You have a strong sense of order and cleanliness
  • You convince with your resilience, flexibility and high willingness to perform
  • You show teamwork and a high level of social skills
  • Environmentally friendly and careful handling of materials and equipment
  • No education/training required
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Occupational pension fund guarantees the financial security of Migros employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points in all Migros branches and various subsidiaries
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in courses of Migros Club School
  • Sports and fitness offers: Discount on fitness subscriptions
  • Mobile Subscriptions: Various Perks on Mobile Subscriptions
  • Mobility: participation of an annual subscription to public transport, discount on fuel (Migrol), reduced annual contribution for mobility carsharing and entitlement to a fleet discount
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates on newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Employee offers: Professional home at one of the most successful and financially sound Swiss companies.
