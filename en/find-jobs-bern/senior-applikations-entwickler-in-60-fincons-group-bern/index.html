Full description

Fincons Group is an IT consulting company that has been shaping the digital future of leading companies in international markets for 40 years. The Fincons Group has made a name for itself by predicting and successfully interpreting new business models and the rapid development of IT systems. She has built up a complete range of services: from research and consulting to design and development, from system integration of leading software solutions to application management, supporting her customers step by step on their digital transformation journey. Fincons is a multinational company with more than 2,600 employees and several offices worldwide (in Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Belgium and the USA), but above all a family business where the founders play a strategic role with dedication and passion, building the company on the same principles of a united and caring family. Tasks:

  • Design and advance semantic models in Power BI, including transferring existing data models and business logics from the SAP BusinessObjects environment.
  • Implementation of key figures and calculations using a performant, maintainable and comprehensible DAX.
  • Migration of existing reports to Power BI and replacement of existing distribution and automation processes.
  • Creation and development of reports with Power BI Report Builder (paginated reports).
  • Ensure sustainable operations through the implementation of standards, the development of comprehensive documentation, and the transfer of knowledge to the development team.

Requirements

  • In-depth practical experience with Power BI as well as a deep understanding of the platform and its technical capabilities.
  • Very good knowledge of DAX for the development of performant and maintainable data models and key figures.
  • Experience with SAP BusinessObjects (including Web Intelligence and Universes) as well as in the migration of reports to Power BI.
  • Secure use of SQL and Power Query (M) for data preparation and transformation.
  • Strong communication skills as well as experience in coaching teams and in the structured transfer of knowledge and operations.
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1/C2) and good knowledge of English.
