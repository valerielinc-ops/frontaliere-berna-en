Advertising Technology Designer — Genossenschaft Migros Aare

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You want to accompany projects from the first idea to the finished assembly and create visible results? Then you are exactly right with us. To strengthen our advertising technology team, we are looking for a committed, independent and customer-oriented personality with creativity, craftsmanship and a high quality awareness.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are the first contact person for our internal and external clients and support them competently in their concerns
  • You process orders independently, from the request to the offer creation to the timely execution
  • You ensure a professional and transparent flow of information to our clients
  • You create, adapt and optimize layouts for advertising materials such as floor adhesives, posters, signaling, labels and other advertising media
  • You take over production data, design it and prepare it with the Adobe programs print and production ready
  • You produce and process advertising technology products with modern digital printing and production systems and ensure a high-quality implementation of various advertising media
  • You process materials professionally, order and manage consumables and support the maintenance of machinery, facilities and stockpiles
  • You assemble and apply advertising technology solutions indoors and outdoors professionally and precisely

Requirements

  • You bring experience: in advertising technology, labeling or signalling and are familiar with design, production, processing and assembly
  • Completed basic or further training: Designer* in advertising technology EFZ
  • You work safely with Adobe programs as well as the common MS Office applications
  • You convince with craftsmanship, technical understanding and an independent, reliable working method
  • You act customer- and service-oriented, organize your tasks efficiently and take responsibility
  • You bring initiative, team spirit and joy to varied projects
  • You are flexible in terms of time and ready to carry out individual assembly and labeling work before opening business
  • You have or are ready to purchase a category B driver’s license and the lifting work platform certificate
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Modern infrastructure: Diverse work zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates on newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance funds
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
