The UniBE is networked. With us, you are part of an international community and benefit from interdisciplinary cooperation.

Their tasks

  • Administrative tasks (e.g. Webpage Updates)
  • Support in qualitative health research, e.g. literature reviews, preparation of documentation, data cleaning, interview transcription.
  • Support for data collection (focus groups, participating observation, interviews) and analysis (MAXQDA) (depending on profile and experience)
  • Reporting and dissemination of research results through common communication channels (depending on profile and experience)

Your profile

  • Studies with thematic or methodological reference (e.g. psychology, sports science, sociology, social anthropology and the like) at the University of Bern or the University of Freiburg (Bachelor or Master)
  • Exact, well-organized, independent working method
  • Good written expression skills and experience in writing texts for different target groups
  • Mastering the MicrosoftTM Office range
  • Good English and German (written and oral)
  • Teamwork

Desirable

  • First work experience in administrative work and text creation
  • First experience in the application of empirical social research (especially qualitative methods)

Working in an international, dynamic environment in a highly motivated and collaborative team Application and contact Incoming applications are continuously screened until the position is filled. With us, you create value. For themselves and society.

About us

The Institute of Social and Preventive Medicine (ISPM) at the University of Bern conducts research in various public health disciplines (www.ispm.unibe.ch). The research group Social Spatial Health Systems Research (www.communityhealth.ch) led by Prof. Dr Phil. Annika Frahsa is located in the paradigm of participatory health research and uses social science theories and methods to investigate the contextual conditions of health in and with different communities.
