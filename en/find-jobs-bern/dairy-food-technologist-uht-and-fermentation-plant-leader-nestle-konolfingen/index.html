Location: Konolfingen, Switzerland Work model: On site / full time, 100% Type of Contract / Duration: Permanent Contract

Be a force for good and help nurture a healthier generation

As a dairy/food technologist plant leader in 1-shift operation at Nestlé Nutrition & Health, your mission is to implement high-quality products and trials safely, reliably and efficiently on our production facilities. You make a difference by working closely with production teams, development, quality, engineering and workshop. This is your chance to achieve significant results that help improve life worldwide through nutritional innovation.

How to do something as a dairy/food technologist plant leader in 1-shift operation

  • Product manufacturing in compliance with safety regulations and according to NGMP/SOPs
  • Preparation and conduct of tests
  • Preparation of work instructions, test protocols and production documents
  • Submitting suggestions for improvement in the development of new products and processes
  • Assistance in improving existing products and processes
  • Troubleshooting and maintenance work on process lines in cooperation with the workshop

This makes you successful

  • Training as a milk or food technologist or in another food profession
  • Good technical understanding of production facilities
  • Professional experience in the field of liquid dairy products or fermentation/filtration is advantageous
  • Curiosity and interest in developing new processes and products
  • Oral and written English skills or willingness to learn them
  • Social competence and team skills
  • MS-Office user knowledge
