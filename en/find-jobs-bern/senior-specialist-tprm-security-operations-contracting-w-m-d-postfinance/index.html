Full description

As a Senior Specialist TPRM Security – Operations & Contracting, you will handle challenging TPRM security cases on your own responsibility throughout the entire third-party lifecycle. You introduce security requirements into evaluations and contracts at an early stage, bring together different interests and ensure a risk-appropriate and comprehensible contract transfer.

You can do that.

  • You will process TPRM security cases from RfI and RfP through security due diligence and KS classification until the contract is signed and ongoing support.
  • You coordinate the technical assessment with information security, IT security/cyber, ICT, BCM, operational resilience and other security areas.
  • You prepare the risk-adequate security attachment and control its coordination with partner management, procurement, legal and risk control.
  • You will accompany contract negotiations with third parties independently and represent the security and resilience requirements of PostFinance clearly, pragmatically and assertively.
  • You assess contract deviations and exceptions and develop compensation measures, risk notes and well-founded decision-making principles.
  • You will analyze certifications as well as ISAE, SOC and other assurance reports and document the security recommendation for onboarding.
  • You support ongoing TPRM tasks, audit and audit requests and continuously develop processes, templates, security attachments and work tools.

You bring that with you

  • Higher degree in Information Security, Computer Science, Business Informatics, Law, Risk Management or a comparable qualification.
  • You have experience in the banking environment and know the essential regulatory requirements for information security, operational risks and third party risk management.
  • You have experience in planning, coordinating and conducting security and risk audits with third parties and can assess and track findings and resulting measures in a structured manner.
  • Several years of experience in third party risk management, security due diligence, supplier security or a comparable environment.
  • Broad understanding of information security, IT security, cyber, ICT, BCM and operational resilience.
  • Experience with security requirements in RfI/RfP procedures as well as with contract annexes, assurance reports and risk-based assessments.
  • Strong negotiating skills and the ability to represent security requirements convincingly to internal stakeholders and external partners.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English.
