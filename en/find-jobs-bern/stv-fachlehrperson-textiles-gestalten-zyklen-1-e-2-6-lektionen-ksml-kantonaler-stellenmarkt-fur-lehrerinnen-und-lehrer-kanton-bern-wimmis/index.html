Portrait Welcome to Wimmis! At the school Wimmis 48 teachers teach about 400 students at three locations. At the school house Oberdorf there are 6 classes. Our team - our strength!

Tasks As a specialist teacher, you teach, promote and support children of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd and 4th classes in textile design. You teach a total of 6 lessons. The lessons will take place on Thursday from 8.20-11.50 and 13.30-15.05.

They teach during the maternity office and a subsequent holiday as a deputy. A continuation from summer 2027 into an indefinite employment is possible.

They are involved in the teaching team and are ready to participate in school and organisational development.

Requirements Do you have a heart for children and can they inspire and motivate you? Do you have a diploma in design? Are you a committed, communicative and full of humor with pleasure and interest in working with the team of the school and the school house? Do you maintain an open and direct exchange with parents and all other stakeholders?

If so, you are exactly the teacher we are looking for for our team!
