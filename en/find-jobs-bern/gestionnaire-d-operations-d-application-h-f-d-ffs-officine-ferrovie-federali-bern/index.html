The “IT Base Infrastructure” solution provides cloud and platform services, data and messaging infrastructure, API management and developer experience for the entire SBB. Our Agile Release Train Application Platform provides the basic infrastructure so that business applications can be implemented and operated quickly, securely and scalably. You will play a key role at the interface of platform services such as databases, containers, IaaS, monitoring, API management and messaging. These are services on which hundreds of applications run and which form the backbone of the digital SBB. You keep the business running, drive further development and keep profitability under control. At the same time, you listen to the market and the digital solution organizations in order to recognize early on where new technologies or services are in demand.
