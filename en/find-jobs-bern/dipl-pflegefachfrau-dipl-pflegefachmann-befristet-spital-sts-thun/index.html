Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking for meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

You will work on a urological center of gravity, but patients from other surgical areas will also be cared for. In the modernized bed station, great emphasis is placed on interdisciplinary cooperation with flat hierarchies. You work in a young and innovative team of nurses and doctors. Through the integration in the entire urology team, close cooperation takes place at eye level beyond all specialist groups in order to ensure first-class patient care. We offer great flexibility in the work plan and are happy to respond to individual wishes, if this is operationally possible.
