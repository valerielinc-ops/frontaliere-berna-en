Full description

Portrait The NMS Bern is a Switzerland-wide unique private law organized, not profit-oriented Educational institution with 175 years of tradition, which works closely with cantonal institutions. At currently three school departments (Volksschule, Fachmittelschule and Gymnasium) and an accredited university institute (Pedagogical University Institute NMS), about 850 children, adolescents and adults learn and study under the NMS umbrella at three centrally located, modern locations in the heart of Bern and at an external location in Guttannen – supported by around 200 employees. More information can be found on our homepage www.nmsbern.ch.

Tasks

  • They teach French in classes FMS 1a, 1b, 2a, 2b and FMS 3 (11 lessons).
  • In addition, you will take 3 Co Learning Space lessons of the FMS 3 as well as 5 lessons in the vessel PLUS (pensum total 18.5 lessons).
  • Lessons will take place on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday.
  • Optionally, accompany students: inside individually as an accompanying person.
  • They participate in FMS conferences. So far a conference is planned: Thursday, 29 October from 16:30 to 18:30.

Requirements

  • They value an appreciative and motivating learning culture.
  • You enjoy working in a team: Exchange, coordination and cooperation with colleagues are a matter of course for you.
  • You have completed or advanced studies in French; A teaching diploma for maturity schools is desired.
  • They are flexible and can enter at short notice.

We offer

  • A manageable, motivated and committed team in an innovative school organised under private law.
  • Salary classification according to cantonal guidelines.
  • Compact timetable three days a week.
  • Place of work in a central location – a few minutes walk from Bern train station.
