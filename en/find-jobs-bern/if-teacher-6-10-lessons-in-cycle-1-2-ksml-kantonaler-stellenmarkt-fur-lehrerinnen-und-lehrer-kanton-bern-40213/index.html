Portrait The municipality of Radelfingen is located on the southwest slope of the Frienisberg and consists of the villages Detligen and Radelfingen as well as other villages in the area. The rural character also characterizes the primary school in the village of Radelfingen in which a basic level and mixed-year classes are run.
