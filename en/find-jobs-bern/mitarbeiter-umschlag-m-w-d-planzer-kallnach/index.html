Full description

As a firmly rooted company in the Seeland region, we offer comprehensive transport and storage solutions and ensure cleanliness with our waste transports in over 30 communities. Our success is based on motivated employees and modern infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • When loading and unloading trucks, you are the absolute professional and control the goods and pallets
  • You sort packages efficiently and carefully according to specified processes
  • Correct scanning of the shipments in the system and ensuring a seamless recording
  • You make sure that the goods come safely and quickly to the right storage place. Chaos? Not with you!
  • The goods you move mainly with floor conveyors from A to B
  • You are responsible for a smooth process and a clean and orderly goods handling

Why do you succeed?

  • Working at night is your thing? Perfect! You are ready to take part in one of our two shifts from 17:00 to 01:00 or from 01:00 to 09:00 from Monday to Saturday
  • You have already gained experience in the warehouse or logistics sector
  • You are already a proud owner of a forklift card
  • Orally you can safely express yourself in German
  • Your efficient, independent and reliable way of working distinguishes you and you are happy to take on responsibility

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times – modern and safe work equipment is available to you
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
