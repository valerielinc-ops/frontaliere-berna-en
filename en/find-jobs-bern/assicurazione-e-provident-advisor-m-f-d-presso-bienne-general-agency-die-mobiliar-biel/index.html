Full description

What awaits you

As such, you offer our customers insurance and pension solutions adapted to their specific needs. You thus actively contribute to the development of the client portfolio of the general agency in Bienne.

What you can achieve with us

Your main tasks will be the acquisition of new individual and business customers, as well as the active monitoring and development of your existing customer base.

Of a considerate nature, you always listen to your customers and provide them with personalized advice as well as tailor-made solutions in all areas of insurance and protection.

As a sociable and communicative person, you have no difficulty building and maintaining a network of clients. You also appreciate open discussions within a dynamic team.

What you bring

  • Basic commercial training completed as well as “AFA Insurance Intermediary” training or willingness to follow them
  • You have several years of professional experience, advantageously in the field of insurance and pension consulting
  • You want to acquire new customers through active networking
  • You are able to work independently, reliably and responsibly
  • Customer-oriented personality, sense of communication and team spirit
  • French mother tongue with good knowledge of German.

Working with us

As part of a motivated team, we offer you varied, varied and independent work, a modern workplace in the city center of Biel as well as excellent working conditions and further training opportunities.

The activity zone includes the city of Bienne and the surrounding municipalities. Immediate entry into office or to be agreed.

What we offer

Participation in the result

Our success also benefits our employees. Each year, we decide on the payment of a profit sharing and its amount.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses. We contribute to continuing training related to work.

Advantages

In particular, we offer up to 25% off premiums, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and various discounts on cultural events.
