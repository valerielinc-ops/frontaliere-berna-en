UniBE is networked. You are part of an international community and benefit from interdisciplinary cooperation.

  • Experience with quantitative methods and data analysis
  • Experience with data visualisation and/or publicly available data platforms is beneficial
  • Experience in teaching at Bachelor and Master level is beneficial
  • Very good English knowledge in word and writing; sound German skills are advantageous
  • Experience in writing grant applications is beneficial

We offer: research activities on current and socially relevant topics. Space for the development of own projects.
