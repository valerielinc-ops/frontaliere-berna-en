Full description

As Head of Network Use Management, you take on management responsibilities at Power Grid. As a member of the management team of the Energy & Grid Customers business unit, you will help shape the further development and lead the area that combines line organization and agile cooperation.

Your field of activity

  • The strategic orientation of network use management is your responsibility. You will continue to develop the area with a view to regulatory, technological and market-side changes
  • With earnings and budget responsibility, you control the area entrepreneurially and represent the network use management in strategic projects as well as to internal and external partners.
  • You will continue to develop the network use management of tomorrow with your team and actively drive the transformation of the process, system and data landscape. In doing so, you will recognize and realize potential for new data-based solutions with tangible added value for our customers.
  • Together with your six-member management team, you lead and design an organization with around 100 employees across several management levels and create space for responsibility and personal development.

You bring that with you

  • Several years of leadership experience in larger organizational units as well as a leadership understanding based on orientation, trust and responsibility.
  • Your mindset in the sense of “servant leadership” in order to grow above the current performance level together with your team and involved stakeholders
  • Entrepreneurial and strategic thinking, courage to question the existing, as well as the implementation strength to turn ideas into concrete solutions
  • Enjoying complex process and system landscapes as well as curiosity and learning ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new topics
  • A university degree in business administration as well as strong communication and negotiation in German and English
