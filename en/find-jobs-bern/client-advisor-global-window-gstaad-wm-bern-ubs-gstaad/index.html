Key responsibilities Are you at your best when you can advise clients and build long-term relationships? Is your investment advice outstanding? For our Global Window Gstaad with international customers, but within Wealth Management Switzerland, we are looking for a new customer consultant from:

  • develops a trusting partnership with our very wealthy customers
  • continuously maintains a customer book and develops it based on a strategic plan
  • working closely with other team members and our specialists to provide high-quality investment solutions
  • adheres to all regulations, policies and standards as a contribution to an effective risk culture

Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from returnees. More information about our program: ubs.com/careercomeback .

The team You work in the wealth management team for wealthy private clients in Gstaad. We are a strong and motivated team.

We put our customers at the center of our advice and provide an outstanding customer experience. We are personal, reliable and sustainable in advising and supporting our customers and offer an innovative, competent and first-class service.

Why you should apply

This position offers you the opportunity to work in one of the most exclusive regions of Switzerland, with a demanding and international clientele. Become part of a team that lives excellence and celebrates success together.

Your skills and experience

  • Degree in banking and further education in banking or university, experience in dealing with UHNWI highly desired
  • multi-year local or regional experience in the Gstaad space or relationship to this space an advantage
  • In-depth expertise in financial products and financial services for high net worth or institutional clients
  • Passion for sales and new customer acquisition
  • High service orientation and the ability to deliver an impressive consulting experience
  • excellent communication and presentation skills
  • Adaptness in dealing with cultural and linguistic diversity, curiosity for technical progress and great openness to digitalization
  • fluent in German, English and if possible French
  • Parking safe in dealing with very upscale customers
  • Intent to make Gstaad and Saanenland part of the life relationship
