Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

As a company electrician, you take on a central role for operational and supply security. You ensure that electrical systems, energy supply and communication systems work reliably. In the event of disruptions, you analyze the cause, initiate the right measures and coordinate external specialist companies if necessary. You not only want to maintain technology, but also take responsibility for a reliable hospital operation? Then strengthen our team in Burgdorf as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement as

Your task: Technology on which the hospital company can rely

  • You ensure the safe operation and maintenance of our electrical and energy supply systems as well as machine and plant control systems and carry out repairs professionally
  • You plan and realize new installations and accompany technical adjustments in the ongoing hospital operation
  • You supervise and maintain our communication facilities and ensure their availability
  • You provide first- and second-level support to medical devices and support in the technical limitation and correction of disorders
  • You systematically carry out quality controls, inspections, repairs and maintenance and document the work carried out
  • In the event of disruptions, you assess the situation, take appropriate measures and coordinate the work of external specialist companies
  • In Pikettdienst, you take on responsibility for the hospital’s technical readiness outside of regular working hours.
  • You maintain the device and plant data in the FM system Campos independently and reliably
  • You get involved where pragmatic solutions and interdisciplinary support are required in technical operations

What you bring

  • You have a completed apprenticeship as an electrical installer:in EFZ or assembly electrician:in EFZ
  • Telematics and communication systems interest you and you move safely in a technically networked environment
  • Knowledge of KNX, SPS and DESIGO is an advantage
  • You are interested in medical technology and are ready to familiarize yourself with the special requirements of a hospital operation
  • You have at least three years of professional experience in low-voltage installations under a qualified person according to NIV and thus meet the requirements for obtaining the company electrician license
  • You think service-oriented, act binding and keep an overview even in the event of disruptions
  • You work independently, structured and responsible and can reliably assess technical priorities
  • You have very good PC user knowledge, especially in Windows and MS Office
  • You have a category B driving licence
