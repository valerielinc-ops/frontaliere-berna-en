Full description

A lot of personal responsibility, a motivated team and a lot of fresh country air: Do these prospects inspire you? We are looking for a competent and motivated personality immediately or by agreement as Health professional We are the public Spitex Gantrisch and responsible for the basic care of around 10,000 inhabitants from the Gürbetal valley to the Langenberg to the Gantrisch. Our strength lies in the individual and resource-oriented care as well as the care of people in their home. With our work, we maintain the quality of life of our clients and promote their independence where they feel most comfortable: at home. Are you looking for a long-term permanent job or a return to care? Do you have a lot of professional experience or have you just completed your apprenticeship? No matter where you are, just contact us! We are happy to take our time and discuss your options together. Your tasks

  • Professional basic and treatment care of our clients in their home.
  • You advise and accompany clients and their relatives and work closely with other institutions.
  • You ensure a smooth exchange within our Spitex team

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training at secondary level in nursing (FaGe, FaBe, FA SRK, dipl.) Housekeeper
  • High social and self-competence as well as team skills
  • A reflective approach to closeness and distance
  • An independent, responsible and well-structured way of working
  • Cat B driving licence and own car

We offer you

  • A versatile, independent and responsible activity – even with complex care situations
  • An appreciative and open working atmosphere
  • A field of work in one of the most beautiful rural regions
  • Opportunities for further development and specialisation in areas such as dementia or vocational training
  • Percentage in mutual agreement (20%-90%)

We look forward to seeing you!
