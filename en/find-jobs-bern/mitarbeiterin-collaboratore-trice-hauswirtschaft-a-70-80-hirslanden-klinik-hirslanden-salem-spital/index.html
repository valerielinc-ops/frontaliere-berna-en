Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Salem-Spital | Bern Occupation by: Immediately or by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For our housekeeping team in the Salem Hospital, we are looking for a committed and reliable person who cares for cleanliness and hygiene with care and dedication – and thus contributes to the well-being of our patients.

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in the Fitness Abo & Entrance to the indoor pool

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You independently carry out maintenance and basic cleaning work
  • In the absence of permanent employees, you support our team as a replacement
  • You make sure that the respective departments are cleaned according to the given standards and guidelines
  • You work efficiently, carefully and responsibly with the available resources
  • Occasionally you take on special tasks as directed by the superiors
  • Damage or defects you recognize early and report them to the daily responsibility
  • The implementation and compliance with the hygiene guidelines are a matter of course for you
  • Your assignments take place both during the day and in the evening in the operating room and on the various departments
  • In addition, you take on a mission on Sunday once or twice a month.
  • Two to three times a year you take a piquette from Saturday to Sunday

Your profile

  • You have profound experience in maintenance and basic cleaning in the hospital sector
  • Dedication, helpfulness and quality awareness distinguish you
  • Even in stressful situations, you keep track and can set priorities correctly
  • You are flexible and will not let short-term changes get you out of concept
  • Customer, service and service orientation are a matter of course for you
  • You are capable of teamwork and enjoy working with employees from different cultures
  • A well-kept appearance and very good knowledge of German round off your profile

For more information, Nadine Stroh, Head of Housekeeping Salem-Spital, is available at T. We look forward to your contact.
