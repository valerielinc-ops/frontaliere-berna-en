Full description

Our heart beats for the Swiss gastronomic sector. We are constantly committed to our clients. In our 31 self-service markets, through external service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a varied assortment, attractive clean brands, specialized and professional advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Suisse SA is part of the international group Transgourmet. The company is a leader in the delivery and self-service trade in Switzerland and has some 2'100 employees, as well as 100'000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals, as well as retail).

Aufgaben

  • Good physical resistance to cold and loads
