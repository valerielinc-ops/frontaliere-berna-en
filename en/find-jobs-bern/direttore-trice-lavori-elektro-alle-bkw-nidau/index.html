We make habitats worth living.

With confidence in our employees, in their strengths and talents, we create professional electrical solutions at the pulse of the latest technologies every day – innovative, strong and personal. Become part of our motivated team!

Your tasks

  • Organisation, preparation and execution of small and large construction sites in compliance with safety regulations and quality management.
  • Leading the assembly team and ensuring personnel time management
  • Procurement of materials taking into account economic efficiency and ecology
  • Date monitoring and compliance
  • Participation in construction meetings
  • Coordination and communication with specialist planning, construction management and third-party companies
  • Expert advice and support for our customers
  • Interface between project management and project execution

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as an electrical installer: in EFZ or assembly electrician EFZ as well as professional experience
  • Motivation and passion to achieve goals together, as well as pleasure in customer contact, distinguish you
  • Thanks to your solution-oriented, flexible and responsible nature, you also master challenging situations reliably and quality-consciously.
  • As a team player with a high level of initiative and assertiveness, you are used to leading your employees in a goal-oriented manner.

We support you with state-of-the-art equipment, a company vehicle for private use and individual training. A companion team, above-average social benefits and numerous employee benefits await you.
