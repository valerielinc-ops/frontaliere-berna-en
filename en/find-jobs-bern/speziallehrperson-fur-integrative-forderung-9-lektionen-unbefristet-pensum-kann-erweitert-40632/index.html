Portrait We are a small, family-run school where relationships and shared learning are at the heart of our efforts. In our comprehensive school, children from 1st to 6th grade learn mixed ages. For our location in Häutligen we are looking for a specialist teacher for integrative promotion for a permanent employment with 9 lessons.
