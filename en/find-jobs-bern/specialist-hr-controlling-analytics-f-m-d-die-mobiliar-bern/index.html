Full description

This is what awaits you

You will become part of the HR Services department, which forms the backbone of our human resources management from HR and wage administration to central services such as HR systems and processes, compensation & benefits and HR controlling and analytics. In a demanding, data-driven environment, your know-how helps shape the basis for informed HR decisions.

This is what you do with us

In HR controlling, you ensure that central HR data can be maintained correctly, processed and used for evaluations. You will work with the SAP systems SuccessFactors and HCM and will be responsible for data on organizational management, job positions and corporate structure. You ensure data quality, answer requests from HR, line and controlling and create evaluations with SAP BW and Visier. You also support financial HR processes such as planning or internal accounting.

In the area of HR Analytics, you are responsible for the ongoing operation of our HR Analytics tool Target and coordinate release updates, tests, system adjustments and technical configurations such as dashboards, roles and permissions. You create HR evaluations for day-to-day business from sights, for example on fluctuation, headcount, absences or organizational structures. At the same time, you optimize processes and reports together with HR consulting, payroll and personnel administration or other specialist departments, simplify or automate processes and document them in a comprehensible manner. You support your colleagues in the efficient use of the tool and actively contribute your expertise to working groups and projects.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed commercial apprenticeship with further education with focus Controlling / Business Administration
  • Experience in day-to-day HR business with SAP HCM and SuccessFactors (SF) and safe handling of Excel for evaluations and data preparation
  • Interest and willingness to use and understand an HR analytics tool (visit) in everyday life (previous knowledge not mandatory)
  • Pronounced data mind set, good conceptual and analytical skills as well as a strong number flair
  • Careful, reliable working method
  • Team-oriented, communicative, ability to take others and communicate practical knowledge in an understandable way
  • Flowing knowledge of German, French and / or English is advantageous

Working with us

You can expect a motivated HR services team that focuses on its customers and supports each other both professionally and humanly. We work closely together, pragmatically and solution-oriented in a lively, communicative and agile working environment.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
