Ophthalmology with a focus on patients instead of administration, in a modern practice network. In Bern, we are looking for a specialist in ophthalmology in the range of 80 – 100% by appointment. In an established ophthalmological practice network with central, state-of-the-art locations, you can expect an appreciative, dynamic environment with first-class conservative eye medicine. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They hold ophthalmological consultation hours and look after patients in conservative ophthalmology
  • You develop the right solution for every eye concern, supported by competent colleagues
  • They focus fully on patient care instead of administrative work
  • They help ensure that patients at the Berne location can rely on first-class eye medicine
  • You are part of a team that lives up to the highest medical and human quality standards

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for ophthalmology or equivalent foreign specialist title with federal recognition
  • Motivation, high quality standards and pleasure in working with and for people
  • Claim to operate eye medicine at the highest level
  • Independent and at the same time team-oriented, efficient working method

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centered, empathetic basic attitude with high quality standards
  • Curiosity, ambition and joy in the further development of the offer
  • Appreciation for the professional exchange with colleagues

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Modern, appreciative and dynamic working environment with flat hierarchy and du culture
  • Regulated working hours without emergency and weekend services
  • Internal and external training opportunities with at least 10 training days per year
  • 5 weeks holidays per year with the possibility to buy up to 10 additional holidays
  • Attractive maternity and paternity benefits, accident and illness, and modern technology in the workplace
