University Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

You want to help shape, think and take on responsibility – in an environment that considers mental health holistically? Then you are exactly right at the Community Psychiatric Center (GPZ) of the University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy. We accompany people with mental illnesses in all walks of life – outpatient, day-inpatient and inpatient. Our goal: to create stability, enable participation and open up prospects. For this we need people like you – strong in the field, clear in the head and open in the heart.

Your tasks

  • You actively participate in the care process and take responsibility for quality and implementation
  • You are specifically involved in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures and coordinate them in an interdisciplinary team
  • You participate in rapports, case meetings and team meetings
  • You contribute your nursing expertise in everyday life and support collegial cooperation
  • You participate in projects and specifically shape your own further education
  • You cover the necessary services in 24/7 operation with early and late service until 21:00 (usually no night service) to enable the operation of the day clinic and stationary beds in the GPZ

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF / FH and ideally bring experience in psychiatric care
  • You work in a structured, independent manner and bring in-depth expertise and high social skills
  • You appreciate the exchange in the interprofessional team and would like to actively shape psychiatric care
  • Even in demanding situations, you remain calm, clear and facing
  • You are safe with digital tools
  • You think along, recognize the need for action at an early stage and want to actively shape your working environment
  • You bring very good knowledge of German in word and writing

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
