Principal EHS Specialist (m/f/d)

About the role

As Principal EHS Specialist (m/f/d), you support the Berne location in all central topics of health, safety and environmental management. They bring in-depth EHS expertise, strong shopfloor proximity and the ability to coach executives, teams and EHS colleagues and accompany them in their development. You combine operational presence with strategic thinking and make a significant contribution to the further development of our health, safety and environmental culture.

This role is aimed at experienced EHS professionals who want to not only use their expertise to solve problems, but actively contribute to the development of people, teams and organizations.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Coaching, mentoring and cultural development

  • Coach and sparring partner for executives, employees and EHS professionals
  • Supporting the professional development of EHS team members
  • Promoting an open learning, feedback and safety culture
  • Providing best practices and lessons learned
  • Supporting leaders in their EHS responsibilities
  • Promoting ownership and a sustainable safety culture at all levels
  • Contribution to the further development of the EHS organisation at the site

Presence in operation and support of the shop floor

  • Regular presence in production, technology, laboratories, logistics and support areas
  • Implementation of safety, health and environmental walks
  • Support for the animal meeting structure (Tier 1 to Tier 3)
  • Identification of opportunities for improvement in daily operations
  • Support teams in implementing effective protection measures
  • Promoting proactive risk and safety perception

Health Protection and Occupational Health

  • Identification and assessment of health-related risks
  • Support for topics such as ergonomics, exposures, noise, climate and mental health
  • Cooperation with medical services and external specialists
  • Support for health monitoring programmes
  • Development and implementation of appropriate prevention measures
  • Promoting health-promoting jobs and wellbeing initiatives

Environmental management

  • Support and further development of environmental management processes
  • Participation in issues such as:
  • Waste management
  • Emissions control
  • Energy efficiency
  • Sewage
  • Hazardous materials management
  • Resource management
  • Sustainability initiatives
  • Support for ISO-14001-compliant processes
  • Implementation of Environmental Walks
  • Support for environmental audits and regulatory inspections

Hazard identification and prevention

  • Systematic identification of health, safety and environmental risks
  • Perform hazard assessments and task risk assessments
  • Assistance in assessing and prioritizing risks
  • Review of the effectiveness of existing safeguard measures
  • Support for the development of sustainable prevention measures
  • Maintenance of relevant ETS risk registers
  • Moderation of more complex risk analyses as needed

Incident investigation and continuous improvement

  • Leading or assisting in investigations of EHS incidents, pSIFs and environmental events
  • Use of structured examination methods such as ICAM, RCA or TapRooT
  • Identification of sustainable improvement measures
  • Integration of lessons learned in processes, standards and training
  • Promoting an open and learning error culture

Systems Thinking and EHS Governance

  • Consideration of relationships between people, technology, organization and environment
  • Contributing to the further development of EHS processes and standards
  • Cooperation in site-wide improvement programmes and strategic initiatives
  • Development of sustainable solutions for root cause elimination
  • Support for the EHS Operating Model and EHS Governance

Requirements and qualifications

Professional experience

  • At least 7 years of professional experience in Environment, Health & Safety
  • Experience in regulated industries such as pharmaceuticals, biotech or chemistry
  • Experience in accompanying, developing or professionally supporting employees and managers
  • Experience in implementing EHS programmes and improvement initiatives
  • Demonstrable ability to influence and successfully accompany changes

Training

Higher education, for example

  • MSc / MAS Safety Engineering
  • MSc Environmental Engineering
  • MSc Process Engineering
  • MSc Life Sciences
  • Studies in natural sciences or a comparable discipline

Further education in Occupational Health, Environmental Management, Coaching or Leadership are beneficial.

Technical competences

  • Very good knowledge of ISO 45001 and ISO 14001
  • Experience in hazard identification and risk assessment
  • Knowledge of health, safety and environmental management
  • Experience in handling hazardous substances and regulatory requirements
  • Knowledge of EHS management systems, reporting and risk management tools
  • Experience with methods such as HAZOP, FMEA or Bow Tie is advantageous

Personal competences

  • Strong communication and moderation capabilities
  • Ability to build trust and develop sustainable relationships
  • Joy of coaching, knowledge transfer and cooperation
  • Convincing appearance at shop floor and management level
  • High personal responsibility and solution orientation
  • Ability to drive change constructively
  • Team orientation and strong stakeholder competence

Languages

  • in German: nehandlungssicher
  • English: very good

About CSL Behring CSL Behring is a global biotherapeutics leader driven by our promise to save lives.
