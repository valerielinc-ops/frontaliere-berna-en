Full description

The UniBE is in the middle of it. With us you benefit from the central location and conveniently located locations.

##

  • Tasks

In the Study Secretariat, you work on classifications, recognitions, remission applications and also advise students on complex issues. In the Examination Secretariat, you accompany our students on the last meters of their studies: You coordinate and test bachelor and master degrees, create and control diplomas, duplicates and certificates and organize our doctoral celebration once a semester. Another part of your task is the supervision and training of our CV learners. We presuppose the willingness to assume responsibility in vocational training. If you do not yet bring the appropriate training as a vocational trainer, we are happy to provide it. In the absence of the head of the dean’s office, you take over his deputy and ensure that business continues reliably. In addition, you supervise the Commission for Research and Young Scientists and, with the organization of the semester meeting of the Faculty's Administration and our team events, ensure that the exchange within the faculty is not neglected. In short: a varied job for someone who likes to organize, takes responsibility, appreciates contact with people and keeps track even if several things are going on at the same time.

Conditions

Completed training Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ or comparable, several years of professional experience, good language skills D/E. Advantage: Course card for the 40-hour course or a federally recognized diploma for vocational trainers or willingness to acquire it.
