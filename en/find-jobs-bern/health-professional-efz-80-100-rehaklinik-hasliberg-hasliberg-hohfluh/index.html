Full description

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of psychosomatic, internistic-oncological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Solothurn, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the mission statement of “REHA competence” with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, warmth, attention and competence.

Their tasks

  • Nursing competent and safe care of our patients
  • Offering rehabilitative care
  • Promote resources to bring about the greatest possible independence of patients
  • Independent preparation of nursing history and planning in simple patient situations with the support of a qualified nurse
  • Independent implementation and control of the measures as well as evaluating and adapting the care process with the support of a qualified nurse
  • Interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation between the different disciplines
  • Active participation in the training of health professionals EFZ

Our expectations

  • They have a sense of quality and responsibility.
  • They have a quick understanding and implement it in everyday work.
  • They bring an independent, patient-oriented and responsible way of working.
  • They are open to new ideas and changes.

Our offer
