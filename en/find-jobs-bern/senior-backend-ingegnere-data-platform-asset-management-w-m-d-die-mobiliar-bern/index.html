Full description

This is what awaits you

As part of the Gold team, you will develop robust backend solutions for the asset management data platform. You combine inventory, market and transaction data from different systems and thus create the basis for reliable data products, reports and analyses. In our international, agile team, you contribute with high quality awareness to ensuring the future viability of our products.

This is what you do with us

In this function, you design, develop and operate our Python and SQL-based data pipelines on Databricks and Azure. You are responsible for stable and high-performance solutions for loading routes, validation and transformation over the entire life cycle – according to the principle “you build it, you run it”. In addition, you help to optimize common standards, for example for data governance and data lineage.

As an experienced developer, you actively share your knowledge and strengthen the engineering expertise in the team with code reviews and professional exchange. If you are interested and suitable, you can gradually assume technical responsibility in the areas of technology, quality or security – for example as a Quality Engineer, Security Ambassador or in solution design and the further development of the technical vision.

As a curious person, we support you with internal training and further education offers on this way.

This is what you bring with you

  • Several years of experience in backend development with Python as well as a sound understanding of software architecture, object-oriented development, design patterns and automated testing
  • Practical experience in data engineering with data-intensive systems, in particular with PySpark, SQL and Lakehouse technologies
  • Experience in the design and operation of productive data pipelines incl. Orchestration, monitoring, alerting and troubleshooting
  • Understanding the interaction of distributed systems, applications and data flows, as well as experience with the integration of components in heterogeneous system landscapes
  • Secure handling of Git, GitLab CI/CD and cloud environments (Azure is an advantage)
  • Analytical, structured and implementation-oriented working method as well as high affinity for automation
  • Communicative and open-minded personality with pleasure in professional exchange and cooperation in an international team
  • Fluent German and good English skills for cooperation with our team members in Vietnam
  • Experience in the financial or asset management environment is a plus

Working with us

Success with you: With us you work in a small, specialized, international hybrid team. We use our expertise in a targeted manner and learn from each other every day, be it technologically, linguistically or in cooperation.

This is what we offer

Training and development

Advantages
