Product Manager Application Platform (m/f/d) 60- 100%, SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Product Manager Application Platform (m/w/d) Bern / Work Smart, starting immediately or by agreement, 60-100% Apply now

What you can look forward to. The "IT Base Infrastructure" solution provides cloud and platform services, data and messaging infrastructure, API Management, and Developer Experience for the entire SBB. Our Agile Release Train Application Platform provides the basic infrastructure that enables business applications to be developed and operated quickly, securely and scalably. You will take on a key role at the interface with platform services such as databases, containers, IaaS, monitoring, API Management and messaging. These are services that hundreds of applications run on and that form the backbone of SBB's digital operations. You keep operations running, drive further development forward and ensure cost-effectiveness. At the same time, you stay closely attuned to the market and the Digital Solution organizations to identify early where new technologies or services are needed. Validate, try things out, shut them down if they fail, scale them massively if they succeed - with built-in quality - that is your playing field. A role with a major impact and plenty of room to shape things. What you can achieve.

  • As Product Manager (m/f/d) Application Platform, you will be responsible for platforms that form SBB's digital foundation and thus the basis for Switzerland's rail operations.
  • You will shape a challenging and very broad area of responsibility within an IT environment that must be designed for the highest availability, standardization and scalability, where your decisions will have a direct impact.
  • You work closely with ARTs such as Identity & Access Management, Development Experience and Cloud Foundation to jointly establish even better, scalable and cost-effective platforms and practices.
  • You ensure that technology does not remain an end in itself, but reliably gets people from A to B every day.
  • You also actively contribute to the further development of the ART as part of the ART leadership and initiate and drive cross-ART initiatives.
  • In close collaboration with Product Owners, development, operations and SaaS providers, you balance requirements and ensure sound decisions in coordination with security, architecture and Business IT.

What you bring.

  • Strong leadership skills to proactively drive initiatives forward, develop teams and employees, foster personal accountability and act effectively in a shared leadership model.
  • An engaging manner, curiosity about technology trends, entrepreneurial thinking and the tenacity to get initiatives involving many stakeholders off the ground.
  • Confident stakeholder management and audience-oriented communication in a complex area of tension between operations, development, business IT, security and architecture. Strong storytelling skills and executive presence to communicate complex platform topics in an understandable way.
  • A sound understanding of infrastructure and platform technologies to work as equals with architects and developers and support technology decisions.
  • Several years of experience in product management for platform services in a scaled, agile environment with many users.
  • A validation-driven approach, with the courage to deliberately try out new things, evaluate them with users and scale them as needed.
