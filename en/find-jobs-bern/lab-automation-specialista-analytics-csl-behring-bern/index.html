Role overview

For our global Technical Operations Organization, we are currently looking for a

Lab Automation Specialist - Analytics (m/f/x) Locations available: Bern

The Opportunity The Lab Automation Specialist is responsible for automating laboratory systems and workflows ensuring seamless integration of automation technologies to enhance lab efficiency, accuracy and throughout.

  • Support the development, implementation and management of lab automation systems, including liquid handlers, robotic arms and LIMS​
  • Collaborate with TES AI/automation group, scientist, engineers and IT teams to identify and implement automation opportunities to streamline laboratory process​
  • Design and optimise automated workflows for sample preparation, analysis, and data management.​
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and optimise automation equipment and software to ensure reliability and operational continuity​
  • Ensure integration of automation systems with existing lab information management (LIMS), electronic Lab Notebooks (ELNs) and data integration​
  • Train lab personnel on the proper use of automated systems and ensure compliance with SOP and safety standards​
  • Together with TES automation lead manage end-to-end automation projects, including planning, validation, deployment, and post-implementation support.
