The holistic physiotherapeutic treatment for people of all ages – this has been our daily focus since its foundation in 1994. Together with the fitness and Pilates range, we can offer a comprehensive range of services that is unique, beautifully located and easily accessible in the Elfenau Quarter.

  • Integrated fitness area

  • 2 employees for our practice administration
