Recruiting Partner — Genossenschaft Migros Aare

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Can we count on your recruiting expertise in the future? As an experienced recruiting partner, Migros Aare offers you a great team and the opportunity to recruit versatile profiles. You take responsibility for the entire recruitment process and bring your expertise to bear in a varied and dynamic environment.

Mansioni

  • Implementation of the entire recruitment process, including job postings, candidate screening, interviews and contract negotiations
  • Ensuring a positive candidate experience through comprehensive support of candidates during the entire application process
  • Build and maintain relationships with internal stakeholders in order to understand the requirements of the positions and to be able to fill them in the best possible way
  • Management of all administrative activities and co-responsibility for the processing of tickets
  • Identification and addressing of qualified candidates via social networks
  • Support for the introduction of a new recruiting tool and active participation in the continuous optimization of recruiting processes
  • Support in employer branding, especially in the definition of HR marketing measures

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of professional experience: as a recruiting partner
  • Completed initial or further training: in the commercial/business sector with further training in HR/Recruiting
  • Communicative and open personality with a high service orientation
  • High self-motivation and own initiative
  • Team-oriented work and ability to work in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Knowledge of SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates on newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
  • Sports and fitness offers: cost-sharing of subscriptions to over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
