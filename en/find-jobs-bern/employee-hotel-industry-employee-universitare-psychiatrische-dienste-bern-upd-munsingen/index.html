COO Münsingen

In the UPZ, we create a place with our restaurant where patients, external guests and employees feel comfortable and like to stay. As part of our hospitality team, your dedicated and hospitable approach will help our guests experience an appreciative environment, a pleasant atmosphere and a balanced, versatile offer.

Their tasks

  • In our self-service restaurant you come into contact with patients, employees and guests every day and can also live out your hosts: interior and service qualities on special occasions.
  • At the checkout you sell fresh products, many of them come from our nursery or are made in our in-house bakery
  • You benefit from family-friendly working hours: Early service begins at 7.15 a.m., late service usually ends at 6.15 p.m.
  • You can expect a dedicated, warm and professional team that is open to your ideas and experiences.
  • You are actively and creatively involved and take on special tasks depending on your interest and abilities
  • With your friendly and attentive behavior you ensure that our guests feel comfortable even during the lively lunch time in the restaurant.
  • From the cash register to the preparation of food to the cooperation on occasions: With us you can expect a varied working day in which you can contribute your versatile skills
  • You will get the opportunity to train yourself professionally with us

Your profile

  • You have an apprenticeship as a restoration specialist or as a specialist in hospitality and ideally bring experience in system catering
  • You are customer- and service-oriented, work independently and keep an overview even in lively situations.
  • You are flexible and willing to work at different times (early, late and weekend services) as well as in other departments of the hotel industry if necessary
  • You have good oral and written knowledge of German

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
