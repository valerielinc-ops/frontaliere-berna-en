Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offers merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Dorfmatt in Münsingen we are looking for a residential area manager in 80-100 % Your tasks

  • You take over the professional and personnel management of the employees and ensure a high quality of the care processes in the department
  • You lead your team with commitment, lead by example and promote motivation and collaboration
  • You support the planning, coordination and organization of everyday care based on our care concept
  • You ensured a smooth flow of information between residents, relatives and the care team
  • You work closely with the management and support them in organizational, administrative and strategic tasks
  • You actively engage in care and take on assigned services, including weekend services
  • You accompany and support learners and trainees in their education and training

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH or professional BP long-term care and care
  • Experience as team management/station management
  • Several years of experience in long-term care
  • Enjoying the leadership, development and motivation of employees as well as working in a team
  • Strong communication and social skills in dealing with residents, relatives and employees
  • You understand Swiss German and speak fluent High German

We offer you

  • An extensive, free training offer via the Senevita Academy for professional and personal development
  • An appreciative corporate culture as well as regular, generous employee events
  • Discounted and balanced food in our in-house gastronomy
  • Attractive perks, e.g. for tanks, supplementary insurance, fitness offers, pharmacies and more
  • Very good social benefits and at least 5 weeks holidays (from 50 years 6 weeks, from 60 years 7 weeks)
