Full description

On behalf of the Canton of Bern, Diaconis’ labour integration offers various labour market measures for the sustainable professional integration of job seekers. One of them is the measure EAR (determination of labour market relevant resources), a location determination and potential assessment for German and French-speaking job seekers. It is aimed at people with an unclear reintegration strategy, if there is a need for clarification on labour market-relevant skills and an assessment of the integration potential for the labour market. The results form an important basis for further professional integration. The assigning authority is the cantonal office for unemployment insurance (RAV). For the management of our new labour market measure EAR we are looking for 1. October 2026 or by appointment at our location Ostermundigen a
