COO Münsingen

You enjoy giving people a good feeling and providing a pleasant atmosphere with your warm nature? As a host on our age psychiatry and dementia unit, you combine service with human proximity: You are close to the patients, have an open ear for their needs and help them feel comfortable and welcome in their daily life. No day is like the other. Between menu orders, snacks and small encounters, you are an important contact person on the station. Together with care, nutritional advice and kitchen, you ensure that the service around the patients works smoothly – and thus become a valuable part of our interprofessional team.

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
