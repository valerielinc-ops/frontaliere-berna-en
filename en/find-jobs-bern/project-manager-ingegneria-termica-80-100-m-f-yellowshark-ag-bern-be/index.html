01 Future employer

For a forward-looking company in the field of building services engineering and renewable energy solutions, we are looking for a dedicated professional to join as a Heating Technology Project Manager. In this role, you will play a central part in planning and implementing modern heating systems and provide customers with expert support throughout the entire project.

You can expect a varied role with a high degree of personal responsibility, direct customer contact and the opportunity to actively contribute to the further development of sustainable energy solutions.

02 Your responsibilities

  • You will be responsible for heating technology projects, from the initial customer consultation through planning and coordination to successful commissioning and handover.
  • In doing so, you will develop suitable solutions together with your customers and provide them with expert advice on modern heating and thermal systems.
  • As the central point of contact, you will coordinate customers, suppliers, installation teams and external specialist partners, ensuring smooth project execution.
  • You will plan and oversee the commissioning of the systems and ensure that completed projects are handed over to customers in a professional manner.
  • You will keep a close eye on schedules, costs and project progress at all times and take early action in the event of deviations.
  • Organizational and administrative tasks such as permits, technical documents and project documentation are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • Through your consulting expertise and professional manner, you will also actively contribute to acquiring new customers and developing existing customer relationships.

04 Your profile

  • A completed vocational apprenticeship as a heating installer, building services planner, or in a comparable technical profession.
  • Additional specialized technical training in building services, heating, or heating technology is an advantage.
  • Professional experience with heat pump systems and ideally initial experience in project management.
  • Technical understanding and a passion for independently organizing and solving complex tasks.
  • A strong customer and service orientation, as well as a confident and professional demeanor.
  • Initiative, reliability, and a structured way of working.
  • A passion for sustainable technologies and the motivation to actively contribute to the further development of modern energy solutions.
