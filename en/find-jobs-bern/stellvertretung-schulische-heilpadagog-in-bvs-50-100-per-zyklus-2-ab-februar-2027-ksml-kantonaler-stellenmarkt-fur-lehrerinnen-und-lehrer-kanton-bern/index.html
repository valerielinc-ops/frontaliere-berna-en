Full description

Portrait “Together for physical, motor and personal development to a self-determined life” The Rossfeld Foundation empowers, accompanies and supports around 450 people with physical, motor and perceptual impairments of all ages in the areas of school, living, daily structure and therapy. Our clients: inside and their needs are in the center. We stand for high service quality, short decision-making paths and dynamic working methods in a changing environment. Rossfeld School Education is the only competence centre in the Canton of Bern that offers schools, residential groups and therapy for children and adolescents with a physical, motor or perceptual impairment in the regular school sector.
