About us The Institute of Anatomy at the University of Bern is part of the Faculty of Medicine and has a teaching mandate in the degree programs in human and dental medicine, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Biomedical Engineering, Biomedical Sciences, and veterinary medicine, as well as in the education and continuing education of specialist and assistant physicians, biomedical engineers, and in paramedical education and continuing education (physiotherapy, laboratory schools, etc.). Ten independent but highly collaborative working groups conduct biomedical research in the fields of didactic morphology, developmental biology and regeneration, Imaging Facility, lung biology, mucociliary clearance, Micro(angio)-CT, microscopic anatomy and structural biology, molecular neuroinfectiology, nanotechnology, and angiogenesis and cancer.
