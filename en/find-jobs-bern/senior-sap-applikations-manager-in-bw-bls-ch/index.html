Full description

BLS relies fully on SAP and therefore fully on you! As Senior SAP Applications Manager: in BW you have an influence on the entire company. Whether on trains, workshops or business processes. Thanks to your work, we manage to get our passengers to their destination safely and on time every day. Your work is everywhere – a great feeling!

This is waiting for you

  • Advise our business units on new SAP Analytics and planning solutions based on SAP Business Warehouse (BW/4HANA), SAP Analytics Cloud and Analysis for Office and implement them in close cooperation with the business units
  • (Partial) management and support of SAP analytics & planning projects over the entire life cycle - from analysis, conception to implementation and operation (front & backend)
  • Model analytics and planning solutions and take on coding tasks
  • Conducting workshops and training
  • Working in interdisciplinary teams

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed studies (FH, university) in computer science, business informatics or comparable studies
  • Several years of profound experience in the conception and implementation of SAP Analytics (BW4, AFO, SAC) and planning solutions (SAC)
  • Very good programming knowledge in ABAP and SQL, knowledge in JavaScript is advantageous
  • Analytical thinking, initiative, communication strength paired with flair for the design of intuitive, modern and visually appealing analytics and planning solutions
  • Flowing German and good English skills in word and writing

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or half-tax subscription
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether at home office or office
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten days of holiday per year and to take a sabbatical of 40 working days every seven years.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.
  • Financial support for external training, a leadership development program and an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? Proud of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
