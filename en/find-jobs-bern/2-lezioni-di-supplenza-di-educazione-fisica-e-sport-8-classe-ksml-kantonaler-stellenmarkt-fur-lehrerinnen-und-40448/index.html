Portrait SCHOOL MAKES NEW!

At the school Regio Koppigen, around 370 pupils from 5 communities are taught, accompanied and supported from cycle 1 to cycle 3. Three school locations are located in Koppigen, one each in Hellsau and in Alchenstorf. The children and adolescents are taught in multi-year classes from kindergarten to 8th grade.

Tasks

  • You take 2 lessons B&S at an 8th grade in Koppigen.
  • They promote young people with a lot of passion and a professional basic attitude in their individual development.

Requirements

  • You have completed a degree at secondary level 1 or equivalent, are about to graduate or have a corresponding specialist diploma.
  • They work independently, network and like to be involved in multi-professional teams.
  • They communicate positively and meet students as well as parents and teachers in an appreciative and solution-oriented manner.

We offer

  • Temporary employment as a deputy until 31.01.2026
  • Classes in a state-of-the-art gym
  • Lessons on Mondays 13.30-15.05
  • Working in an innovative and family school with a lot of heart
  • A motivated, helpful and collaborative team
  • Space for your own ideas and developments
  • An appreciative environment
  • a supportive school and MR management
  • good public transport connection and free parking.
