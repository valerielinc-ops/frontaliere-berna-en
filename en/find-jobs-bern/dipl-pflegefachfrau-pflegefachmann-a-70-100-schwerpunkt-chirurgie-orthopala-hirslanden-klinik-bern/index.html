Place of work: Hirslanden Salem-Spital | Bern Occupation by: by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

You Can Expect This When We Are

  • We are a diverse team of young and old, working part-time and full-time
  • In our department, team spirit is very important!
  • Life is serious enough, so we like to mix a dash of humor
  • For your well-deserved break, we also offer you a delicious «zmorge» and a unique view of the mountains and the city of Bern, as can be seen from the picture book
  • In addition, you can expect attractive benefits (participation in the mobility costs and the fitness subscription, discount in the Salem pharmacy)

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • Your tasks include the assumption and implementation of a high-quality care of patients taking into account our nursing mission statement.
  • You take care of the perception and assessment of care situations, an adequate response in acute situations as well as the careful execution of medical prescriptions
  • You keep a complete and comprehensible documentation (maintenance planning & process)
  • Active participation in projects, participation in working groups and support of the department management and the responsible care in ensuring the quality of care
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the team and exchange with various professional groups / departments
  • Assistance in the care of learners

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional, you have already gained experience in the acute hospital
  • Enjoy the guidance and support of learners/practitioners and interns
  • Good PC knowledge and a clean and careful working method are required
  • You are flexible and are used to irregular working hours in shifts
  • You are capable of teamwork, your desire for knowledge and your self-responsible and empathetic personality round off your profile

For more information, Nadja Friedrich, Head of B1/B2, at T or Amina Prada, HR recruitment partner, at amina.prada@hirslanden.ch is at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that for data protection reasons we cannot consider paper dossiers (will not be returned) nor applications by email. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
