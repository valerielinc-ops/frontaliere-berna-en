Career Starter Signalling 80-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Career starter signalling Bern, 01.04.2027, 80-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Already from day one you take on responsibility in exciting projects. In our inspiring working environment, you and your team can make a big difference and network within the company. Thanks to permanent employment, after completing the Career Starter Program, your course is set for a specialist, project or management career. Career Starter Program: Signalling | SBB The program starts on 01. April 2027 and your commitments should be completed so that you can fully contribute. You can move that.

  • Intensive training: During the 12 months of the programme, you will acquire extensive knowledge in railway safety engineering, including signalling and train safety systems, through various funding agencies, courses and workshops. After that, you are well prepared to start as a specialist:in or project manager:in the domain of security systems.
  • Exciting projects: You are actively involved in projects and take on the first project management in system development, plant planning, risk analysis or safety proof management.
  • Expert knowledge: Through qualified analyses and concepts, you support specialists and line managers and contribute significantly to the successful implementation of demanding and innovative projects.
  • Networks: With your overview, your network and your developed knowledge about the tasks, processes and functions in the entire value chain of the security systems domain, you will create the decision-making basis for your professional development at SBB.

You bring that with you.

  • You are enthusiastic about the railway system and the mobility of the future.
  • You have a fast comprehension and great willingness to learn as well as above-average analytical skills.
  • A winning appearance and team player qualities distinguish you. You are also ready to make a high commitment to common goals and have a high quality and customer orientation.
  • You have a degree in a technical or natural sciences degree (Uni / FH / HF). Your German is so good that you can easily keep up with a German-speaking team. Knowledge of French is a great advantage.
