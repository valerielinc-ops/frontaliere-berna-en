About us

The hospitals fmi AG with over 1,600 employees include the two hospitals Interlaken and Frutigen, the health center in Meiringen, the senior centers in Frutigen and Interlaken, the walk-in clinic and various practices.

The Seniorenpark Frutigen of the hospitals fmi AG offers a home in the three residential groups Niesenblick, Elsigblick and Maiensäss a total of 65 residents, of which 15 places are specifically intended for people with dementia.
