  • You lead demanding business projects within SBB Energy using classic and agile project management methods.
  • You analyze existing planning, construction and operational processes. You develop and describe the use cases of digitalization in processes along the life cycle of traction power systems.
  • You are also responsible for deriving information requirements and data needs from these use cases and providing the necessary tools and resources.
  • The methodical support of those responsible for the construction project and training of employees are also part of your responsibilities.
  • You are the contact person and coordination point in the energy division for the internal BIM competence center SBB. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are a team-oriented personality who enjoys interdisciplinary collaboration. Your analytical, conceptual and networked thinking allows you to develop, organize and coordinate new topics independently.
  • You have already gained experience in the development of use cases in digitalization and the definition of their requirements, as well as in the analysis and modeling of processes.
  • In-depth knowledge of the BIM method and standards as well as a good understanding of planning, construction and project processes in the Swiss construction industry are required for this position.
  • Ideally, you have several years of professional experience in the field of digital planning and construction as well as experience in using it in the execution phase.
  • To perform this role, you have a university degree in civil engineering, civil information technology or a similar field.
