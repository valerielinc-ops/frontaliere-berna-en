Full description

Portrait In the lower Emme school, more than 1000 students are taught at the three locations in Bätterkinderen, Utzenstorf and Wiler by 125 teachers.

At Bätterkinderen, a total of 20 school classes are run from kindergarten to the 9th school year.

Tasks

You will teach the following on Monday afternoon, Tuesday and Thursday in the 3/4b:

  • 2L German
  • 2L Math
  • 4L NMG
  • 2L Music
  • 3L B&S

They work together in a motivated, committed and collaborative team. You benefit from a mentorship and school social work. We also focus on learning guidance/coaching. In addition, we are on the road with "Schulehandel" and work on a company health management for our employees.

If you are a teacher who is passionate about teaching and is equipped with an open and positive attitude, then we look forward to meeting you.

Requirements

Ideally, you have obtained or are on the way to obtaining one of the following diplomas: Bachelor in Primary Education or equivalent diploma

We offer

As part of our team, you have the opportunity to pursue a varied and interesting activity. The Bätterkinden location is easily accessible by public transport. We also offer you free parking directly on the school grounds.

You get a laptop. We work with Microsoft products as well as teacher office and fold.
