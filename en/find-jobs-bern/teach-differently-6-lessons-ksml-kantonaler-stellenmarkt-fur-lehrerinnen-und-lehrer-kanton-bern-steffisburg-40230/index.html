Full description

Portrait The Sunneschyn Steffisburg is a special elementary school with training and living places. The offer includes 14 school classes from

  1. until the 10th year of school, five lunch tables and a day school offer for external students, four boarding accommodation groups and training places in various service areas. Detailed information about the Sunneschyn can be found on our homepage www.sunneschyn.ch.

Tasks

In our middle class b (cycle 2/3) 10 students are currently taught. From 01.01.2027, at the latest 01.02.2027, we are looking for a teacher with curative education or similar additional qualification. The workload is 6 lessons each on Friday. They accompany students with cognitive impairment and behavioral problems. The guidance of an intern is part of the task, as is the individualization and inner differentiation of the lessons. The starting point is the level of development of the child. Orientation in the curriculum 21. Cross-class cooperation is desired.

Requirements

  • Initiative teacher - preferably with curative education (Diplom/Master in School curative education).
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and new challenges.
  • Flexibility and resilience are among their strengths.

We offer

  • Exciting and demanding work in a collegial, interdisciplinary environment with many design possibilities.
  • Contemporary forms of cooperation and file management via Internet platform.
  • Temporary employment according to cantonal guidelines, with an option for renewal.
  • On request, support from experienced and motivated teachers.
