Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

You know the officiant from practice and would like to use your pharmaceutical experience in the future where new ideas for pharmacies arise? At medinform, you have the opportunity to actively shape basic medical care, develop new pharmaceutical services and incorporate your knowledge into innovative training courses. In this way, you contribute to further developing the role of pharmacies in Swiss healthcare. The best of both worlds: You are directly employed by medinform and work in a personal environment with short decision-making paths and plenty of room for manoeuvre. At the same time, medinform is part of the Galenica network and you benefit from the diverse possibilities and a strong network in the Swiss healthcare sector.

You can do that.

  • You will pick up on current developments in basic medical care and use them to develop innovative training and pharmaceutical services
  • You design and supervise a wide range of educational offers with FPH-accredited courses for pharmacists and other employees in pharmacies
  • You prepare scientific findings in an understandable way, create training concepts and develop practical case studies
  • You will gain qualified speakers and work with professionals from various health professions
  • You accompany face-to-face, online and hybrid events professionally as well as organizationally, supervise speakers and participants and bring your expertise yourself as a speaker or during case presentations in front of an audience
  • You plan the educational offers needs-oriented and economical, win sponsors and ensure the professional independence of the further education

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a pharmacy degree and are interested in official pharmacy and basic medical care
  • You enjoy communicating pharmaceutical knowledge in an understandable and practical manner, first experience in further education or adult education is an advantage, but not a requirement
  • You are interested in basic medical care and would like to actively shape the extended tasks of pharmacies
  • You think entrepreneurially, recognize customer needs and implement your projects independently, structured and reliable
  • You communicate openly with professionals, speakers and sponsors and enjoy working in a bilingual team
  • You have very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of French in word and writing, at least at level B2

This is what we offer you

An activity with effect

A versatile field of activity

A dedicated, bilingual team

You can expect a collaborative cooperation in a small company with short decision-making paths and high personal responsibility

Flexible work design

Work location Zurich in walking distance to the main station with the possibility of a partial home office

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-shaping healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
