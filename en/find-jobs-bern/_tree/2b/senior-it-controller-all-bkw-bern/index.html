Full description

Shape how Group IT is financially managed. You will be responsible for budget, service billing and reporting and develop IT controlling with international standards, automation and AI. This way you create transparency for informed decisions.

Your field of activity

  • You coordinate Group IT revenue, volume and service budgets and coordinate them with internal customers:
  • You prepare the periodic billing of the IT services and ensure a complete, correct and traceable processing
  • With reports as well as revenue, cost and volume analyses you create transparency for management and control
  • Service owners and executives advise you on budget, service prices and billing and combine finance and IT service management
  • Budgeting, forecasting, calculation, accounting and reporting standards you develop continuously and in projects
  • You are involved in the introduction of IT controlling standards (e.g. technology business management) as well as automation, digitalization and the targeted use of AI in IT controlling.

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in IT controlling
  • Practice in IT cost accounting and a good understanding of the financial management of IT services
  • Experience in the use of AI tools and the technology affinity to think ahead with existing processes
  • You work independently, proactively and consistently and keep the focus even with resistance
  • With a secure appearance and clear communication, you build bridges between different stakeholders
  • Very good German and English skills
