Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Demand and consumption analyses: Conducting demand and consumption analyses to optimise material disposition and procurement strategies, taking into account trends, seasonalities and plan-actual deviations
  • Replacement times and stockpiling strategies: monitoring and optimisation of replacement times, stockpiling, reporting and security inventories in the ERP system, taking into account material criticality, availability and cost-effectiveness
  • ERP system maintenance and scheduling parameters: maintenance and continuous optimization of material master data, scheduling parameters and batch sizes in the ERP system as well as checking and ensuring the plausibility of order proposals generated from them

Over the area

The range of services offered by the Tracked Vehicles business segment focuses on the provision of services for Swiss Army tracked vehicles as well as international customers.

In the context of business cases, the operational readiness of the Swiss Army systems with our competence in the areas of project planning, maintenance and system support is primarily ensured.

Whether the customer commissions an extension of use, the new development of solutions for the benefit of the platforms or the maintenance of the systems or subsystems at RUAG, our focus is on costs, quality and adherence to deadlines.

With our expertise in order & contract management, integrated logistics and system support, we are at the disposal of our customers to provide the required services.

As a dispatcher in Maintenance & Material Control, you ensure continuous material availability through system-supported planning and control. This includes the analysis of consumption and demand data, the management of replacement times, reporting and security stocks and the proactive management of warehouse management. A central task is also the management of defect assemblies to ensure maintenance processes. The role is closely linked to work preparation (AVOR) and purchasing and is in direct contact with project and system managers.
