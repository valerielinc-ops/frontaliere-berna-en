Full description

Are you enthusiastic about application development and would like to pass on your knowledge? In Swiss Post’s vocational training, you combine your passion for computer science with a meaningful task: you accompany young people as they enter their professional lives and shape the next generation of computer scientists. With you, we create space for new ideas, independent learning and personal development.You can expect a varied job in a dynamic training environment with a committed vocational training team. You will gain freedom to further develop basic training technically and methodically and benefit from attractive employment conditions and flexible work opportunities.

You can do that.

  • You design and accompany the basic training of our computer science learners in the field of application development.
  • You plan and lead practice-oriented training sequences and modules and impart current expertise in software and application development.
  • You accompany the learners in their professional and personal development, promote their independence and create a learning environment in which they can try new things, take on responsibility and grow on their tasks.
  • Together with the vocational training team, you will develop the training offers further in a technical and methodical way. You deal with modern technologies and new forms of learning and bring your own ideas.
  • You prepare the learners for their further training path and the demands of the professional world.

You bring that with you

  • You have in-depth training and several years of professional experience in computer science in the field of application development. You know the latest technologies, methods and working methods of software development and develop your expertise continuously.
  • Ideally, you will know the everyday training of computer science learners and the environment of a basic education from your own experience.
  • Vocational training is important to you. You enjoy passing on your knowledge to young people in an understandable, practical and motivating way.
  • With patience, empathy and high social skills, you promote young people individually and at the same time formulate clear expectations.
  • Training as a trainer: at the level of SVEB for ÜK leaders or a comparable qualification is an advantage. Alternatively, you are ready to acquire this qualification as part of your activity.
  • You enjoy working in a team, taking on responsibility and bringing your own ideas. In addition, you act independently and structured and convince with strong communication and organizational skills.
