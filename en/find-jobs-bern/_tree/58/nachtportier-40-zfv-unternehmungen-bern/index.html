Full description

Welcome to the capital of Switzerland. Centrally located in the heart of the city, the 3* Hotel Ador is a central place of work that offers plenty of space for personal development and your own ideas. Private and business travellers from all over the world stay here in the 59 rooms, linger in the café-bar-lounge or meet in the 17 meeting rooms. With state-of-the-art technical equipment in the meeting rooms, the hotel offers a wide range of options for events. Our team takes care of unique experiences and unforgettable stays. Are you our new team member who will support us as a night porter (40%) as of 01 December 2026 or by appointment? You usually work the night shift between Monday and Sunday from 10:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. Your task menu:

  • Ensuring the process at the reception
  • Reception, care and farewell of hotel guests
  • Check-in and check-out of hotel guests
  • Operation of the telephone and information center
  • Acceptance and processing of incoming reservations
  • Implementation of professional complaint management
  • Completion of various administrative tasks
  • Completion of general tidying and cleaning tasks
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the working environment
  • Perception of the host role
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Responsible for the safety of guests and operations
  • Telephone support for guests of the sister hotel Hotel Arabelle
  • Processing of daily closings
  • Creation of the mise en place for breakfast Your recipe for success:
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in the hotel industry or in the service sector
  • Very good oral and written German and English skills
  • Engaging and charming
  • Willing to learn and customer-oriented
  • Service-oriented
  • Flexible and solution-oriented
  • Structured and organised What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are promoted. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 52,000 and CHF 60,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
