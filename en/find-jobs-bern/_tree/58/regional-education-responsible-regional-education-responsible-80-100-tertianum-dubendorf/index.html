Full description

Following the retirement of our long-standing incumbent, we are looking to fill a position by mutual agreement for the regions of Bern, Aargau, Solothurn and Basel:

Regional Education Officer 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • Ensuring, supporting and further developing the quality of training at the assigned businesses
  • Conducting site visits and advising and supporting vocational trainers, education officers and managers on training matters (nursing and other professional fields)
  • Training and, where needed, providing intensive support to vocational education officers and trainers at the sites
  • Supporting apprentices and students in challenging situations and collaborating with internal key contacts, schools and education authorities
  • Planning and conducting training sessions and knowledge-sharing events in the field of vocational education and training, attending apprentice events and contributing to regional career fairs
  • Contributing to projects to further develop the training of apprentices and students within Tertianum

Your profile

  • Completed training as a registered nurse (HF) or equivalent, and extensive experience in vocational education and training
  • Current or completed qualification at SVEB 2 level or equivalent
  • Strong advisory, coaching and communication skills, and a passion for supporting people in their development
  • Strong interpersonal skills, resilience and the ability to quickly adapt to new teams and structures
  • Enjoyment of a varied role involving regular travel within the assigned region (GA pass provided)
  • Very good German skills; ideally, good to very good spoken and written French skills

Why Tertianum? It is important to us that our employees can maintain a good work-life balance. This includes a wide range of benefits, e.g.:

  • Flexible working arrangements combining site visits and working from home
  • 6 weeks of vacation
  • Internal and external professional development opportunities
  • An additional day off during your birthday month
  • Daily lunch for CHF 10
  • Discounted mobile and internet plans with Sunrise
  • Discounts on selected supplementary health insurance policies
  • Various fleet discounts

You can find more benefits on our website .

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
