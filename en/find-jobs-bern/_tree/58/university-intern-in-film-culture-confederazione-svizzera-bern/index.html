Full description

Put to the Point

They support the Director of Film Culture in supervising the film cultural institutions with which the BAK has concluded a service agreement. This includes evaluating dossiers, preparing and following up meetings and keeping minutes. They independently prepare dossiers on film festivals, actively participate in the organisation of the Swiss Film Award and support the service Film Culture in Communication.

You can make this contribution

  • Manage a wide range of film culture dossiers (funding and controlling film festivals, mediation and film publications)
  • Organising meetings, minutes, coordination with all parties involved
  • Participating in the organisation and implementation of the Swiss Film Award
  • Provide information orally and in writing as well as prepare external communication (e.g. editorial newsletter)
  • Manage document storage
  • Edit dossiers mainly in German and French, coordinate translations

  • Strong ability in oral and written communication
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and passive knowledge of the third official language as well as good knowledge of English are beneficial

HR BAK NB

Live the culture!

The Federal Office for Culture (BAK) is the specialist authority for fundamental cultural policy issues, for the promotion of culture and for the preservation and mediation of cultural values.

The Film Funding Area promotes the independent production and evaluation of Swiss films as well as co-productions with foreign countries. It also promotes film culture in Switzerland (film festivals, film publications, further education, film promotion, etc.) as well as the Swiss Film Archive.

The offices of the Federal Department of the Interior (EDI) are systematically committed to good working conditions and the health of their employees and are awarded the label Friendly Work Space.
