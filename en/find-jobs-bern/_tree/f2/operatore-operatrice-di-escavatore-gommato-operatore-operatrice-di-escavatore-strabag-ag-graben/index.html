Full description

At STRABAG, around 89,000 people are making progress at more than 2,400 locations worldwide. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterize not only our projects, but also each and every one of us. Whether in construction and engineering, road and civil engineering, bridge and tunnel construction, in building materials production, project development or building management - we continue to think about construction: innovation, digitalization and sustainability are central points on our way to building tomorrow. STRABAG is pursuing a clear sustainability strategy. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are just as integral parts of what makes us a company and how we work. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow in new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's make progress!

Pneubaggerführer:in / Baggerführer:in Your contribution with us

  • You serve modern excavators and, if necessary, other construction machinery in road and civil engineering
  • You carry out excavation, digging, laying and loading work precisely and safely
  • You actively support your team on the construction site and if necessary also outside the cabin
  • You prepare your machine independently, check it before every use and use attachments professionally
  • You take care of the maintenance, care and value of your excavator
  • You think along, recognize work processes early and contribute to an efficient construction site process
  • You work consistently according to our safety, quality and environmental standards

What matters to us

  • You have practical experience as a Pneubaggerführer:in or as a crawler excavatorführer:in with the aim of switching to the Pneubagger
  • A training in road, underground or road construction is advantageous, but not mandatory. Crucial are your technical understanding, your machine feeling and your motivation
  • You are enthusiastic about large machines, precise work and changing construction site deployments
  • You work reliably, independently and take responsibility for your machine and your work
  • Safety, quality and a clean handling of the machine are a matter of course for you
  • You are team-oriented, ready to work and keep an overview even on challenging construction sites
  • You have the driving licence category B. The M1/M2 is an advantage or you are ready to purchase it
  • You can communicate well in German

Our added value for you

  • You can expect a modern machine park with efficient pneubaggers, the latest technology and modern attachments
  • You work on varied road and civil engineering construction sites and take responsibility for your machine
  • A strong and collaborative team supports you and shares its knowledge with you
  • You will receive a thorough training on the pneubagger by experienced machinists: inside and superiors
  • With suitable training and development opportunities, you can expand your know-how and take on additional responsibility
  • Attractive employment conditions and short decision-making processes round off our offer

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
