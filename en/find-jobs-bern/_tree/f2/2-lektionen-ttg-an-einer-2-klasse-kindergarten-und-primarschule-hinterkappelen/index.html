Full description

Portrait The Hinterkappelen Primary School is an urban suburban school. Hinterkappelen is a district of the community Wohlen. Our school facility is located in the middle of the Kappelenring, in a traffic-free zone and is optimally connected to public transport. Our lively school currently houses a total of 16 classes, two per school year and four kindergartens. The college comprises about 50 teachers. On the school grounds are a day school, sports halls, a football field and a play and basketball court - of course accessible to all children.

Tasks

For a great 2 class of our primary school we are looking for a dedicated teacher who enjoys teaching and enriches our team. You like to design with children, promote their creativity and accompany them with pleasure in discovering materials and craft techniques. Whether your focus is on technical or textile design – both are possible. The appointment will take place from 1 December 2027 or by agreement. They design a varied and differentiated teaching according to curriculum 21, cultivate a warm and appreciative approach to the children and appreciate the cooperation with parents and college.

Have you become curious? We look forward to hearing from you!

Requirements

You are a qualified teacher (Bachelor Primary Education/seminary patent) or in training, with a positive attitude towards the students, guardians and colleagues. They are ready to support and further develop the existing school building structure and look forward to working together in the class and step team as well as in the entire college.

We offer

We offer a temporary or permanent position at our diverse primary school, where you can contribute your values and count on good cooperation in the step team, in the college, with specialists, school social work and co-management. We also offer you a mentorship. Good infrastructure and excellent connections to public transport, as well as free parking are further advantages of this attractive location. Interested? We look forward to your application!
