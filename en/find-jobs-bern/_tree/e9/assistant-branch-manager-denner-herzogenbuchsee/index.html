Full description

Work location: Denner AG, Branch 352 Herzogenbuchsee Bernstrasse, Bernstrasse 33, 3360 Herzogenbuchsee

Would you like to take charge for the day from time to time? But you're not ready to be a deputy manager yet? Here you can show what you can do. Pass on your knowledge. Gain leadership experience. And, of course, learn a great deal. Denner. This is our store.

Important notes

Career changers and people returning to work are welcome.

Duties

  • Pay special attention to the freshness of fruit, vegetables and bread, and fight food waste with well-planned orders
  • Order, receive and check fresh products—and, of course, all other goods—and stock the shelves in your store
  • Implement store layout guidelines correctly
  • Maintain order and cleanliness in the branch and warehouse
  • Operate and balance the till
  • As an assistant, guide, support and help employees develop—and set a good example for them

Requirements

  • Ideally, more than two years of work experience in food retail
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EBA): in food retail or a related occupation would be ideal
  • Enjoy working with fresh products and, ideally, already have professional experience with them
  • Willing to work outside the usual “nine to five” hours from time to time—and enjoy your free time when others are working
  • Physically fit; some boxes weigh up to 10 kilos
  • Enjoy being around people, with a good dose of warmth and a positive attitude
  • German (very good proficiency)

What we offer

  • Part of the Migros Group: A strong brand rooted in retail under the umbrella of the Migros Group
  • Healthy working conditions: High standards for occupational safety, ergonomics and hygiene.
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 5 extra weeks of parental leave (19 weeks) for mothers at 100% pay. Fathers enjoy 4 paid weeks with their new arrival.
  • Fair pay: 13 monthly salaries and regular salary increases for everyone instead of bonuses for a few.
  • Mobile phone plan: The choice is yours: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise, stay well connected from just CHF 10.-.
  • Migros Bank: Get better interest rates and fees on your personal, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Employee benefits: 10 percent off your next big shop (twice a year).
  • Vacation: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone, and even more days for work anniversaries.
  • Leisure and cultural activities: Various discounts on car rentals, hotels, events and much more.
  • Targeted development: From dishwasher to millionaire? Maybe not quite, but anyone with drive and talent can progress quickly.

Contact

Application & Contact Fabienne Contini Sales Recruiter, Swiss Plateau

Recruitment process

  • Initial application screening: We review applications on an ongoing basis, create a shortlist and share it with the manager for review. The decision on whether you proceed or are removed from the recruitment process will be communicated by phone or email. Time until response: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or phone interview: If we receive a large number of applications, we may hold a personal phone or video interview. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates better in a short time and invite suitable people to the next round. Time until response: Up to one week
  • Trial shift: A trial day or half-day gives you the chance to get to know your future work environment and team members better. It also gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Time until response: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & contract issuance: After our verbal job offer, the key contract terms are verified once again. Once agreed, the contract is prepared and sent by post. Time until response: Up to three weeks

Note for recruitment agencies For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Employment percentage: 80-100%

Requirements

  • Idealerweise mehr als zwei Jahre Berufserfahrung: im Detailhandel (Lebensmittel)

  • Abgeschlossene Berufslehre (EBA): im Detailhandel (Lebensmittel) oder verwandter Berufsgattung wäre ideal

  • Freude an frischen Produkten und im Idealfall schon berufliche Erfahrungen damit

  • Bereit, schon auch mal jenseits von «nine to five» zu arbeiten – und die Freizeit zu geniessen, wenn andere arbeiten

  • Körperlich fit, manche Colis sind bis zu 10 Kilo schwer

  • Gerne unter Menschen, eine gute Portion Herzlichkeit und eine positive Grundhaltung

  • Deutsch (sehr gute Kenntnisse)

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
