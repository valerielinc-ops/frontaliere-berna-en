Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You develop cross-channel marketing campaigns for the B2B market (gastronomy) from target group needs and given marketing goals
  • You take ownership of your projects and proactively drive them forward. You clarify the task, plan and control tasks, deadlines, budgets and resources and ensure clear responsibilities and binding decisions
  • You are responsible for the brand management of our own brands. In addition, you support the introduction of private label products
  • You campaign based on defined KPIs and derive optimizations
  • You work in cross-divisional projects of the marketing team

Requirements

  • You have profound experience in the development of cross-channel marketing concepts and can derive clear goals, relevant messages and coordinated measures from a task and convincingly justify your recommendations.
  • You have managed marketing projects independently and have strong project management skills (planning, control, implementation, coordination of different stakeholders)
  • Business Administration studies or further training in marketing desired
  • Good creative and marketing understanding
  • High sense of responsibility with a can-do mentality
  • Very good and stylish knowledge of German in word and writing is a must
  • Affinity for gastronomy and enjoyment of cuisine

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
