Full description

University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Biel Psychiatry offers comprehensive psychiatric care. At the crisis intervention station, we support you in acute emergency situations. In the ambulatorium, you will receive competent, timely and individual treatment after an initial consultation.

Their tasks

  • You will gain insight into the interdisciplinary work with inpatients
  • They are involved in conducting psychological questionnaire surveys
  • They support the inpatient admission, diagnosis and therapy planning of patients
  • They participate in group therapies and participate in their design and management
  • They take part in discussions with inpatient patients and, if necessary, take on individual tasks, for example as part of the medical history
  • They look after patients in various activities, such as walks outside the clinic
  • You will be accompanied by a psychologist and carefully introduced to your tasks by the team

Your profile

  • You have an advanced degree in psychology (Master's degree)
  • You are an independent, curious and initiative personality
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team and in a multicultural environment
  • You are interested in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You have German as your mother tongue and have good oral French skills

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
