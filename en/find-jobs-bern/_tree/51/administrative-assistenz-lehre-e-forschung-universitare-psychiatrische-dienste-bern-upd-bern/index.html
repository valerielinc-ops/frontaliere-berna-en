University Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Behind every successful study are people who not only advance scientific findings, but also ensure that everything works smoothly in the background. At the University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy, you will support a dedicated research team in gaining new insights into mental health. With your organizational talent, your eye for detail and your enjoyment of versatile tasks, you create the administrative foundations to make innovative research possible. You can expect a varied activity at the interface of science, university and clinical practice.

Your tasks

  • You are the central contact person for adult psychiatry researchers
  • You are the interface between clinic and research in all administrative matters
  • You are responsible for the administrative handling of research positions – from personnel planning and recruitment to retirement
  • You carry out absentee and time checks and support with personnel administrative questions
  • You will manage operating and third-party loans and coordinate the debtor and creditor workflow in the university systems
  • You manage the cost centers of research adult psychiatry and support in financial processes
  • You contribute to the organization and coordination of events, meetings and other events
  • You ensure efficient administrative processes and contribute to professional support of research operations

Your profile

  • You have a completed commercial education or an equivalent qualification
  • You bring several years of professional experience, ideally in university or administrative environment
  • You work safely with MS Office and ideally have knowledge of SAP or comparable systems
  • You convince through your high service orientation as well as your professional and social skills
  • You organize your tasks independently, structured and reliable
  • You work precisely and keep track of different requirements
  • You appreciate team collaboration, are flexible and tackle challenges in a solution-oriented way
  • You have very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English in word and writing

The UPZ

The University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
