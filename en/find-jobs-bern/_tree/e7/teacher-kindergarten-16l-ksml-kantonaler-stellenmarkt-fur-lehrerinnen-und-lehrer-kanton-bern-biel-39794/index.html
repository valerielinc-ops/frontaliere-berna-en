Full description

Portrait The primary school Mühlefeld comprises 17 classes, including 5 kindergarten classes. Kindergarten Erlacherweg is beautifully located in the Mühlefeld quarter. It was freshly renovated and relocated by summer 26. In the same building there is also a French-speaking kindergarten class. The garden and parts of the kindergarten are used together. As a German-speaking team, we are well connected and work closely together at the stage. We teach diversely with emphasis on the Churer model. Nonviolent communication (after Rosenberg) is important to us. We are supported by a school social worker.

Our students attend a bilingual day school.

Tasks

They teach in kindergarten Erlacherweg. Mutual support and cooperation are paramount. The working days are Monday and Tuesday morning.

In addition to the class teacher, a specialist teacher (1Nami lesson, 5L DAZ and 4L teamteach) and an IF teacher (2L / week),

Requirements

You have training as a teacher or are in training. They are motivated to work in a heterogeneous environment. We look forward to an experienced and committed teacher.

We offer

Good support from an ingenious college with established forms of cooperation. We work with nonviolent communication, the Churer model and cooperative forms of learning. Good accessibility: 7 minutes by bus from Biel station, 5 minutes by bicycle or 20 minutes on foot.

We offer variety and challenge at a lively school. Cooperation is spontaneous and close. Individual further education as well as an interdisciplinary exchange are part of this with us. In addition to a permanent position, we also offer:

  • Digitally equipped classrooms and personal laptop
  • Attractive working hours
  • Attractive place of work
  • Mentorship
  • School curative teachers on site
  • well-functioning school house team with trained class teachers
  • Working in step groups
  • Support from school management
  • Support from school social worker
  • Communication with parents with KLAPP

The city of Biel offers various discounts (indoor swimming pool and fitness subscription, rail check, ...)
