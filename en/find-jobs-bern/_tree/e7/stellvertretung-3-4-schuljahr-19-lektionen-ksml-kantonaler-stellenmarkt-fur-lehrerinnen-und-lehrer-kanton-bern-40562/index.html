Full description

Portrait From Bern, Wohlen is easily accessible by post bus. If you like it close to nature and sporty you take the bike. A beautiful cycling path leads from the Länggasse to the Wohlensee. Our schoolhouse is centrally located in the village with a view of the lake and the Alpine chain.

We lead the school entrance level into basic levels, the subsequent school years into two multi-year classes. We attach great importance to the social competence of our students. Conflicts are for us ways to prepare children for life. Diversity gives us color. The guiding principle 26/27 is: "Get out into learning", we implement it consistently.

Tasks

For our 3/4. A class we are looking for a cheerful and upstanding personality for the 2nd semester (1.2. - 31.7.27) as a deputy class teacher. Your assignment days are from Monday to Thursday, with 3 lessons planned on Wednesday and Thursday. One of these is a lesson in team teaching.

  • You teach the subjects M, D, NMG, F, textile works, sports and 1 lesson BG.
  • You take over the structure of the class leadership as best you can.
  • The 17 school children are looking forward to you.

Requirements

  • You have a degree and a teaching diploma for Cycle 2, or bring experience for Level 3/4.
  • You are sensitive, clear in class leadership and flexible.

We offer

Our school facility is well maintained and has a modern infrastructure. The teaching team in Wohlen is the best college far around, you can look forward to that. With us, it is possible to be creative and to help shape everyday school life. The conferences take place every 4-5 weeks on Wednesday afternoon and are a creative source of collaboration and quality assurance. Our schoolchildren come from educational homes and are well looked after.
