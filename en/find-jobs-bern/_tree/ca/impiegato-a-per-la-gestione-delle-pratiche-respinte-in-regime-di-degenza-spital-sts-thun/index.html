Full description

You check and process incoming insurance inquiries and maintain the corresponding correspondence with insurers and other cost carriers. In addition, you check completed stationary cases for completeness of the documentation and request missing documents from the responsible internal secretariats if necessary. You work closely with medical coding and patient billing. In addition, you support the medical coding both administratively and professionally and thus contribute to a smooth process.
