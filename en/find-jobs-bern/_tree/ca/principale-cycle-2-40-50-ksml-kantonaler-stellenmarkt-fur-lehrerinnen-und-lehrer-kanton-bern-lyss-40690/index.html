Portrait In the municipality of Lyss with over 16,000 inhabitants, around 1,920 students are taught in 4 school locations.

Tasks

As part of the vocational assignment, together with two other school management, you will take over the professional, organizational and personnel management of the Grentschel School. You are a member of the school management conference Lyss and are under the technical and personnel of the department management Education + Culture. They are responsible for the implementation of cantonal and municipal requirements and for cross-sectoral cooperation with the municipality. An important part of the task involves the further development of the school and the quality of teaching, as well as the advice and support of teachers and guardians in the event of disagreements. A teaching activity is conceivable to increase the level of employment.
