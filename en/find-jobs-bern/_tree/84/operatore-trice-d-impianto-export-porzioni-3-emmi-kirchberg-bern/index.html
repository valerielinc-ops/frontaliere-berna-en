Full description

Become part of our cheese crew - Pack enjoyment with passion! For us, everything revolves around fine cheese specialties - and people who with heart and hand ensure that they arrive perfectly with our customers worldwide.

You can do that.

  • You operate and monitor the production plant for our cheese production 🧀 and perform the necessary cleaning and maintenance work dutifully. ✔️
  • Due to your organized and exact working method, you always keep an overview of the documentation of the shift sequence as well as the safety and hygiene regulations. 📋
  • You take over the leadership and support of the assigned line employees and guide them. 👥
  • Your technical understanding helps you to process and bill the production orders properly. 💻
  • You actively contribute ideas and suggestions for improvement. Happiness

You bring that with you

  • You have completed vocational training as a plant manager: in or in the food environment (cheeser:in, butcher:in, baker:in, etc.) 🥐🍗 , at best with associated professional experience.
  • Your technical flair for machines and goods management systems (SAP) makes your day-to-day work easier. ⚙️
  • You bring a high understanding of hygiene and safety measures 🧼 .
  • You are flexible in terms of working hours (2-3 shift) and mobile. 🏎️
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German (min. B2).

This is what we offer you

  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Further education and development opportunities: Emmi focuses on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
