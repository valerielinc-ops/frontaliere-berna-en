The General Secretariat EFD (GS-EFD) is the central staff body of the department with the core areas of staff, communication, resources, legal service, digitalization/ICT and the delegates for multilingualism of the Confederation as well as the Digital Administration Switzerland (DVS). The Swiss Digital Administration (DVS) is affiliated to the General Secretariat of the Swiss Federal Department of Finance (EFD). DVS coordinates and manages the digital transformation between the federal government, cantons and municipalities, develops the strategy for digital administration and is committed to the expansion of basic services and the dissemination of electronic services. It promotes the standardisation and harmonisation of technical and professional processes, sets up working groups in which experts from administration, business, academia and civil society participate and networks with related organisations.
