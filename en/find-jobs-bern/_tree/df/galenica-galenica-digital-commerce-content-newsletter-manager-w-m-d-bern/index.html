Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

You connect e-commerce with good content and know how a newsletter becomes a successful sales channel? With us, you take responsibility for the newsletter from the first idea to the evaluation and develop digital content along the customer journey. You combine creativity with a clear view of performance and commercial goals.

You can do that.

  • You are responsible for our newsletter channel from A to Z: from topic and campaign planning to text, briefing and implementation to shipping, testing and evaluation.
  • You develop high-selling newsletters and e-commerce content, continuously optimize them based on relevant KPIs and thus strengthen conversion, sales and customer loyalty.
  • You plan and create content for landing pages, brand pages, and category pages, ensuring a consistent customer journey across digital touchpoints.
  • You coordinate internal and external stakeholders and ensure that campaigns convince in terms of content, visual and commercial.
  • Together with industry partners, you will develop retail media formats and digital advertising solutions and bring their campaigns effectively to our digital sales areas.
  • You analyze the performance of your measures, derive concrete optimizations and continuously develop newsletters and e-commerce content.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of professional experience in e-commerce, newsletter/e-mail marketing or digital marketing and have already managed a newsletter channel independently.
  • You can implement newsletters holistically from topic planning and text to coordination and dispatch to analysis and optimization.
  • You have a strong sense of sales-oriented content and can formulate complex messages clearly, attractively and in line with target groups.
  • You understand customer journeys, conversion optimization, and relevant e-commerce KPIs and use data to continuously improve your actions.
  • You have a completed degree in business administration, e-commerce, marketing or a comparable education and bring the corresponding practical experience.
  • You communicate negotiably in German, very well in English and ideally also in French.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
