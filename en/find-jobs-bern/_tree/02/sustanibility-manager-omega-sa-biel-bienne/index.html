Full description

Introduction of society

OMEGA is a prestigious watch brand worldwide known for its quality, precision and innovation requirements for over 170 years. Our watches are among the most beautiful in the world and are characterized by their state-of-the-art technology and timeless style.

Over the course of our long history, we have broken records of precision, redefined sporting timing and maintained our position at the cutting edge of Haute Horlogerie.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams on almost every continent with their talents, ambitions and inspirations.

Description of post

  • You define, coordinate and develop the sustainability strategy of OMEGA SA and TISSOT SA and the resulting projects
  • You pilot decarbonisation initiatives, monitor the carbon balance and analyse key performance indicators
  • You develop a true culture of sustainability through engaging awareness, training and communication
  • You support the various departments in achieving their objectives and coordinate the CSR bodies
  • You encourage innovation through eco-design and continuous improvement projects to reduce our environmental impact

Profile

  • You have a graduate degree (Master) in sustainable development, environment, energy, CSR or equivalent
  • Training supplemented by an AEAI degree in fire safety.
  • You benefit from a proven experience in sustainable development, compliance, responsible procurement or environmental project management
  • You have a solid knowledge of sustainability reporting standards, regulations and requirements

Qualifications

  • Excellent communication and cross-cutting project management skills
  • A developed spirit of initiative, strong autonomy and the ability to unite teams around common objectives

Languages

  • French as a mother tongue and good knowledge of English and German

Requirements

  • Vous êtes titulaire d’un diplôme universitaire supérieur (Master) en développement durable, environnement, énergie, RSE ou domaine équivalent

  • Formation complétée par un diplôme AEAI de chargé de sécurité en protection incendie.

  • Vous êtes au bénéfice d’une expérience confirmée dans le développement durable, la conformité, les achats responsables ou la gestion de projets environnementaux

  • Vous avez une solide connaissance des normes, réglementations et exigences de reporting en matière de durabilité

  • D’excellentes compétences en communication et en gestion de projets transversaux

  • Un esprit d’initiative développé, une forte autonomie et la capacité de fédérer des équipes autour d’objectifs communs

  • Le français comme langue maternelle ainsi que de bonnes connaissances de l’anglais et de l’allemand
