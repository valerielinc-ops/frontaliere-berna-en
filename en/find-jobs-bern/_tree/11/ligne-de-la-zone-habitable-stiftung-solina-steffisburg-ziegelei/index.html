More than Care Quality Starts with You

Residential management cadre from 80% Steffisburg Ziegelei from January 2027 or by agreement We offer adult people in need of support a perspective in an environment where they feel secure and where they are cared for individually and professionally. We live appreciation, promote team spirit and celebrate our cohesion – with team events, personal parties and joint excursions. With our two pool models, we create flexibility in operational planning and relieve permanent employees. So that free days really remain free. You have the potential, we clear the way. With an above-average internal training program and the opportunity to take on additional functions, we support your professional development. Thanks to our open feedback culture, you always stay one step ahead: helping to shape instead of working. Discover all the advantages

How you work

  • Management and organisation of a living area with 24 residents

  • Responsibility for the implementation of self-image and leadership understanding
  • Budget responsibility
  • Further development of the caring process

What makes you

  • Professional and leadership experience in similar institutions
  • Further training in the field of leadership advantage or willingness to complete it
  • You have a solution-oriented, appreciative and empathetic approach to people and are able to motivate the team
  • Decisiveness, innovativeness, joy of living and contributing skills
