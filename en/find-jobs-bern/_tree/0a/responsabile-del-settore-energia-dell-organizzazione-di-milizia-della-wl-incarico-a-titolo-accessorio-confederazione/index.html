Put to the Point

They lead the energy department and ensure the supply of the country with fossil fuels, electricity, energy wood and drinking water in severe shortages.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21207

You can make this contribution

  • Assessment of the supply situation and securing the achievement of the supply targets together with the departments and the office
  • preparation, application and monitoring of the necessary preparation and implementation measures in the event of a serious deficiency
  • Responsibility for their effectiveness
  • Implementation of the energy management strategy together with the departments and the office
  • Coordination of the work of and maintenance of contacts with experts from business, science and administration

This makes them unique.

  • In-depth expertise, experience and a broad network in the field of energy, experience in electricity are beneficial
  • Safe appearance and above-average social and communication skills
  • Several years of leadership experience
  • High resilience and assertiveness, experience in security of supply and crisis management beneficial
  • Good knowledge of a second official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

We ensure supply in case of shortages

The Federal Office for Economic Affairs is the national competence centre for questions of security of supply. In the event of shortages, it supports the private sector in securing the country’s supply of essential goods and services.

Additional information

Entry as of January 1, 2027 or by arrangement. The post shall be filled in an ancillary office.
