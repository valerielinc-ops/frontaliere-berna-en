As part of the State Secretariat, the Department of Technology and Prosperity (ATEC) supports the departmental management of the FDFA and the Federal Council in integrating sectoral issues into foreign policy. This applies in particular to finance, the economy, the environment, health, energy, science and technology. It thus contributes to a coherent, sectorally coordinated Swiss foreign policy. ATEC is further developing science and technology diplomacy for Switzerland. It anticipates the impact of technologies, creates new initiatives through partnerships and promotes a trusting approach to technologies and their effects. Together with the UN department and other stakeholders, it bundles actions to strengthen the International Geneva as a hub for digital governance and promotes its exchange with Switzerland as a technology hub. In the preparation of the Geneva AI Summit 2027, ATEC is responsible for the work area "International Relations and Diplomacy". Within the Federal Administration, ATEC ensures efficient, impact-oriented cooperation and an open dialogue between the FDFA and the responsible agencies. It uses the 2030 Agenda to link the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) with the other foreign policy goals across sectors. It is responsible for coordinating the fight against corruption and implements Switzerland’s maritime strategy. In international committees, ATEC represents Switzerland on topics such as money laundering and terrorist financing, corruption, climate, biodiversity, space and polar regions (Arctic/Antarctic).
