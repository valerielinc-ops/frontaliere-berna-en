Connect, control – for the security of Switzerland.

The General Secretariat of the VBS is the department’s own staff organisation. It supports the head of the department as a member of the Federal Council and in the management of the department. The GS-VBS develops the political strategy and guidelines for the administrative units and supports and controls their implementation. Furthermore, it ensures planning, controlling and coordination at departmental level in the cross-sectional areas of personnel, finance, law and computer science, but also in security policy or in the area and environment.
