Full description

Portrait The Rubigen Primary School is a school in the agglomeration of Bern with modern infrastructure and very good public transport connection. The two kindergartens, the two basic level classes and the 6 primary classes from 1 to 6. School year are located on a spacious school area in the immediate vicinity of nature. We also run a day school directly in the schoolhouse. Further information can be found at www.schule-rubigen.ch

Take responsibility and actively shape our school day! Due to an illness-related failure of the school management, we are looking for a committed, initiative and experienced leader as

Tasks

During the illness-related absence of the school management, you take over the operational management of our school with a total of ten classes in cycle 1 and 2 (two kindergarten classes, two basic levels and six primary classes).

Your responsibilities include in particular

  • 75% employment as school management, 17% school pool
  • Educational, personnel and organisational management of the school
  • Leadership, accompaniment and support of teachers and other pedagogical staff
  • Monitoring school and teaching development
  • Responsibility for school organisation and annual and breakdown planning
  • Promoting constructive cooperation within the team
  • Cooperation with parents, the Commission Education, Youth and Sport, the School Secretariat and specialised bodies
  • Responsibility for transparent and reliable communication
  • Ensuring cooperation with the day school

Requirements

  • Knowledge of the Bernese education system
  • Knowledge of the political, administrative and legal structures of school organisation
  • Leadership experience in school or in a comparable field
  • Ideally, a completed school management training
  • An independent, solution-oriented and resilient personality
  • Enjoy the leadership and further development of our school

We offer

  • A versatile and responsible management task with creative scope
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in a future-oriented school
  • A motivated and committed team
  • Support from the Commission Education, Youth and Sport, the School Secretariat and specialised bodies
  • The opportunity to contribute your own ideas and actively shape the school

The workload can be divided (school management 75% and school pool 17%)

Would you like to take responsibility and accompany our school in a special situation? We look forward to your application!
