Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Design and implement scalable data architectures and data models for complex use cases
  • Establishment, development and operation of data pipelines for structured and unstructured data
  • Development of suitable solutions for data storage, data integration and batch and streaming processing
  • Building semantic data models and developing and using ontologies and knowledge graphs
  • Using AI and machine learning methods to analyze data and develop data-based solutions
  • Integration of data and ML solutions into existing software and system landscapes via APIs and services
  • Cooperation in the areas of data governance, data lifecycle management and auditing
  • Management of security, access and authorization concepts for data, platforms and data science environments

You bring that with you

  • Completed training in data science, computer science or equivalent specialisation; or lateral entrants with comparable practical experience
  • Several years of experience in the development and implementation of complex data-driven solutions
  • In-depth knowledge of relational databases, object stores and vector databases
  • Very good knowledge of data engineering, especially data modeling, data pipelines, storage technologies and streaming
  • Solid understanding of Kubernetes-based environments and enterprise storage, especially NetApp
  • Independent, analytical and solution-oriented working method
  • fluent knowledge of German
  • English language skills (level B2) are beneficial
  • Military career as a senior non-commissioned officer or officer of advantage
