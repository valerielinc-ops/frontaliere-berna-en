Their tasks

Communication

  • Substantive responsibility for the communication contributions of the CDE
  • Planning, design, coordination and further development of CDE communication formats
  • Technical support for teams responsible for communication tasks
  • Editorial responsibility and coordination of communication contributions from the projects
  • Responsible for interfaces with the Communication Department of the University of Bern

Workflows

  • Coherence of strategic processes: Planning, coordinating, documenting, reviewing and following up internal institution workflows with responsible parties
  • Feedback loops: prepare management decisions, accompany them organizationally, document them
  • Ensure information flow between units, internal communication and follow-up initiatives
  • CDE governance: coordinate planning and reporting to the CDE Board and university management and track action lines
  • Structured documentation and storage as well as maintenance of the corresponding support tools

Their role

The Centre for Development and Environment CDE is the Swiss competence centre for sustainable development. By linking basic knowledge with targeted application, CDE researchers work closely with international partner institutions. Our expertise covers a broad field of sustainability and brings together numerous scientific disciplines. We develop transdisciplinary approaches directly in regional contexts – from the Bernese Oberland to Kenya – where our research contributes to sustainable development prospects. As one of the strategic centers of the University of Bern, the CDE also has the task of anchoring sustainable development in the university as a whole and further developing it for teaching and research. For effective internal and external communication, we are looking for a well-organized, structured and initiative specialist who complements our team as responsible for communication and workflows, 70%. They shape the communications portfolio and take over organizational basic work for the coherent management of the complex tasks at the CDE. For the new position to be created, we are looking for a learning specialist with an interest in new things who thinks strategically and at the same time ensures that processes run smoothly. You are a communication professional, bring clarity to structures, value excellent services and support our teams in management and management. They like to take responsibility, organize carefully and purposefully, work with passion and keep the overview, even if the pressure is high. They have qualified training in communication and experience in science communication. A background in project management or corresponding professional practice is essential. Ideally, you will have a professional interest in sustainability, be familiar with university operations and be prepared to contribute to a careful, respectful and collaborative form of work at the critical interfaces of our centre.
