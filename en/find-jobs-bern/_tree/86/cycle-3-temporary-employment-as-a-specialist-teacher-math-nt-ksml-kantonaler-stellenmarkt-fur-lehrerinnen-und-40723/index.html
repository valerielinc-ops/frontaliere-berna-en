Full description

Portrait The Belp elementary school has eleven decentralized kindergartens, a primary school located in the three school facilities Dorf, Mühlematt and Neumatt as well as a cycle school on the Belpberg. The classes of secondary level I are located in the central upper level center Mühlematt. This includes 17 classes as well as one class RIK plus. Around 45 teachers teach about 350 students in models 3b (7th and 8th grades) and model 4 (9th grades). Additional support instruction (IBEM) and school social work are an integral part of our support offer. School events (sports, culture, etc.) as well as camps and excursions are just as much a part of our program as our apprenticeship platform in March. A day school, a well-developed elective subject and supplementary offer (AdS), the voluntary school sport and the task help expand the offer of the managed schools.
