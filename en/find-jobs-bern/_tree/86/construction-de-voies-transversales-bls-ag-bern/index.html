Full description

Anyone who works outdoors and wants to make a difference is in the right place with us. Together, we maintain tracks, points and construction trains – for safe and punctual journeys. Do you have technical or trade training? Then you are in the right place with us. You are part of a team that gets things done, takes responsibility and knows that without you there would be no railway. With us, you can take the next step – towards training as a construction-train driver.

What awaits you

  • Maintain, repair and modernize tracks, points and rail systems so that rail traffic runs safely and reliably
  • Maintain and secure the surroundings along the route – for example, embankment work, keeping the clearance gauge clear and ensuring sightlines
  • Identify and rectify technical faults in the route area, including during assignments outside regular working hours
  • After completing the training, operate construction trains in maintenance and construction operations and ensure safe journeys

What you bring

  • Professional experience in construction, skilled trades, metalworking or a technical environment
  • Enjoyment of practical outdoor work – including in wind, weather and changing conditions
  • Technical understanding, a careful way of working and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to work night and weekend shifts (around 40–50 %)
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good eyesight and hearing for safe work in the track area

Your benefits – these are what you can look forward to

  • We support the most flexible planning possible for your tours and shifts
  • Get a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare travelcard.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are additional allowances for night and weekend shifts.
  • There is an option to purchase an additional five to ten vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external professional development and an exciting internal range of learning opportunities and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. More than that: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and Simplon. What connects us in all this? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
