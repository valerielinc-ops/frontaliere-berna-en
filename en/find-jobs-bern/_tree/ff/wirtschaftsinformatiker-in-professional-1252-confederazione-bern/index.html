Full description

  • Professional responsibility (thematic ownership) for the New Production Model (NPM) within the main department Defence Platform
  • Advising and supporting product owners, product managers and other stakeholders in questions relating to the cost accounting and billing of IT and business IT services in the ITSM Suite

  • Implementation of quality checks, identification of optimization potential and support of the cleaning of data stocks
  • Bringing technical expertise to the further development of the ITSM suite ServiceNow and formulating requirements for new functionalities in the field of discovery
  • EFZ degree in business informatics, higher vocational training or university degree in business informatics, computer science or business administration related to computer science or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in IT service management, preferably in a complex IT organization or an enterprise environment as well as in-depth knowledge in service catalogue management, IT financial management as well as cost accounting and service billing
  • Very good knowledge of ITSM frameworks, especially ITIL® 4, as well as experience in the definition, introduction and continuous development of ITSM processes
  • Experience in process management as well as in the analysis, optimization and documentation of business and service processes
  • Knowledge of agile working methods such as Scrum, Kanban or SAFe are advantageous
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking, strong quality awareness as well as the ability to control professional topics independently and to advise different stakeholders in a targeted manner
  • Enjoy working in interdisciplinary and cross-functional teams as well as addressee-oriented and convincing communication
  • good active knowledge of an official language and passive knowledge of a second official language
  • Good English skills
