Full description

Put to the Point

As a university intern, you will support the regional coordination of neighbouring states and cross-border cooperation of the Europe Department. They participate in the content and logistical preparation of bilateral visits, in the preparation of reports, in research and in the organisation of events. They are also following developments in neighbouring countries and in cross-border cooperation. In addition, you will support the activities of the Swiss Embassy in Liechtenstein, which is affiliated to the European department.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20410
  • Location: Bundeshaus West, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Prepare dossiers for bilateral meetings and visits in terms of content and logistics (at the level of the Federal Bureau, department head, State Secretary FDFA, etc.).
  • Diplomatic correspondence: write letters and diplomatic notes and answer citizens’ letters.
  • Create contributions and analyses on neighbouring states for other units of the Federal Administration.
  • Material and logistical support for the organisation of events (conferences, dialogues, etc.).
  • Research on neighbouring countries and update information on the FDFA website.
  • Support for the Swiss Embassy in Liechtenstein.

This makes them unique.

  • University or technical college degree in international relations, political, social or legal sciences or in a related field of study.
  • Excellent editorial and analytical skills
  • Careful and structured working methods.
  • Resilience and teamwork.
  • Ability to complete multiple tasks in parallel and meet deadlines in a dynamic environment.
  • Very good oral and written knowledge in at least two official languages and excellent knowledge of English. Good knowledge of Italian is an advantage.
  • Interest in European policy and EU member states (corresponding prior knowledge is a plus).

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

The State Secretariat (STS) manages the development, coordination and planning of foreign policy and is responsible for reporting to the political authorities and the management of Switzerland’s foreign network. It is responsible for implementing Switzerland’s foreign policy strategy. The most important dossiers include cooperation with the United Nations (UN), the development of relations with the European Union (EU), and security and peace policy.
