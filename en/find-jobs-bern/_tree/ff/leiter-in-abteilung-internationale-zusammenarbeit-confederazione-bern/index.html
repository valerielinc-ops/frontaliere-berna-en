Migration moves

The State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) is responsible for all issues relating to the right of aliens and asylum in Switzerland. In the International Directorate, the International Cooperation Directorate is responsible for shaping bilateral relations with countries of origin and transit and for managing multilateral cooperation. The aim is effective migration governance and a coherent migration foreign policy.
