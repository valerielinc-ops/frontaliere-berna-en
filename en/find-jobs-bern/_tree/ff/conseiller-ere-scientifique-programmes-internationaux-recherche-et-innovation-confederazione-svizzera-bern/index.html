Put to the Point

Are you committed, dynamic and would like to use your knowledge to promote Swiss research and innovation cooperation with international partners? The International Research and Innovation Programme is responsible for Switzerland’s participation in international programmes and initiatives. This includes in particular EU programmes such as Horizon Europe and Digital Europe, as well as other international initiatives in the field of research and innovation. They evaluate and examine international research projects, monitor their financial implementation and contribute to the efficient and effective implementation of Swiss research and innovation funding.
