Full description

The Department of Operations of the SSR is looking for an 80%-100% director for RTS TV News.

Your Mission

Attached operationally to the editor-in-chief of the TV News, you ensure the production of the editorial programs and are also called upon to make special pages outside. In close collaboration with the publication, you supervise the entire image section of the newspaper as well as other TV news productions. You also ensure the coordination of the various trades on the editions and productions you carry out.

For this position, we would like to meet candidates who wish to engage in the editorial work of TV News. Given the very specific nature of the activity, appropriate training is foreseen.

Your profile

  • Proven experience in the realization or in a profession related to the image.
  • Acute sense of narrative and excellent command of the language of the image.
  • Good general culture, strong knowledge of current issues, editorial sensitivity and understanding of the issues that shape our society.
  • Use of image tools and techniques.
  • Medium and high communication capacity.
  • Very high capacity and pronounced taste for teamwork.
  • Ability to lead a team.
  • Ability to make quick decisions, very high resistance to stress and pressure.
  • Rigor and autonomy, good organizational and anticipation skills.
  • Very flexible and available in schedules (irregular schedules, frequent work on weekends, evenings and holidays)
  • Good knowledge of German and English

What we offer you

  • A dynamic working environment, at the heart of the news.
  • Various projects, between studio shows, special editions and outdoor performances.
  • Close collaboration with experienced editorial, artistic and technical teams.
  • The opportunity to participate in media coverage and major events.
  • An innovative technological environment, encouraging experimentation and new forms of narrative.
  • Opportunities for continuing training and the development of poly-competences.

Thank you for sending us your complete dossier: cover letter, CV, copies of diplomas and certificates, as well as a video presentation of 1 minute 30 maximum exclusively through the RTS job portal. Only applications submitted through this site will be considered.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need an inspiration: Welcome to the world of RTS!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the core of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy for innovation and quality.
  • Living digital: In our country, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content is part of the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
