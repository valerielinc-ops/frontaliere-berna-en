with craftsmanship and a flair for green work

You like to work practically and are looking for a task where you can use your craftsmanship? With us, you can expect a versatile working day with exciting tasks around construction, maintenance and environmental care.

Your field of activity

  • You work actively in civil engineering, building construction and environmental care and ensure that all work is done professionally
  • In doing so, you take care of structural maintenance and repair work and ensure that plants and buildings are maintained well.
  • Excavators, vehicles, tools and other work equipment are regularly maintained and maintained so that they are ready for use at all times.
  • You carry out your assignments independently, comply with the applicable regulations and consult with your manager if necessary
  • You work on various construction sites, working in tunnels or in wind and weather
  • In addition to the practical work, you also carry out administrative tasks and keep your manager informed about the progress of the work and special incidents.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic artisanal education and enjoyment of working practically
  • Professional experience in the maintenance and maintenance of machines or in environmental care give you an advantage
  • Your craftsmanship, in-depth knowledge of materials and processes as well as experience in your field round off your profile
  • With MS Office and SAP you can cope, a truck or lifting platform certificate as well as knowledge of fire partition are a plus
  • Category B driving licence, ideally you also have the BE category
  • Good knowledge of German, French is an advantage
