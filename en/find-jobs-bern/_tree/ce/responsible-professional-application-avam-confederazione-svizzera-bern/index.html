Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is the federal competence centre for central questions of labour market policy and unemployment insurance. The labour market/unemployment insurance service area shapes the professional framework of enforcement and accompanies its digital development. As part of the Market and Integration department, the RAV/LAM/KAST Technical Implementation Support Group works at the interface of enforcement, departments, computer science and control. In this function, you take on a central technical role in the control and further development of the specialist application AVAM. They help ensure that AVAM is developed consistently, legally compliant, user-oriented and future-proof in conjunction with business processes, data, environmental systems and legal requirements. In this way, you make a significant contribution to the digital transformation of public employment and unemployment insurance.

  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-19942

You can make this contribution

  • Control the specialist application AVAM technically and further develop it over the entire life cycle
  • Consolidate, prioritize and translate technical requirements from enforcement, departments, projects and other stakeholders into feasible solutions
  • Developing conceptual bases, target images and decision bases for the further development of AVAM
  • Ensure coherence between specialist application, business processes, data, legal requirements and connected systems
  • Help shape AVAM’s technical roadmap and participate in release, introduction and further development planning
  • Represent the AVAM specialist perspective in projects, specialist committees and cross-sectoral projects and act as SPOC

This makes them unique.

  • Higher education (HF, FH or equivalent) as well as further education in requirements engineering, business analysis, business informatics, process management or comparable
  • Several years of experience in a comparable environment related to specialist applications, business processes, product control or digital transformation
  • Experience in professional control or further development of complex specialist applications
  • Ability to translate strategic objectives into technically viable, legally compliant and user-oriented requirements
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking skills, high personal responsibility, negotiating skills and enjoyment of cross-sectoral cooperation
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages in word and writing
  • English skills are beneficial

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
