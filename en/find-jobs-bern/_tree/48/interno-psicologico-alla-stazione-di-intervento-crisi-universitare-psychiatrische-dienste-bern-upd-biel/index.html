Psychiatry Bienne UPZ Münsingen

The Psychiatry of Bienne offers complete psychiatric care. At the Crisis Response Station, we accompany you in acute emergencies. At the ambulance, you get a competent, fast and personalized treatment following an initial evaluation interview.

Your tasks

  • Discover the interdisciplinary work with hospitalized patients
  • You participate in the production of psychological questionnaires
  • You contribute to hospital admission, diagnosis and treatment planning
  • You participate in group therapies and contribute to their design and animation
  • You are involved in interviews with hospitalized patients and may take care of certain tasks, such as anamnesis
  • You accompany patients in various activities, including walks outside the clinic
  • You benefit from the support of a psychologist and an introduction to your tasks by the team

Your profile

  • You have reached an advanced stage of your studies in psychology (master)
  • You are a self-reliant, curious and proactive person.
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team and in a multicultural environment
  • You have a special interest in interdisciplinary collaboration
  • You are native French and have good oral knowledge of German

UPZ

The University Psychiatric Centre in Bern (UPZ) is the main centre of competence in psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region as a university clinic. It provides psychiatric care in the canton of Bern throughout the care chain, from early identification to outpatient treatment, day care and stationary treatment, to rehabilitation and forensic psychiatry. With more than 30 sites, UPZ offers a healthcare network covering the entire territory. The UPZ also makes an important contribution in the fields of education, training and research.
