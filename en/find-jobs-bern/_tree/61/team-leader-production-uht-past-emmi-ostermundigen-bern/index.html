Full description

Freshness in focus, processes under control - At the traditional location Ostermundigen we passionately produce popular classics such as Emmi Caffè Latte, finest yogurt, Emmi Energy Milk and refreshing Glace. In a dynamic environment that stands for freshness, speed and the highest quality, we are looking for people who want to think, shape and grow with us.

You can do that.

  • You are responsible for the personnel and professional management of approximately 35 employees in the field of filling UHT/Past.
  • You make sure that the processes, procedures and all existing regulations (cleanliness, hygiene, etc.) are always adhered to.
  • You take responsibility for the smooth process for the team bottling UHT/Past.
  • You coordinate and organize your team independently and are the first point of contact when problems arise.
  • You are actively involved in day-to-day business on all machines.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed vocational training as a plant manager: in or in the food environment (cheeser: in, dairy technologist/milk chinologist, etc.).
  • You bring several years of experience in the manufacturing industry, preferably in the food sector.
  • You have already gained experience in the personnel and professional management of medium-sized teams.
  • Your open-minded, approachable and innovative personality will take you forward with us.
  • You have very good MS Office knowledge as well as good SAP user knowledge.
  • You bring negotiable knowledge of German in word and writing.

This is what we offer you

  • 6 weeks vacation: Enjoy six weeks vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to seven weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Effective leadership development: Our Excellence Leadership program offers tailor-made training and modern learning methods to support you in your development into an outstanding leader in a practical and future-oriented manner.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
