University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Psychotherapy Day Clinic PTK The UPZ offers comprehensive psychiatric care in the capital region. From early diagnosis to outpatient and inpatient treatment to rehabilitation – we cover the entire supply chain. Our university hospitals are not only responsible for special psychiatric care, but also make a significant contribution to university research, teaching and training of all professional groups. The Clinical Social Service of Adult Psychiatry (EPP), consisting of around 45 dedicated employees, supports patients in coping with social problems. The psychotherapy day clinic PTK is located at the Bolligenstrasse 111 location and offers an interdisciplinary day-care facility for adults. The focus of treatment is on psychotherapy. Here, 18 to 20 patients are treated with depression, anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders, post-traumatic stress disorders or personality disorders.

Your tasks

  • You will collect social anamneese and carry out comprehensive social work assessments
  • You advise and accompany patients as well as, if necessary, their relatives and other caregivers during day-inpatient therapy in family, financial, professional and legal matters
  • You will develop suitable connection solutions and coordinate cooperation with external institutions, authorities and specialist departments
  • You work closely with the multi-professional treatment team, with external institutions and the patient’s family environment.
  • You are responsible for leading a patient group of the PTK
  • You are interested in psychotherapeutic processes

Your profile

  • You have a diploma (BSc or MSc) in Social Work
  • You have several years of professional experience in psychiatry / clinical social work and have good knowledge in the field of social insurance
  • You work independently, structured, solution-oriented and networked
  • You are a flexible, resilient and reliable personality with a high sense of responsibility and strong teamwork skills
  • You appreciate the fact that you are constantly developing professionally and that you are committed to contributing your skills in the field of clinical social work

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
