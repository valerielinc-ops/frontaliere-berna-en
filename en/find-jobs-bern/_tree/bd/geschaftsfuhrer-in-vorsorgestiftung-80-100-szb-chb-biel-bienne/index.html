Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The Welfare Foundation of the Hospital Center Biel operates the mandatory and super-compulsory occupational provision for the employees. As Managing Director, you assume overall operational responsibility for our pension facility and, together with a small, committed team, ensure professional and customer-oriented support for the insured and affiliated companies.
