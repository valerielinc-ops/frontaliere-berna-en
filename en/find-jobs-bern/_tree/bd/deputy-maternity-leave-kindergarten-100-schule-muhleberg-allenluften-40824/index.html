Full description

Portrait Our school includes around 330 learners, 49 teachers, a school social worker, an MR team, a school management team and a waiting team surrounded by two school buildings, a modern triple sports hall incl. Climbing wall and gymnastics room, a large sports field, a day school, a library and a youth meeting place. The school is located in beautiful rural surroundings, about 15 km from Bern and Murten and can be reached in 17 minutes by train/bus from Bern.

Tasks

  • They accompany and support 18 children in kindergarten everyday life.
  • They work closely and constructively with the kindergarten team.
  • They maintain an appreciative and open exchange with parents, specialists and other participants.

Requirements

  • Ideally, they have a pedagogical education (primary teaching diploma or bachelor’s degree for the preschool or primary level).
  • You already have your first professional experience as a teacher.
  • They are a responsible, communicative and resilient personality and meet children, parents and other caregivers with appreciation and professionalism.
  • The deputy may also be divided between two teachers.

We offer

  • You can expect a helpful and committed team that supports you in everyday school life.
  • In sports, you will be actively accompanied and relieved by a class help.
  • There are two parallel classes on site, with which a close and collegial cooperation is maintained.
  • You benefit from a very good support from the MR teacher.
  • A modern and well-developed infrastructure, including an attractive forest square, is at your disposal.
  • Free parking is available directly at the schoolhouse.
  • You are very welcome at the school Mühleberg.
