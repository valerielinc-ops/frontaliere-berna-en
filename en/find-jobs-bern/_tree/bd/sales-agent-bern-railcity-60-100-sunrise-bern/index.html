Full description

At Sunrise, we work closely together as a team and are close to our customers in the shop every day. Our culture combines respect, openness and new ideas with a strong team spirit. We support each other, take responsibility in everyday life and know that every voice counts and every perspective makes us better.

Our passion motivates us to try new things and to continuously develop – always with the aim of creating a positive customer experience.

You like to work in a team, take on responsibility and are in direct contact with customers: inside? Then become part of our team.

What You Expect

Our front-line teams shape Sunrise’s customer experience. With personal advice, customized solutions and top service, every contact becomes a long-term customer relationship.

Your challenge

  • You are the face of Sunrise and inspire our customers with your positive charisma, your targeted advice and the sale of our diverse Sunrise products.
  • You build long-term customer relationships and provide a first-class atmosphere in the shop.
  • You support customers with repair and service concerns and act professionally and solution-oriented.
  • You keep track of stock and inventory and perform administrative tasks efficiently.
  • You live and represent the Sunrise values – innovation, customer proximity and top service.

Your kills

  • You have completed training in the retail trade, ideally with experience in the telecommunications or multimedia industry – or you are a motivated lateral entrant with a sales flair.
  • You bubbly with enthusiasm for technology, telecommunications and digital trends.
  • Your passion for sales is noticeable and your professional appearance makes you a real sales professional.
  • You enjoy dealing with people and the ability to quickly recognize their needs.
  • Thanks to your patience and technical understanding, you will become an everyday hero for our customers.
  • You have the flexibility to work shifts in a shop that is open seven days a week, including regular evening and weekend assignments.
  • You have very good knowledge of German, every other language is a plus.

At Us, you work in a dynamic and multinational environment where every voice is heard, perspectives are shared and values are lived. Diversity is not just a program for us, but part of our DNA. Regardless of who you are or where you come from, at Sunrise you will find your place. We create an inclusive environment in which all employees feel belonging and can fully develop. Our commitment to equality and diversity is also externally confirmed: Sunrise is a partner of “Advance Gender Equality in Business”, winner of the Swiss LGBTI label and certified with “Fair‐ON‐Pay Advanced” for equal pay for work of equal value.
