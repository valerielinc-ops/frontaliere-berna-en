Full description

Portrait The Hinterkappelen Primary School is an urban suburban school. Hinterkappelen is a district of the community Wohlen. Our school facility is located in the middle of the Kappelenring, in a traffic-free zone and is optimally connected to public transport. Our lively school currently houses a total of 16 classes, two per school year and four kindergartens. The college comprises about 50 teachers. On the school grounds are a day school, sports halls, a football field and a play and basketball court - of course accessible to all children.

Tasks

From 01. February 2027 we are looking for a class teacher in a 5th grade as part of a half-year deputy. You will teach your class during 20 lessons in the subject areas of NMG, German, mathematics and movement and sports. Furthermore, you teach 4 lessons (mathematics and M&I, partly in the TT) in the parallel class. They design a varied and differentiated lesson according to curriculum 21 and build a sustainable, warm relationship with the children, the guardians, as well as the colleagues. They work closely with the team of the parallel class and the curative teacher.

Requirements

You are a qualified teacher (Bachelor Primary Education/seminary patent) with a positive attitude towards the students, guardians and colleagues. They are ready to support and further develop the existing school building structure and look forward to working together in the step-by-step team and in the overall college.

We offer

We offer a temporary position at our diverse primary school from 1 February to 31 July 2027, where you can contribute your values and count on good cooperation in the step team, in the college, with specialists, school social work and co-management.

  • We offer you a mentorship.

In addition, the close cooperation within the class and parallel class team as well as the great support from the SHP should be highlighted. Furthermore, the NMG topics are already tracked and developed by the end of the school year. Good infrastructure and excellent connections to public transport, as well as free parking are further advantages of this attractive location. Interested? We look forward to your application!
