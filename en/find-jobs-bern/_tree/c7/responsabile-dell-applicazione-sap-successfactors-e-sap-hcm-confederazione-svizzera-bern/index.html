Full description

Put to the Point

For the entire federal administration, you and your team are responsible for the smooth operation of HR IT instruments, especially the personnel information and personnel management system, and are actively involved in the digital transformation.

  • Date of entry: 01.02.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 614-21027
  • Location: Eigerstrasse 71, 3007 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the operation of the nationwide HR-IT instruments, in particular SAP SuccessFactors Talent Management, professionally, supervise and further develop through change requests
  • Participate as sub-project manager/in "Technology/Fachbetrieb" in nationwide projects from initialization to introduction
  • Actively implementing the digital transformation of HR processes
  • Advise administration units on application issues (2nd level support) as well as analyze requirements and develop solutions
  • establish and update training documents, user manuals and technical information
  • As a speaker at specialist courses

This makes them unique.

  • Completed higher specialist training (FH, HF) in computer science and/or human resources
  • In-depth knowledge of SAP SuccessFactors solutions and SAP/HCM
  • Several years of experience as a sub-project manager, ideally familiar with agile project management (SAFe) and project management method HERMES; experienced in dealing with ITIL processes, functions and roles
  • Holistic process- and goal-oriented thinking, good negotiating skills, communication skills and assertiveness
  • Systematic, independent, precise and service-oriented working methods
  • High commitment and customer orientation
  • Good knowledge of a second official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The staff, our task

The Swiss Federal Personnel Office (EPA) is responsible for the personnel policy of the Federal Administration. It develops the foundations and tools to successfully manage and develop human resources in departments and administrative units. It defines the personnel strategy, develops the federal personnel law, controls the nationwide personnel resources, provides an education and training offer and operates the centrally controlled HR informatics.
