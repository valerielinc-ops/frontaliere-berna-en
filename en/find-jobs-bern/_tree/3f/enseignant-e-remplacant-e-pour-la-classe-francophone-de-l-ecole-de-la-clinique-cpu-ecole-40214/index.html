Full description

Portrait The school of the University Clinic of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy for Children and Adolescents (CPU) is spread over four sites: Ittigen, Burgdorf, Bienne and Spiez. Pupils, six to nine per class, are enrolled in classes with different age levels. As a public school, the clinic school follows the curriculum of the Romaned Study Plan (PER). Education is on the one hand individualized, but the emphasis is also on interclass activities and fostering group cohesion.

Tasks

You are responsible for class management and teaching in a class of several age levels. Working days are Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. You teach 20 lessons and participate in interdisciplinary work. Workload 80%. The contract runs from October 14 to December 11, 26.

Profile required We are looking for a dynamic and proactive person who, in addition to teaching and classroom management, appreciates interdisciplinary collaboration and wants to get involved in the daily life of the clinic. Professional experience is required

  • Further training in curative pedagogy is desired.

We offer

  • You would have interdisciplinary collaboration with children's psychiatrists, psychologists, social educators, staff specialized in psychiatry.
  • We offer you an open, challenging and demanding business culture, as well as an interesting and exciting work environment.
  • Wage and employment conditions are in accordance with cantonal directives and internal regulations.
