Full description

UniBE is always at the cutting edge. With us, you help solve important social issues.

Your residency training

  • Assessment, stabilisation and management of emergency and ICU patients under direct and indirect specialist supervision
  • Daytime, evening, overnight and weekend clinical duties
  • Rotations in Internal Medicine, Surgery and other relevant specialties
  • Clinical rounds, journal clubs, hospital rounds and resident seminars
  • Teaching veterinary students and veterinary support staff
  • Clinical research and publications meeting ECVECC credentialing requirements

Your profile

  • Veterinary degree and eligibility to practise in Switzerland
  • Small animal rotating internship or equivalent supervised clinical experience
  • Strong clinical reasoning, communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work effectively in a multidisciplinary team
  • Commitment to clinical excellence, postgraduate training and science
  • Good German, French and English communication skills (C1); German is the principal local language, with many French-speaking clients and referring vets

Specialist mentorship, structured teaching and clinical research in an international university hospital Application and Contact Please email a motivation letter, CV and two recommendation letters to With us, you create value. For yourself and for society.

About the residency

The multidisciplinary Small Animal Clinic offers a three-year ECVECC-approved residency preparing for the ECVECC certifying examination. Three ACVECC/ECVECC Diplomates provide supervision and mentorship. Facilities include a dedicated emergency and trauma area, a fully equipped ICU, a 24-hour in-house laboratory and advanced diagnostics and treatment. Your residency training

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
