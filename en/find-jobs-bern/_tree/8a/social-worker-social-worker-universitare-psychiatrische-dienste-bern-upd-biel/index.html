Full description

Psychiatry Biel UPZ Münsingen

Do you want to accompany people with mental illnesses holistically and include social, professional and personal areas of life? In the Ambulatorium of the Psychiatry Biel you can expect a versatile and bilingual working environment in which you can contribute your social work expertise. Our interprofessional team offers competent, timely and individual psychiatric treatment and accompanies people with a wide range of mental illnesses. As part of the UPZ’s outpatient offering, you will work closely with specialists from medicine, psychology and nursing as well as with established specialists and external partner organisations. You support our patients with social, financial, legal and professional issues and develop concrete perspectives for their everyday life together with them. You can expect varied tasks, a lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to sustainably strengthen people in challenging life situations.

Your tasks

  • You plan and coordinate support measures in the core areas of work, housing, budget, law and migration
  • You provide psychosocial counselling in acute crises, in cooperation with the outpatient treatment team
  • You support our patients especially in social security matters
  • In your day-to-day work, you work closely with authorities (e.g. the adult and child protection authority and social services) as well as legal and specialist services (e.g. counselling centres, outpatient services).
  • You will contribute your valuable experience in psychosocial counselling and actively participate in the compliance and optimization of our high quality standards
  • You accompany our patients individually and process-oriented
  • You get the opportunity to network with social institutions, institutions and organizations (e.g. in the protected living and working area)
  • You work in an interprofessional team consisting of doctors, psychologists, nurses and the secretariat

Your profile

  • You have a University of Applied Sciences degree / Bachelor in Social Work
  • You have extensive knowledge of social law, but also medical-psychiatric oriented
  • You are distinguished by consulting skills and experiences
  • Additional qualification in social security law and further training in systemic counselling are a big plus
  • You are open, resilient and reliable with a good pinch of humor
  • You have a strong organizational talent
  • Ideally, you already have experience in psychiatry
  • You have good oral knowledge of the German and French language

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
