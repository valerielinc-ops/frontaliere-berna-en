Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Bern (Klinik Beau-Site, Salem-Spital, Klinik Permanence, OPERA Bern)Occupation per: 01. January 2027 or by appointment type of employment: Indefinite reference number: 74534

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Risk Management ensures effective risk management, internal control system and crisis and business continuity management at the locations of the entire Mittelland healthcare region (5 clinics, 2 outpatient units) – according to the methodology and with the tools of the Hirslanden Group. For this function, we are looking for you as a risk management specialist.

YOUR TASKS

  • You carry out the annual Top Risk Assessment together with the management team of the clinics in the care region either yourself or coordinate the results and ensure the consistent follow-up of the derived measures
  • You coordinate and moderate workshops on the risk and control self-assessments (internal control system) with the risk owners, reviewing the results critically and ensuring the follow-up of the measures
  • Further analyze events, identify their causes, derive lessons learned and effective measures from them and accompany their implementation
  • You coordinate crisis and business continuity management and moderate BCM testing (crisis unit exercises)
  • In addition, you will produce addressable risk reports for the clinic directorates, the regional chief operating officer and the head risk management at Hirslanden group level and present the key findings in the management team of the respective clinics and outpatient units.
  • You promote a risk-conscious culture by advising the clinic directorates, conducting training for management and cadres and actively engaging in the Hirslanden Group’s risk manager network.

Your profile

  • You have a bachelor's or master's degree in business administration or economics, an additional training in risk management, business continuity management or compliance is an advantage
  • You will bring at least 3–5 years of professional experience in risk management, compliance, consulting, or internal or external audit with, experience in healthcare is an advantage
  • Ideally, you could already gain experience in project and change management
  • As a person, you distinguish yourself through high initiative, an independent working method and entrepreneurial thinking
  • You will be confident and diplomatic with management, enjoy moderating and have strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • You bring the willingness to travel occasionally within the supply region and participate in network meetings of the Hirslanden Group 1-2 times a year
  • You have very good German and good English skills in word and writing
  • You are experienced in dealing with MS-Office, application knowledge of risk management tools or governance, risk & compliance tools (GRC) are an advantage

Some of your benefits to us

  • Family atmosphere with du-culture and cooperation at eye level
  • Modern workplaces in the heart of the city of Bern
  • A wide range of benefits (e.g. publishing, public transport coupons, discount fitness subscriptions, etc.)
  • Discounted food options in restaurants in clinics
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • Additional free time through holiday purchase of up to two weeks
