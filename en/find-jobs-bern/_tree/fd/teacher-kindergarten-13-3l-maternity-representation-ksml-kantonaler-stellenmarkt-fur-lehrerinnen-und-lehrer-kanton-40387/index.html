Portrait Discover Pieterlen: Our school is located on the beautiful Jurasüdfuss! We teach around 650 children and adolescents from kindergarten to the 9th school year. Teach in a team of motivated/helpful teachers. Accompany teams of solidarity-based teachers who maintain an active exchange. Joy and motivation to accompany the children in everyday life and inspire them for new things Readiness for cooperative, creative cooperation in the step team Teaching in a heterogeneous kindergarten class with currently 15 children Working days are: Tuesday and Thursday morning & afternoon
