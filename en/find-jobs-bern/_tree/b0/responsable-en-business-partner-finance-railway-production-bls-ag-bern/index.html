Full description

You assume overall responsibility for the security area within railway production. With your expertise in security strategies, incident analysis and legal bases, you ensure that our customers and employees can travel safely and without worry. And should something happen anyway, you ensure together with authorities and partners that cases can be clarified.

This is waiting for you

  • Professional and organizational leadership of the railway production security team
  • Development and implementation of security strategies, concepts and standards
  • Analysis and processing of security-relevant incidents incl. Video evaluations
  • Representation of BLS in external bodies as well as before law enforcement authorities and courts
  • Preparation of reports, criminal complaints and legal documentation for authorities

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial education supplemented by further training in the field of security
  • Sound knowledge of StGB, BGST, PBG, EBG and the Video Surveillance Regulation
  • Several years of experience in security, investigations or in the administrative environment, ideal with management responsibility
  • Communicative strength in German, additionally French or Italian beneficial
  • Readiness for flexible missions and impeccable reputation

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year
  • Financial support for external further education as well as an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? Proud of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
