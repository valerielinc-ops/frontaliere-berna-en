Team Leader Data Management - Sales partners (m/f/d) 60-100%

Move Switzerland with us. Team Leader Data Management - Sales partners (m/f/d) Ostermundigen / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

You lead and develop an interdisciplinary data management team. In collaboration with the Product Owner, you create the conditions for lasting success and foster the development of skills, collaboration and organization. You actively shape the future of our data management and work closely with divisional management, Product Management, architecture and other specialized fields. Exciting topics await you around SAP MDG, S/4HANA, basic data, data quality, automation and modern data solutions – with great flexibility, flexible forms of work and attractive advantages. What you can move.

  • As a team leader, you supervise and develop employees and create an environment in which responsibility is encouraged and professional and personal development is successful.
  • Together, with the Product Owner, you ensure the optimal alignment of priorities, required skills and available capabilities.
  • You develop skills and abilities in a sustainable way, identify skill gaps early and define appropriate development measures.
  • You plan in a forward-looking way the composition of the team as well as the resources and capabilities, and participate in the recruitment and integration of new employees.
  • You promote effective collaboration, support continuous improvement, and help the team resolve obstacles and conflicts constructively.
  • You are actively contributing to the development of data management and automation of business processes, and you are accompanying changes in the digital transformation.

What you bring.

  • With experience in leadership and employee development, ideally in a data-driven environment, you provide a solid foundation.
  • In the IT, digitalization or transformation fields, you have already held a leadership role or a responsible function and reliably complete projects.
  • You have a good understanding of data management as well as business processes driven by data and automated.
  • You are familiar with agile and product oriented working methods and have knowledge in the SAFe environment. Ideally, you have experience in the SAP environment, especially SAP MDG and S/4HANA.
  • holds a university degree (HES/Univ.) in management computing, Data Science or in a comparable discipline; ideally complemented by leadership training.
  • You master German at a professional level (C1); any other national language is an asset.

Your salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This commitment is in accordance with the Code of Obligations, taking into account a comparison with the team, market and industry.
