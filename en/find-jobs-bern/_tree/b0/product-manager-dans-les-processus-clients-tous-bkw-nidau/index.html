Full description

At BKW, you take responsibility for customer processes around the grid connection and the power supply. You develop these further in a targeted manner, combine the operative business with agile product development and ensure efficient solutions that convince customers and specialist partners.

Your field of activity

  • You take over the professional end-to-end responsibility for customer processes in grid connection and network customer management and develop this continuously
  • Processes such as emergency control and monitoring grid connection are analyzed and optimized with regard to customers, owners and specialist partners
  • In close exchange with the operational business, the agile product development teams and communication, you will develop practical optimizations
  • You prioritize further developments, make professional decisions and coordinate implementation
  • In case of technical or system-side problems, you involve the responsible specialists and accompany the solution finding
  • You actively accompany the introduction of new processes, collect targeted feedback and check whether the implemented adjustments in everyday operations achieve the desired effect and create sustainable added value.

You bring that with you

  • Degree at level HF, FH or university (Bachelor/Master) in a technical, business or comparable discipline
  • At least five years of practical experience, ideally in the energy industry or a regulated environment, as well as enthusiasm for technical topics relating to grid connection and electricity
  • Experience in agile product development and a good understanding of customer needs and the customer journey
  • Strong hands-on mentality, conceptual thinking and the ability to successfully put ideas into practice
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method with high personal responsibility and initiative
  • Communication-strong, convincing and assertive personality who feels comfortable in the operative business and in the agile working world and actively drives forward topics
