Full description

Responsibility

  • You are a sparring partner for leaders when it comes to cultural change
  • You actively participate in the redesign of our leadership offerings – always close to the mission statement, the values and our competence model
  • You develop programs for leaders and teams and accompany them as a coach or moderator through the transformation
  • You are familiar with topics such as talent management, diversity, employee surveys and BGM and are happy to support your colleagues in these topics.
  • Depending on your experience and profile, you take on additional responsibility in projects and topics of personnel and organizational development.

Requirements

  • A completed study of work or organizational psychology is mandatory. Everything else, like a recognized education as a coach, is a bonus
  • At least two to five years of experience in a comparable role in personnel and organizational development – so you have already gained solid knowledge and experience in our craft
  • Passion and know-how to accompany change and development processes and to design innovative HR solutions
  • Strong conceptual thinking, coupled with a secure appearance, strategic foresight and a clear focus on results
  • Personal responsibility, self-management and assertiveness? You have just as much as an excellent communicative competence
  • You love working interdisciplinary and exchanging ideas with different stakeholders – in short: You are a team player through and through
  • The readiness and flexibility for occasional assignments in other locations
