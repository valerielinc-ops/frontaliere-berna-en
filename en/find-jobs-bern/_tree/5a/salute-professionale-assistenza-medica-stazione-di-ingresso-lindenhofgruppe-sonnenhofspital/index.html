Full description

Tasks

You look after our patients at the entrance station and ensure a smooth process before the surgery.

  • You receive and record the patients, raise vital values, allergies and medications and inform them about the pre- and post-operative procedure.
  • You accompany them to the anesthesia pre-disclosure and then hand them over to the surgical area.
  • Your tasks also include mainly administrative tasks, obtaining medical reports and preoperative investigations such as blood sampling and ECGs.
  • You can expect regular working hours from Monday to Friday, either from 05:30 to 15:00 or from 08:00 to 17:00.

Requirements

  • You have completed your training as a medical assistant (MPA) or health specialist and ideally bring appropriate professional experience.
  • Conscientious, self-responsible and quality-conscious work as well as good IT knowledge distinguish you.
  • A stylish German rounds off your profile.
