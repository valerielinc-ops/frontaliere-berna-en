Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

Do you want to not only further develop our CRM and customer engagement platform, but also shape it technically? At Galenica, you take responsibility from solution architecture to concrete implementation with Microsoft Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Azure. Together with product owners, departments and development teams, you will create scalable solutions and develop our platform sustainably.

You can do that.

  • You will analyze business and technical requirements and develop sustainable solutions for Microsoft Dynamics 365 and the Power Platform.
  • You design the solution architecture and implement requirements yourself with Dynamics 365, Power Apps, Power Automate and Dataverse.
  • You design integrations with environments and Azure services, taking into account data, security, scalability and long-term maintainability.
  • With technical standards, reviews, tests and appropriate governance, you ensure high solution quality and support deployments and lifecycle management.
  • You will advise product owners, departments and key users on technical and functional issues and work closely with internal teams and external implementation partners.
  • You bring new impetus from the Microsoft ecosystem and see how Microsoft Copilot, automation and AI technologies can make sense of our solutions.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement, in particular with Sales and Customer Insights, as well as good knowledge of the Microsoft Power Platform.
  • You have experience in the analysis and implementation of business requirements and understand CRM, integration and customer engagement processes.
  • You’ll be familiar with solution architectures and technical concepts and ideally bring experience with Azure services as well as API, data and integration architectures.
  • You preferably have knowledge of C#, .NET, JavaScript or TypeScript as well as experience with Azure functions, APIs, logic apps or similar technologies.
  • You work in a structured and quality-conscious manner and are familiar with agile working methods and ideally CI/CD and DevOps processes.
  • You enjoy working with different stakeholders, share your technical know-how and are interested in new opportunities around Microsoft Copilot and AI.
  • You communicate in German and also have knowledge of English.

We fill this position without external personnel.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported education and training as well as internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
