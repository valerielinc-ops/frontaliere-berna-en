Full description

Portrait

The municipality of Sumiswald with its villages of Sumiswald, Wasen and Grünen is located in the middle Emmental. Although the community is rural, it is easily accessible by public transport and offers a modern infrastructure. Since August 2025, together with the school location Chramershus, Heimisbach, we have been the Sumiswald plus regional school. In total we teach six kindergarten, 18 primary classes and nine secondary school classes.

Tasks

  • They cover our speech therapy needs. Currently at the Wasen and Sumiswald locations.
  • You support and accompany individual children or small groups (mainly in the oral area).
  • They carry out diagnostics and therapy, write specialist reports and work with specialist departments, other teachers and parents.

Requirements

  • Diploma/Bachelor in speech therapy.

We offer

  • Collaboration and support from our IF team (mentorship can be offered by the current job holder).
  • Work location in Sumiswald in a central, well-equipped speech therapy room, in the psychomotor room in Wasen
  • Easily accessible by public transport.
  • A permanent position

Requirements

  • Diploma/Bachelor's degree in speech therapy

  • Availability to cover speech therapy needs at the Wasen and Sumiswald locations

  • Experience in supporting and accompanying individual children or small groups, mainly in the oral area

  • Ability to carry out diagnostics and speech therapy

  • Ability to write specialist reports

  • Ability to collaborate with specialist services, other teaching staff, and parents

  • Availability of up to 20 lessons per week (incl. 3L bVSA and 2L eU, divisible)

  • Permanent position with easy accessibility by public transport
