Full description

With joy on the way Do you like working independently in a professional team? Are individual care and high quality care important to you? Then we look for you! FaGe / Housekeeper 40 – 90 % They care for people in their home and help to shape their everyday life. You have the appropriate professional qualification and may have already gained initial experience, so that you can take on your tasks independently and responsibly after a thorough introduction. We offer a varied and independent position in a dynamic service operation. They benefit from advanced employment conditions as well as an extensive range of further education and training. When re-entering the profession or individual development, we are happy to support you.

Portrait From Langnau and Eggiwil we provide the people in the upper Emmental with Spitex services. It is important to us to be close to the people, but also to have a good quality of care and to work together with relatives, doctors and institutions.
