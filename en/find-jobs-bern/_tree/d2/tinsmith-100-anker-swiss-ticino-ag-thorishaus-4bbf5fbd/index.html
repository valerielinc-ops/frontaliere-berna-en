Full description

Spengler 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a temporary or permanent employment more than 3 years of experience as a Spenglerpolier

Your challenge

  • Cost-consciously manage and coordinate construction sites in technical and personnel terms in consultation with the project manager.
  • Work on projects from A to Z, in the workshop and on the construction site.
  • General anti-skimming and lightning work
  • Align in the workshop and mount on the construction site

This is what you bring with you

  • A completed apprenticeship as a Spengler/in EFZ. Advantageous with or in further development to the Spenglerpolisher or site manager.
  • Interdisciplinary knowledge in the field of building envelope
  • Your pleasant and winning way makes it easier for you to manage your employees and contact customers
  • Organizational talent with a high sense of responsibility
  • Cat. B driving licence (you will be provided with a company vehicle – also for the commute)

What we offer you

We rely on people who think, tackle and take responsibility. With us you can contribute your strengths and develop professionally and personally. You can expect a variety of tasks, exciting projects and a working environment in which you can get involved and make a difference. EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
