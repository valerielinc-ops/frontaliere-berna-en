Full description

In the background, you will reliably ensure that everything runs smoothly in the railway production management team. With your organisational skills, you will keep the pace, link people, topics and decisions at the right time. In this way, you will help turn ideas into movement – and BLS reliably pick up speed day after day.

This is waiting for you

  • Ensuring smooth office management with a central hub function
  • Planning and coordination of the agendas of the entire railway production management team with prudence, structure and foresight
  • You will prepare topics for the management team, manage pending issues and keep track of all tasks
  • You enjoy organizing workshops and retreats and are committed to projects and working groups
  • With our internal platform PIAZZA, you keep the team up to date and maintain the exchange with communication

What you bring to the table

  • Commercial training and experience in office management, further training as a management assistant is a plus
  • Committed and resilient personality with a heart for organizing
  • High level of independence, discretion and a keen sense of priorities
  • Experienced in the use of MS Office and digital tools
  • Very good written and spoken German, supplemented by French and English skills are an advantage

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA travelcard 2. Class, a GA travelcard 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Organize your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year
  • Financial support for external training courses as well as an exciting range of internal learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
