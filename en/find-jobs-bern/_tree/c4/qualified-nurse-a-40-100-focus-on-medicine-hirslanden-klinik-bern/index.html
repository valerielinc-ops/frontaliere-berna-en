As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Assumption and implementation of professional care and care of patients in the medical/orthopedic department according to our nursing mission statement
  • Assessing care situations, adequate response in acute situations, seamless management of care documentation as well as recording of all services provided
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with our observing physicians and other areas
  • Participation in the daily visits of physicians to ensure a good flow of information
  • Active participation in projects and support of department management and the entire team to ensure the best possible quality of care
  • Assistance in the care of learners

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional
  • You will benefit from several years of professional experience in the acute hospital
  • Good knowledge of German and ease of use with the computer are required
