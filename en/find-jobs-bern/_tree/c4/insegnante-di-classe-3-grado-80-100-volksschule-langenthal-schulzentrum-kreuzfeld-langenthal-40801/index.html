Full description

Portrait Alongside the kindergarten, the Kreuzfeld School Centre is one of the four school centres of the Langenthal Elementary School. It is located in the center of the city (about 16'000 inhabitants) and is easily accessible by public transport. The approximately 400 children and young people are taught by about 45 teachers in 20 primary classes. In addition, about 40 children and young people are trained in 4 classes for special support. Lessons take place in the two school buildings Kreuzfeld 1 (cycle 2) and Kreuzfeld 2 (cycle 1). Further details can be found at www.volksschule-langenthal.ch or https://www.instagram.com/schulekreuzfeld langenthal

Tasks

As a class teacher, you teach your subjects and your workload at a third grade, initially limited to two years.

Requirements

  • You have a Bachelor of Arts in Primary Education or a teaching patent of a teacher seminar.
  • You are an independent, dutiful, communicative and resilient personality and a good relationship with the students is of central importance to you.

We offer

  • -A temporary employment according to cantonal employment conditions (LAG).
  • -Diverse options for teaching according to curriculum 21.
  • An exciting workplace with a high level of personal responsibility.
  • -A very good, well-rehearsed step team. (3-4 parallel classes per year)
  • -All teachers employed by us have appropriate training.
  • Great support through mentoring, curative education and school social work.
  • -A very good infrastructure.
