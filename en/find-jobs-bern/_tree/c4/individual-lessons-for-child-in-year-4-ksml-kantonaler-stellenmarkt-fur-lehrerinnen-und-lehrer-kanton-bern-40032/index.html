Full description

Portrait The school Wald is a small, family school on the Langenberg and is located on the edge of the forest between the villages Zimmerwald and Englisberg. At the moment, 70 children are taught in a kindergarten class and three mixed-age school classes from the 1st-6th year of school.

Tasks

Individual lessons for children in the 4. School year, which is not allowed to attend school for health reasons until probably in October. They teach on three or four half-days (DI, MI and FR, morning or afternoon) 2-3 lessons each (weekly 6-8 lessons) in the child’s apartment in Zimmerwald: mathematics, German and French. Assignments in consultation with the class teacher.

Requirements

You have a primary teaching patent or a Bachelor for the primary level and, if possible, bring professional experience as a teacher. They are a dutiful, communicative and resilient personality and are accustomed to dealing with children of this age.

We offer

  • Good support from the class teacher
  • Easily accessible by public transport: Bern - U-pass (train) followed by post bus to Zimmerwald Post, then 10 minutes walk
