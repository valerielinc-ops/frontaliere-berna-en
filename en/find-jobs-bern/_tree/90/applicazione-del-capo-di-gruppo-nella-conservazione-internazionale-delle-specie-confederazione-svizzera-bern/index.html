Put to the Point

They are responsible for implementing Swiss legislation in the context of international species protection and illegal marine fishing and thus actively contribute to the conservation of global biodiversity. In doing so, you rely on your management and enforcement experience as well as sound knowledge of the legal application.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20621
  • Location: Schwarzenburgstrasse 155, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Implementation in the fields of international species protection (CITES), import of marine fishery products (IUU) and import of fur personnel, organizational control, quality assurance and technical development
  • Designing and digitally developing approval systems efficiently and punishing violations in a uniform, comprehensible and legally compliant manner
  • Prosecute illicit trade activities at the border, domestically and online trade as well as technically accompany and coordinate legal follow-up and sanctions
  • Promote risk-based and impact-oriented enforcement, develop appropriate control concepts and coordinate cooperation with the BAZG, cantonal enforcement bodies and external control bodies
  • shaping projects and cross-cutting topics in the areas of digitalisation, process development, data and knowledge management and legislation
  • Establish, operate and further develop control regimes in the areas of fur and IUU certification programmes

This makes them unique.

  • Law or biology, environmental or natural sciences degree related to the application of law
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in the implementation of federal law or in a comparable regulatory environment as well as in the application and enforcement of legal bases
  • Experience in authorisation and control procedures, in cooperation with law enforcement authorities and in managing projects and interdisciplinary working groups
  • Strong leadership, communication and stakeholder competence
  • Negotiable communication in two official languages and in English
  • Category B driving licence
