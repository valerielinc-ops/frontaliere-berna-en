Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

Are you interested in financial markets and would like to put your theoretical knowledge from your studies into practice? Then this could be an exciting opportunity for you! In PostFinance's risk management, we are responsible for the financial risks of our balance sheet. Our goal is to actively manage PostFinance's interest rate and liquidity risks. With us, you will learn the basic concepts and methods of risk management. Since we cover a wide range of topics, there are various opportunities to contribute and develop your skills with us, depending on your strengths and interests

What you can do

  • You will help manage one of the largest bank balance sheets in Switzerland
  • You will support us in actively managing interest rate and liquidity risks
  • You have an important role in the further development of the tools used
  • You will measure interest rate and liquidity risks using existing models, interpret the results and prepare them

What you bring to the table

  • You have a bachelor's degree in physics, mathematics, computer science, engineering, economics or economics that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your bachelor's degree
  • Independent, structured and systematic work
  • Strong curiosity, great willingness to learn and the willingness to deal with economic issues
  • Programming skills, for example in R
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are an advantage
