Tasks

We are seeking a Data Analyst to join a leading player in the high-precision industrial sector. In a demanding and innovative environment, you will play a key role in ensuring the quality, consistency, and reliability of the data used by operational teams.

  • Responsibilities:Create and maintain data records, ensuring their consistency and integrity.Ensure alignment between data stored within information systems and actual operational activities.Analyze data quality, identify discrepancies, and implement the necessary corrective actions.Actively participate in continuous improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing data reliability and stability.Contribute to the definition, evolution, and maintenance of standards related to operational times and associated industrial data.Perform analyses and estimations required for production time management and ensure the entry of reference data.Support migration, system evolution, and transformation projects by ensuring data quality, compliance, and consistency.Provide first-level support to business teams in handling anomalies, investigations, and operational requests.Participate in functional testing campaigns.Collaborate with a wide range of stakeholders in a cross-functional and multidisciplinary environment.

  • Requirements

Qualifications:Degree in an industrial field or an equivalent qualification.Proven experience in data management and data governance within an industrial environment.Strong knowledge of ERP systems, as well as PLM solutions.Experience with functional testing in multi-system environments.Ability to analyze, structure, and ensure the reliability of complex datasets.Knowledge of Apriso FlexNet and Tableau is considered an advantage.Excellent analytical, communication, and collaboration skills in a multidisciplinary environment.Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and a methodical approach.Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work effectively within a team.Fluency in French
