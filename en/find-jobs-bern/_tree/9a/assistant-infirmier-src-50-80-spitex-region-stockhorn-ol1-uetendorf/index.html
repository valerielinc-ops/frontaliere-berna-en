We are a SPITEX organization with 24 employees. Our catchment area comprises 6 municipalities with around 14,000 inhabitants. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an agreement Nursing assistant/in SRK, 50 - 80 % They care for and support sick, disabled and elderly people and enable them to remain in their usual environment. As an independent, responsible specialist with completed SRK course, if possible with SPITEX experience (possibly re-entry) and high professional and social competence, you are one of the pillars of our team.
