Put to the Point

To strengthen our team, we are looking for two experienced data compliance professionals. Both functions include a comparable range of tasks with different technical focuses. For one position, we are looking for a professional with in-depth experience in governance and further development related to audit and internal control system (ICS), for the other a professional with in-depth experience in the implementation and organizational anchoring of data compliance.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 403-20215
  • Location: Guisanplatz 1A, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • To operate, develop and anchor data compliance management system (DCMS) in the organization and monitor its effectiveness.
  • Further develop governance principles, guidelines, roles and processes and convert them into systems and operational workflows.
  • Identify and evaluate data compliance risks as well as accompany the implementation of appropriate measures to control them.
  • Carry out and further develop risk-based examinations and assessments. Derive and track improvement measures.
  • Lead or accompany projects and change projects at the interface of law, organization and IT and advise directorates and departments on data compliance requirements.
  • design and implement training, awareness-raising and communication measures.

This makes them unique.

  • university degree at master’s level or comparable education as well as in-depth knowledge of data protection and information law.
  • At least five years of practical experience in compliance and governance as well as in-depth experience in the development and implementation of governance structures, standards, roles and processes or compliance management systems.
  • In particular, the governance and further development position seeks in-depth experience related to audit and internal control system (ICS); Experience in risk management is beneficial.
  • Strong understanding of data, information systems and business processes as well as their legal and organizational relationships.
  • Experience in the management or support of complex projects and change projects in the compliance and governance environment as well as the ability to quickly capture, structure and communicate complex issues in a user-friendly manner.
  • Good oral and written knowledge in two official languages as well as in English, each at least at level B2.
