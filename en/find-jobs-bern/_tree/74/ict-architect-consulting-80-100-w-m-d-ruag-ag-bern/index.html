Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Support and advice of projects and specialist areas in the disciplines of enterprise architecture, business architecture and/or system architecture
  • Model-based acquisition of actual and target architectures based on meta-models and architecture frameworks (e.g. TOGAF, ArchiMate, arc42)
  • Development of architectural documents and decision-making basis (e.g. architectural sketches, system and interface overviews) using different project methods such as SAFe or HERMES
  • Analyze relationships between business processes, organizational structures and systems
  • Applying the customer’s architectural principles and frameworks as well as checking solutions for conformity
  • Conduct specialist presentations and training on architecture methods, metamodels and concepts

You bring that with you

  • Completed training in (economic) computer science or related field (higher education or higher vocational training) or equivalent experience.
  • Field-proven application of ArchiMate and experience with BPMN or UML as well as knowledge of architecture frameworks (e.g. TOGAF, arc42).
  • Experience in the structured translation of customer requirements into architectural models and documents as well as enjoyment of customer contact (workshops, presentations)
  • Analytical, structured working method and rapid incorporation into new specialist domains
  • Ideally, military knowledge or interest in getting involved in this area

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
