Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services for older people and employs around 4'300 staff members. Outpatient and inpatient services complement each other seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is Switzerland's leading provider of private home-care services. The Care Quality Team at Senevita Casa (Spitex) is looking for an employee to start immediately or by agreement, who will work for Senevita Casa as a practice partner and quality officer.

Advanced Practice Nurse (APN) 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • Advising, supporting and empowering the nursing teams at the individual Senevita Casa locations in challenging care situations
  • Independently conducting nursing assessments, clinical examinations and evidence-based interventions at the individual Senevita Casa locations in particularly complex care situations
  • Identifying practice-relevant issues and recognizing development potential
  • Developing, implementing and monitoring the implementation of guidelines, nursing and quality standards
  • Conducting training sessions and continuing education for the nursing teams at the individual locations
  • Contributing to overarching quality topics

What you bring

  • You have a Master of Science in Nursing degree and several years of practical professional experience in geriatrics, preferably with experience in the Spitex environment
  • Enjoy working as a coordinator at various interfaces
  • Strong interpersonal skills, the ability to communicate appropriately with different audiences even in difficult situations, and to bring the various strands together as a team player
  • The ability to work in a focused and goal-oriented manner in demanding change situations
  • Willingness to work at various locations within Switzerland (willingness to travel)
  • Good spoken and written knowledge of French and German is an advantage
  • Knowledge of Italian and/or English is an advantage

We offer you

  • A varied role with opportunities to shape it
  • A high degree of independence and personal responsibility
  • Good employee benefits and at least 5 weeks of vacation (6 weeks from age 50, or 7 weeks from age 60)
  • Various discounts (fuel, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacies, etc.)
  • Our training and continuing education offering is impressive

Sounds interesting? - We look forward to meeting you in person! Send us your application documents online.
