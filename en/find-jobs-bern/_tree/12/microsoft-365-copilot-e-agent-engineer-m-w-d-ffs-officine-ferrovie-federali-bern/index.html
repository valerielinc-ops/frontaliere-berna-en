Microsoft 365 Copilot & Agent Engineer (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Microsoft 365 Copilot & Agent Engineer (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You help shape the digital workplace of around 35,000 employees and ensure a modern, intuitive working environment that enables productive and location-independent collaboration. The focus is on developing Copilot services for Microsoft 365 and bringing artificial intelligence safely and responsibly into the everyday life of users. You can expect an interdisciplinary, agile environment with close cooperation between engineering, security, data protection and the specialist areas. You take responsibility, have a tangible impact and continue to develop around Microsoft 365, AI engineering and governance. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As an Application Engineer with a focus on Microsoft 365 Copilot and Agents, you design, develop and test together with the specialist areas Copilot Solutions and Agents, in particular with Microsoft 365 Copilot Agent Builder and in addition with Copilot Studio.You advise the specialist areas technically, coordinate with Product Owner, Architecture, Security, Data Protection and Platform Teams and ensure compliance with our AI and Agent Governance.
  • From intake to prototyping and deployment to operation, review and commissioning, you accompany the entire lifecycle of the agents and gradually assume responsibility.
  • Using Microsoft Graph connectors, REST APIs and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), you securely bind company data and functions and implement suitable concepts for authentication, permissions and accesses.
  • Together with the team, you ensure the stable operation of the Copilot services, analyze incidents and problems, process technical requests in second and third level support and improve services based on your findings.
  • Changes in the Microsoft Message Center and product roadmap are actively tracked, evaluating configuration, governance and support impacts, and coordinating tests, rollouts, and maintenance of configuration, monitoring, documentation and runbooks.

You bring that with you.

  • With your analytical and structured working method, you solve technical problems in a targeted manner and communicate complex issues in an understandable manner to different target groups.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft 365, Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Studio as well as relevant data and authorization models. Familiarity with Microsoft Graph and REST APIs to securely connect enterprise data.
  • Practice in the development and integration of AI, cloud or automation solutions. Programming experience in at least one language, preferably Python,.NET or C#, as well as handling version management and continuous delivery.
  • You understand the basics of generative AI, large language models (LLM), retrieval augmented generation (RAG) and appropriate evaluation methods. Knowledge of authentication and authorization with Entra ID and secure data access in the enterprise environment.
  • Team-oriented and strong in communication, you work closely with specialist areas, security, data protection and platform teams.
  • Completed studies in computer science or business informatics or comparable education and training as well as several years of relevant professional experience. German at level C1 and English at level B1.
