Full description

Portrait Lengnau School is a living place of learning and personal development. Our school covers from kindergarten to 9. Around 650 students, accompanied by a dedicated team of 85 teachers.

The continuous development of our school is particularly important to us – both for our students and in the college. The municipality of Lengnau supports us with the necessary resources to provide optimal learning conditions for young people in our modern learning and working spaces.

We are also happy to introduce our school in more detail under the following link: https://youtu.be/9EKn3A0rN2Y?si=NtkdkoyVqmRoZMrf

Tasks

We are looking for a 4. Class a teacher, student of a PH or a person who knows well with children and can support a teacher on Wednesday morning. Depending on the possibilities and person, further support would be required on Monday or Thursday morning. Similarly, the form of support depends on the person making the request (depending on the level of training, team teaching or class help is agreed).

One of the tasks would be that you accompany the class into the sport on Wednesday morning.

In addition, your tasks are

Help with everyday and not directly relevant activities (e.g. accompaniment on the way to the gymnastics lessons, supervision of the SuS when arriving and leaving at school).

  • Accompaniment of individual children with the aim of mastering the processes at school independently (e.g. behavior during classes, in group or individual work).
  • Supervise certain processes (e.g. group or individual work) and ensure in different rooms (e.g. group room, hallway, break area).
  • Administrative and logistical support during lessons (e.g. preparation of classrooms, collection of student documents, etc.).

Requirements

-Due to the heterogeneity of the class, we prefer a person who has the teaching diploma or is on the way to it. Alternatively, you have experience in the educational field.

  • You are a dedicated, resilient and humorous personality with pleasure in working with children.
  • They are motivated to accompany, promote and support the children along with the teachers on their learning path.
  • They have the willingness to make arrangements and work together within the class team and college.
  • You are flexible to the extent that you support the teachers once on an excursion or an event in the evening.

We offer

A dedicated, open-minded and helpful college awaits you at an innovative school.

We offer

  • An appreciative and collegial cooperation
  • Support through reliable and practical school management
  • Modern infrastructure and contemporary teaching materials
  • School social work, day school and other support services
  • short decision-making paths and room for manoeuvre in class

Tolerance, respect and appreciation shape our school culture. We look forward to welcoming you to our team. Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application.

Application interviews are ongoing. The position can be filled as soon as we have found the right personality.
