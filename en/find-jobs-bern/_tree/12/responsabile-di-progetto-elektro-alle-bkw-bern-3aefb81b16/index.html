Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Electrical projects up to CHF 4 million | Own project team | Plenty of design space

At swisspro NW in Bern, you lead challenging electrical projects, lead your own project team and actively shape our future. Short decision-making paths, plenty of space and a team that holds together await you. Your ideas are not only welcome with us, they are in demand.

Your tasks

  • You lead your own project team, foster collaboration and create an environment where everyone can get involved.
  • You accompany your projects in the Berne area independently from planning to successful completion.
  • You take on a wide range of electrical projects with a volume of up to CHF 4 million, from residential construction to catering and healthcare to industry, pharmaceuticals and building automation.
  • You always keep an eye on the quality, safety, deadlines and profitability of your projects.
  • You create schedules and assignments and make sure that intermediate and final billings are done cleanly and on time.
  • You also have the numbers of your projects under control: Monthly figures, accounting status and liquidity you keep an eye on and, if necessary, follow up on time.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an electrical installer: in EFZ and bring further training in the field of electrical project management as well as several years of professional experience.
  • You know the project business from practice and know what is important for a successful implementation.
  • Organizing, assuming responsibility and clearly communicating are important to you even if several projects are running at the same time.
  • You enjoy working with people and understand how to take your team as well as your customers.
  • You want to contribute your experience, take on responsibility and develop professionally and personally.
