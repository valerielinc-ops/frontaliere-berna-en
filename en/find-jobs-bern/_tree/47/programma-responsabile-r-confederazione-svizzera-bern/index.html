Put to the Point

In the performance area of economic cooperation and development, the Infrastructure Financing (WEIN) Department provides support for the provision of economic infrastructure in the framework of the implementation of economic and financial assistance to developing countries, for example in the areas of energy, mobility, water and urban development.

  • Entry date: 01.02.2027 or by arrangement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20850
  • Location: Holzikofenweg 36, 3007 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Support and take responsibility for selected regional and thematic infrastructure dossiers
  • independently assess and structure infrastructure projects and programmes as well as accompany their implementation
  • Working closely with government agencies, companies and banks and other (multilateral) donors
  • Develop conceptual contributions to the establishment and development of the instruments and the programme
  • Draft positions and contributions on topics and regions in the area of responsibility

This makes them unique.

  • University or ETH degree
  • At least five years of professional experience in the economic or infrastructure sector and experience in cooperation with developing countries and in project management
  • Experience in planning, financing, infrastructure management and/or business development of utilities (e.g. energy, transport) is beneficial
  • Analytical skills, editorial skill, negotiating skills and resilience
  • active knowledge of two official languages in word and writing and passive knowledge of the third official language; very good oral and written English skills due to frequent international contacts

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
