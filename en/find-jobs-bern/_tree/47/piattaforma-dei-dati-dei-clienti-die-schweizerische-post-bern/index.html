Full description

You understand how to transfer the business value for the departments into technical innovation? You have technical A-resinity? Then we should get to know each other!

You are the product owner of customer data platform and you are responsible for the future of the customer interaction with the post office. You are responsible for central data products around customer master data and analytics.

You Can Make That Do

  • You are responsible for the technical and strategic development of the Customer Data Core along the entire product life cycle, ensuring the fulfilment of business value, reusability, quality, governance and compliance.
  • In cooperation with the DevOps team, you take responsibility for fulfilling the SLAs of the service and budgeting development costs.
  • In a diverse and complex environment, you coordinate the collaboration between business and IT and the company as a central contact person for your product and keep the focus on the essentials.
  • Together with stakeholders from business, architecture, data science, analytics and engineering, you develop a clear product vision and roadmap. You will also be able to update requirements, features and technical developments with a focus on measurable added value for the specialist departments and the customers of the post office.
  • You ensure that new data products, data sources and analytical capabilities are seamlessly integrated into the CDC platform. Together with the DevOps team, you develop innovative information-driven solutions for topics such as reporting, segmentation, contact strategy, campaign management and customer analytics.
  • Based on defined key figures, you measure the success of your products and continuously conduct optimization measures, with customers always at the center.

You bring this with you

  • Higher technical or higher education in the fields of computer science, business computer science or equivalent qualifications forms the basis of your expertise.
  • You have many years of experience as a Product Owner in the development of the software as well as in stakeholder management and can exchange views with suppliers or partners on technical topics and combine social skills with a consistent customer orientation.
  • In the data environment, you know the relevant reputables and understand the basics of data modelling and the security requirements of different data classifications.
  • You are well-known with the current collaboration tools and the current network of agile frameworks.
  • German skills and good English skills complete your profile and enable you to actively participate in multilingual projects.
