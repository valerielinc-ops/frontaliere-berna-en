Put to the Point

They actively shape the governance of the WBF and develop new department-relevant topics from conception to implementation. They lead complex change projects, are responsible for governance and security issues at departmental level and ensure coordinated cooperation with the offices.

  • Entry Date: By Agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20753
  • Location: Theaterplatz 8, 3011 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Develop, develop and further develop new areas of responsibility related to departmental governance
  • Leading internal transformation projects and actively controlling process optimizations in the Department and General Secretariat
  • Advise the department and offices of the WBF on governance issues and ensure the implementation of the recommendations of the Swiss Federal Financial Control (EFK) and the Corporate Governance Principles of the Federal Government
  • Organize and coordinate information security in the department in close cooperation with the offices of the WBF
  • Responsible for, handling and coordinating the topics of physical security, emergency management, exemplary role of the Federal Administration in the climate sector and building management at the WBF in the department
  • Further develop the crisis organisation of the WBF in accordance with federal requirements and provide leadership support in the event of a crisis

This makes them unique.

  • University degree (uni, FH/HF), preferably in business administration, or equivalent education. Several years of experience in managing governance projects in a political environment are beneficial
  • Active, independent and well-structured personality with self-responsible and solution-oriented working methods
  • Analytical approach to open up new topics, tackle challenges constructively with foresight and derive implementable options for action
  • Interest in topics of legally binding governance as well as the effect of governance and leadership in the federal administration
  • A strong sense for different target groups whose needs are recognized and win with convincing arguments for upcoming projects
  • Very good active knowledge of two official languages and passive knowledge of a third official language
