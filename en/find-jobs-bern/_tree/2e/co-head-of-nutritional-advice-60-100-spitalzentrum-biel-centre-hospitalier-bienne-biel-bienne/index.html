Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

Do you want to take on leadership, stay close to nutrition advice and make a difference together with a committed team? Then we look forward to meeting you. As co-leader of nutrition consultancy, you lead the areas of inpatient and outpatient nutrition consulting together with your co-leader and actively shape its professional, personnel and organizational development. Entrance by 1. December 2026 or by agreement

Co-Head of Nutritional Advice, 60-100%

They do.

  • Leadership, support and promotion of the employees of the ambulatory nutrition consultancy
  • Ensuring high-quality, patient- and evidence-based nutrition advice
  • Organization and further development of processes, structures and the team

  • Promoting an open culture of feedback, learning and reflection

That sets them apart.

  • Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Advice
  • Professional experience of several years
  • management experience or further training in personnel management and management in the healthcare sector, or willingness to do so
  • Very good technical and practical knowledge
  • Strong leadership, communication and social skills
  • Joy of development, change and collaboration
  • Good knowledge of German and French in words and writing

They benefit from it.

A versatile management function with room for manoeuvre, professional proximity to everyday work and the opportunity to further develop nutrition consulting together with your co-management.
