SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. In this exciting and responsible position, as Finance Business Partner (m/f/d), you actively shape the financial management of several Group divisions. You will gain insight into the diverse Group services and experience the daily cooperation with various stakeholders. As a co-pilot, you work closely with the business managers and contribute significantly to the strategic orientation, financial management and successful implementation of projects. Look forward to an exciting working environment in which your skills are promoted and you contribute to the sustainable performance of SBB. Become part of the Finance Group divisions team and move Switzerland with us. You can move that.

  • As a Finance Business Partner (m/f/d), you are the driving force behind the financial management of several Group divisions in close cooperation with the responsible business managers regarding business issues and decisions. This includes joint responsibility with the business and active participation in the roadmap, projects and risk management.
  • You shape the financial future of the Group by developing consistent and resilient financial plans for the Latest Estimate, budget and medium-term corporate planning.
  • You play a key role in achieving your financial goals by providing management with sound decision-making, performance assessments and proactive action proposals.
  • You live a sustainable performance management culture by conducting constructive-critical dialogues on an equal footing with business managers and optimizing the necessary financial control instruments.

You bring that with you.

  • You have a sovereign appearance to be able to contribute and implement the interests of finance diplomatically but clearly and have good communication skills at various hierarchical levels up to the management.
  • Your high solution and ambitious implementation orientation is combined with great flexibility and resilience.
  • You have at least 5 years of controlling/financial or consulting experience, ideally in performance management. Initial leadership experience and knowledge of SAP-FI/CO are beneficial.
  • We presuppose an economic (specialist) university degree with a focus on finance & controlling, business administration, strategy or an equivalent further education.
  • In German you communicate verbally and in writing stylish confident (level C2), good knowledge of English and another national language are advantageous.
