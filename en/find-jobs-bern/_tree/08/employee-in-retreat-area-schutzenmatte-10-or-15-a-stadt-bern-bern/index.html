Full description

We are looking for two personalities for the field of socioculture. The retreat room on the Schützenmatte is a low-swelling retreat and shelter for people in the nightlife. We accompany people in stressful situations, in crises, in violent or borderline experiences, as well as in mental or physical overload. The offer is mainly on the Schützenmatte during the nights from Friday to Saturday and Saturday to Sunday. The work is done in the team of two and in cooperation with other actors on site. We work calmly, attentively and professionally: with a clear attitude and without an intervention order. The principle applies: as little as possible, as much as necessary. Gender equality and diversity are important to us. We are pleased about applications from FINTA* people.

Tasks

  • Support, support and advice for protection seekers during the night
  • Presence work and low-swelling contact in public space
  • Capability of action in conflict, crisis and stress situations
  • Cooperation with partners on site and placement at specialist offices
  • Participation in team meetings, supervision and further training

Requirements

  • Training in social, health or psychosocial matters
  • Experience in dealing with people in stressful or draping situations
  • Security and reflectability in conflict and crisis situations
  • Ability to endure and remain present
  • Interest in low-level and presence-oriented work
  • Willingness to work at night and weekends
