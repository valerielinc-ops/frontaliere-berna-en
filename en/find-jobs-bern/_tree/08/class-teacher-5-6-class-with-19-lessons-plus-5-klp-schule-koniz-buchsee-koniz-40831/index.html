Full description

Portrait The school Köniz Stecke is a quarter school in the center of Köniz with 23 classes and 470 pupils, who are taught by 55 teachers in basic and multi-year classes. The didactics of age-mixed learning have a correspondingly high weight. A very lively and innovative school with a great and well-positioned team is looking forward to your application.

Tasks

From January 2027 we are looking for a new class teacher at one of our 5th/6th classes.

The teaching days are as follows

Monday morning and afternoon, Wednesday morning, Thursday morning, Friday morning.

The subjects of instruction are as follows

NMG 4L, French 3L (incl. 2L half-class lessons), German 6L (incl. 2L half-class lessons), English 2L, Sport 3L and M/I 1L In addition, 5% for the class magisterium.

The transition talks are already organized and do not affect the employment.

The position is limited until the end of school year 26/27.

Here we only carry basic levels and multi-year classes. Are you convinced of the pedagogy of mixed age learning and interested in working at a larger but very collegial school? Then we are very happy to expect your application with the usual documents by mail or KSML portal.

Requirements

We are looking for a motivated teacher with the following qualifications:

  • Completed or ongoing studies Pedagogical College
  • Enjoying mixed-age teaching
  • Enjoyment of working in a team
  • Independent and responsible working practices
  • Resilience and flexibility

We offer

-An innovative and vibrant school Integration into the team of Cycle 2 Collegial support and opportunity for supportive cooperation -Working cooperation within the committed college Support from school management, school social work and teachers -A school that is easy to reach with public transport
