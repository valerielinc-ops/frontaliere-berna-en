You can make this contribution

  • Epidemiological expertise in the field of infectious diseases: statistically analyze data from various FOPH monitoring and reporting systems (R) and interpret them in the sense of the Public Health Action Cycle
  • Write epidemiological reports for various communication channels (BAG web, presentations, conference contributions, media inquiries, etc.)
  • Maintain cooperation with partners at national and international level such as national reference laboratories and university institutions and organise events
  • Working on cross-sectional projects and tasks within the FOPH, with other federal offices and cantonal offices (e.g. workshops with the cantonal medical services, national strategies, updating of legal bases, reporting guidelines, answering political inquiries)

This makes them unique.

  • Academic degree in medicine or natural sciences with further education in epidemiology / biostatistics and/or Master of Public Health
  • Good knowledge of the analysis and presentation of epidemiological data using R
  • Infectious diseases and/or microbiology education or training or proven continuous training
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking as well as good editorial skills, knowledge of the procedures in the federal administration are advantageous
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages and English
  • Team and communication skills, personal responsibility, goal and result orientation, flexibility in crisis situations, resilience
