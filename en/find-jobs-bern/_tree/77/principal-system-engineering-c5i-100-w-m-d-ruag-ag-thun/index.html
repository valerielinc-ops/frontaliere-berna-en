Full description

Responsibility

  • Professional responsibility for System Engineering C5I
  • Overall responsibility for the design, specification and further development of ICT system and infrastructure architecture
  • Advising project teams on complex technical issues as well as architecture and infrastructure issues
  • Analysis and continuous optimization of the system and platform landscape to ensure a stable, scalable and powerful ICT infrastructure
  • Definition of technical standards and best practices for the operation and development of the platforms
  • Promote cooperation with teams from other disciplines for interface optimization

Requirements

  • University degree in computer science or equivalent education supplemented with a system engineering further education
  • Several years of profound experience in the design, implementation and further development of complex ICT infrastructures
  • Broad understanding of technology and experience in the design of modern system architectures
  • Extensive knowledge of network, firewall and server technologies in security-critical environments
  • Structured and conscientious personality with maker qualities and high willingness to perform
  • fluent knowledge of German
  • English skills (level B2) and French are advantageous
  • Military career as a senior non-commissioned officer or officer of advantage
