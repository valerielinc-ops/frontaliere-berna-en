Questions about the application

Recruiting NDB/SRC/SIC/FIS

Together for the safety of Switzerland!

The NDB deals with early detection and combating terrorism, violent extremism, espionage, the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction and their carrier technology, and cyber attacks on critical infrastructures. In addition, the NDB provides and evaluates important information in security policy. It thus provides decisive contributions to the comprehensive assessment of the threat situation.

At federal level, the NDB primarily serves the Federal Council, the departments and the military leadership with its products. It also supports the cantons in the maintenance of internal security and the law enforcement authorities at federal level.

Women and persons from the French and Italian languages communities are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
