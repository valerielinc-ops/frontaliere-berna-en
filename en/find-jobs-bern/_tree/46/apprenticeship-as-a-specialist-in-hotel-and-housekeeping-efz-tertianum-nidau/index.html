Full description

Get your EFZ through an apprenticeship in housekeeping. With us, you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a specialist hotel industry business, a supportive working environment awaits you from day one. During your training, you will be accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

For the house Tertianum Villa Sutter in Nidau we are looking for you at 1. August 2027 for the

Apprenticeship as a specialist / specialist hotel industry-housekeeping EFZ

You will learn this with us

  • General cleaning work
  • Laundry supply and ordering
  • Care for guests and take over the service
  • Receive and process feedback and information from guests
  • Supporting the kitchen team
  • Implementation of operational processes and compliance with work quality requirements
  • Decorating rooms and premises

You bring that with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic achievements
  • Great willingness to learn and a sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Careful appearance, good manners as well as hygiene awareness and sense of order
  • Creativity and an eye for detail
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying versatile challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
