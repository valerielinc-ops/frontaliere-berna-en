Full description

Portrait We are a lively village school with around 550 students, spread over three school locations and five kindergartens. A new school building is currently being built that expands our learning and living space and opens up new educational opportunities. We see this development as an opportunity to actively shape schools and implement innovative ideas. We attach great importance to an appreciative community in which children, teachers and professionals can feel comfortable and develop. We see diversity as a strength and design our teaching so that all students can learn and grow according to their individual requirements. A dedicated, interdisciplinary team works closely together and continuously develops teaching. Cooperation, mutual support and an open communication culture shape our everyday life. Due to the cantonal reallocation of the lessons, we are allowed to expand our MR team by two full-time positions from SJ 27/28.

Tasks

  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of individual funding measures in cycle 2
  • Inclusive promotion of students in classroom teaching or small groups
  • Close cooperation with class teachers, specialists and school management
  • Advising teachers and parents on curative pedagogical issues
  • Participation in site discussions and funding planning
  • Active participation in school and teaching development

Requirements

  • Completed education in curative curative education (or in training)
  • Experience in integrative setting is an advantage
  • Enjoy working with heterogeneous learning groups
  • Teamwork, flexibility and high social skills
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Interest in school development and inclusive education

We offer

  • An appreciative and supportive team culture
  • Close cooperation in teaching
  • room for manoeuvre in the promotion and teaching development
  • Contemporary employment conditions and training opportunities
  • Flexible working time models and a good work-life balance
  • Technical support by college and school management
