Do you not only want to manage a brand, but actively shape its future? You can do that.

About us

We are a unique, specialized company in the pharmaceutical and home care sector, emerged from our pharmacy in the heart of Interlaken. Bichsel is a leading provider of pharmaceutical contract manufacturing (drugs) for healthcare professionals, with a focus on the Swiss hospital landscape, but also for doctors and pharmacies. We cover a key gap in the availability of important medicines. Bichsel plays a central role in home care services at the interface of inpatient to outpatient care, especially in artificial nutrition and infusion therapies.
