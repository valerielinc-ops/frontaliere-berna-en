Put to the Point

They play a central role in the strategic development of an ambitious real estate portfolio of the Federal Civil Administration. You make an important contribution to a sustainable, needs-based and future-oriented use of real estate.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 620-20451
  • Location: Fellerstrasse 21, 3027 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for the strategy, profitability and sustainable development of the allocated real estate portfolio and align the use of real estate in the long term
  • Participating in the real estate strategy of the federal civil administration, setting strategic guidelines for projects and developing sustainable and demand-oriented solutions
  • Planning and controlling project loans for the respective portfolio, assessing loan applications, securing financing and initializing strategic projects and the maintenance program
  • Advise departments and offices on strategic issues in German and French, assess their needs, conduct negotiations and develop the basis for decision-making for the relevant bodies
  • Communicating BBL decisions and ensuring their consistent implementation and representing BBL’s interests vis-à-vis internal and external partners at strategic level

This makes them unique.

  • university degree or comparable higher education, preferably in real estate management, architecture, construction, business administration or a related field of study
  • Several years of experience in strategic real estate, portfolio or project management as well as proven experience in the management of complex projects and in cooperation with different stakeholders
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills, high level of business expertise and the ability to quickly grasp complex relationships and develop sustainable solutions
  • Negotiation-proof and convincing appearance, a high level of consulting competence as well as the ability to combine different interests and to represent decisions confidently even in demanding situations
  • Very good knowledge of two official languages
  • Knowledge of third parties is advantageous
