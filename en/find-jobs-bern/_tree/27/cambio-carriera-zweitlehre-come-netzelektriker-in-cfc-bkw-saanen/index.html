Full description

Apprenticeship start August 2027, earlier employment possible (Gstaad)

Are you tired of your current job and want to reorient yourself? Do you want to work outside and a job with a future? As a grid electrician: in you actively shape the energy supply. You can expect team spirit, interesting construction sites and financial security during your training.

Your tasks

As a future grid electrician, you will take on a versatile and varied area of responsibility. From your base, you and the team actively contribute to the electricity flowing into the households. You build, operate and maintain our facilities in the region and carry out assembly work in the low or medium voltage network. You help connect a newly built building to the distribution grid and ensure the correct wiring at a transformer station. You work with different materials such as wood or metal, perform work on the ground or in height and operate larger and smaller machines and vehicles.

You bring that with you

  • You are ready to complete the two- or three-year apprenticeship as a grid electrician: in EFZ
  • You want to reorient yourself and are motivated in a team from the beginning
  • You like to work outside and are not afraid to work at altitude
  • Your team can always rely on you
  • Ideally, you have a category B driving licence

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
