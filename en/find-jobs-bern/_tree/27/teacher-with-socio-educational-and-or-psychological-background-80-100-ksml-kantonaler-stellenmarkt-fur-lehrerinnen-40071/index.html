We are looking for the special elementary school Klipp & Klar, transitional schooling area in Ipsach, immediately or by appointment

A teacher with a social-educational and/or psychological background 80-100%

Tasks

As a special elementary school in the canton of Bern (bVSA), Klipp & Klar provides individual schooling for normally and highly gifted children and young people. At the Ipsach location, we offer a school bridging period for a maximum of 10 children and young people after completing their stay in a psychiatric program for a maximum period of 13 weeks.

The focus of individualized schooling in individual and group settings is ensuring continuity in the learning process. The aim is to promote stability and orientation as well as reintegration into a long-term school solution in the mainstream or special school sector.

In addition to specialist lessons, everyday practical, social and emotional skills are also promoted.

We trust in the development of potential

  • Experience in designing individual and holistic educational offerings for children and young people.
  • Openness to working in an innovative team and contributing pedagogical knowledge.
  • Open to seeking creative solutions to a variety of difficulties.
  • Open to teamwork and at the same time able to act independently.
  • Social pedagogical knowledge for designing the daily structure.
  • Enjoy networking and writing reports
  • Ability to build relationships quickly.
