Put to the Point

As an expert in cleaning technology, you maintain the impressive federal buildings in Bern and the surrounding area. Apply today for the apprenticeship from August 2027. We look forward to seeing you!

  • Date of entry: 01.08.2027

You can make this contribution

  • Clean and maintain equipment, machines and tools

  • Team-oriented and good manners
