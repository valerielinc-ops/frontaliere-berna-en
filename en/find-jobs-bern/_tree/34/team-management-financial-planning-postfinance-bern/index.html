Full description

We continue to develop financial planning as an integral part of customer advice and support our customers in important financial decisions at different stages of their lives. Together with your team, you ensure first-class consulting quality, strengthen financial planning as an integral part of our consulting offer and further develop the business area with a view to future customer needs.

You can do that.

  • You lead, coach and develop a regional team of financial planners: within and promote an appreciative, performance-oriented cooperation, also across different locations
  • You are responsible for the achievement of your team’s goals and control the relevant performance and quality indicators
  • You ensure a consistent, high-quality and customer-oriented consulting experience.
  • You support your team in challenging client mandates and bring your financial planning expertise to complex consulting situations
  • You will continue to develop financial planning strategically and provide impetus for the expansion of entrepreneurship, succession and estate planning
  • You work closely with customer consulting, sales, specialist areas and other stakeholders and position financial planning as an important success factor in customer consulting
  • You recognize market trends and customer needs at an early stage and derive concrete further developments for offering, consulting and cooperation
  • You represent financial planning internally and externally and make an active contribution to the successful development of the business

You bring that with you

  • Training or further education as a financial planner: with a federal certificate, financial planning expert: in, CFP or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in holistic financial planning as well as in-depth knowledge in the areas of pensions, retirement, asset structuring, taxes and succession and estate planning
  • Initial management experience or proven experience in the professional management and development of employees
  • Enjoy empowering people to actively shape change and lead teams successfully even at a distance
  • Entrepreneurial thinking as well as experience in the further development of services, consulting approaches or business fields
  • High social, communication and networking skills as well as the ability to attract different stakeholders for common goals
  • Negotiation-secure knowledge of German as well as ideally negotiating-secure knowledge of French, as well as willingness to travel regularly within the region

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
