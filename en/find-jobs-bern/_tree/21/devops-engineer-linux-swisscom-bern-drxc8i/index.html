# DevOps Engineer Linux

Il tuo futuro inizia

We accompany the army on its digitalisation journey – with the aim of strengthening Switzerland’s defence capability and making targeted use of the opportunities offered by technological progress. The new digitalisation platform forms the basis for an independent and crisis-resistant ICT infrastructure. As DevOps Engineer Linux, you will continue to operate, automate and develop a highly secure private cloud for the Army’s digital platform. Together with an interdisciplinary team, you ensure a stable, highly available operation and use modern technologies and DevSecOps principles. In this way, you make a direct contribution to the digital transformation of the army and the security of Switzerland.

  • Operation and automation of a highly secure private cloud in the environment of the army’s digital platform
  • Responsibility for operation, monitoring, incidents, changes and the continuous development of the platform
  • Co-design of a highly available and resilient infrastructure according to DevSecOps principles
  • Use of state-of-the-art technologies in a safety-critical and innovative environment
  • Cooperation in agile teams and direct contribution to the digital transformation of the Swiss Army

Your skills

  • Degree (ETH/Uni/FH) in computer science or equivalent degree or sound professional experience
  • Several years of experience in administration of Linux systems and automation with Ansible
  • Certifications such as RHCE (Red Hat Certified Engineer), LFCE (Linux Foundation Certified Engineer) or similar beneficial
  • Experience with virtualization solutions (KVM) and high-availability environments
  • Experience with container technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) is an advantage
  • Knowledge of scripting and monitoring tools (Bash, Python or Pearl)
  • Experience with modern DevOps practices, CI/CD tools and agile working methods (SAFe, Scrum)
  • Ability to penetrate complex issues and develop pragmatic solutions
  • Very good German in word and writing
  • Appropriate reputation for personal safety inspection SEPOS

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
