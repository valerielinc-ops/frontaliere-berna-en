To support our team at the Ambulatorium Biel/Bienne we are looking for 1. July 2026 or by appointment a Clinical Psychologist Workplace Klinik Südhang Ambulatorium Biel/Bienne Employment Level 60% Job as of 01.07.2026 or by appointment PDF What makes your work particularly attractive They carry out addiction psychiatric examinations as well as outpatient psychotherapies and support us with treatments for our cooperation partners. As part of the investigations, you create an individual treatment plan with the patients, whether in the sense of an inpatient withdrawal, a day clinical or outpatient treatment. As part of outpatient treatment, you will conduct psychotherapeutic discussions and write patient-related reports and testimonies. Likewise, if you are interested, it is possible to work in our special session “ADHD and Addiction” and gain experience or to build up a group offer that is close to your heart and fits into our patient-centered attitude. You will be supervised closely by senior psychologists and psychiatric specialists. What distinguishes you You have a degree in psychology and practical experience in a psychiatric clinic. Furthermore, you have completed psychotherapy training or are about to complete it. Contact with people who are affected by serious addiction diseases, as well as interprofessional and networked work give you pleasure. You are able to conduct conversations in German and French or are ready to improve your foreign language skills in the course of work. Experience in mindfulness-based therapy is desirable. What you can look forward to is a challenging and exciting job with creative leeway in an experienced team. Participation in conceptual issues is desired. The working atmosphere is characterised by partnership and there are attractive professional training opportunities.
