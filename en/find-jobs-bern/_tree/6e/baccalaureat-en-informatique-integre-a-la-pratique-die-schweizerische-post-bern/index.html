Full description

Your education

  • In the one-year internal basic training, you build up your practical skills independently in modular learning units and smaller projects.
  • You study at the University of Applied Sciences for two to three days and specialize in the areas of Data Science, Enterprise Computing (application development), Information Security or System Engineering.
  • In practice, you can develop in a 50 percent effort according to your interests in various IT areas of the Group.
  • After four years you graduate with the Bachelor of Science and are ready for the jump into working life.

You bring that with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should fulfill the following points:

  • You have completed your secondary school with the Federal Matura or you are in possession of an ICT foreign professional maturity.
  • It is your desire to combine your university studies with professional practice.
  • You are interested in information and communication technologies, logic and engineering.
  • Your strengths lie in mathematics, German and English.
  • You enjoy working in a team, you are reliable, responsible, discreet and have perseverance.
