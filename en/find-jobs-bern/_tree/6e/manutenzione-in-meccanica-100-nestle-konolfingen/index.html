Short overview position

550 employees, who are responsible for the production of high-quality infant food for Germany and abroad, shape the dynamic, highly automated working environment of the Konolfingen factory.

You can expect interesting, varied and challenging tasks in which safety, quality, delivery readiness and cost awareness are in the foreground.
