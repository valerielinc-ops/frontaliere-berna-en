As a PiBS'ler at SBB, you will go through an individually tailored training program in the first few months, in which you will learn the basics of computer science resp. Business Informatics learns and is supported by our team of practical trainees. After completing the training program, the rotation begins by different development teams, with whom you make a valuable contribution to a functioning and modern IT. So you get to know the big and complex world of SBB IT, acquire many different skills and expand your network. During the entire 4 years, your personal manager accompanies you and supports your individual development. We reward you fairly with CHF 2'850.- (at 100%) per month. You can move that.
