University Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

At the University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ), the University Hospital for Forensic Psychiatry and Psychology offers inpatient, day-inpatient, outpatient and outreach treatment for people with psychiatric diseases who are in detention or in the context of court-ordered measures in treatment. On our forensic-psychiatric acute care unit Etoine you can expect a demanding and versatile working environment in which you can contribute your professional skills and further develop. Together with a committed interprofessional team, you will accompany our patients in a structured, milieutherapeutic setting and make an important contribution to a professional and safe treatment. Do you feel addressed? Then we look forward to meeting you!

Your tasks

  • You accompany and support patients in the forensic-psychiatric acute area in everyday life and support them in coping with their individual challenges
  • You take on nursing and medical-technical tasks within the scope of your competences and implement medically ordered measures
  • You support patients in promoting their independence and their everyday skills
  • You actively participate in the design and implementation of the milieutherapeutic setting
  • You take on internal tasks and additional tasks according to the distribution of tasks and do them independently, reliably and responsibly
  • You recognize changes in the mental and physical state of the patients and forward relevant observations to the treatment team
  • Through your professional appearance, your situation-appropriate actions and your knowledge of de-escalation, you contribute to a safe station everyday life.
  • You show your own initiative and commitment in taking over and implementing assigned tasks
  • You work closely with the interprofessional treatment team and participate in the implementation of individual care and treatment planning
  • You document your nursing activities and observations carefully and comprehensibly

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a health EFZ specialist or health EFZ specialist
  • You bring many years of experience in care, ideally in acute psychiatry or forensics.
  • You are psychologically resilient, stay calm even in challenging situations and act thoughtfully
  • You have strong social skills and meet people with a professional, appreciative and respectful attitude
  • You are interested in dealing with people in complex psychiatric treatment situations and bring openness to the forensic field of work
  • You are interested in aggression management and de-escalation and are ready to develop your skills in this area
  • You work responsibly, reliably and reflectively and adhere to agreed safety and treatment standards
  • You appreciate the partnership in the interprofessional team and actively participate in joint processes

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
