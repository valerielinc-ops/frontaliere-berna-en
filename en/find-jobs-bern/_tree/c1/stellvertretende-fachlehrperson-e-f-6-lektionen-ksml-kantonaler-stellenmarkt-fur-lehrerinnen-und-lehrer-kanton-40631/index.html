Full description

Portrait The school Dennigkofen is located in the municipality of Ostermundigen with about 18'000 inhabitants. Our school teaches 590 pupils from kindergarten until the 9th year of school. In cycle 3 we lead into model 4 (Twann) Further information on www.dennigkofen.ch

Tasks

We are looking for you! Are you looking for a new challenge in a heterogeneous, multicultural environment? You appreciate the close cooperation in the college, with the class teacher and the school management?

From February 2027 we are looking for a teacher for the subjects Mathematics, Nature Engineering and English. The job is temporary until the end of the school year.

The timetable is as follows

Mo: 10.20 - 11.55 Math (7th grade) Di: 08.10 - 09.55 Math (7th grade) / 10.20 - 11.55 English (9th grade) Mi: 08.20 - 09.55 or 10.20 - 11.55 NT (7th class) Do: 07.30 - 09.05 NT (7th class) Fr: 07.30 - 08.20 Math (7th grade)

Requirements

We expect you to

  • Ideally, teaching entitlement for secondary level 1 or in the Master's degree programme
  • Qualification for Mathematics, NT and English
  • Willingness to cooperate in a team, with school management, school social work and authorities
  • Cooperation in school and teaching development

We offer

  • Exchange and cooperation at the level and throughout the college will be given priority
  • Support from school management and the existing team
  • Well-established special teaching (all trained special teachers)
  • School social work on site
  • modern ICT infrastructure (personal teacher equipment, SuS 1:1 equipped with laptop)
  • We judge under the year grade free (with predicate)
  • large, diverse, innovative school
  • heterogeneous classes
  • Support in daily work (DaZ, IF, SSA, School Secretariat)
  • we like to test new forms of teaching and learning, are open to new things
