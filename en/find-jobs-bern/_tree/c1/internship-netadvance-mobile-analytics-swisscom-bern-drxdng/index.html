Internship NETAdvance - Mobile Analytics

Your future starts here Mobile communications are now an indispensable part of our everyday lives. Whether streaming, gaming, video calls or IoT: none of this would work without a high-performance network infrastructure. At Wireless Access Production, you will work in the Technical Leadership team on steering the mobile communications world of tomorrow. With innovative solutions in the areas of automation, digitalisation and process optimisation, you will help ensure that millions of people in Switzerland are optimally connected at all times. In this 12-month, practice-oriented student programme, you will immerse yourself in the fascinating world of network, infrastructure and innovation at Swisscom NET. You will actively work in the Technical Leadership team and, in parallel, develop a future-oriented innovation project. During the programme, you will build a strong network within NET and gain valuable insights beyond the country's borders through an international visit. The programme starts in February 2027.

  • Support the further development of innovative products and processes (AI, automation, cloud, digitalisation)
  • Support with the analysis, design and implementation of digitalisation initiatives
  • Data analysis and scripting to optimise infrastructure processes
  • Provision of KPIs/dashboards via the One Data Platform (ODP)
  • Digitalisation of mobile communications sites (Digital Twin) / Building Information Model BIM

Your skills

  • Bachelor's or master's degree (university/university of applied sciences) in computer science, data science, engineering or related disciplines (degree completed no more than one year ago)
  • Initial knowledge in data analysis, AI and/or cloud technologies
  • Good skills in scripting/programming (e.g. Python, SQL, BI tools, Kubernetes or similar)
  • Enthusiasm for new technologies
  • Quick grasp and a structured, independent way of working
  • Proactive and team-oriented personality
  • Systems thinking, analytical thinking and an understanding of business relationships
  • Very good German and English
  • French is an advantage

At our company, you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or from home. In doing so, you will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a wide range of attractive benefits that will enrich your work experience. These include a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
