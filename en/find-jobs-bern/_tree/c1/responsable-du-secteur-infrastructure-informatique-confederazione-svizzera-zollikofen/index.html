Put to the Point

You want to strategically design IT infrastructure and actively advance the future? Together with your teams, you will develop our IT landscape sustainably, set clear priorities and create space for self-responsible action. You control central infrastructure projects such as the network lifecycle, the modernization of the network architecture as well as the conversion and further development of our data centers. With strategic foresight and a modern understanding of leadership, you develop your teams forward.
