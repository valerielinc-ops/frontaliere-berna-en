These are your main tasks

  • Preparation of financial and pension plans for private clients
  • Analysis of the financial situation and development of needs-based solutions in the areas of provisioning, investments and financing
  • Advice and support of customers in planning their retirement
  • Preparation and presentation of planning results as a basis for further consultations
  • Close cooperation with the customer advisors and support in the care of pension customers
  • Active participation and further development of our financial planning offerings

This is what we want from you

  • Completed education or training in the financial sector (FA, HF, FH or similar)
  • Professional experience in financial planning, pension counseling or a related field
  • In-depth knowledge of pension, retirement and financial planning
  • Analytical thinking and joy in communicating complex relationships in an understandable way
  • Communicative, service-oriented and reliable personality
  • Independent, structured and careful working method

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
