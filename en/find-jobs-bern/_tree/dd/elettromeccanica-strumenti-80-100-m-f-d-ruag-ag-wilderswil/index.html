Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Management and further development of the Electromechanic & Instruments division with around 15 employees
  • Ensuring the maintenance of military and civil aviation components as well as the required MRO capabilities and customer support
  • Responsibility for quality, lead times, efficient workflows, budgeting and cost centers as well as the achievement of financial targets
  • Promotion and development of employees as well as ensuring the necessary expertise, training and knowledge transfer
  • Participation in projects and working groups as well as implementation of lean management and continuous improvement measures

You bring that with you

  • Technical training, ideally in the field of electromechanics, with further training at HF level or equivalent qualification
  • Leadership experience, business understanding and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Positive, motivating leader with pleasure in inspiring employees and achieving performance together
  • Strong solution orientation, pragmatic approach and an uncomplicated hands-on mentality
  • Good SAP skills, English language skills at level B2 as well as Swiss nationality (depending on function)

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
