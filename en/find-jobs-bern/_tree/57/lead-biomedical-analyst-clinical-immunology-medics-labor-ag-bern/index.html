  • Organizational, professional and personnel management of 12 employees

  • Technical validation, plausibility control and technical interpretation of laboratory results

  • Completed training as a certified biomedical analyst HF

  • Team-capable and resilient personality with an exact and independent working method
  • Good communication skills, also across departments
  • Enjoy working with automated analysis systems
  • Readiness for evening and Saturday services
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of French are a plus
