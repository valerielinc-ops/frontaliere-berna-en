Your assignment

  • Perform the different finishing stages of the components, from pre-lucidation to final finish.
  • Adapt the techniques, abrasives and work tools according to the pieces, materials and the desired result.
  • Perform manual and mechanical polishing work, in particular on complex or delicate surfaces.
  • Accurately perform locking and felting operations.
  • Ensure respect for the shapes, sizes and surface characteristics required.
  • Carefully examine the pieces after treatment to detect any aesthetic defects or abnormalities.
