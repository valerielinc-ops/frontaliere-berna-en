Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is part of the Federal Competence Centre for all core questions of labour market policy and unemployment insurance (ALV). The labour market / unemployment insurance benefit area is responsible for the unemployment insurance law. As part of the Finance and Controlling department, the Group on Free Movement of Persons and Maternity Insurance is responsible for ensuring the correct implementation of bilateral contracts with the EU/EFTA countries in the area of free movement of persons. They take responsibility for the financial settlement of cross-border commuters and work in an international environment with domestic and foreign partners.

  • Support the liquidity planning of the ALV Fund and ensure financial advances to unemployment insurance
  • Provide technical information to Swiss and foreign authorities

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation.
