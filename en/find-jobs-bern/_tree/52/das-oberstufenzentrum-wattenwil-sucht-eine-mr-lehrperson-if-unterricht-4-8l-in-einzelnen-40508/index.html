Full description

Portrait At the Wattenwil High Level Center, students from 7th to 9th grade (real and secondary level) from Wattenwil and several surrounding communities are taught. In the school year 2026/27, approximately 240 pupils attend classes at the upper school centre. We are committed to a contemporary, good school in a climate of trust and mutual respect.

Tasks

The individual promotion of students takes place in the individual setting as far as possible within the class. Goals are defined in close cooperation with the class team and implemented together. The MR teacher draws up funding plans, carries out learning status surveys and is responsible for the documentation. The exchange with parents and external specialists takes place regularly.

Requirements

Education in curative curative education or willingness to acquire it. Great interest and pleasure in inclusive promotion. Working with the class team. Cooperation with team, parents and specialist bodies. Participation in conferences and training.

We offer

  • 3-6 lessons IF lessons
  • Experienced and competent teams, solid structures, convenient working areas and well-functioning and supportive environment of external specialist departments.
  • Organization and regular exchange in the MR team, institutionalized intervision and further education.
  • Experienced MR - teachers who can take on a mentorship.
