# DevOps Engineer DDI Services

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

We accompany the Swiss Army on its digitalisation journey. The goal of the digital transformation is to strengthen Switzerland’s defence capability and to use the opportunities offered by technological progress as a success factor. As a DevOps engineer with a focus on DDI services, your focus is on engineering, stabilizing and safely developing the corresponding services and platforms. You ensure standardized, reproducible and safe operation across different environments.

  • Close cooperation with the product owner as well as development and operations teams to implement the project goals
  • Engineering, operation and continuous optimization of DDI services
  • Ensuring the quality, stability and security of the services operated
  • Active participation in the continuous development of the project platform and operational processes
  • Using modern technologies to support a secure and stable private cloud infrastructure

Your skills

  • Graduate degree or EFZ in computer science with several years of professional experience, or lateral entrants with equivalent training and professional experience in computer science
  • Very good German (minimum C1 level)
  • Experience in the integration of DDI systems (Bind, Infoblox, Microsoft DNS) with network, security, and CloudSerivces (API-based)
  • Solid basic knowledge in network technology (load balancing, HA, DNS Anycast, BGP, firewall, IPTables, routing and switching)
  • Experience with virtualization (VMs, hypervisors, containers)
  • Experience with automation (Ansible, Python, Git, API) and in dealing with IOS/IOS-XE/IOS-XR including configuration, hardening and error analysis (CCNA level)
  • Teamwork and independent working methods

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
