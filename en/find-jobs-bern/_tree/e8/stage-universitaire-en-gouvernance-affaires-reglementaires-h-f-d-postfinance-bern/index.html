Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

As a team with a broad range of topics, we are responsible for various cross-cutting issues in Governance & Regulatory Affairs and ensure that PostFinance is managed responsibly and in accordance with regulatory requirements. In this way, we proactively monitor political events, examine new legal developments and regulatory proposals, and ensure that the bank's political interests are represented. We promote integrity in everyday life and offer clear and practicable governance structures. In this way, we create orientation and reliability in an increasingly complex and dynamic regulatory environment, and we enable both our employees and the bank to meet the high standards set at all times.

What you can do

  • As part of regulatory monitoring, you will monitor and check regulatory and political developments for their relevance for PostFinance, carry out clarifications and ensure reporting and addressee-oriented information for (primarily internal) stakeholders
  • You will further develop the technical use of the application for regulatory monitoring in coordination with other internal departments
  • You will prepare conflict of interest checks in connection with mandates (including documentation and committee agenda) and take care of the Code of Conduct mailbox
  • You will be actively involved in the further development of the departments of Public Affairs and Regulatory Monitoring as well as Integrity Management
  • You can also get involved in other topics of the team, depending on your interests and skills.

What you bring to the table

  • You have a bachelor's degree in economics, law or political science that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your bachelor's degree program
  • Analytical, networked mindset and very good written expression, ideally with a good understanding of regulatory issues or the legislative process
  • Great interest in the interplay of business, politics and law as well as openness to the broad tasks of a bank
  • Precise, reliable and independent way of working, initiative and willingness to think outside the box
  • Very good knowledge of German; other languages are an advantage
