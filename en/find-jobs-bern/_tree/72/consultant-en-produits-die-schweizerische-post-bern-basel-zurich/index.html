Full description

Are you enthusiastic about international trade and would you like to support Swiss companies in accessing international markets? Do you want to actively shape the future of our export business? As a product consultant, you advise companies from German-speaking Switzerland on their international flows of goods and develop new sales opportunities together with various areas. With you, we strengthen our position as a competent partner for international shipping solutions and develop our export business sustainably.

You can do that.

  • You win and develop new customers in the international export business and accompany them from initial contact to successful implementation.
  • You will recognize the needs of our customers and develop suitable shipping, customs and e-commerce solutions.
  • You will present customer-specific solution concepts and market our international focus and growth products such as EU Direct, GLS and Urgent Business.
  • In addition, you systematically develop sales opportunities and document them in our lead and CRM systems.
  • You support the market launch and positioning of new export solutions and participate in strategic and operational business development initiatives.
  • In addition, you will build up a strong internal and external network and bring customers and market feedback to the further development of our offer.
  • You transfer won customer projects to account management in a structured manner and accompany the successful implementation.

You bring that with you

  • You have a commercial or business education or training at tertiary level, for example HF, FH or university.
  • In addition, you bring experience in sales, customer consulting, business development or account management and enjoy the active acquisition of new customers.
  • Ideally, you will bring knowledge in the international export or logistics environment and use digital sales and CRM tools safely in everyday work.
  • In addition, you will recognize customer needs and develop suitable solutions and concrete business opportunities.
  • You think entrepreneurially, take a structured approach and convince with a secure appearance and negotiating skills.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and have good (oral) knowledge of English.
