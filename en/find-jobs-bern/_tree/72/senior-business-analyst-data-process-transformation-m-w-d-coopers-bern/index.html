Full description

For our customer from the logistics industry in Bern, we are looking for a:n motivated:n and open-minded:n Senior Business Analyst (Data & Process Transformation).

Our customer is driving the further development of central control, billing and data processes. In the field of tension between business, data management and IT, there is the opportunity to make a significant contribution to the transparency, cost-effectiveness and sustainability of business-critical processes.

Your tasks

  • Analysis, concretization and structured specification of existing processes and technical requirements
  • Conducting complex data, process and cause analyses to identify optimization potential
  • Transfer of technical definitions to new data bases as well as development of technically feasible determination and calculation logics
  • Close cooperation with departments, architecture teams and development units during analysis, conception and implementation
  • Derivation, definition and monitoring of technical test and acceptance scenarios
  • Analysis and evaluation of deviations as well as technical support in finding solutions
  • Ensuring the traceability of technical rules, logics and decisions through structured documentation
  • Support for the introduction of new solutions and support for the transfer to operational operations
  • Participation in the further development of data-driven control and billing processes in the area of cost-performance accounting and revenue protection

Your profile

  • At least five years of professional experience as a Business Analyst, Requirements Engineer or equivalent
  • Sound experience with data models, data flows and data-driven business processes
  • Strong analytical skills and experience in the structured recording, analysis and specification of professional requirements
  • Experience at the interface between business, architecture and software development
  • Good SQL knowledge for independent data analysis
  • Knowledge of AWS and/or PL/SQL is an advantage
  • Safe use of Jira, Confluence or similar tools
  • Solution-oriented, pragmatic and team-oriented personality with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
