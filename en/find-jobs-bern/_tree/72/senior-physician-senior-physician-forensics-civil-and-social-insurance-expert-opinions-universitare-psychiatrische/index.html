University Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The Universitary Psychiatric Center (UPZ) Bern is involved in reviews and statements by the University Hospital for Forensic Psychiatry and Psychology (FPP) for a research-based, profound and socially relevant clarification of people with mental illnesses in legal contexts. The interdisciplinary team combines clinical psychiatry, psychology, law and social sciences to carry out well-founded civil and social security assessments. The aim is to provide legal practitioners with psychological expertise – and thus make an important contribution to public safety and modern psychiatric care. The FPP offers you the opportunity for actuarial and forensic further education at university level. Above-average performance is rewarded as part of a bonus system.

Their tasks

  • They independently prepare civil and social security reports
  • They are supported by assistant physicians and assistant psychologists of the clinic, whose supervision and guidance you take over.
  • They carry out necessary exploration at the UPZ and in external facilities in the Canton of Bern
  • You participate in general psychiatric background service
  • You are actively involved in education and training for psychiatry at the University of Bern
  • They support ongoing research projects and, if interested, can develop their own projects within the framework of the clinic’s research concept.

Your profile

  • You have a specialist degree in psychiatry and psychotherapy or are about to graduate
  • Ideally, you have the certification of Swiss Insurance Medicine (SIM) or want to acquire it at our training centre.
  • It is advantageous if you demonstrate experience in the preparation of expert opinions
  • They work in a structured, responsible and self-initiative manner.
  • You are strong in communication and secure in dealing with internal and external contacts
  • They have an above-average knowledge of German in words and writing. Knowledge of French is an advantage

The UPZ

The University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
