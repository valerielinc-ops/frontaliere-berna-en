In a nutshell

The Free Trade Agreements/EFTA Division (WHFH) is responsible for free trade policy and free trade agreements (FTAs) as well as Switzerland's other bilateral negotiation processes with partner countries outside the EU. The WHFH Division leads the negotiations and coordinates the related work. The Division is also responsible for specific thematic areas within the framework of FTAs (e.g. trade and sustainable development).

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the Confederation's centre of excellence for all core issues of economic and labour market policy and is part of the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research (EAER). It promotes the opening of international markets for goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is involved in economic development cooperation. It plans economic and trade policy measures for the benefit of developing countries, Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
