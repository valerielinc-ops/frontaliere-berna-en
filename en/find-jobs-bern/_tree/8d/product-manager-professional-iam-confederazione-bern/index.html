Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is part of the Federal Competence Centre for all core questions of labour market policy, employment services, unemployment insurance and labour market statistics. The Department of Informatics for Unemployment Insurance (ALV) Switzerland is responsible for the digitization of business processes as well as the operation of the business applications of the Unemployment Fund as well as the employment services of job seekers and their reintegration measures into the labour market. As Product Manager Professional Identity & Access Management (IAM), the position is responsible for the Nevis unemployment insurance platform and shapes its further development with modern approaches such as Zero Trust and Self-Sovereign Identity. As an interface between technology, department and politics, it positions IAM as a strategic enabler of digitalization in a complex, regulatoryly demanding environment (26 cantons, federal government, BIT).

  • Entry date: by immediate or by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21042
  • Location: Holzikofenweg 36, 3007 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Assume responsibility for the ALV Nevis IAM platform and further develop, operate and scale it
  • Analyze market trends (e.g. Zero Trust) and evaluate modern IAM approaches (e.g. Decentralized Identity)
  • Coordinate requirements of all stakeholders (e.g. cantons, federal administration and IV agencies) and ensure uniform IAM processes and high acceptance
  • Coordinate priorities and roadmaps with specialist areas, IT architecture, security and suppliers and represent ALV in intercantonal committees
  • Identify vulnerabilities in existing IAM processes and initiate process automation as well as define KPIs and derive improvement measures
  • Ensure compliance with legal requirements and minimize operational risks, including through regular access reviews
  • Collaborate with security teams, implement security policies and prepare audits

This makes them unique.

  • Graduate degree in business informatics or equivalent
  • Several years of professional experience in product management or IAM, ideally with Nevis or comparable solutions (e.g. Microsoft Entra ID), as well as experience in public administration and with agile product management
  • Knowledge of relevant IAM standards (e.g. OAuth 2.0) and security frameworks (Zero Trust and ISO 27001) as well as technical understanding of RBAC, attribute-based access control and Identity Federation
  • Basic knowledge of scripting (PowerShell, Python) to automate IAM processes
  • Experience with API integrations and cloud IAM benefits
  • Negotiation skills and solution-oriented thinking to balance competing interests as well as the ability to communicate complex IAM concepts in an understandable way to technical teams and executives
  • Passive knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English
