Full description

Their main tasks

You create opportunities, you can multitask, you like people! Then we look for you: as the hub of our law firm in the heart of Bern, with management responsibility, training assignment and space to rethink our working world with AI.

Your duties as facilitator

  • Independent and forward-looking planning of three specialist areas with and for focus partners
  • Independent management of the administrative daily business: correspondence, legal documents, dossier management, invoicing, deadline and deadline control
  • Ongoing coordination and prioritization with the three focus partners, the central services, the assistance functions and the management
  • Organizing and conducting events for clients and employees.

Leadership and training

  • Professional management of a team of assistants: inside within the framework of a large mandate: operational planning, training, quality assurance.
  • Optional practical training for our learners: You supervise one:n learners:n in your team for six months per apprenticeship year (two learners, offset).

Digitalisation and AI

  • They bring AI into our everyday work: They recognize potential, test new solutions and implement them together with the team.
  • As a super user for Elle (our ERP and case management system), you are the first contact person in the company, train Kolleg:innen and develop the system together with our IT.

What you bring

  • Completed basic commercial training (EFZ) or comparable training, ideally with further training (e.g. executive assistance, office management, paralegal, legal secretariat)
  • Several years of experience in office management, in assistant functions or staff assistant functions, preferably in a law firm or a comparable environment
  • Desire for leadership – You have already led a team or want to take the step now
  • Completed vocational training course or willingness to complete it
  • A flair for AI and digital tools as well as the will to actively improve processes
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, further language skills are an advantage
  • Organizational talent, resilience and keeping an overview when a lot is running simultaneously
  • Discretion, reliability and a high service orientation

What we offer you

  • Modern infrastructure – modern, stylish office space in the heart of Bern as well as modern IT infrastructure
  • Space for development - both professional and personal: we promote individual strengths
  • FLEXIBILITY - Work models that fit your life (40h/week, paid breaks, 16 weeks of maternity leave with 100% pay)
  • ADDITIONAL FRIDAY – on Christmas and New Year days off work and early office closing before public holidays
  • EDUCATION – Multiple opportunities for specialization and growth
  • PERSPECTIVES - in an established and future-oriented law firm

About us

We are a leading Swiss law firm with offices in Basel, Bern, Geneva, Lausanne/Sion, Lugano and Zurich as well as internationally in Shanghai and Tokyo. We advise discerning national and international customers – companies, organisations and private individuals – in a highly competent manner in all legal matters.
