About us

aebiinform AG in Oberburg has been an established physiotherapy practice for over 20 years – we are there for our patients when it comes to getting the body back fit, mobilizing muscles and getting the best version of them out! We focus on people, the joy of movement and individual care.

What to expect

  • A large, modern practice with over 200 m2 area and a versatile therapy and training offer and fitness room.
  • You look after patients from the region who are assigned by doctors and institutions. Our focus is on orthopaedics, traumatology, rheumatology and sports physiotherapy.
  • You will become part of a motivated and well-positioned team where cohesion, mutual support and humor are given priority.
  • Documentation is also part of this, but we do it quickly and efficiently! We are modern and digital!

What you should bring

  • A completed training physiotherapist / physiotherapist with SRK recognition
  • You are a team player, but you can also do the solo therapist.
  • You want to develop and expand your skills.
  • Enthusiasm for our patients, pleasure in exercise and a large portion of professional pride.

What we offer

  • Modern, established physiotherapy and fitness room in top quality.
  • Joint planning of working hours in the team so that everyone has the opportunity to use their deserved Fridays flexibly and suit their needs.
  • A cool team that sticks together and laughs together.
  • Further education opportunities so that you always stay up to date.
  • Modern employment conditions also regarding holidays and wages are a matter of course!

Sounds good? Then we look forward to meeting you!
