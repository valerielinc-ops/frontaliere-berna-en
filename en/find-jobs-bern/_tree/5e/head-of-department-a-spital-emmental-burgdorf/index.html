Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital, we live an uncomplicated, family working atmosphere. Our teams appreciate the very good development opportunities and the balanced skill & grade mix in the departments. Lean management is not a buzzword for us, but everyday life – just like family-friendly working conditions. Autonomous service planning is particularly important to us: With us, the team plans together – for more co-determination and satisfaction. For our interdisciplinary department at the Burgdorf site, which has 26 beds and comprises about 46 employees as well as students and learners, we are looking for a first step. April 2027 or by agreement

Create care, strengthen people – your field of action with us

  • Responsibility for professional, patient-oriented care in the sense of our nursing mission statement
  • Professional advice and support of the team in complex care situations
  • Co-responsibility for the personnel, technical and organizational management of the department
  • Active participation in the design and further development of nursing processes and quality standards
  • Cooperation in day-to-day care and responsibility for the care process as well as its documentation
  • Promoting constructive interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Participation in change processes as well as in the introduction and support of new employees
  • Collaboration in cross-sectoral projects and processes

You need this to be successful

  • Completed training as a nurse (AKP, DN II, HF, FH)
  • Acute experience as well as interest or experience in a leadership role
  • Delight in motivating, empowering and helping to shape employees
  • High social skills, empathy and responsibility
  • Structured, solution-oriented and independent working method
  • Interest in the further development of your own department and openness to new ideas
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B2 for diplomas from abroad)

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
