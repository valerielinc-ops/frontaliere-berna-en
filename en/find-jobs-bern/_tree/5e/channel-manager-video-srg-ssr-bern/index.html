Full description

The unit distributes video on all platforms (Play, YouTube, TV) and directs programming strategies to ensure consistency, performance and visibility of content. It actively supports digital transformation and supports editorial teams in the programmatic implementation of our Play first goals."

We are recruiting a Youtube & Play RTS Video Manager to strengthen the existing team.

Your Mission

You ensure the programming and presentation of content on YouTube RTS channels and, more occasionally on Play RTS. You bring a fine understanding of the uses and expectations of the audiences on Youtube in order to inform the choices of programming and distribution. You understand and use usage data and audiences to identify opportunities for optimisation of offers and actively participate in the programming and publication of the Youtube RTS offer, and more specifically in the programming of Play RTS. You contribute to programmatic coherence between different presences and vectors. You feed the day before Youtube uses, video platforms.

Your profile

  • University education in social sciences, communication sciences, media studies, interactive design, digital marketing, data analytics or equivalent.
  • Excellent understanding of YouTube, its recommendation mechanisms, visibility and audience usage.
  • Excellent knowledge of distribution logics on Youtube, and good understanding of video platforms.
  • Good knowledge of audience analysis and digital performance tools.
  • Great editorial convenience, with a keen sense of titration and optimization of descriptions on YouTube.
  • Ability to work cross-cuttingly with editorial, marketing and distribution teams to optimize the reach and commitment of our Youtube and Platform offerings.
  • Good knowledge of the RTS universe, its offerings and its audiences.
  • Good English mastery and German knowledge

What we offer you

  • A network of talented collaborators and a motivated and supportive team
  • A dynamic and stimulating environment that fosters autonomy, initiative and innovation

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
