Full description

What awaits you

In this position, you will support our customers proactively and easily in the event of a claim. In this way you contribute to trust in Mobiliar as the most personal insurance in Switzerland.

This is what you do with us

The focus of your job is to support our customers in an uncomplicated manner in the event of a claim - from the report to the conclusion.

You skillfully bring your attention and service orientation to the support of the entire process and thus provide customers with security regarding claims processing.

You conduct discussions and negotiations with customers and claims partners.

What you bring with you

  • Completed training in the insurance industry
  • Professional experience in claims processing
  • Enjoying contact and supporting customers in the event of a claim
  • Proactive and independent way of working
  • Responsible, empathetic and open personality
  • You are a German or French native speaker and have a good knowledge of the other language

Work with us

We offer you a versatile, varied and independent job in a motivated team, a very modern workplace in a central location in the city of Biel as well as excellent working conditions and further training opportunities.

Your workload is negotiable from 80%. The position will start on January 1, 2027 or by agreement.

In order to exercise this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the qualification of insurance intermediary VBV, non-life profile. If this is not available, we will be happy to support you with tailor-made internal training.

This is what we offer

Profit sharing

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Every year a decision is made about the distribution of a profit share and its amount.

Further training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant further training.

Perks

We offer, among other things, up to 25% premium discounts, discounts on mobile phones as well as free flat-rate subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
