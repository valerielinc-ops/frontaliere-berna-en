Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make as significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Armed Forces and other emergency and security organizations can comprehensibly carry out their tasks at all times.

You can move it

  • Lead complex space projects from initial proposal through development, integration and operational delivery
  • Manage technical programs covering ground segment software, satellite operations and Earth Observation (EO) systems
  • Coordinate technical teams, external partners and institutional customers
  • Review technical architectures, specifications and Interface Control Documents (ICDs), ensuring quality and compliance
  • Plan and manage project milestones, budgets, resources and risks, including formal reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR)
  • Support technical proposals, customer presentations and business development activities
  • Work closely with senior management and contribute to the strategic development of our space activities

What you should bring with you

  • Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Aerospace Engineering or a comparable technical field
  • At least 7 years of experience in technical program management, ground segment systems, satellite operations or related space projects
  • Strong IT background, particularly in software architectures, networking, cloud infrastructure and system integration
  • Experience with ESA projects, ECSS standards and formal technical reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR)
  • Hands-on approach with the ability to review technical documentation and actively drive project execution
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with confidence in complex and security-sensitive environments
  • Experience in military or defense-related projects is a strong advantage
  • Fluent English
  • German or French is an advantage

Do you not fully meet all the requirements? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people to join our team. We place great emphasis on diversity and equal opportunities and aim to build a versatile team where everyone can fully utilize their personal strengths. In particular, we encourage all people, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a position in a military-related environment to apply.

About the field of activity

The C5I Business Area of RUAG supports the Swiss Army in the implementation of ICT projects, with a specific focus on the Cyber Command. In this context, we have the opportunity to contribute to highly secure and sovereign IT systems. Over the past eight years, we have grown from zero to 260 employees (Business Area C5I), offering a unique and dynamic environment with a startup character. This is a unique opportunity to be at the forefront of the Swiss Army's digital transformation. If you want to contribute to the security policy landscape, you've come to the right place. We work in an agile manner and consistently follow the DevSecOps model. With the C5I-Campus, we have created an innovation space and offer highly attractive workplaces. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
