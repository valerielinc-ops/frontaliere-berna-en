Full description

We are looking for a committed specialist in neurophysiological diagnostics for night service in our sleep laboratory / our epileptological monitoring station. Night Watch Sleep Laboratory / Epileptology 20-40 % Place of work: Tschugg rehabilitation clinic Where your job does rehab. Neurorehabilitation with one of the largest Parkinson's facilities in Switzerland, in the middle of the Bernese Seeland. And over 100 years of epileptology experience, combined with the most modern epilepsy center. The environment at the rehabilitation clinic Tschugg is also good for the approximately 320 employees. Here, advanced medicine meets flat hierarchies and a lot of room for manoeuvre. Through close exchange with care and therapy, you and your ideas advance.

You can look forward to this

  • Attachment and re-adhesion of EEG electrodes according to the 10-20 system
  • Continuous monitoring of EEG and ECG discharges during the night
  • Detecting and Documenting Epileptic Seizures and Sleep Events
  • Technical control of derivatives (signal quality, electrode control, artifacts)
  • Adaptation and correction of electrodes as needed
  • Care and reassurance of patients during the night (only 2 patients per night)
  • Use in the 2-week rhythm in two consecutive nights

We are pleased about this

  • Safe handling of the 10-20 electrode system, the appropriate training is offered
  • Good observation skills and technical understanding
  • Resilience at night duty
  • Independent, calm and responsible work
