Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offers merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Burgdorf we are looking for a nursing assistant / in SRK 50 - 100% Your tasks

  • You will help our residents with body care, dressing and lining, and mobilization, taking into account their individual needs and abilities.
  • You motivate and activate the residents to use their existing resources and promote their independence as part of daily activities
  • You check for changes in the well-being or behavior of the residents and immediately pass this information on to the responsible nurse
  • You document carefully and accurately the services provided and observations according to our standards
  • You work hand in hand with the care professionals and FaGe's to ensure optimal and holistic care

You bring that with you

  • You have training as a nursing assistant or equivalent, recognized in Switzerland
  • You have a big heart for older people and value appreciative, empathetic and resident-centered care
  • Ideally, you already have several years of professional experience in long-term care - even re-entrants are welcome.
  • You enjoy working in a team, are helpful and respectful in dealing with colleagues and actively contribute to a positive working atmosphere
  • Very good knowledge of German is very important for communication with our residents and in the team

We offer you

  • The opportunity to work very independently and to exchange ideas with an experienced team
  • Extensive and free training on the Senevita Campus and the Senevita Academy, which inspires ongoing development
  • Discounted food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Attractive Senevita benefits (SBB half-ax, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Regular team events and shared experiences that strengthen cooperation
  • Generous holiday scheme
  • Our statement "closer to people" counts for our employees as much as for our residents

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
