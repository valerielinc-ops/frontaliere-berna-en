# IT audit

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

You plan and conduct risk-based IT audits in the Swisscom Group. You strengthen the quality, security and compliance of our IT and processes. In a dedicated team, you work with modern methods and tools and further shape our audit practice. We maintain an open, collegial cooperation and promote your development.

  • Independent planning and implementation of risk-based IT audits in group companies
  • Assessment of the adequacy and effectiveness of IT controls and identification of IT and network risks
  • Support for financial and process audits and the creation of clear audit findings
  • Further development of audit planning, audit methods and internal tools

Your skills

  • Degree (ETH/uni/FH/HF) in business informatics, computer science or business administration or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in IT audit, assurance or in a comparable environment, ideally in large companies or with IT service providers
  • High affinity for IT, cyber, networks and telecommunications; interest in AI and AI governance
  • Experience with data analytics, AI-supported methods and audit tools
  • knowledge of relevant standards such as NIS2, ISO 27001 or NIST; Certification such as CISA or CIA or willingness to acquire it
  • You think conceptually, recognize tendencies and connections and derive conclusive conclusions
  • Independent, structured and reliable; pragmatic in implementation
  • Communicative and team capable
  • Enjoying the exchange with different stakeholders
  • Very good German and English in word and writing

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
