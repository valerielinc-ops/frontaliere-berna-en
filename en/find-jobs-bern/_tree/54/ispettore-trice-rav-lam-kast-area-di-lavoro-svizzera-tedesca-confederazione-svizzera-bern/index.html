Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is the federal competence centre for all core questions of labour market policy and unemployment insurance. The “Labour Market / Unemployment Insurance” service area supervises the Unemployment Insurance Act. The Audit Service Department is responsible for financial, compliance and IT audits as well as the needs-oriented preparation of information on the audit results. The compliance group RAV/LAM/KAST of the audit service checks the legally compliant and uniform implementation of the legal requirements at the cantonal enforcement agencies of the unemployment insurance (ALV).

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation.
