Full description

In our diverse team, we ensure that PostFinance meets the high standard of due diligence in the area of anti-money laundering and tax compliance. We analyze risky business relationships and transactions. We document our findings comprehensively and comprehensibly. As part of the clarifications, we meet our customers at eye level. As an open-minded and helpful team, we master every challenge.

You can do that.

  • You will analyse unusual customer situations and assess risks in the areas of money laundering, sanctions and compliance. You carry out telephone clarifications, obtain missing information and ensure compliance with all requirements.
  • You review risky business relationships and transactions. You document your assessments precisely in reports as a basis for internal decisions and regulatory evidence.
  • You ensure compliance with legal requirements and internal guidelines. For this purpose, you will check, among other things, AIA, FATCA and customer documentation requirements.
  • You are the contact person for customers, customer consultants and specialists in compliance and regulatory issues and explain complex issues in an understandable way.
  • You follow regulatory developments and money laundering trends, share your knowledge in the team and support the further development of processes and standards.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by higher specialist training (HF/FH/Uni)
  • First professional experience in the compliance environment, preferably in the financial services industry
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously and set priorities in a dynamic environment
  • Analytical thinking, high quality standards and strong judgment and decision-making skills
  • Self-employed working with pleasure in taking responsibility and contributing your own solutions
  • Negotiation-proof German and very good English skills as well as a flair for comprehensible and precise documentation

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
