Full description

We make habitats worth living.

You combine leadership, technology and entrepreneurial thinking: Together with your team, you professionalize the application and support department in the context of water infrastructure, overcome silos and develop processes and the portfolio in a future-oriented manner.

Your tasks

  • You lead the Applications & Support team with up to five employees in terms of expertise and personnel, set clear priorities and strengthen your own responsibility.
  • You organize and coordinate support, operation and application topics and ensure a stable, customer-oriented operation of the IoT solutions.
  • You work around 30 percent operationally in 1st and 2nd level support and coordinate the organizational measures for 3rd level support.
  • You control and report the service quality on the basis of support volume, reaction and solution times and ensure traceable escalations.
  • You simplify and professionalize support and operational processes, guidelines, documentation and the knowledge base and promote the transfer of know-how in the team.
  • You review and develop the IoT portfolio and the IT architecture economically and future-oriented (business cases), lead IoT projects and build bridges between internal and external stakeholders.

Your expertise

  • You have at least three years of experience in the technical and personnel management of a team of comparable size.
  • You know the IoT landscape from practice – from development and further development to stable operation.
  • You bring a basic technical training with EFZ, for example in computer science, automation or electronics, or a basic commercial training with targeted further development in the IoT or application support environment. A successfully completed tertiary training in computer science, systems engineering or automation (HF) is an advantage.
  • You communicate in German and can communicate in French and English (level B1).
  • You work in a structured, reflective and solution-oriented way and tackle operational topics with a high level of practical relevance yourself.
  • You understand service-oriented business models, connect different stakeholders and are ready to travel within Switzerland up to 10 percent.

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
