Full description

Portrait The Educational Center for Hearing and Speech HSM Münchenbuchsee is a service center with a wide range of support and advice for children and adolescents with severe hearing and speech impairments and their environment. We train normally gifted pupils with hearing and/or language impairments from basic to secondary level I.

For our language healing class in Spiez we are looking for a class help / class assistance by agreement

Tasks

  • You support the teacher in class and accompany the children in everyday school life.
  • You help individual children and small groups cope with learning and everyday situations.
  • You accompany the children in transitions, in joint activities and in social situations.
  • You support the teacher in structuring everyday school life and in organizational tasks.
  • You will help provide teaching materials and create a learning-enhancing environment.
  • You accompany the class on excursions and special school activities.
  • You work closely with the teacher and the class team.
  • You bring that with you

Requirements

  • You enjoy working with children and young people.
  • You are patient, empathetic and meet children with an appreciative and respectful attitude.
  • You are reliable, responsible and resilient.
  • You can adjust to different children and situations and keep calm and humor even in challenging moments.
  • You are ready to join a class team and work according to the guidance of the teacher.
  • You have good communication skills and can express yourself clearly and age-appropriately.
  • Experience in caring for or accompanying children is beneficial, but not a requirement.

We offer

  • You will be supported by a dedicated team and a school management.
  • A varied and meaningful activity in a committed class team.
  • The opportunity to accompany and support children in their development and in their school day.
  • An appreciative collaboration in an interdisciplinary environment.
