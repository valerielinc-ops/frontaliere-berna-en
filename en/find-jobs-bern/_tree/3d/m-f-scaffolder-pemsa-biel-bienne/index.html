You will join our partner teams in the Swiss scaffolding industry and take care of:

  • Mount and disassemble scaffolding
  • Ensure maintenance, handling and transport of equipment
  • Respect the safety standards and directives of the SUVA
  • Ensure safety on site

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have a CFC as a scaffolder.
  • You have at least 3 years experience in Switzerland in the scaffolding industry.
  • You have a driving license.
  • Master the French.
  • You're comfortable working on the team, outdoors and at altitude.
