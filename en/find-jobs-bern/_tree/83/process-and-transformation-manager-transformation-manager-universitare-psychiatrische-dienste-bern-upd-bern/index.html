Full description

University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

You like to think networked, recognize potential for optimization and want to actively shape the digital transformation in the healthcare sector? Do you enjoy questioning processes, developing innovative solutions and accompanying employees on the path of change? Then you are exactly right with us! At the University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ), you are actively involved in the further development of our clinic. Together, we drive digitalization, optimize processes and create innovative solutions that support both our employees in everyday work and the quality of patient care.

Your tasks

  • You will analyze existing clinical processes and processes within the Clinic Information System (KIS), identify weak points and derive concrete optimization measures from them.
  • You develop and implement efficient, quality-oriented processes, accompany their implementation and further development and actively shape the operational merger
  • You coordinate and develop clinical research and biobanking processes and connect the areas of clinic, research, digitalization and process management
  • You accompany the digital transformation, develop digital working methods and work closely with the responsible managers
  • You design and conduct training sessions and workshops and support employees in the application of new processes and digital solutions
  • You accompany change processes with suitable change management methods and promote the active involvement of employees
  • You identify innovative technologies and accompany their introduction and integration into existing systems

Your profile

  • You have a bachelor’s degree or equivalent education, ideally in healthcare or medical informatics
  • You bring three to five years of professional experience in process, transformation, change or quality management, preferably in psychiatric or general health care
  • You have experience in the analysis, design and optimization of processes and are familiar with digital systems, ideally a clinic information system
  • You have good knowledge of computer science and a strong affinity for technology
  • You convince through your communication ability, innovation and initiative
  • You think analytically and networked, work independently and understand how to win different stakeholders for change
  • You have very good knowledge of German
  • English skills are beneficial

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
