# Business Engineer

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

As a business engineer in Team Phoenix, you are the central link between requirements and implementation. You will actively work with business stakeholders, analyze requirements, structure professional needs and accompany their implementation from the idea to the productive solution. With your understanding of processes, data and system contexts, you ensure that sustainable solutions are created and the network construction of Switzerland is secured. You support both the ongoing operation and the future development of the system landscape and actively contribute to the knowledge transfer within the team.

  • Analysis, specification and prioritization of business requirements in the system landscape of the ART with focus on Oracle DB, SAP and Camunda
  • Close collaboration with product owners, departments, architecture and development teams
  • Design and optimization of business-critical processes and solutions
  • Accompaniment of requirements from the idea to successful implementation in an agile environment

Your skills

  • Training (FH/uni/ETH) in business informatics, computer science or comparable professional experience
  • Experience in Business Engineering, Requirements Engineering or Business Analysis
  • Experience in the analysis of complex business processes and requirements management
  • Understanding agile methods (Scrum, SAFe) as well as data and system landscapes
  • Analytical and structured working methods and strong networked thinking
  • Communication strength and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • High initiative, sense of responsibility and solution orientation
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • French advantage
