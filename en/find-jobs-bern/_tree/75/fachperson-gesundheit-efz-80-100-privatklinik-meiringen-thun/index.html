Full description

In the private clinic Meiringen people with all forms of psychological suffering find admission, treatment and care. The third-largest psychiatric clinic in the Canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient primary psychiatric care of the eastern Bernese upper country. Care and care take place according to current medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge.

As a member of Swiss Leading Hospitals, Privatklinik Meiringen is one of the leading private clinics in Switzerland.

At the PZJE (Psychiatry Center for Young Adults), we treat young adults between the ages of 16 and 25 with diseases from the entire spectrum of psychiatry with an extensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment. The center comprises 30 beds, with the center divided into an area of crisis intervention and a psychotherapy area. The treatment is based on the latest evidence-based medical, therapeutic and nursing knowledge.
