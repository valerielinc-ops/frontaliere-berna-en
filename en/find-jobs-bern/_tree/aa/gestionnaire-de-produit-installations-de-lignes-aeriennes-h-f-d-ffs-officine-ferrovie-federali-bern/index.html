Product Manager Airline Facilities (m/f/d) 60-100%

Move Switzerland with us. Product Manager Airline Facilities (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, immediate start or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

We move Switzerland - without electricity, no trains run. Join us to operate the Swiss railway power grid and ensure that we can deliver the power to the railway infrastructure managers' contact lines. Here you will find more information about our projects and areas of activity: Energy To find out what benefits we offer, such as children's allowances, the free AGM or travel abroad at a reduced price (also for the family), visit our website under Benefits. What you can move.

  • As an expert in high voltage lines and cables, you are the main contact person within the SBB.
  • In collaboration with users, you develop and manage the product strategy for high voltage lines and cables throughout the life cycle.
  • You define the internal technical specifications for the planning, construction, operation and maintenance of high voltage installations and ensure their sustainable development.
  • For the power supply of SBBs, you are responsible for the technical certification of high voltage lines and cable systems.
  • You represent SBB Energy in national committees and working groups, you actively promote SBB interests in the development of industry standards and standards, and you maintain a strong network with users, manufacturers, authorities and other partners.

What you bring.

  • A convincing and inclusive person, you have strong communication skills, a strong sense of implementation and a structured approach.
  • You bring several years of experience as a product designer or designer and/or have in-depth knowledge of the use of high voltage means of operation.
  • Higher education (HES/EPF/technical university) in electrotechnics will allow you to carry out this demanding mission.
  • You are familiar with the laws and standards governing energy technology and the national and international safety standards and requirements in force.
  • You have a very good knowledge of German (minimum level C1) and a good knowledge of French (minimum level B1).

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the K level of requirements and is eligible for a level 1 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits.

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the greatest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
