When it comes to estate and inheritance matters, our customers need someone who can provide them with competent and understanding support. Basic commercial training (EFZ) and several years of experience in telephone customer contact

Empathy, sensitivity and a confident demeanor when dealing with customers

Excellent knowledge of spoken and written German as well as very good knowledge of Italian and French
