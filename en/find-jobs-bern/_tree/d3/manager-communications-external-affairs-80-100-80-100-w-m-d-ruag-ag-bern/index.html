Full description

Around 3000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland every day. They ensure that the Swiss army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks at all times.

You can move that

  • You support the media work of RUAG and process media requests. If you have suitable topics, you will take over the speaker function. - You research and prepare complex topics from companies, politics and security in a comprehensible and appropriate manner and process Q&As, statements, language regulations, briefings and other communication documents. - You act as a business partner Communication on selected topics and projects and support departments in the preparation and implementation of external communication measures.
  • You monitor and analyze political, parliamentary and regulatory developments with relevance for RUAG. - You support the preparation and follow-up of sessions, political business and selected dossiers. - You research and work on the basis of political stakeholders, start stakeholder profiles and support the preparation and follow-up of discussions, visits and political events.
  • Together with the Team External Affairs, you will ensure that relevant information from media, politics and business environment is recognized at an early stage, processed in a structured manner and made usable for the external positioning of RUAG. - You will work closely with the Director External Affairs and Lead Media work.

You bring this with you

  • You have a degree in higher education or a comparable education, ideally in communication, journalism/P journalism, political science, economics or a related field. - You have several years of experience in journalism, media work or corporate communication and have a good understanding of the Swiss media landscape. - Experience in a media office or as media speaker is advantageous.
  • You enjoy research and basic work, can quickly capture complex issues, identify relevant information and prepare them precisely, intelligibly and appropriately. - You know the political system of Switzerland, its institutions and decision-making processes and are interested in public action as well as political and regulatory issues.
  • You have an understanding of the special conditions of companies close to the federal government and are interested in Swiss security and defence policy; experience is of benefit. - You write in German in a style-proof manner, have good knowledge of French and English. - You convince by a structured, reliable and independent working method, discretion and joy in team collaboration.

Are you not fully qualified? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which all their personal strengths can be fully developed. We encourage all people, regardless of their gender, who may have concerns about taking up a job in a poor environment, to apply.
