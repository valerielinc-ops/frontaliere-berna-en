Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

Are you interested in regulatory issues, compliance management and how a modern financial service provider works? Would you like to apply the knowledge you gained from your studies in practice and contribute to the integrity of PostFinance at the same time? Then you've come to the right place.

In the 2nd Line compliance team, you will support us in identifying, assessing and monitoring compliance risks. You will gain exciting insights into regulatory requirements, governance issues and risk management of a leading Swiss financial services provider.

What you can do

  • You will support the implementation of compliance risk analyses and the assessment of regulatory issues
  • You will be involved in the monitoring and further development of the compliance framework and gain insights into control processes
  • You will analyze facts, conduct research and prepare findings for various stakeholders in a way that is appropriate for the target group
  • You will support the preparation of statements, reports and decision templates for departments and management
  • You will be involved in the assessment of new regulatory requirements and support their implementation within PostFinance.
  • You will be in contact with internal departments and learn about the interaction between compliance, risk management, legal and business

What you bring to the table

  • You have a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Law or Economics that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your Bachelor's degree
  • Interest in compliance, risk and governance issues in the financial services environment
  • Analytical thinking skills and the ability to work on complex issues in a structured way
  • Independent, reliable and careful way of working
  • Enjoy familiarizing yourself with new topics and taking on responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German, other languages are an advantage
