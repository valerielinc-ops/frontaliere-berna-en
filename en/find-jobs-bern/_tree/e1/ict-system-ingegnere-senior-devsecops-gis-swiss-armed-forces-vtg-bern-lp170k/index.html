Full description

In a nutshell

Become part of Cyber Command and actively help shape the digitalization and, in particular, the digital networking of the Swiss Armed Forces. Contribute to this unique task – making civilian mobile communications and fiber-optic networks usable for the Armed Forces – in a dynamic working environment.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20505
  • Work location: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make a contribution by

  • Build and operate a geographic information system platform in an automated manner using the DevSecOps methodology (microservices, container technology, Kubernetes, orchestration)
  • Iteratively drive continuous development in design and automating/orchestrating (release automation) and build new platforms
  • Contribute to the standardization of projects at federal level
  • Prepare decision-making documents for the introduction of new IT components
  • Take on tasks throughout the entire DevSecOps cycles

This is what makes you unique

  • Engineer (UNI/ETH/FH); or technician (HF) in computer science, business informatics or a similar discipline with several years of professional experience
  • Very good, practical knowledge of automating IT solutions in the DevSecOps environment using the products ArcGIS, Red Hat OpenShift, Ansible and GitLab
  • Comprehensive and up-to-date experience in Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery as well as in programming or scripting languages such as Python, .NET, Powershell, Bash, as well as Linux (RHEL) and Windows Server
  • Knowledge of process and architecture notations such as BPMN and ArchiMate
  • Conceptual and goal-oriented work, a high level of team spirit and work experience in an agile environment according to SAFe, and knowledge of agile tools (Git, Jira, Confluence); willingness to continue training in GIS topics
  • Good knowledge of a second official language and good active English skills

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in action Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and contribute different perspectives.
  • Health at work We support and advise our employees on physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for expertise in the areas of cyber and digitalisation. We combine our enthusiasm for our mission with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Armed Forces and our partners in Switzerland's security network. This gives the Swiss Armed Forces the necessary edge in knowledge and decision-making – for a secure Switzerland.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: the Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 occupations.
