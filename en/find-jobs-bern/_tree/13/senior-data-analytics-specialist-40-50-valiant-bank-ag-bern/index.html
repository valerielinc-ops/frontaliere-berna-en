Full description

Simplify together. Change together. For Valiant, data has a strategic value. In the company strategy Valiant 2029, Valiant is investing substantially in the modernization of the platforms. The goals: A Hub & Spoke organization with decentralized capabilities and strong data governance.

They shall

  • Understand the business requirements of existing reports and, together with the Reporting & Controlling Team and the specialist departments, transfer them into modern data products and power BI solutions
  • Professional users in dealing with new technologies such as Power BI and Snowflake specifically empower, train and coach and thus create an important basis for a successful and sustainable change of the organization
  • in close cooperation with Data Governance and HR, develop the basis for the promotion of Data Literacy
  • gain valuable insights through direct contact with a wide range of business areas, share experiences and support and accompany them in the creation, explanation and interpretation of data analytics
  • have a formative role in achieving the strategic goals of the data strategy by actively shaping the transformation, migration and visibility of the topic of data & analytics for the future
  • operate in a dynamic work environment where your ideas count, diversity is valued and every perspective is considered important

They bring

  • training in business informatics or business administration with:
  • profound experience in the design and implementation of BI solutions based on Microsoft SSAS Tabular and in particular Power BI with
  • Skills and experience in change management, accompanying organizations. Training skills are a big plus.
  • very good communication skills at various levels and experience in business analysis with
  • Experience in basic concepts such as data understanding, data visualization/IBCS, data storytelling and key figure definition with
  • Experience in the banking or financial sector is an advantage, but not a must
  • Having an entrepreneurial mindset that helps us set the right priorities in this complex world
  • Humor and teamwork and a pragmatic solution orientation

About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial services provider operating exclusively in Switzerland. It offers private customers and SMEs a comprehensive, easy-to-understand offer in all financial matters. Valiant is locally anchored in the following 15 cantons: Aargau, Basel Landscape, Basel City, Bern, Freiburg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. It is also present throughout Switzerland thanks to innovative, digital services. Valiant has a balance sheet total of 38.2 billion francs and employs around 1200 employees – of which more than 70 are apprentices.
