Compensation specialist:in 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Compensation specialist:in Bern / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You work in a small and technically strong team in the area of compensation and design challenging topics around wages, wage systems and employment conditions according to GAV and OR. As a central contact person for complex issues, you work closely with HR consultants, HR business partners, the HR community and management teams. With your contribution, you contribute to personnel policy requirements and create attractive and sustainable working conditions for around 33,000 employees. You can move that.

  • As a compensation specialist, you develop concepts, processes and instruments in the area of compensation and employment conditions, ensuring fairness, transparency and competitiveness.
  • In doing so, you drive forward the professional development and link legal and personnel policy framework conditions with the requirements of the business.
  • In HR projects, you take on the professional responsibility, lead strategic and digitally effective initiatives and accompany the implementation until completion.
  • With analyses and benchmarks, you identify trends and develop well-founded recommendations and decision-making principles.
  • You prepare templates for the HR management, Group management and Board of Directors in terms of content.
  • You advise HR management, HR business partners, HR consulting and other HR services in all questions regarding compensation and employment conditions.

You bring that with you.

  • Analytical conceptual thinking, a high degree of structuring ability and the joy of converting complex questions into practical solutions characterize you.
  • Sovereign communication at all hierarchical levels and cooperative cooperation with different stakeholders in a large environment are easy for you.
  • You bring in-depth know-how in compensation, wage system, job architecture and employment conditions according to GAV and OR.
  • With your proven many years of experience in this role, ideally at Group level, as well as experience in the management and implementation of strategic HR projects, you can convince everyone.
  • Your pronounced affinity for numbers and your safe handling of Excel as well as the ability to process analyses clearly and comprehensibly support you in your task.
  • A completed degree in business administration or HR management as well as a relevant further education in compensation or project management round off your profile. Very good knowledge of German (C2) and good knowledge of another national language is a must.
