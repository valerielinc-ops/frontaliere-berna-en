Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

Together we shape the future of paediatric medicine In the Maison de la Santé, just a few steps from Biel station, we create a place where children and their families are at the centre of attention and feel safe, understood and well looked after. With MEDIN Kids, a new outpatient children’s centre is being built in the Maison de la Santé in Biel with a wide range of specialized consultation hours. Different medical and therapeutic disciplines work closely together and bundle their expertise for comprehensive, coordinated care of children and adolescents under one roof. The offer includes, among others, developmental paediatrics, psychology, paediatric physiotherapy and paediatric ergotherapy. Interprofessional cooperation enables short paths, coordinated treatment concepts and holistic care that is consistently oriented towards the needs of children, adolescents and their families. As a member of our team, you take responsibility for your patients and actively participate in the development and development of this forward-looking outpatient children’s center.
