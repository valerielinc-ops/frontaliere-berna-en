Full description

This is what awaits you

As an assistant invoicing payroll, you are part of the management team, which jointly processes the payroll declarations in the area of accident and collective sickness allowance insurance.

This is what you do with us

The core of your job is the accounting and assessment of pay amounts. You are in contact with general agencies and make clarifications. You triage the post and answer telephone and written requests. Further activities within the department are possible.

This is what you bring with you

  • Service orientation with high quality standards and IT affinity
  • Enjoy working with numbers
  • Independent personality with a concentrated working method and quick comprehension
  • Fluent German skills, good oral French skills are a plus

Working with us

You work with other helpers in the team and experienced employees support you. Together you live a communicative, solution-oriented and independent work culture.

Your use is limited from December 2026 to max. End of April 2027. We are looking for helpers for 2.5

  • 3.5 months (December
  • March) and for 4.5 months (December
  • April). Your workload is 100% and your place of work is in Bern. More about our commitment as an employer can be found below.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
