Full description

We are an engineering company with company headquarters in Burgdorf and offer comprehensive services in the fields of civil engineering, building and railway construction, official surveying and engineering / railway surveying, as well as municipal water management, geoinformatics and building information modeling (BIM).

Your tasks

  • Drawing of project plans for structural structures in building construction
  • Building construction, basic construction, steel construction and repairs with focus on reinforced concrete construction
  • Plan preparation and construction controls
  • Support for Submissions
  • Working with 3D models and BIM models

Your strengths

  • You have completed your apprenticeship as a draftsman EFZ Specialist in Civil Engineering
  • You have very good CAD knowledge (advantageous Allplan)
  • They enjoy working in a team, are independent, reliable, communicative and flexible
  • They work accurately and efficiently
  • You are a proactive, ambitious and solution-oriented personality
  • You can express yourself stylishly in the German language, other languages are advantageous
