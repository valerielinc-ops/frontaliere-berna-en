Portrait We are a community school with six high school classes. Lessons take place in Model 4 in mixed-level classes, with great emphasis on team teaching and collaboration.

Tasks

As a class teacher, you take on the pedagogical and organizational leadership of a class, depending on the workload, also in the team.
