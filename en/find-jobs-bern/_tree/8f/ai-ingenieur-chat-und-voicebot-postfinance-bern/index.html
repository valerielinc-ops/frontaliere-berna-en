Full description

At PostFinance, you shape the future of customer service. As an AI engineer, you develop new AI-based functions, improve the quality of our chat and voicebots and ensure stable, secure operation. Together with your interdisciplinary team, you automate processes and use Generative AI where it creates real added value. So you move PostFinance and make the service for our customers easier and smarter.

You can do that.

You develop and optimize our chat and voicebots based on modern conversational AI and GenAI technologies

Implement, integrate and operate LLM-based customer service solutions

Develop intelligent AI solutions with RAG, Embeddings and AI Agents to improve response quality and process automation

You design prompts, guardrails and quality mechanisms for powerful, secure and compliant AI applications

Optimize voicebot capabilities including ASR/TTS integration, call flows and telephony processes

You will continuously analyze, test and improve bot performance using KPIs, user feedback and AI evaluations

Bring new features together with architecture, security, DevOps and business teams from idea to production

You implement new functions into the productive environment, ensure stable operation and support in 3rd-level support

You bring that with you

Higher education in computer science, business informatics, data science or comparable qualification

Several years of experience in software development, ideally with Python

Practical experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and their productive use

Know-how in at least one of the areas RAG, Embeddings, Vector Databases, Conversational AI, Agentic AI or Prompt Engineering as well as knowledge in Guardrails

Experience with AWS Bedrock or similar AI or cloud platforms as well as API integrations

Familiarity with Kubernetes, Helm or GitOps and experience in productive operation and in the further development of solutions

Analytical and pragmatic working methods as well as very good German and English skills

Knowledge in chat and voicebot platforms, GenAIOps/MLOps, AI evaluation, voice interactions as well as in the areas of security, audio streaming, data protection and responsible AI as an advantage
