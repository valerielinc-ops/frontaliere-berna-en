Place of work: Hirslanden Salem Hospital | Bern

Composition by: 01. August 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 69768

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Hirslanden Bern AG includes Klinik Beau-Site, the Salem Hospital and Klinik Permanence with a total of around 350 beds. At all three locations we offer apprenticeships as a specialist health EFZ with training start on 01. August 2027.

The Hirslanden Group offers you a diverse and attractive education. You will work in an experienced team, gain insights into different areas and be accompanied by a vocational trainer throughout the learning process. You also have the opportunity to complete an external internship in a psychiatric clinic.

YOUR TASKS

  • You perform medical procedures, such as blood draws or blood pressure measurements
  • You look after and care for patients at different stages of life
  • You support patients in everyday life
  • You take on administrative and logistical tasks
  • You do homework tasks
  • You work closely together in the team

Your profile

  • You have completed elementary school (secondary school or very good degree)
  • You are interested in healthcare
  • You enjoy working with people
  • You have a very good knowledge of German
  • You like working in a team
  • You have a good understanding and organizational ability

Our offer

  • Attractive and central workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Family atmosphere with you - culture
  • Professional support and individual support in the learning process
  • Extensive benefits offer (e.g. publishing, public transport coupons, fitness subscriptions, etc.)
  • Discounted food options in restaurants in clinics
  • Financial contribution to teaching equipment
  • Annual learning excursion

For additional information, Martina Obrecht, vocational training officer, is available at martinamanuela.obrecht@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
