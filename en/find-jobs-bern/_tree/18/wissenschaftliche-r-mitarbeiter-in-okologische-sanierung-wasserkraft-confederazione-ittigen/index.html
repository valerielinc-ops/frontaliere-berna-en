Full description

  • Responsible for the assessment and financing of rehabilitation projects in the area of fish migration in the assigned cantons (Romandie and German-speaking Switzerland)
  • Accompanying and advising the assigned cantons (and other stakeholders) in the field of fish accessibility (Romandie and German-speaking Switzerland)
  • Specialist and contact person for subject-specific questions on the ecological rehabilitation of fish migration obstacles (deer, hydropower plants, locks, thresholds,..)
  • Coordination of energy policy activities in the Water Department and representation of the department in internal and external bodies
  • Co-responsible for the development and further development of implementation bases in connection with the financing of rehabilitation measures (fish migration, slosh-sunk, sliding budget) and contact person for financing issues in rehabilitation projects
  • Responsible for handling political transactions related to the financing of the hydroelectric clean-up
  • Technical or university degree in aquatic ecology (biology, environmental sciences, environmental engineering) or hydraulic engineering with good knowledge of aquatic ecology/fishing
  • Professional experience in aquatic ecology (planning, monitoring or implementation of projects), if possible in ecological rehabilitation hydropower
  • Experience and interest in energy policy issues and financial issues
  • Professional and project management experience in science, administration or a specialist office
  • Independent, goal-oriented and solution-oriented working method, good communication skills and teamwork
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, knowledge of a third official language
