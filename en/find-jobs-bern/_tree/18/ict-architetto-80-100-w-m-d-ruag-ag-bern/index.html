Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Supporting projects in the field of architecture as well as advising various departments and stakeholders on architecture issues
  • Model-based capture of actual architectures and definition of target architectures based on metamodels and architecture frameworks (e.g. TOGAF, ArchiMate)
  • Analyze the overall contexts of business processes, organizational structures, computer science and embedded systems
  • Create, develop and check compliance with principles and guidelines for the corporate and business architecture
  • Lead architectural projects to develop secure, robust and flexible system landscapes using agile methods such as Scrum or SAFe
  • Conducting specialist presentations and training on architecture methods, metamodels and concepts

You bring that with you

  • Graduate degree in business informatics, computer science, mathematics or equivalent education with relevant professional experience
  • Experience with architecture frameworks (TOGAF, arc42) and modeling (ArchiMate, BPMN, UML), knowledge in business analysis and business administration
  • Analytical, networked working methods as well as experience with agile methods (e.g. SAFe)
  • Strong skills in problem solving, abstraction and joy in dealing with complexity
  • Communication and decision-making competence with business focus

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
