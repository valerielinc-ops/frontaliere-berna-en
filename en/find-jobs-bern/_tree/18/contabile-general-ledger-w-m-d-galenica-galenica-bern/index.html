Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

You do not want to simply record figures, but take responsibility and actively improve processes? With us you accompany companies competently through the degrees and ensure a high quality in the ledger. You will work in a seven-person team that supports each other and bring your ideas to the optimization and digitalization of our financial processes.

You can do that.

  • You take responsibility in the ledger and prepare the half-year and annual accounts according to IFRS and OR.
  • You ensure the quality of the financial statements, record them in the consolidation tool and plausibility of the corresponding information.
  • You keep the investment accounting according to Group specifications and book receipts, disposals, sales and depreciation.
  • You create the VAT statements and ensure their correct and timely submission and payment.
  • You question existing processes, bring in new ideas and use modern tools such as Microsoft Copilot to make processes more efficient.
  • You are involved in projects in the financial sector as well as in topics relating to the internal control system. Project participation was already part of the previous listing.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed the Swiss certificate in finance and accounting or are currently in the corresponding further education.
  • You demonstrably bring your first professional experience in the ledger and are balance sheet and financial statement safe.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of IFRS and OR accounting and work routinely with Excel and other MS Office applications.
  • You are tech-savvy, enjoy working with SAP-based processes and enjoy trying out and using digital tools like Microsoft Copilot.
  • You work exactly and independently, take on responsibility and get involved in labor-intensive final phases.
  • You communicate safely in German at level C1
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported education and training as well as internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
