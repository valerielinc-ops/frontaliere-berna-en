Put to the Point

As a business owner, you are the central interface between our payers and service providers and actively participate in the further development of our services. You understand the needs of our internal and external partners, prioritize requirements and accompany them from the idea to the implementation. You network the right contacts, coordinate the cooperation and actively participate in projects and transformation projects. With your focus on customer needs and practical solutions, you provide important impetus for the continuous development of our services and service management. You want to take responsibility, actively participate and make a difference together with us? We look forward to meeting you!

  • Date of entry: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21072
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for the relationship with the assigned payers and representing the Cyber command as the central contact person to internal and external payers
  • Identify, analyze and prioritize the needs of the recipients and ensure their consideration in performance planning, service development and provision of services
  • Advise payers on technical issues and accompany them from requirements collection to the successful implementation of agreed adjustments
  • Coordinate cooperation between payers and providers, manage escalations and promote continuous improvement of service quality
  • Participate in projects, programmes, committees and transformation projects and contribute the interests of the payees to the further development of the service portfolio
  • Support the establishment, operation and continuous development of the services and services of the Cyber Command and thus make a significant contribution to the further development of service management

This makes you unique

  • University degree (FH, ETH or University, Bachelor/Master) in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Engineering or a comparable subject area
  • Multi-year subject-specific training (e.g. CAS, MAS or EMBA) in ICT strategy, IT management, digital government, enterprise architecture, leadership or public management
  • At least 10 years of professional experience in the ICT environment, of which several years in a strategic or managerial function, preferably in the federal administration, in the VBS or in security-relevant organizations
  • Proven experience in stakeholder and relationship management as well as in advising executives and organizations
  • Experience in managing or accompanying complex transformation, digitization or organizational projects
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and passive knowledge of a third official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
