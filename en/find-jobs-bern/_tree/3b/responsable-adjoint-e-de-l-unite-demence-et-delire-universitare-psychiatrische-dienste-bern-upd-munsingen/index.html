University Hospital for Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Do you want to take on responsibility, actively shape the care and develop yourself professionally and personally? On our station dementia and delirium for people over the age of 65, we are looking for a committed personality who enjoys taking on leadership responsibility together with the station management and ensuring high-quality, person-centered care together with the team. At the Clinic for Old Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy, you can expect an appreciative working environment in which humanity, quality and innovative approaches go hand in hand.

Your tasks

  • You support the station management in the technical, personnel and organizational management of the station and represent them in their absence
  • You accompany, promote and support the care team in everyday care and contribute to an open and constructive cooperation
  • You ensure patient-oriented, needs-based and professional care and promote the continuous development of care quality
  • You work closely with the station management and the interdisciplinary professional groups and support a coordinated and efficient cooperation
  • You are actively involved in the further development of care processes, workflows and station-related projects
  • You take responsibility in the day-to-day organization of the station operation and support the team even in complex care situations

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF / FH or an equivalent degree
  • You bring a management training or are motivated to develop in the field of leadership and management.
  • You have a contemporary understanding of care and implement a situation- and patient-friendly care process-oriented and interdisciplinary networked
  • You meet people with empathy, patience and appreciation and understand how to motivate employees and support them in everyday care
  • You communicate clearly and appropriately, enjoy working in a team and contribute to the cooperation in a solution-oriented manner
  • You like to take on responsibility, think ahead and want to continuously develop your leadership skills
  • You have stylish knowledge of German as well as good IT user knowledge, especially in the common office programs

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
