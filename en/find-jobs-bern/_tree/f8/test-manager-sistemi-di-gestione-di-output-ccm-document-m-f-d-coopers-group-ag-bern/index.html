Full description

For our customer from Bern, we are looking for:n experienced:n and open-minded:n Test Manager - Output / CCM management systems.

This 50% position may be combined with the Test Engineer (50%).

Your tasks

  • Development and responsibility of the test strategy and the test concept for the replacement of the existing CCM, in particular the old/new comparison (production of documents with identical data stream on both systems)
  • Planning, controlling and monitoring of all test activities from conception to implementation, in close coordination with product owner, development, architecture and specialist areas
  • Define a solution approach for documents that cannot be triggered manually (e.g. reminders, month-end processing), for example via simulation, time control or batch test environments
  • Establish test data management with use-case-based test data (e.g. real estate financing) in compliance with data protection requirements
  • Coordination of the test phases (integration test, end-to-end test, performance/mass test, UAT) and management of the defect management
  • Transparent test reporting to project management and steering committees as well as assessment of introductory maturity (go/no-go)

Your profile

  • Several years of experience as a test manager in large system replacement or migration projects
  • Experience in the banking environment is beneficial
  • Knowledge of Customer Communication Management / Output Management (e.g. OpenText Exstream, Quadient Inspire, Compart, Messagepoint or similar)
  • Good knowledge of the software development process in the agile environment (e.g. SAFe/Scrum)
  • Strong skills in leadership, coordination and communication with stakeholders from the field, IT and operations
  • Training/Certificate ISTQB Advanced Level Test Manager (or equivalent)
  • Certificate of good repute (no evictions or criminal records)
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English
