Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Our coding team ensures that every inpatient treatment case can be fully documented, correctly encrypted and billed on time. You also keep track of extensive patient dossiers, are familiar with the medical language and like to work exactly? Then we look forward to meeting you. As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement we are looking for:n

Task area

  • You check patient records for completeness, compile coding medical records and independently manage the pendences list of open cases
  • You request missing medical documents (such as exit and operation reports) from clinics and external practices and archive coding-relevant documentation professionally
  • You are the contact person for the medical profession and secretariats for questions relating to case documentation
  • You are administratively responsible for the timely and data protection-compliant dispatch of medical documents (e.g. via HIN-Mail) to health insurers and trusted physicians for reporting inquiries and complaints of inpatient cases
  • You complete the correspondence as well as general administrative tasks of the team Coding
  • You support the Head of Coding & Medical Controlling in the preparation of revisions, the preparation of evaluations and other administrative tasks

Profile

  • Completed training as medical secretary: in (e.g. H+) or comparable training in the medical-administrative area
  • Safe knowledge of medical terminology
  • Professional experience in hospital or healthcare, ideally in coding, billing or patient administration
  • Knowledge of SwissDRG, ICD-10 and CHOP benefits
  • Secure handling of MS Office and clinical information systems
  • Precise, independent and reliable working method and high discretion
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
