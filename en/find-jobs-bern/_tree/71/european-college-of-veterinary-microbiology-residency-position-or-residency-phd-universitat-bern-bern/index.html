Full description

The UniBE is ambitious. With us, they perform excellence and compete with the best.

Responsibilities and tasks

  • Learning the various diagnostic laboratory methods
  • Case management incl. Reporting on results and communication with customers (in German and French)
  • Cooperation in weekend/holiday service
  • Assistance in the training of students
  • Preparation of scientific publications
  • when combining with PhD: working on the PhD project

Conditions

  • A completed study of veterinary medicine
  • PhD or PhD degree (not for the combined residency PhD position)
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Language requirements: fluent in German and English and at least basic knowledge of the French language
  • Clinical experience is beneficial.

Individual Career Support Application and contact With us, you create value. For themselves and society. We are looking for a flexible and motivated veterinarian with a high interest in diagnostic microbiology. The work in the Diagnostic Department combined with corresponding Externships, is designed to acquire sufficient knowledge to take the examination of the European College of Veterinary Microbiology (Board Examination). For candidates with a passion for research, there is also the possibility of a combined residency PhD program. The minimum duration for the residency is 3 years, for the combination with PhD 6 years.
