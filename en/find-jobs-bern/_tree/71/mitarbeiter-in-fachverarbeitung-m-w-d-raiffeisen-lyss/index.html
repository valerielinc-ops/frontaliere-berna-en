25.09.2026 Lyss

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Seeland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Aarberg, Biel, Büren, Lyss, Ins and Studen, we are looking for a motivated personality as an employee for our location in Lyss: in specialist processing.

What awaits you?

  • Shared responsibility for the compliant and timely settlement of all credit transactions in the retail and corporate segment
  • Processing of loan applications: recording and checking for completeness of documents, preparation of loan templates and contract documents, timely disbursement and redemption, netting and repatriation of credit positions
  • Processing of periodic new permits, construction loan processing, management of electronic customer dossier and debt letter management (Terravis)
  • Support of credit advice in administrative matters
  • Telephone and written communications with offices, insurance companies and other banks

What do you bring?

  • preferentially completed banking education with initial experience in the credit sector
  • However, lateral entrants with a commercial background are also welcome!
  • You feel comfortable in dealing with and applying regulatory requirements
  • Practical user knowledge of the MS-Office range and IT affinity
  • High quality and responsibility awareness
  • Self-employed and precise working methods
