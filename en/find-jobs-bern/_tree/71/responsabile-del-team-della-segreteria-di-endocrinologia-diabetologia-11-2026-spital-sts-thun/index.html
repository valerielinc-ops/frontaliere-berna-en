Full description

You take over the technical and personnel management of the secretarial team and ensure efficient organization and coordination of the consultation hours of four doctors and several diabetes specialist consultants. In the future, the coordination of nutritional advice will also be added.

You ensure smooth processes in the secretariat, continue to develop existing processes and actively support your team in day-to-day business – be it at the patient’s reception, on the phone or in the preparation and follow-up of the consultation hours.

The establishment of our planned metabolic center also opens up the prospect of taking on a further management function in the future. Tasks: • A versatile turntable function with responsibility, scope for design and development prospects • A well-established, motivated team that supports each other and works together easily across borders • An appreciative leadership culture at eye level, with trust, openness and a pinch of humor • Attractive training opportunities supported by the hospital • Individual choices for holidays and pension funds, suitable for your life situation • An attractive environment with diverse leisure and nature experiences between the lake and mountains Requirements • You have a commercial or MPA basic education with a corresponding further education with professional and managerial experience in the hospital environment or a larger practice • You have an independent, reliable and responsible way of working, a quick understanding and a strong organizational talent • You like to further develop existing processes and enjoy contributing new ideas and actively shaping processes • You bring enthusiasm for working with a dedicated, dynamic team and have the ability to keep track even in lively situations • You meet our patients with empathy and commitment and attach great importance to a high quality of service A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region.
