Your mission

  • Build, maintain and repair low and medium voltage power grids
  • Work on air and underground networks
  • Perform laying, shooting, connecting and joining cables
  • Install and maintain electrical paintings, transformation stations and network equipment
  • Participating in public lighting and connection work
  • Read and interpret technical projects and schemes and perform field survey
  • Intervention in case of faults and ensure maintenance of plants
  • Strictly comply with the standards, safety requirements and procedures in force
  • Collaboration with the team leaders and the various operators in the yards

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have achieved an AFC as a network electrician or equivalent training recognized in Switzerland
  • You have experience in building and maintaining electrical networks
  • Feel comfortable with outdoor work, regardless of weather conditions
  • Do not suffer from dizziness and feel comfortable working at altitude
  • You are autonomous, precise, reliable and equipped with a good team spirit
  • You have sense of responsibility and strictly respect safety standards
  • You are available for repair operations or for a return service if necessary
  • It is mandatory to have category B driving licence; the BE or C1 driver's licence is an advantage
  • You have a good command of French, both written and spoken
