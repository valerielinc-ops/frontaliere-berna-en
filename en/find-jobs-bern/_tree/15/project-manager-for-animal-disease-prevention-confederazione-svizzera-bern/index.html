Put to the Point

In view of the increasing threat of animal diseases in Switzerland, increased cooperation and coordination with the cantons, the scientific circles and the various players in the industry is central. For this purpose, the BLV receives additional financial resources until 2029. In cooperation with your project group and other departments of the BLV, you will make a valuable contribution to the development and implementation of a clear framework for program control, which sets priorities and ensures that the funds are used transparently and comprehensibly in accordance with the law, and enables structured, efficient reporting.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21151
  • Location: Schwarzenburgstrasse 155, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure operational management of the entire portfolio: setting priorities, consolidating budget, commitments, resources, contracts, risks and results, coordinating internal and external interfaces, developing documented decision-making bases for the distribution of funds that are comparable, introducing financial reporting and quarterly reports for target achievement
  • Ensure availability of vaccines to act promptly in the event of an animal disease
  • Develop coordination, communication and measures for crisis prevention with internal and external actors under the additional funding received by the FFO
  • Coordinate areas of expertise and cooperation with other federal offices, federal agencies and the cantons

This makes them unique.

  • Studies of veterinary medicine as well as experience in program and project management
  • Several years of professional experience and experience in animal disease control and incident and crisis management
  • Initiative Personality with a positive attitude
  • Personal responsibility, flexibility, high resilience as well as analytical and networked thinking and acting
  • High IT affinity
  • Strong communication skills, mastery of two official languages

For the welfare of humans and animals

The Federal Food Safety and Veterinary Office (BLV) is the federal competence centre for food safety, nutrition, animal health, animal welfare and species protection in international trade. With respect, competence, trust, commitment, creativity and openness, we make a great contribution to the common good. As an employee of the BLV, you actively promote the health and well-being of humans and animals.

The reconciliation of work and family as well as the professional development opportunities are highly appreciated by the employees and managers of the BLV.

The offices of the Federal Department of the Interior (EDI) are systematically committed to good working conditions and the health of their employees and are awarded the label Friendly Work Space.
