Full description

University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The Schwartzlin station of the Universitary Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) offers a specialized, twelve-week psychotherapeutic treatment program with a focus on cognitive behavioral therapy. You accompany patients with various mental illnesses – including anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders, trauma follow-up disorders, personality disorders, stabilized psychosis and multiple diagnoses with substance use disorders. As a health specialist, you are in the middle of everyday therapeutic life. You accompany people who deal intensively with their mental illness and support them in trying out new behaviors and strengthening their independence. Your presence, your powers of observation and your ability to build sustainable relationships are especially in demand. Together with an experienced interprofessional team, you will contribute to a safe, appreciative and therapeutically beneficial environment.

Your tasks

  • You support our patients in coping with their everyday life and accompany them during their inpatient stay
  • You promote everyday practical skills, independence and social skills and support patients in using their personal resources
  • You actively shape the daily routine and accompany patients in milieutherapeutic activities and group offers
  • You support the implementation of individual therapy goals in everyday care and help patients to test new experiences and behaviors in everyday life
  • You will notice changes in your mental and physical health attentively and bring your observations into the interprofessional exchange
  • You carry out nursing and medical engineering activities according to your area of competence and ensure professional documentation
  • You work closely with the qualified nurses as well as the medical, psychological and therapeutic specialists

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a health professional EFZ
  • You are interested in psychiatry and in particular in the psychotherapeutic accompaniment of people with anxiety and obsessive-compulsive diseases, trauma follow-up disorders and personality disorders
  • You enjoy supporting patients in everyday life, activating them and promoting their existing abilities
  • You meet people with psychiatric disorders with understanding, patience and an empathetic attitude
  • You notice changes in behavior and well-being attentively and can pass on your observations in a differentiated way.
  • You like to contribute to the design of a structured and therapeutically oriented station everyday life
  • You work reliably, flexibly and responsibly and appreciate the exchange and cooperation in the interprofessional team
  • You are open to professional development and would like to deepen your competences in the field of psychiatric care

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
