As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients and very well-qualified medical doctors.

YOUR TASKS

  • You design, update and prepare existing print materials such as flyers, brochures, forms and cards professionally.
  • You create ready-to-print data, take over format adaptations, image processing and technical print preparation.
  • You digitize existing communication tools and maintain interactive and fillable PDFs.
  • You implement layouts and templates according to Hirslanden Corporate Design and ensure a structured storage of open graphic files and templates.
  • If necessary, you support with photo and video recordings, video editing, simple animations, subtitling and photo retouching.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a mediamatist, graphic designer or comparable qualification.
  • Ideally, you will have at least two years of professional experience as well as a good understanding of layout, pure drawing, image processing and print preparation.
  • You work independently, structured and exact and keep an eye on deadlines and priorities even for short-term assignments.
  • You are flexible, communicative and enjoy working closely with marketing & communication as well as internal departments.
  • German or French is your mother tongue
  • Good knowledge of the other language is an advantage.
  • Experience with photo and video recordings, video editing, simple animations or photo retouching is an additional plus.

Why is it worth working with us?

  • We work respectfully and respectfully at eye level with each other
  • Possibility of working with flex and leisure plus models
  • Up to 4 additional free days per year as compensation for changing time (pension-dependent)
  • Above-average night and weekend allowance of CHF 10.- per hour
  • Many discounts and perks in the fitness center, over 100 service providers, leisure events and mobile subscriptions
