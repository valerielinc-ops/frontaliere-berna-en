Full description

Portrait The six municipalities of the right Zulgtal above Steffisburg have merged to form the MR region Zulg. All children of the 6 primary schools and the upper secondary school are taught in standard classes. While the integrative promotion takes place in the respective locations, speech therapy and awareness and movement promotion is offered decentrally in the region.

Tasks

You work inclusively with students of cycle 1 & 2 (total 2 classes: basic level, intermediate level) and with the rule teachers. You are concerned about the advice of parents, as well as the exchange with all stakeholders (rule/MR school management, teachers, MR team, parental counselling, children and adolescent psychiatric service). You are part of the regional MR team with joint meetings, training, etc.

Requirements

We look forward to you, a person who has a big heart for students who need support and who is enthusiastic about their work. You are a team player: good networking is important to you and a support-oriented cooperation is a matter of course for you.

For us, an education as a curative teacher is important, but also your person. Therefore, we would also like to get to know you if you are still in training as a curative teacher (or have experience as a teacher at this level and have an affinity and interest in supporting and accompanying children in need of support).

We offer

  • an exciting, varied field of work with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Open and helpful team and school management who are happy to help you
  • Regular exchange with EB
  • Place of work in beautiful rural idyll
  • free parking at the school house is available
