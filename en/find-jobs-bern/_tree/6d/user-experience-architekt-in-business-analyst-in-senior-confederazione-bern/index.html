Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is part of the Federal Competence Centre for all core questions of labour market policy, employment services, unemployment insurance and labour market statistics. The Department of Informatics for Unemployment Insurance (ALV) Switzerland is responsible for the digitization of business processes as well as the operation of the business applications of the Unemployment Fund as well as the employment services of job seekers and their reintegration measures into the labour market. As part of the IT department, the ITAS3 team is responsible for the operation, maintenance and further development of eGovernment applications as well as the service management of IT services. It ensures stable operation and 2nd and 3rd level support. In addition, ITAS3 is involved in the training of IT learners and thus contributes to the sustainable development of specialists.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
