Full description

Portrait Riggisberg is a place with about 3100 inhabitants, beautifully located at the end of the Langnberg. The school can be reached by car or public transport in about 30 minutes from Bern or Thun. Parking is freely available. Our school comprises 20 classes, divided between kindergarten up to secondary level I. The classes of primary level are mixed in age and conducted in two stages. At intermediate level, there are two 3rd/4th classes in the current school year, a 4th/5. Class and two 5th/6th classes. Cycle 1 is taught at a separate location, the sub-level centre at the Abeggstrasse. The Riggisberg School also runs a basic and intermediate level (3-6th class) at the Rümligen site.
