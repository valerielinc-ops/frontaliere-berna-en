Put to the Point

Are you interested in shaping the further development of the army? Do you have a vision of the geopolitical and technological world of the future, know how to deal with data and artificial intelligence, and have the ability to design decision-making bases for recipients? Located in the Army Staff, you contribute to prospective planning in the Defense Group.

  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21051

You can make this contribution

  • Developing scientific basis for decision-making in the field of research, anticipation and prospective analysis
  • Procure and analyse reports and research results from national and international reports on medium- and long-term developments in security policy, economics, military doctrine, technology, society and ecology
  • Steering, capturing and evaluating the research needs of the various organisational units of the Defence Group
  • Assess scientific publications on topics relevant to armed forces development
  • Training in prospective analysis and anticipation for the benefit of the Defence Group

  • Experience in strategic or prospective analysis, anticipation or research management
  • Experience in using databases and artificial intelligence
  • Very good knowledge of security policy and general military knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of a second official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The Army Staff (A Staff) supports the Chief of the Army in the leadership of the Departmental Department of Defence and is responsible for the overall military planning and development of the Swiss Army. Here, in accordance with the political guidelines, the strategic foundations, the armed forces development and the army and armaments planning are developed and tasks in the international security context are performed. The command, control and coordination of all cross-cutting areas (personnel, finance, real estate) also fall under the responsibility of the Army Staff.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
