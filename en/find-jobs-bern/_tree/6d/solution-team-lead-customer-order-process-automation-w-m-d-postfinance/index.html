Full description

As Head of Solution Team Customer Order Process Automation (COPA), you take on responsibility at the interface of customers, technology and innovation. Together with your team, you digitize and automate end-to-end customer processes so that issues can be handled easily, quickly and digitally. With your sense of technological trends, your leadership experience and your implementation strength, you create the framework for successful, self-organized teams. This will help PostFinance further expand its position as a digital innovation leader in the Swiss financial world.

As a line manager of the solution team “Customer Order Process Automation”, you are also part of the management team of IT Banking Journeys.

You can do that.

  • Together with the Solution Product Owner and the Solution Architecture Owner, you will form the professional leadership in the trio for the Solution Team Customer Order Process Automation as part of our Enterprise Agile Delivery Organization.
  • You are responsible for the delivery capability of the entire solution team with around 100 employees and optimize throughput, quality and resource use together with the delivery team leads.
  • You work closely with relevant suppliers and ensure their smooth integration
  • You ensure that obstacles are solved within the Solution Team and in cooperation beyond your own Solution Team
  • You create optimal working conditions to make the organization more agile and innovative and to promote the leadership of increasingly self-organized teams
  • You promote continuous learning and challenge and promote systematic development
  • You embody agile values and principles, live an Agile Growth Mindset and actively support Agile Leaders and the organization in the application of agile practices and the development of the corresponding mindset

You bring that with you

  • Tertiary degree in computer science, business informatics and/or business administration
  • Several years of experience in a management position, for example as a line manager in an agile environment, as a Release Train Engineer or Agile Leader
  • Extensive practical experience in the application and further development of agile practices and frameworks (e.g. SAFe, Scrum, Kanban, DevOps) as well as in agile product and software development
  • Strong communication skills with strength in moderation and conflict resolution
  • In-depth experience in team and personnel development as well as organizational development and change management
  • Knowledge of process automation with Camunda, UiPath and individual developments based on Java and Spring Boot as an additional advantage
  • Complete knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in words and writing
