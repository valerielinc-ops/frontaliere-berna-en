Put to the Point

Passenger Cars (FSPW) is the fleet manager of the professional military’s personal service vehicles. We ensure mobility in cooperation with our military and civilian partners. The small and dynamic team convinces with a lot of passion for vehicles, a modern fleet management software and a high service-oriented way of working.

  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20760

You can make this contribution

  • Caring for professional military personnel throughout the life cycle of service vehicles from A to Z (for example, advising on vehicle selection, examining offers, responsible for maintenance and repair requests as well as professional and commercial invoice processing)
  • Answer questions in after sales (e.g. questions from professional military personnel, garages, internal and external partners, technical and commercial matters, etc.) from all 3 language regions of Switzerland
  • Operational and interdisciplinary supervision and management of personal service vehicles of professional military personnel
  • Function as a turntable function vis-à-vis internal and external locations
  • For the benefit of the owners, take over the administrative support during vehicle changes
  • Cooperation in internal and cross-divisional projects (armasuisse, suppliers, army staff, car importers, etc.)

This makes them unique.

  • Basic technical training as an automotive mechatronics technician with further training for / to the Federal Government. Business economist in the automotive industry, technical merchant or equivalent training
  • Very good technical understanding and analytical, networked and holistic thinking; supplemented by very good IT knowledge (SAP knowledge of advantage) and operational accounting
  • Several years of experience in the vehicle industry, preferably in vehicle fleet management and comprehensive knowledge of business relationships and life-cycle cost processes according to the principles of the TCO model
  • Conceptual and structured thinking, teamwork, customer- and service-oriented action, negotiating skills and assertiveness, skill in oral and written expression, as well as good knowledge of operational accounting
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of at least one second official language
