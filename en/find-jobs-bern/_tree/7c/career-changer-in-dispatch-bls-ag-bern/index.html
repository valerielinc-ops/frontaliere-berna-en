Full description

A construction site in southern Germany, a defective train in front of the Lötschberg tunnel or a storm in Domodossola? Such events upset our plans. But we remain on course – strong in solutions and unshakable. If you are also storm-proof in turbulent moments and quickly do the right thing, then you fit perfectly into our team. During the instruction, you will acquire both theoretical basics and practical skills for your future profession. Our intensive training, combined with your motivation, will prepare you optimally for your new task.

This is waiting for you

  • Plan for locomotive personnel in Switzerland and quickly provide replacements in the event of breakdowns
  • Dispatch locomotives on international routes – from Benelux to Italy – and react immediately in the event of technical malfunctions
  • Be a hub for our customers, partner railways and internal colleagues and keep you informed about changes
  • Detect deviations at an early stage and act flexibly in the event of delays or staff absences
  • Ensure punctuality, keep the transport schedule stable and plan economically

What you bring to the table

  • A completed 3-year apprenticeship or Matura
  • Enjoyment of resource planning and team collaboration, with a keen sense of people
  • Positive attitude towards irregular working hours (7x24 in 3-shift operation)
  • Good knowledge of Italian or English
  • A cool head even in the most hectic situations, thanks to organizational talent, networked thinking and solution-oriented attitude

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • We promote the most flexible planning of your tours and shifts
  • Get a GA travelcard 2. Class, a GA travelcard 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are surcharges for workloads at night and on weekends.
  • It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external training and an exciting internal learning and course offer are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About BLS Cargo

We can't move mountains, of course. It doesn't matter. Because as experienced alpinists in rail freight transport, we see mountains as a challenge and not as an obstacle. We transport goods weighing tons from the North Sea through the Alps to the Mediterranean, and that is a tricky task. We master it in a team with our approximately 450 employees in Switzerland, Belgium, Germany and Italy. Our customers are international, our roots are local. This is another reason why it is exceptionally exciting to work for BLS Cargo.
