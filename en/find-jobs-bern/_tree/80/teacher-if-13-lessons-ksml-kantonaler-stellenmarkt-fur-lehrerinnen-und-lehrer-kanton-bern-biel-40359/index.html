Full description

Portrait The primary school Mühlefeld comprises 17 classes, including 5 kindergarten classes. The schoolhouse is beautifully located in the quiet Mühlefeld quarter. We are well connected and work closely together at the stage. We teach diversely with emphasis on the churner model. Nonviolent communication (after Rosenberg) is important to us. We are supported by a school social worker. French classes from the Champ-du-Moulin primary school also work in our school. Our students attend a bilingual day school.

Tasks

You teach in a 1st grade with a stint of 13 lessons as an IF teacher. At our school, IF teachers take on all the lessons of a class (bVSA, eU, IF and MRDV). In this sense, we attach importance to close cooperation and good cooperation in the class team.

Requirements

They love working with children of different cultures. They have teaching experience in the field of curative education or corresponding life experience and like to work in a team. They ideally have a curative education, a PH degree or other qualification for this employment.

We are looking forward to a dedicated teacher who likes to work with heterogeneous classes. There is at most the possibility to increase the lessons by summer 27 (IF at 1-6 classes).

We offer

We offer permanent employment at a lively school. You will be supported in your work by the college, the school management as well as the school social work and the day school. Cooperation is close and cordial. We are a committed college with established forms of cooperation. Individual further education as well as an interdisciplinary exchange are part of this with us. We also offer:

  • An attractive participatory hour planning
  • adequate classrooms, a personal laptop and digital means of communication
  • Good introduction
  • Attractive place of work (only 7 minutes walk from Biel station)
  • Mentorship
  • well-functioning school house team with trained class teachers
  • Working groups by area of interest (health, nonviolent communication, tax group, sports day, school festival, ...)
  • Bilingualism (work in the mother tongue, no special knowledge of French necessary)
  • Support from school management
  • School social worker and day school on site
  • Working with KLAPP (communication with parents)

The city of Biel offers various discounts (indoor swimming pool and fitness subscription, rail check, ...)
