Full description

Portrait Appreciation – Joy – Trust – Openness – Respect – Warmth

The Anker-Dorf Ins is a well-developed and attractive community with around 3,900 inhabitants in the middle of the 3-lake region. The school complex is beautifully embedded in the center of Ins. In a motivated and committed team of 30 teachers and additional specialised teachers, around 300 students are taught in cycles 1 and 2. A school secretary, day school principal and staff, school social workers, housekeepers and members of the authorities also participate in the successful school day.

As a result of the retirement of our co-principal, we are looking for 1. August 2027 as a supplement to our co-school management team

Tasks

  • As a member of the school management, you take on the operational and personnel leadership in Cycle 1 with a lot of leadership and esprit.
  • You design school and teaching development in cooperation with the co-management, the college and the authority
  • Represented together with your job partners You are the school against the outside and towards the administration and authority
  • With a strong team, they develop a forward-looking strategy taking into account current trends
  • They maintain the exchange and cooperation with the municipal and regional educational institutions and with the municipal administration
  • They represent the primary school in various committees

Requirements

  • A recognised teaching diploma or equivalent education
  • Ideally, you have completed the training for school management or are ready to complete the course "THE school is leading"
  • Organisational and leadership competencies
  • Clear communication, negotiating skills and assertiveness
  • Experience and pleasure in accompanying change processes
  • A flair for organizational tasks and enjoyment of process optimizations
  • They appreciate dealing with different people and stakeholders, are capable of teamwork and can also master turbulent situations professionally.

We offer

  • An exciting and versatile leadership function
  • Comprehensive familiarization and cooperation with two experienced co-directors with a high degree of independence and responsibility
  • A primary school, with a good infrastructure and a very committed college
  • The opportunity to shape and shape leadership and organizational structures
  • Culture of mutual respect, openness, helpfulness, trust and warmth
  • Good cooperation with an open-minded commission and local authorities
  • Well-connected educational institutions
  • Employment according to cantonal guidelines
  • The workload can be supplemented by additional lessons if necessary within the framework of the joint workload planning for the school year 2027/28
