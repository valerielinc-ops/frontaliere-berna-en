Business Analyst Scheduling and Operations Management (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Business Analyst Scheduling and Operations Management (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In our digital organization for timetable and capacity, you will use modern computer systems to shape the future of rail planning and control. Our Agile Release Train Analytics and Datahub is at the heart of developing and operating applications that support core business processes in passenger and freight transport. Around the topics of train formation and formations, we implement the upcoming modernizations in timetable planning and operational management and thus create noticeable benefits for travelers and customers. You work in a committed, interdisciplinary team with a lot of creative leeway, short decision paths and good opportunities for further development in a scaled agile environment. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a business analyst, you control the implementation of capacity management measures and thus make a measurable contribution to reliable and efficient rail transport.
  • You will analyze and concretize the measures for CM1 (Capacity Management Step 1) within our Agile Release Train and consider existing solutions and use cases.
  • From the technical requirements, you derive features and user stories and formulate them clearly and feasibly.
  • You create and maintain documentation for different target groups.
  • You support the Product Owner in Team Castor in all clarifications and coordination tasks.

You bring that with you.

  • With your analytical and structured way of working, you keep track of frequent changes in context and communicate safely with different stakeholders.
  • At least 5 years of experience as a business analyst in complex IT products forms the basis of your work.
  • Strong technical understanding helps you to translate technical issues into viable technical solutions.
  • Ideally, you have in-depth railway knowledge in the field of formations and roadmap and have completed a university degree in computer science, business informatics or a comparable education.
  • As a team player with your own initiative, you are involved in self-organized teams and know agile methods such as Scrum, Kanban and SAFe.
  • You bring German to at least level C1 and English to at least level B2.

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This job is in requirement level K, regional allowance level 1.
