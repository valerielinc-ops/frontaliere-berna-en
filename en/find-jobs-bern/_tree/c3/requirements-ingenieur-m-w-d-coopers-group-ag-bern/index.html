For our customer in Bern we are looking for a committed personality as a requirements engineer.

Your tasks

  • Accompaniment of technical solution finding from conception to solution design until introduction
  • Close collaboration with product owners, architects and development teams
  • Analysis, collection and documentation of professional requirements

  • Strong end-to-end thinking as well as experience in testing and quality assurance
  • Very good moderation and communication skills in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Certification in Requirements Engineering is advantageous (e.g. IREB, BABOK, IIBA or similar)
  • Correct repute without enforcement or criminal records
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
