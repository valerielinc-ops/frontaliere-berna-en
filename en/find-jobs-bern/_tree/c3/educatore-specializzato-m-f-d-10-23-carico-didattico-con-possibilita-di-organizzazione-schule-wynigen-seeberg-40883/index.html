Tasks

They accompany and support children with special educational and developmental needs. You are currently working primarily in a 1./2. Class as well as with a child with bVSA int in the 3rd class, there are also many individual design possibilities.

We offer

We want to use your expertise in our school in a targeted and profitable way. There are various possibilities to set and further develop your own professional priorities, depending on your personal interests:

  • You have your own ideas for the development and implementation of further approaches with which you can support and strengthen children and teachers in everyday school life. Welcome!
