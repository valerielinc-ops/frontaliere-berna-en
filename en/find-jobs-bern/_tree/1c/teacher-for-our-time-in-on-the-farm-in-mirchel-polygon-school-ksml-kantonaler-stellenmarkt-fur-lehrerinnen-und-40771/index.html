Full description

Portrait The Polygon School is a special primary school in the heart of the city of Bern. The school currently has 10 classes and offers space for up to 53 pupils from cycle 1 to cycle 3 at its locations in the city of Bern (Moserstrasse, Wasserwerkgasse, Badgasse) and in Mirchel (farm class).

In each class, pupils with special educational needs are taught and accompanied by teachers and social workers.

Responsibilities

Start of work: 1. February 2027 | Limited until 31. July 2027 | Workload: 12 to 16 lessons | Assignments on three to four mornings from Monday to Thursday, by arrangement

Sometimes learning needs a different place. Our Time-in on the Farm is a temporary place of learning for up to four children who currently cannot be taught in their core class in a special elementary school setting. The focus is on individual school support and personal stabilization. You will work together with another teacher and a social worker.

These are your tasks

  • You will teach and accompany the students individually according to their learning and support goals.
  • You create a safe learning environment through sustainable relationships, clear structures and rituals.
  • You strengthen the students in their self-efficacy, self-regulation and social skills.
  • You will work closely with the local team.

Requirements

What you bring to the table

  • You have a recognised teaching diploma; an additional curative education qualification or corresponding experience is an advantage.
  • You enjoy working with children and young people with special academic, social and emotional needs.
  • You meet challenging behavior with calmness, clarity and an appreciative attitude.
  • You work independently, flexibly and reliably and appreciate the exchange in a team.

We offer

This is what we offer you

  • A versatile and meaningful task in an extraordinary place of learning.
  • A small learning group with room for individual support and intensive relationship work.
  • Design possibilities for real-life, action-oriented learning.
  • Cooperation in a multi-professional team and support from the responsible managers.
  • Assignments on three to four mornings from Monday to Thursday (by arrangement).
  • A fixed-term employment from 1. February to the end of July 2027. The time limit is based on a planned adjustment of the offer.
