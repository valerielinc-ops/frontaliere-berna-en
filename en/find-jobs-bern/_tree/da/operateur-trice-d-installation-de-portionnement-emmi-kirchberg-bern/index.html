Full description

Become part of our cheese crew - Pack enjoyment with passion! For us, everything revolves around fine cheese specialties - and people who with their heart and hand ensure that they arrive perfectly with our customers worldwide.

You can do that.

  • You take responsibility for monitoring and operating the production facilities assigned to you for the production of our cheese portions.
  • You guide and supervise the line employees assigned to you during your shift.
  • You have your plant completely under control and produce all orders carefully & flawlessly according to the specification of the production planning.
  • You are responsible for the proper and clean execution of the production reports as well as the constant compliance with all specified safety and hygiene measures.
  • You are interested in various improvement processes and are actively involved.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have completed vocational training as a plant manager: in or in the food sector (cheeser:in, baker:in, etc.).
  • You have a strong technical flair for machines and goods management systems (SAP).
  • You have a high understanding of hygiene and safety measures.
  • You are flexible in terms of working time (up to 3 shifts) and are mobile.
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German (at least B2).

This is what we offer you

  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
