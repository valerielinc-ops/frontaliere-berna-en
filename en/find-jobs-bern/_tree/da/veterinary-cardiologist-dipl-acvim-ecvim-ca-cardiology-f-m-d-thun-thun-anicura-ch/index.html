Jetzt bewerben Wir antworten gewöhnlich innerhalb von zwei Wochen AniCura Veterinary Clinic Thun is a center for small animal medicine and is one of the largest clinics in Switzerland. In addition to general veterinary services, our customers also benefit from specialists in the fields of surgery/orthopedics, internal medicine, dermatology, cardiology, neurology, rehabilitation, etc. A motivated team of over 85 employees is committed to the needs of our customers and their pets every day. We are shaping the future of veterinary care together through collaboration and knowledge sharing in a value-oriented company. AniCura offers modern, high-quality veterinary care for pets at 400 European locations. To strengthen our team in Thun Switzerland , we are looking for a Veterinary Cardiologist (Dipl. ACVIM/ECVIM-CA – Cardiology) (f/m/d) This position is available as a permanent role. Your expertise for the well-being of our animal patients! Are you an enthusiastic, board-certified veterinary cardiologist looking to join a dynamic, well-equipped and highly collaborative team? Join us in the heart of the Swiss Alps at our growing referral and specialty clinic in beautiful Thun , where quality of life meets cutting-edge veterinary medicine. We are seeking a cardiologist to lead and further develop our cardiology service.

Your tasks

  • Diagnosis and treatment of cardiologic cases (inpatients and outpatients)
  • Performing advanced diagnostics (echocardiography, ECG, etc.)
  • Client communication and case management
  • Collaboration with internal medicine, surgery, and emergency departments
  • Mentorship of interns and junior veterinarians
  • Building a good relationship with our referring veterinarians, sharing knowledge, and connecting by phone and email in order to develop the cardiology referral service
  • Advice and support for patient owners and referral veterinarians
  • Participation in the further training of colleagues and the expansion of the neurology department

We offer

  • Modern, technically well-equipped veterinary clinic with its own MRI and CT scanner
  • Individual training and further education measures to promote your professional, organizational and personal skills
  • Modern workplace in beautiful Thun, surrounded by lakes and mountains
  • A friendly and well-organized team
  • International exchange with different practices/contact persons
  • Feedback culture with regular employee appraisals
  • Good public transport connections

We wish

  • Completed studies in veterinary medicine and Diplomate of ACVIM (Cardiology) or ECVIM-CA (Cardiology), or board-eligible
  • Passion for veterinary medicine and empathy in dealing with patients and their owners.
  • Team player, strong communicator and service-oriented
  • Fluent in English or German (willingness to learn German is a plus)

We offer you a challenging job in a lively, progressive company where you will have the opportunity to help shape the future of Tierklinik Thun. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
