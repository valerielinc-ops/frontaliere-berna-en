# Internship Social Media & Content Creation

Il tuo futuro inizia

As an intern, you will become part of our employer branding team and work with us to shape Swisscom’s employer image. You’ll develop creative content, produce videos, images and animations and bring your ideas directly to our social media channels. You take responsibility for your own channel and support us in exciting employer branding projects.

  • Conception and production of creative content (video, image and animation) for our social media channels
  • Independent support and further development of a social media channel, from content planning to publication
  • Development of creative storyboards and visual concepts for campaigns and individual posts
  • Editing and editing videos and images for a convincing visual appearance
  • Introducing new ideas and trends as well as actively shaping employer branding campaigns and other projects

Your skills

  • Ongoing or completed studies (FH/uni) in marketing, communication, multimedia production or a related field
  • High social media affinity, enthusiasm for digital trends and a good sense of target group-oriented content
  • First practical experience in content marketing and social media content creation is beneficial
  • Good knowledge of Adobe Creative Cloud, especially Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop and Illustrator
  • Creativity, a good eye for design and passion for visual storytelling
  • Independent, structured working method and pleasure in implementing your own ideas
  • Open, communicative personality with a sense for the right words
  • Very good German and English
  • French advantage

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
