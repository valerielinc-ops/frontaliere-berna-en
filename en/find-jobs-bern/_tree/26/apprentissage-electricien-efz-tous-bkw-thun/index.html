Full description

Your scope of work

As an EFZ electrician, you create, maintain and repair electrical installations in buildings professionally. You lay cables in accordance with regulations, mount distribution boxes, connect electrical devices and gradually commission them. A reliable team awaits you, with whom you will take on assignments in various locations, such as households, agriculture, commerce or industry:

  • You realize photovoltaic systems and building automation systems according to detailed plans.
  • When handling electricity you act reliably and responsibly and always observe safety regulations.

What you bring

  • Interest in technology
  • Good colour perception
  • Communicative and team player
  • Willingness to travel
  • To perform work precisely, fine motor skills and safe tool handling are required
  • Reliable and meticulous
