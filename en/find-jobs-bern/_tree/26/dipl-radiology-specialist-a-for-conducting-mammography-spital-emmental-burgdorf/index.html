Perspective that inspires: safe, competent, human You are looking for a great employer? Come to the Emmental Hospital. With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock. At the Institute of Diagnostic Radiology and Neuroradiology, we supervise imaging diagnostics at both locations and are also responsible for conventional X-ray examinations of several large-scale practices in the catchment area. We offer a wide range of diagnostic and therapeutic procedures – from X-ray and mammography to ultrasound to CT and MRI. We attach great importance to a friendly atmosphere, short distances and interdisciplinary cooperation – for our patients and for our team. In order to strengthen our team at the Institute of Diagnostic Radiology and Neuroradiology at the Burgdorf and Langnau sites, we are looking for a:

Task area

  • You take over the leadership of the mammography team
  • You carry out radiological examinations independently and in close cooperation with the medical team (e.g. conventional X-ray, DL, CT, MRI experience is beneficial)
  • You look after patients professionally and empathetically – from preparation to examination to follow-up care
  • You consistently implement the principles of radiation protection and ensure high image and process quality
  • You work in an interprofessional environment with Radiolog:inside, nursing, medicine and referrer:inside the region
  • Depending on your experience and interest, you can participate in specialized offers, such as interventional CT examinations or cardiac MR.

Profile

  • Diploma as radiology specialist HF or equivalent education recognised in Switzerland (applications from abroad: PreCheck is a must)
  • Delight in modern imaging and interest in a wide range of examinations in the acute hospital
  • High customer orientation and communication strength in dealing with patients of all age groups
  • Responsible work in hospital operations and willingness to perform assignments according to service planning

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
