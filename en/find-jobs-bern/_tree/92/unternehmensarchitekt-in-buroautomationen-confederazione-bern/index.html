The Federal Chancellery – The Federal Council Staff Office

As a staff office of the Federal Council, the Federal Chancellery supports the government in the preparation, implementation and follow-up of the Federal Council meetings. It leads and coordinates the communication of the Federal Council and develops political planning for the government. It organises events of the Federal Council in corpore and supports the Federal Bureau. The Federal Chancellery also organises the votes and elections at federal level. It is the focal point for initiative or referendum committees and examines the formation of referendum requests. In addition, the Federal Chancellery supports and coordinates the digital transformation of the Federal Administration.
