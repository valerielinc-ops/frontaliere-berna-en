Full description

Key responsibilities Do you always put clients at the center of everything you do? Are you passionate about creating a welcoming and positive atmosphere? As additional support for our team in Bern, we are looking for a new colleague (female or male) to join us as an Event Attendant on an hourly, on-call basis (on average, approximately 65 hours per year). In this role, you will be responsible for welcoming guests and providing excellent client service at a variety of events, including client gatherings, press conferences, and third-party events involving UBS. You will support the event manager and play a key role in ensuring each event runs smoothly and safely.

The team The Event Attendants are a motivated and dynamic team working across different regions in Switzerland. Together, we actively support each other at exciting regional events, creating memorable experiences for our clients. We are part of the Group Functions business unit, which provides a wide range of logistical services.

Your skills and experience

  • High degree of flexibility and mobility
  • Communicative and open personality
  • Well-groomed appearance, impeccable manners, and absolute discretion
  • Proactive approach with strong service orientation and high quality awareness
  • Committed, a team player, and resilient
  • Excellent hosting skills and organizational talent
  • Confident handling of digital devices (e.g., smartphone, tablet, PC)
  • Very good command of French and English
  • German and any additional language is an advantage

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
