Move the Switzerland.

The Federal Office for Transport (BAV) ensures that public transport in Switzerland is safe, efficient, economical and as environmentally friendly as possible. BAV is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

As an employee of the BAV, you move Switzerland. You take responsibility in long-term and challenging projects. The BAV offers you the opportunity to make a significant contribution to the successful development of Switzerland in a modern working environment.
