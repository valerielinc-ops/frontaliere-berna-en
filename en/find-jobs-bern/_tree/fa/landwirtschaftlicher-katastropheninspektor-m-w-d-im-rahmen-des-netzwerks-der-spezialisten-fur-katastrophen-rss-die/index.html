Full description

What awaits

As part of a future retirement, the West Snisters Team prepares the succession by ensuring a structured transmission of know-how. Within the Network of Disaster Specialists (RSS), you benefit from a targeted development to become an agricultural accident inspector. Fully integrated into the West's sinister team from day one, you are gradually building your expertise through community support and practical field training.

What you can do with us

In this function, you are autonomously responsible for managing and settling complex matters concerning our agricultural clientele, mainly in French-speaking Switzerland as well as in the bilingual cantons of Fribourg, Bern and Valais.

You analyse insurance coverage and liability issues, prepare damage calculations and conduct investigations and demanding negotiations with insured persons, their legal representatives and mandated experts.

You also provide specialized technical support to the disaster services of our general agencies and participate in projects, concept development, training and development activities.

What you bring

  • Federal master's degree in agriculture at the ES/HES level or equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience in agriculture or in an activity closely related to agriculture
  • Proactive, communicative, autonomous and persuasive personality, with a sense of negotiation, a strong entrepreneurial spirit and strong relational skills
  • Solutions and customer-oriented approach, coupled with a professional and assured attitude
  • Willingness to complete further training in insurance
  • French mother tongue and very good knowledge of German

Develop your expertise within the Network of Specialists on Disasters (RSS): This function is part of our Network of Disaster Specialists (RSS). Through this development programme, we are specifically preparing specialists for key and highly specialized disaster-related functions. We thus preserve valuable knowledge, create real prospects for professional development and prepare the talents for the expertise functions of tomorrow.

From your first day, you are fully integrated into the Western Disaster Team and actively participate in operational activities. Accompanied by experienced specialists, you gradually develop your skills through a practical and practical approach while taking on more responsibility over time. Your evolution is based on an individual development plan with clear goals, defined jointly with you, your leader in the Western Disaster Team and the leadership of the RSS.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
