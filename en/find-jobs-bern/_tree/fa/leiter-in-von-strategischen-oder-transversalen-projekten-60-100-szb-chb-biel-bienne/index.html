Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

For our main location in the hospital center Biel in the Beaumontquartier as well as for our external locations near the station Biel, we are looking for an experienced project manager in the hospital area for the planning and implementation of our numerous projects.

Head of strategic or transversal projects, 60 - 100%

They do.

  • Management and implementation of complex, multidisciplinary projects at our two locations (Beaumont, Bahnhof Biel), from feasibility study to commissioning/recommissioning.
  • Analysis of the requirements, the solutions considered and the associated business cases.
  • Establish a suitable project organisation and lead the project team with internal and external persons.
  • Managing project controlling with regard to quality, deadlines, costs, resources and risks as well as project reporting to the hospital management and the Board of Directors.
  • Ensuring an optimal process through open communication with the parties involved and our departments.
  • Negotiations with our external partners and suppliers.
  • Collaboration in the development of the Project Management Office.

That sets them apart.

  • Completed university studies in the field of project management.
  • Several years of professional experience and expertise in project management.
  • Knowledge and professional experience in a hospital or in health care.
  • Analytical and systemic thinking as well as goal-, solution- and customer-oriented.
  • Self-employment, high social skills, communication skills, decision-making and assertiveness as well as stress resistance.
  • Feels comfortable in both concepts and implementation.
  • Excellent communication skills at all hierarchical levels.
  • Mastery of standard IT tools and common software (MS Office, architecture).
  • Fluent knowledge of German or French with good knowledge of the other language or willingness to deepen it.

They benefit from it.

  • A meaningful activity to the full satisfaction of our patients.
  • A collaboration with bilingual colleagues from many different professions in a dynamic environment.
  • Diverse professional perspectives with the opportunity to complete individual post and further education.
  • Safe and modern working conditions (home office, crèche, employee benefits, staff restaurant with reduced prices, etc.).
