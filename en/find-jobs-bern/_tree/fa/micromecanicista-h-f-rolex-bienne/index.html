Full description

Introduction

For our Mechanical sector, we are looking for a (M/F) Micromechanic whose main mission will be to mount tools and small equipment, as well as to manufacture complex mechanical parts in small series on conventional machines.

Responsibilities

  • In charge of mounting, adjusting and developing production tools used by the entire Manufacture
  • Work closely with the technical office, production and project managers to ensure the timeliness and quality of the equipment delivered
  • Actively participate in validation and certification processes

Profile

  • Micromechanic CFC or equivalent
  • Being comfortable in manufacturing small parts in conventional machining
  • Mastering the reading of technical drawings and experience in the field of assembly of mechanical precision assemblies
  • Demonstrate a great rigor in finishes (polishing, grazing, adjusting, cleanliness,...)
  • Conscious, applied and with a quality-based vision to achieve in every detail
  • Being integrated into a small team and actively participating in group cohesion
  • Demonstrate daily dynamism and flexibility

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
