Full description

The UniBE is versatile. With us, you can expect a wide variety of topics and a lot of design freedom.

Their main tasks

  • Management of the Mechanical Workshop
  • Leadership of workshop staff (2-3 employees), main responsible for training polymechanics (1-2 learners)
  • Planning, design and production of special equipment using CAD
  • Project responsibility, work planning and timely production
  • Support of the working groups and the surrounding university institutes for mechanical problems
  • Evaluation of new machines and devices, procurement of tools and equipment, preparation of offers and invoices, purchase of project-specific materials and management of the company’s own material warehouse.

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training and several years of professional experience as a mechanic, polymechanic or toolmaker
  • Training HFP or HF
  • Professional trainers
  • You work solution-oriented and appreciate challenges
  • Advanced knowledge of materials (plastics, precious metals, special materials)
  • Knowledge of CNC processing prerequisite
  • Knowledge of electronics, vacuum technology and welding (TIG) is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
