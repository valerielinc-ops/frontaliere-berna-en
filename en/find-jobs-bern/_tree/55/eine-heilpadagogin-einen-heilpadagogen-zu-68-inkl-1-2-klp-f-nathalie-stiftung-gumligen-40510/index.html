Portrait Do you value a meaningful task in a professional environment? Are you looking for a challenge and want to deal intensively with pedagogical questions in the field of ASS?

The Nathalie Foundation’s curative day school supports and cares for children and adolescents with complex developmental disorders (multiple disabilities, perceptual disorders and autism spectrum disorders).

For a holiday representation in a class cycle 1, we are looking for 15. February 2027 to 14. May 2027

Tasks

  • Together with your job partner, you are responsible for the individual promotion of four students in a class in Cycle 1
  • You plan your lessons independently and document them according to internal requirements
  • You are in close contact with the other class teacher
  • They work together constructively with the interdisciplinary team, the boarding school and the parents
  • They guide trainees

Requirements

  • Completed training in clinical or school curative education or teacher with experience working with children with multiple disabilities
  • An independent and flexible personality with humor, empathy and team spirit
  • Experience working with children with complex disabilities/ASS beneficial
  • Knowledge of the Affolter model, TEACCH and UK are desirable

We offer

  • A versatile and demanding working day with possibilities for 1:1 promotion and many design possibilities
  • School hours and days are given: Tuesday 9.00 – 16.00, Thursday 9.00 – 16.00 and Friday 9.00 – 11.00
  • Collaboration in a committed and interdisciplinary team with supportive leadership and ownership
  • An open and honest, professional and personal exchange
  • A place where employees see themselves as independent, flexible personalities who show humor, have empathy and want to work in a team
  • An employment according to cantonal guidelines
