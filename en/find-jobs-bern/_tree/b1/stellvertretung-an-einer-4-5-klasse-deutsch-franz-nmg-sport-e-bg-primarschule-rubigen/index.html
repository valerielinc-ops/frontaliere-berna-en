Portrait The Rubigen Primary School is a school in the agglomeration of Bern with modern infrastructure and very good public transport connection. The kindergartens, the basic level classes and the primary classes from 1 to 6. School year are located on a spacious school area in the immediate vicinity of nature. Tasks
