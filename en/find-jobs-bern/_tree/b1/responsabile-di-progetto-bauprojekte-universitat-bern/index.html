We are

The University of Bern trains more than 19,400 students and employs more than 8,000 employees in around 150 institutes and the central department. For these activities, it uses an extremely diverse spatial infrastructure spread over around 130 properties. The Department of Construction and Space designs and develops the spatial infrastructure of the University of Bern and together with the Office for Land and Buildings AGG ensures their needs-based planning, project planning and management. At the turn of the year 2027, the area of maintenance management will be integrated into the department of construction and space. In this way, we bundle our expertise in the areas of strategy, construction and management. They can do that.
