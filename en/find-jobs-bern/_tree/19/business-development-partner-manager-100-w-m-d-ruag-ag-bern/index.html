Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

Are you a strategic networker with a sense of new business opportunities and enjoyment of building long-term partnerships? Do you move safely in the environment of authorities, security organizations and technology partners and understand how to bring different interests together? Then you can expect a versatile key position at RUAG with great creative leeway and direct influence on business development.

  • You build strategic technology and product partnerships and develop them continuously.
  • You will maintain relationships with key people from the VBS, the Swiss Security Association (SVS), the critical infrastructures (KRITIS) and relevant federal and cantonal authorities.
  • You will recognize needs and new business opportunities, develop existing business fields and open up new markets.
  • You design the product and service portfolio in cooperation with Sales, Product Management and the technical and operational units.
  • You will lead challenging sales and business development processes, negotiate contracts and build long-term framework agreements.
  • You will contribute to the further development of the corporate strategy and take over the function of Capture Manager for selected projects in cooperation with the Bid Team.
  • You coordinate and support cross-cutting contract acquisition activities, including offer processes and customer and partner meetings.
  • You represent RUAG vis-à-vis authorities, political organisations and strategic partners as well as at trade events, trade fairs, in industrial networks and relevant federal and cantonal commissions.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training at a technical college or university, ideally with a technical background and a business education (e.g. CAS, MAS or MBA).
  • You will preferably bring in-depth industry knowledge and/or military cadre or police training.
  • You convince through trustworthiness, discretion and integrity as well as a high degree of independence and initiative.
  • You are a reliable, structured and positioned personality with a strong understanding of complex relationships and entrepreneurial issues.
  • You have strong consulting and moderation skills as well as the necessary negotiating skills and persuasive power in dealing with different stakeholder groups.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of English. Knowledge of French is an advantage.

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

Your advantages

Your well-being is important to us. That is why you have many advantages with us. These include, for example:

  • Wage and ancillary benefits: We offer you a performance-oriented and market-oriented salary, 13 monthly wages and generous premiums and allowances. Together with other ancillary services, this results in an attractive overall package.
  • Flexible working: We attach great importance to our employees finding a healthy balance between work and private life. That is why we offer flexible working hours and home office options, among other things.
  • Perks: At RUAG you benefit from perks in the areas of mobility and food. In addition, you benefit from attractive offers on our benefit portal and have fleet discounts at various renowned car brands.
  • Career and further education opportunities: Promoting and developing the potential of our employees is important to us. Through training, courses and further education, we support them in continuously developing their skills in order to achieve their professional goals.
  • Leisure activities: Our employees are close to our hearts. That is why we give them a day off for their birthday, offer a diverse sporting offer and the opportunity to receive additional days off against pay deduction in addition to the annual holiday credit.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
