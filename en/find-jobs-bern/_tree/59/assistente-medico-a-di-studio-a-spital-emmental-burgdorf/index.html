Full description

In the middle of the beautiful Emmental – Your new challenge is waiting for you! With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Our Institute of Radiology and Neuroradiology supervises imaging diagnostics at both hospital locations and is responsible for the diagnosis of conventional X-ray examinations of several large-scale practices in the catchment area. Thanks to the subspecialization of radiology and an independent neuroradiology department, we guarantee the highest diagnostic quality. At both locations, we offer the entire spectrum of diagnostic and therapeutic radiological procedures – from conventional X-rays to CT and MRI to interventional procedures. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
