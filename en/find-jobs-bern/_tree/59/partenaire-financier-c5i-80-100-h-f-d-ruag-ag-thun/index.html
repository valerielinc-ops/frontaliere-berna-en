Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Strategic consulting of management in the area of High Secure IT / Cyber on financial, operational and commercial issues
  • Build and maintain a strong business partner relationship with project managers, engineering, cyber teams and senior management
  • Deputy Director of Finance Business Partnering incl. Leadership of selected finance topics and representation in management committees
  • Ensuring a financially robust decision-making basis for investments, programmes and security-critical projects
  • Responsibility for segments in the area controlling defense incl. Forecasting, budgeting and monthly performance reporting
  • Analysis of complex projects and program structures (including cyber programs, high-secure IT infrastructures, defense systems)
  • Creation of economic calculations, scenarios, risk analysis and KPI dashboards
  • Support for the further development of finance processes, standards and tools (Controlling to Accounting interface)
  • Participation in internal and external audits, ICS projects, month, quarter and financial statements as well as M&A activities
  • Ensuring compliance with internal policies, defense regulatory and audit requirements
  • Co-shaping the digital transformation in the finance sector, participating in the introduction of BI tools and identifying new technologies to increase efficiency and improve quality

You bring that with you

  • Higher business education (FH/uni) and/or a subject-specific further education with a focus on finance/controlling
  • Several years of professional experience (min. 5 years) in controlling, ideally in business partnering or area controlling
  • In-depth knowledge of finance and accounting
  • Knowledge of data analytics or automated forecasting methods as well as AI tools
  • Experience with POC evaluation methods (Percentage of Completion) or controlling in project business in technically demanding environments
  • Experience in the technology or defense environment is advantageous, but not absolutely necessary
  • Very good analytical skills, high numerical affinity and strong process understanding
  • Experience with SAP S/4 Hana (modules CO, FI, PS) is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

Over the area

The Business Area C5I of RUAG MRO Holding AG supports the Swiss Army in the realization of ICT projects with a specific focus on the Cyber Command. This is your chance to contribute to highly secure and sovereign IT systems. Over the past eight years, we have grown from a small “start-up” from zero to more than 260 employees and offer a unique, dynamic environment. Here you can work at the forefront of the digitization of the Swiss Army. Do you want to contribute to the security environment? Then you are exactly right with us. With the C5I campus, we have created an innovation space for highly secure IT systems and offer the most attractive workplaces.
