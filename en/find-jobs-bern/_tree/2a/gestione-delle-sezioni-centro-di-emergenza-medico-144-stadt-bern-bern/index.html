Full description

You are a committed personality, think ahead and keep track even in demanding situations? Then take responsibility for an important interface in the rescue sector. As section manager, you and your team will ensure that help arrives quickly, professionally and reliably where it is needed.

Tasks

  • You lead the employees of the medical emergency centre 144, promote their development and create a motivating environment in which personal responsibility and team spirit are lived.
  • You are responsible for the smooth, professional and operational operation of the medical emergency call center 144 and drive the technical, organisational and technical development of the emergency call center and the operational control systems.
  • You optimize processes and processes around emergency call processing, alarming and scheduling and ensure compliance with quality standards and IVR requirements
  • With innovative ideas and an eye for new developments, you advance the professional, organizational and technical development of the emergency call centre
  • You will work in partnership with the fire department 118, the regional emergency services in the Canton of Bern, the Health, Social and Integration Directorate (GSI) and other partner organisations and promote national networking.
  • You control projects, represent the interests of the medical emergency call center 144 internally and externally and actively participate in the further development of the area of planning and deployment.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a dipl. Rescue EMTin HF as well as further training in leadership, management and / or business administration, training as a deployment dispatcher with federal law. Professional ID is an advantage
  • Several years of professional experience in a medical emergency call center as well as proven experience in the management of employees or the assumption of technical and managerial responsibility
  • In-depth knowledge of the rescue system and the procedures of a medical emergency call centre as well as a well-established network in the health and rescue sector
  • Completed leadership training in the rescue service, ideally as an operational manager (EL San) or readiness to complete it
  • You convince through leadership, initiative, decision-making and the ability to act solution-oriented even in challenging situations
  • Entrepreneurial and networked thinking, strong communication skills as well as organizational and negotiation skills distinguish you
