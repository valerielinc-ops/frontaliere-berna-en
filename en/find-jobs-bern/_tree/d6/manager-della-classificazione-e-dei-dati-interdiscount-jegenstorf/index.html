At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • You will continuously develop our classification scheme for product data and thus ensure a consistent, clear and high-quality database – always with a view to a convincing product experience for our customers:
  • You control data transformations and automation processes and implement efficient solutions for data maintenance and integration – with an entrepreneurial understanding of quality, cost and impact
  • You optimize internal data structures in close cooperation with the relevant specialist areas, so that data can be provided in a comprehensible, usable and tailored to the needs of customers.
  • You accompany the connection of new external data sources and ensure their smooth integration into our systems in order to continuously expand the information content and added value of our product data.
  • You ensure data quality, recognize and process problem cases proactively and adhere to the agreed service levels in master data maintenance – solution-oriented and responsible

Requirements

  • You have completed commercial or technical training – ideally supplemented by further training in the areas of data management, computer science or process management
  • Alternatively, bring a comparable profile or relevant study
  • You have strong analytical skills and an in-depth understanding of data quality, data management and process optimization
  • You are curious, willing to learn and can quickly familiarize yourself with new product and product worlds – you are enthusiastic about the characteristics and special features of our products
  • You work in a structured, self-responsible manner and appreciate close cooperation across different disciplines
  • You have a high IT affinity, very good user knowledge in MS Office and ideally experience in dealing with ERP systems such as SAP
  • You communicate in German and English in a style-confident, precise and addressable manner – in word and writing

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
