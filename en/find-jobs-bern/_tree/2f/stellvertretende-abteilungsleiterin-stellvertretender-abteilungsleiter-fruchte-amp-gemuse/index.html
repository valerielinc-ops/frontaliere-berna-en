Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You monitor actions, labeling and sales places according to the concept
  • You recognize customer wishes and forward them to the department management
  • You support your manager in day-to-day business and train employees and learners “on the job”

Requirements

  • You work reliably, efficiently and have a well-kept appearance
  • You are fit to deal with MS Office
  • You approach people openly and enjoy customer contact
  • You are resilient and stay friendly even in hectic moments
  • You speak fluent German and French and enjoy working in a team
