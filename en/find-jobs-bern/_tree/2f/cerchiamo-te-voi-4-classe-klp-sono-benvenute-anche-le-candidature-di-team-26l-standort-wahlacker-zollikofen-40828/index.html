Full description

Portrait Primary level Zollikofen / Wahlacker location

In Zollikofen there are 5 schoolhouses with kindergartens at 3 locations, 1 day school, 12 kindergarten classes, 33 primary school classes and 1 IKD (Intensive German course).

Our mission statement

At our school, every child can realize their potential. Our attitude towards children and adults is benevolent. The active cooperation of all participants lays the basis for the school development of the child. We create flexible learning environments and focus on children’s resources. Through reflection, we ensure quality, sustainability and stay on the pulse of society.

Tasks

As a class teacher for the 2nd semester at level 4, you teach all subjects except TTG (26 lessons). They maintain an active cooperation with the class team, the college and the school management. They are looking for an exchange with the special teachers (IF, DaZ, speech therapy, psychomotorik). You are committed to an open and trusting involvement of the parents of your students: inside.

> The job starts on 8.2.2027 during the sports holidays - lessons start on 15.2.2027

Requirements

  • You have a teaching diploma at primary school level or are in the last year of training.
  • They like to work with students from different cultures.
  • They are empathetic in dealing with the learners and have a good and clear classroom management.
  • You are interested in participatory cooperation with your colleagues and the school management.
  • You are motivated to be openly involved in the design of your work environment.
  • They are team- and solution-oriented and have humor.

We offer

  • Temporary employment until the end of July 2027 with an option to permanent employment from August 2027.
  • A lively and diverse school.
  • A committed, supportive and motivated college.
  • Competent support through school social work.
  • Good experience with the public transport.
  • Free use of PubliBike :-
