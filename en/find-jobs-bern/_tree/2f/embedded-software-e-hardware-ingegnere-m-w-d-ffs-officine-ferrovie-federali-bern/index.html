Embedded Software and Hardware Engineer (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Embedded Software and Hardware Engineer (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.02.2027, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You work in a team that strengthens the safety, availability and reliability of fleets and thus makes a significant contribution to reliable travel. In the focus of Automatic Passenger Counting AFZ, you will help design a future-oriented overall system that records passenger flows and provides data for planning and operation. Together with a collaborative team, you advance system integration on the vehicles, accompany supplier management and ensure stable operation. Work Smart enables you to work regardless of location and a good balance between work and private life. Further development in the field and insight into modern vehicles and IT architectures are part of everyday life. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You take responsibility for the Automatic Passenger Counting system and ensure quality, stability and availability across interfaces.
  • You help shape new technologies on the vehicles and ensure robust operation of the passenger counting systems.
  • Across the entire lifecycle, you will manage the required infrastructure from evaluation to operation and orderly decommissioning of individual components.
  • Automation for hardware, software and firmware is implemented on the vehicle devices and increases efficiency and reproducibility.
  • You will provide competent advice to internal bodies and suppliers and ensure a transparent exchange with the team and relevant stakeholders.
  • With a systematic approach, you take on conceptual tasks and drive improvements continuously.

You bring that with you.

  • You work independently, act in a solution-oriented manner and take on responsibility with a high level of initiative.
  • As a team, you communicate clearly in the technical environment and enjoy working in an interdisciplinary manner.
  • Experience with embedded interfaces and protocols such as ITxPT, MQTT, Ethernet and TRDP.
  • With Linux, you’ll be confident and perform system management and analysis with Bash and Wireshark.
  • You have a university degree in computer science or electrical engineering or you have comparable professional experience of several years.
  • We require knowledge of German at level C1 and knowledge of English at level B2. Knowledge of French or Italian is an advantage.

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This job is in requirement level K, regional allowance level 1.
