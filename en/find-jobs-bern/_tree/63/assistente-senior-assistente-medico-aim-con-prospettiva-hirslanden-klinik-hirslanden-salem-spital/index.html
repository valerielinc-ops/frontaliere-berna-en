Full description

Work location: Hirslanden Bern - Salem Hospital, Beau-Site Clinic, Permanence Clinic | Bern

Casting: by arrangement

Type of employment: temporary

Reference number: 73080

The Department of General Internal Medicine (DAIM) is a clinical center of excellence with three locations in Bern (Beau-Site Clinic, Salem Hospital, Permanence Clinic). It covers the entire spectrum of general internal medicine and interdisciplinary clinical emergency medicine.

To strengthen our team, we are seeking an experienced resident physician with advanced training and an interest in taking on expanded professional responsibility. With suitable qualifications, we offer an attractive career path to deputy attending physician.

YOUR ADVANTAGES WITH US

  • Targeted professional development and gradual preparation for the role of deputy senior physician.
  • Gradual assumption of professional responsibility and supervisory tasks
  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • A collegial, dynamic team
  • Fair shift planning and time tracking – we operate on a 46-hour week.
  • Paid changing time
  • Attractive benefits: discount at the Salem pharmacy, contribution to the gym membership, admission to the indoor swimming pool, and contribution to mobility costs.

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can enjoy: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional support and guidance for junior resident physicians in their daily clinical practice
  • Interdisciplinary treatment of patients from various medical specialties in the emergency departments
  • Care of inpatients in the field of general internal medicine – across all three Hirslanden clinics in Bern
  • Treatment of complex and cross-systemic diseases
  • Care of surgical patients with relevant internal medicine comorbidities
  • Close collaboration with the treating specialist team to ensure comprehensive and competent treatment.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed medical studies with corresponding license to practice medicine or MEBEKO recognition
  • You are at least in 3. or 4. Year of training in General Internal Medicine or related discipline
  • Clinical experience in inpatient care and/or emergency medicine
  • Interest in a longer-term development within DAIM with the prospect of a position as deputy senior physician.
  • High social skills, teamwork skills and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Enjoyment in providing professional support and guidance to younger colleagues
  • Excellent German skills, both spoken and written.

APPLICATION PROCESS

  • Online application with complete application dossier (CV, cover letter, diplomas, previous work references, MEBEKO recognition for foreign degrees)
  • Review of your documents and feedback
  • Invitation to a job interview
  • Final decision

For further information, the DAIM team is happy to assist you at T. We look forward to hearing from you.

Please note that for data protection reasons, we cannot consider either paper applications (which will not be returned) or applications submitted via email. Therefore, please apply exclusively online using this tool.
