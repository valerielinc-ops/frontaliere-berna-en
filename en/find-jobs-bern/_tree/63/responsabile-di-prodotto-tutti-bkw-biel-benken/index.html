Full description

We make habitats worth living.

You will continue to develop our market offering and advance technical products and services from idea to market launch. As part of succession planning, you will be able to develop further into Head of Product Management within about three years.

Your tasks

  • You implement the product strategy and further develop our product lines.
  • You control and optimize the product portfolio over the entire life cycle.
  • You develop requirements and business cases and thus create well-founded decision-making bases for product development.
  • You develop pricing strategies and calculation guidelines and analyze margins, markets and competition.
  • You lead development projects and accompany new products from the idea to the successful market launch.
  • You impart product knowledge in presentations, documentation and training courses and represent the company at trade fairs and events.

Your competence

  • You have completed technical or commercial training as well as further training in product management, for example a CAS.
  • You bring at least five years of professional experience in product management.
  • You have a good understanding of mechanics and electrical engineering as well as a high affinity for digital applications and smart products.
  • You work safely with MS Office, PDM and ERP systems.
  • You act autonomously and solution-oriented, communicate openly and enjoy working in teams and projects.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German and good to very good knowledge of French.

Hinni AG is a successful service and production company. Since its foundation, we have been dealing with hydrants and the water pipe network. Development, production, sales as well as maintenance and auditing of fire hydrants are our business areas – 115 employees are our “capital”.
