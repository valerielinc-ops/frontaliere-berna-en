About us

The hospital fmi AG with over 1,600 employees includes the two acute hospitals Interlaken and Frutigen, the psychiatry of hospital fmi AG, the health center in Meiringen, the senior centers in Frutigen and Interlaken, the emergency service with 5 locations, the walk-in clinic in Interlaken and various practices.
