Solar assembly 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a temporary or permanent employment More than 3 years of experience as a solar installer

Your challenge

  • Installation of photovoltaic systems on steep and flat roofs.
  • Pre-assembly and installation of substructures, modules and cable systems.
  • Assistance in electrical preparatory work (in cooperation with electrical specialists).
  • on-site quality controls and safe and clean work execution.
  • Documentation of work and handover to the project team.
  • Maintenance and small service work on existing plants.

This is what you bring with you

  • Experience in crafts or construction (e.g. roofer, carpenter, metalworker, fitter – or lateral entrants with technical skill).
  • Safe working on roofs and height.
  • Physical fitness and clean, reliable work.
  • Category B driving licence.
  • Knowledge of German.
  • Experience in PV assembly is an advantage
  • Operation of in-roof or special systems is advantageous

What we offer you

  • Secure permanent employment in a growing company.
  • Modern workplace and high-quality assembly tools.
  • Real team culture: We support each other, work clean and reliable.
  • Short decision paths and direct communication.
  • Fair compensation
