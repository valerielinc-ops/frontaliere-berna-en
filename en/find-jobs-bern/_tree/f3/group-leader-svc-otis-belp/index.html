Huhnerhubelstrasse 64, Postfach 123, 3123 Belp Basic function For the service group Berner Oberland we need your support! You are responsible for the operation of the elevator systems in the assigned areas of operation in the Bern area. You work closely with your service technicians in the field, who in turn are responsible for the periodic maintenance work as well as the rapid are responsible for troubleshooting. As a representative of OTIS, you also serve our customers as part of our service excellence policy.

Main tasks

  • Training of new employees and monitoring compliance with maintenance and safety policies
  • Transport of materials from the warehouse to work areas
  • Support of the service supervisor in organizing tours, planning and team coordination
  • Advising and supporting service technicians in complex maintenance work and troubleshooting
  • Persons exempted from lifts
  • Identification of security risks and participation in the implementation of improvement measures
  • Ensure compliance with absolute corporate principles (safety, quality, ethics) and all applicable policies, standards, processes and instructions

Qualifications/Experience/Knowledge

  • You have electromechanical knowledge and may be familiar with old and new technology and like smartphone apps
  • You lead the assigned employees technically and do coaching on the job
  • You coordinate the tasks in the team in case of bottlenecks
  • You plan your tasks independently, know the principle "learning by doing" and approach your own questions proactively
  • You like to approach people, show a friendly attitude towards your environment and build your "network"
  • You like to carry personal responsibility and are not too bad to get your hands dirty
  • You have a driving license with category B
  • Excellent knowledge of German

Personal skills

  • Organizational, analytical skills and sense of priorities
  • Customer orientation, service orientation
  • Proactivity, ability to take the initiatives
  • Employees demand and promote, create meaningfulness
  • Reliability, results orientation
  • Adaptability and load capacity

What we offer you

  • It awaits you a task with a lot of freedom and responsibility
  • In addition, you will be equipped with a mobile phone and your own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • We care about your personal development. In concrete terms, this means that we support further training and internal promotion opportunities are open to everyone.
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Good pension fund
  • Catering allowance
