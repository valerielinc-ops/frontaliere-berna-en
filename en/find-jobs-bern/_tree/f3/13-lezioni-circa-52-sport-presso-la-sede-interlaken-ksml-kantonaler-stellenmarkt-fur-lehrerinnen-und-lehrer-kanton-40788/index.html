Full description

Portrait At Interlaken Gymnasium with its department in Gstaad, more than 450 students and 70 teachers learn and teach. This makes the Interlaken Gymnasium the smallest public school in the canton of Bern. We offer our student body from the extensive and expansive Oberland a wide range of main subjects and supplementary subjects as well as a bilingual graduate degree. Our school is characterised by a personal atmosphere & a four-day week and we maintain diverse international relationships.

The Gymnasium Interlaken occupied on the 1. February 2027 a temporary workload in the field of sport until the end of the school year 2026/27:

Tasks

They teach at our high school at the Interlaken location the compulsory subject of sport at the levels GYM1-GYM4 and the supplementary subject of sport at the level GYM3.

Requirements

  • Completed specialist studies
  • Teaching diploma for Maturity Schools for Sports or shortly before completion of training
  • Willingness to cooperate in the Sports Council as well as in comprehensive school events and projects.
  • Flexibility and readiness for alternative physical education. Our new gyms are under construction and can be used from the beginning of March 2027 at the latest.

We offer

We offer temporary employment in an excellent, collegial working environment at an innovative school in the Bernese Oberland.
