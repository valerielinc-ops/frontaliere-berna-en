# DevOps Engineer Firewall

Il tuo futuro inizia

We accompany the army on its digitalisation journey with the aim of strengthening Switzerland’s defence capability and making targeted use of the opportunities offered by technological progress. The new digitalisation platform forms the basis for an independent, secure and crisis-resistant ICT infrastructure. As DevOps Engineer Firewalling, you are responsible for the operation, further development and protection of security-critical network and application environments. The focus is on modern firewall, WAF and zero-trust solutions as well as highly available security services for the army’s digital platform. This directly contributes to the secure digital transformation and resilience of the Swiss army.

  • Operation, configuration and further development of enterprise firewalls, web application firewalls (WAF) and VPN and remote access solutions
  • Responsibility for the security and availability of critical network and application services through modern protection mechanisms such as Zero Trust, DDoS protection and bot management
  • Analysis and resolution of complex network and security incidents using traffic analysis, logs and packet captures
  • Planning and implementation of highly available security architectures including failover and load balancing concepts
  • Close cooperation with network, security, DevOps and application teams as well as the automation of security processes and modern infrastructure services

Your skills

  • Degree (uni/ETH/FH) in computer science or comparable professional experience
  • Several years of practical experience in the field of firewalling, network security or infrastructure operation with modern firewall and WAF technologies
  • Knowledge of network and security protocols such as TCP/IP, NAT, VPN, IPS/IDS and SSL/TLS
  • Experience in the secure operation of highly available network and security architectures and traffic management (load balancing and reverse proxy technologies)
  • Very good understanding of typical web application risks and corresponding protection measures as well as solution-oriented working methods in the incident and troubleshooting environment
  • Knowledge of automation, infrastructure-as-code or API integration is an advantage
  • Knowledge of cloud and hybrid environments (e.g. Azure, AWS or Kubernetes-based infrastructures)
  • Very good German in word and writing

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
