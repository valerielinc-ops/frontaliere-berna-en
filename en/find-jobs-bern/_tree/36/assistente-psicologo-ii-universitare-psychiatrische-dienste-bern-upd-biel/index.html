Full description

University psychiatry clinic and adult psychotherapy

At the Biel-Seeland Psychiatric Centre, we are committed to providing comprehensive psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care for people in different life situations. In collaboration with relatives, external professionals and other medical disciplines, we develop individualized care and accompany our patients on their way to more stability and a better quality of life. We offer a stimulating and varied activity, with great autonomy, within a university environment. You will be part of an open and committed team composed of professionals from different disciplines, where professional exchanges and collective reflection are important. Depending on your interests and needs, you will have access to internal and external continuing training opportunities. You will also have the opportunity to participate in research projects, develop your professional skills and actively contribute to the evolution of psychiatric care provision in the Bienois region. The contract is initially concluded for a fixed period of two years. An extension is possible, if necessary until the qualification of specialist is obtained.

Your tasks

  • You monitor patients in outpatients, under the supervision of specialist psychologists and specialist doctors
  • You conduct admission consultations, diagnostic assessments and outpatient treatments under supervision. You also maintain exchanges with relatives and professionals involved in care
  • You develop and animate, if necessary, group therapeutic offers
  • You participate in interdisciplinary consultations and bring your professional expertise to collaboration with other disciplines

Your profile

  • You have a university degree in psychology, with a specialization in clinical psychology, and have undergone in-depth training in psychotherapy
  • You appreciate the work done in an interdisciplinary team and in a multicultural environment. You are interested in complex psychological problems
  • You have French or German as your mother tongue and have good oral knowledge of the other language
  • You have experience in the field of ADHD diagnostic assessments

UPZ

The University Psychiatric Centre in Bern (UPZ) is the main centre of competence in psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region as a university clinic. It provides psychiatric care in the canton of Bern throughout the care chain, from early identification to outpatient treatment, day care and stationary treatment, to rehabilitation and forensic psychiatry. With more than 30 sites, UPZ offers a healthcare network covering the entire territory. UPZ also makes an important contribution in the fields of education, training and research. More information
