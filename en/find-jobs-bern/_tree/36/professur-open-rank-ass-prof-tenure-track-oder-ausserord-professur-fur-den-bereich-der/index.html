About us

At the Faculty of Theology of the University of Bern, the Institute of Christian Catholic Theology has to fill the position of assistant professor tenure track or an extraordinary professor for the field of historical research and the history of Old Catholicism on 01.08.2027. In cooperation with the Chair of Systematic Theology and Ecumenism, the advertised professorship is responsible for Christian Catholic research and teaching at the Theological Faculty of Bern. The conversion of the assistant professorship with Tenure Track takes place after fulfillment of the qualification criteria and after appropriate evaluations at the latest after six years.
