System integrator/in KNX

80–100

With around 130 employees, Elektro Burkhalter AG is one of the leading providers of electrical installations, computer science & telematics (ICT), automation, photovoltaics, building technology as well as service and maintenance in the Bern region. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an integrator/in KNX by immediate agreement or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Programming, configuration and commissioning of KNX systems
  • Implementation of service and maintenance orders on existing KNX systems
  • Cooperation in the development of technical concepts
  • Creation of customized solutions
  • Participation in the development of a network of building contractors, architects and planners
  • Preparation and maintenance of technical documentation and reports
  • Training and guidance of plant operators
  • Cooperation with other departments for the development of tailor-made solutions

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training in the electrical industry
  • Continuing education, ideally with a focus on automation, computer science, system or building technology, or a comparable qualification
  • experience in the field of training and with visualization servers or the willingness to learn these skills
  • interest and willingness to further training in specific fields
  • IT-Aness and well-founded MS-Office skills
  • Very good German language skills in word and writing
  • team skills: You like to work in a team and actively contribute to the joint success
  • Innovation: You bring creative

Ideas and interested in new technologies - Openness: You communicate clearly, transparently and are ready to face new challenges - Structured and solution-oriented working methods: You work and find efficient solutions for complex tasks

We offer

  • Exciting, future-proof and digital working environment
  • working days end at 16.15
  • 40 hours per week
  • Modern vehicle for business and private journeys
  • Support and participation in training
  • Reductions in partner companies (fitness, hotels, electronics, insurance, telecoms providers, etc.)
  • Service age gift every 5 years in the form of a premium or additional holiday days
