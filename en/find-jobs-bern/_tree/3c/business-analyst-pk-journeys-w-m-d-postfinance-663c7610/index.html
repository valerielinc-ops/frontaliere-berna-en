Full description

At PostFinance, as a business analyst, you actively shape the future of the Swiss financial world. You optimize the quality of our products and channels in the Customer Experience business unit with a focus on client journeys for private customers: in the areas of investing, provisioning, digital assets and financing. In multifunctional, agile-oriented teams, you accompany the technical requirements analysis and process management. So you shape the banking of tomorrow and strengthen the innovative power of PostFinance sustainably.

You can do that.

  • You work actively in interdisciplinary teams and thus drive the implementation of optimizations and innovation ideas

You bring that with you

  • Graduate degree or higher education in economics and social sciences
  • In-depth knowledge in the banking sector, in particular in the areas of investment and pension funds and several years of experience in business analysis
  • Strong communication skills, negotiating skills and high social skills
  • Strong teamwork and enjoyment of working in multifunctional teams
  • Excellent analytical and conceptual skills and a networked mindset
  • Negotiating knowledge of German and English
