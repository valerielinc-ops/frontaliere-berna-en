The ElCom - for a secure power supply and fair tariffs

The Swiss Federal Electricity Commission (ElCom) is the independent state regulatory authority in the electricity sector. It monitors compliance with the electricity supply and energy law, security of supply in the electricity sector and electricity prices. ElCom is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).
