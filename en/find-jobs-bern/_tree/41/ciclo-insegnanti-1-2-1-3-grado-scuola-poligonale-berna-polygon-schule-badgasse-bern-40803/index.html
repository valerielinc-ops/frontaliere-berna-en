Full description

Portrait The Polygon School is a special elementary school in the heart of the city of Bern. The school currently runs 10 classes and offers at its locations in the city of Bern (Moserstrasse, Wasserwerkgasse, Badgasse) and in Mirchel (farm class) space for up to 53 students from cycle 1 to cycle 3.

In each class, students with special educational needs are taught and accompanied by teachers, social educators or social educators.

Tasks

Job start: 1 February 2027 (or by appointment) | Permanent employment | Pensum: 14–15 lessons | Working days until summer 2027: Monday morning, Tuesday and Wednesday morning

At Polygon School we accompany children with special educational, social and emotional needs. For our class in cycle 1/2 (1st–3rd class) at the Badgasse location, we are looking for a dedicated teacher who enjoys designing the school together with other specialists.

With us, a teacher and a social pedagogue jointly assume responsibility for the class. The class team is supported by a school curative teacher and a school social worker. The focus is on sustainable relationships, a safe place to learn and individual learning and development opportunities.

These are your tasks

  • You plan and design a differentiated lesson that is oriented towards the individual learning and support goals of the children.
  • You create a safe and conducive learning environment with clear structures, rituals and sustainable relationships.
  • You empower the children in their self-efficacy, self-regulation and social skills.
  • Together with the social pedagogue, you take responsibility for the educational leadership and development of the class.
  • You work closely with the school curative teacher, the school social worker and other specialists.

Requirements

This is what you bring with you

  • You have a recognised teaching diploma for the primary level
  • An additional curative education qualification or corresponding experience is beneficial.
  • You enjoy working with children with special school, social and emotional needs.
  • You bring a lot of patience, empathy and resilience, and you also encounter challenging situations with calmness, clarity and an appreciative attitude.
  • You design your lessons creatively, differentiated and orient yourself to the individual development opportunities of the children.
  • You work independently, flexibly and reliably and appreciate the close interdisciplinary cooperation.

We offer

This is what we offer you

  • A versatile and meaningful task in a small class with room for individual promotion and intensive relationship work.
  • Close partnership between teacher and social pedagogy with joint responsibility for the pedagogical leadership of the class.
  • Support by a school curative pedagogue, a school social worker and the responsible leaders.
  • Design room for creative forms of teaching and the further development of our pedagogical offer.
  • A dedicated, multi-professional team that lives cooperation and mutual support.
  • An indefinite job with a set of 14-15 lessons.
  • Until the summer holidays of 2027, the missions will take place on Monday morning, Tuesday and Wednesday morning.
