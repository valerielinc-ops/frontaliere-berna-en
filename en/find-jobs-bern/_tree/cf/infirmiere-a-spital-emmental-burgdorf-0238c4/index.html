Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital offers demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and takes into account their own resources. In our manageable company, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. We regard individuality as enrichment. We offer jobs at the two locations Burgdorf and Langnau. We are a successful training facility of the BZ Pflege.

The focus of our work is the care of caregivers, the promotion of independence and quality of life as well as a professional interdisciplinary cooperation. We accompany older people with psychiatric diseases in acute and complex life situations and create perspectives for everyday life together with them. For our openly run inpatient old-age psychiatry with 17 beds at the Burgdorf location, we are looking for a committed and professionally experienced psychiatrist.

Task area

  • Resource- and person-centered care and support of life-experienced people.
  • Support and maintenance of independence as well as promotion of the everyday skills of our patients
  • Participation in therapeutic group offers
  • Crisis intervention and support in challenging situations
  • Interprofessional treatment planning and care
  • Early detection of somatic state changes and initiation of corresponding nursing measures

Profile

  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Interested in psychiatric care and working with the elderly
  • Experience in psychiatry or old age psychiatry is beneficial
  • Enjoyment in the design of therapeutic relationships in the context of caregivers
  • Responsible and reflective working methods
  • Strong teamwork and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • High social and communication skills
  • Resilient, flexible and solution-oriented personality
