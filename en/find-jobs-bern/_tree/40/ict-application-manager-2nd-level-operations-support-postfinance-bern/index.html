Full description

At PostFinance, you are actively shaping the future of digital banking. With your technical know-how and team spirit, you support the integration of our core banking applications and develop innovative solutions. You ensure that our systems remain stable, modern and customer-oriented. This will help PostFinance to remain a digital innovation leader in the future and inspire with fresh solutions around the money.

You can do that.

  • You locate, limit and fix disruptions in various applications
  • You quickly analyze disruptions thanks to your technical understanding and repair them sustainably
  • You ensure the operation of applications and tools in project teams as well as their transfer to productive operation
  • You advise and support various support levels as well as users with questions about applications and processes
  • Together with internal and external partners, you drive forward the continuous improvement of our systems and processes

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic training as a computer scientist: in EFZ, ideally supplemented by further education at the level of HF, FH or university
  • First professional experience in application support
  • Knowledge in UNIX, SQL and shell scripting as well as experience with Kubernetes as an advantage
  • Independent and resilient personality with willingness for piquetting and occasional assignments outside regular working hours
  • Very good knowledge of German and good oral English
