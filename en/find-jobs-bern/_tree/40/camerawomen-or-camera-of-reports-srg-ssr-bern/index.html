Full description

The RTS is looking for news reports for its Department of Operations, Production Unit, several Camerawomen or Cameramen, including Focus and Local Colors; and Valais region.

Your Mission

You will need to create television images with your camera to reflect the environment and atmosphere of a given production or situation, according to the direction of production and production requirements.

You will carry out successive operations with care and precision to contribute to the realization of a show by guaranteeing the ethical and artistic rules of the shooting and in accordance with the requirements of editing and broadcasting.

You will install your equipment to master the quality and continuity of light and shooting. You will also ensure the first-level maintenance of the equipment entrusted to you and handle it carefully.

Finally, you will also perform this activity as a team of two.

Your profile

  • Experience confirmed as a camerawoman or reporter cameraman.
  • Technical CFCs or equivalent training
  • Confirmed experience of means and methods of production and good computer knowledge.
  • Good general culture.
  • Good stress management and excellent physical condition.
  • Availability to work in irregular hours and for frequent travel.
  • Good knowledge of English, German an asset
  • Driver's licence cat. B imperative.
  • Mounting experience, a plus.

What we offer you

  • A work rich in emotions within a sector that crosses all media of your RTS magazines.
  • Work in line with transmedia trends, at the cutting edge of the latest audiovisual production technologies.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
