Full description

Portrait Signau is located between Konolfingen and Langnau in the beautiful Emmental and is easily accessible by S-Bahn. About two kilometres from Signau is the school house Mutten with two classes. Here we have been leading a basic level class since August 2025. Presumably on August 2028, the children of the primary level will attend classes at the Signau campus. The base stage will also move. In addition to the basic level, we run two kindergartens in the municipality of Signau, six primary classes and nine secondary classes.

Tasks

They teach as co-class teachers at the basic level at the Mutten location and later in Signau to the extent of 80 to 90 percent. Your colleague has taught at various kindergartens for a long time. This is why you focus on the older children of the basic level.

  • They are flexible and can quickly adapt to new situations.
  • Working with parents is important to you.

We offer

  • Established and willing students.
  • A significant part of the lessons take place in team teaching.
  • Lots of support from the co-class teacher.
  • A dedicated school management.
