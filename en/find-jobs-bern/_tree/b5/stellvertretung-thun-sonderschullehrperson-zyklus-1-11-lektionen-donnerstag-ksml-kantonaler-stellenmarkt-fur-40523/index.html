Full description

Portrait Focus on resources is a special elementary school with locations in Thun and Ursellen.

The offer is aimed at children and young people with challenging behavior. This is reflected, among other things, in demanding group dynamics and difficulties in emotion regulation. The aim is to perceive the children and young people in their resources and to promote them in a targeted manner. On this basis, technical and interdisciplinary competences are consolidated and further developed.

Resources focus on individualized teaching. A binding cooperation with parents as well as a close cooperation with specialists, other learning locations and the local community are central components of the concept.

More information about the school and the team can be found at the following link: https://www.prima-familia.ch/besondere-volksschule

Due to an operation, a team member drops out on Thursday, November 19 for 4 lessons in Ursellen/Konolfingen and on November 26 for 7 lessons in Thun. On 19.11. the lessons take place exceptionally in Ursellen, because a choir project is carried out with a different location.
