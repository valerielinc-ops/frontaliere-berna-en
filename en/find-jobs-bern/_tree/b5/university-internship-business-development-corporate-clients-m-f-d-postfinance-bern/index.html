Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

In the Corporate Clients Development team, you will work at the interface of strategy, data & AI, sales and business development. Together, we will further develop the corporate client business and support the implementation of strategic projects in the organisation. You will gain insight into key future topics of corporate client business, such as data-based sales management, customer experience, digitalisation and artificial intelligence, and will play an active role in this. With your contribution, you will help translate strategic goals into concrete measures and effectively advance the Corporate Clients business unit. As a university intern, you will not just be part of it, but will actively contribute, take on responsibility and help drive forward topics that are relevant to the Corporate Clients business unit.

What you can do

  • Based on the corporate customer strategy, you will derive concrete fields of action for the further development of our sales and service model and support their implementation in the organization.
  • You will take on your own work packages in cross-departmental projects, clarify requirements with specialist departments, develop conceptual foundations and advance the topics step by step
  • You will develop the basis for decisions, prepare content in an understandable way and introduce it into suitable exchange and management vessels
  • You will be involved in innovative topics related to data and AI and help to harness new possibilities for a data-based and future-oriented way of working.
  • You will take on an important role as a hub between different stakeholders and learn to drive strategic topics across divisional boundaries.

What you bring to the table

  • You have a bachelor's degree in economics, banking, business administration, digitalization or a comparable field that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your bachelor's degree
  • Basic understanding or even initial experience in the field of financial services as well as an affinity for digitalization, data and AI topics is an advantage
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills, strategic thinking and enjoyment of tackling complex issues in a structured and goal-oriented manner
  • Clear, addressee-oriented communication in German and enjoyment of cooperation and stakeholder management; other languages are welcome
  • High level of self-motivation, independent way of working and implementation skills; motivated to take responsibility and visibly make a difference with your contribution
