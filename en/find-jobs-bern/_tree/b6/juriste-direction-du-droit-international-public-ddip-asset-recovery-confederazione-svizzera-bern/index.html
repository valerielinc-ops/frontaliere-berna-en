On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

The Directorate for International Law (DV) is the federal competence centre for international law issues. It mainly deals with legal issues relating to Switzerland’s international relations and foreign policy. The Directorate also contributes to the further development of international law by participating in the negotiation, conclusion and implementation of international treaties. Compliance with and promotion of international law has always played a priority role in Swiss foreign policy and contributed to its coherence and credibility. The DV protects the interests of Switzerland in terms of international law and monitors the correct interpretation and application of international law by the Swiss authorities, both in Switzerland and abroad. The management also includes the Swiss Maritime Office in Basel.
