Full description

Responsibility

  • Professional and personnel management of the Investigation Team
  • Overall responsibility for handling and coordinating investigation cases
  • Responsibility for the complete and comprehensible documentation of all cases
  • Ensuring an objective, independent and appropriate investigation of cases
  • Independent conduct of internal investigations, including planning, conducting interviews, analysing facts and preparing investigation reports
  • Regular reporting
  • Preparation and implementation of the monthly IAB

Requirements

  • Completed law degree
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in conducting and leading internal investigations
  • Very good understanding of compliance requirements, governance and relevant processes
  • Experience in the management and further development of employees
  • Strong analytical, communicative and team-oriented skills
  • fluent knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of French and Italian is an advantage
