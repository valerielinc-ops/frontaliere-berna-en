Full description

The University of Bern is interconnected. At home, you are part of an international community and benefit from interdisciplinary collaborations.

Specification

  • During her tenure, the recruit will conduct a doctoral thesis (monography) on a subject she proposes. The project focuses on modern and contemporary literature of French expression (XIX-XXI century) and mobilizes the sociology of literature and/or a media approach to the literary fact. This may include, for example, mediating literature (criticism, prizes, festivals, public readings, bookstores and publishing houses), public life in the press, on the radio, in podcasts or on digital platforms, literary controversies and affairs, sound or performance forms of literature, or writers' positions in the media.
  • The project does not need to be included in the Chair's research program. However, it must be able to dialogue with its axes, in particular the project " Augmented Literature: Voices, Circles and Controversies", which crosses literary analysis, media sociology and sound studies.
  • The thesis, carried out at the University of Bern, will be directed by Prof. Mathilde Zbaeren and followed by a supervisory commission, in accordance with the doctoral regulation of the Faculty of Letters. A doctoral agreement guarantees protected research time and regular meetings with the thesis director.
  • The thesis covers 90% of working time. The remaining 10% is devoted to the Chair's research projects: participation in research meetings, assistance in organizing scientific events (days of study, symposia) and contribution to the Chair's research and development activities.
  • The position presupposes participation in the Institute's scientific life, including research seminars and doctoral meetings.

Requirements

  • Master's or equivalent diploma in French or comparative literature, obtained at the latest upon entry into office. Minimum rating: 5 (Switzerland); 16 (France)
  • Great motivation for writing a thesis work in French literature
  • Good knowledge of contemporary literature; interest in literature sociology and media study
  • Experience in field investigation, work on audio or audiovisual archives or digital humanities is an asset.
  • Ability to collaborate in teams
  • Organizational sense, especially for personal work
  • Availability to participate in scientific conferences and meetings and to develop a scientific network
  • Good linguistic knowledge (French as a mother tongue or C2; knowledge of German and English)

Named International Application and contact We welcome your application, which will include the following documents:

  • Letter of motivation
  • Curriculum vitae (with list of publications if applicable and two reference persons)
  • Diplomas (with notes)
  • Draft outline of the thesis project (maximum three pages, including bibliography) presenting the subject, the corpus, the questions and the method
  • Extract from master's work (approximately 20 pages and bibliography)
