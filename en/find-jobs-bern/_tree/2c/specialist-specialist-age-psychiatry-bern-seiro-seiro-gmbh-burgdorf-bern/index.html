Old-age psychiatry with time for people, in a hospital with short distances and a real team spirit. In Burgdorf, Canton of Bern, we are looking for a senior physician or senior physician in old-age psychiatry in the amount of 80 – 100% by appointment. She expects a regional clinic with specialized age psychiatric services, in which patient-centered care and interdisciplinary cooperation are the focus. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • You take over the medical responsibility for the diagnosis and treatment of older people with mental illness

  • They take over the supervision and further training of assistant doctors*

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title in psychiatry and psychotherapy, ideally with focus or interest in old-age psychiatry
  • Experience in the care of elderly people with mental illness
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, at least level C1

That sets them apart.

  • High social competence as well as self-responsible, independent working methods
  • Enjoyment of working in a multi-professional and interdisciplinary team

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with short decision paths
  • Structured support for training
  • Collegial, appreciative working environment in a multi-professional team
