SAP Consultant Treasury & Risk Management (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. SAP Consultant Treasury & Risk Management (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As part of the S/4 transformation, we are migrating the IT landscape of Treasury to SAP S/4HANA by January 2028. We are looking for an experienced SAP Consultant with a focus on Treasury & Risk Management including FX Exposure Management. In an agile, product-oriented team (SAFe), you combine business understanding, customizing experience and expertise. In this way, you design and implement the new treasury landscape and ensure safe and efficient financial processes for SBB. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • You assume responsibility for implementing S/4HANA solutions in the areas of Treasury & Risk Management (including Analyzer), FX Exposure Management and interfaces to trading platforms.
  • You contribute your knowledge, critically question requirements and design a coherent design for realization.
  • Together with colleagues in treasury and related departments, you will continue to develop processes and actively promote innovations.
  • You prepare analyses and decision bases and translate complex topics into clear concepts.
  • Until the complete conversion to S/4HANA, you will ensure the stable operation of the existing SAP landscape together with external partners (support, incident management, handovers).

You bring that with you.

  • With a strong focus on results, you drive topics forward proactively, stay persistent and get the information you need for viable results.
  • You have several years of experience in SAP Finance with a focus on treasury and risk management, FX exposure management and the connection of trading platforms and have successfully implemented S/4HANA solutions preferably in the standard.
  • In-depth technical know-how in SAP S/4HANA as well as practice in the integration of adjacent modules and systems will be brought to your daily work.
  • With high motivation and commitment, you continuously develop yourself and your environment and bring the willingness to familiarize yourself with additional treasury modules such as cash and liquidity management.
  • You work cooperatively in a team, ask the right questions, share knowledge and communicate in a targeted and precise manner in order to find good solutions together.
  • You have completed a business education or a degree in business informatics and have very good German (minimum C1) and good English skills (minimum B2).

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This position is in the requirement level L, regional allowance level 1.
