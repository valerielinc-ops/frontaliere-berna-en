Purchasing Clerk Passenger Transport Services — PostAuto AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you interested in purchasing topics and administrative processes? Are you enthusiastic about digital tools and an activity with many interfaces? In this role, you ensure a smooth process in the procurement of passenger transport services and take on a central role in the coordination of requests, contracts and orders. With you, the administrative and operational processes run efficiently, reliably and service-oriented.

You can do that.

  • You manage and develop the central e-mail inbox for the purchase of passenger transport services, process requests, carry out the triage and ensure a timely response.
  • In addition, you accompany contracts based on existing templates from creation via signature to complete filing and make the best use of your organizational skills.
  • You ensure that regular incoming data from our service providers is correctly recorded and maintained in the systems.
  • In addition, you check invoices and open strikes, take on administrative tasks and thus support a smooth process, bringing your quality awareness daily.
  • You check and process order requirements, record orders on time in the system and ensure that all contractual requirements are met.
  • In addition, you track orders and performance recordings precisely, control processes efficiently and develop them continuously, making an important contribution to success.
  • You manage internal and external accounts as well as permissions and support with technical challenges for smooth collaboration.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed commercial training and ideally bring experience in purchasing and/or administration.
  • You’ll also be confident with Office M365 and quickly familiarize yourself with new tools, allowing you to quickly find your way into new tasks.
  • You work precisely, structured and independently, even if several topics demand your attention at the same time.
  • In addition, you are interested in digitization and simple automations, which you independently implement and further develop, whereby you contribute your ideas profitably.
  • You convince with commitment, quality awareness and a positive attitude towards change.
  • In addition, you communicate verbally and in writing very well in German. You also have good oral knowledge in a second national language, which makes it easier for you to work with different stakeholders.
