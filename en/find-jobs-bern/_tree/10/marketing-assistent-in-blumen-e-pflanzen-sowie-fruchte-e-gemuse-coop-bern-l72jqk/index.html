Marketing assistant: in flowers and plants as well as fruits and vegetables

Coop Cooperative — Bern, BE, Svizzera

  • Job opening: 01.02.2027
  • Specialization: Category Management / Procurement
  • Location: Coop, Riedbachstrasse 161, 3027 Bern
  • Pensum: 80% - 100%

Tasks

  • Active support of points of sale in the daily business of flowers and plants and fruit and vegetables
  • Daily disposition of flowers and plants and fruits and vegetables from different suppliers
  • Regular telephone and written contact with sales outlets and suppliers
  • Prepare point of sale information in accordance with target groups
  • Planning, budgeting and processing of promotions
  • Creation of regional marketing concepts and various controllings
  • Support for project-related tasks as well as workshops on new concepts
  • Processing of points of sale quality concerns
  • General administrative tasks

Requirements

  • Commercial basic education with degree
  • Professional experience in marketing and product management is advantageous
  • Very good PC user knowledge (MS-Office), SAP R/3
  • Good oral and written language skills in German
  • Process-oriented, proactive and independent working method
  • Open, resilient and reliable personality
  • Affinity of the flowers and plants as well as fruits and vegetables sectors
  • Willingness to develop further with the opportunity to assume additional areas of responsibility in the future
  • Enjoyment at work in a dynamic environment as well as interest in the market
  • Discounts: 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription
