Public Transport Customer Service Representative — PostAuto AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you interested in excellent customer service and do you want to actively shape modern digital solutions? Are you enthusiastic about working efficiently on customer concerns and at the same time identifying optimization potential? In this twelve-month function, you combine personal advice with data-driven work and gain valuable experience in a dynamic public transport environment. With you, we create a customer service that not only reacts, but thinks ahead.

You can do that.

  • You look after our passengers professionally and empathetically at the point of sale, on the phone and in writing and answer questions about timetables, fares and offers.
  • In addition, you control customer concerns independently and solution-oriented, coordinate cooperation with internal bodies and make informed decisions even in the case of complex inquiries, which makes an important contribution to customer satisfaction.
  • You document customer interactions in the CRM system, assess goodwill requests within your competency framework and analyze recurring concerns.
  • In addition, you will work closely with different specialist areas and initiate sustainable improvements to our services, which will bring your know-how to the table.
  • You use modern digital tools and AI applications to optimize processes, find solutions faster and drive innovation.
  • In addition, you take on responsibility in shift operations on weekends and public holidays and benefit from a fair working time model that allows you to have free weekends on a regular basis.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed commercial basic education in public transport or an apprenticeship as a specialist or Professional customer dialogue and bring practical experience in customer consulting or complaint management.
  • In addition, you use MS Office, especially Excel with formulas, filters and pivot tables, safely for analyses and evaluations, using your strengths optimally.
  • You think analytically, recognize connections quickly and derive sound recommendations for action from data and customer feedback.
  • In addition, you use CRM systems and digital communication platforms safely and are enthusiastic about new technologies and AI applications, which convinces us.
  • You keep calm even in hectic situations, enjoy working in a team and convince through a pronounced service and customer orientation.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in German and have very good knowledge of Italian. Any other national language is beneficial.
