These tasks inspire you

  • Laboratory, hygiene and laws: You are responsible for carrying out internal hygiene controls in the company and coordinate the laboratory analyses closely with the laboratory. In addition, you evaluate and interpret analysis reports based on legal requirements such as the HyV (hygiene regulation).
  • Operational monitoring: You like to work operationally and take over the monitoring and the implementation of the periodic tasks. Test equipment monitoring is also your responsibility.
  • Complaint management: You are jointly responsible for the complaints system and coordinate the improvement measures with purchasing, production and sales.
  • Standardization and training: You consistently enforce hygiene and quality standards (according to GMP and FSSC certification). In addition, you support and help with training and the creation of training documents.
  • You actively support your colleagues in quality assurance with personnel bottlenecks, projects and day-to-day business.
