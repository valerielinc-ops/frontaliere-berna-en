Full description

This is what awaits you

The No. 1 legal protection insurance on the Swiss legal protection market is growing successfully. We continue to expand and seek reinforcement.

In this position you are part of the Legal & Services 2 department of Protekta. Together with your team of lawyers, you are responsible for handling cases in the field of legal protection.

Being right, showing empathy and thinking economically – are you ready for this challenge?

This is what you do with us

The focus of your job is on handling legal cases in the area of legal protection.

In the area of consulting legal protection, you not only check dry paragraphs, but also accompany customers in often emotional exceptional situations. You always keep the big picture in mind: the insurance policy, the legal situation, the profitability and a clear communication at all levels.

With the legal know-how, you quickly arrange complex issues. You analyze the entire insurance contract of our customers and check well-founded whether and to what extent there is a claim to an insurance service.

You will support those seeking advice in difficult and emotionally stressful situations professionally, calmly and solution-oriented. You will also conduct oral and written negotiations with our customers, counterparties and legal representatives.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed legal studies (Federal Dipl.) Legal professional / -man / BLaw / MLaw
  • Ideally with practical experience from legal protection insurance or claims settlement
  • Customer-oriented, negotiating-strong and flexible working method
  • Working principle
  • Strong business understanding
  • Team-oriented, initiative and open-minded personality
  • Open attitude towards digital issues
  • Flowing German in word and writing, English, French or Italian skills are a plus

Working with us

An established and motivated team of lawyers is waiting for you. Together with the team, you live a communicative, solution-oriented and independent way of working in a dynamic environment in constant growth. The job offer is aimed at both experienced legal professionals and lawyers: women who value a contemporary and healthy work-life balance at good conditions, as well as young professionals who have modern onboarding and individual mentoring at their disposal.

Your workload is between 60% and 80% negotiable and the place of work is Bern.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
