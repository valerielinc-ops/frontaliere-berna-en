What awaits you.

Get on board, first-class employer! An innovative working environment awaits you, with many motivated and motivating colleagues. Continuing training is not neglected for your individual professional development. We offer many advantages - Let yourself be convinced: Social benefits. What you can move.

  • You build and maintain a variety of rail traffic safety facilities such as rail signs, remote control systems, power supplies, free-state control systems, ETCS beacons, level crossings, etc.
  • You proceed with the commissioning and decommissioning of security facilities.
  • You analyze and lift the inconveniences at the facilities.
  • After an in-house training, you provide picket service (home in the vicinity of Biel, accessible in 30 minutes).

What you bring.

  • You have a CFC of automatic, electrical, electronic or equivalent profession (branch with electrical orientation).
  • You are prepared to undergo in-house training as a safety technician and regular in-service training.
  • You have a high sense of responsibility and sensitivity to security issues.
  • You have a Class B driver's licence.
  • Good oral and written knowledge in French or German and knowledge of the other desired language (minimum B2).
