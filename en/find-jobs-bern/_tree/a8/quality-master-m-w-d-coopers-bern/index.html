Full description

For our customers from the insurance industry in Bern, we are looking for a:n experienced:n, motivated:n and open-minded:n Quality Master.

In order to ensure high software quality within an Agile Release Train (ART), we are looking for an experienced person who is responsible for test management, drives the further development of quality standards and accompanies the change towards a more automated test landscape.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for quality assurance within the Agile Release Train (ART)
  • Ensuring the defined test strategy and a structured and efficient test procedure
  • Coordination and control of cross-ART and cross-functional tests in a complex system landscape
  • Create transparency about quality status, risks and dependencies and derive appropriate measures
  • Support of product managers and epi owners in acceptance and quality-related decisions
  • Ensuring test requirements, test documentation and coordination of test cases and test data at feature level
  • Support in setting up and optimizing concepts for test data provision in dependent system landscapes
  • Professional leadership and support of the quality engineers as well as moderation of quality-relevant votes
  • Further development of the test procedure with a particular focus on test automation and end-to-end testing
  • Analysis and coordination of dependencies related to cloud migrations and hybrid system environments
  • Active participation in the quality management community and promotion of knowledge exchange
  • Support in establishing an internal successor and ensuring the sustainable transfer of know-how

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science or comparable technical training
  • Several years of professional experience (at least 5 years) in test management or in a comparable role
  • Deep experience in agile software development environments, ideally with SAFe
  • Practical experience in the introduction and implementation of test automation
  • Good knowledge of software development, especially with Java and SQL
  • Certifications in testing, for example ISTQB Advanced Level or iSQI Certified Agile Tester
  • In-depth understanding of end-to-end testing, test data management and complex system dependencies
  • Enthusiasm for agile methods and high affinity for quality and automation topics
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as logical-systemic thinking
  • Communicative, assertive and team-oriented personality with leadership skills
  • Very good German and English skills
