Full description

Portrait The Curative Education School Lyss is a day school for children and adolescents with a mental or multiple impairment. We teach around 115 students in 18 classes (basic to upper and orientation level) between the ages of 4 and 18.

Tasks

Psychomotor treatment of children and adolescents with special needs Therapeutic investigation Implementation of psychomotor support offers in individual setting and in small groups Collaboration, in particular with the existing PMT therapist Documentation (funding diagnostic reporting, administrative)

  • Tasks

Organization of the therapy offer (waiting list management, maintenance therapy material)

Requirements

Recognised training in psychomotor skills (or at completion of training) Enjoying the challenges of everyday school life Commitment to students with clinical needs Practical experience in psychomotor work with children and young people Resilience and positive communication skills Close cooperation with teachers and specialists in the context of comprehensive support High level of teamwork and willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation

We offer

Supportive introduction to the task Engaged and initiatives team The opportunity to develop professionally and personally Bright, modern and large therapy room with modern equipment Annual working hours within the framework of 38 school weeks and remuneration according to cantonal guidelines Working day freely selectable
