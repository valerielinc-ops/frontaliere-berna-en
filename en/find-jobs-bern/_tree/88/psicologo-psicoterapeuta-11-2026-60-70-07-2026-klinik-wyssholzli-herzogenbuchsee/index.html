Klinik Wysshölzli is a specialized psychiatric and psychotherapeutic private clinic in Oberaargau (Herzogenbuchsee, BE).

Job: Psychologist psychotherapist 11.2026–60% 70 07/2026.

Clinic Wysshölzli is a leading psychiatric and psychotherapeutic specialist clinic for women with addictions and eating disorders. Our main location in the Oberaargau, Canton of Bern, in a quiet location convinces with wonderful nature and proximity to Bern and Olten. The clinic offers 37 inpatient treatment stations and two ambulatories. Assignments are made from all cantons of German-speaking Switzerland, the patients enter planned. We are looking for a psychologist 60 - 70% by 1 November 2026 or by appointment:

  • Your tasks
  • With your psychotherapeutic work in individual and group settings, you make a valuable contribution and offer our patients a safe space so that they can further develop their strengths.
  • You take over the therapy planning and the case management. Our patients are often in hospital treatment with us for several months.
  • You are in intensive exchange with the team of psychologists. Interdisciplinary cooperation and the regular involvement of relatives are a matter of course for us.
  • Our versatile and exciting work is complemented by psychotherapy with outpatients and the conduct of preliminary discussions for the indication test.
  • They document disease courses and write reports.
  • Your profile
  • You bring great pleasure to a deeper psychotherapeutic work with mostly young women and their relatives.
  • You have completed or already advanced psychotherapy training.
  • You have several years of clinical experience, preferably with eating disorders or dependency disorders. With your networked thinking and ideas, you contribute to the continuous development of our therapy program.
  • With your open, uncomplicated and collegial nature, you fit into our lively team.
  • You are resilient and enjoy independent and interdisciplinary work. Your chance
  • With a lot of independence, you take on an interesting, responsible and versatile activity. In our modern, manageable company, the collegial, open and uncomplicated culture as well as the interdisciplinary cooperation are highly appreciated.
  • There is internal and external supervision as well as further training opportunities. -
  • We offer discounted food options as well as a free parking space. We look forward to your application.
