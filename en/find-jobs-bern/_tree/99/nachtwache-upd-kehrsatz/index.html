Full description

wita Directorate for Living, Integration, Day Structure and Work

An important link between the clinical treatment of people with mental illnesses and the targeted inclusion of people with psychiatric experience is the rehabilitative facilities in the areas of living, working and daily structure. The WohnPassage Weidli is a transitional residential facility with integrated employment with 22 places for people with mental health restrictions who want to take a step towards independent living after a hospital stay or after an outpatient crisis. These places are guided in various properties in the municipality Kehrsatz. Accompanying users at WohnPassage Weidli takes their individual sensitivities into account, provides support in crisis situations and helps to maintain or build social relationships - users are offered the opportunity to prepare for an independent life by being able to practice and strengthen the necessary skills.

Your tasks

  • As a night service, you take care of 22 users
  • If necessary, you are the contact person for the users and give medication
  • You are responsible for keeping the night’s sleep
  • You independently perform various administrative tasks
  • The service time is from 20:45 - 07:45, you can usually withdraw to the rest room from 24:00

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a health professional (FaGe) or care professional (FaBe) EFZ or bring other professional experience in social and health care
  • You have experience dealing with people who have psychological difficulties
  • You are ready to also cover night services on weekends and public holidays
  • You distinguish yourself through your social skills, patience and resilience
  • You bring good PC user knowledge and are ready to do administrative tasks

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
