Full description

Put to the Point

You are committed to strengthening the cyber capabilities of the Swiss Army. As part of the Cyber Training Center (CTC), you will design the Cyber Training Range (CTR) for challenging cyber exercises in a dynamic environment. You plan and coordinate cyber trainings, develop the CTR service offering and work closely with military, civilian, national and international partners.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20535
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Further develop and design the Cyber Training Range (CTR) and design new services and training offers taking into account technical possibilities, benefits and cost-effectiveness
  • Planning, coordinating and executing sophisticated cyber trainings and exercises and continuously improving their quality and effectiveness
  • Analyse the needs of military and civilian user groups and derive innovative training offers as well as future-oriented further developments of the Cyber Training Range
  • Promote cooperation with partners from the federal government, cantons, industry, partner nations and NATO, as well as actively support international cyber exercises
  • Systematically evaluate and further process experiences from cyber exercises in order to strengthen operational excellence and continuously optimize the Cyber Training Range
  • Coordinate and lead projects, external partners and employees as well as represent the Cyber Training Center in committees, projects, presentations and other events

This makes you unique

  • Higher vocational training or (specialised) university degree in computer science (bachelor) or a comparable discipline and several years of professional experience in computer science, DevOps and cybersecurity
  • experience in the management and implementation of training and education
  • Experience with military-tactical and simulation-based exercises is advantageous
  • Result-oriented thinking and the ability to identify needs from the army environment and civil-military context and to align innovative services of the Cyber Training Range (CTR) accordingly and to switch between technology and operational implementation on the one hand and strategic thinking and product vision on the other hand depending on the context and to combine both perspectives
  • Customer-oriented action and sovereign communication with internal and external stakeholders
  • High degree of independence, initiative and curiosity as well as the willingness to implement new technologies and solutions
  • Active knowledge in two official languages and passive knowledge in the third official language as well as very good knowledge of English

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Employing the Swiss army makes sense and brings many advantages. Convince yourself of Army.ch/advantages. Offers from recruiters are not taken into account.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
