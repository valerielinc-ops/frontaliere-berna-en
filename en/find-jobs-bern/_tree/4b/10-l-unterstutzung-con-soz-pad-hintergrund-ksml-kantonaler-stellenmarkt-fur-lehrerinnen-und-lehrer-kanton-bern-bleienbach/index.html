Full description

Portrait The elementary school Lotzwil is a school association of the municipalities Lotzwil, Bleienbach and Rütschelen. Currently, 470 students from kindergarten to 9th grade (second and real) attend the 25 classes of our school.

Tasks

  • You teach during Wednesday-Friday 12 L. As a class teacher, you support and accompany the class.
  • Subjects: 3 L. Math, 3 L. German, 4 L. NMG, 2 L. French at the parallel class.

Requirements

Ideally, you are a student or a qualified primary teacher. You are resilient and a team player.

We offer

Well-established team, compact timetable. By changing staff, the job owner reduces her current workload. She teaches the class Monday, Tuesday. For questions/needs, this supports the new setting.

The advertised workload can be expanded / continued depending on the planning in the new school year. As this is still open, the position is advertised as temporary until the end of the school year.
