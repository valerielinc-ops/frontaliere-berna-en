Full description

Portrait The school Lauenen is a family village school in the middle of an idyllic mountain landscape at 1250 m above sea level. As a multi-class school, we promote age-mixed learning. Together we shape the further development of our school and accompany the children on their individual educational path. We create an appreciative learning environment in which they can develop professionally and personally. You can get an insight into our school at www.schule-lauenen.ch. At the beginning of the school year 2027/2028, we are looking for a committed part-time teacher for the 1st/2nd grade with a workload of around 30%. The lessons include music, musical primary school and sports as well as departmental teaching.

Tasks

Our school is in an exciting development process. Together as a team, we continue to shape our school profile and promote individual learning through cross-class projects. They bring a positive and open attitude as well as sound professional skills. In doing so, the child and his or her individual development are at the center of their pedagogical action.

Your responsibilities include in particular

  • They teach the 1st / 2nd grade as part-time teacher in the amount of 9 lessons.
  • They teach the fields of music, musical primary school, sports and departmental teaching.
  • They maintain close cooperation with the class teacher, the kindergarten teachers and the entire college.
  • They make cooperation with parents open and constructive.
  • They are involved in comprehensive school events and projects and are open to new ideas and developments.
  • You take on tasks at school and level as part of your professional assignment.

Requirements

They have a bachelor’s degree for the primary level and ideally bring their first professional experience. New entrants and lateral entrants with corresponding experience and completed training modules are also welcome. Do you appreciate working at a small school, enjoy taking responsibility for the class and feel comfortable in age-mixed learning settings? Do you enjoy working in a team and at the same time appreciate the opportunity to implement your own ideas? Then the school Lauenen could be your new place of activity! Apply now for our 1st/2nd class! With us, you can expect an environment in which you can contribute your pedagogical strengths and actively shape everyday school life.

We offer

  • A permanent employment in accordance with cantonal guidelines.
  • Professional support of school development by PHBern.
  • A dedicated team with appreciative and cooperative cooperation.
  • A family school environment with a pleasant working atmosphere, age-mixed learning and lived integration as well as space for own ideas and participation.
  • Support from the school committee, especially in the search for suitable accommodation.
