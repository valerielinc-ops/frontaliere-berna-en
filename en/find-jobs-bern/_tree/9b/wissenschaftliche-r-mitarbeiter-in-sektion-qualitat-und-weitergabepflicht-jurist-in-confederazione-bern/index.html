Put to the Point

Are you concerned about improving the quality of services and patient safety? Are you interested in monitoring the obligation of service providers to pass on benefits? Apply now – the FOPH is looking for a motivated person to perform these tasks.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20235

You can make this contribution

  • Legal and interdisciplinary issues in the implementation of health insurance law
  • Implementation of quality development measures in accordance with Article 58 et seq. accompany KVG, in particular participate in the development of conceptual and strategic foundations for quality development, examine quality contracts and prepare their approval by the Federal Council
  • Accompany the implementation of the provisions on the obligation to pass on pursuant to Article 56(3)(b) and (3)bis of the KVG, in particular cooperate in the monitoring of compliance with the obligation to pass on and implement administrative procedures
  • Prepare reports and opinions on quality development and on disclosure
  • Prepare and participate in legislative activities in the field of quality development and on-sharing obligations

This makes them unique.

  • University degree as a lawyer, preferably with a lawyer's patent or similar additional qualification, professional experience in the federal administration advantageous
  • Comprehensive knowledge of health insurance and health law, Swiss health care and administrative law
  • Editorial skills, good sense of analytical and conceptual working methods
  • Independent and structured working method with an above-average degree of personal responsibility, communication and teamwork ability
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages and, if possible, passive knowledge of the third official language

  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.
