Information Security Officer — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you interested in information security and confident navigating between specialist departments, projects and management? Would you like to communicate security topics clearly and support their implementation? Then we are looking for exactly you! With you, we can decisively advance information security and inspire others to embrace it.

What you can achieve

  • You strengthen information security within your area of responsibility and continuously develop it further.
  • Together with the specialist departments, you identify and analyze security risks and develop pragmatic solutions to reduce them.
  • You prepare security requirements, risks and decision-making information in a clear and audience-appropriate manner.
  • As a competent point of contact, you advise stakeholders on information security matters and support the implementation of security requirements.
  • You bring together different interests and develop viable solutions.
  • In initiatives and projects, you work closely with various stakeholders and introduce security topics at an early stage.
  • Through targeted awareness and empowerment activities, you strengthen security awareness and firmly embed information security in the organization over the long term.

What you bring

  • You have a university degree in computer science, information security, or equivalent training.
  • You have already gained professional experience in Cyber Security, Risk Management or Governance.
  • You have experience working with information security standards, methods, and governance processes.
  • Thanks to your strong communication and consulting skills, you convey complex matters clearly and in a way tailored to the audience.
  • You bring together different interests and guide different perspectives toward common solutions.
  • You enjoy working with various stakeholders and are confident navigating a dynamic matrix environment.
  • You communicate confidently in German and additionally have knowledge of a second national language as well as English.

Location-independent work is possible; 1-2 days of on-site presence per week in Bern are required.
