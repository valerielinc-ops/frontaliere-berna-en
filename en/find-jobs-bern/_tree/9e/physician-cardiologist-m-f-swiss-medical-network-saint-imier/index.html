Full description

As part of your activity, you ensure the specialized care of patients in cardiology, in close collaboration with medical and nursing teams.

Your main missions

  • Provide specialist consultations in cardiology for the diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of cardiovascular diseases.
  • Perform and interpret diagnostic examinations, including electrocardiograms, echocardiograms and stress tests.
  • Develop individualized therapeutic strategies tailored to patient needs and history.
  • Follow-up of patients with chronic cardiac conditions, in a continuous care approach.
  • Perform, according to your skills and credentials, certain technical acts in collaboration with the specialized teams.
  • Work closely with other medical specialties to ensure comprehensive and coordinated care.
  • Actively participate in the quality of care and proper maintenance of medical records.
  • Federal diploma of doctor or equivalent recognized in Switzerland.
  • Title of specialist in Swiss cardiology or current MEBEKO recognition.
  • Clinical experience in cardiology and mastery of major diagnostic examinations.
  • Ability to work independently while fostering interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Excellent relationship skills, sense of responsibility and patient-centred approach.
  • Motivation to invest sustainably in a community care structure.

Joining the Réseau de l'Arc is integrating a dynamic medical environment, where collaboration between different specialties and proximity to patients is essential.

We offer you

  • A varied and stimulating medical activity, offering real autonomy in your practice.
  • A working environment that promotes interdisciplinary exchanges and continuity of care.
  • A technical tray adapted to the practice of cardiology.
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development.
  • Attractive conditions of employment and remuneration.
  • The opportunity to contribute to the development of cardiology care in the region.

We pay particular attention to the diversity of career paths and encourage applications from individuals who wish to become involved in community, human and quality medicine. Entry into office: Immediately or to be agreed

Place of work: Hospital of Saint-Imier and/or establishments of the Réseau de l'Arc

Activity rate: To be agreed

Community-based institutions with multi-disciplinary expertise in patient service, the Moutier Hospital and the Saint-Imier Hospital offer quality services in the somatic and mental health fields. With nearly 1'200 employees, the hospitals and Medicentres of the Réseau de l'Arc provide community care to a population of more than 50,000 inhabitants. In order to strengthen our offer of cardiology care at the Saint-Imier site, we are looking for a medical specialist in cardiology who wants to invest in a multidisciplinary team and contribute to quality care, focusing on patient needs.
