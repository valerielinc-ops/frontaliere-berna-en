Full description

Portrait At Interlaken Gymnasium with its department in Gstaad, more than 450 students and 70 teachers learn and teach. This makes the Interlaken Gymnasium the smallest public school in the canton of Bern. We offer our student body from the expansive Oberland a wide range of main subjects and supplementary subjects as well as a bilingual graduate degree. Our school is characterised by a personal atmosphere, a four-day week and diverse international relationships.

The Gymnasium Interlaken is looking for the period from 11 January to 2. July 2027 a substitute for music:

Tasks
