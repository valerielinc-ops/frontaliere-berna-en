Every day at PHOENIX Pharma Switzerland we have an important task: to provide millions of people with medicines and health products. With our team of over 1,000 dedicated employees, we ensure that these products are reliably and continuously distributed throughout Switzerland. Our various business units make an active contribution to health, and this is where your role comes into full force. Our success is based on the professionalism and commitment of our employees, who embody our corporate culture. Be the heartbeat of healthcare. As Space Management & Visual Merchandising Manager, you actively shape the sales areas of our pharmacies. You are responsible for space planning, optimize the use of our sales areas and accompany new openings and transformations. You coordinate the implementation of category management and continuously develop our visual merchandising concepts and POS materials. The position is limited in time as maternity representation and offers you the opportunity to take on responsibility in a wide range of tasks and to contribute your experience immediately. Are you looking for a varied job where you can combine creativity, data understanding and organizational talent? Then you are exactly right with us. We value commitment, initiative and team spirit.

The heart of your work

In your role as Space Planning Manager (m/f/d) – maternity representation, you are responsible for:

  • Responsibility for space management, space allocation and the creation, maintenance and optimization of the layout and placement plans of our pharmacies.
  • Analysis and optimization of the use of our sales areas with the aim of optimally exploiting the space efficiency and economic potential.
  • Planning and implementation of space concepts taking into account the product range structure, customer needs and strategic specifications.
  • Support of new pharmacy openings and transformations as well as coordination of the implementation of category management taking into account the requirements of all areas involved.
  • Ensure coordinated and efficient implementation of category management concepts during new openings, renovations and transformations.
  • Creation, adaptation and further development of surface and layout plans as well as ensuring their timeliness and correct implementation.
  • Coordination of cooperation with various internal stakeholders and ensuring efficient implementation of land planning.
  • Use of Perspektix or comparable tools for space planning, the creation and adjustment of layout and placement plans as well as the optimization of our sales areas.

What makes our hearts beat faster

  • Completed training with a federal professional card or a comparable qualification.
  • At least three years of professional experience in a relevant field of activity.
  • Very good practical knowledge in Perspektix or comparable tools are a prerequisite for this function.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to address challenges in a structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented way.
  • Critical thinking as well as the ability to question existing concepts and processes and to identify potential for improvement.
  • Strong coordination, organisation and flexibility capabilities.
  • Good data knowledge and the ability to analyze, interpret and use data for optimization and decision-making.
  • Empathy and emotional intelligence as well as a good sense for different stakeholders.
  • Very good oral and written communication skills.
  • Secure handling of common IT applications and digital work equipment.
  • Very good knowledge of German or French (at least C1) as well as good knowledge of the other language (at least B1).
  • Resilience and the ability to maintain an overview and set priorities even in demanding situations.
