Full description

Portrait Around 1,000 pupils attend Grauholz School in 50 classes in the contracted municipalities of Urtenen-Schönbühl, Bäriswil and Mattstetten. Cycle 1 comprises around 17 classes at all three locations, with a focus on Urtenen-Schönbühl. All school facilities are easily accessible by public transport.

The school management team and the school secretariat are located at the school complex Lee in Urtenen-Schönbühl. The school management team includes the school management of the three cycles and a school management assistant in cycle 2, the school management MR and the management of the day school. The team is led by the Head of Education.

We consciously encounter heterogeneity. We value diversity and individuality and maintain the principle of care at Grauholz School.

Tasks

You take over the pedagogical and administrative-organizational leadership of Cycle 1 with around 17 classes and about 38 teachers. The introduction of a school management assistant in cycle 1 for support is possible. Staff management is your responsibility.

At the same time, you will be relieved by the head of education in various higher-level areas, for example:

  • the management of ePM,
  • school development;
  • public relations;
  • community-related tasks.

Together with a dedicated, motivated and supportive school management team, you will implement the goals and measures of the educational strategy and the school program.

Requirements

Du

  • know the Bernese school system,
  • have pedagogical training,
  • bring experience in school management,
  • have completed school management training or are ready to complete it in a timely manner,
  • likes to initiate innovations and successfully accompany change processes,
  • work in a structured manner and keep an overview even in demanding situations,
  • are interested in the education of the future,
  • convinced by high social, leadership and communication skills,
  • are resilient and capable of criticism and conflict,
  • Commit to the concerns of children, parents and employees,
  • have good knowledge of ICT users.

We offer

  • a varied and responsible management task with a great deal of room for manoeuvre,
  • careful training and coaching by the Head of Education,
  • a trusting and collegial cooperation in the school management team,
  • a dedicated, age-mixed college,
  • a professionally run school secretariat,
  • modern and contemporary school infrastructures,
  • on-going multiannual planning for extensions and refurbishments of school sites;
  • well-established school social work,
  • an efficient ICT infrastructure.
