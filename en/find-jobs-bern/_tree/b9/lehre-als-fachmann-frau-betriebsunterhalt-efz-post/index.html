Apprenticeship as a facility maintenance specialist EFZ — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you skilled in craftsmanship, are you motivated by varied tasks and do you love being in the fresh air? Then an apprenticeship in company maintenance is just right for you! During the three-year basic education you acquire the tools for a successful entry into the professional world.

Your education

  • From the very beginning, you work in the team and join the post office and partner companies in the company maintenance.
  • As an all-rounder: you ensure a functioning, clean and orderly infrastructure for the postal workers.
  • You maintain and clean buildings, machinery and outdoor facilities, monitor the building technology, carry out repairs, repair structural damage, maintain green areas and deal with disposal.
  • During your apprenticeship, you will, among other things, complete a stage in a nursery and acquire the knowledge to maintain green areas.

You bring that with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should fulfill the following points:

  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • Craftsmanship gives you pleasure.
  • You have a good practical and technical understanding.
  • Reliability and self-responsible action are among your strengths.
  • You are physically resilient and like to be in contact with people.
