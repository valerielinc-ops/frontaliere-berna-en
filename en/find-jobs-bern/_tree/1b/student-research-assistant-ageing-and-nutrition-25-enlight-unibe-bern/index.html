Duties and Responsibilities

The Institute of Primary Health Care of the University of Bern (BIHAM) is dedicated to train and foster the next generation of primary care physicians and to carry out research in primary health care, epidemiology, and public health. The BIHAM offers a lively, interdisciplinary environment with excellent methodological support and access to a large network of practicing primary care physicians. The Ageing and Nutrition Research Group is seeking a student assistant to support the activities of the Research and Education in Ageing Collaborative Team (REACT), a thematic network funded by the ENLIGHT European University Alliance. REACT brings together researchers from six European universities to advance interdisciplinary research, education and knowledge exchange in healthy ageing. You will work closely with an international team of researchers and support the coordination of ongoing research collaborations, educational initiatives and scientific communication activities. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Coordinating meetings with international partners, preparing agendas, taking minutes and following up on agreed actions.
  • Supporting the coordination of collaborative scientific publications, including communication with co-authors and monitoring deadlines.
  • Maintaining project documentation and tracking ongoing activities, milestones and deliverables.
  • Assisting with the organisation of scientific meetings and educational activities.
  • Supporting scientific communication and dissemination activities.
  • Contributing to project reporting and documentation.
