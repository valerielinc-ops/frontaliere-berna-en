Full description

Portrait The assignment region Wattenwil is responsible for special teaching in the region Wattenwil, Seftigen, Gurzelen, Forst-Längenbühl and Burgistein.The gifted support can be offered at the location Wattenwil or Seftigen.

Tasks

  • The gifted support supports students with special strengths, interests or a high learning potential.
  • Through in-depth, expanding and creative learning opportunities, their individual skills are specifically promoted.
  • The aim is to create challenging learning opportunities that support curiosity, self-employment, problem-solving skills and personal growth.
  • The gifted promotion takes place in groups in Wattenwil or Seftigen.

Requirements

  • Ideally, the teacher for the promotion of the gifted has an appropriate further education (CAS or MAS) or in curative education.
  • The teacher teaches with his own teaching goals and ensures the transfer to the class.
  • Regular exchange with teachers and parents

We offer

  • Broad infrastructure for various projects
  • Exchange in the MR assignment region Wattenwil with a school management MR
  • Temporary employment until summer 2027 with option for continuation
