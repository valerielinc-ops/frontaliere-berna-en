Full description

University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Are you interested in clinical and neuroscience issues in the field of suicidality and suicide prevention? Would you like to further develop your scientific career in an interdisciplinary research environment and contribute to a better understanding and treatment of people with suicidal experience and behavior? The multidisciplinary research group of PD Dr. phil. Anja Gysin-Maillart and Prof. Dr. med. Kristina Adorjan at the University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Bern deals with the early detection, understanding and treatment of suicidal experience and behavior in adulthood. Our research combines clinical questions with empirical and neuroscientific methods such as clinical interviews, questionnaires, neuropsychological procedures, ecological momentary assessment (EMA) and digital and physiological measurement methods. For reinforcement, we are looking for a committed postdoc who would like to further develop his/her scientific profile and design independent research projects in the field of suicide research and suicide prevention. We offer a scientifically challenging position in an interdisciplinary environment with opportunities to develop our own research projects, support for publications and third-party funding, access to existing research projects and data sets, and national and international networking. In addition, you benefit from mentoring, training and development opportunities as well as participation in scientific conferences.

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
