Managing Group-wide AI Transformation & Innovation

Do you want to shape AI at the corporate level? In this key role, you develop forward-looking strategies, promote innovation, connect people and ideas and create sustainable business benefits for the entire company.

Your field of activity

  • Overall responsibility for AI transformation & innovation (strategy, roadmap, budget, KPIs, risks)
  • Development and implementation of the AI strategy at Group level
  • Promote AI innovation by identifying, prioritizing and scaling AI use cases along the value chain
  • Establishing AI governance, compliance and operating models
  • Promoting a data-driven, innovative corporate culture incl. Change management
  • Establishment of a Group-wide network and close cooperation with management and stakeholders
  • Leadership of international, interdisciplinary teams and communities

Your strengths

  • Completed university studies in economics, computer science, engineering or a comparable field of expertise
  • At least 10 years of professional experience in transformation, digitalization, AI, innovation or consulting
  • Several years of management experience of large Group programs
  • Demonstrate success in scaling digital solutions with business impact
  • Sound understanding of technology (AI, data, automation, platforms) as well as strong competencies in program, change and stakeholder management (up to C-level)
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, high implementation competence
  • Very strong communication and presentation skills in German and English
  • Ideally, you will bring industry knowledge in the energy or infrastructure environment.
  • Willingness to travel within the DACH region
