You can look forward to that.

You will lead and develop an interdisciplinary team in the field of data management. Together with the Product Owner, you create the conditions for sustainable success and promote the further development of skills, collaboration and organization. You actively shape the future of our data management and work closely with departmental management, product management, architecture and other specialist areas. Exciting topics around SAP MDG, S/4HANA, master data, data quality, automation and modern data solutions await you – with plenty of room for manoeuvre, flexible forms of work and attractive benefits.   • As a team leader, you lead and develop employees and create an environment in which responsibility is taken on and professional and personal development succeeds.
  • Together with the Product Owner, you ensure that priorities, required skills and available capacities fit together optimally.
  • Targeted, you develop skills and capabilities further, identify skills gaps early on and derive suitable development measures.
  • The team composition as well as resources and capacities you plan ahead and participate in recruiting and onboarding new employees.
  • You promote effective collaboration, support continuous improvement and help the team to solve obstacles and conflicts constructively.
  • The further development of data management and the automation of business processes you actively shape and accompany changes in the context of the digital transformation.

You bring that with you.

  • With experience in the management and development of employees, ideally in a data-oriented environment, you have a solid basis.
  • In IT, digitization or transformation, you have already taken on a leadership or responsible role and lead projects reliably to the goal.
  • A good understanding of data management as well as data-driven and automated business processes sets you apart.
  • You are familiar with agile and product-oriented working methods and have knowledge in the SAFe environment. Ideally, you have experience in the SAP environment, especially SAP MDG and S/4HANA.
  • We presuppose a university degree (FH/uni) in Business Informatics/Data Science or Comparable, ideally supplemented by a further education in Leadership.
  • In German, you are sure to negotiate (level C1), every other national language is a plus.
