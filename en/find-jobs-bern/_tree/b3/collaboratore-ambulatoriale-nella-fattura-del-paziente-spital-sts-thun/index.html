Full description

You are responsible for the correct invoicing (including performance control) according to legal, collective agreement and operational regulations. You create mainly ambulatory billings and provide information when asking questions about issued invoices. Furthermore, you act as a contact person for questions from internal service providers and work closely with upstream and downstream areas. You coordinate clarifications between service providers, patients, clinics and insurers. In addition, you work in the rejection management and process invoice complaints from the outpatient service area including cancel and create new invoices.

Entrance at 1. March 2027 or by arrangement.
