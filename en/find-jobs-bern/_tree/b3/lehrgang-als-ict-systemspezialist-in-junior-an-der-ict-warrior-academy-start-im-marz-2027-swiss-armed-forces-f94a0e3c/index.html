Full description

In a nutshell

Have you completed your apprenticeship as an IT specialist and want to get your professional career off to a flying start? The ICT Warrior Academy is the Armed Forces' center of expertise for civilian education and further training in the field of ICT and cyber. Here, you have the unique opportunity to spend 12 months in Switzerland's most modern IT environment, put your skills to the test, and train to become an IT whiz. At the same time, you enjoy all the benefits of a permanent position with Cyber Command and, after passing the exam, have the opportunity to build a long-term career with us. Would you like to learn more about the program? Then register with us for a virtual information event at www.armee.ch/ict-warrior-academy. A position with the Swiss Armed Forces makes sense and offers many benefits. See for yourself at armee.ch/vorteile. Women are underrepresented here – your application is particularly welcome!

  • Start date: March 1, 2027
  • Employment type: fixed-term
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-19697
  • Work location: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

What you can contribute

  • Learn how to work with modern data center technologies (including Docker, Kubernetes, Openshift, Ansible) and become familiar with complex network, server, and storage solutions and automations
  • Learn about automating Windows and Linux platforms and using system management and monitoring tools
  • Locate and resolve faults and performance issues in hardware and software
  • Complete a series of German-language basic training courses in network and server technology (some of which are completed with recognized industry certifications)
  • Learn the basic principles of cyber security and understand how Switzerland protects itself against cyberattacks
  • Co-develop and operate solutions (DevSecOps) during practical assignments in order to apply what you have learned directly

This is what makes you unique

  • Completed training as an IT specialist EFZ in the field of platform development/application development/IT operations with 2-3 years of relevant professional experience, alternatively a baccalaureate or comparable training or practical experience
  • Enthusiasm for complex systems comprising modern hardware and software, as well as basic knowledge in the areas of networking, servers, storage, operating systems (Windows/Unix/Linux), virtualization, scripting and software development
  • Initial professional experience in the operations of large, complex IT environments, as well as in the use of system management and monitoring tools, is an advantage
  • Willingness to learn, enthusiasm, good comprehension, "Out-of-the-Box" thinking and enjoyment of teamwork

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in practice Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the area of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for expertise in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine our enthusiasm for our mission with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile ways of working. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Armed Forces and our partners in the Swiss security network. This enables Kdo Cy to give the Swiss Armed Forces the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a secure Switzerland.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 staff in four military and around 200 civilian professions – and do so at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Their applications are therefore particularly welcome.
