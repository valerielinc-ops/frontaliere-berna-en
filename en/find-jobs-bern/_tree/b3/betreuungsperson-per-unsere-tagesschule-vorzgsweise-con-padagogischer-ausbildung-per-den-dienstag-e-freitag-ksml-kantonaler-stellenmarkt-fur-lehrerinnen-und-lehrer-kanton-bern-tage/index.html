Full description

Portrait Lengnau School is a living place of learning and personal development. Our school covers from kindergarten to 9. Around 650 students, accompanied by a dedicated team of over 90 teachers. In addition to the school, we run a modern day school, which welcomes around 70 children per week in various modules from kindergarten to 9th grade.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a warm, committed and reliable Personality who enjoys working with children and adolescents Appreciative and competently accompanied.

We are happy to present our school facilities in more detail under the following link: https://youtu.be/9EKn3A0rN2Y?si=NtkdkoyVqmRoZMrf

Tasks

Caring for children and young people in day-school life.

  • Tuesday: 07.00–08.00 and 11.30–18.00
  • Friday: 07.00–08.00 and 11.30–18.00

Its tasks shall include

  • Planning, support and implementation of age-appropriate activities
  • Individual support and promotion of children and young people
  • Support for conflicts and challenging situations
  • Promoting self-employment and social cohesion
  • Accompanying the children on the school and kindergarten path
  • Participation in the work in day school everyday life
  • Cooperation and exchange in the day-school team

Requirements

  • Educational or socio-educational training or related profession completed
  • Experience in working with children and adolescents in accompaniment until promotion
  • Enjoy working with children and adolescents of different ages
  • An empathetic, appreciative and patient attitude
  • A calm, balanced and resilient personality
  • Responsibility and reliability
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Willingness to participate in team meetings and training
  • Swiss German is an advantage

We offer

An open-minded and committed team, with an experienced and supportive day school management.

A supportive community, which is particularly concerned about the continuous development of our school and the necessary resources to provide young people with optimal care and learning conditions in our modern learning and working spaces.

We look forward to an open, heartfelt and committed personality, who with their pedagogical expertise contributes to ensuring that children and young people in our Day school feel comfortable, safe and secure.
