Full description

The University of Bern is renowned. With us, you enjoy the excellent reputation of an institution with a long tradition all over the world.

Tasks and objectives

Within the framework of teaching, the faculty is in particular responsible for the curriculum of studies in Christian Catholic theology. This mission is part of the context and collaboration with the Faculty of Theology and the other study courses offered there. Since the content of the teaching will also be offered in German two years after entry into office, the applicant must or acquire the German knowledge necessary for that purpose. The tasks associated with the faculty charge also include the acquisition of research funding as well as the supervision of bachelor/master's and thesis papers. In addition, the profile of this position includes international networking and collaboration within the framework of academic self-government.

The person appointed to this position must have obtained a degree of doctor (excellent statement) in the field of Christian Catholic theology/old Catholicism or a doctorate comparable with a historical profile. For an extraordinary faculty position, we also require an enabling thesis or equivalent qualification. We are looking for a committed person, ready to change his teaching and research profile. We would also welcome applications from individuals with academic didactic experience or equivalent qualifications. Named International Application and contact For your application, please send us a cover letter outlining your current research profile as well as your interest in the position you are looking for. In addition, we need your updated CV, which will give us an overview of your academic background and current activities. Please also send us a complete list of your publications and courses you have conducted so far, including the results of the evaluations. Your application must contain another essential element, namely a research concept established for the next five years and outlining your planned research activities. If applicable, you can add a brief representation of your authorization project. In addition, we ask you to give us a teaching concept that will describe your didactic approaches and your vision of teaching. If you have already requested external funding, we would be grateful if you could provide us with the relevant information. About us

The post of assistant professor with conditional pre-titularization or extraordinary professorship for the field of historical research and the history of old Catholicism must be provided from 1 August 2027 at the Faculty of Theology of the University of Bern, and more specifically at the Institute of Catholic-Christian Theology. In collaboration with the Chair of Systematic Theology and Ecumenism, the position of professor is responsible for the research and teaching of Catholic-Christian theology at the Faculty of Theology in Bern. The post of assistant faculty member with conditional pre-qualification will become an extraordinary faculty member, subject to meeting the required qualification criteria and after completion of the corresponding evaluations no later than six years after completion.
