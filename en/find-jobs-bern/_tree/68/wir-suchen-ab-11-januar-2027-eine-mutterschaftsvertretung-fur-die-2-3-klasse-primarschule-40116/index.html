Portrait Leissigen is a rural community located on the Thunersee. At our school, about 100 children in kindergarten and 4 primary school classes are taught by a dedicated college.

Tasks

17 children are looking forward to starting the new school year with you. Your fee is 27 lessons including KL lesson (without music). It is possible to divide the workload between a maximum of two teachers (application as a team). They work with experienced part-time teachers. You can actively shape our school.

Your working days are Monday and Tuesday morning and afternoon, Wednesday morning, Thursday morning and afternoon, Friday morning

They have a basic education (seminary teaching qualification for the primary level, Bachelor of Arts in Primary Education) or equivalent education. They enjoy working with children, are motivated, committed, independent and responsible. They are open to cooperation in the class team and in the college and pay attention to appreciative and communicative parental work.
