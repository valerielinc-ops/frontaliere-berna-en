You have gained years of experience in the pediatric practice, employed or in your own practice, and are looking for a new task with freedom of design? In a group practice in the Bernese Oberland you build up the pediatric and adolescent medicine according to your ideas, in the amount of 50-100%, with the certainty of a permanent job and mountains and lake in front of the door. Group practice is part of a practice group for basic care that is broadly networked in German-speaking Switzerland. A well-established family doctor team is working on site, and paediatrics and adolescent medicine is now being rebuilt. As the first pediatrician or pediatrician at the location, you shape the pediatric offer from the beginning. Experienced paediatric colleges are at your side for the exchange: inside other locations of the group. The practice group takes over the entire administration, so that you can concentrate on your patients and the setup. The practice is located in the Bernese Oberland, in a mountain village on the shore of a turquoise lake, surrounded by peaks well over 2000 meters. Here you work where others spend their holidays: In summer, hikes attract to waterfalls, shipping, swimming and sailing, in winter ski resorts and snowshoe tours close by. Interlaken and the Jungfrauregion are within reach, and by train you are in Bern or Lucerne in about an hour.

Their tasks

  • Self-responsible development and management of child and adolescent medicine at the site
  • Comprehensive outpatient care for children and adolescents and advice for parents and families
  • Close cooperation with the family doctor team on site
  • Professional exchange with paediatric colleges: inside other locations of the group
  • Participation in practical development and participation in quality programmes

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in paediatric medicine, acquired in Switzerland or Germany
  • Several years of experience in outpatient pediatrics, as a practice owner:in or employed in a practice
  • Joy of building, experience in practice organization and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Sovereign, empathetic attitude towards children, parents and team, very good knowledge of German
  • A MEBEKO recognition is not a prerequisite: Medimeo accompanies you through the procedure and supports you with advice and financial support

Their advantages

  • Build your own pediatrics, without investment and without entrepreneurial risk
  • Professional exchange with experienced pediatric colleges: inside the group, especially in the start-up phase
  • Attractive salary with turnover share: Your construction will pay directly for you
  • Pension fund including super-compulsory: The wage share above the legal minimum is also insured, the employer pays with
  • Continued pay in the event of illness 90% instead of the usual 80% in Switzerland
  • Congress fees, FMH membership and professional liability insurance are covered by the employer
  • No administration: Wage, patient and insurance bills are handled by the practice group
