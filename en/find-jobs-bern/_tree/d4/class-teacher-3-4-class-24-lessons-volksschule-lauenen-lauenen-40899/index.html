Full description

Portrait The school Lauenen is a family village school in the middle of an idyllic mountain landscape at 1250 m above sea level. As a multi-class school, we promote age-mixed learning. Together we shape the further development of our school and accompany the children on their individual educational path. We create an appreciative learning environment in which they can develop professionally and personally. You can get an insight into our school at www.schule-lauenen.ch. From school year 2027/2028 we are looking for a committed class teacher at the 3./4. of about 80%. The mission includes the subject areas German, mathematics, NMG, music, BG, sports and a rotation lesson.

Tasks

Our school is in an exciting development process. Together as a team, we continue to shape our school profile and promote individual learning through cross-class projects. They bring a positive and open attitude as well as sound professional skills. In doing so, the child and his or her individual development are at the center of their pedagogical action.

Your responsibilities include in particular

  • You take over the class leadership and teach the 3rd / 4th class as a class teacher in the scope of 24 lessons.
  • They teach the faculties German, Mathematics, NMG, Music, Visual Design (BG), Sport and a rotation lesson.
  • The remaining part of the course is taken over by the teacher already working in the class with basic pedagogical knowledge.
  • They maintain a close cooperation with the part-time teacher, the teachers of the kindergarten and the entire college.
  • They make cooperation with parents open and constructive.
  • They are involved in comprehensive school events and projects and are open to new ideas and developments.
  • You take on tasks at school and level as part of your professional assignment.

Requirements

They have a bachelor’s degree for the primary level and ideally bring their first professional experience. New entrants and lateral entrants with corresponding experience and completed training modules are also welcome. Do you appreciate working at a small school, like to take responsibility for your own class and feel comfortable in age-mixed learning settings? Do you enjoy working in a team and at the same time appreciate the opportunity to implement your own ideas? Then the school Lauenen could be your new place of activity! Apply for our 3/4 now. Class! With us, you can expect an environment in which you can contribute your pedagogical strengths and actively shape everyday school life.

We offer

  • A permanent employment in accordance with cantonal guidelines.
  • Professional support of school development by PHBern.
  • A dedicated team with appreciative and cooperative cooperation.
  • A family school environment with a pleasant working atmosphere, age-mixed learning and lived integration as well as space for own ideas and participation.
  • Support from the school committee, especially in the search for suitable accommodation.
