Full description

Freestar-Informatik AG has been operating successfully on the Swiss market since 1998. Through continuous growth and expansion of core competencies, Freestar-Informatik AG offers its customers a versatile IT service and consulting portfolio. For our customers, we are constantly looking for IT specialists and executives. In doing so, we attach the highest importance to ensuring that applicants, customers and jobs fit together optimally. For our customer, the Swiss state railway company, we are looking for a team-oriented, responsible and independent personality as soon as possible.

  • Receiving, transmitting and correcting malfunction messages
  • Answering questions from end users as well as responsibility for the tickets created
  • Capture, classify, prioritize and assign tickets in the required quality in the Service-Now Ticketing System
  • Processing of orders and management of access authorizations
  • Assistance in the continuous improvement process of the service desk
  • Completed EFZ training preferably in the computer science field
  • First experience in the service desk environment and in IT service management with service now
  • Knowledge of the MS-Office range and ticketing tools is desirable
  • Willingness to shift work is an advantage (7x24 Service Desk)
  • Knowledge of German level C2 as well as knowledge of French and/or Italian level B2 mandatory
  • Self-employed field of activity
  • Option on home office after familiarization
  • Young and dynamic team
  • Job as of March 2026 or as of now
