The staff, our task

The Swiss Federal Personnel Office (EPA) is responsible for the personnel policy of the Federal Administration. It develops the foundations and tools to successfully manage and develop human resources in departments and administrative units. It defines the personnel strategy, develops the federal personnel law, controls the nationwide personnel resources, provides an education and training offer and operates the centrally controlled HR informatics.
