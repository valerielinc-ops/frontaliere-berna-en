Full description

The RTS is looking for a host collaborator.

Your Mission

Within the host team, you have the following responsibilities:

  • Reception and orientation: to welcome visitors, collaborators, suppliers and providers, to provide telephone reception and to direct the interlocutors to the relevant services.
  • Access and badge management: ensure visitor registration, distribution and retrieval of badges, keys and other means of access, in accordance with security procedures.
  • Administrative management and communication: handling calls, e-mails and requests addressed to the reception, following up and forwarding information to the relevant interlocutors.
  • General Services and Logistics Support: Receive mail and parcels, manage found items, track consumables orders, and ensure the reception area is properly maintained.
  • Monitoring and proper operation of the site: performing visual checks of common spaces, identifying possible anomalies, reporting response needs and collaborating with technical and security services.
  • Coordination and continuous improvement: collaborate with the various services of the RTS, contribute to the improvement of reception services and participate in ad hoc projects related to the operation of the site.

Your profile

  • SWCs for trade employees, customer relations officers, retail workers or training deemed equivalent.
  • Professional experience in hospitality, reception, general services, operational support or hotel environment.
  • Experience in a multi-service or public-friendly environment is an asset.
  • Interest in media and public service mission of RTS.
  • Mastery of oral and written French. Knowledge of a second national language or English is an asset.
  • Flexibility and availability: ability to adapt to the needs of the service and to ensure timely replacements, including in the event of an unexpected absence of a colleague.

What we offer you

  • A stimulating and varied working environment within a public service media company.
  • A versatile function, at the heart of the daily life of the RTS, with regular contacts with many interlocutors.
  • A dynamic and collaborative team, where the spirit of self-help and the quality of service are essential.
  • Diverse responsibilities that offer autonomy, initiative and development opportunities.
  • Attractive working conditions within a recognised institution, attentive to the well-being of its employees.
  • The opportunity to make a concrete contribution to the proper functioning of the infrastructure and to the quality of the reception of our sites.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
