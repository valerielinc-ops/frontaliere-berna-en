Full description

Up to 300 people are catered for every day at the Mobilcity restaurant. The restaurant has 260 seats, plus another 70 seats in the courtyard and on the terrace. In the bistro, guests can either enjoy a table-service business lunch or a convivial tavolata, or they can help themselves to their food directly at the counter. With its wide range of options, Mobilcity Restaurant is also ideal for events, catering and functions. The events area, in particular, is to be expanded. At the same time, we fulfil our social responsibility as a culinary partner of Nanas Lunchbox. True to the ZFV philosophy, food is cooked fresh on site using regional, ecologically sustainably produced and fair-trade ingredients. Are you our new team member who will support us as Head Chef from 4 January 2027 or by arrangement? You will work 80–100% of full-time hours between Monday and Saturday. Depending on the rota, working hours are from 06:30 to 15:30 or from 13:00 to 22:00. Your menu of responsibilities:

  • Management and organisation of the entire kitchen area
  • Ensuring a high quality of food and actively participating in production
  • Further development of an inspiring, innovative and market-oriented range of products
  • Analysing guest and customer needs, as well as identifying and implementing relevant trends
  • Responsibility for purchasing, merchandise management and inventory, taking into account budget and profitability targets
  • Costing, formulation and maintenance of the inventory management system
  • Recruitment of new employees in collaboration with People & Culture
  • Managing, supporting and developing employees, as well as strengthening team building
  • Responsibility for administrative tasks as well as compliance with quality standards and ZFV guidelines
  • Ensuring sustainable and resource-efficient handling of food, materials, equipment and machinery
  • Embodying the ZFV leadership model "We make others successful" and fostering a culture of trust, courage, transparency and joy
  • Actively drive forward the vision and strategy, prioritise goals and measure their implementation, and further develop ZFV's sustainability and environmental strategy Your recipe for success:
  • Basic training as a cook: Swiss Federal Certificate of Proficiency (EFZ) and further training as a head chef with a Swiss Federal Certificate of Competence
  • 10 years of professional experience in the catering or hotel industry, as well as 5 years of management experience as a sous chef
  • In-depth knowledge of costing, inventory management and administrative processes
  • Good IT user skills in MS Office (especially Word and Excel)
  • Good knowledge of the german language (fluent in written and spoken)
  • Entrepreneurial and strategic thinking, as well as an agile, innovative way of working
  • Strong focus on goals and customers
  • Strong team spirit, willingness to learn and openness to change
  • Leadership skills with the ability to successfully lead and develop employees What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you will be nurtured. We work together and focus on everything that is beneficial. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 70,000 and CHF 83,200 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for the catering industry with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
