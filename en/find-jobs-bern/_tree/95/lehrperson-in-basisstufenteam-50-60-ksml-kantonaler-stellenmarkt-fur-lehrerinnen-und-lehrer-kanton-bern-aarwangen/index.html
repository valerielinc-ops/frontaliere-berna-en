Full description

Portrait Welcome to Aarwangen! The school Aarwangen is located in the Oberaargau in the agglomeration of Langenthal. We see ourselves as an integrative school. Around 540 children are taught in 27 age-mixed classes, of which five are kindergarten classes. Our school is run by a supportive school management team. The cooperation between the teachers is very much appreciated by everyone. All classes are supported by curative school teachers and DaZ teachers. In the school complex Sonnhalde are three school buildings, two kindergartens, the day school, the sports facilities, the swimming pool with teaching swimming pool and the hall. Three other kindergartens are located in the village. From the school year 2027/28 we also lead a basic level class in Schulhaus Ost.

Tasks

They teach the newly opened base level class with a weekly course of 14-16 lessons. The basic level team works closely with our specialist teachers (IF, DaZ, speech therapy). The function of class teacher is divided in the team. Together with your job partner, you set up the new basic level rooms, jointly design the timetable and adapt the internal concept. In addition, the class team will be accompanied for entry by the PH Bern.

Requirements

You have a corresponding level diploma or will soon complete it. Preferably, you complement the basic level team with your professional experience at level 1 / 2 class or at a basic level. They are a dutiful, communicative, committed and resilient personality and are accustomed to dealing with children, teachers and parents. They like to teach in the team and are interested in the model of the basic level.

We offer

  • Permanent employment with 14-16 lessons instruction and 2.5% function as a class teacher.
  • Design possibilities for the new basic level team in setup, concept & hour planning.
  • Professional accompaniment by PH Bern (first information on 30.10.26)
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a dedicated school house team
  • Support by competent specialist teachers, an experienced step team, as well as school social work on campus
  • Cheap location: 5 min. Walk time to the public transport connection Aarwangen Vorstadt, free parking available
