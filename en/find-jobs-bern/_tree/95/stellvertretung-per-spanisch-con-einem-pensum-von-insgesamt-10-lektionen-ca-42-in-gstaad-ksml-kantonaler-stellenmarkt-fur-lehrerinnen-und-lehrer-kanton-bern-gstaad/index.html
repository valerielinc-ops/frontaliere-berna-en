Portrait At Interlaken Gymnasium with its department in Gstaad, more than 450 students and 70 teachers learn and teach. This makes the Interlaken Gymnasium the smallest public school in the canton of Bern. We offer our student body from the extensive and expansive Oberland a wide range of specializations and supplementary subjects as well as a bilingual graduate degree. Our school is characterised by a personal atmosphere & a four-day week and we maintain diverse international relationships.
