Full description

In the Customer Center of PostFinance you are in direct contact with our customers and recognize their financial concerns. You support them with competent advice through various channels and provide your commitment to positive experiences. In doing so, you strengthen the connection of our customers with PostFinance and actively contribute to joint success in a dynamic environment.

You can do that.

  • You are the first contact person to professionally process the telephone and written inquiries of our customers regarding products and services
  • You recognize and use sales opportunities proactively by offering customers suitable products and services
  • With your empathetic, communicative and open nature, you inspire our diverse customers and create a positive customer experience.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic education or service experience
  • Enjoy telephone customer contact as well as ideal experience in customer service and sales
  • Excellent oral communication skills in German as well as in a second national language (French or Italian)
  • Secure application of multiple IT applications and communication tools (telephone, email, chat)
  • Ability to orient in a fast-paced environment with changing shifts and empathy for different customer needs
  • Willingness to have a 100% presence during the introductory phase of three to six months as well as the possibility of later adjustment of the employment level and home office activity by agreement
