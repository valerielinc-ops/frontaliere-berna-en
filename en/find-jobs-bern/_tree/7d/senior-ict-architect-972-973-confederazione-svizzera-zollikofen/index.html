Put to the Point

Are you an ICT architect with passion? You want to contribute your knowledge, experience and hands-on mentality to the BIT? As an ICT architect, you have the unique opportunity to navigate complex and specialized ICT architectures from the previous IT provider world into new business fields and to make a substantial contribution.

You can make this contribution

  • ICT architectures and services are planned efficiently, robustly and economically and implemented in agile teams
  • Define system boundaries, subsystems and interfaces and ensure the overall architectural picture, especially in the context of unbundling (iTASK)
  • Define, review and continuously develop architecture quality and governance
  • Develop, document and maintain reference, solution and system architectures and support the technology roadmap
  • Develop transition architectures, roadmaps and migration solutions, taking into account security standards and regulatory requirements
  • Advising management, projects and specialist/IT teams, transferring knowledge and actively shaping strategic development in architecture communities and committees

This makes you unique

  • University degree (bachelor/master) in engineering or economics, ideally supplemented by a certification in Enterprise Architecture (e.g. TOGAF)
  • Several years of experience as an ICT, solution or enterprise architect, preferably with experience in public organizations or IT service providers
  • In-depth knowledge in Enterprise Architecture as well as in the design and documentation of data, application, technology, security and network architectures
  • Experience with architecture governance, technology portfolios and roadmaps as well as agile methods such as Scrum and SAFe
  • Strong analytical and communication skills, coupled with problem-solving skills, consulting and negotiation skills and an independent and customer-oriented working method
  • Experience with modern technologies such as microservices, container management and DevOps
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages
