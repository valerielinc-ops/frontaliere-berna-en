Full description

For our customers in the banking environment in Bern, we are looking for an experienced business analyst to support a strategically important compliance and risk program.

Your tasks

  • Close cooperation with product managers and professional stakeholders to collect, analyze, validate and prioritize requirements
  • Analysis of complex banking and organizational processes as well as their comprehensible translation into technical and non-technical requirements
  • Identification and documentation of processes, relationships and problems in the compliance environment
  • Development of technical concepts and specifications for implementation
  • Definition and coordination of test cases as well as support for user acceptance tests (UAT)
  • Working in an agile environment according to SAFe principles

Your profile

  • (specialised) university degree, higher specialised examination or equivalent education
  • Extensive experience as a business analyst in complex transformation or compliance projects
  • Very good understanding of banking and organizational processes in the area of compliance
  • Sound knowledge in the field of anti-money laundering (AML)
  • Several years of experience in business analysis or a comparable role
  • Experience in collecting, analysing and documenting requirements
  • Correct repute (no enforcement and no criminal records)
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
