Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Digitally designing what moves people. You are not just looking for a job, but a task with meaning? Then you are right with us! For our ICT at the Burgdorf location, we are looking for a:n

Your sphere of activity We are expanding our ICT team and looking for dedicated support in the field of workplace engineering. In the role as ICT System Engineer Network, you ensure the stable operation and continuous development of our network Together with a well-established ICT team, specialists from medicine and care as well as external partners and manufacturers, you consistently develop our network and security landscape – sustainable, highly available and secure, as a reliable foundation for everyday clinical life. Everyday work is versatile, demanding and makes an important contribution to the smooth functioning of our hospital.

  • Operation, maintenance and further development of our entire network infrastructure (LAN, WLAN, WAN, perimeter) based on HPE Aruba, Alcatel-Lucent and FortiGate
  • Ensuring a high-availability, high-performance and secure network environment for 24/7 hospital operations
  • Implementation of changes and maintenance windows (evenings, marginal times, weekends)
  • Development, planning and consistent implementation of new network and security concepts, in particular zone and segmentation concepts, site-to-site VPN, wireless design and firewall policies
  • Self-responsible management of technical sub-projects from analysis to design to rollout incl. Coordination of internal stakeholders and external partners
  • Troubleshooting, clean monitoring and maintenance of up-to-date, robust documentation
  • Active monitoring of the market and bringing new technologies and solutions (e.g. SD-WAN, NAC, ZTNA, automation) into our roadmap
  • Experience with certificates from a Microsoft PKI and public CAs, including integration into applications, web servers, firewalls, VPN and 802.1X
  • Close cooperation with the adjacent infrastructure and application teams. You understand their world, but consciously carry the network responsibility

What distinguishes you

  • Completed computer science training (EFZ, HF/FH or equivalent)
  • Several years of experience in the Windows client environment with in-depth know-how in the enterprise network
  • In-depth practical experience with FortiGate firewalls (certification NSE 4 or higher beneficial) as well as HPE Aruba Switching and Wireless (ACMA/ACMP, ACSA/ACSP beneficial)
  • In-depth knowledge of certificates, switching, routing, WLAN architectures, VLAN design, site-to-site VPN as well as firewalling and perimeter security
  • In-depth knowledge of certificate management. From issuance via internal Microsoft PKI (AD CS) to procurement and management of public certificates (e.g. for web services, VPN and WLAN)
  • Experience in the design and implementation of zone concepts and network segmentation in complex, regulated environments
  • Solid understanding of the adjacent infrastructure (server, virtualization, directory services, client/medical connection). You think comprehensively and recognize connections beyond the network plate edge
  • Experience in managing (sub)projects. You plan cleanly, communicate clearly and bring topics to a binding conclusion
  • Strong security awareness. Especially in a sensitive hospital environment with patient data and business-critical applications
  • Readiness to take on Pikett missions for a total of four weeks per year

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
