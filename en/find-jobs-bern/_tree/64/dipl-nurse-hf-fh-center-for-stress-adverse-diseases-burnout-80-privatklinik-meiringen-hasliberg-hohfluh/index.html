Full description

The Meiringen Private Clinic is located in the impressive landscape of the Haslital, in the Bernese Oberland. Here people with all forms of psychological suffering find reception, treatment and care. The third-largest psychiatric clinic in the Canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient primary psychiatric care of the eastern Bernese upper country. Care and care take place according to current medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge.

As a member of Swiss Leading Hospitals, Privatklinik Meiringen is one of the leading private clinics in Switzerland.

The team of the Au Soleil station at the Hasliberg site serves people with fatigue syndrome and stress-related diseases who require extensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment. This service mandate is based on the latest scientific findings in an individually defined treatment and care process. Psychosocial reintegration is considered a priority treatment goal.

Their tasks

  • Implementation of patient-oriented professional care in the context of the care process and caregiver work
  • Ensuring a high quality of care in the sense of best practice
  • Active participation in the treatment process in interprofessional cooperation
  • Participation in interdisciplinary treatment meetings
  • Participation in improving quality and process flows
  • Accompaniment and guidance of students/learners in day-to-day care

  • Completed degree at HF level or equivalent SRC recognition in foreign diplomas
  • Professional experience in psychiatry is beneficial
  • Great commitment and much professionalism
  • High service orientation towards patients
  • Situational communication skills
  • Self-employed, responsible and resilient personality
  • Precise and goal-oriented working methods
  • Collegial and social team behaviour
  • Enjoyment in a varied field of work and activity
