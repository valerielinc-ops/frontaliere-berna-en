Linux System and Automation Engineer (m/f/d) Test Environment 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Linux System and Automation Engineer (m/f/d) Test Environment Bern / Work Smart, 01.02.2027, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You work in a team that strengthens the safety, availability and reliability of fleets and thus ensures a stable travel experience for customers. Focusing on customer information on vehicles, you actively shape the further development of a complex overall system and combine software and hardware into a reliable solution. Work Smart enables you to work regardless of location and a good balance between work and private life. In the team you will find short paths, a lot of creative leeway and perspectives for your professional development. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You are responsible for test automation for the overall customer information system on the vehicles and thus contribute to the quality, safety and availability of the fleets.
  • In the test laboratory, you implement conversions and maintenance and develop the infrastructure further so that hardware and software adaptations are detected and treated at an early stage.
  • You plan and perform tests in the laboratory to ensure the stability and quality of the overall system.
  • You will advise internal bodies and suppliers competently and ensure clear communication with the team and relevant stakeholders.
  • You will gladly take on conceptual tasks and continuously improve processes and results with a systematic approach.

You bring that with you.

  • Structured and solution-oriented working method, high level of initiative and independence with a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Teamwork and clear communication with different stakeholders as well as problem-solving skills and willingness to learn.
  • Practice in the automation of test, operation and deployment processes with tools such as Ansible, Cucumber and Xray.
  • In-depth Linux knowledge for system management and system analysis with Python, Git and Bash.
  • University degree in computer science or electrical engineering or comparable several years of professional experience as well as very good knowledge of German at least at level C1 and English at least at level B2. Knowledge of French or Italian is an advantage.
