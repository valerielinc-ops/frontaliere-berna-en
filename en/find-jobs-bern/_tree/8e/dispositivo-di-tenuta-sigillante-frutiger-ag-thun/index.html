As a sealer you ensure that buildings are permanently protected from wind, water and other environmental influences. With various waterproofing systems, you contribute to making buildings energy-efficient, sustainable and comfortable. You often work on roofs and construction sites and are part of exciting construction projects. Your tasks

  • You install sealing systems made of bitumen, plastic and other special materials.
  • You prepare construction sites and set up your workplace safely and professionally.
  • You plan work, coordinate interfaces with other professions and check substrates.
  • You maintain and repair existing waterproofing systems and perform dismantling work.
  • With your work, you support the energy efficiency of buildings and the protection of our environment.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
