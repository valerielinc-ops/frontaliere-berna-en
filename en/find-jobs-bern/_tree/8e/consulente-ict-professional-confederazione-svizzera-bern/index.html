Put to the Point

Do you want to act as a link between the recipients and the various service providers? Become part of our collegial team, which is responsible for information and communication technology (ICT) in business processes and structures, future challenges and digitalization in command operations.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-19796
  • Place of work: Papiermühlestrasse 20, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Advise customers in command operations on all ICT topics and actively shape long-term strategies
  • Manage smaller ICT projects, participate and plan for larger ones, and budget the need for IT services (hardware and software)
  • Coordinate and implement ICT requirements, training and standards in cooperation with professional superusers
  • Ensure rapid and competent problem solving and ensure the availability of ICT resources in accordance with service level agreements and service contracts
  • Prepare, test and implement system changes (hardware and software) in accordance with applicable specifications

This makes them unique.

  • University of applied sciences or university degree (FH, ETH, University Bachelor/Master) towards computer science or comparable education
  • Computer science EFZ is an advantage
  • In-depth knowledge of requirements engineering as well as experience working with business analysis teams
  • Analytical and networked thinking as well as high customer and service orientation
  • Independent, targeted and quality-conscious working method
  • Strong communication skills, teamwork skills and good oral and written knowledge of a second official language
