Full description

Portrait The municipality of Jegenstorf with approx. 5800 inhabitants can be reached from Bern by public transport in just under a quarter of an hour. On foot you can reach the two school locations Gyrisberg and Säget within 5-10 minutes.

Currently, around 680 school-aged children are taught in 35 classes and the day school and school social work round off the offer of our school.

Tasks

At both locations, students from different classes come to you on Tuesday and Thursday between 10.15 and 11.50 a.m. and are looked after in a small group. The maximum group size is 8 students.

If desired, the lessons (2 x 4 L.) can also be taken by a teacher, this then on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday (each from 10.15-11.50 clock).

The Ruheinsel relieves both the students, the teachers and the class structure and has proven itself in a pilot phase.

Requirements

We are looking for someone who has a lot of experience in the school context and can respond to the different needs of the individual students. Quietness, pedagogical clarity and certainly a great deal of humor are of central importance.

We offer

  • An exciting work with students of cycles 1 and 2.
  • A task that is developing.
