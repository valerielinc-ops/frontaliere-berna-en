Full description

Directorate Human Resources

The University Psychiatric Center (UPZ) Bern stands for modern and high-quality psychiatric care. In order to attract the right specialists in a demanding job market, we design our recruitment professionally, in line with target groups and with a convincing employer appearance. In this versatile function, you combine recruiting with employer branding and actively shape the candidate journey from first contact to employment. You contribute your ideas, help with the further development of our recruiting measures and ensure that the UPZ is visible as an attractive employer.

Your tasks

  • You accompany application processes across all occupational groups and hierarchical levels and ensure a professional candidate experience
  • You will supervise our recruiting tool Umantis, support executives in the application and help to further develop our recruiting processes
  • You build talent pools, maintain them and target potential candidates
  • You implement personnel marketing measures and campaigns, supervise our career page and online employer profiles and thus strengthen the visibility of the UPZ
  • You select suitable recruiting channels and continuously develop our processes and measures based on key figures and feedback

Your profile

  • You have a completed basic education or study in business administration, psychology or a similar subject area. Training in HR, recruiting or marketing is an advantage
  • You bring in-depth experience in recruiting and applicant management and enjoy employer branding and personnel marketing
  • You are digitally affine and ideally have experience with applicant management systems and CMS
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented and keep track of several parallel tasks
  • You communicate in a stylish and addressable manner and enjoy working with different stakeholders
  • You are service-oriented, creative and proactive and like to bring your own ideas for further development
  • You have very good knowledge of German, English and French are an advantage

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
