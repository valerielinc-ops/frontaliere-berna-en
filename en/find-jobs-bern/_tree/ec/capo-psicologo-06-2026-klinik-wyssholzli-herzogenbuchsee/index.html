Klinik Wysshölzli is a specialized psychiatric and psychotherapeutic private clinic in Oberaargau (Herzogenbuchsee, BE). Job: Chief psychologist 06/2026. The clinic is on the hospital list of the Canton of Bern, has a performance contract with the cantonal health and welfare directorate and contracts with the health insurers. The location in Oberaargau, Canton of Bern, in a quiet location convinces with magnificent nature and proximity to Bern, Solothurn, Olten and Langenthal.
