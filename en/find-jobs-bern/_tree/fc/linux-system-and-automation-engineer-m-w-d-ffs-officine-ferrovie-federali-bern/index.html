Linux System and Automation Engineer (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Linux System and Automation Engineer (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027, limited to 1.5 years, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You contribute directly to the safety, availability and reliability of trains and rail vehicles. In this role, you design modern Linux infrastructures and automate system configurations based on GitOp’s principles in an environment strongly influenced by Operational Technology. Operational Technology includes the networked technical systems in vehicles and plants that enable safe and stable operation. You can expect a small agile team in which cooperation, knowledge transfer and personal responsibility are lived. Thanks to Work Smart, you can work at home or on the go, depending on your job. Professional development is part of everyday life and your ideas are heard. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As part of a small agile team, you are responsible for cloud systems and edge systems on vehicles, making a measurable contribution to the safety and availability of the fleet.
  • You manage the required infrastructures over the entire life cycle from evaluation to operation and decommissioning of individual software components.
  • You automate system configurations according to GitOps principles and use Infrastructure as Code methods.
  • You develop the monitoring and alarming further and ensure transparency about the condition and performance.
  • You advise internal bodies and suppliers and ensure communication with team and stakeholders.
  • You will gladly take on conceptual tasks and proceed in a structured and solution-oriented manner.

You bring that with you.

  • You work independently and solution-oriented and bring a high initiative into the cooperation with the team.
  • With your clear communication and your structured way of working, you can also keep track of complex situations.
  • Profound experience in the operation and engineering of Linux servers ideally with Red Hat Enterprise Linux.
  • Practice in the development of automation solutions for servers and networks preferred with Ansible and experience with Infrastructure as Code in a public cloud such as Amazon Web Services.
  • Very good scripting skills in Bash Python and Terraform.
  • A university degree with a technical orientation, such as computer science or an equivalent qualification, as well as very good knowledge of German at least C1 and good knowledge of English at least B2.
