Put to the Point

They are the deputy head of communication of the Office and at the same time responsible for digital communication and stakeholder communication of the Federal Office for Economic Supply (BWL) and Economic Supply (WL). You are also involved in risk and crisis communication. They work within the Federal Administration at the interface with the economy and make a direct contribution to coping with crises in the economic supply of the state.

  • Entry date: by immediate or by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20619

You can make this contribution

  • Fully deputy and support of the management Communication
  • Responsible for the conception, development, implementation and operational management of digital communication (internal and external communication)
  • Responsible for the conception, coordination and operational implementation of stakeholder communication of the Federal Office for Economic Supply (BWL) and the Economic Supply (WL)
  • Leading and coordinating projects, advising supervisors, working with external partners and working in internal and external working groups
  • Participation in crisis management and participation in crisis unit meetings of business administration and administration as well as coordination of the information flow in the context of crisis communication between all parties involved
  • Responsible for producing publications and assisting with answering media inquiries and organising events

This makes them unique.

  • Master's degree in communication sciences, further education in crisis communication or management beneficial
  • Several years of proven professional experience, preferably in a specialty of Land Economic Supply
  • Excellent knowledge of digital communication channels and tools as well as very good knowledge of digital tools and software (CMS, ESP, Microsoft 365, Adobe)
  • Strong ability to quickly develop communication content in form and content for different target groups
  • Flexibility, independence, resilience, high service and solution orientation
  • Very good knowledge of an official language and good knowledge of a second official language and knowledge of English

We secure the supply in case of shortages

The Federal Office for Economic Affairs is the national competence centre for questions of security of supply. In the event of shortages, it supports the private sector in securing the country’s supply of essential goods and services.
