Full description

You are a language talent - speak fluent French or Italian and German - like to organize and keep track of many parallel tasks? Would you like to start recruiting and actively shape Post’s recruitment? With you we will find the talents of tomorrow!

You can do that.

  • You independently manage recruitments, mainly in high volume recruiting (e.g. delivery, passenger transport and customer advice), and are the first point of contact for leaders with questions.
  • Together with the leaders, you sharpen the requirements profiles and plan the recruitment process and responsibilities.
  • You create job advertisements in French or Italian as well as in German, define the appropriate tender strategy and publish the advertisements on the appropriate channels.
  • When pre-selecting applications, you work closely with leaders and make sure they invite the right people to an interview. The leaders then lead the conversation without your accompaniment.
  • In our talent pools and databases you actively search for suitable profiles and include them in the selection process.
  • As an ambassador: in the post you provide a positive candidate experience and a professional recruitment process.

You bring that with you

  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in French or Italian and have good knowledge of German (at least B2).
  • Thanks to your professional experience, it is easy for you to advise different stakeholders competently and service-oriented.
  • You are an organizational talent, keep track of several parallel tasks and set the right priorities.
  • With your winning nature, you convince your counterpart, gladly take on responsibility and actively participate in the team.
  • You are digitally affine, enjoy working with new applications (e.g. TEAMS, SharePoint, etc.) and find your way around in different IT systems.
  • You meet changes and new ideas with openness and you enjoy developing solutions together.

Location-independent work and home office are possible, 2-3 days (depending on the time) presence in Bern (Wankdorf) are required.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
