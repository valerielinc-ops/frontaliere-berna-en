Full description

Portrait School Stocken-Höfen – at the foot of the Stockhorn, Learning together, living together, speaking together, strong together.

The school Stocken-Höfen is attended by around 100 students in 5 multi-year classes at two locations. Cycle 1 is taught in Niederstocken, Cycle 2 in Hofen. The college consists of around 20 motivated teachers.

We are looking for a dedicated, experienced class help for Kindergarten Niederstocken as of 1 February 2027.

Tasks

As a class help, you support the kindergarten teacher in everyday school life. The activity is not linked to educational training.

Their responsibilities include

  • Supervision of children during group or individual work
  • Accompaniment of individual children during transitions (e.g. change to gym classes)
  • Assistance with everyday processes such as arrival, wardrobe, bus companionship
  • Administrative support, e.g. material preparation
  • Assistance with project weeks and excursions

Requirements

  • Reliability and responsibility
  • Joy of working with children
  • Experience in care, school or day structures
  • Flexibility in dealing with changing situations

We offer

  • An appreciative team and a clear introduction
  • Meaningful activity in school everyday life
  • Modern working environment
  • Opportunity to help shape the school
  • Free parking
  • Public transport
