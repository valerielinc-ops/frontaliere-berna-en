This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You will play an active role in shaping the further development of our process landscape and process governance. Together with the specialist departments, you will critically review existing workflows, drive improvements in process and quality management, and ensure more efficient processes and an improved customer experience.

Your area of responsibility

  • Together with the specialist departments, you will further develop the process governance and process architecture of BKW Power Grid and promote the clear definition and consistent application of existing process roles, standards and structures
  • You will analyse existing end-to-end processes, identify potential for improvement and develop solutions that simplify workflows, increase efficiency and improve the customer experience
  • You will further develop the continuous improvement process (CIP) using proven methods and promote a culture in which improvements are embedded for the long term
  • Together with the specialist departments, you will define meaningful process metrics (KPIs), review the effectiveness of implemented measures and identify any further need for action
  • In cross-functional projects, you will bring together different perspectives, facilitate workshops and support Process Owners and teams in implementing changes
  • In quality management, you will plan and conduct internal audits, support improvement measures and review their long-term effectiveness

What you bring

  • Completed university degree in business, engineering or natural sciences, or comparable training
  • Several years of experience in process and quality management and ideally knowledge of the energy sector
  • Sound knowledge and practical experience in process analysis, process governance and optimisation methods such as Lean Management, Six Sigma and the continuous improvement process (CIP)
  • Experience in cross-functional projects, workshop facilitation and collaboration with different specialist departments
  • Analytical, conceptual and solution-oriented way of working, as well as preferably experience with internal audits
  • Initiative, a desire to shape things, holistic thinking, as well as very good German and good French language skills
