Full description

The canteen at the Gymnasium Neufeld in Bern has 260 seats and a garden terrace with 60 seats. The offer in the Mensa Gymnasium Neufeld includes three different menus as well as a free choice buffet every lunchtime. True to the ZFV philosophy, we cook fresh on site with regional, ecologically sensibly produced and fair-trade food. Are you our new team member who will support us as a buffet/office employee as of December 1, 2026 or by appointment? You will work in an approx. 30% workload from Monday to Friday from 11:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. The position is limited from December 1, 2026 to May 31, 2027. Your task menu:

  • Serving the menus during the lunch service
  • Washing of dishes, cutlery and all kitchen utensils
  • Operation of the cash register during off-peak hours
  • Disposal and waste separation
  • Maintenance and care of the machines in the washing-up room
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the working environment
  • Completion of general clean-up and cleaning work
  • Implementation and assurance of quality standards and ZFV guidelines (hygiene and occupational safety, service, etc.) Your recipe for success:
  • Experience gained in the gastronomy or hotel industry
  • Experience in the washing-up room is an advantage
  • Oral German language skills
  • Goal-oriented and customer-oriented
  • Winning and charming charisma
  • This is what we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 52,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary).
