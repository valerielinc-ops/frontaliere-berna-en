Full description

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of psychosomatic, internistic-oncological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Solothurn, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the mission statement of “REHA competence” with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, warmth, attention and competence.

You are an organizational talent, keep an overview even in hectic situations and appreciate a versatile activity with responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us! To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and service-oriented personality who supports our management in the varied daily routine of the clinic and actively shapes the further development of our clinic.

Their tasks

  • Administrative and organisational support of management
  • Planning and coordination of meetings, visits and events
  • Maintenance of the clinic website and creation and updating of communication means
  • Managing our management system (IMS) and ensuring the corporate design
  • Organisation and administration of internal and external training
  • Editing of our employee magazine “Föhngflüschter” as well as other administrative tasks in the personnel area

Our expectations

  • Commercial training or further education as assistant director:in
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function
  • Independent, structured and forward-looking working method
  • Strong organisational talent, flexibility and service orientation
  • Strength of communication, stylish German and absolute discretion
  • Very good MS-Office knowledge and enjoyment of digital applications

Our offer

  • Multifaceted and responsible work with personal responsibility and scope for design
  • Close cooperation with the management and insight into exciting clinical projects
  • Family working atmosphere with flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Collegial and dedicated team with a lively du-culture
  • Attractive employee offers, discounted food and a wide range of further training opportunities
  • Unique workplace in the beautiful mountains of the Haslital
