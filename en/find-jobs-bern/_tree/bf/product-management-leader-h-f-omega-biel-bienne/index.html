Full description

Introduction of society

OMEGA is a prestigious watch brand worldwide known for its quality, precision and innovation requirements for over 170 years. Our watches are among the most beautiful in the world and are characterized by their state-of-the-art technology and timeless style.

Over the course of our long history, we have broken records of precision, redefined sporting timing and maintained our position at the cutting edge of Haute Horlogerie.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams on almost every continent with their talents, ambitions and inspirations.

Description of post

  • Ensure the operational leadership of the Product Management team, coordinate its activities and support Product Managers in their daily lives;
  • Piloting the portfolio of Montres projects, ensuring an overall vision of its progress and ensuring the proper management of priorities;
  • Support and challenge Product Managers in the development of their products, from project definition to industrialization;
  • Identify and anticipate risks, dependencies and bottlenecks, and coordinate actions necessary to resolve them;
  • Ensure the quality and reliability of Product Management reports to the Head of Product and the Product Committee;
  • Maintain and develop a high level of operational excellence within Product Management through processes, tools, working methods and monitoring standards;
  • Represent Product Management and ensure close collaboration with the Technical Bureau, Industrialization, Quality and Supply Chain;
  • Actively participate in the various coordination bodies related to product development: monitoring of approvals, industrial submissions, quality monitoring, industrialization and other key milestones;
  • Manage, support and develop Product Managers, promote their autonomy and contribute to a demanding, collaborative and results-oriented team dynamic.

Profile

  • Experience in Product Management and Product Development, ideally in a demanding industrial environment;
  • Diploma from a high school of management, engineering or training deemed equivalent;
  • Good understanding of the development and industrialization processes of a product and ability to collaborate effectively with multidisciplinary teams.

Qualifications

  • Natural leadership, with the ability to accompany, challenge, develop and unite a team;
  • Excellent organizational and prioritisation capabilities to maintain a global vision while remaining close to operational challenges;
  • High capacity for analysis, problem solving and decision-making;
  • At ease in a demanding and under pressure environment, with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously while remaining structured, pragmatic and oriented solutions;
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to interact with both operational teams and governance bodies;
  • A sense of responsibility, initiative and strong focus on delivery and results.

Languages

  • Perfect mastery of French and English;
  • German is an asset.
