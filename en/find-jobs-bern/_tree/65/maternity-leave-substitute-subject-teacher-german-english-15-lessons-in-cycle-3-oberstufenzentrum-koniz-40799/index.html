Full description

Portrait The Oberstufenzentrum Köniz is located in a beautiful, central location. At the site, around 250 SuS are currently taught in 12 classes in the Manuel model. At the beginning of March 2027 we are looking for maternity leave

Tasks

You teach a set of 15 lessons on two sevenths and one eighth. Class in German and English: 7a (second) > 5 lessons in German 7e (SpezSek) > 4 lessons English (of which 2 lessons in half class) 8a (second) > 5 lessons German (of which 1 lesson IVE) The deputy can be taken over 50% from now, probably 100% from January 11. Working days: Monday morning, Tuesday, Friday morning

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have completed training for cycle 3 or are in training as a teacher.
  • You are a communicative, team capable and dutiful personality.
  • Working with the children is your focus; you accompany and promote them effectively.
  • You enjoy working in a dedicated, thinking and helpful team.
  • You are committed to good parental work and actively participate in school projects (project weeks, winter camp, school festival).
  • Flexibility on the date of employment (may vary due to maternity leave)

We offer

  • Temporary employment according to cantonal employment conditions (LAG)
  • Support for induction by experienced college
  • Pleasant working environment in a helpful, committed and motivated team
  • Classrooms with modern infrastructure
  • Excellent public transport connection: The school is 13 minutes' drive from Bern Train Station.
