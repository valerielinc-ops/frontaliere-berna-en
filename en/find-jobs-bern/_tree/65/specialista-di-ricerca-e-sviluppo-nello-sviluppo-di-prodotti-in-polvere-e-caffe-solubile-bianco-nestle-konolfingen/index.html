Be a force for good and grow through experiences that push you further

As a R&D Specialist for Powder Creamer Product Development and Soluble White Technology Development at the Nestlé Product Technology Centre Coffee , your mission is to lead product and technology development for the innovation and renovation of the soluble white category, shaping the future of powder creamers and supporting our factories with technical expertise. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with scientists, quality and manufacturing teams, marketing, application groups and factories, locally and internationally. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a R&D Specialist

  • Delivering competitive soluble powder products for coffee that meet consumer needs.
  • Leading the discovery of innovative concepts, products, processes and equipment solutions that shape the future of powder creamers and soluble white technologies.
  • Deliver projects with with clear goals and milestones, that ensure food safety, regulatory compliance and on-time delivery
  • Turning ideas into reality by designing and running trials from laboratory and kitchen scale to pilot plant and factory scale, so innovations land right the first time.
  • Supporting factories and local market projects with practical solutions, anticipating technical challenges to keep projects on track.
  • Building alignment on key technical decisions and sharing knowledge across expert networks, sites and departments.

What will make you successful

  • Applying a scientific mindset to understand how processes and technologies define & deliver product properties.
  • Bringing strong expertise in soluble powder product development, powder structure and drying processes, and foaming properties
  • Engaging and influencing stakeholders at every level with confident, clear presentations.
  • Collaborating as a team player with a sharing mindset and a strong entrepreneurial spirit.
  • Communicating fluently in English; any additional language is a plus.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
