Full description

Portrait The school 2556 Schwadernau / Scheuren is located on the Aare and offers with its great change a lot of space for learning and discovery.

The children from Schwadernau and Scheuren attend the kindergarten and the primary level at our school together. In the school building Schwadernau two kindergarten classes and two 1st – 3rd classes lead. The Scheuren School House has two 4th – 6th classes at home.

Currently, around 110 students are accompanied by 19 teachers.

Tasks

As a class help, you accompany the children of the 1st – 3rd class on Wednesday from 10.15 to 11.45 in the swimming pool. They support the teacher during swimming lessons, help with the care of the children and help ensure that the swimming lessons take place safely, varied and in a pleasant atmosphere. You are an important support for the teacher and a valuable companion for the children.

Requirements

This is what you bring with you

  • Joy and skill in dealing with children from 1st to 3rd grade
  • Responsibility and reliability
  • Patience, empathy and a positive charisma
  • Joy of movement and the element of water
  • Good swimming knowledge and safety in water
  • Independent and team-oriented working method
  • Willingness to actively support the teacher during swimming lessons

We offer

In our small, family-run schoolhouse you can expect an open and appreciative school culture. A helpful team, a modern infrastructure with a screen and visualizer in each classroom and your own work equipment are at your disposal.

Have you become curious? We look forward to your application and to welcoming you to our team!
