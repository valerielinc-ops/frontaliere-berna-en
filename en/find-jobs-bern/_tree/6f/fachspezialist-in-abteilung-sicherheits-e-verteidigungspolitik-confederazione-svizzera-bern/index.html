Tackling new security challenges!

The State Secretariat for Security Policy (SEPOS) is the competence center of the Federal Department of Defence, Civil Protection and Sport (VBS) for security policy and information security. It shapes the federal security policy and ensures its coherence. SEPOS has four priorities for this purpose. It anticipates security policy changes and derives strategic options for action from them. It coordinates security policy instruments and coordinates national and international cooperation. The prerequisite for these activities is information security, which is guaranteed by SEPOS.
