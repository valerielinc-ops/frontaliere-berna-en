Full description

Are you interested in threat management and would you like to support employees and managers in challenging incidents? Are you enthusiastic about investigations, security and cooperation with authorities? In this function, you will work on threat cases and further develop threat and event management of logistics services. With you, we actively strengthen the safety culture and provide competent support in challenging situations.

You can do that.

  • You will handle threat management cases with a focus on Western Switzerland and support throughout Switzerland if necessary.
  • In this role, you will continue to operate and develop the “threat management” hotline and event management, bringing your expertise to bear.
  • You accompany employees and line managers in the event of incidents, advise security experts and support directly affected employees.
  • You document threat cases for internal reporting and law enforcement requests, ensuring compliance with relevant data protection regulations.
  • You will conduct training and awareness-raising activities on threat management and personal safety, actively strengthening the security culture.

You bring that with you

  • You have basic technical, logistical or commercial training with corresponding training in the other areas as well as training in threat management.
  • Several years of experience in event and emergency management are your strengths and practical knowledge at a comparable organization or in the environment of blue light organizations you also bring.
  • You have proven experience in threat management and dealing with aggressors, and a good knowledge of the legal environment.
  • In addition, you bring practical experience in investigations, research and investigations and convince through stress resistance, networked thinking and a solution-oriented and pragmatic way of working.
  • You are a communicative, open and assertive personality, advise competently and bring team orientation and organizational talent.
  • You have very good knowledge of a first national language. Any other national language is beneficial.

In order to promote linguistic and gender diversity (and due to the current team composition), we are particularly pleased about applications from women or people who speak French or Italian.
