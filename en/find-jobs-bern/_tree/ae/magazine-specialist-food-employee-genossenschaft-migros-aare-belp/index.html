Full description

Do you want to combine your sales talent with your coordination skills? Are you interested in warehousing and goods management and also like to communicate with our customers? As a magazine / employee of food in the 50/50 model, you are responsible for ensuring that the right products end up in the right place.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, MM Belp, Steinbachstrasse 28, 3123 Belp

Important information

Start of work 05:40 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You coordinate the warehouse management
  • You load and unload the trucks
  • You advise and serve our customers with pleasure and your expertise
  • An attractive presentation of goods, efficient goods management and ensuring availability are your focus
  • You support the departments 50/50 Magazin & Food with great commitment and dedication.

Requirements

  • Ideal professional experience: in the field of warehouse management & food
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales, you make shopping a positive experience
  • You have a good physical condition
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • German (good knowledge)
