Full description

Introduction

For our Quality & Sustainability Division, we are looking for a Data Analyst (M/F) whose main mission will be to collect data from the different information systems, make them reliable, process them and present them in relevant indicators.

Responsibilities

  • Collaborate with all Manufacturing entities to collect, consolidate and build up data
  • Analyze relevance and consistency of data and results produced
  • Use and manipulate complex databases to meet analytical needs
  • Return results in the form of clear and user-friendly charts
  • Ensure regular dissemination of indicators to the various stakeholders, including the Directorate
  • Contribute to the definition, deployment and monitoring of new indicators

Profile

  • Technical training at Master level or equivalent
  • Experience in handling and crossing large volumes of data
  • Ability to work independently and as a team, with a strong spirit of collaboration
  • Excellent listening capacity to understand and meet the needs of our customers
  • A rigorous, methodical person with an excellent synthesis spirit
  • Use of the Table Tool
  • Mastery of Microsoft 365 tools (Office, Teams, OneNote, Monday), and especially Excel
  • Knowledge of SAP, business intelligence tools (BI) and Power Query

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
