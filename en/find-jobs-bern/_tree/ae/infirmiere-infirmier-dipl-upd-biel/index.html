Your tasks

  • With your skills and interpersonal relationships, you contribute to high patient satisfaction

UPZ

The University Psychiatric Centre in Bern (UPZ) is the main centre of competence in psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region as a university clinic. It provides psychiatric care in the canton of Bern throughout the care chain, from early identification to outpatient treatment, day care and stationary treatment, to rehabilitation and forensic psychiatry. With more than 30 sites, UPZ offers a healthcare network covering the entire territory. The UPZ also makes an important contribution in the fields of education, training and research.
