Full description

We are taking PostFinance's customer service to a new level with intelligent, connected solutions. We develop modern services, automate processes and connect cloud-to-on-prim systems to make our chat and voicebots even more powerful.

As an embedded tester / test automation engineer in the Delivery Team Next Gen Bot, you and our developers are ensuring the continuous quality of our AI-based bot platform. What distinguishes us is a motivated interdisciplinary team that works together with confidence and at eye level across locations. We give you space to actively shape, try out new things and be brave.

Tasks

  • You work with developers to ensure the continuous quality of our product through to application integration
  • you design and conduct demanding tests for our application through to application integration based on requirements and risks and document test specifications and results in the corresponding test management tools
  • you automate regression tests for APIs, web applications and voicebot functionalities and continuously develop the existing test automation
  • you test language dialogs and voicebot functionalities and

Consider different languages, formulations and customer situations

  • you analyze, reproduce and document errors and accompany their repair together with developers: inside also across teams
  • you support with triage and management of bugs in Jira and work together with external teams and suppliers if necessary
  • you actively integrate the customer view as well as usability and quality aspects into the team and engage in the community of practice of the Embedded Tester

Requirements

  • Well-founded experience in software, testing automation in complex and distributed system landscapes
  • experience with integration and regression tests and testing interfaces to connected systems
  • experience in testing AI-based applications or interest in new test approaches for GenAI and LLM-based systems
  • experience in test automation, API, web and voice testing; knowledge in STEP as an advantage
  • experience in professional test management and structured test documentation; knowledge in HP Quality Center as an advantage
  • good knowledge in STEP

technical understanding of REST-Services and cloud and on-prem systems; knowledge of voicebot technologies as a plus - structured, analytical and quality-oriented working methods with a high level of initiative and joy in working together in an international and interdisciplinary team - excellent German and English skills; knowledge of other national languages as an advantage
