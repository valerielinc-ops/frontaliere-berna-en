Full description

We offer caring and professional care and care around the clock in our three nursing homes Oranienburg, Belvoir and Altenberg as well as the senior residence Villa Sarepta. The proximity to the city and the Salem Hospital as well as the own medical team make our houses the first choice for pleasant and safe living in old age. Diaconis offers people with different needs a home and welcomes guests for holidays and transitional stays at any time. We search immediately or by agreement

Pool staff (all training levels) 10 - 60%

Your tasks with us Work flexibly and at the same time have enough time for your private life? With our care and support pool you design your assignments to fit your everyday life. Are you motivated, spontaneously deployable and appreciate varied working days? Then we look forward to meeting you.

  • Depending on your education and experience, you will take on professional and appreciative care as well as care of our residents in our houses for living and care. The needs and well-being of people are at the center of your actions at all times.
  • As an employee in the care pool, you work flexibly at different locations or support teams during temporary assignments, for example in the event of staff shortages or longer-term absences.

Here's what you bring with you

  • Training as a Dipl. Nurse, as FaGe or AGS, SRK
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Appreciative attitude
  • Social and self-competence
  • High load capacity and flexibility
  • Rapid understanding of new tasks and a careful working style

Your advantages with us

  • You design your working days and workload flexibly according to your possibilities
  • You specify your available days of use yourself
  • Mind. 6 weeks of holidays plus 9 guaranteed holidays
  • Flexible annual working hours
  • Centrally located and modern workplace in a beautiful location in the city of Bern
  • Various discounts and offers from partner companies
