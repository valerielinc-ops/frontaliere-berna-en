Put to the Point

Are you passionate about media and digital communication? Then design with us the media-friendly and target group-oriented communication of the command Cyber! In your exciting everyday work, you produce content for various communication channels – from social media to internal information platforms to print products. You create text, images, videos and graphics for training, further education, events, TechTalks and much more. You ensure that our Internet and intranet sites remain up-to-date, create visual content and implement creative ideas skilfully. You work routinely with the Adobe Creative Cloud as well as with camera, microphone, tablet and other modern media technology. Become part of our team and actively shape the digital presence of Command Cyber!

You can make this contribution

  • Pick up the needs of customers and lecturers and independently develop solutions
  • Manage social media channels, make content suggestions and create photo, video and graphic content
  • Maintain intranet and Internet sites in the three national languages
  • Learning to take responsibility in a team

Diversity, dynamism, perspectives – teaching professions of the Swiss Army

The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
