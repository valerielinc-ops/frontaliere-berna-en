Put to the Point

The Department for the Prevention of Noncommunicable Diseases manages, monitors and coordinates measures for the prevention of noncommunicable diseases, addiction and mental health. The associated Section of Enforcement and Data Systems is responsible for implementing the Cancer Registration Act (KRG), the Narcotics Act (BetmG) and the Tobacco Products Act (TabPG). In doing so, the section ensures legally compliant and efficient execution, continuously develops the associated data systems and ensures their reliable operation as a basis for effective task fulfillment.
