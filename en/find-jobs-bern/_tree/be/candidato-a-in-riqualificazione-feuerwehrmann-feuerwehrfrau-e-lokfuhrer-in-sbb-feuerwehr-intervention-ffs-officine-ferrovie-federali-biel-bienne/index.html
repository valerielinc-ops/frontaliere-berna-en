You can look forward to that.

At 15 locations in Switzerland, the SBB Fire Brigade Intervention with 350 dedicated employees and modern emergency vehicles ensures fast and safe event management. At the same time, we are railway specialists, train drivers and firefighters and take care of the safety of our customers around the clock. Through your cooperation, you help ensure that rail traffic runs smoothly again as quickly as possible. As part of disruption management, Intervention SBB supports public emergency organisations and is actively involved in event prevention. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits and more information about the profession can be found here. You can move that.

  • In the event of an alarm as well as during exercises, you as a train driver move out with the extinguishing and rescue train with your team and ensure the safety of our customers.
  • In the event of incidents that affect rail traffic, you are jointly responsible for the protection of people, animals, the environment and railway infrastructure.
  • On site, you will ensure the safety of all travelers, fight fires on and off the rails, provide oil and chemical defense and technical assistance.
  • You regularly train the various workflows and techniques to ensure smooth event management at all times. You are in use in the 7x24 layer model.

You bring that with you.

  • Thanks to your high sense of responsibility and security, you understand the dependencies and effects on your team and your work. With us, togetherness and community in the team count, on which you can rely at any time. This includes enjoying working independently and being physically and psychologically resilient.
  • You have a completed vocational apprenticeship with a federal certificate of ability (EFZ). You already have the ticket for B100 locomotives or are ready to complete the corresponding internal training.
  • You have respiratory protection, experience as a firefighter and ideally also the C1 118 test.
  • The willingness to provide shift service in the 7 x 24h model on site is a matter of course for you.
  • In the German language you are confident of negotiation (verbal and written level C1) and you are ready to complete a French diploma at level A1+
  • In the application process, we will ask you for a current criminal record and enforcement record (not older than 3 months). The document can be ordered at the invitation for personal acquaintance.
