The heart of your work

In your role as HR Service Specialist (m/f) 60-100% you are responsible for:

  • Ensure full support of the employee life cycle, from recruitment to exit.
  • Responsibility for the wage processing of a defined employee portfolio, including processing the variables and preparing the data for the payment.
  • Assuming the management of absences and supporting the social insurance (reporting, tracking, coordination and monitoring of cases).
  • Ensure the quality and reliability of HR data in internal systems.
  • Preparation of reports and statistics for the financial department (HR Controlling).
  • Conduct reconciliation in cooperation with the accounting department.
  • Support executives in the preparation and follow-up of HR conversations as well as ensuring smooth onboarding and offboarding.
  • Active participation in HR projects, especially in the optimization of processes, tools and systems.

What makes our hearts beat faster

  • Commercial education, supplemented by a certificate as HR specialist HRSE and/or by the Swiss Federal Professional Card in Human Resources (Option A).
  • More than 3 years of experience in HR administration and in wage processing.
  • Process orientation and pronounced awareness of detail, in-depth HR expertise as well as very good priority and appointment control.
  • Digital skills or knowledge in the field of artificial intelligence in HR are a real plus.
  • A reliable, solution-oriented, proactive and independent personality with a great interest in administrative tasks and continuous improvement.
  • Very good knowledge in MS Office, HR software (Abacus) and time recording systems (OnTime).
  • Ideally bilingual (French/German), alternatively native language competence in one of the two languages and at least level B2 in the other. English at least level B2.
