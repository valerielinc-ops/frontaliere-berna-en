Full description

They take care of outpatient emergencies in the field of orthopaedics, general and visceral surgery, urology and ENT and, under the supervision of cadre doctors, learn to perform small emergency procedures such as nasal tamponades, wound care, drainage inserts (puncture/thoracic drinagen) and repositions. The shock room management as well as the concomitant care of the orthopaedic, surgical and urological bed stations in case of emergency incidents, e.g. at night, under supervision of appropriate cadre doctors, are also included. Operations also take place seasonally in the emergency center location Zweisimmen. If interested, a rotation onto the orthopaedic bed station can also take place incl. Assistance in the office hours and in the operating room.
Job starts on 1 January 2027 or by appointment. Tasks: • Foreign year or half-year of outpatient interdisciplinary emergency, e.g. suitable for prospective family doctors • Possibility of rotation on an explicit orthopaedic station • Possibility of recognition of 1 year of clinical emergency medicine for further training SGNOR • Recognised training centre for orthopaedics/traumatology, orthopaedic surgery and musculoskeletal traumatology, category B1 (2 years of orthopaedics and 3 years of traumatology) • Collegial working atmosphere • Flexible holiday planning with the option of additional holidays • Versatile local recreation area in the immediate vicinity of the workplace Requirements • Completed medical studies (Switzerland or EU with MEBEKO recognition) • Professional and social skills for good interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation • Style confident in the use of the German language (word and script), at least C1 A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a wide range of activities in the family environment, you can also expect the possibility of flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region.
