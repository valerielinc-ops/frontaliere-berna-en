Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population? In the Neurological Clinic (recognized C) more than 7000 patients are examined every year on an outpatient basis. The neurological departments include a Stroke Unit, a Memory Clinic, a clinical study center and a neuropediatric consultation. In cooperation with neurological practices and the nearby university clinic, specialists can contribute and deepen their experience in the entire field of outpatient, inpatient and highly specialized neurology.
