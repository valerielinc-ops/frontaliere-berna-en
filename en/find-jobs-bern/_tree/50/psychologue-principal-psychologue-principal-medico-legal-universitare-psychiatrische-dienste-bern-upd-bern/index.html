University Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The University Psychiatric Center Bern is involved in the ambulatory of the University Hospital for Forensic Psychiatry and Psychology for a research-based, effective and socially relevant treatment of people in forensic contexts. The interdisciplinary team combines clinical psychiatry, psychology, law and social sciences to develop new pathways in diagnostics, therapy and relapse prevention. The aim is to promote the mental health, self-responsibility and social reintegration of patients in the long term – and thus make an important contribution to public safety and modern forensic psychiatric care.

Their tasks

  • You take over the leadership of the psychological team and are responsible for guidance, leadership, training and supervision
  • They are responsible for the implementation of psychological-psychotherapeutic individual and group therapies within the framework of judicially and executively ordered measures
  • They handle all correspondence and produce reports, testimonials and assessments for assigning authorities, health insurance companies, IV, etc.
  • They undertake crisis interventions and psychological emergency care in close cooperation with the chief physicians
  • They work closely with the staff of other institutions and authorities, in particular with the probation and enforcement services, but also with social services or housing
  • They develop the treatment program conceptually in cooperation with those responsible and actively participate in research projects if necessary.
  • They regularly participate in in-clinic training, such as Journal Club, case presentations and supervision

Your profile

  • You have a university degree in clinical psychology and have completed psychotherapy training
  • You have several years of clinical experience in the care of people with acute and complex mental disorders
  • You have forensic-psychological professional experience or experience in law enforcement
  • They are characterized by high competences and enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation
  • They have a high degree of comprehension in dealing with complex issues and are characterized by a pronounced psychological resilience.
  • You have very good knowledge of German, ideally also good oral French skills (all other language skills are advantageous)

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
