Full description

Portrait Our schoolhouse is located in the village center of Oberbalm. We lead three primary classes (for 2 school years each) and a kindergarten class.

From now on, we are looking for a motivated, committed and ideally experienced teacher for the relief of the class teacher or specialist teachers.

Tasks

Mixed class 3/4., 20 children

Reasons for use

  • Individual children interrupt classes regularly and take much of the LP’s attention
  • LP/IF cannot do justice to learning-impaired S/S due to the demanding care of individual children
  • Challenging behavior sometimes leads to burdening children / LP

Objective

Relief of teaching: Additional personnel support should enable the LP to maintain their teaching even in difficult conditions --> presence in the classroom and support of the LP during certain lessons / half-days

Tasks

  • Accompaniment of individual S/S in case of overwhelming / challenging behavior.
  • Accompaniment of an S from the class, if temporary separation is useful (on behalf of the LP).
  • Support/care of S/S with learning difficulties in working phases
  • Accompaniment of small groups in need of support in group work
  • Support for transitions in class
  • If applicable, Presence in particularly stressful lessons

By agreement with the school management and the class teacher:

  • Start
  • Number of half days per week (e.g. 2HT for around 10 weeks)
  • Working days, subjects (part of the presence at the DI Vm would be optimal!)
  • Operational forms

Requirements

The expected qualification

Experience as a teacher 3./4. or class assistant or in child and youth care. Experience in dealing with challenging behavior (safe occurrence, assertiveness). Soft skills: empathy, patience, “open eyes and ears”, adaptability

We offer

An assignment of initially 60 lessons, which can be combined with your other activities and obligations by agreement. (Extension of the measure is possible.) Temporary employment in a small, motivated, helpful and established college, pleasant working atmosphere, basically dear students, a present school management and a benevolent cooperation with the school commission. Free parking spaces / the Postautostation are located directly in front of the schoolhouse (15 min. from BHf Köniz).

Throughout the entire time, close cooperation with our experienced and highly committed class teacher. Assumption of prepared and clearly structured orders or joint planning.
