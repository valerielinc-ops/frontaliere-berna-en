Full description

Portrait The upper school Langnau (OSLA) is part of the school Langnau which in total approx. 1'000 students in 49 classes at 7 locations. The four school leaders and the school management MR organize themselves under the direction of the general school director in the school management conference as an operational management body.

Around 300 young people attend OSLA. They are taught by 35 teachers in 14 grades and one RIK+ class.

Tasks

You lead the upper level Langnau operationally in pedagogical, personnel, organizational and administrative matters.

  • They are responsible for school development and quality assurance.
  • They represent the school outside and are members of the school management conference.
  • They take over and process higher-level dossiers for the Langnau School by agreement.
