Full description

Portrait Alongside the kindergarten, the Kreuzfeld School Centre is one of the four school centres of the Langenthal Elementary School. It is located in the center of the city (about 16'000 inhabitants) and is easily accessible by public transport. The approximately 340 children and young people are taught by about 40 teachers in 17 primary classes. In addition, about 40 children and young people are trained in 4 classes for special support. Lessons take place in the two school buildings Kreuzfeld 1 (cycle 2) and Kreuzfeld 2 (cycle 1). Further details can be found at www.volksschule-langenthal.ch or https://www.instagram.com/schulekreuzfeld langenthal
