Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

We design marketing that is relevant to customers and makes a measurable contribution to customer loyalty and business development. Our task includes the conception, planning and implementation of one-off and recurring (journey-based) marketing and communication measures. During the university internship, you will work on two exciting focal points: You will further develop the SmartPoints bonus program as a versatile private customer loyalty and marketing tool and support market development for corporate customers. In doing so, you will combine customer needs, data and effective marketing communication and gain insight into the entire campaign process - from the initial idea to implementation and success monitoring.

What you can do

  • You will support the development and implementation of marketing and communication measures around the SmartPoints bonus program in order to increase visibility, activate users and integrate the program more closely along relevant customer journeys
  • You will support the planning and implementation of marketing and communication measures for corporate customers, especially around needs-based offers and solutions in payment transactions
  • You will analyze market, customer and campaign data, derive insights and prepare decision-making bases and presentations in a way that is appropriate for the target audience
  • You coordinate the cooperation with various internal and external stakeholders and ensure smooth implementation
  • You will take on your own work packages from the idea to the success control and actively contribute your perspective to the team

What you bring to the table

  • You have a bachelor's degree in business administration, marketing, communication or a comparable field that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your bachelor's degree
  • Enthusiasm for marketing as well as interest in customer loyalty, loyalty programs, data-driven marketing and corporate banking
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking skills as well as an affinity for communication and the ability to prepare complex content in an understandable and addressee-oriented way
  • Independent, structured and reliable way of working combined with initiative and team spirit
  • Very good knowledge of common Microsoft Office applications
  • Open, curious personality with a great desire to learn and assertiveness
  • Very good knowledge of German; other languages are an advantage
