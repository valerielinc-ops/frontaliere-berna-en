Full description

Portrait Your work has an impact – for Bern and for the people who live and work here. Shape the community and the future of this city with us: visible, tangible, sustainable. At the Directorate for Education, Social Affairs and Sport, the focus is on people. The School Office is the competence center of the public kindergarten and school system.

The elementary school Lorraine/Wylergut belongs to the school district Breitenrain-Lorraine and connects two quarters with their own character. Around 350 children learn here, accompanied by a college, where cooperation and an open approach are important. Together with your co-management, you lead around 80 employees. You listen, decide and take the school step by step together with your team.

Tasks

  • With the college, you develop strategies that are effective in everyday life. What changes in society and pedagogy, you have in mind and make it usable for school.
  • You lead with clarity and motivation, give orientation and win your team for common goals.
  • You represent the school to parents and children, in the administration and in the neighborhood and ensure that you experience it as an open and reliable partner.
  • You share the leadership with an equal co-school management. In the school management team of the school circle you work actively.

Requirements

  • You have an educational education, know the lessons from your own experience and have proven yourself in working with children.
  • You like to look ahead, notice early where something is moving, and stick to changes until they work.
  • Conversations with very different people are yours. You convince with good arguments and also listen when it gets uncomfortable.
  • You have completed or already started a school management training or a comparable leadership training.
  • You like working in a team and know how to win colleagues for an idea.
  • You feel the diversity in the schoolhouse as an enrichment. Families from different cultures and living environments are met with curiosity and respect.

We offer

  • A motivated college that tries new things and treats you with openness.
  • Thanks to a co-direction, you can share responsibility, ideas and even difficult decisions.
  • Plenty of design space: You set priorities and actively develop the location.
  • Support by the school district commission and in-depth exchange in the school management team of the school district.
  • Support by school offices and specialist departments of the city of Bern in personnel, finance and organizational issues.
  • A centrally located workplace that is easily accessible by public transport and bike.
