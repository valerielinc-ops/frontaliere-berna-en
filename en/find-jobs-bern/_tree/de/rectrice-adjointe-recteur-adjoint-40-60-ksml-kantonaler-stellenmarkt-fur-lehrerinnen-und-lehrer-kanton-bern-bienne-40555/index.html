Full description

Portrait The Gymnase de Bienne et du Jura bernois places a position of

Tasks

  1. Participate in the conduct and development of the school within the management team.
  1. To take over some of the responsibilities, classified below in three areas. Their distribution within the management team will take into account your preferences and school needs.

Students and subjects

  • Bilingual gymnasial classes: student monitoring and operational management, in collaboration with the Gymnasium Biel-Seeland (GBSL), our partner school
  • Monolingual gymnastic classes: followed by a flight of students
  • Admissions: treatment of extraordinary and extra-cantonal admissions (gymnasial and ECG)
  • Promotions: promotion management, related to administration

Education and quality

  • Quality: follow-up of internal and external evaluations and quality arrangements, coordination of EPRs
  • Learning autonomy: follow-up of projects related to cross-cutting skills
  • Continuing training: pedagogical monitoring (following the evolution of society) and organization of continuing training for teachers

Organization and security

  • School year: coordination of school and cultural events, management of calendars (with GBSL)
  • Replacements: replacement management in collaboration with the Rector and Vice-Rector
  • Security and emergency: responsibility for crisis and emergency concepts, alarm management (with GBSL)
  1. Conduct periodic evaluation interviews with faculty members.
  1. In parallel, provide teaching at a fixed rate with you.

Profile required In possession of a teaching diploma for mature schools (gymnase) or for professional maturity. Training in schools (CAS or equivalent) is an asset. Experience confirmed in teaching at the gymnasium, general culture school or business school, ideally complemented by an initial experience of school management or project management. Organizational sense, ability to lead projects and work as a team. Relationship, discretion and decision-making capacity, including in sensitive situations. High interest in educational development and school organisation, spirit of innovation. Good knowledge of German is desired (close collaboration with the Gymnasium Biel-Seeland).

We offer

A rate of 40 to 60% in the Directorate, to be chosen according to your situation, supplemented by a rate of education defined with you. The possibility of asserting your interests in the distribution of files. A welcoming team, marked by a strong spirit of collaboration and mutual assistance. A Francophone school in a bilingual environment, in close collaboration with GBSL. A remarkable framework, on the shores of Lake Biel.
