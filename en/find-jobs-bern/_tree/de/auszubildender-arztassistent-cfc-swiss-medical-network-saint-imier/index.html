Full description

During your apprenticeship, you will discover the various aspects of the profession of medical assistant in our medical centres and consultations. Accompanied by experienced professionals, you will gradually develop the skills needed to practice your future profession.

Your main missions will be as follows

  • Welcome and guide patients.
  • Participate in the administrative management of consultations and appointments.
  • Provide various medical secretarial duties.
  • Assist physicians in consultations and examinations.
  • Perform certain medical and technical acts as part of your training.
  • Use computer tools and medical management software.
  • Respect the existing rules of confidentiality, hygiene and safety.
  • Develop your professional skills and autonomy as you learn.

You reach the end of your compulsory schooling and want to discover the world of work. You are interested in the field of health and appreciate human contacts. You like to learn, discover new things and invest in what you're doing. You are a person you can count on and enjoy working with others. Finally, you are comfortable with the computer tools of everyday life and motivated to build your professional future. This training will allow you to gain experience, develop new skills and take your first steps in a stimulating and rewarding environment.

During your apprenticeship, you will have the opportunity to discover different areas of outpatient medical practice, to develop your autonomy and build your professional experience gradually.

We offer you

  • Comprehensive and diverse training within a recognized health institution.
  • Personalized support by committed and experienced trainers.
  • A modern, dynamic and stimulating working environment.
  • A concrete immersion in health and patient contact.
  • Conditions that encourage learning, progressive autonomy and the development of your skills.
  • The possibility to contribute actively to the quality of care of our patients.

Entry into office: August 2027 Training place: Medicentres and/or Hospitals of the Arc Network (Moutier and St-Imier) Offers accompanied by a curriculum vitae, the last four school bulletins and other usual documents (including a cover letter) are to be transmitted via our recruitment platform. Deadline for application: October 31, 2026
