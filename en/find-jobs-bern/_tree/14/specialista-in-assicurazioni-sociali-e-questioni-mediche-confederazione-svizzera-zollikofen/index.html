Put to the Point

This office offers the opportunity to participate in social protection issues in the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDF). As a specialist and trusted person, you are responsible for questions relating to occupational prevention and medical matters. You follow the developments in your field, develop the conceptual foundations and advise in complex situations. They contribute to the social protection and health of FDFA staff working in different international contexts.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21155
  • Place of work: Eichenweg 5, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Develop, implement and update concepts, instructions and processes in the areas of occupational prevention and medical matters
  • Internal offices provide advice and technical support on occupational prevention and health issues abroad
  • Coordinate and manage dossiers on medical matters, in particular travel medicine, vaccinations and health risks
  • Follow legislative and regulatory developments, analyse their impact and develop recommendations tailored to the needs of the FDFA
  • Further develop medical services for employees working in sometimes difficult international contexts
  • Perform administrative tasks related to the function
  • Oversee complex cases in the field of occupational prevention and medical matters
  • Participation in FDFA working groups and internal and external projects.

This makes them unique.

  • Swiss Federal Professional Card in Personnel Provisions or Social Security or equivalent training
  • Very good knowledge of the Swiss pension system
  • Proven experience (minimum 5 years) in social security, ideally in an international context
  • Ability to read, understand and interpret legal texts and regulations
  • Strong interest in health issues and health risk management
  • Excellent editorial and communication skills.
  • Ability to deal with numbers
  • Organizational skills and careful and independent working methods
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and passive knowledge of the third official language.

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

The Directorate for Resources (DR) manages the use of personnel and finances to implement foreign policy goals. It operates the worldwide Swiss representation network. The Directorate shall provide the logistics, telematics, management and controlling services necessary for the management of the department. The department’s legislation, application and legal advice also fall within the remit of the Resources Directorate. It is also subordinate to the Federal Travel Office, which organises and buys international business trips.
