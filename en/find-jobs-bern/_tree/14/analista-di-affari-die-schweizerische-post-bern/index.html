Full description

Are you interested in intelligent systems that accompany millions of letters and packages every day? Are you enthusiastic about analysing complex processes and developing sustainable solutions together with various specialist areas? In this role, you will shape the further development of a business-critical coding solution at the interface of IT, logistics and automated sorting systems. With you, we ensure reliable, future-oriented processing of shipments throughout Switzerland.

You can do that.

  • You analyze and document professional and technical needs and derive clear, implementable system requirements and specifications according to defined standards.
  • Together with specialist departments, IT departments and software suppliers, you develop solution variants, clarify interfaces, dependencies, and effects on existing processes, which allows you to optimally contribute your expertise.
  • You coordinate changes between business, IT, operations and external partners: inside and accompany them from the requirement to the productive introduction.
  • You plan releases, create test concepts and test cases, carry out integration tests and ensure production readiness with the participants.
  • You analyze incidents across different systems, interfaces and processing steps, identify their causes using logs, monitoring data and alerts, and derive sustainable solutions to stabilize and optimize operations.
  • You will help shape the technological development of our application landscape and use modern, also AI-supported tools.
  • Together with the team, you ensure reliable operation and take on regular day and day care services.

You bring that with you

  • You have a university degree in computer science, business informatics or a related field and bring several years of experience in business analysis, application management, systems and integration engineering or IT operations.
  • You can translate professional needs into technical requirements and communicate securely between business, operations, development and external partners:
  • You are inspired by complex systems, interfaces and asynchronous message flows and you analyze error images structured on the basis of logs and monitoring data, which makes a valuable contribution.
  • You bring experience with tests and releases and take responsibility from analysis to introduction
  • Knowledge in AWS, Kubernetes, APIs, Kafka, MQTT, Splunk, Grafana or image and text recognition are advantageous.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in German and English, work independently and methodically and actively participate in agile teams.

You are ready to work regularly in Bern as well as at locations of parcel and letter centres throughout Switzerland.
