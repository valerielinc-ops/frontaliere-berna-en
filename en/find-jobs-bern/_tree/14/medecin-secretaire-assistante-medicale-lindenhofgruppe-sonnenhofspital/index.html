Full description

What you will do

  • You manage the electronic medical records (inpatient and outpatient) in KISIM and Rockethealth.
  • You independently contact patients and call them in for examinations.
  • You are also responsible for coordinating and maintaining the practice's Outlook calendar.
  • You prepare medical reports from dictation and edit them carefully.
  • You also perform ECGs and ensure that long-term ECGs are attached correctly.

What sets you apart

  • You completed your training as a medical secretary or medical practice assistant.
  • You already have experience in acute care or in a medical practice.
  • Treating people with respect and appreciation is a matter of course for you. You also stand out for your enjoyment of your work and a positive attitude.
  • Even when patient situations vary, you keep an overview and do not lose your composure easily in hectic moments.
  • You have very good German skills and good spoken French skills.
  • You work on Mondays, Thursdays and Fridays.
