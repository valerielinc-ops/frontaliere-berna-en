Put to the Point

Want to create content that makes Switzerland visible worldwide? 2027 marks a special presidential year for Switzerland with numerous events and diverse communication challenges. To accompany this extraordinary year, the communications department of the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDF) is looking for a creative, versatile personality with enthusiasm for digital media. In a dynamic and multidisciplinary team, you participate in the conception and production of content for the FDFA’s communication channels and support the media support of national and international events. This is what we offer you

You can make this contribution

  • Audiovisual Production: You design, shoot and edit videos for different formats and platforms, especially Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube.
  • Graphical design: You develop visuals, animations, infographics and content for digital communication channels.
  • Social Media: You contribute to the development of original and innovative content – from the first idea to publication.

HR Admin EDA

On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

The General Secretariat (GS-EDA) supports and advises the department head in his daily work. It prepares the business for Parliament and the Bundesrat and informs the public about the activities of the department. It also supports contract management in the FDFA, manages the compliance office and checks the effectiveness of risk management, controls and management and monitoring processes. It also ensures equal opportunities in the FDFA and deals with all historical issues affecting the department. The General Secretariat is also subordinated to Switzerland, which implements the strategy of the Federal Council for Swiss state communication.
