Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Analysis of Swiss Army procurement projects and, together with the business units, derive strategic innovation priorities.
  • Build and maintain innovation pipeline: identify, evaluate and prioritize new technologies, business models and growth areas.
  • Lead cross-division innovation projects of strategic importance and anchor innovation methods in the company.
  • Initiate and lead innovation projects according to the venture clienting approach, from start-up identification and selection to proof of concept to business case and transfer to line.
  • Professionally lead project teams and work together with internal stakeholders, startups, universities and other external partners of the innovation ecosystem.

You bring that with you

  • Master or Bachelor in Business Administration / Industrial Engineering / or technical studies with further education e.g. MBA
  • Min 5 years of relevant experience in innovation management, product management, business development or similar
  • In-depth understanding of the Swiss arms environment, procurement processes (armasuisse, VBS) and relevant technology fields
  • Methodological competence in innovation management (venture clienting, design thinking)
  • Experience in business case creation, market analysis and project management (especially innovation projects)
  • Entrepreneurial, strategic and networked thinking with structured prioritization
  • Power of persuasion, leadership without formal hierarchy, discretion and readiness for personal security checks

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
