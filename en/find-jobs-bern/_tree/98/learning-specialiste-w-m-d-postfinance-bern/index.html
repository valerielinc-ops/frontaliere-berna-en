Full description

At PostFinance you shape the learning world of tomorrow. Together with your team, you will design innovative and effective learning opportunities, prepare them for target groups and develop them in line with current trends and requirements. This ensures that our sales representatives have the right skills and can continuously develop. With your commitment, you contribute to strengthening PostFinance as a digital innovation leader.

You can do that.

  • You advise our clients: inside holistically on learning and development topics
  • You analyze learning needs and develop suitable development measures and effective learning settings from them
  • You will design creative, didactically thought-out and varied e-learnings and use modern authoring tools
  • You take on responsibility for training in projects
  • You create training concepts and lead the working group of specialists:inside and front representatives:in
  • You are responsible for your learning content and develop it together with the relevant specialists: inside and module managers
  • You ensure the effectiveness of your training modules, take feedback and implement targeted improvements

You bring that with you

  • Commercial or media-related vocational education and training in (e-) learning or adult education
  • In-depth knowledge of modern learning methods and authoring tools as well as a good feeling for creative and didactically effective learning opportunities
  • Strong communication and consulting skills as well as a secure attitude towards different stakeholders
  • Experience in project work as well as in the conception, coordination and implementation of training measures
  • Experience in sales, banking or insurance environment is beneficial

You are also interested in PostFinance products for private customers:

  • Knowledge of PowerAutomate and PowerApps are an additional plus
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of French is an advantage
