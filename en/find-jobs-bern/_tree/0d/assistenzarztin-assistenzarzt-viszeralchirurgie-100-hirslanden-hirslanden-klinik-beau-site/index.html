Klinik Beau-Site – as part of the Hirslanden Group Bern – stands for medical excellence, interdisciplinary cooperation and patient-oriented care. Our visceral surgery clinic is recognized as a category B2 training facility and offers the full range of ordinary and complex visceral surgery including robot-assisted surgery (da Vinci).

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality by early 2027 or by agreement.

ITS TASKS

  • Care of inpatient and outpatient visceral surgery patients
  • Assistance and independent execution of operations with teaching by tutor
  • Co-design of daily visits, indication consultation hours and interdisciplinary reports (e.g. Tumorboard)
  • Participation in the visceral surgical service system of the clinic Beau-Site

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed medical studies (recognition of MEBEKO for foreign diploma mandatory)
  • Ideally, 1–2 years of clinical experience in surgery
  • High social skills, teamwork and enthusiasm for the field of visceral surgery
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. C1)

Our offer

  • Sound and structured training (category B2) in a highly specialized environment
  • Regular internal training and support for external courses
  • Attractive employment conditions of the Hirslanden Group with outstanding social benefits
  • Collegial, dynamic small team and short lines of communication, 1:1 supervision and teaching
