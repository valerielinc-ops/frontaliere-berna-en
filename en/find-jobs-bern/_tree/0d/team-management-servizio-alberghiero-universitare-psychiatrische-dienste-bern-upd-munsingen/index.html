Full description

COO Münsingen

At the University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ), you and your team ensure a high quality of service and a smooth process in the field of hotel service. As Team Leader Hotel Industry Service or Team Leader Hotel Industry Service, you lead and support your team, coordinate daily work processes and actively participate in operational operations. With your service-oriented nature, your organizational talent and your eye for quality, you contribute to a professional and hospitable atmosphere. You can expect a varied task in which you take on leadership responsibility, develop your team and actively shape daily events.

Your tasks

  • You lead, accompany and support your team and promote an appreciative and constructive cooperation
  • You plan and coordinate the daily work assignments and ensure smooth processes in the area of hospitality service
  • You are actively involved in day-to-day operations and support your team where you are needed
  • You ensure a friendly, attentive and professional care of our guests
  • You are the contact person for your team, support your employees with questions and promote their personal responsibility as well as their professional and personal development
  • You keep an overview even under high loads, set priorities and find pragmatic solutions for everyday operational challenges
  • You pay attention to compliance with quality, hygiene and safety standards and live them in everyday work
  • You recognize optimization potential, bring your own ideas and contribute to the further development of workflows
  • You work closely with the other areas of the hotel industry and care and contribute to a good cross-divisional cooperation

Your profile

  • You have leadership experience, ideally in service, in the hotel industry or in a comparable service company
  • You bring a strong service orientation and enjoy creating a professional and hospitable atmosphere
  • You like to take responsibility, make situational decisions and act solution-oriented
  • You enjoy working practically and appreciate the combination of management tasks and active participation in day-to-day operations
  • You are a communicative, reliable and team-oriented personality who can motivate, support and promote employees
  • You work independently, structured and forward-looking and keep an overview even in lively situations
  • You have organizational talent, flexibility and a good feeling for the needs of guests and patients
  • You have good oral and written knowledge of German

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
