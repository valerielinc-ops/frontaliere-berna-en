# DevOps Engineer Fortinet

Il tuo futuro inizia

We accompany the army on its digitalisation journey with the aim of strengthening Switzerland’s defence capability and making targeted use of the opportunities offered by technological progress. The new digitalisation platform forms the basis for an independent, secure and crisis-resistant ICT infrastructure. As DevOps Engineer Fortinet, you are responsible for the operation, further development and integration of central Fortinet services such as Web Application Firewall (WAF) and Remote Access Services (RAS) into a highly secure system landscape. Together with network, infrastructure and application teams, you will design modern security solutions and drive innovation in a powerful, highly available environment. In this way, you make a direct contribution to the secure digital transformation of the Swiss army.

  • Operation, maintenance and further development of Fortinet platforms with focus on Web Application Firewall (WAF) and Remote Access Services (RAS)
  • Integration of security solutions into a highly secure and complex ICT landscape including its components and systems
  • Professional contact point for Fortinet products and support for architecture, integration and operational issues
  • Close collaboration with application, network, infrastructure and security teams in a DevOps-oriented environment
  • Co-design of modern technologies and innovative security services within a powerful and highly available infrastructure in the security network

Your skills

  • Degree (ETH/Uni/FH) in computer science or equivalent degree or sound professional experience
  • Several years of experience in the field of Fortinet products
  • Very good engineering skills (methodology, processes & models) in large, heterogeneous and distributed ICT infrastructures
  • In-depth, practical experience in automation and programming (PowerShell, Ansible) as well as connection of heterogeneous APIs
  • Experience with orders / work in areas with high / increased need for protection (financial sector, blue light organizations, etc.)
  • Very good German in word and writing
  • Appropriate reputation for personal safety inspection SEPOS

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
