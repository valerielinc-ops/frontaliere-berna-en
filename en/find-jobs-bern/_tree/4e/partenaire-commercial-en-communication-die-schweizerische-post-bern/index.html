Full description

As a communications consultant, you build the bridge between the communications department and the functional areas assigned to you. You recognize communication opportunities at an early stage, create orientation in complex topics and ensure that the right messages have an impact. With you, we also develop a convincing communication strategy for challenging content.

You can do that.

  • You advise stakeholders from the areas of strategy and sustainability.
  • From the perspective of the relevant stakeholder groups, you will analyze strategic projects and developments and derive the communicative approach from them.
  • You develop targeted communication strategies, concepts, narratives and core messages and translate complex content into understandable and impactful stories.
  • Together with the departments and communication teams, you plan, coordinate and implement effective communication measures across various channels.
  • You create or control target group and channel-oriented content such as messages, contributions, presentations or storylines. In addition, you ensure their quality and effect.
  • For consistent communication, you work closely with specialist departments, lead business partners and other competence centers.
  • You keep an eye on trends, developments and potential communication topics and proactively bring your perspective into strategic discussions.

You bring that with you

  • You have a university degree or a comparable education and training in communication, public relations, business administration or a related field.
  • Several years of experience in a comparable communication function, ideally with a focus on strategy, corporate or financial communication, enables you to control even complex topics safely.
  • Business and strategic contexts you understand quickly and provide the most demanding content addressable, comprehensible and multimedia.
  • You approach topics on your own responsibility, think in a networked way and recognize the expectations and needs of different stakeholders at an early stage.
  • You succeed in bringing together different interests, convincing stakeholders and advancing communication projects in a targeted manner.
  • You represent clear positions with well-founded arguments and also address challenging topics openly and constructively.
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German is required. Every other national language is a plus.
