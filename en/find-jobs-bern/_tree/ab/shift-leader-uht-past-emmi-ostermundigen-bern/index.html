Full description

Freshness in focus, processes under control - At the traditional location Ostermundigen, we passionately produce popular classics such as Emmi Caffè Latte, the finest yogurt and refreshing glace. In a dynamic environment that stands for freshness, speed and the highest quality, we are looking for people who want to think, shape and grow with us.

You can do that.

  • You are responsible for the management and promotion of plant operators: inside during your shift in 3-4 shift operation and ensure the smooth and efficient production process incl. pause detachments and shift transfers safely.
  • You coordinate the personnel deployment plan (allocation to the machines, break detachments, etc.) as well as the necessary cleaning and maintenance work.
  • You act as the first point of contact for employees during the shift and provide proactive support in the event of problems or queries.
  • You regularly exchange ideas with the other shift leaders and also ensure seamless communication with the production management.
  • You control the processes and systems for their correctness, collect data correctly in the MES and consistently implement the hygiene, quality and safety regulations in your shift.
  • You hold annual meetings with the employees in your group and represent the goals and key figures of the department towards the employees.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of professional experience in the food industry as well as completed vocational training in a relevant environment.
  • You have a strong understanding of leadership and already have experience in leading a smaller group.
  • You are tech-savvy and can take action if the machines become disturbed.
  • You have extensive knowledge of SAP and MES and feel comfortable using the common MS Office tools.
  • You speak fluent German.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Further education and development opportunities: Emmi focuses on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
