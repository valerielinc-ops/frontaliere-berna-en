Full description

Do you want to start your career and take the next step within 9-12 months?

Then apply for our Store Manager trainee program!, WHAT WE ARE

You will have the chance to join a retail trade company that wants to be the first choice of candidates as an employer. As a JYSK ambassador you will get the following advantages:

  • A five-week trainee program with interactive training on all important aspects of JYSK's filial guidance, accompanied by experienced sales and HR experts who train you and coach you.
  • An experienced mentor from the organization is available to you and your development needs
  • During the program you will be used as a deputy branch manager and will take responsibility from the beginning
  • Opportunities for own development and responsibility
  • Above average revenue bonus for the entire team
  • 20% discount on JYSK and other Lars Larsen Group companies
  • Top pension fund benefits
  • 5 weeks of holidays, which increase every 5 years of service
  • Other advantages such as above-average accident insurance, various activities (annual celebration, team events) and great anniversary gifts WORUM DOES IT IN OUR TRAINEE PROGRAMME

Our program offers high quality training in leadership, management and branch operations, combined with practical experience and theoretical knowledge. You usually work and learn in close cooperation with other trainees and your mentor.

  • Five modules of one week duration: theoretical knowledge, role playing, group work, team building and discussions in plenary
  • Between the modules you will be trained with an assigned store manager mentor at the workplace and receive coaching
  • After completion of the modules, a celebration awaits you to celebrate the completion of the program The ultimate goal of the Store Manager Trainee program is to prepare you to run your own branch in the near future.
  • Commitment to your and the development of your colleagues
  • You communicate in a motivating and inclusive way and are able to create a positive working environment
  • Optimally, you already have experience in leadership, motivation and development of a team
  • You are result-oriented and have a passion for retail
  • You have the ability to remain calm and determined even in stressful situations
  • You feel well taken care of in a dynamic working environment in constant change
  • You like physical work, have a hands-on mentality and take the lead with good examples
  • You are flexible and ready to work on Saturdays too BEWIB YOU TODAY!
