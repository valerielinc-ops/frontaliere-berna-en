Full description

For a long-term and technologically demanding transformation program, we are looking for an experienced ICT integration architect (Workplace & Collaboration) for our customer from Bern.

In this key role, you will develop sustainable architectures for modern communication and collaboration services and ensure their sustainable integration into the existing ICT landscape.

Your tasks

  • Define and develop the target architecture for workplace and collaboration services
  • Development of architectural concepts for communication, collaboration and knowledge management solutions
  • Evaluate new technologies and derive architecture standards and best practices
  • Creating reference architectures and technical specifications for projects
  • Ensuring the integration of workplace services into existing platform and service landscapes
  • Coordinate solutions with security, network, cloud, infrastructure and IAM teams
  • Conducting architecture reviews and ensuring compliance with applicable requirements
  • Advising projects and stakeholders on architecture and technology topics

Your profile

  • Several years of experience as an ICT or Solution Architect in the workplace or collaboration environment
  • Higher education in computer science, business informatics or equivalent qualification
  • Sound knowledge of modern collaboration and communication platforms
  • Experience with cloud-based workplace solutions and digital workplace concepts
  • Understanding Security, Network, IAM and Infrastructure Architectures
  • Experience in developing target architectures and technology roadmaps
  • Strong conceptual skills as well as a high quality awareness
  • Very good knowledge of German and strong communication skills
  • Proper reputation is mandatory
