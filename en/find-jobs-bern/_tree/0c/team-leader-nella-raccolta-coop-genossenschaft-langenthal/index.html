Team leader: in picking

Coop Cooperative — Langenthal, BE, Svizzera

  • Job opening: by agreement
  • Specialist: Logistics / Transportation
  • Location: Coop, Nordstrasse 1, 4900 Langenthal
  • Pensum: 100%

Tasks

  • Together with the other team leaders, you lead and coordinate an area with around 50 employees on site in the warehouse area and ensure that the day-to-day operations run smoothly.
  • In the event of deviations, disruptions or personnel challenges, you act as the central contact person, analyze the causes and initiate suitable measures quickly.
  • You keep an eye on the ongoing logistics processes and ensure that quality standards, safety regulations, operational specifications and internal guidelines are consistently adhered to.
  • Good cooperation between the teams you actively promote and drive the continuous optimization of the process

Requirements

  • A completed basic training as a logistician: in EFZ or a comparable training forms your professional basis. Further training as a logistics specialist or logistics specialist with a federal certificate is an advantage
  • First management experience in an operational logistics, production or shipping environment
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, a structured and solution-oriented way of working as well as a secure overview even in high work volumes and demanding situations distinguish you
  • As a responsible, assertive and team-oriented leader, you motivate employees, make decisions and strengthen cohesion in the team.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good knowledge of the common IT systems round off your profile. Experience with warehouse management or logistics systems is an advantage
  • Discounts: discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group, cheaper mobile and prepaid subscriptions on Coop Mobile, Reka card: discount on subscription, discount on GA subscription
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, loyalty bonuses, savings plan at the pension fund
  • Working time: 5 weeks holidays, flexible working time models
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training, talent management with a wide range of development opportunities
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, free participation in various company runs
