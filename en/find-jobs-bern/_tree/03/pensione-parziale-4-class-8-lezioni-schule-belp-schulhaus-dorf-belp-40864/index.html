Portrait The Belp elementary school has eleven decentralized kindergartens, a primary school located in the three school facilities Dorf, Mühlematt and Neumatt as well as a cycle school on the Belpberg. The classes of the upper secondary level I are located in the central upper secondary center Mühlematt. In addition to additional supporting lessons (MR), students can receive targeted and individual support in an induction class and in an intensive German language development course. A day school, a well-developed elective subject and supplementary offer (AdS), the voluntary school sport and the task help expand the offer of the managed schools.
