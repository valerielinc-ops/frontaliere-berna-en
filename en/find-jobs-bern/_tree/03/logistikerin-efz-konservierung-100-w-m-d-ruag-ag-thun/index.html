Full description

Do you master the truck like Lewis Hamilton his Ferrari? With the word “camp”, do you not think of the summer holidays as scouts, but of your workplace? Then you are right in the logistics / warehouse department of RUAG. Here you are jointly responsible for ensuring that goods, for example for tanks or aircraft, are delivered and delivered in time so that the security of Switzerland is guaranteed.

You can move that

  • Unpacking and testing of delivered parts and assemblies
  • Professional packaging of assemblies in special containers and spare parts
  • Preservation of components and assemblies for long-term storage
  • Planning and arrangement of auxiliary and operating equipment and operating elements
  • Ensuring an efficient and quality-appropriate working method taking into account lean management principles (MUDA / avoiding waste)
  • Help ensure product quality, delivery capability and productivity
  • Active participation in the optimization of processes as part of the continuous improvement process (CIP)

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic training as logistician EFZ
  • min. 5 years of professional experience in logistics
  • Good MS Office knowledge (Word, Excel), SAP (4Hana beneficial)
  • Forklift and crane test, or readiness to complete it
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Readiness for operations in other logistics teams

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
