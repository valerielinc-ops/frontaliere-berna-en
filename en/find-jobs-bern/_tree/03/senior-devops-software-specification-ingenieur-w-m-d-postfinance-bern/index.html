Full description

At PostFinance, we continue to operate and develop our Polarion ALM requirements management platform while building a modern specification-as-code approach. We are a team under construction and are looking for you as a Senior DevOps Software & Specification Engineer. You are tech-savvy, an experienced developer with DevOps mindset, who has fun building toolchains and standards, guiding requirements sustainably as code and supporting the user teams in the transition to modern working methods. In this way, we can empower the teams to promote their T-shaping, guide their artifacts as code and increase AI readiness. In doing so, we create the basis for consistent traceability from requirements to implementation to productive use and make a significant contribution to accelerating software development at PostFinance.

You can do that.

  • You take responsibility for the operation, maintenance and further development of our requirements management platforms
  • You run our established requirements management tool Polarion and actively shape its continuous development into a modern, future-oriented solution
  • You will design and implement a modern requirements-as-code solution with a Spec-Driven-Development approach.
  • You develop and operate solutions, integrations and various automations throughout the software delivery lifecycle toolchain.
  • You use CI/CD pipelines and AI-based tools for development, analysis, testing and documentation
  • You will work closely with developers, business analysts, testers, architects, product owners and other stakeholders from PostFinance’s approximately 100 delivery teams.

You bring that with you

  • Higher education or further education in computer science (HF, FH, Uni/ETH) or comparable practical experience, with good knowledge of German and English
  • At least 5 years of experience as a Software Engineer, DevOps Engineer or in a similar technical role
  • Experience in the operation and further development of mission-critical applications and platforms
  • Good knowledge of Java, Spring Boot, TypeScript, Angular, REST APIs and CI/CD pipelines
  • Experience with grown enterprise landscapes, ideally with Polarion ALM, Groovy or similar technologies
  • High quality awareness, strong problem-solving skills and interest in modern engineering practices, AI tools and Specs-as-Code
