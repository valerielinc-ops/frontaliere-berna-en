Full description

Portrait Are you our new leader, who will complement our school management team in the field of secondary education and enrich it with your leadership and expertise?

The departmental management of secondary school I is responsible for the pedagogical and organizational management of cycle 3 at the schools in Rüegsau. At this level, young people from the municipalities of Rüegsau, Hasle b.B., Lützelflüh and Affoltern are taught in 10 rule classes and one class for special promotion (KbF). The classes are currently taught in Model 3a, but the school is in an exciting school development process in the direction of Model 4, which will be implemented from the school year 2028-29 in the year class 7. Subsequently, the model 4 is implemented in the 8th grades in the school year 2029-30 and one year later in the 9th grades (from this point onwards at the entire stage). This process of model change was initiated by the teachers, is supported by the authorities and is constructive at all levels and the participants meet it with great openness.

The management of the secondary level I has its place of work on the school grounds in Rüegsauschachen and is embedded in the school management team of the schools Rüegsau, which consists of the general school management, the management for the kindergarten and primary level as well as the management for the MR area (4 people).

The entire school consists of four kindergartens, twelve primary classes and ten secondary classes at the school locations Rüegsauschachen (three kindergartens, nine primary classes and all secondary classes) and Rüegsbach (two primary classes and one kindergarten). In the current school year, about 500 pupils from kindergarten to the 9th treasury are taught at the schools in Rüegsau.

The schools Rüegsau are characterized by short service and local routes. At the school location Rüegsauschachen - including within the newly constructed school building (Holzhuus) in 2022 - there are teacher work and break areas, the school management and school administration office, meeting rooms, the school social work, as well as the rooms of speech therapy, psychomotor skills, classes for special promotion, two gyms and classrooms for currently about 400 children and young people.

Tasks

The tasks of the management of secondary level I are described in the management concept of the schools Rüegsau as well as in the function diagram of the schools Rüegsau. They comply with the cantonal requirements and the school-internal proven regulations. This includes the pedagogical and operational management of the upper secondary level I area with the tasks

  • Staff management
  • The pedagogical direction
  • Quality development and evaluation
  • Organisation and administration

At the upper secondary level I, the management of the level leads and leads approximately 22 employees.

Possibly and by agreement, the management of the secondary level I will teach the part of the lessons (30-45% lessons) at the primary level (possibly 5/6 grades) or in the MR area in order to avoid a conflict of roles (being supervisor and team member in the same team). We have had good experience with this clarity of roles.

Requirements

  • You have completed school management training recognised in the Canton of Bern or are ready to complete it.
  • You are capable of teamwork, reliable, flexible, loyal and resilient, have a quick comprehension, organizational skills, enjoy working with young people and adults and the conversation (session management) is yours.
  • They are ready to support already ongoing projects and concepts and can identify with the guiding principle of the schools Rüegsau and their values (see homepage www.schulen-ruegsau.ch in the part "About us").
  • You are ready to participate in the development of the school as well as on occasions within your professional assignment.

We offer

  • You are not alone in your work at our school. Together, the school management team bears the responsibility and masters exciting and enriching challenges for the benefit of the students and the teachers.
  • Secondary level I is well prepared for you. They are professionally introduced to the task and can build on the proven. The teaching team is characterised by a high level of commitment and professionalism. It is mixed in age, but tends to be characterized by younger people and people between the ages of 30 - 45 years.
  • You will be supported in your daily work by a dedicated school administration (100% workload) and the school social worker (65% workload) at the location of the future workplace (school area Rüegsauschachen).
  • A workplace in the beautiful school house "Holzhuus", newly built in 2022, is waiting for you.

  • Their employment is indefinite.
  • At the school, a motivated and committed school management and specialist team works with you.
  • The town of Rüegsauschachen is easily accessible by public transport and by car.
