Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population? Our HR team “People & Culture” is looking for a dynamic, motivated and open personality for the HR Administration department.

We are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Personnel in Human Resources 80-100 %

You do

  • You take on a wide range of tasks in personnel administration and ensure professional handling of all administrative processes from entry to exit.
  • You collect and maintain all data along the entire employee lifecycle in SAP HCM, from entry to exit.
  • You create employment certificates and other personnel-relevant documents.
  • Together with your team, you are the contact person for personnel administrative concerns of our employees.
  • You handle special cases and individual requests and are also in exchange with external bodies.
  • You manage the staff administration counter and are a competent point of contact for our employees.

This sets you apart

  • You have completed a commercial training as well as further training as a clerk / clerk staff.
  • You bring 2–3 years of experience in personnel administration and wage processing.
  • You have very good MS Office knowledge and ideally experience with SAP.
  • You work independently, carefully and quality-consciously and convince with your organizational talent.
  • Your mother tongue is French or German and you have very good oral and written knowledge of the other language (level B2).

You will benefit from this

The SZB offers a bilingual and versatile working environment in which people from a wide variety of professional groups work together. Appreciative cooperation, mutual support and good cooperation across the different areas are important to us. With us you can contribute your experience, take on responsibility and develop professionally. In addition to the employment conditions according to our GAV, you benefit from various discounts, including discounts in our pharmacy, public transport, for your leisure time as well as in our cafeteria and our staff restaurant. In addition, we offer a good connection to public transport and a weekly offer with yoga, meditation and group courses.
