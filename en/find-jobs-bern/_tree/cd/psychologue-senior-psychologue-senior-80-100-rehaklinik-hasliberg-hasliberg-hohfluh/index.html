Full description

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of psychosomatic, internistic-oncological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Solothurn, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the mission statement of “REHA competence” with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, warmth, attention and competence.

Their tasks

  • Leadership and supervision of psychologists in psychosomatic rehabilitation according to the biopsychosocial approach
  • Close cooperation with the medical and nursing cadre and the entire rehabilitation team
  • Stationary patient care
  • If desired, ambulatory patient care on a fee basis
  • Participation in projects

Our expectations

  • Completed psychotherapy training
  • Team-capable, independent, active and friendly personality with organizational talent and ideally leadership experience
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Interest in direct clinical psychotherapeutic activity

Our offer

  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a family environment with a flat hierarchy and open communication
  • Family-friendly and predictable working hours
  • Workplace in the recreation area with a wide range of leisure activities
  • Attractive employment conditions and remuneration
  • If you are interested, care of your own outpatients on a fee basis is possible
