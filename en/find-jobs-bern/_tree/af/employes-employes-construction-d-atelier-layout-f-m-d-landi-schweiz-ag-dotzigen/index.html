Store construction is part of the “Expansion & Realisation” division and reports to the Marketing department. The team is responsible for ensuring that goods are stocked in LANDI stores during renovations and new builds. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and flexible person to join us as a store construction/layout employee, by agreement.

Nadine Grindat HR Business Partner +41 58 433 98 47

These tasks will inspire you

  • Work in LANDI stores throughout Switzerland. You will spend 80%-90% of your time staying overnight in a hotel away from home.
  • Creating and implementing specified layouts on time.
  • Dismantling and assembling wall shelves and product displays during renovations.
  • Stocking LANDI products according to templates and store plans.
  • Assisting with the continuous improvement of the store setup process and the search for new presentation options and merchandise carriers.

We look forward to candidates with this profile

  • Completed vocational training (retail or skilled trade)
  • Category B driving licence, forklift certification is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of the MS Office suite
  • German or French as a native language, with good communication skills in the other language
  • good spatial visualization skills, independent and responsible
  • team-oriented, flexible and in good physical condition

Discover your benefits

  • Education and training: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external training.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. Starting from 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks, and from the 60. Age even 7 weeks. In addition, the purchase of additional vacation days is possible.
  • Family leave: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent takes 3 weeks of family leave at full pay.

  • Benefits and discounts: Among other things, our employees benefit from discounts on shopping and fuel, credits for REKA-Geld, and discounted mobile plans for themselves and their families.
