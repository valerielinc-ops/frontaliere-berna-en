Are you interested in socio-political issues? As a university intern in the field of research and evaluation, they contribute to evidence-based foundations for political decision-making processes.   • Entry date: 01.03.2027 or by arrangement

  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20927

You can make this contribution

  • Participate in meetings, workshops and events with internal and external partners
  • Administrative support for research and evaluation

This makes them unique.

  • Completed university degree (master or doctorate), in the fields of economics or social sciences

  • Good editorial skills as well as experience with mainly quantitative evaluations of administrative and survey data, but also qualitative analyses
  • Ability to learn quickly and independently about new topics – at the same time a team-oriented way of working
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and very good knowledge of English
