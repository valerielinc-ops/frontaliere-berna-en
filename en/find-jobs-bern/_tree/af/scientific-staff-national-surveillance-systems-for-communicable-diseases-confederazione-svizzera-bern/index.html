Put to the Point

They directly contribute to the modernisation of the surveillance of communicable diseases in Switzerland. With your expertise, you shape the digitalization of central monitoring systems and create the basis for a timely, high-quality and efficient use of health data for the protection of the population.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20403

You can make this contribution

  • Further develop digital surveillance systems for communicable diseases by designing, modelling and integrating health data
  • Design, implement and optimise digital reporting and data exchange processes between health actors and national surveillance and control systems
  • Analyze health information systems (e.g. LIS, KIS, PIS) and model and parameterize data flows, interfaces and exchange formats
  • Apply and further develop interoperability standards (e.g. HL7 FIHR, SNOMED CT, LOINC) and translate the technical requirements into technical solutions

This makes them unique.

  • University degree in Health Informatics, Medical Informatics, Bioengineering, or a comparable discipline
  • Experience with health information systems (LIS, KIS, PIS), data exchange or digitalization projects in the healthcare sector
  • Knowledge of interoperability standards and data exchange formats (e.g. HL7 FHIR, SNOMED CT, LOINC) and the ability to translate professional requirements into technical solutions
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and if possible passive knowledge of the third official language
