Full description

Portrait The municipality of Hindelbank runs a school every three cycles. Around 420 students are taught in four kindergarten classes, nine primary school classes and seven secondary school classes. In cycle 3, together with the municipality of Krauchthal, we lead a transparent model. The MR area is also organised through both municipalities; the MR management is subordinate to the school Hindelbank. The approximately 50 teachers benefit from a contemporary infrastructure and a benevolent and supportive working culture. The school is easily accessible by public transport, parking is available for free. there is the possibility of an inexpensive lunch meal at the lunch table.

Tasks

They accompany and supervise the class as a class teacher. They teach the subjects NMG, German, English, Mathematics, M&I, BG and Sport. Assume according to your employment You have other tasks for the school as a whole (school development, conferences, school events, etc.).

They have basic educational training (seminary teaching qualification for the primary level, Bachelor of Arts in Primary Education) or equivalent training.

  • Support from school management, secretariat and school authority
  • Support by qualified curative pedagogue and school social work
  • a favorable meal at the lunch table of the upper school
