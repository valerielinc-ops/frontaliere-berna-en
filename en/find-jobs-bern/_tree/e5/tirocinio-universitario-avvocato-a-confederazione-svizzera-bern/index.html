Put to the Point

The Department of Law is the operational legal department of the BLV and oversees a wide range of topics: food and nutrition, plant protection products, animal welfare and animal health, species protection, as well as administrative criminal law, data protection and public law. Do you have a flair for these topics? Then a dedicated team looks forward to your support during a year.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20757

You can make this contribution

  • Legal clarification and participation in legislative procedures in the areas of food and nutrition, plant protection products, animal welfare, animal diseases, veterinary medicinal products and species protection
  • Writing of criminal and objection decisions as well as statements and complaints to courts
  • Data protection and public law: assessment of processing regulations, answering requests
  • Preparation of opinions in the framework of the Office Consultation

This makes them unique.

  • Master’s degree in Law (very good track record in public law, degree less than 18 months ago on taking up the job)
  • Adaptness in written expression preferably in German
  • High self-motivation and rapid understanding
  • Identification with the values of the BLV
  • Active knowledge of two official languages
