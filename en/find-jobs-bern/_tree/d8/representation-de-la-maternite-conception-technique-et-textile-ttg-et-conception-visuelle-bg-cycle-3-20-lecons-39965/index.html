Portrait At the Schul Münchenbuchsee, around 1200 children and adolescents are taught in 12 kindergarten classes, 32 primary classes, 15 secondary classes, 1 KbF, 1 learning studio and one IKDaZ class. The educational offer of the school is supplemented by a day school and the school social work. In the Schulhaus Bodenacker we lead 15 classes in Model 4 as well as a KbF class.

Tasks

As a maternity representative, we are looking for a specialist teacher for:

  • 8 lessons of technical design Sek 1 (Monday and Wednesday afternoon)

Requirements

You have a teaching diploma Sek 1, are in training or have experience in design activities. The Schulhaus Bodenacker is easily accessible by public transport, parking is available directly at the school house. The place may be divided. Job entry and end of employment can still change depending on the pregnancy course and date of birth.
