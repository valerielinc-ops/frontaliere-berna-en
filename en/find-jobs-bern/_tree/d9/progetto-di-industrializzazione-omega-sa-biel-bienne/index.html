Full description

Introduction of society

OMEGA is a prestigious watch brand worldwide known for its quality, precision and innovation requirements for over 170 years. Our watches are among the most beautiful in the world and are characterized by their state-of-the-art technology and timeless style.

Over the course of our long history, we have broken records of precision, redefined sporting timing and maintained our position at the cutting edge of Haute Horlogerie.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams on almost every continent with their talents, ambitions and inspirations.

Your main mission will be to organize and monitor projects of industrialization and continuous improvement for watchmaking components. You will follow in particular the implementation of the industrialization of the new products, until the delivery of the quantity of launch of the new product. You will manage continuous improvement projects and ensure coordination between the various departments (Product/Design, Technical Office, Quality, Supply Chain, Purchasing, Production, Customer Service).

Description of post

  • Product industrialization:
  • To monitor the industrialization of the components of new products and collections in collaboration with project teams and suppliers.
  • Ensure robust processes, identify and manage risks (cost, quality, delay).
  • Integrate new technologies and support suppliers in their qualifications.
  • Evaluate and optimize industrial efficiency among partners.
  • Continuous improvement:
  • Identify and propose improvement projects
  • Conduct corrective actions to improve product reliability.
  • Develop and validate technological solutions for the improvement and optimization of industrial products and processes
  • Technically supporting quality in problem solving
  • Conduct process audits and follow action plans.

Profile

  • Experience: Minimum 5 years in a similar position, with solid expertise in industrialization of watchmaking clothing components.
  • Training: Diploma of Engineer (Master) in Micromechanical or Mechanical.
  • Technical expertise:
  • Experience in managing industrialisation projects, ideally in the watchmaking dressing sector.
  • Mastery of Catia or equivalent software in a PLM environment.
  • Knowledge of SAP (consultation and understanding of MM, PP, QM modules).
  • Familiarity with LEAN tools.
  • Cross-cutting competencies:
  • Ability to work in a cross-sectional team with different departments and suppliers.
  • Ability to conduct multiple projects simultaneously.
  • Natural leadership, educational sense to motivate and unite.
  • Excellent communication skills: know how to expose and defend your arguments.
  • Autonomy, sense of responsibility and strength of proposal.
  • Knowledge of technical watchmaking vocabulary.
