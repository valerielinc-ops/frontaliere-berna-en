Network Specialist / Engineer (Workplace & Infrastructure) (m/f/d) Liebefeld | Pensum: 80-100 % Work at the heart of healthcare and become part of #teamPHOENIX! Every day at PHOENIX Pharma Switzerland we have an important task: to provide millions of people with medicines and health products. With our team of over 1,000 dedicated employees, we ensure that these products are reliably and continuously distributed throughout Switzerland. Our various business units actively contribute to health care. An efficient and secure IT infrastructure is a key prerequisite for our success. Our success is based on the professionalism and commitment of our employees, who embody our corporate culture. Be the heartbeat of healthcare. Do you want to actively shape the digital infrastructure of a leading healthcare company? Then you are exactly right with us! As a Network Specialist / Engineer, you take on a central technical role in the further development, operation and transformation of our network and workplace infrastructure. You ensure that our IT systems and locations are reliably connected and that our internal customers can count on stable, secure and efficient services. Are you looking for a varied technical challenge where you can contribute your expertise, take responsibility and drive innovative solutions? Then we look forward to meeting you!

The heart of your work

In your role as Network Specialist / Engineer (Workplace & Infrastructure) (m/f/d), you are responsible for:

  • Planning, operation and continuous development of our network infrastructure (LAN/WAN/WLAN, SD-WAN, firewalls, VPN/SASE and Internet Connectivity).
  • Technical responsibility for network, workplace and infrastructure services and ensuring their stability, security and performance.
  • Central technical contact person for external network and infrastructure providers including coordination of technical requirements, clarifications and escalations.
  • Participation in the evaluation of new solutions, offers, transitions and service acquisitions as well as monitoring of SLAs, service quality and provider services.
  • Processing demanding support requests in the L2 / L3 area around network, workplace and infrastructure, including ensuring structured ticket management.
  • Active participation and standardization of our network and infrastructure landscape, especially in the environment of distributed multi-site and retail networks.
  • Technical cooperation and responsibility in network transformation, rollout, migration and maintenance projects – from technical inventory and conception to implementation, documentation and operational transfer.
  • Ensuring a high level of network resilience and taking redundancy and business continuity requirements into account.
  • Continuous observation of technological developments and introduction of new ideas to optimize our IT infrastructure.

What makes our hearts beat faster

  • Completed higher education in the IT field (bachelor) or an equivalent education. Alternatively, you convince through sound and demonstrable professional experience.
  • At least 3–5 years of professional experience in a larger company, ideally as a network specialist or network engineer.
  • In-depth knowledge of modern network architectures and security systems, in particular LAN/WAN/WLAN, SD-WAN, routing, firewall, VPN/SASE, network segmentation, NAC, Zero Trust and redundant network designs.
  • Very good knowledge in Microsoft administration, especially M365, Entra ID, Intune and Active Directory.
  • Good knowledge of workplace technologies, infrastructure services and hybrid environments (Entra ID and physical Active Directory).
  • Experience in the operation of complex and distributed multi-site infrastructures as well as in the technical control of external providers.
  • Experience in network transformation, rollout, migration and maintenance projects as well as in provider control.
  • Certifications in the network and security sector (e.g. Cisco CCNP, Fortinet or Palo Alto) are beneficial.
  • Microsoft certifications (e.g. MS-102) and knowledge of project management methods such as ITIL, PMI, PRINCE2 or IPMA are a plus.
  • Very good knowledge of German or French as well as good knowledge of the other language (at least level B1 in both languages).

You are a solution-oriented and independent personality who keeps a cool head and smile even in demanding situations. You work in a structured way, like to take on responsibility and keep track of several parallel tasks. With your communicative and team-oriented nature, you understand how to bring together different stakeholders and solve technical challenges pragmatically and goal-oriented.

  • Tailor-made advantages: We offer you 6 weeks of holidays and other attractive benefits such as employee benefits and a good pension solution.
  • Compatibility of private and professional life: Benefit from flexible working hours and up to 20% mobile office.
  • Further education: Join the PHOENIX Academy and benefit from further education tailored to your needs.
  • Career: You have the opportunity to develop nationally and internationally within the PHOENIX Group.
  • Team spirit: Team spirit is important to us. We promote this with regular small and large events and an open, uncomplicated cooperation.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
