The dissertation is located in the field of experimental personality and differential psychology and aims to examine interindividual differences in basic cognitive abilities as well as their connections with intelligence and creative thinking. Within the project, virtual reality (VR) paradigms are developed and evaluated, the established psychometric

  • Transfer tasks for capturing cognitive processes into everyday contexts. The focus shall be on:
  • Tasks to measure working memory, attention and processing speed. In addition to classical performance indicators such as reaction times and response accuracy, psychophysiological procedures such as heart rate variability (ECG) or eye tracking will be used. In addition, the position includes support in teaching in the Bachelor and Master degree programmes Psychology of the University of Bern.
  • Requirements

An above-average university master’s degree in psychology is expected. The job requires very good knowledge of German and English. Very good statistical knowledge and programming experience (e.g. R, Python, experimental software Unity) are particularly desired. Furthermore, a strong interest in experimental research and experience in deriving and evaluating psychophysiological data (EKG) are advantageous. In addition, there should be pleasure in the supervision of students in their final theses as well as in the implementation of courses offered in German.
