About us

The Institute of Social and Preventive Medicine (ISPM) of the University of Bern is actively involved in research, service and teaching in the fields of public health, clinical epidemiology, biostatistics and international environmental health (www.ispm.unibe.ch). With around 180 employees, we are a diverse and dynamic institute with a wide range of research and teaching activities. As of 01.01.2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a committed and experienced leader in the role of leader: in Central Services. The position is for the time being limited to 2 years with an option of extension. In this varied management role, you assume overall responsibility for the areas of human resources, finance, and IT with a team of around 10 employees. Together with your team, you ensure the professional and efficient organization of the central services of the institute. As a central interface between the research groups and the directorate, you actively participate in the exchange and cooperation between the leading areas. They bring together the different requirements of the institute, advise the institute management on overarching issues and support the implementation of strategic decisions. An essential task is also the financial and organizational management of the institute. They keep an eye on the overall budget and financial developments and ensure forward-looking and reliable planning even in the case of continuously changing framework conditions. You set priorities and develop viable solutions for the institute.
