Full description

Portrait At Rapperswil Primary School we teach around 220 children. Our dedicated team consists of 30 teachers, SHP and other employees. Together we accompany the children in four kindergartens and eight primary school classes. We especially appreciate our location in the middle of the green and yet close to the city.

Tasks

You teach one morning a week (preferably on Wednesday) at the Ruppoldsried Kindergarten Centre. This morning you design independently and with much joy. At the same time, you maintain a regular exchange with the class teacher.

Requirements

  • You are an established, uncomplicated and warm personality.
  • You enjoy welcoming the youngest children of our school and lovingly accompanying them in their first two years of school.
  • You have an educational education or experience in teaching.
  • Having your own vehicle is an advantage, as travelling by public transport is a bit cumbersome.

We offer

You can expect a well-established and helpful team, who will welcome you and support you if necessary. The school management also has an open ear for your concerns and suggestions. Our kindergarten centre is located in beautiful, rural surroundings and offers a wide range of play and learning opportunities inside and outside. A great forest square also invites you to discover and experience nature together with the children.
