Put to the Point

In this function, you work on versatile systems in the field of genius and bridge materials and focus your daily work on polyester and plastics (GRP) work. They combine craftsmanship with mechanical tasks and are primarily responsible for professional maintenance. If you like to work practically, have a soft spot for plastics and polyester processing and take responsibility for quality, an exciting and diverse working environment awaits you.

You can make this contribution

  • Perform maintenance work on bridge systems as well as on other genius material polyvalently
  • Manufacture of workpieces, individual items and mechanical appliances and technical documentation
  • Working and maintaining military equipment and training materials made of plastic, metal and wood
  • Advise, accompany and control customers (troops and third parties) in handling and returns with regard to driving safety, accident prevention, maintenance, cleaning and vehicle inventory
  • Document work carried out, ensure quality controls and contribute to compliance with safety regulations

This makes them unique.

  • Completed vocational training as polymechanic, agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ, construction machinery mechanic EFZ or equivalent mechanical training
  • Professional experience in mechanical manufacturing and maintenance, ideally in the field of machinery, equipment or military materials
  • Good knowledge of plastics processing (especially polyester and GRP work) as well as experience with metal and wood processing and conventional machines
  • Independent, structured and quality-conscious working methods as well as craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Teamwork, reliability and flexibility as well as a willingness to work in a variety of fields
  • Oral and written knowledge of a second official language as well as pleasure in dealing with customers (troop and third parties)
