Full description

Portrait For our school in the most beautiful location in the Worblental we are looking for a curative school teacher for the next school year. Our IF team is looking forward to a committed and interested new member.

https://www.stettlen.ch/bildungfs

Tasks

Clarifications / counselling of families / teaching of children of primary school age. Exchange with the entire IF team from the school circle and professional exchange with the Kolleg:innen of our MR-Verbundsgemeinden (Vechigen - Bolligen). The school facilities in Stettlen are easily accessible to each other on foot.

Requirements

Master of Arts in curative education (or equivalent diploma); started training for / SHP. Commitment and sensitivity in the field of curative education. They accompany and support students:women at primary or secondary level as IF teacher.

  • In cooperation with the IF and rule teachers, they promote the students according to their individual needs.
  • They regularly exchange views with the class team, specialist positions and school social work.

We offer

Pensum of 20-24L. Level and number of lessons will be discussed in advance with the SL and will be set at the commute meeting at the end of February / beginning of March. Accompaniment of young people zT in multi-year classes. Separate IF office in the main building and workplace on site. The existing, motivated IF team is looking forward to a positioned, communicative and active new member. Overall manageable school with primary, real and secondary classes (around 350 SuS, 50LP).

Good accessibility of the place of work with public transport (Libero basic subscription) Free parking at the school
