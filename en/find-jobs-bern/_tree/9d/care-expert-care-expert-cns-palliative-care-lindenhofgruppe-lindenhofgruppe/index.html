What you do

  • You ensure and develop the quality of care together with specialist development, care management and education

  • As part of the shared leadership team and the interprofessional palliative care consultancy service, you actively shape the cooperation with
  • You advise and accompany patients and their loved ones person-centered, especially in complex situations

What sets you apart

  • Professional experience in the field of palliative care is desired and you advantageously bring knowledge and interest in the field of oncology
  • Early Palliative Care is not a foreign word for you
  • You are eager to actively shape the specialized palliative care department and to break new ground in palliative care and oncology
