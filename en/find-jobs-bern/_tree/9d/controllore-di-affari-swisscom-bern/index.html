# Business controller

Il tuo futuro inizia

As a Business Controller, you and your team are responsible for controlling Wireline products for private and business customers. You plan, analyze and control our financial indicators reliably and prepare well-founded decision-making bases. You work independently, think pragmatically and actively seek the exchange with different stakeholders. With your commitment, you contribute to further developing our services and achieving a sustainable impact.

  • Controlling Wireline products for private and business customers
  • Responsibility for planning, budgeting, forecasts and monthly reporting
  • Create business cases and prepare the basis for decision-making in cooperation with stakeholders
  • Analyse and interpret key figures and identify opportunities and risks at an early stage

Your skills

  • Master’s degree (Uni/FH) in accounting, controlling or comparable training
  • Several years of experience in finance and controlling
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office, SAP and IBM Planning Analytics
  • Experience in creating budgets, forecasts and business cases
  • Analytical thinking and the ability to quickly identify connections and derive sound conclusions
  • Committed, responsible and independent working methods
  • Communication strength in exchange with different stakeholders and hierarchical levels
  • Very good German and English skills

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
