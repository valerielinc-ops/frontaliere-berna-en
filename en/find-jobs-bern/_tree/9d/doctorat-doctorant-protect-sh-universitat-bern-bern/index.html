About us

The research department of the University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (KJP) of the Universitarian Psychiatric Center (UPZ) Bern deals with questions of the origin as well as the prevention, early detection and treatment of mental disorders in childhood and adolescence. At the interface between research and care, we have the unique opportunity to gain scientific insights directly from everyday clinical practice and to transfer these into diagnostic and therapeutic concepts in a timely manner. We use a wide range of modern research methods to better understand mental disorders and to develop and evaluate innovative diagnostic and therapeutic methods – increasingly also digitally. In this context, our working group under the direction of Prof. Michael Kaess is a doctoral student for a doctoral project on “Self-Harm and New Media” within the framework of PROTECT-SH, a joint project with partners in seven countries funded by Horizon Europe. PROTECT-SH investigates in a multicentre longitudinal cohort, through which mechanisms the use of social media promotes or protects self-harming and suicidal behavior in adolescents and young adults. This will combine clinical interviews, questionnaires, Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA) and passive smartphone data

  • Based on this, a digital intervention is developed and evaluated in two large randomized-controlled trials (prevention and clinical intervention).

You will be involved in the implementation of the cohort study and the two intervention studies at the Berne site – from recruitment to diagnostic surveys to the support of participants in the EMA and intervention phases – and will work on questions about the mechanisms between social media and self-harming behavior or the effectiveness of the digital intervention. We offer an attractive research environment in an internationally successful research group, comprehensive supervision of the dissertation, diverse training opportunities (e.g. clinical-diagnostic interviews, modern statistical procedures) and networking in a large European consortium. The position is limited to 3 years with optional extension to 4 years. The remuneration is carried out in accordance with the guidelines of the University of Bern. Their tasks
