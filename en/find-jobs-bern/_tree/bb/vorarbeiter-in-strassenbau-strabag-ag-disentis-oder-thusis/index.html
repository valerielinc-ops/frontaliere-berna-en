Foreman: in road construction Your contribution with us

  • You lead your team on the construction site and take action yourself if necessary
  • You organize and coordinate the daily work in road and civil engineering
  • You support the polisher in the planning of personnel, machines and materials
  • You ensure a professional, secure and timely execution
  • You lead daily reports, create dimensions and document the construction progress
  • You work in road, surface, trench, plant management and sewer work
  • You make sure that the requirements for quality and work safety are adhered to

What matters to us

  • You have completed basic training in construction, ideally as a road builder: in EFZ
  • You have completed further training as a foremen: or bring appropriate professional experience
  • You are familiar with road and civil engineering and enjoy working directly on the construction site
  • You can lead a team and organize work independently
  • You work reliably, solution-oriented and keep an overview even with a lot of operations
  • You will communicate in German
  • You have the driving licence category B

Our added value for you At STRABAG you work with modern construction technology, high-performance construction machinery and high-quality equipment.
