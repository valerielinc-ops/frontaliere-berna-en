Full description

Portrait Schliern is located southwest of the city of Bern and is easily accessible by public transport (bus no. 10). The school is located on the southern edge of the village and borders the recreation area with agricultural farms.

At our school, 20 school and 8 kindergarten classes are currently being taught. The 560 children are accompanied by a total of about 86 teachers in their daily work. We perceive heterogeneity positively, attach great importance to a good relationship with the pupils: inside and the parents. We are characterized by a good team spirit. Our focus in school development is the "New Authority".
