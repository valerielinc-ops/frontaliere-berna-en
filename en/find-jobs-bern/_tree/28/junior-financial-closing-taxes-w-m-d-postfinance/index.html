Full description

In the Competence Center for Accounting and Financial Accounting, you ensure that PostFinance operates on a high-quality and reliable financial database. Together we ensure correct accounting in accordance with IFRS and RVB and thus create an important basis for the financial management of PostFinance. If you like numbers, contexts and precise work, you are exactly right with us. You take responsibility for your own topics and contribute with your work to high-quality degrees and reliable financial reporting.

You can do that.

  • You contribute to the preparation of PostFinance’s monthly, quarterly and annual accounts in accordance with IFRS and RVB
  • You independently assume responsibility for defined topics in the ledger and in the secondary books
  • You support the financial reporting to the Group and the preparation of the statutory annual accounts
  • You process inquiries and ad hoc orders in your subject areas independently and reliably
  • You are involved in projects and actively participate in the further development and optimization of our processes

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic commercial training and ideally further education in finance and accounting
  • First practical experience in accounting, finance or accounting
  • Enjoying numbers and a strong understanding of financial contexts
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Openness to change and motivation, to further develop existing processes
  • Good knowledge of Excel
  • Experience with SAP as Plus

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
