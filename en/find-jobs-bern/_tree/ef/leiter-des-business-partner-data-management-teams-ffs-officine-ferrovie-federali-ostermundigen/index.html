Head of business partner data management team 60-100% | FFS - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of the business partner data management team Ostermundigen / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or on date to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What we offer.

Direct and develop an interdisciplinary team in data management. Together with the Product Owner you create conditions for sustainable success and promote skills development, collaboration and organization. You actively contribute to shaping the future of our data management and work closely with industry management, product management, architecture and other specialist departments. You will find exciting themes related to SAP MDG, S/4HANA, personal data, data quality, automation and modern data management solutions, with wide margins of action, flexible work forms and attractive benefits. What you can move.

  • As a Responsible, direct and develop employees, creating an environment where responsibility is assumed and professional and personal development is promoted.
  • Together with the Product Owner, ensure that priority, skills and skills available are optimally aligned.
  • Develop skills and skills in a targeted manner, quickly recognize any gaps and appropriate development measures.
  • Plan the team's composition far-sightedly, as well as the resources and skills needed, and participate in recruiting and onboarding of new employees.
  • Promote effective collaboration, support continuous improvement and help the team overcome obstacles and conflicts constructively.
  • You actively contribute to the evolution of data management and automation of business processes and accompany changes in digital transformation.

What you bring with you.

  • You have experience in running and developing people, ideally in a data-oriented context, and have a solid professional experience.
  • In IT, digitalization or transformation you have already held leadership roles or assumed responsibility and carry out projects reliably.
  • You have a good understanding of data management and automated and data-based business processes.
  • You are familiar with agile and product-oriented working methods and have knowledge within SAFe. Ideally you have experience in the SAP environment, especially with SAP MDG and S/4HANA.
  • You have a higher degree of study (SUP/University) in management informatics, Data Science or equivalent; it is a preferential title for an improvement in leadership.
  • Know advanced German (C1); knowledge of a further national language is a plus.

Salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This assumption is governed by the bond code and is based on a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
