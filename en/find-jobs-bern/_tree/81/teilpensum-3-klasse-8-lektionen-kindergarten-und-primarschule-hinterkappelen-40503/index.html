Full description

Portrait The Hinterkappelen Primary School is an urban suburban school. Hinterkappelen is a district of the community Wohlen. Our school facility is located in the middle of the Kappelenring, in a traffic-free zone and is optimally connected to public transport. Our lively school currently houses a total of 16 classes, two per school year and four kindergartens. The college comprises about 50 teachers. On the school grounds are a day school, sports halls, a football field and a play and basketball court - of course accessible to all children.

Tasks

From 10. January 2027 we are looking for a teacher in a third class. You will teach your class during 8 lessons in the subject areas of French, music, German, as well as exercise and sports on Thursday (full day) and Wednesday (07.30-09.05 h). They design a varied and differentiated lesson according to curriculum 21 and build a sustainable, warm relationship with the children, the guardians, as well as the colleagues. They work closely with the class teacher.

Requirements

You are a qualified teacher (Bachelor Primary Education/seminary patent) with a positive attitude towards the students, guardians and colleagues. They are ready to support and further develop the existing school building structure and look forward to working together in the step-by-step team and in the overall college.

We offer

We offer a permanent position at our diverse primary school from the 10th. January 2027, where you can contribute your values and count on good cooperation in the step team, in the college, with specialists, school social work and co-management. We offer you a mentorship.

  • In addition, the close cooperation within the class team and the great support from the SHP should be highlighted.
  • Good infrastructure and excellent connections to public transport, as well as free parking are further advantages of this attractive location.
