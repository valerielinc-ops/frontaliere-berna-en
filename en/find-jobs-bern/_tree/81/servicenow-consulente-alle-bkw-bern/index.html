Full description

ServiceNow is your universe – and you actively shape it. You bring people, requirements and technology together, develop suitable solutions and ensure that ideas become functioning services.

Your design space

  • You analyze and specify requirements with the departments, moderate workshops and translate needs into features and stories
  • From technical requirements, you develop solution designs and evaluate variants according to feasibility, maintainability, scalability, security, operation and integration
  • ServiceNow solutions and automation are designed and implemented end-to-end – from configuration to technical adjustments
  • You control internal and external developers technically and ensure that the implementation meets the requirements, designs and platform specifications
  • New ServiceNow functionalities and technologies are tested for their benefits and bring suitable approaches to the further development of the platform
  • In 2nd- and 3rd-level support, you analyze complex disruptions and derive sustainable improvements for the supported solutions.

You bring that with you

  • You bring in-depth practical experience in configuration and automation with ServiceNow
  • With ITSM, CMDB, HRSD or Vulnearbility Response you know well
  • You have experience in scripting and can independently implement technical adjustments in ServiceNow
  • You master German and English safely and can communicate understandably with different target groups
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented and combine pragmatic approach with a sense of responsibility
  • As a high-communication team player, you convey technical content appropriate to the addressee and also represent solutions to different stakeholders.
