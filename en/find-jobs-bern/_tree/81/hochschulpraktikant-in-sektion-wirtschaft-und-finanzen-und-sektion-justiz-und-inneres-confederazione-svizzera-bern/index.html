Put to the Point

As a university intern, you support the sections Economics and Finance (WF) and Justice and Home Affairs (JI) on the pulse of Swiss European policy. Its main tasks are the coordination of existing and the negotiation of new bilateral agreements with the EU. You will get a privileged insight into Swiss European policy, the functioning of the Federal Administration and the institutions of the EU.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20731
  • Location: Bundeshaus Ost, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • provide specific analyses related to bilateral agreements with the EU;
  • research on EU-relevant topics in the areas of migration and security, financial, tax and trade issues; agriculture, veterinary and health;
  • They support the coordination of EU processes in the area of migration and security (Council working groups, notifications).
  • They contribute the position of the European Directorate-General in the context of official consultations and answer citizens’ inquiries about Swiss European policy.
  • They develop background and speech notes for visit dossiers and participate in the preparation of the annual brochure "Switzerland-EU in figures";
  • They maintain regular contact with other offices of the Federal Administration, Swiss embassies abroad and the Swiss mission in Brussels

This makes them unique.

  • They have a strong and demonstrable interest in European integration and Swiss European policy;

  • They are characterised by an independent and efficient working method and are able to coordinate across departments;
  • They have very good knowledge of two official languages as well as English.

On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

The State Secretariat (STS) manages the development, coordination and planning of foreign policy and is responsible for reporting to the political authorities and the management of Switzerland’s foreign network. It is responsible for implementing Switzerland’s foreign policy strategy. The most important dossiers include cooperation with the United Nations (UN), the development of relations with the European Union (EU), and security and peace policy.
