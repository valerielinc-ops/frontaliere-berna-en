Full description

You ensure a coherent and professional appearance of our sales outlets. You realize design and communication concepts directly on site, accompany new openings and create attractive worlds of experience for customers at events and special events.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You ensure a uniform and professional brand presence at our points of sale
  • You assemble, replace and update advertising and communication media directly on site
  • You coordinate and realize design projects for events and events together with internal and external partners
  • You take over the purchase of consumables, manage the warehouse and keep an eye on budget requirements
  • You carry out quality controls and derive improvement measures from them

Requirements

  • Professional experience: as a polydesigner or as an advertising technician
  • Completed basic or further education: in Polydesign 3D, advertising technology or a comparable field
  • You work independently, reliably and find pragmatic solutions
  • You bring team spirit and like to take part in craft work
  • You use MS Office securely in everyday work, knowledge of Adobe Creative Cloud is an advantage
  • You are in possession of category B driving licence
  • in German (negotiation confident)
