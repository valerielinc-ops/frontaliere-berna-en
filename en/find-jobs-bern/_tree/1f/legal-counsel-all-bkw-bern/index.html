Full description

Temporary for 2 years

Would you like to deal with legal issues directly related to energy supply, digitalization and government procedures? In this role, you take on responsibility in a future-relevant environment and design solutions with a tangible effect.

Your field of activity

  • You conduct legal proceedings relating to the smart meter rollout in public authorities and courts independently from initiation to conclusion
  • Legal issues relating to smart meters, data protection and data transmission as well as in the area of basic services (energy and grid connection) will be assessed and developed and represented in the internal offices.
  • In discussions with customers and authorities, you represent the legal positions of BKW consistently and appropriately
  • You create legal documents, contracts and memoranda and answer official questions
  • Together with the specialist areas, you will continue to develop processes, templates and information content
  • Collaboration with internal specialists and external stakeholders enables you to gain diverse insights into technical and regulatory topics

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed Swiss law degree (master level) and ideally a law patent with several years of experience as a lawyer, corporate lawyer or in the field of public administration or courts
  • Your quick comprehension and technical understanding help you to classify complex issues safely
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss administrative and procedural law as well as contract law You as well as an above-average commitment and a convincing appearance
  • You enjoy working in a team, enjoy contact with customers and are interested in technical connections, especially in connection with infrastructure and digital technologies
  • Your structured working method and a high level of commitment are prerequisites for your success
  • You master German stylishly and you also communicate professionally in French

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
