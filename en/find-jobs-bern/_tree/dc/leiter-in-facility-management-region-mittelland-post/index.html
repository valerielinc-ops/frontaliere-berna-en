Head of Facility Management, Central Plateau Region — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you want to play a key role in facility management and lead an interdisciplinary team in the Mittelland region? You combine strategic leadership with a strong understanding of operational facility services such as engineering, home maintenance and cleaning? We look forward to your application. With you, we are shaping the future of Post’s facility management.

You can do that.

  • As Head of Facility Management of the Mittelland region, you are responsible for the personnel and professional management of a team of 12 directly subordinates and approx. 120 indirectly subordinated employees: responsible.
  • You are responsible for Facility Services in the Mittelland region. Your area of responsibility includes the headquarters of Post and PostFinance in Bern, other large properties in Bern and Basel as well as the area organization Mittelland with house maintenance, cleaning and technology.
  • You develop the organization in your region further and ensure that processes and structures are optimally tailored to the needs of our partners.
  • With your support in planning and implementing the strategy, you contribute significantly to further development. You control resources efficiently, based on KPI and target agreements.
  • As a central regional contact person, you maintain dialogue with our partners and ensure binding, cross-sectoral cooperation.
  • As a member of the management team, you take on joint responsibility for the strategic and targeted further development of Post’s facility management.

You bring that with you

  • You have higher education or further education in facility management, business administration or a comparable field of expertise.
  • In addition, you bring several years of management and professional experience in commercial facility management, real estate management or a comparable environment and convince us.
  • In addition, you think and act entrepreneurially, control organizations based on key figures and develop sustainable solutions, which makes an important contribution.
  • You convince with your strategic and conceptual way of thinking and make informed decisions even in demanding situations.
  • In addition, you communicate appropriately, promote cross-divisional cooperation and build sustainable relationships at different hierarchical levels, which you succeed successfully.
  • You communicate very well orally and in writing in a first national language and have good knowledge of a second national language.

In order to promote linguistic and gender diversity, we are particularly pleased about applications from women or people who speak French or Italian.
