Full description

The human resources area managed at the national level is responsible and manages the human resources activity of the entire SSR. Human resources and the HR Management team within HR Services support SSR employees on all personnel issues, provide a modern range of complementary training and training, and are responsible for the holistic implementation of advanced fundamental HR processes.

Your Mission

We are looking for an HR specialist with immediate effect or to agree. The SSR generally allows for self-employment and flexible work, but we also need a certain presence in Bern and the regions. You will assume a central role in the HR Services of the SSR and will be responsible for:

  • You will support HR partners in all administrative issues and HR processes as the first point of contact
  • You will accompany the recruitment process, write employment contracts and references, and manage a wide range of correspondence
  • You will manage personnel data with changes in SAP HR and other systems such as Umantis
  • You will be the contact person for employees and managers
  • You will take care of all administrative life cycle management
  • You will be required to perform standard HR reporting and analysis
  • You will participate in optimization and harmonization via national projects

Your profile

  • Business vocational training followed by corresponding HR training (HR Management)
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar function, including knowledge of SAP HR module
  • Appreciation of administrative, self-employment, self-employment and contact with persons, as well as team work
  • Because of the possibility of a supra-regional assignment, the language skills in German and French are required (Italian in addition would be an asset)
  • In the global transformation of the company towards Digital, digital skills assets are strongly desired
  • Opening to change and ability to adapt with serenity in a changing environment

What we offer you

  • A varied and stimulating working environment in a dynamic and national media enterprise
  • Various tasks with personal responsibility and creative freedom
  • A college team that supports each other and shares its knowledge

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
