At the interface of justice, police and migration

The General Secretariat is the central staff and support body of the departmental leadership in the Federal Ministry of Justice and Police. It creates the conditions so that the head of the FYD can fulfill the government tasks. The basis for decision-making for Federal Council, Parliament and other important business is provided, activities of the department are initiated, planned and coordinated, as well as the funds available are managed.
