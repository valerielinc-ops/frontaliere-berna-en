Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

Do you want to be responsible for challenging ICT projects from the idea to the successful introduction and actively participate in an agile organization? Then at Galenica IT you can expect a versatile task at the interface between business, IT, value streams, platform services and external partners. Depending on your experience and the assumed project responsibility, the classification as a professional or senior takes place.

You can do that.

  • You lead ICT projects from initialization to successful transfer to operation.
  • You are responsible for project goals, scope, deadlines, quality, costs, resources and risks.
  • You control project budgets, which can range from several 100,000 CHF depending on the project.
  • You will lead interdisciplinary project teams professionally and coordinate internal and external partners.
  • You’ll work closely with our value streams, platform and product teams, and divisions.
  • You create transparency through professional reporting and prepare decisions for clients and management.
  • You will moderate workshops and project committees and accompany changes up to sustainable anchoring.
  • You are proactive in the further development of our project management standards, methods and governance.

You bring that with you

  • Higher degree in computer science, business informatics, business administration or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in managing ICT projects
  • Good knowledge of classical, agile and hybrid project management methods, SAFe is an advantage
  • Experience in stakeholder, risk, change and supplier management
  • Strong communication, moderation and problem-solving skills
  • Independent, structured and results-oriented working method
  • Very good German and English skills, French skills are an advantage
  • We expect a recognized project management certification (IPMA, PMP, PRINCE2 or HERMES).

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country.
