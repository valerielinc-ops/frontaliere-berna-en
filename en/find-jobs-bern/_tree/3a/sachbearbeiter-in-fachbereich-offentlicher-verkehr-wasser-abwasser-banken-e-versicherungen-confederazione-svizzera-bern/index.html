Price monitoring combats abusive prices of market-powerful companies and operates in the area of administered prices. The price monitor can make recommendations to authorities and reduce prices by mutual agreement or by order. It informs the public about its analyses, assessments and results. Price monitoring is an administrative office assigned to the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research WBF, but operates independently.
