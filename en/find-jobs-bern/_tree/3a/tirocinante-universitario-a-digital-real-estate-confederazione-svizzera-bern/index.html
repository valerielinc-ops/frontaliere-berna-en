Put to the Point

Fresh from college or in the middle of college? With us, you will gain valuable practical experience at the interface of project work, support, documentation and coordination. As part of a university internship, you will support our team in the organization and further development of digital work processes and make an important contribution to the quality and traceability of projects and services. You will gain insight into the project and support environment of the Federal Administration, work closely with various specialist bodies and learn how digital platforms, processes and standards are used and further developed in the construction and real estate context. You will deepen your organizational, analytical and communicative skills and gain a solid foundation for your further career path.
