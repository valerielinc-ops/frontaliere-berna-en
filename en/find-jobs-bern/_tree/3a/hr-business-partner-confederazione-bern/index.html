Put to the Point

You want to shape HR strategically and at the same time work close to the people and their concerns? In this versatile function, you combine the advice of the management and executives with the further development of central HR topics. You take responsibility for the entire Employee Life Cycle, Personnel Development and Occupational Health Management and contribute your ideas to the further development of the organization. A versatile task awaits you with creative leeway and short decision-making paths, in a small team and a multilingual working environment in which German and French are the focus.

You can make this contribution

  • Act as a sparring partner for the management in the implementation of the strategy, the further development of the organization and the design of change processes
  • Advising and accompanying managers in personnel and labour law issues as well as in demanding management situations throughout the entire Employee Life Cycle - from personnel planning, recruitment, development to release
  • Advising employees on all HR topics
  • Responsible for the personnel development of EAK, including training budget, further education measures, talent management and succession planning, as well as occupational health management with occupational health promotion and case management
  • Control of HR processes throughout the entire Employee Life Cycle, in close cooperation with HR administration, as well as further development of HR tools, policies and processes
  • Analyzing HR key figures, creating reporting and deriving appropriate measures as well as being responsible for internal HR communication and organising events
  • Managing HR projects and employees in cross-divisional projects in order to sustainably develop the organization

  • Entrepreneurial, analytical and networked thinking and experience in the implementation of HR strategies, organizational and personnel development
  • Responsible, solution-oriented and independent working method
