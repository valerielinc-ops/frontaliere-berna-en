Scrum Master (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Scrum Master (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You will help to further optimize and automate rail traffic in Switzerland with a new Traffic Management System. In an agile environment, you work together with ten agile teams in the Agile Release Train Capacity Management and design products from idea to operation. Short decision-making paths, collaborative cooperation and a lot of space await you to anchor effective working methods and develop yourself professionally and methodically. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • As a Scrum Master, you coordinate and develop two teams into successful and self-organized agile teams and thus strengthen the joint delivery capability.
  • You moderate Scrum events, promote impact-oriented collaboration, make obstacles visible and support continuous improvement.
  • In the Agile Release Train, you actively participate in joint activities and key topics and contribute to smooth value delivery across team boundaries.
  • In close cooperation with other Scrum Masters, you make dependencies transparent, help solve impediments and promote cross-team collaboration.
  • You exemplify personal responsibility and support the teams in taking responsibility and making sound decisions.
  • With a clear view of team dynamics, you accompany change processes and support people in acting effectively in a complex environment.

You bring that with you.

  • As a Servant Leader, you convince with a natural flair for people and a strong interest in team processes and dynamics in complex situations.
  • With open communication and a cooperative approach, you strengthen trust, cooperation and clarity in the team and in the organization.
  • An independent, pragmatic and responsible way of working characterizes you and you drive personal development and continuous improvement noticeably forward.
  • Experience with scaled agility to SAFe and coaching you bring and you are well versed in the use of Jira, Miro, Microsoft Teams and Confluence.
  • A certification as a Scrum Master or a comparable education is available and a further education in SAFe rounds off your profile.
  • You communicate safely in German and English in word and writing and industry knowledge in the railway context is an advantage.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This job is in requirement level K, regional allowance level 1.
