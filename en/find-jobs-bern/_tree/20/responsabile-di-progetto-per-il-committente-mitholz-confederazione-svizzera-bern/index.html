No risks from ammunition in Mitholz – in this complex and unique project, exciting and diverse challenges await you as well as a motivated project team.

  • Entry date: by agreement

  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21119

You can make this contribution

  • Design, implement and monitor complex (special) civil engineering, environmental and material management processes
  • Management of construction projects in the environmental sector (e.g. hydraulic engineering and environmental compensation measures) from the preliminary study, realization to the transfer of operation/use
  • Assuming process and result responsibility for costs, deadlines and quality for the assigned work packages
  • Assign and lead the external planner teams and contractors as well as monitor their performance
  • Leading the project teams and working groups with the active involvement of other project roles (e.g. security officers, operators, construction-related ordnance clearance)
  • Ensuring the exchange of information and active coordination with the other subprojects in the Mitholz project

This makes you unique

  • University of applied sciences or university degree in construction (e.g. civil engineer), cultural or environmental engineer
  • Further training in business administration or additional training in project management (Certified Project Manager IPMA Level C)
  • Several years of functional experience and experience in the project management of large projects (ideally for material management processes or in the infrastructure and/or environmental sector)
  • High communication skills, self-responsibility and teamwork
  • You have oral and written knowledge of a second official language
