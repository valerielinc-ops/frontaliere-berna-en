Full description

Portrait TOGETHER STRONG - Relationship work, determination, networking and the experience of self-efficacy are our focus. With a lot of commitment and good framework conditions, we work with the children for the future of our society. In a heterogeneous environment, we provide the basis for lifelong learning.

Tasks

As a class help, you take on the role of a class assistance. The teacher gives you specific tasks. For example, they guide individual children in an activity, play with a group of children and take on further tasks depending on the subject area. The current call includes 14h accompaniment in three different classes (3rd, 5th and 6th class). An hour division by class is conceivable.

The following weekly assignments are planned until the summer holidays 2027:

  • Mondays from 08.00-12.00 & 13.45-15.45 (6th class)
  • Wednesdays from 7.15 am to 9.15 am (5th class)
  • Tuesdays from 13.45-15.45 / Wednesdays from 10.00-12.00 / Thursdays from 13.45-15.45 (3rd class)

Requirements

  • Joy in heterogeneous groups
  • Flexibility
  • Flair to work with children/teens
  • Willingness to influence in the dynamic children’s group

We offer

  • Close cooperation with the class team
  • Compact timetable
  • Good public transport connection

We look forward to seeing you!
