Full description

University Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

We are a place of many possibilities for all dedicated professionals. Knowledge, skills and ideas count. Origin, age and gender not. Would you like to make a decisive contribution to the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of children and adolescents in the Canton of Bern? Then engage as a senior psychologist in the Bern ambulatorium of the University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (KJP). The Ambulatorium Bern is the central point of contact for patients of the entire KJP and thus forms the heart of ambulatory care. Our offer covers a wide range from triage to crisis intervention to diagnosis and treatment of children and adolescents with a wide range of mental illnesses. As part of an interdisciplinary management team, you have the chance to further develop the offer and actively shape it. Become effective where many things come together and your expertise is particularly in demand. Bring your passion for psychotherapy to the care of patients, innovative diagnostic and treatment concepts as well as the training and professional support of young colleagues. Do you keep track of complex situations, take on responsibility and develop viable solutions together with your team? Then you are right with us!

Your tasks

  • You lead the ambulatory together with the senior psychologist and other members of the management team
  • You will play a key role in the guidance and training of psychological and medical assistants in evidence-based diagnosis and treatment of children and adolescents with involvement of the extended system (family, school, education)
  • You take on conceptual work, develop the diagnostic and therapeutic offer and supervise psychological and medical assistants
  • You participate in the care of children and adolescents as part of the concept of the Ambulatorium Bern
  • You act at the interface between clinic and research and support recruitment in research projects
  • You have opportunities for engagement in the teaching and internal training programs of the KJP

Your profile

  • You have successfully completed your psychotherapy training and gained experience in a similar environment
  • You want to apply, pass on and conceptually shape diagnostic and treatment concepts according to current evidence-based guidelines
  • You are motivated and committed together to shape the future of child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy and to create new treatment offers
  • You want to take on the challenge of professionally and personnel competently leading and designing and implementing complex processes and concepts
  • You enjoy taking responsibility and training a new generation of psychotherapists
  • Teamwork and interdisciplinary cooperation are an enrichment for you
  • The networked and interdisciplinary cooperation with people inside and outside our clinic is yours
  • You are motivated to support treatment-related research

The UPZ

The University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
