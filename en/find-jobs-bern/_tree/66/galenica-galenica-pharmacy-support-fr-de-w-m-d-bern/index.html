Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

You know the processes in pharmacies, want to use your experience in a new environment and at the same time delve deeper into the world of IT and support? With us, you combine your expertise with direct customer contact and support users: competent in German and French. With your practical knowledge and your solution-oriented nature, you also contribute to the ongoing development of our support. The place of work is Bern or Écublens, with monthly presence at the other location.

You can do that.

  • You provide the 1st-level support in German and French and accompany users: competent and customer-oriented.
  • You analyze, process and prioritize requests and incidents structured and solution-oriented.
  • You bring your understanding of processes in the pharmacy and retail environment to the support.
  • You work with modern customer service solutions like Jira and contribute to the continuous improvement of support processes and documentation.
  • Together with internal partners, especially Triapharm, you will design, organize and support training and support activities for the teams in the pharmacies.

You bring that with you

  • You have an apprenticeship as a pharmaceutical assistant: in EFZ or an equivalent apprenticeship, as well as at least 5 years of professional experience in a pharmacy.
  • You speak German or French and can communicate in the other language and understand it.
  • You know the processes in pharmacies and/or retail and bring experience in customer service or in a customer-oriented environment.
  • You have good knowledge of Triapharm software
  • Experience with Jira or similar service management tools is a plus.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented, communicate clearly and are interested in new technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI).

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
