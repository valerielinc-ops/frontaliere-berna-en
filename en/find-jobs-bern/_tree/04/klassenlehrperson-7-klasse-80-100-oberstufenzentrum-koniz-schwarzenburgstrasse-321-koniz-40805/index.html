Full description

Portrait The Oberstufenzentrum Köniz is located in a beautiful, central location. Currently, 250 SuS are taught in 12 classes in the model Manuel. For the school year 27/28 an additional 7th class will be opened. This is why we are looking for 1. August 2027 to complement our team

Tasks

  • You are a class teacher at a real or mixed real/second class.
  • At your class, you teach a minimum of 60 percent or more. The following subjects are available for selection: German, Franz, Math, English, NT, RZG, M&I, BG, Music, Sport. ERG is set, TG, TxG and WAH are also optimally possible. AdS, IVE and subject areas at other classes depending on offer and arrangement.
  • On Thursday from 16.20 to 18.00 clock there is blocking time, which is used for conferences and exchanges.

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have completed training for cycle 3 or are in training as a teacher.
  • You are a communicative, team capable and dutiful personality.
  • Working with the children is your focus; you accompany and promote them effectively.
  • You enjoy working in a dedicated, thinking and helpful team.
  • You are committed to good parental work and actively participate in school projects (project weeks, winter camp, school festival).
  • You can also think outside the box and are not afraid to bring in new ideas.

We offer

  • Permanent employment under cantonal employment conditions (LAG)
  • Assistance in familiarization by experienced college, school management, school social work and domestic service
  • Pleasant working environment in a helpful, committed and motivated team
  • Classrooms with modern infrastructure
  • Excellent public transport connection: The school is 13 minutes' drive from Bern Train Station.
