SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. You want to rethink controlling and use AI, data and automation to make financial processes faster and more effective. In performance controlling, you work together with experienced colleagues for selected business areas of SBB. We convert controlling to clean data models, AI-based reports and automated processes. You go ahead, try new things, and handle models and data carefully. The position is aimed at graduates: women who want more than standard. The post is provisionally limited to 2 years. After expiration, the permanent employment option can be checked. You can move that.

  • After familiarization, you take over step by step completion, planning and reporting and contribute to reliable results.
  • You analyze deviations, check data for plausibility and provide insights for decision-makers to understand.
  • With AI-supported tools, you develop reports and analyses and align them consistently with the control requirements.
  • Data from different sources is modeled and linked to robust basics and ensures quality.
  • With automation, you reduce manual processes and drive the use of AI in controlling from the idea to implementation.

You bring that with you.

  • Curiosity, initiative and the willingness to constructively question the existing character distinguish you.
  • A careful and analytical way of working is a matter of course and you deal responsibly with models, data and results.
  • First knowledge of Power BI, Anaplan, Python or SQL is available or you learn new tools quickly independently.
  • You already use AI tools such as Copilot or ChatGPT and want to use their potential in controlling specifically.
  • A completed university degree (uni or ETH) in business administration, finance or business informatics is mandatory.
  • You master German at least at level C1 and ideally you have knowledge of another national language at least at level A2.
