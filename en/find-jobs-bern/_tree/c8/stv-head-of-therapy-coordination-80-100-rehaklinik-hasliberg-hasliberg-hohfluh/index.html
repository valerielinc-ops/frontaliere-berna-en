Full description

The Meiringen Private Clinic is located in the impressive landscape of the Haslital, in the Bernese Oberland. Here people with all forms of psychological suffering find reception, treatment and care. The third-largest psychiatric clinic in the Canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient primary psychiatric care of the eastern Bernese upper country. Care and care take place according to current medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge. As a member of Swiss Leading Hospitals, Privatklinik Meiringen is one of the leading private clinics in Switzerland.

To strengthen our medical-psychological treatment teams in inpatient adult and old-age psychiatry, we are looking for psychologists in further education. If you value a versatile clinical activity in an interdisciplinary environment, we look forward to receiving your application. We actively promote your postgraduate education, and you get the opportunity to gain clinical experience and deepen your therapeutic skills.

Their tasks

  • Case management within an interdisciplinary treatment team under professional supervision
  • Coordination and control of the inpatient treatment of adult patients with clinical pictures from the entire psychiatric spectrum
  • Implementation of psychotherapeutic treatments in individual and group setting as well as management of system discussions
  • Conduct of clinical and test psychological diagnostics
  • Documentation of treatment courses and preparation of reports and other relevant technical documents
  • Taking over foreground day services
  • Ensuring professional, patient-oriented and smooth care during the inpatient stay
  • Close cooperation with doctors and other specialists in the interdisciplinary team

Our expectations

  • Completed university degree in psychology
  • Ongoing Swiss-recognised psychotherapy training or planned timely start of training
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • High social competence and appreciative treatment of patients and work colleagues
  • Open, resilient and flexible personality with a strong sense of responsibility
  • Good communication skills and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • Interest and enthusiasm for interdisciplinary cooperation in a multi-professional team
  • Independent, reliable and committed working method

Our offer

  • Exciting and varied work in the inpatient psychiatric environment
  • Opportunity to get to know different specialist stations and gain valuable experience in different areas
  • Working in an interdisciplinary, professional and committed team
  • Regular supervision as well as various further education and training opportunities
  • Contemporary working and employment conditions with attractive social benefits
  • Extensive benefits, including a free day on the birthday, free parking, various free sports offers as part of health promotion and varied staff events
  • Own day care center on the clinic area
