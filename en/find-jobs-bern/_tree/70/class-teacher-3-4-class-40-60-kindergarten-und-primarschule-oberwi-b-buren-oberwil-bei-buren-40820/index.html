Full description

Portrait At the rural school Oberwil near Büren more than 80 pupils in 4 classes (1x kindergarten, 1x 1./2. grade, 1x 3./4). Class and 1x 5./6. class) accompanied by around ten teachers. We maintain a family and trusting relationship with each other and the personal encounter is very important to us. This applies above all to the learning environment of our children, but also to the treatment of their parents. We are a dedicated college that looks forward to rethinking the school with you and creating an environment where everyone feels comfortable. A school with a heart.

Tasks

Line of a 3./4. Class with focus on independent/self-determined learning and potential development of children

  • Creating a positive and supportive learning environment
  • Accompaniment and support of students in their personal and academic development
  • Subjects by arrangement
  • Class teacher
  • active and committed participation and further development of a new school character in cooperation with our team
  • Participation in the 5th/6th class (NMG)

Requirements

  • Completed training as primary instructor with teaching ability for cycle 2
  • Positive attitude towards children and joy in working with children
  • Willingness to recognize and promote the individual strengths of children
  • Commitment and creativity in shaping students’ learning environment
  • Willingness to engage with our team to develop regular exchange and development opportunities

We offer

  • Permanent employment under cantonal employment conditions
  • a personal and family atmosphere in a dynamic and supportive working environment
  • Space for own ideas and projects that enrich everyday school life
  • Opportunity to be a team dedicated to the development of a future-oriented school
  • the school social work and the school commission support the school where they can
  • institutionalized parental work in the form of the family network and a very helpful parenting who is committed to the school and supports their children
