Full description

Are you ready to play a crucial role behind the scenes of Migros Aare? As part of our logistics team, you are responsible for ensuring that the right products are always in the right place. Your independence and efficient working methods are of crucial importance.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

Working hours with start at 20:30, 22:00 and 24:00 and 2-3x per year Sunday bets from 10:00 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You ensure an efficient and timely loading process
  • You remove pallets from the plant and distribute them to the defined storage spaces
  • You ensured a smooth internal flow of goods
  • You consistently comply with the applicable safety regulations according to ISO 45001
  • You ensure compliance with food safety standards
  • You collect relevant data in the system by scanning SSCC labels
  • You pick articles and distribute them as needed
  • You support other areas if necessary and are flexible

Requirements

  • Professional experience: goods logistics
  • Completed basic or further education: an advantage as a logistician
  • You have a category R forklift pass and have extensive practical experience in safe handling of industrial trucks
  • You are flexible
  • You work independently, structured and efficient
  • You convince through your reliability and your strong sense of responsibility
  • You also keep an overview in a dynamic environment and work concentrated and persistently
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions of over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
  • Targeted promotion: Promotion of our employees at all levels with targeted personnel development measures
  • Food: Extensive culinary offer at discounted prices
