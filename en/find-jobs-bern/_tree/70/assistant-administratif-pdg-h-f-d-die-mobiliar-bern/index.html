Full description

This is what awaits you

As an Administrative Assistant CEO, you are part of the CEO office and ensure that the daily work of our CEOs runs smoothly. You are the first point of contact for internal and external requests to the CEO and work closely with her and the team of the CEO office.

This is what you do with us

The focus of this versatile position lies in the organizational and planning concerns in the CEO office as well as in the administrative support of the CEO in day-to-day business. You are used to dealing with and communicating with different stakeholder groups.

You will lead the CEO’s agenda forward, assist with correspondence, coordinate meetings and take responsibility for their smooth running. You always keep an overview of dates and pendences. In addition, you coordinate selected events and take over the management of guests and partners.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic commercial training with some years of experience in a comparable position, ideally with further training as assistant director: in and knowledge of the insurance industry
  • Strong organisational skills, forward-looking and structured working methods, even under time pressure
  • Style-safe written expression and step-appropriate, winning appearance
  • Uncompromising discretion and reliability
  • Curiosity and a sense of new technologies, especially in the field of AI
  • Strong process thinking as well as enjoyment of the pragmatic further development of existing and AI-supported working methods
  • Mother tongue German or French with very good knowledge in the other language, good knowledge of English is advantageous

Working with us

Exciting and varied activities and a dedicated team await you.

You gain insight into the topics that move the furniture and work with people from all areas of the group. Your place of work is Bern and the workload is 80%. Flexible working time models and home office are a matter of course for us

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
