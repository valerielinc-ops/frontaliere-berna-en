Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • As a project manager/customer service, you are responsible for the successful planning and implementation of smaller projects in the field of ballistic protection systems for armoured vehicles.
  • You coordinate interdisciplinary teams and control projects from technical conception through development to series maturity
  • You will work on innovative passive protection solutions against a wide range of known threats in the symmetrical and asymmetric operational environment
  • Independent customer service support including repair processing and spare parts management
  • With your technical understanding and structured working methods, you make a decisive contribution to the development of efficient and reliable protection systems for demanding operational scenarios.

You bring that with you

  • Completed technical training, ideally with further training in project management or a comparable qualification
  • Initial experience in project management, technical order processing or customer service
  • Technical understanding and interest in mechanical, technical or vehicle-related systems
  • Ability to independently plan, coordinate and successfully complete smaller technical projects
  • Enjoying direct customer contact as well as solution-oriented processing of technical inquiries as well as the willingness to familiarize yourself with new technical topics and products
  • Secure handling of common MS Office applications
  • Experience with ERP systems is an advantage
  • Good organizational skills as well as a pragmatic personality with communication strength and a high team spirit
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method, coupled with a strong sense of responsibility
  • Flowing German and good English skills (level B2)

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
