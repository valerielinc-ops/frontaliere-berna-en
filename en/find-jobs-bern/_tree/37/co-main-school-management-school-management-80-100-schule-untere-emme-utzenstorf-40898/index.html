Full description

Portrait Our municipal association runs the school of the participating municipalities Bätterkinden, Utzenstorf, Wiler and Zielbach. Around 130 teachers teach around 1,000 pupils at the three locations Bätterkinden, Utzenstorf and Wiler. We are looking for a co-main school management for the lower Emme (SuE) school from 1 August 2027.

Tasks

Co-head of school (20%)

  • human resources;
  • Secretariat management at Bätterkinden,
  • class and boarding organisation;
  • Head of staff at school.

School management (60 - 80%)

  • Site management Bätterkinden Cycle 2 and 3,
  • line cycle 2 SuE,
  • Health promotion with SSA,
  • BGM,
  • Pensen including SAP – ePM,
  • ensuring internal and external communication,
  • Participation in various working groups.

Requirements

  • They have completed school management training.
  • They have knowledge of the Bernese school system, in particular about development priorities in cycles 1 to 3, and have several years of teaching and leadership experience.
  • They are reliable, resilient, flexible, capable of teamwork and committed.

We offer

  • Committed, open college,
  • exchange and cooperation in the school management team,
  • School management secretariat,
  • Committed and competent authority that strategically leads and supports the school.
