Full description

Workplace: Spagyros AG, Neufeldstrasse 1, 3076 Worb

You keep an overview even when tasks are varied and you want to actively contribute to sales and marketing success? At Spagyros AG, you ensure the smooth running of the activities through reliable data, up-to-date content and proactive organization. You collaborate with various internal and external interlocutors and thus benefit from an exciting overview of the field of complementary medicine.

Your area of action

  • You manage the equipment for point of sale (POS) and promotions, check the inventory and ensure that the necessary equipment is always available.
  • You prepare the sell-in and sell-out data from different sources and prepare monthly sales overviews as well as the basis for market and assortment analyses.
  • You update product, customer and conditions data in different systems and guarantee high quality and consistency of information.
  • You keep the company's website up-to-date, adapt content and manage certificates of analysis as well as other product documents.
  • You support Brand Management in marketing, sales and product projects and coordinate collaboration with agencies, printing companies and other providers.
  • You take on organizational and administrative tasks, coordinate external service meetings and contribute to the effective organisation of the Sales & Marketing team's daily life.
  • In your role of interface, you interact regularly with the technical management.

Your assets

  • You have already gained experience in an administrative or assistance function.
  • You have good practical knowledge of Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook and easily become familiar with other systems.
  • You work rigorously, reliably and autonomously and can effectively organize your tasks even when priorities change.
  • You are interested in complementary medicine and want to deepen your knowledge of the products and specificities of this market.
  • You master French at the mother tongue level and have good knowledge of German, or vice versa.
  • You show flexibility and commitment and enjoy working with different specialized areas as well as external partners.
  • Ideally, you have been trained as a drug addict, laboratory/laborantine or pharmacy assistant/ assistant.

We provide this position without external recruitment agencies.

We offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation, with the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation or taking leave without pay
  • Pension fund with advantageous distribution – we cover nearly 2/3 of the premiums
  • Miscellaneous discounts in our pharmacies and other major brands
  • Opportunities to expand its skills through encouragement of training and development measures and opportunities for internal change
  • Taking into account the needs of families, with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of salary) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of salary), and participation in children's funds
  • Nominal actions at preferential prices – not only engage professionally for Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly by department/position

About us

Spagyros SA is a growing company in the field of complementary medicine, manufacturing and marketing of spagary products, gem therapy, herbal therapy, homeopathy and synbiotics. Since 2021 Spagyros SA has been a member of the Galenica Group and is based in Worb (BE) and La Malmaison, St-Brais (JU). A GMP certified pharmaceutical company, it produces and distributes all its hand-made medicines in Switzerland. Remedy of Spagyros. Created by nature. Perfect by hand.
