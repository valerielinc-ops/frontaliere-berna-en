Full description

PostFinance is driving the strategic modernization of its CRM landscape. As an Architecture Owner, you combine technology and business: you penetrate our CRM processes and use them to create sustainable, secure, stable and simple technical solutions.

You can do that.

  • Together with Product Owner and Delivery Team Lead, you will form the leadership trio of the Delivery Team CRX.
  • You are responsible for the technical architecture of our CRM and develop it together with the team and other architectural roles.
  • You take responsibility for a stable and smooth technical operation and ensure that architecture and implementation meet the requirements for availability, maintainability and operational capability.
  • You will deal intensively with the CRM business and its business processes.
  • You reduce complexity, develop clear solutions and communicate them so that stakeholders can make informed decisions.
  • You decide when standard functionality, configuration, extension or integration is the right solution.
  • You define technical guidelines, architectural principles and coding guidelines and ensure their application as well as high code and solution quality.

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience as a senior developer, tech lead or software architect and in-depth knowledge of software and solution architecture.
  • Very good understanding of clean code, software quality, APIs, integration, data models, security and technical operations.
  • Good knowledge of JavaScript/TypeScript or comparable technologies and pleasure in bringing your technical know-how hands-on.
  • A strong interest in business and business processes as well as a willingness to understand the CRM business in depth.
  • Experience with complex standard software or platform solutions is an advantage.
  • Ability to structure, simplify and understandably communicate complex relationships
