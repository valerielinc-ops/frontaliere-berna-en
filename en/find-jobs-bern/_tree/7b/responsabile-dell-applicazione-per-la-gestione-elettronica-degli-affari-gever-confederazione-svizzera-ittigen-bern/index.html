Full description

Put to the Point

They are responsible for the use and further development of electronic business administration (GEVER) in the office and supervise the application Acta Nova. Together with the specialist areas, you simplify business processes and automate them where appropriate.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21126

You can make this contribution

  • Requirements and change management: Assess, prioritize and coordinate the requirements of the departments for business administration (GEVER) and monitor implementation
  • Release management: Organize tests and acceptance of new GEVER versions and coordinate their introduction in office
  • Incident and problem management: process more complex application questions and disruptions in second level support and, if necessary, clarify them with the responsible service provider
  • Process management: analyze business processes with the specialist areas, map them in GEVER and automate them where appropriate
  • Data storage: regulate business-relevant storage and organize archiving
  • Training: Coordinate training on GEVER and conduct in-depth training

This makes them unique.

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Business Engineering, or a related subject or equivalent education
  • Experience in the management of an electronic business management system as well as in document management and archiving. Good technical understanding of configuration, tests and interfaces. Knowledge of the GEVER application Acta Nova is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of process management in the optimization of business processes and experience in managing projects
  • Pronounced service orientation, clear communication and the ability to assess different requirements and to promote projects independently
  • Oral and written knowledge of a second official language at level B1
