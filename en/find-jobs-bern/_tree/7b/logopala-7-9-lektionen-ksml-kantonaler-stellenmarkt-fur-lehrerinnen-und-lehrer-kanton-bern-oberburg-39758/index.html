Full description

Portrait The four municipalities of Affoltern i.E., Hasle b.B., Lützelflüh and Rüegsau benefit from the offer of speech therapy. The region is called the MR region Brandis. The headquarters of the MR region Brandis is Rüegsau, which is why the schools Rüegsau are an appointing authority. The advertised workload concerns the school district of the schools Rüegsau. If necessary, the workload can be supplemented with further advertised workloads in the field of speech therapy of the aforementioned municipalities.

Tasks

As a specialist, you are responsible for the special teaching speech therapy of the students assigned to you. They accompany and advise teachers, parents and students assigned to them in the field of speech therapy. They participate in the team of the department of speech therapy, the special teachers (specialist exchange), as well as in the interdisciplinary work in the college. The amount can also be divided or extended.

Requirements

  • Dipl. Speech doctor (EDK), Bachelor of Arts in Speech and Language Therapy, equivalent or in training
  • Joy of accompanying and supporting children

We offer

  • Well-equipped central room for logopaedic support. No need to travel by car.
  • Good ICT infrastructure. From a period of 7 weekly lessons, a personal teacher device (laptop) will be handed over to you.
  • Office 365 school account
  • Introduction and accompaniment by the MR management, as well as another speech therapist of the MR region Brandis.
  • Permanent employment
