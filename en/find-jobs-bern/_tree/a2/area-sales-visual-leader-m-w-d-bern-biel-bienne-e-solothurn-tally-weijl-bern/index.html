AREA SALES VISUAL LEADER (m/f/d) – BERN, BIEL/BIENNE AND SOLOTHURN

Area of application · Bern, Biel/Bienne and Solothurn. You are regularly on site in the local stores.

We are here to make you feel sexy, confident and UNSTOPPABLE.

Since 1984, TALLY WEiJL has been on an exciting, crazy journey celebrating fashion, passion, independence, fun, love and community. Today, we are present in 7 markets with around 400 stores, a strong online presence and a dedicated community. Show us how #UNSTOPPABLE you are!

Your Contribution to Our Common Success

  • You will lead and develop the store managers in your region and help them recruit and train new employees.
  • You implement our visual merchandising concept according to the TALLY WEiJL guidelines and train and motivate the store visual merchandisers of your region.
  • You provide excellent customer service and a merchandise presentation that invites you to shop.
  • You control the sales development, the inventory results and the gross wage costs of your stores. Together with your teams, you derive measures to achieve the agreed goals.
  • You check the store routines against checklists, accompany inventories and evaluate the results.
  • In cooperation with our operations team in Basel, you ensure that the right goods are available in the right quantity in the stores.
  • You’ll track your region’s omnichannel KPIs and work with store teams to improve them.

That draws you out

  • You have several years of experience in sales and visual merchandising, ideally in the young fashion sector.
  • You bring initial leadership experience and enjoy motivating and developing teams.
  • You have a sense of trends, styling and colors and creatively implement your ideas on the sales floor.
  • You work independently and structured, have a good understanding of numbers and set clear priorities.
  • Contact with customers makes you fun. Communication and teamwork are among your strengths.
  • You speak fluent German. Knowledge of French is a big plus.
  • You like to regularly visit the stores in Bern, Biel/Bienne and Solothurn.

Your Advantages in Us

  • Professional development in other specialist areas and international markets
  • A varied task with room for manoeuvre and personal responsibility
  • Attractive employee discounts
  • An open, team-oriented company culture that focuses on fashion and fun at work

Are you also UNSTOPPABLE and want to be part of our unprecedented fashion story in our unstoppable TALLY family?

Then share your individual talent with us and send your application documents directly to Carlotta Actis Barone at c.barone@tally-weijl.com.
