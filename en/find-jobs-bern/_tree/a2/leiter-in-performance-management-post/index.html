Head of Performance Management — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you want to develop people and make collaboration effective? Does it appeal to you to build a new team and actively shape their future? You can expect seven committed employees and a management team that focuses on trust, open communication and joint development. Together, it creates the basis for coordinated control of quantities, personnel and quality. With you, we develop performance management and create a sustainable impact for logistics services.

You can do that.

  • You take over the leadership of the newly created Performance Management team and develop a team from different disciplines that grows together, takes responsibility and pursues common goals.
  • In addition, you and your team ensure that decisions are based on a coordinated view of quantities, personnel and quality. In this way, you recognize conflicts of goals early on and develop solutions with sustainable benefits for the entire organization.
  • You actively shape the strategic development of performance management. You develop future visions and strategies, accompany projects and create the conditions for new ideas to have an impact.
  • You position your team as a valued point of contact for questions about performance management. To do this, you work with a wide variety of people, build strong partnerships with operations, process management, finance, IT and other stakeholders and successfully advance topics.
  • As a member of the planning and control team, you also work closely with the other team leaders and help shape the development of the entire department.

You bring that with you

  • You bring leadership experience and develop people even in demanding situations. In addition, you have built teams or accompanied them through changes and successfully advanced strategic topics.
  • Even in complex situations, you keep an overview, set priorities and give orientation.
  • You enjoy working with different stakeholders and successfully. You actively network, build trust and bring people together for a common solution.
  • You communicate complex content in an understandable way, create clarity and move safely at all levels of the organization.
  • You will benefit from experience in logistics, planning, employment law or quality assurance. More important, however, is your interest in understanding connections and quickly familiarizing yourself with new topics.
  • You communicate orally and in writing very well in a first national language and have good knowledge of a second national language.

In order to promote gender diversity, we are particularly pleased about applications from women.
