Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

Designing modern age medicine in a team In our clinic for geriatrics, we combine medical expertise with holistic, patient-centered care. At the same time, we attach great importance to an open, appreciative work culture with short decision-making paths and real cooperation on an equal footing.Our clinic comprises an acute-geriatric bed station, a memory clinic and mobile geriatrics with care of a retirement and nursing home. Our multidisciplinary, bilingual team is led by two geriatricians in modern co-direction. Various specialists from nursing, therapy, speech therapy, nutritional and social counselling, the medical profession and administration work together – characterised by mutual appreciation and team spirit.For our acute-care profession we are looking, from January or by agreement, for a committed, structured and empathetic personality as

Chief Medical Secretary / Station Assistant / in 80-100 %

You do

You are the organizational heart of our acute care team and make sure that everything runs smoothly in the background.

  • Administrative discharge for the chief physicians and station doctors
  • Organisation and coordination of the station day
  • Planning of therapies, examinations and discussions
  • Implementation of geriatric assessments in older patients
  • Preparation and correction of medical reports (French & German)
  • Preparation and coordination of weekly interdisciplinary meetings

This sets you apart

  • Training as MPA or FaGe with additional training as medical secretary
  • French at native language level (or equivalent) and very good knowledge of German
  • Joy of contact with older people
  • Strength of organisation and initiative
  • Safety in dealing with medical terminology and IT systems
  • Team spirit and interest in actively shaping our acute medicine

You will benefit from this

  • A meaningful activity with tangible added value
  • A motivated, bilingual team with modern co-management
  • Design leeway and development possibilities
  • Regulated working hours without night or weekend services
  • Progressive employment conditions (GAV) and high job security
