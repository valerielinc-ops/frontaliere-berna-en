Full description

Portrait The Salome Brunner Foundation works for around 300 children and adolescents with language development disorders and learning difficulties. With our various schools, we see ourselves as an important part of the educational landscape of the Canton of Bern.

The language healing class Ins is a special class for children with complex language disorders. The children are accompanied and supported in their development by specially qualified professionals. The small class makes it possible to respond to the individual needs of the students. Speech therapy and psychomotor skills take place mainly as individual therapy.

Tasks

  • Cooperation in an interdisciplinary team
  • Participation in school organisation and development as part of your workload

Requirements

  • completed study of speech therapy or study
  • Joy and interest in working with children with special needs in the field of language, communication, behavior and learning
