Workplace: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

You know the pharmacy of officine thanks to your practical experience and want to use your pharmaceutical expertise where new ideas for pharmacies arise? At medinform, you are actively involved in the development of basic medical care, the creation of new pharmaceutical services and innovative continuing training. You are thus helping to change the role of pharmacies in the Swiss health care system. The best of both worlds: You are directly employed by medinform and work in a human-sized environment, with short decision-making processes and great flexibility. At the same time, medinform is part of the Galenica network, allowing you to enjoy many possibilities and a solid network in the Swiss health care system.

Your area of action

  • You follow current developments in the field of medical care and develop, on this basis, innovative continuing training and pharmaceutical services
  • You design and follow up on a variety of training courses including FPH accredited courses for pharmacists, pharmacists and other pharmacy collaborators
  • You make scientific knowledge accessible and understandable, develop training concepts and develop practical case studies
  • You recruit qualified practitioners and collaborate with specialists from different health professions
  • You accompany the events in presentation, online and hybrid, both technically and organizationally, and follow up on the participants and participants.
  • You plan training offers according to needs and economic aspects, acquire sponsors and guarantee the professional independence of continuing training

Your assets

  • You've completed your studies in pharmacy and you're interested in official pharmacy and basic medical care.
  • You take pleasure in conveying pharmaceutical knowledge in an understandable and practical way
  • An initial experience in continuing training or adult training is an advantage, but is not a prerequisite.
  • You are interested in basic medical care and want to participate actively in the development of the expanded missions of pharmacies
  • You have the entrepreneurial spirit, know how to identify the needs of clients and carry out your projects independently, structured and reliable
  • You communicate openly with specialists, stakeholders and sponsors and enjoy working in a bilingual team
  • You have a very good command of German and good knowledge in French, both oral and written, minimum B2 level

We offer you

About us
