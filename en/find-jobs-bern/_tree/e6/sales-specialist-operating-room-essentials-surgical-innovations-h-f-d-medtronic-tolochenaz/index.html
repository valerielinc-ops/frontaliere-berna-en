Full description

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact. A Day in the Life Welcome to Medtronic, we improve the quality of life of our patients

In order to strengthen our sales team in the Surgical Innovations division, we are looking for a committed personality as a sales specialist medical technology – surgery & OP (m/f/d) for the support of the cantons of Bern, Solothurn, Jura, Neuchâtel, Freiburg, Vaud, Wallis and Geneva.

In this position, you distribute our product portfolio in the area of Operating Room Essentials, in particular surgical suture materials, access products, instruments and ligatures.

It is a field service position within the sales area. Your home office serves as an organizational basis, while you spend most of your working time directly with our customers. Occasional visits to a Medtronic location may be required for meetings or training.

Responsibilities include, as follows

  • They serve existing customers and gain new customers in the field of surgery and surgery.
  • They build long-term relationships with healthcare professionals, decision makers and opinion leaders.
  • They present our medical products and accompany product launches.
  • They organise and conduct product training for healthcare professionals.
  • They further develop existing collaborations and identify new business opportunities.
  • They observe market and competitive developments and represent Medtronic at specialist congresses and events.

Required knowledge and experience

  • They have medical, technical or commercial training or a corresponding degree or a comparable qualification.
  • Ideally, they bring experience in the distribution of medical products. Medical specialists with an interest in switching to the medical field service are also welcome.
  • They communicate convincingly, present safely and enjoy direct contact with customers.
  • They work independently, structured and goal-oriented and bring their own initiative and negotiating skills.
  • They are fluent in German and French and have good knowledge of English.
  • You are safe in dealing with MS Office and ideally live within the sales area
