Full description

The UniBE is in the middle of it. With us you benefit from the central location and conveniently located locations.

This is what awaits you with us

  • Independent processing of creditors and debtors with a focus on expense statements and monthly controls
  • Administrative management of third-party loans and supporting cooperation in financial reports, budgets and forecasts
  • Administrative organisation of our courses
  • Co-organization of scientific conferences and supervision of international visiting scientists (AEC Visitor Program)
  • Maintenance of our databases, deputy in personnel administration and other general administration tasks

  • Stylistic German and very good English skills for daily exchange in our international environment
  • A reliable and open personality with pleasure in contact with very different contact persons

About us

The University's Institute of Theoretical Physics (ITP) conducts research in the field of elementary particle physics and is involved in university teaching. The ITP employs around 60 people from Germany and abroad and is part of the Albert Einstein Center for Fundamental Physics (AEC), a strategic research center of the University of Bern. To strengthen our institute administration, we are looking for a committed, heartfelt organizational personality with flair for numbers. In this versatile hub function, you make a significant contribution to ensuring that both our financial processes and everyday academic life run smoothly. This is what awaits you with us

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
