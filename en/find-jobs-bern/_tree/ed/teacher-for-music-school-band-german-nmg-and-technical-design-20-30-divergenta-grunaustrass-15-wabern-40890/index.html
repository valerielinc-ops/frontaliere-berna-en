Full description

Portrait Divergenta is a young, innovative private and special elementary school with a focus on project-oriented, trilingual (German-English-French) and individual learning. We specialize in neurodivergent children and adolescents from the 5th grade to the English-International Matura. From February 2027 we are looking for a teacher for our project class. Depending on the subjects taken over, the fee is 20-30% (distributed on Tuesday and Wednesday). For the school year 2027/28 there is the possibility to increase the burden. Applications for individual subject areas mentioned are also welcome.

Tasks

Your tasks

You plan, design and take responsibility for teaching in your subject areas based on the curriculum 21, the school concept and the individual learning and support goals.

You will develop differentiated, action-oriented learning opportunities for our age- and level-mixed project class. You combine professional learning with practical and creative projects and create space for research, experimentation and independent work.

You design a clearly structured, low-distraction and neurodiversity-friendly lesson. You consider different learning requirements, talents and needs and make tasks, processes and expectations understandable.

When taking over music/school band, you lead joint music making, promote musical expression possibilities and develop projects with the students that are based on their interests and abilities.

In technical design, you accompany the students: from their own idea through planning to implementation and convey a careful, safe use of materials and tools.

You accompany the students: inside as a reliable reference person. You strengthen their independence, self-efficacy and personal responsibility and support them in developing learning strategies and a sustainable work organization.

You lead your learning group in a clear and appreciative way, apply agreed rules calmly and consistently, and support students in dealing with frustration and conflict.

You review and document learning progress, provide helpful feedback and assess performance in your subjects. You bring your observations and professional assessments into the joint funding planning and, if necessary, into location discussions.

You work closely with class teachers, learning coaching and teaching assistants. You coordinate learning goals and support and ensure clear agreements and reliable handovers.

As part of your workload, you participate in team meetings, further education and teaching development and contribute your expertise and ideas to joint school projects.

Requirements

  • A completed teaching degree or an equivalent qualification suitable for the intended teaching as well as teaching experience.
  • Professional and didactic competence in one or more of the advertised areas: music/school band, German, NMG and technical design.
  • Delight in project-based, individualized teaching and the ability to convey complex content in an understandable, clear and practical manner.
  • Interest in neurodivergent learning profiles and a willingness to align your lessons with different learning requirements, talents and needs. Experience with neurodivergent children and adolescents is welcome.
  • High relationship competence and pleasure in working with small, heterogeneous learning groups. You meet the students: facing and respectful and give them orientation through clear expectations and reliable boundaries.
  • A structured, independent and reliable way of working. You take responsibility, comply with agreements and address questions or difficulties at an early stage.
  • Flexibility in dynamic school life and the ability to keep track of changes and find constructive solutions.
  • initiative, a positive attitude and humor. You approach challenges openly and use experience to further develop your teaching.
  • Enjoying close cooperation and co-teaching as well as the willingness to plan, reflect and develop lessons together.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as enjoyment of bilingual teaching.
  • Desire to actively shape a young school, contribute your own ideas and support jointly agreed developments.

We offer

  • Small classes with a care key of 1:6.
  • A permanent employment with employment conditions and remuneration in accordance with the cantonal guidelines.
  • Close cooperation and co-teaching in a committed, international and experienced team.
  • A meaningful, varied activity with creative scope for your lessons and your own projects.
  • A young, innovative school with a lived “fail-forward” culture: We test new things, reflect on experiences and learn from mistakes together.
  • Partner schools in England with opportunities for professional exchange and job shadowing.
  • Internal training and opportunities for professional and personal development.
  • Bright, modern and centrally located rooms.
  • The basic possibility of increasing the workload to the school year 2027/28.
