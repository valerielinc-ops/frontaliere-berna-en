Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Responsibility for safe and stable operation, maintenance and further development of safety-relevant systems and services
  • Support and administration of client and server infrastructures based on Windows and Linux as well as virtualized environments
  • Conduct systematic error analyses and evaluate system and log data for the development of sustainable solutions
  • Installation, configuration and maintenance of software and specialist applications
  • Perform software updates and ensure patch and release management as well as security measures
  • Monitoring, automation and continuous optimization of operational processes using appropriate scripting and automation solutions
  • Assistance in incident management and technical support remotely and on site
  • Preparation and maintenance of operations manuals, technical documentation and checklists

You bring that with you

  • Training or higher vocational training in computer science, alternatively lateral entrant with comparable professional experience
  • In-depth practical knowledge in the field of operating systems, especially Windows and Linux
  • Knowledge of IT security, especially in the protection of IT systems and networks
  • Experience with automation tools such as B. Ansible as well as knowledge of script languages such as PowerShell or Bash
  • knowledge of ITIL processes, in particular incident, change and problem management
  • Experience with agile methods is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Good knowledge of German and English (level B2)
  • Category B driving licence required
  • Military career as a senior non-commissioned officer or officer of advantage

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

The Business Area C5I of RUAG MRO Holding AG supports the Swiss Army in the realization of ICT projects with a specific focus on the Cyber Command. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
