Full description

You like to get up early and you're looking for a physically stimulating activity? Don't look anymore. Your place is here. Your sense of direction allows you to meet all the challenges inherent in road traffic.

Make a difference

  • Early in the morning (around 6 a.m.), you load the distribution vehicles with your team and you prepare your tour.
  • Through your work, you mark the urban landscape of La Neuveville and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the timely and irreproachable distribution of parcels and letters in your area.
  • During your tour, you answer questions from our clients, and you contribute to their satisfaction.
  • You work regularly on Saturdays, the services being distributed among the team.

What you bring

  • Thanks to your fitness, you are able to load and lift 30 kg packages regularly without any problems.
  • You like to spend time on the road, you have a category B driver's license and ideally A1, or you agree to spend it before you start working.
  • You appreciate teamwork, but you also like to travel independently and you're comfortable with the big vehicles on the road.
  • You're reliable and you work fast. Good oral knowledge in French as well as good mastery of digital devices complete your profile.
