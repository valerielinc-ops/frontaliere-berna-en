Are you looking for a job where you can develop professionally, make your working hours flexible and where personal responsibility is really lived? Then you will find optimal conditions for motivated therapists who want to make a difference. We are a dynamic team of 9 therapists and a secretary – and look forward to reinforcement. From now on or by agreement we are looking for a Physical therapist (60-90%). Our practice in Breitenrain/Bern offers bright, beautiful old building facilities and an environment in which we predominantly treat patients with versatile, demanding and complex musculoskeletal complaints. Experience in trigger point therapy, dry needling, manual therapy, neurodynamics and palpatory anatomy is ideal – but it can also be developed in a targeted manner. Very good knowledge of German is a prerequisite, another national language is a plus. Team culture is important to us: we focus on open exchange, close cooperation and short decision-making processes. Regular internal training – including inputs from my international teaching activities in the field of Dry Needling/Trigger Point Therapy – supports your personal and professional development.
