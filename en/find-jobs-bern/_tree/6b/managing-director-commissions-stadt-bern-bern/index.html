Full description

Tasks

  • You manage the business and organization of the Business Auditing Commission and the Education, Social Affairs and Culture Commission of the City Council of Bern
  • You are the first point of contact for the Presidents and members of these commissions as well as the administration at their interface
  • You advise and support them in matters of fact and procedure
  • You ensure the professional conduct of the commission meetings usually two Monday evenings per month
  • You write resolutions, lectures to the city council, reports, correspondence and media releases
  • You take on a wide range of tasks for the parliamentary services such as clarifications, project management or training

Requirements

  • You have a university degree in law, history, political science or a comparable discipline
  • You are interested in political processes and bring several years of professional experience in staff work or in the environment of politics and administration
  • You have project experience and work structured, organized and forward-looking
  • You think analytically and networked and lead effectively even without formal authority
  • You communicate clearly and appropriately – verbally and in writing
  • You are a motivated and established personality, work independently and convince with flexibility, reliability and loyalty
  • You use IT tools securely and are interested in their further development
