Full description

At PostFinance, you are actively shaping the future of digital banking. You ensure that payments, transactions and account management work smoothly and PostFinance further strengthens its role as a digital innovation leader in the Swiss financial world. With your commitment, you take responsibility and bring in new ideas. Together with an innovative team, you move PostFinance sustainably.

You can do that.

  • You check and match booking data in national and international payment transactions (SIC, euroSIC, SWIFT) as well as in internal interface accounts
  • You discuss differences together with internal and external partners and ensure that payment transactions via Swiss and foreign banks are handled correctly
  • You collect booking documents in SAP
  • You check the coverage of incoming payment orders
  • You process internal and external requests for coordination in interbank payments

You bring that with you

  • Commercial or comparable training completed
  • Professional experience in payment transactions and accounting as an advantage
  • Strong initiative, team spirit, communication skills and a flair for numbers
  • Self-contained, efficient and customer-oriented workflow
  • High cost and quality awareness
  • native language German as well as good oral and written English skills
