Put to the Point

No risks from ammunition in Mitholz – in this complex and unique project, exciting and diverse challenges await you as well as a motivated project team.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21118
  • Location: Guisanplatz 1b, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the technical, organisational and financial management of the cross-cutting area of safety with the main tasks of safety, health, availability and emergency management
  • Responsible as "Safety Responsible Mitholz" at the overall project level all safety-relevant tasks: risk assessment according to the accident regulations, fire/explosion protection, construction site/industrial safety, health protection, etc.
  • Responsible for establishing and developing the operational, preparedness and emergency organisation of the project as well as cooperation with the civilian forces
  • Controls and monitors the safety measures in the subprojects and is responsible for the measures at the project level (e.g. operation of the measurement/alert system and the use of natural hazard observers)
  • Develops specifications for the security areas and supports the sub-projects in the planning and implementation of security measures and coordinates cooperation with specialist bodies and authorities for security issues
  • Conducts training for safety management and monitors compliance with and documents audits

This makes you unique

  • Basic training in the field of safety (e.g. safety engineer), occupational hygiene, construction or environmental sciences with a university degree
  • Ideally with further training in the areas of safety, occupational hygiene
  • Proven experience as security officer of construction projects (e.g. for large-scale infrastructure projects)
  • Willingness to provide annual 4 to 6 weeks of Pikettdienst in the standby organization of the Mitholz project
  • Organisational and project management competencies for the development and development of operational and preparedness organisation
  • You have oral and written knowledge of a second official language
