Full description

At BKW Power Grid, you take responsibility for the reliable operation of our utility platform. While SAP IS-U is moving from Central IT to Power Grid, you are actively involved in the development and development of stable operational processes and operational supplier management.

Your field of activity

  • Together with internal and external partners, you are responsible for the stable, secure and economical operation of your assigned utility services.
  • You monitor service quality and service levels, identify risks, coordinate actions and escalate deviations in time
  • Ensure timely processing of incidents, problems, changes, releases and service requests for your services
  • You take over new or changed services operationally according to defined requirements and keep an eye on quality and costs
  • In the ongoing SAP IS-U transition, you will be involved in establishing operational processes and operational supplier management.
  • You will continue to develop services and operational processes, promote automation and actively shape technical lifecycle management

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in technical ICT operations, application management or operational IT service management
  • Practical experience with ITIL-based processes for incident, problem, change, release and service request management
  • Experience in the operational management of internal stakeholders and external partners as well as in the coordination of responsibilities
  • Understand the end-to-end interaction of business services, technical services, service instances and infrastructure
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented, communicate securely in German and English and connect different stakeholders
  • Knowledge of ServiceNow, SAP or the Swiss energy industry round off your profile
