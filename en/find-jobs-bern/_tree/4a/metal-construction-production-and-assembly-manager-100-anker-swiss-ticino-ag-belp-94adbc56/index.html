Metallbau Production and Assembly Manager 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement, we are looking for a temporary or permanent position More than 3 years of experience as metal construction production and assembly manager

Your challenge

As Chief Installer, you assume responsibility for the professional installation of our metal construction projects on the construction site. You coordinate the processes on site, lead your assembly team and ensure that deadlines, quality and safety requirements are met. You work closely with the project management, customers and other craftsmen and keep an overview even in hectic situations. Your tasks also include the independent organization of the assembly work and active cooperation on the construction site.

This is what you bring with you

You have completed training in metal construction and bring experience in assembly as well as ideally in a senior function. You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented and like to take responsibility. A professional attitude towards customers and employees is a matter of course for you. In addition, you are team-capable, resilient and have the driving license category B.

What we offer you

  • Great working environment
  • Long-term deployments
  • Attractive employment conditions with good pay
  • A team that is there for you and your needs
