Full description

Your professional experience in the field of flowers distinguishes you as well as your competent advice to our customers? Do you also have a sense of numbers? Then you fit us perfectly. In the role of the specialist manager, you take over the professional management of the team. We look forward to meeting you.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, MM Belp, Steinbachstrasse 28, 3123 Belp

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You advise and serve our customers with pleasure and your expertise
  • An attractive presentation of goods, efficient goods management and ensuring availability are your focus
  • You ensure that the defined processes in your department are adhered to

  • Your tasks include handling returns and handling complaints
  • In this role, the focus is on the professional leadership of the team

Requirements

  • Ideally, several years of professional experience: in the retail trade specialization Flowers / Plants or as a florist
  • Completed basic or further education: in the retail trade specialization flowers / plants or as a florist of advantage
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales, you make shopping a positive experience
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • You have leadership experience
  • in German (negotiation certain)
