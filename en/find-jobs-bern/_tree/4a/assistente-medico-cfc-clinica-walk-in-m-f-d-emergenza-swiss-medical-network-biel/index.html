The Biel-Bienne Medicentre is part of the Réseau de l'Arc and is in the centre of Biel. With approximately 1,200 employees, the hospitals and medical centres of the Réseau de l'Arc meet the medical needs of more than 50,000 inhabitants. They are known for their dynamism, their infrastructure and their desire to always offer quality services close to people. The Medicentre is made up of independent general practitioners or specialists who work in teams and interdisciplinarity. Our outpatient surgical clinic welcomes patients for outpatient procedures and our Walk-In-Clinic, an emergency polyclinic, supports patients without an appointment. To complement our emergency team, we are looking for a CFC medical assistant right now.
