Full description

Portrait The school Herzogenbuchsee combines kindergarten and primary school in the center of the Oberaargau. Thanks to its location on the important traffic corridor between Bern and Olten, it is easily accessible and takes care of children from Herzogenbuchsee and Niederönz from kindergarten to 6th grade. Currently, around 760 students visit the five school and kindergarten locations, where they are accompanied by a dedicated and versatile team of teachers. In addition to the standard lessons, the school also organizes the offers in speech therapy and psychomotor skills for the surrounding schools in the region.

Tasks

You take over the maternal representation and together with your class teacher or co-class teacher a lively lesson in a 1./2. Class at our location in Niederönz and takes responsibility for 14-18 lessons (German, NMG, Technical Design, MGS and movement and sport). With creative ideas, clear structure and a lot of heart, you accompany the students: women in their first years of school, promote them individually and ensure a motivating learning climate. You work closely in the team, actively participate in the lesson planning and shape the school day varied, appreciative and child-friendly. Working days are Mo, Mi and Do. Deputy Duration: Probably from Christmas holidays to summer holidays.
