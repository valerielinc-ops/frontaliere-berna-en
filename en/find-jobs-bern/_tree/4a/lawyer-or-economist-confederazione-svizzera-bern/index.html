Put to the Point

The performance area of export controls and sanctions in SECO will be responsible for the implementation of the Investment Audit Act (IPG), which was adopted on 19 December 2025. The IPG provides for comprehensive audits of certain investments of foreign players in Swiss companies. This affects a wide range of different sectors of the economy (sensitive technologies/armament industry, power plants/energy supply, health, telecom and transport infrastructures, etc.).

You can make this contribution

  • Examination of investments by foreign investors in Switzerland on the basis of legal, economic and security policy issues;
  • preparation of decision-making bases for the sake of the hierarchy, including motions to the Federal Council, as well as providing legal information on the scope of the IPG;
  • Representation of Switzerland in bilateral and multilateral negotiations on investment audits and coordination of examination procedures and cooperation with the competent federal authorities and other national and foreign authorities;
  • Participation in the further development of the IPG, for example in the parliamentary legislative process;
  • Participation in the development of digital working instruments as well as guidelines, leaflets, checklists and other practical foundations for investors, companies and legal representatives.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
