fedpol in action for Switzerland – anytime and anywhere

fedpol in operation for Switzerland – anytime & anywhere. fedpol develops and operates national information systems and performs supervisory, licensing and enforcement tasks in weapons and explosives law. The Directorate of Police Systems and Identification (PSI) of fedpol ensures the secure operation and further development of central police information systems and databases, is responsible for the national hub for police information exchange and provides identification and forensic services for domestic and foreign partner authorities. The Department of Biometric Identification (BiomID) provides services in the field of biometric identification for law enforcement and security authorities. It processes and compares fingerprints, DNA profiles and other biometric data, confirms hits according to the four-eyes principle and thus supports the identification of persons and traces in the national and international context.
