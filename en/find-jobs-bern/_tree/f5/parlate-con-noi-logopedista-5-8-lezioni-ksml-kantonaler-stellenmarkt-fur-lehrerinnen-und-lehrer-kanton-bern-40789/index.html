Full description

Portrait The Educational Center for Hearing and Speech HSM Münchenbuchsee is a service center with a wide range of support and advice for children and adolescents with severe hearing and speech impairments and their environment.

We train normally gifted pupils with hearing and/or language impairments from basic to secondary level I.

From now on or by agreement | Burgistein location (Burgiwil)

Tasks

  • You will treat the children individually and in small groups with a course of 5-8 lessons
  • You work closely with the other two speech doctors at the location
  • You also work integrated in the class association
  • You write logopaedic reports
  • You participate in class and parent events
  • You also go to the swimming pool or on excursions

Requirements

  • You have a completed study of speech therapy or are ready to complete the study part-time.
  • You enjoy and are interested in promoting children with language impairments.
  • You like to work interdisciplinary and appreciate the exchange with other specialists.
  • You have a great deal of serenity, humor and endurance and enjoy challenges
  • You like to be part of a team and actively participate.
  • You see the success and the resources in the students

We offer

  • An innovative and motivated team with a lot of commitment and joy in working together.
  • An appreciative and interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Space for your own ideas and professional development.
  • A meaningful and varied activity in a special school environment.
  • A small job that can be combined well with an existing professional activity.
