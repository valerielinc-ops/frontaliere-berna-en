Full description

This is what awaits you

In this role, you are part of the Business Transformation department, which accompanies our organization in the digital transformation and has a lasting impact on the furniture business model.

This is what you do with us

In the SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) role Epic Owner (project manager), you lead agile projects and further developments of new software solutions in the field of non-life insurance. You help your business contacts in dealing with agile working methods of software development. As a link between requirements, stakeholders and the implementation organisation, you make an important contribution to the modernisation of products and processes.

Together with the business units, you drive the identification, evaluation and realization of modernization and process optimization potential – with targeted use of AI, automation and other new technologies.

You focus on customer centering. In addition, you help to accompany participating units in change processes and focus on sustainable organizational development.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed degree (HF/FH/Uni) in Business Administration, Business Informatics, Process Management or Requirements Engineering
  • Professional experience as an epic owner (project manager), product owner and/or business analyst or in process management desired
  • Experience in the non-life insurance industry is beneficial
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of working in different and changing interdisciplinary teams
  • Curiosity about complex questions, desire for conceptual tasks, pleasure in accompanying change in transformation projects and solution-oriented working methods
  • High self-employment, pronounced initiative and pleasure in actively using the scope for action as an epi owner
  • Fluent knowledge of German, French and English are a plus

Working with us

A motivated team of Epic Owner and a powerful implementation organization are waiting for you. Together we are developing the future of furniture.

Your workload is negotiable from 80%, flexible working time models and location-independent work in the home office are open to you.

Job starts on 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
