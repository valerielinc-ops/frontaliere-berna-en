Head of IT audit — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you want to make a difference, take on responsibility and help shape the post of tomorrow with a dedicated team? We are active in all areas of the Post Office at all organizational levels and examine entire areas, individual business and support processes, projects or concrete products and services at different depths. We use modern instruments and rely on data-based analyses. Apply now, because with you we set new standards.

You can do that.

  • In this exciting role, you will be responsible for the audits on IT topics throughout the Group and ensure compliance with the relevant compliance requirements, standards and procedures.
  • You conduct conversations, analyze information, communicate the complex relationships to the tested.
  • You identify vulnerabilities, evaluate the associated risks and develop optimization proposals to increase efficiency and minimize risks.
  • You ensure the proper execution of the audit procedures in accordance with the audit program and adhere to high quality standards.
  • Furthermore, you support the business auditors: in the assessment of IT activities and in the use of data analysis tools, both before and during the exams.
  • You actively promote the transfer of knowledge within the team and are involved in internal projects for the continuous development of the corporate audit.

You bring that with you

  • You are an IT auditor:in or computer scientist:in and have several years of professional experience in operational computer science.
  • Knowledge in the areas of IT governance, outsourcing, cloud, IT/cyber security or zero trust is an advantage.
  • You have a university degree in the areas of business administration or business or computer science, ideally supplemented with further education (CISA or similar) or you are ready to complete one.
  • Good knowledge of the relevant standards and frameworks (e.g. COBIT, NIST, CSA, ISO 270xx, ISAE/SOC, CIS Controls) round off your profile.
  • You capture connections quickly, work in a structured way and bring a strong team spirit.
  • You can present complex issues simply, understandably and in line with the target group. In order to communicate with the various contact groups, you communicate very well orally and in writing in German and have good knowledge of a second national language and / or English.

For you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. Join the transformation of our company into the «Post of Tomorrow»! We offer you a responsible, versatile and exciting job with work location Bern and up to 40% home office. We look forward to your application.
