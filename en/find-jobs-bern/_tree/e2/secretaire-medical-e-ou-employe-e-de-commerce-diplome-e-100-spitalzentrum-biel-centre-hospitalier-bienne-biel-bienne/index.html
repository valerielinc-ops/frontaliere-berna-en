Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

For the addition of our secretariat in the Cardiological Clinic we are looking for an immediately or by agreement:

Medical secretary or graduate / 100%

They do.

  • Responsible for the optimal procedure and professional organization of cardiological consultation hours
  • Patient reception, receipt of telephone calls, appointment arrangement
  • Organisation of semi-stationary and stationary procedures (PM implantations, coronary angiographies, electrophysiological examinations, etc.)
  • Writing medical reports in German
  • In addition, general secretarial work such as archiving (electronic), dispatch (electronic) are also part of your daily tasks
  • Administrative support for doctors
  • Open and manage cases in SAP
  • Statistics

This draws you

  • Medical secretary or graduate with professional experience – preferably in a hospital
  • High willingness to provide services
  • Strong organisational skills and precise and forward working methods
  • German mother tongue with good oral French skills necessary
  • Team-capable, reliable personality with a lot of commitment

They benefit from it.

We offer you an exciting workplace in a dynamic environment with modern infrastructure and an excellent interdisciplinary working culture. The Biel Hospital Center offers you attractive employment conditions according to a collective employment contract and has a restaurant with advantageous prices for the staff as well as a day care center. In in-house language courses you can refresh your French or German skills.
