Full description

This is what awaits you

With a view to retirement, the property damage West provides for early succession planning with a structured transfer of know-how. During your time in the specialist network Schaden (FNS) you develop specifically to the damage inspector: in agriculture. During your assignment, you will be fully integrated into the Team Property Damage West and build up your expertise in a practical and step-by-step manner.

This is what you do with us

In your role, you are responsible for the independent processing and regulation of complex claims cases in the business sectors of our agricultural customers, mainly in Romandie (as well as in the bilingual cantons of Freiburg, Bern and Wallis).

You assess coverage and liability issues, create damage calculations, and also carry out demanding clarifications and negotiations with policyholders and their legal representatives as well as experts.

In addition, you support our general agency damage services in technical issues and work in projects, concepts as well as in education and training.

This is what you bring with you

  • Master farmer (HFP), study agronomy (HF/FH) or equivalent education
  • Several years of professional experience in agriculture or related professions
  • Initiative, communicative, independent and assertive personality with negotiating skills as well as strong business thinking and high social skills
  • Solution- and customer-oriented working method as well as professional and sovereign appearance
  • Willingness to continue education in the insurance sector
  • French mother tongue and very good knowledge of the German language

Your development path in the specialist network damage

This position is part of our specialist network Schaden, FNS for short. With this development program, we specifically build specialists for important and specialized tasks in the damage area. In this way, we secure valuable knowledge, create professional prospects and prepare talent for future specialist functions.

From day one, you are fully integrated into Team Sachschaden West and actively participate in day-to-day business. Experienced specialists will accompany you in expanding your knowledge in a practical manner and gradually taking on more responsibility. Your development is based on an individual development plan with clear goals. This is determined together with you, your leader in the property damage West and the management of the FNS.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
