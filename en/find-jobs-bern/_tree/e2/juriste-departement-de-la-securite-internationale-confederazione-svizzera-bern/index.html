Put to the Point

As a lawyer in the Export Controls and Private Security Services section, you work in a legal and practice-oriented political environment. National and international political developments are of central importance for your daily work within the framework of the implementation of the Federal Act on Private Security Services Provided Abroad

  • Date of entry: 01.12.2026
  • Type of employment: permanent
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20617

You can make this contribution

  • Processing of reports under the Federal Act on private security services provided abroad, inter alia in direct exchange with companies and in coordination with other federal agencies
  • Preparation of orders and criminal complaints
  • Analysis of the activities of companies in the sector, in particular with regard to the reporting obligation
  • Accompanying legislative projects as well as monitoring and analysing national and international developments in the field of private security services and participating in the further development of Swiss policy
  • Participation in international negotiations and multilateral processes in the field of private security services and representation of Swiss positions
  • conducting research, preparing thematic, corporate, activity and country analyses, and participating in the drafting of the annual report of the competent authority in the field of private security services
  • Participation in projects of the Section (workshops, conferences and educational events), as well as in information and awareness-raising work within and outside the Federal Administration
  • Deputy in drafting opinions on export control and other related issues.

This makes them unique.

  • University degree (Master) in law and work experience as a lawyer in the private and/or public sector
  • Specialization in administrative and/or international law is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of the relevant norms of Swiss public law and the Swiss political system as well as the foreign policy context
  • Strong understanding of the domestic and foreign policy context of work, political sensitivity and diplomatic skills (situational appearance and ability to build a network)
  • Excellent analytical and editorial skills
  • Proven versatility and flexibility: broad general knowledge, ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new areas of expertise, willingness to support team members on their dossiers
  • mastery of two official languages in word and writing and good knowledge of English; excellent knowledge of Italian (ideally mother/working language) is beneficial.

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

The State Secretariat (STS) manages the development, coordination and planning of foreign policy and is responsible for reporting to the political authorities and the management of Switzerland’s foreign network. It is responsible for implementing Switzerland’s foreign policy strategy. The most important dossiers include cooperation with the United Nations (UN), the development of relations with the European Union (EU), and security and peace policy.
