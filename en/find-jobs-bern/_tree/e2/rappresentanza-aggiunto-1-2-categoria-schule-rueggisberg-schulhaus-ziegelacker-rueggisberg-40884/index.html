Full description

Portrait Our country school is located on the Langnberg, about 15 km south of Bern. In the Ziegelacker School House, students are taught in 6 classes from kindergarten to 6th grade. Our schoolhouse was reopened a few years ago after a comprehensive renovation and offers excellent infrastructure and a wonderful view.

Tasks

They cover the lessons of the class teacher who is on vacation. They work fully (26 lessons) and teach all subjects except TTG. In addition, if possible, you cover the offer of the school "Blockflute" (1 lesson) and take over the class teacher* office. The deputy lasts 6 weeks (15 February - 25). March 2027) between the sports holidays and Easter.

Requirements

  • They have an educational education. Ideally, you have a seminary primary teacher patent or a Bachelor for the primary level.
  • They are capable and motivated to lead a two-year class and implement age-mixed learning.
  • You are a dutiful, communicative and resilient personality.
  • You take responsibility as a class teacher.

We offer

  • An employment according to the cantonal specifications.
  • A very small class with only 13 SuS.
  • A motivated, happy team that values collegiality and sustainability.
  • Free parking at the school building and hourly connection to the public transport.
  • A well-developed, modern infrastructure in the schoolhouse.
