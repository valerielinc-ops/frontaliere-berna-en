Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Dorfmatt in Münsingen we are looking for a specialist / health professional EFZ 60%-100% with an additional function vocational trainer / Your tasks

  • You take on the day-to-day responsibility and ensure a smooth process in the day-to-day care.
  • You actively participate in an optimal interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • You are jointly responsible for the classification of residents by means of interRAI LTCF and RAI HC.
  • You advise relatives as well as internal and external contacts competently and sensitively.
  • You participate in the training of our learners and accompany them in their professional development.
  • With your commitment, you shape a positive working atmosphere and promote an efficient and appreciative working environment.

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training as FaGe EFZ, specialist long-term care or equivalent training
  • Vocational training course completed
  • Knowledge of RAI- HC/ interRAI LTCF (of advantage)
  • Further education in treatment care and preferably experience in geriatrics
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (min B2)
  • Strong social skills as well as a high degree of empathy and communication skills
  • Good organisational skills and structured working methods

We offer you

  • New and modern infrastructure (buildings, IT, etc.)
  • A varied and responsible field of responsibility
  • An appreciative leadership culture and internal training
  • Extensive training offer
  • Attractive benefits (SBB half-ax, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 weeks or from 60 years 7 weeks holiday)
  • Regular team events and shared experiences that strengthen cooperation
  • Our statement "closer to people" counts for our employees as much as for our residents.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
