Full description

Do you control the truck like Lewis Hamilton does his Ferrari? With the word “camp”, do you not think of the summer holidays as scouts, but of your workplace? Then you are right in the logistics / warehouse department of RUAG. Here you are jointly responsible for ensuring that goods, for example for tanks or aircraft, are delivered and delivered in time so that the security of Switzerland is guaranteed.

You can move that

  • Taking of incoming material in the assigned area of responsibility
  • Provision and completion of material on the basis of setup lists
  • Operation of the infrastructure such as truck, roller, crane, etc.
  • Packing and unpacking of incoming goods
  • Preparation of goods for incoming goods inspection
  • Carrying out internal and external transport by means of passenger cars, electric forklifts, etc.
  • Logistical support in current projects on site
  • Booking of incoming and outgoing goods in the ERP

You bring that with you

  • Completed training for logistician/EFZ
  • Good IT user knowledge and experience in dealing with modern ERP systems (SAP)
  • Handling digital warehouse management
  • Reliable and independent work
  • Team-ready, flexible and motivated
  • Service-oriented action
  • Language skills: German is good in word and writing, English is advantageous
  • Forklift certificate of advantage
  • Crane driver's license advantageous, driver's license B

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
