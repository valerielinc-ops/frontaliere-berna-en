Full description

Do you want to make a difference in your education that really counts? Then you are exactly right with us! As a learner, you are an important part of our team: you accompany people in everyday life, support them on the way to more independence and ensure that they feel well taken care of. At the Klinik Schönberg you learn step by step everything you need – with a lot of practice, a strong team and professional support. Apply now for the teaching start in August 2027!

What we offer you

  • A professional and dedicated Bildner team with individual support and regular exchange
  • A strong team with a lot of cohesion and numerous team activities
  • A varied working environment and internships in other areas of the clinic (restoration, medical practice, physiotherapy, etc.) so that you get to know different areas and expand your skills
  • Contemporary employment conditions with progressive non-wage benefits
  • Development opportunities (further training, FaGe training)

You bring that with you

  • You have completed compulsory schooling at real or secondary school level
  • You enjoy dealing with people and would like to accompany our patients on the way to self-employment
  • You are attentive, take responsibility and attach importance to good manners
  • You enjoy working in a team and are good at organizing
  • You are open to irregular working hours and weekend services
  • You bring interest and willingness for a trial apprenticeship with us
