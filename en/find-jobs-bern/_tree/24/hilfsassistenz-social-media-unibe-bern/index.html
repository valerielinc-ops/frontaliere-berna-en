Full description

The UniBE is versatile. With us, you can expect a wide variety of topics and a lot of design freedom.

Tasks

  • Independent recording and publishing of delivered content (suitable equipment and tools are provided)
  • Cooperation in the creation and situational adaptation of channel-specific content (text, image, video)
  • Active participation in monitoring, community management and success monitoring for the respective communication channel

Requirements

  • Matriculation at a university in HS 2026/2027 and FS 2027
  • Knowledge of social media work for business units
  • Experience in writing texts of different formats and varieties and publishing
  • Excellent knowledge of community management
  • Good analytical and conceptual ability
  • Early adaptor of new and young social media channels and formats
  • Good knowledge of image and video editing (Premiere Pro, CapCut, Photoshop, Figma)
  • Readiness for monitoring services
  • Very good language skills D/E/F (verbal and written)
  • Communicative, committed, quality-conscious, team-capable, flexible
  • Interest in scientific and university topics

About us

The University of Bern is one of the largest universities in Switzerland with over 19'000 students. It is networked nationally and internationally and sets a focus worldwide with interdisciplinary research. Channels and Content in the Department of Communication & Marketing is the central unit for content and storytelling at the University of Bern. We develop and manage the channels of the umbrella brand, tell the stories of the university and make their achievements visible. To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or later an assistant for social media, 50%, limited to 1 year.
