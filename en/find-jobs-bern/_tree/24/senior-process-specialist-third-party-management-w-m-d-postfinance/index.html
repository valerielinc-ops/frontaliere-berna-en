Full description

At PostFinance, you actively shape the future of third-party management. In the Competence Center Procurement & Third Party Management you develop frameworks, methods and processes and ensure their effective implementation. You work closely with business, risk, compliance and other specialist departments and ensure transparency and compliance throughout the entire lifecycle. This will help secure strong partnerships and strengthen PostFinance’s innovative power.

You can do that.

You maintain and develop the TPM framework further, including instructions, manuals, methods, operationalization of regulatory requirements (e.g. FINMA) and process descriptions

You control and develop the end-to-end lifecycle for third-party relationships – from needs clarification to onboarding, contract and risk management to monitoring, re-assessment and exit.

You take on the technical responsibility for TPM-relevant tools, data models, the central partnership register and evaluations and ensure their further development

You create management and control reports and create transparency about partnerships, investments, sub-accordants as well as relevant risks and measures

You are the central point of contact for TPM stakeholders from the 2nd Line and work closely with Business, Third Party Management, Risk, Compliance and other specialists

You support the continuous improvement of TPM processes and unite regulatory, professional and operational
