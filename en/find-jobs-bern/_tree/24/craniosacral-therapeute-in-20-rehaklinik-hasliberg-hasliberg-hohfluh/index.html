Full description

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of internistic-oncological, psychosomatic and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the mission statement of “REHA competence” with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, warmth, attention and competence.

Their tasks

  • Implementation of Craniosacral Treatments in Rehabilitation Patients
  • Active participation in individual, rehabilitative therapy plans in close cooperation with the medical profession, nursing and therapy teams
  • Professional documentation of the treatments and courses of therapy carried out
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the multi-professional rehab team
