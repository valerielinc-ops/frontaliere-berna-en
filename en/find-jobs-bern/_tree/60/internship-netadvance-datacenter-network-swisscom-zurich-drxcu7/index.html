# Internship NETAdvance – Datacenter Network

Il tuo futuro inizia qui

In this 12-month, practice-oriented student programme, you will immerse yourself in the fascinating world of network, data center infrastructure and innovation at Swisscom NET. You are actively involved in the Datacenter Network & Orchestration (DCN) team at Datacenter Connectivity. At the same time, you are involved in designing a future-oriented innovation project. The DCN team is responsible for the infrastructure and orchestration of the classic hosting of Swisscom AG. The DCN team is young and has deep knowledge of routing, switching and software development. During the program, you will build a strong network within NET and gain valuable insights beyond national borders through an international visit. The program will start in February 2027.

  • Participation in the further development of innovative projects such as Future Hosting Platform
  • Insight into the operation and further development of data center network platforms
  • Automation and scripting to optimize network and infrastructure processes
  • Active participation in agile project teams (Scrum/SAFe) and in an innovation project

Your skills

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree (Uni/FH) in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering or related disciplines (degree not more than one year ago)
  • initial knowledge of network technology,
  • In-depth knowledge of AI/LLM and/or cloud technologies
  • Good skills in scripting/programming (e.g. Python, SQL)
  • Enthusiasm for new technologies and agile methods (Scrum, SAFe)
  • Rapid comprehension and structured, independent working methods
  • Proactive and team-oriented personality
  • Networked, analytical thinking and understanding of business relationships
  • Very good German and English
  • French advantage

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Discover your benefits.
