Full description

Together for a self-determined life at home Familiehilfe Liechtenstein is the leading provider of outpatient care, care and support in Liechtenstein. With our services, we enable people of all ages to live independently in their familiar environment. Quality, humanity and innovation are at the heart of our actions. In the course of the further development of our organization, we are looking for a committed, professionally experienced and entrepreneurial personality as Head of Care and Care (Chief Nursing Officer, CNO) Their tasks

  • Professional, human and organizational management of the area of care and support as well as participation in the strategic orientation of family help as a member of the management
  • Leadership and support of directly subordinate cadres
  • Ensuring and further developing quality of care and care
  • Responsibility for the development, implementation and further development of concepts, policies and processes
  • Promoting appreciative, interprofessional cooperation
  • Management and support of change and development projects
  • Representation of family assistance vis-à-vis external partners and stakeholders

Your profile

  • Completed tertiary level nursing diploma (HF, FH or equivalent)
  • Leadership experience in healthcare, ideally in outpatient care and care
  • Further training in management, management or business administration
  • Strong strategic and conceptual capabilities
  • High social skills, communication skills and decision-making skills
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and an appreciative leadership style

We offer them

  • A responsible leadership function with a large creative scope
  • The opportunity to actively shape the future of outpatient care and care in Liechtenstein
  • Direct cooperation with the Chairman of the Executive Board, management of corporate processes and operations as well as the Board of Trustees
  • Working with a dedicated and competent leadership team
  • Modern working conditions and contemporary infrastructure
  • Attractive employment conditions as well as development and training opportunities

Interested? Then we look forward to your complete application to karriere@familiehilfe.li. Michelle Posch, President of the Board of Trustees, is at your disposal for further information by calling +423 236 00 58.
