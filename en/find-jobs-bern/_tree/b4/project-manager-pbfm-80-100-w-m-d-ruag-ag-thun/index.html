Full description

RUAG Real Estate AG is a real estate competence centre and a subsidiary of RUAG MRO Holding AG. We plan and implement tailor-made room, construction and service solutions within our own real estate portfolio at central locations in Switzerland. Our focus is on the duality of investment and operational properties, especially for special properties in the areas of aviation, armament technologies and highly secure infrastructures. In addition, our room solutions and operator services create optimal conditions for the business success of our customers. With a holistic approach along the entire real estate lifecycle, digital solutions and long-term customer relationships, we stand for the highest level of professionalism and sustainable value creation.

You can move that

  • Responsible for the integration of sustainable construction and operational standards taking into account ecological, economic and social aspects
  • Planning and control of all phases of the building life cycle – from planning to construction to operation and subsequent renovation or decommissioning – with a focus on sustainable use and maintenance
  • Supervise construction work, ensure compliance with construction and safety standards, and coordinate all parties involved to ensure smooth running of the project
  • Analysis and optimization of operating costs during the use phase to ensure long-term profitability
  • Introducing construction and safety standards as well as owner and operator requirements into projects
  • Ensuring quality, deadlines and costs in the projects by applying the PBFM approach
  • Supporting cooperation in the various planning and management bodies

You bring that with you

  • Training in Facility Management with a focus on building-related FM
  • Very good knowledge of sustainable construction as well as SIA/GEFMA guidelines
  • Several years of experience in planning and implementing construction projects and in-depth knowledge in project management
  • Communicative personality with negotiation skills as well as the ability to keep track of challenging projects
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as high quality and responsibility awareness
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Safe handling of digital media, CAD and CAFM systems
  • Category B valid driving licence
