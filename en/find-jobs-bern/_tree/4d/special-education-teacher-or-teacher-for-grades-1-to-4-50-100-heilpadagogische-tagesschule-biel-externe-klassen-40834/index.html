Portrait We are a bilingual special school and look after children aged 4 to 18 years with special educational and educational needs.

For our new location in Magglingen we are now looking for a

Tasks

They teach in a class with children who have special educational needs. They develop and realize pedagogical projects primarily for outsiders. They work with school management, educational teams and parents. They work interdisciplinary.

Requirements

They have a school curative education or have an equivalent education or experience. They are capable of teamwork and maintain close cooperation with various actors. They feel comfortable dealing with children with special needs. They like to take responsibility and work solution-oriented. They are stress resistant, conscientious and flexible. They are adept at talking to parents and professionals. They are resilient and have the ability to handle demanding situations professionally. They benefit from training in autism spectrum disorders.

A challenging job in a motivated educational team. A varied and creative activity. A respectful and collegial cooperation and a good working atmosphere. Conditions of employment according to cantonal guidelines.
