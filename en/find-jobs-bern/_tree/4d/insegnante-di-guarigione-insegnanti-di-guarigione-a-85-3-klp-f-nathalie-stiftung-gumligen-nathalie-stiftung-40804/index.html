Full description

Portrait Do you value a meaningful task in a professional environment? Are you looking for a challenge and want to deal intensively with pedagogical questions in the field of ASS? The Nathalie Foundation’s curative day school supports and cares for children and adolescents with complex developmental disorders (multiple disabilities, perceptual disorders and autism spectrum disorders).

Tasks

  • Together with your job partner, you are responsible for the individual promotion of five students in a class in Cycle 1
  • They plan their lessons independently and document according to internal requirements
  • You are in close exchange with the other class teacher and take over the reference work of 2 students.
  • They work constructively with the interdisciplinary team, the boarding school and the parents and participate in school and quality development.
  • You take part in internal specialist consultations, training courses and events
  • They guide trainees

Requirements

  • Completed training in clinical or school curative education or teacher with experience working with children with multiple disabilities and/or willingness to acquire the necessary know-how
  • An independent and flexible personality with humor, empathy and team spirit
  • Experience working with people with complex disabilities/ASS
  • Knowledge of the Affolter model, TEACCH and/or UK is desirable
  • High resilience and joy in promoting students with challenging behavior

We offer

  • A versatile and demanding working day with possibilities for 1:1 promotion and many design possibilities
  • Fixed teaching hours: Tuesday and Thursday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.,

Wednesday 9.00 – 1.15 pm (team teaching) and Friday 9.00 – 11.00

  • Collaboration in a committed and interdisciplinary team with a supportive leadership and support
  • An open and honest, professional and personal exchange
  • A place where employees see themselves as independent, flexible personalities who show humor, have empathy and want to work in a team
  • Permanent employment according to cantonal guidelines and internal as well as external training opportunities (also temporary employment up to the end of SJ possible)
  • Teamteaching is limited for one year, with option to continue
