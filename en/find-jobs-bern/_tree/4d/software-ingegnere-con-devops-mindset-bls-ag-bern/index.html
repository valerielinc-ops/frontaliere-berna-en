Full description

Together with an interdisciplinary team, you will build, develop and integrate the new platform for reliable and future-oriented passenger information. In doing so, you connect development and operation and ensure that technology and teamwork intertwine seamlessly. This is how you will shape the digital future of BLS with us.

Here’s what’s waiting for you

  • Further develop new functions and interfaces for passenger information in the trains and accompany the solutions through to productive operation
  • Continuously develop our CI/CD, build, test, deployment and operational processes and implement meaningful automations
  • Analyze and rectify faults, ensure sustainable solutions together with the team and take responsibility for the software in operation
  • Plan and accompany releases, change and maintenance work and ensure the long-term maintenance of our applications
  • Collaborate with product owners, our business departments, other IT teams, and external partners to bring your technical perspective from requirement to operation

What you bring to the table

  • Completed training or further education in computer science, software engineering or a comparable technical background
  • Several years of experience in software development and the willingness to take responsibility for your solutions even in productive operation
  • Good knowledge of Java or another object-oriented programming language as well as experience with CI/CD and modern development tools, knowledge of containers, GitLab or OpenShift are a plus
  • A good understanding of the operational requirements of productive applications and enjoyment of analyzing errors down to the root cause and automating recurring tasks in the long term
  • A structured and pragmatic way of working, strong analytical skills and enjoyment of working together across team and subject boundaries

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2. Class, a GA travelcard 1. Class or a Half-Fare Card
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office
  • Organize your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten days of vacation per year and to take a sabbatical of 40 working days every seven years.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.
  • Financial support for external training, a management development program and an exciting internal learning and course offer are available to you.

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.

  • Mehrjährige Erfahrung in der Softwareentwicklung und die Bereitschaft, Verantwortung für deine Lösungen auch im produktiven Betrieb zu übernehmen

  • Gute Kenntnisse in Java oder einer anderen objektorientierten Programmiersprache sowie Erfahrung mit CI/CD und modernen Entwicklungswerkzeugen, Wissen rund um Container, GitLab oder OpenShift sind ein Plus

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
