Full description

For our client in Bern, we are looking for two experienced, motivated and open-minded Senior Python Streaming & Data Engineers (RabbitMQ / Snowflake / PostgreSQL)

For this 2-month project (service and remote) we are looking for an experienced team of two engineers who together with an experienced engineering team will develop a powerful and reliable Python-based streaming and data integration solution. The goal is the processing of trade, market and file-based data from RabbitMQ as well as their reliable provision in Snowflake or PostgreSQL.

The focus is on developing production-ready data pipelines, optimizing performance and data processing, and implementing robust recovery, replay and backfill mechanisms.

The two engineers, who are jointly responsible for the entire data processing chain, ideally complement each other with their technical focus as follows:

  • Streaming & Performance: Emphasis on RabbitMQ consumer development, Python Concurrency, performance optimization and stable processing under high load
  • Data Integration & Recovery: Focus on Snowflake/PostgreSQL, reliable data acquisition and recovery, replay and backfill processes

Both focuses require practical experience with RabbitMQ and verifiable project experience in productive environments.

Tasks

  • Development and optimization of a scalable Python application for processing RabbitMQ data streams
  • Implementation and further development of RabbitMQ consumers for productive streaming environments
  • Integration and configuration of data acquisition in Snowflake and Snowflake Postgres
  • Ensuring reliable message processing including acknowledgments, Redelivery, backpressure and deduplication
  • Design and implement robust recovery, replay and backfill processes
  • Optimization of latency, throughput and CPU and memory consumption
  • Analysis and correction of performance bottlenecks, peak loads and failure cases
  • Ensuring data integrity and data consistency for live and historical data
  • Integration of reloading processes using Kestra and dlt (dltHub) in collaboration with the internal Data Ingestion Engineer
  • Development of automated tests and performance and load tests
  • Integration of the solution in Azure DevOps CI/CD and implementation of basic monitoring functions
  • Close cooperation with internal platform and engineering teams as well as documentation and knowledge transfer

Profile

  • Very good practical experience in Python development, especially with long running productive applications
  • In-depth experience with RabbitMQ, in particular consumer development, message processing and error handling
  • Practical experience with streaming systems under real load and their performance optimization
  • Good knowledge of Snowflake and PostgreSQL, especially in the field of Data Ingestion and Data Integration
  • Experience with recovery, replay, backfill, deduplication and data consistency
  • Very good understanding of acknowledgements, Redelivery, Backpressure and Message Queuing
  • Knowledge of Python Concurrency, CPU/Memory Profiling and Performance Optimization
  • Experience in developing and operating production-ready data pipelines
  • Knowledge of automated tests, CI/CD and monitoring
  • Experience with Azure DevOps and modern development and deployment processes
  • Knowledge in Azure, AKS, Kestra, dlt, Kafka or Debezium
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as strong teamwork
  • Experience working with internal platform, development and operations teams
