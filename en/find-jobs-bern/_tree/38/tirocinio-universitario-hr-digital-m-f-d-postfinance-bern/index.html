Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

With us, you will actively shape the digital future of HR. As part of HR Business Excellence, HR Digital connects people, processes, data and technology to make working in HR easier, smarter and more effective. Together with various HR departments, we develop digital solutions, automate workflows and create added value for our HR department. In doing so, you will help to understand new possibilities around artificial intelligence at an early stage, to transfer them securely to concrete HR applications and to enabler and anchor modern working methods in HR.

What you can do

  • You will work on the development and implementation of digital solutions for HR processes
  • You will develop understandable tools and enabling offers for digitization and AI in HR, carry them out together with us and support HR professionals in using digital tools, data and AI safely and effectively
  • You will support data evaluations and reporting and help to process relevant information in an understandable way
  • Together with us and the HR departments, you will examine how data analyses, processes and working methods can be digitized or simplified and contribute your knowledge

What you bring to the table

  • You have a Bachelor's degree in Business Informatics, Digital Business, Business/Data Analytics, Business Administration or a comparable field that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your Bachelor's degree
  • Initial experience with digital tools (Microsoft 365/Copilot), data, process optimization or AI is an advantage
  • Enjoyment of independent work and a flair for preparing and presenting topics related to tools, data and digital solutions in an understandable way
  • Networked and solution-oriented way of thinking, initiative and strong team play
  • Very good knowledge of German; other languages are an advantage
