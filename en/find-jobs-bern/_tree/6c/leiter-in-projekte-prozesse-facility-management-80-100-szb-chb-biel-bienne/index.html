Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population? Facility Management is a key partner for the smooth operation of the hospital and ambulatory health facilities. With the areas of technology, buildings & areas, security, catering, housekeeping and supply chain services, we ensure a reliable infrastructure and diverse services for high-quality patient care. With a planned new hospital building and various ongoing construction projects, we are actively shaping the future of our hospital. Already in the spring of 2027, the commissioning of an approximately 4,000 m2 area in a new building at the Biel station is scheduled.
