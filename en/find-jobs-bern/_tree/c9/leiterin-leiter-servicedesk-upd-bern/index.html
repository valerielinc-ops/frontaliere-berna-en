Full description

ICT Directorate

As head of the service desk of the UPZ Bern, you are responsible for the efficient organization and management of a team that acts as the first point of contact for more than 1,000 employees – whether from the medical or psychological field, care or administration. This is not about anonymous implementation, but about real thinking, fast implementation and active support. You ensure that the service is fast, solution-oriented and empathetic and work closely with the specialist areas to ensure smooth processes. With your leadership approach, you promote the development of your team and actively contribute to the development of the service desk as a proactive support.

Your tasks

  • You take over the professional and organizational management of the service desk and ensure smooth operation and continuous development
  • You support your team with patience, know-how and heart – and ensure that 1st-level support cases are handled efficiently and customer-oriented
  • You coordinate the prioritization and processing of tickets in the ITSM tool, keeping an eye on quality, deadlines and service level agreements
  • You actively promote the expansion and maintenance of the IT knowledge base and ensure clear, comprehensible and sustainable documentation
  • You are responsible for the lifecycle management of end-user devices – from procurement to rollout to decommissioning
  • You create and maintain comprehensible service descriptions and make the services of the service desk transparent
  • Ensure support for conference and meeting rooms so that meetings start smoothly at any time

Your profile

  • You have completed an IT education or an equivalent qualification – ideally supplemented by further training in the field of leadership or IT service management
  • You have several years of experience in the service desk or IT support and have already taken on leadership responsibility
  • You are safe and routine with the MS Office range and ITSM tools
  • You convince with strong communication and leadership skills as well as a high level of service awareness
  • You have a very good knowledge of German – every other language is a welcome plus

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
