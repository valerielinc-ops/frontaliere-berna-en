Objective

The main objective of ESA's Genesis mission is to realize an ultra-stable, global Earth-fixed coordinate system (aiming for 1 mm accuracy and 0.1 mm/year stability necessary to monitor climate change relevant processes). The Genesis satellite will combine the four space geodetic techniques, Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS, like GPS), Satellite Laser Ranging (SLR), Doppler Orbitography and Radiopositioning Integrated by Satellite (DORIS), and Very Long Baseline Interferometry (VLBI), on a highly calibrated and stable platform. The adequate analysis of these mission data requires significant upgrades and developments in scientific software packages, like for instance the Bernese GNSS Software maintained and used at AIUB. The position of the Senior Research Assistant is scheduled for two years and will coordinate the significant amount of software modifications, that need to be done in parallel. In this context, the development environment and process used at AIUB shall be reviewed and advanced where necessary. In the frame of this project a number of decisions have to be taken (regarding, e.g., software design, data handling, High Performance Computing, HPC), where professional input from a software engineer is welcome. The activities will also include contacts to external partner institutions such as the Bundesamt für Kartographie und Geodäsie (BKG), Germany, the Research Institute of Geodesy, Cartography and Topography (VUGTK), Czech Republic, and the Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany. The planned developments will be performed in close exchange with ESA's Genesis Science Exploitation Team and the IAG/IERS Joint Working Group, where IAG is the International Association of Geodesy and IERS stands for International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service.

Education

The candidate is expected to have successfully completed a Master or Ph.D. thesis in computer science, astronomy, geodesy, physics, or a related topic. Experience in software engineering (we use modern Fortran, C++, Python, and Perl) is mandatory, a background in space geodetic data analyses, ideally using the Bernese GNSS Software package, is beneficial. The candidate should speak and write English fluently. The candidate should start working in Bern as soon as possible. The position is scheduled for two years with an option for an extension to three years.
