Put to the Point

The Service for Preventive Protection of the Army (DPSA) forms the first line of defense of the army. It monitors and assesses the military security situation and takes precautionary measures to protect the army from espionage, sabotage and other illegal acts.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21079

You can make this contribution

  • Support the implementation of operational support for all missions of the service in the areas of logistics and leadership support
  • Responsible for the service’s material readiness and ensuring the availability of funds
  • Support the development of operational concepts for the technical sensors
  • Support the case handling of the service and the pre-evaluation of information
  • Support the logistical concept and ensure implementation
  • Responsible for the maintenance and management of all means, including sensors of the service

This makes them unique.

  • Officer or non-commissioned officer of the Swiss Army, fulfillment of the requirements of the Ordinance of the VBS on military personnel (Art 7 or Art 8)
  • University degree (bachelor) or technical college with technical/informatics orientation and 5–7 years of functionally relevant professional experience or EFZ/FH/HF with 7–10 professional experience in a professional formation of the army or in the police landscape
  • Several years of operational experience in missions of the army abroad
  • Experience in logistics planning and leadership support, knowledge of the SAP system
  • Good oral and written knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English

Your commitment to security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civil employees – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
