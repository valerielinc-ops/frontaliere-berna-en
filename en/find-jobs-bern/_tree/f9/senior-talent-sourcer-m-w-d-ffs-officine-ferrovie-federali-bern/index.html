Senior Talent Source (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Senior Talent Source (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

The future of SBB begins with the right people. As an Active Sourcer, you bring together talents and opportunities, recruit specialists and executives for key positions and advise executives up to the top management level. Look forward to a dedicated team that shares knowledge, supports each other and together wins the best talent for SBB. You can move that.

  • You are responsible for demanding active sourcing mandates from order clearance to the handover of qualified candidates and thus support the filling of strategically important functions for SBB.
  • Together with recruiting partners and executives, you will develop targeted search and address strategies.
  • Identify talent, analyze markets and build a sustainable talent pipeline.
  • With your professional direct approach, you win candidates for SBB and create a positive candidate experience.
  • As a sparring partner, you advise executives up to the top management level on talent markets, search strategies and recruiting trends.
  • You will actively contribute your know-how to the further development of sourcing methods, processes, tools and data-based recruiting approaches.

You bring that with you.

  • You convince through communication strength, consulting competence and a secure appearance at all hierarchical levels.
  • As a sparring partner, you build trusting relationships with candidates, executives and other stakeholders.
  • You analyze complex questions quickly, act in a solution-oriented manner and distinguish yourself through an independent and structured working method with a high quality standard.
  • You have several years of experience in active sourcing, executive search, recruiting or recruitment consulting and have already been able to successfully support challenging search mandates.
  • A degree at HF, FH or university level or an equivalent education forms your professional basis.
  • You communicate in German (level C2) and ideally have knowledge of French (at least level B1).

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This job is in requirement level K, regional allowance level 1.
