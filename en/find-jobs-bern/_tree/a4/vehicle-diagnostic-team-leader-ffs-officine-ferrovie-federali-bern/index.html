Team leader: in vehicle diagnosis 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in vehicle diagnosis Bern, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In vehicle diagnostics, you make a direct contribution to the safe, reliable and customer-oriented mobility of Switzerland. Together with your team of ten diagnosticians, you ensure that technical faults on the rolling stock of passenger transport are quickly detected, analyzed and permanently remedied. The missions take place mobile directly at the station or in the assigned rayon. You combine technical know-how with modern leadership and thus create the conditions for a high availability of our fleet. In this varied role, you take on responsibility for employees, processes and technical developments. You work closely with different specialist areas and actively shape the future of vehicle diagnosis. Our team is characterized by high level of expertise, mutual support and pragmatic solution orientation. In addition, the function offers you attractive further education and development opportunities in leadership and technology. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad – also for the family – can be found on our website under Benefits and more information about the profession can be found here. You can move that.

  • You lead a team of ten diagnosticians, both professionally and in terms of personnel, and ensure efficient vehicle diagnosis at the Berne location in compliance with the applicable safety regulations.
  • In addition to the management task, you actively support your team on site and independently take on diagnostic and disruptive missions on the vehicle. This will make a significant contribution to the safety and availability of vehicles and systems.
  • The scheduling and time management of your team is your responsibility. You make sure that enough staff is available at the location at all times.
  • You are also responsible for the office and location infrastructure and ensure its proper use and smooth operation.
  • Working closely with helpdesk, maintenance, operations and other partners, you will develop sustainable solutions to increase the safety, availability and reliability of the vehicle fleet.
  • By analyzing fault images and key figures, you identify potential for optimization and accompany technical innovations as well as digitalization projects in the area of vehicle diagnostics.

You bring that with you.

  • Complex technical relationships are conveyed in an understandable way, make well-founded decisions and can justify them in a comprehensible way.
  • Leadership experience in a technical environment or the potential and motivation to develop specifically into a leadership function distinguishes you.
  • You have completed basic technical training, for example as a mechatronics engineer:in, automatic engineer:in, electrician:in or in a comparable technical profession.
  • Ideally, you will have experience in vehicle diagnosis, maintenance or technical support of vehicles.
  • The willingness to work partly in shifts as well as at night and on weekends, you bring with you.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (at least C1), ideally also knowledge of French, as well as a driving license of category B.
