Full description

Put to the Point

As a central point of contact, you bring together contracts, suppliers and specialist areas and ensure that takeovers are successful on time and quality-assured. You accompany the entire life cycle of the specialist application – from contract analysis to procurement and safe transfer of operations.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • By agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 609-19772
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Analyse contracts and service packages to be adopted with regard to degree of absorption, duration and specificities
  • Evaluate technical and contractual transferability to BIT standard operations and coordinate with product owners and specialist areas on takeover risks and conditions
  • Provide operational support to product owners by creating order requirements, procurement support, and budget documentation and tracking across the application lifecycle
  • Perform life cycle planning (roadmap, releases, changes, replacements) as well as coordination of supplier performance according to the contract (SLAs, key figures, quality) and participate in documents for WTO procurement
  • Ensure service readiness from takeover date (monitoring, backup, patch management, access management) and participate in security documentation (Schuban, ISDS, ICT basic protection)

This makes you unique

  • Completed tertiary education (HF, FH, Uni) in computer science, business informatics or ICT management
  • Compensable by equivalent professional experience
  • Several years of professional experience in ICT service management, in particular in the acquisition and operation of specialized applications required
  • Project experience in transition/transformation of applications into operational operation desired
  • Secure handling of ITIL processes (incident, problem, change, release, service level management)
  • Knowledge of public procurement law (BöB/VöB) incl. Contract controlling and management of supplier services (SLA/KPI, escalation, extensions)
  • Basic knowledge of security documentation (Schuban, ISDS, ICT-Grundschutz) for active participation in security concepts
  • High personal responsibility and secure prioritization under time pressure
  • Convincing mediation between customers, suppliers and internal teams
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages
