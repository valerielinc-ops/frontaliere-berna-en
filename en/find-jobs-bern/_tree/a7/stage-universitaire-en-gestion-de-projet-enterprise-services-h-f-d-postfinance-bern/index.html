Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

The financial sector is constantly evolving, and customers expect modern solutions and smart innovations when it comes to money. PostFinance is a diversified, innovation-driven financial services provider. In doing so, we rely on forward-looking tools and technologies that we develop or adapt specifically for the Swiss market.

Innovation Management creates the conditions for a standardized and agile innovation approach across companies in order to be able to react quickly to customer needs. We implement projects within the specified time and resources as well as the required quality and act as a center of excellence for the promotion and implementation of innovation.

What you can do

  • You will support projects from initiation to completion, learning about the processes, methods and governance of innovation and project implementation at PostFinance
  • You coordinate interdisciplinary teams and promote cooperation between business, IT and other relevant stakeholders
  • You create transparency about progress, risks, dependencies and goal achievement and thus support well-founded decisions at all levels
  • You can easily monitor the progress of the project and ensure that schedules, quality requirements and agreed goals are met
  • You will accompany the introduction of new technologies and contribute to the further development of future-proof platforms and services
  • You are interested in transformative initiatives in the areas of Risk, Compliance & Legal, Finance, Corporate Development & People and IT & Operations and you are able to think your way into different topics

What you bring to the table

  • You have a bachelor's degree in economics, engineering or computer science that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your bachelor's degree program
  • Great interest, basic understanding or initial experience in the field of project management
  • Independent way of working, initiative and ability to self-organize paired with strong team player qualities
  • Willingness to solve problems together in project teams
  • Enjoyment of coordinating and managing project teams and promoting interdisciplinary exchange
  • Level-appropriate communication as well as the ability to communicate and present complex issues in a clear and understandable way
  • Very good knowledge of German
