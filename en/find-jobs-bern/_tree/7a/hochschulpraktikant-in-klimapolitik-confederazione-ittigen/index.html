Put to the Point

Help shape Swiss climate policy for the period after 2030 and gain immediate insight into the work on legislative proposals and other political deals.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20870
  • Place of work: Mühlestrasse 2, 3063 Ittigen

You can make this contribution

  • Participating in the revision of the CO2 law for the period after 2030 and further climate policy business
  • Support official consultations and consultations, systematically collect and evaluate opinions
  • Prepare the results report on the consultation on the CO2 law after 2030 and coordinate the coordination with participating specialist bodies
  • Participate in the processing of parliamentary initiatives and in the preparation of reports and decision-making bases

This makes you unique

  • University studies (bachelor or master), for example in political, economic or environmental sciences
  • Analytical and conceptual skills for the structured evaluation of complex information
  • Interest in Swiss climate and environmental policy as well as in political decision-making processes
  • Very good written expressiveness and pleasure in the comprehensible preparation of facts
  • Independent, careful and reliable working methods and organizational skills
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and English and, if possible, knowledge of a third official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Quality of life for today and tomorrow.

The Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN) is responsible for the sustainable use of natural resources such as soil, water, air and forests, for protection against natural hazards and for the preservation of biodiversity. It is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

We keep an eye on Switzerland today and tomorrow and assume responsibility for the successful development of our country in challenging projects. The FOEN offers the opportunity to make a significant contribution to a sustainable Switzerland in a modern working environment.

Additional information

Entry date: 01.01.2027 or by arrangement. Internship period 01.01.2027 - 31.12.2027. Conditions for a university internship: students and graduates (admin.ch)
