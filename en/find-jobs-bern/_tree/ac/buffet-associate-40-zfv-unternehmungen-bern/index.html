Full description

This work location includes a cafeteria. The meeting place in Bern creates an inviting meeting place for students and employees. Our team delights an average of 1,224 guests here from Monday to Friday with a varied offering - from daily changing menus to sandwiches and snacks to internal catering. We are looking for a committed and friendly personality as a buffet employee (40%) immediately or by appointment. You will support our team flexibly and as needed from Monday to Friday on an hourly basis. Your main work will be at lunchtime, usually from 11:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. In addition to your work with us, you will also support our Mensa Uni Tobler, Mensa Gesellschaftsstrasse or GeWo if necessary and, together with the on-site teams, ensure everything runs smoothly. Are you motivated, like to lend a hand and ensure that everything runs smoothly and your guests are satisfied with your warm manner? Then we look forward to getting to know you! Your task menu:

  • You receive and advise our guests in a friendly manner and serve the cash register
  • You will prepare the menus during the lunch service and ensure that the buffet runs smoothly
  • You will prepare the mise en place and ensure an appealing presentation of the food
  • You support caterings and special events
  • You will ensure cleanliness and order and comply with ZFV quality standards as well as hygiene and occupational safety guidelines
  • You handle food, materials and equipment carefully and pay attention to the sustainable use of resources Your recipe for success:
  • You have already gained practical experience in the gastronomy or hotel industry
  • You enjoy contact with guests and are friendly and service-oriented
  • You like to work in a team, are reliable and actively help in day-to-day business
  • You are open to new things, like to learn new things and can adapt to changing requirements
  • You have a good oral knowledge of German in order to be able to communicate with guests and in the team
  • A Progresso apprenticeship is an advantage This is what we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are promoted. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 52,000 and CHF 60,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
