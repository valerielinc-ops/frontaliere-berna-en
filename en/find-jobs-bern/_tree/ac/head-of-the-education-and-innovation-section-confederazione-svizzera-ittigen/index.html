Put to the Point

With your strong leadership skills and expertise, you and your team are responsible for the coherent overall strategy and its implementation in the fields of environmental education, environmental research and environmental technology promotion of the Office in close cooperation with actors in science, business and education.

  • Date of entry: 01.04.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21150
  • Location: Mühlestrasse 2, 3063 Ittigen

You can make this contribution

  • Lead and further develop the Education and Innovation Section in professional, organisational, financial and human terms
  • Grants for environmental education and technology projects based on transparent criteria and through a systematic selection process
  • Develop the environmental research concept and coordinate research activities in the Office
  • Developing scientific and conceptual foundations as well as political business in the areas of environmental education, innovation promotion and research
  • Actively foster cooperation with science, organizations of the working world, companies, associations and other federal offices

This makes them unique.

  • University degree, preferably in the scientific direction
  • Knowledge and sound knowledge in research management, education and innovation landscape
  • Strong analytical skills, networked and strategic thinking and political sense
  • High personal responsibility and resilience
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and knowledge of a third official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Quality of life for today and tomorrow.

The Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN) is responsible for the sustainable use of natural resources such as soil, water, air and forests, for protection against natural hazards and for the preservation of biodiversity. It is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

We keep an eye on Switzerland today and tomorrow and assume responsibility for the successful development of our country in challenging projects. The FOEN offers the opportunity to make a significant contribution to a sustainable Switzerland in a modern working environment.

Entry date: 01.04.2027 or by arrangement.
