About us

The hospitals fmi AG with over 1,600 employees include the two hospitals Interlaken and Frutigen, the health center in Meiringen, the senior centers in Frutigen and Interlaken, the walk-in clinic and various practices.

The Weissenau Seniorenpark of Spitäler fmi AG sees itself as a competence center for the care and care of elderly people who can no longer live at home due to the severity and impairment of their health status. The institution offers 75 residents a home, of which 12 places are specifically designed for people with dementia.
