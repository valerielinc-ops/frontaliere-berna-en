Role overview

The Psychiatric Services SRO provides regional, community-based and patient-oriented psychiatric care for 80,000 residents.

We have modern, integrated care structures such as emergency triage, station-equivalent mobile acute care, integrative psychiatric care, social psychiatric and psychotherapeutic outpatients at 4 locations with special offers, as well as rehabilitation day clinic, acute day clinic, old-age psychiatry with ambulatory and day clinic and an inpatient offer with 34 beds in the Langenthal hospital.

We have the SIWF recognition as a training institution of category A for 3 years, both outpatient and inpatient.
