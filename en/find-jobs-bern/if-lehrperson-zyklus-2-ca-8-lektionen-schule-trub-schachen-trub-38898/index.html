Full description

Portrait The MR Kander- und Engstlig Valley is responsible for special lessons in 5 municipalities. To strengthen our team we are looking for a healing pä-ediologist for the municipality of Reichbach.

Tasks

  • You plan and design inclusive teaching together with the class teacher.
  • You promote and support the children in the class association, in individual or group SESSION.
  • With your authentic style you advise guardians and teachers.
  • You work interdisciplinary with the MR team (IBEM team), school leaders and specialist offices.
  • The lessons take place in cycles 1 and 2 in three primary schools.

Requirements

  • You have completed or are willing to complete a curative education.
  • With pleasure and interest you are involved in the internal and interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • You have a high level of communication, team and reflection skills.
  • With your reliable and open personality you are committed to the students and support the college.
  • You meet the everyday challenges with flexibility, serenity and a pinch of humour.

We offer

  • We have our own office with a lot of teaching material in Frutigen.
  • The team consists of experienced, motivated and dedicated special teachers, who would like to exchange ideas with another Heilpädagogin/other curative teacher.
  • We attach great importance to interdisciplinary cooperation.
