Full description

Portrait Leubringen/Evilard is located above the city of Biel and can be reached very easily and quickly by public transport (Funic all 10'). We are a village school with two languages under one roof and house eight German-speaking and six French-speaking kindergarten and primary school classes. The mood is familiar and benevolent. Many more information about us, our festivals, rituals and attitude can be found on our website.

Tasks

They teach 12L at 1st grade (11 SuS). The 12 lessons are distributed on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday (4L German, 3L NMG, 2L TTG, 2L Music, 1L Sport). The lessons take place partly in a team with the class teacher. The deputy is expected to start from the winter holidays (job entry 11.01.2027), at the latest from the 2nd semester (job entry 01.02.2027). It may be that the holiday is extended after the regular 16 weeks.

Requirements

You have the Bachelor of primary education or another suitable education. They are familiar with the LP 21 and with modern forms of teaching. At best, bring teaching experience with young children.

We offer

  • very experienced class teacher who will gladly support you with questions
  • A modernly equipped classroom
  • Small, hearty class :-!
  • it is possible to get lunch from our day school (and our cook cooks well ;-)!)
  • Free early bird yoga for teachers on Wednesday morning
