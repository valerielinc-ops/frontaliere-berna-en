Full description

Your expertise ensures that our trains are storm-proof and reliable – in sunshine, wind and snow. You uncover hidden faults, bring systems back into sync and ensure that passengers reach Bern from Lucerne relaxed and on time. This keeps Switzerland moving and gives people confidence and security every day.

What awaits you

  • Localizing complex faults in railway vehicles and their components
  • Tracking, monitoring and analyzing the progression of vehicle faults and preparing reports for resolving the faults
  • Close collaboration with the department responsible for railway engineering
  • Participation in technical working groups
  • Advising, supporting and training team leaders and employees

What you bring

  • Successfully completed technical vocational training (EFZ)
  • Qualification as a certified electrical engineering technician HF, certified systems technician HF, or equivalent further training
  • Experience in the maintenance of rail vehicles is an advantage
  • Analytical personality with strong people skills
  • Willingness to work nights and weekends

Your benefits – what you can look forward to

  • We support the most flexible possible planning of your tours and shifts
  • Get a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a Half-Fare Travelcard.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are additional allowances for working at night and on weekends.
  • It is possible to purchase an additional five to ten vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external training and an exciting range of internal learning opportunities and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company, and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What do we have in common? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
