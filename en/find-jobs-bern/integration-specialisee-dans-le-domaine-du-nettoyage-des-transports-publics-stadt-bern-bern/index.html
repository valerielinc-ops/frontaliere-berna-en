Full description

At the Directorate for Education, Social Affairs and Sport, the focus is on people. The KA supports training and unemployed people in their professional and social integration and performs tasks in the asylum and refugee sector.

To promote professional and social integration, we offer work training in cleaning in public transport. Together, we contribute to cleanliness and value preservation for vehicles, waiting halls and stops. Tasks: • You ensure a smooth work organization and accompany and support the program participants in a practical manner • You plan and coordinate the assignments together with the team • You accompany new program participants at the start and support them with questions in everyday work • You will teach language skills in short daily German courses and create new teaching content • You support administrative tasks by creating simple statistics and actively engage in everyday life Requirements • You bring high social and communication skills and enjoy dealing with people from different backgrounds • You have experience working with people from the asylum sector • You are an organizational talent, you enjoy being guided and on the way • You have basic knowledge of cleaning or are ready to acquire it We offer: For sense and city life With us you change something. You help shape city life, carry responsibility and make Bern more liveable. In the city of Bern, more than 4,500 people work together across five directorates and more than 80 job profiles. We are committed with passion and conviction to the people who live here, who work here. Together we shape the community and the future of this city – visible, tangible, sustainable. for our city. For Bern.
