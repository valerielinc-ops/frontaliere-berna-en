Full description

“Grow with children – start your future now!” Day care offers children a safe, appreciative and stimulating living space outside of class. As a day care learner, the majority of you work with children in cycle 1 (4-8 years) and cycle 2 (8-12 years). Children are cared for in age-separated groups. Apply now and become part of a profession with meaning, heart and perspective.

Tasks

  • You look after school-age children and promote them according to pedagogical principles
  • You learn to work with the entire care team
  • You create activities and guide them
  • You provide diverse learning experiences for children and young people

Requirements

  • You have completed Real or Secondary School
  • You enjoy dealing with children and young people
  • You enjoy working in a team, are communicative and can deal with conflicts
  • You are balanced, resilient and flexible
  • You have a high sense of responsibility, can feel good about others, are helpful and patient

We offer

Diverse and fair More than 20 exciting apprenticeships await you with us – from crafts to administrative. As a versatile apprenticeship company, we value the different role models and pay all apprenticeships the same wage – and learners from 25 even a little more.

The city of Bern is committed to sustainable vocational training and offers you a wide range of opportunities for your career. Around 200 apprentices are currently completing their training in over 20 different professions – accompanied by more than 300 experienced employees who pass on their knowledge and enthusiasm. With us you not only learn your dream job, but also actively shape life in our city. Become part of a team that works with joy, responsibility and heart for Bern.
