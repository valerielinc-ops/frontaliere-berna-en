Full description

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of psychosomatic, internistic-oncological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Solothurn, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the mission statement of “REHA competence” with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, warmth, attention and competence.

Their tasks

  • As head of department, active participation in cadre retraits and cadre meetings as well as participation in projects
  • Professional and human leadership responsibility for three MPAs
  • Support and organisation of the daily routine for doctors
  • Planning and coordination of consultation hours and medical examinations
  • Collection, control and dispatch of medical reports
  • Conduct of laboratory and X-ray examination and ECGs

Our expectations

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as medical:r practice assistant:in EFZ or specialist Health EFZ with further training in the laboratory area
  • Leadership experience is beneficial
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Good IT user knowledge, especially in Windows and MS Office
  • Strong organisational skills and enforcement

Our offer

  • Independent and responsible work
  • Flat hierarchies
  • Current employment conditions and regulated working hours without weekend service
  • Professional and personal development through interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Regular internal training opportunities
