Tasks

  • You support us in the reconstruction of our open age psychiatric acute care unit and can use your expertise and experience in a targeted way.
  • You help with the implementation of our new station concept and continue to develop it with us
  • Together with your interprofessional treatment team, you will contribute to a stabilization of patients and thus to a greater quality of life.
  • You support our vocational trainers in the training of learners and can pass on your experience to the next generation Requirements
  • You are Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH (or have a diploma recognised in Switzerland)
  • You have experience in psychiatry or experience in somatics with an interest in psychiatry
  • You ideally bring professional experience in caring for older people
  • You are enthusiastic about interprofessional cooperation and complex psychiatric issues
  • You enjoy working with people in acute crisis situations We offer: Family? Yes! With our day-care centre and flexible working time models, you can reconcile family and work.
