Full description

Your design space

  • You analyze project-specific requirements and control the digital planning process for civil engineering, power plant and hydraulic engineering as well as plant construction
  • The responsibility for model-based project management within the existing software landscape lies in your area of responsibility
  • You create relevant project documents, support users: in the daily use of BIM tools and conduct BIM coordination sessions across disciplines
  • In the further development of the BIM methodology, you actively shape processes and optimize model-based processes such as BIM2Field or Field2BIM
  • You support the team in the further development of the strategy and get involved in the integration of AI in the BIM environment

Your strengths

  • You have a completed degree in architecture, civil engineering, geomatics, computer science or a comparable field
  • Several years of practical experience in BIM projects in roles such as coordination, planning or management
  • The use of Autodesk BIM and CAD tools is familiar to you and you use them safely in everyday project life
  • You work in a structured way, quickly identify connections and independently find suitable solutions for complex questions
  • A strong interest in digital solutions and a secure use of common IT tools
  • Very good German and good English skills allow you to communicate clearly across team boundaries
