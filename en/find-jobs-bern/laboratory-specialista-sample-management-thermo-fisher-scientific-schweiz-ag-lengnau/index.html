Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities:
  • Manage the end-to-end sample lifecycle, including receipt, inspection, LIMS registration, aliquoting, storage, distribution, shipment, and final disposition
  • Ensure proper chain of custody, tracking, and documentation for all samples in compliance with GMP and internal procedures
  • Perform sampling activities for raw materials, plant utilities, clean utilities, environmental monitoring, and cleaning validation
  • Coordinate and support sampling activities at external warehouses (3PL) and manage interactions with third-party laboratories
  • Support QC laboratory operations, including preparation, testing activities, and general lab maintenance (equipment, waste management, cleanliness)
  • Assist in testing raw materials, in-process samples, intermediates, and finished products, as well as microbiological analyses (bioburden, endotoxin, PCR)
  • Organize and oversee sample logistics, including storage conditions, inventory, shipment, and timely delivery to internal and external stakeholders
  • Maintain accurate and compliant documentation within LIMS and other systems, ensuring data integrity at all times
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams, providing clear communication on sample status and supporting daily operational meetings
