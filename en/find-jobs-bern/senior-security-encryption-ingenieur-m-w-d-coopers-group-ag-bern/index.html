Full description

For a long-term and technologically demanding transformation program, we are looking for two Senior Security / Encryption Engineers for our customer from Bern.

For this digitalization program, we are looking for experienced engineers from the IT security sector who can help shape security architectures, further develop encryption solutions and accompany modern security concepts from strategy to implementation. They work on topics such as data protection, cryptography, security engineering, security assessments and zero trust.

Your tasks

  • Development and advancement of modern security, data protection and encryption solutions in complex ICT environments
  • Analysis of existing security architectures as well as identification of risks, vulnerabilities and potential for improvement
  • Design and implementation of security and data protection services as well as integration into existing platforms and systems
  • Collaboration in the development of security architectures, encryption concepts and zero-trust and security-by-design initiatives
  • Conducting security assessments, risk analysis and cross-technology security assessments
  • Evaluation of new technologies as well as advising specialists and stakeholders in the areas of cyber security, cryptography and data protection
  • Supporting project, architecture and engineering teams in security-relevant issues as well as promoting security best practices
  • Creation of concepts, decision bases, technical documentation and proof of concepts along the entire lifecycle of security solutions

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in the field of cyber security, security engineering, security architecture or information security
  • In-depth knowledge of encryption, cryptography, data protection or secure architecture
  • Experience with established security standards and frameworks such as ISO 27001, NIST, CIS benchmarks or comparable best practices
  • Know-how in one or more of the following areas: HSM, Key Management, PKI, Database Encryption, Post-Quantum Cryptography, Zero Trust or Security Assessments
  • Understanding complex ICT, network and infrastructure environments and their security requirements
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method with a high quality standard
  • Communication strength as well as the ability to communicate complex technical topics in an understandable way and to pick up different stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German and good technical English
  • Proper reputation is mandatory
