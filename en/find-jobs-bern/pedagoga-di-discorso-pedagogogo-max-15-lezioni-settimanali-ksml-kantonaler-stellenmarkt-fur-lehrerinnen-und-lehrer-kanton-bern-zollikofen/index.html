Full description

Portrait We are the Foundation for blind and visually impaired children and young people Zollikofen - a private, party-political and confe-tional neutral organization with a charitable objective. Our task is to promote and accompany people with special needs from birth to young adulthood. We offer additional services to parents, teachers of public schools and special elementary schools as well as to professionals.

Our core competencies lie in the promotion and accompaniment of young people with visual impairment, blindness, multiple impairment or from the autism spectrum.

At our school we are looking for one or more of our

Tasks

  • You clarify children and adolescents in a disabling manner and perform prevention, diagnostics and therapy for school children.
  • You work together with the teachers, parents and specialist offices.
  • You design the therapy lessons individually or in small groups, partly also in the classes, according to defined funding requirements.
  • The pupils are in the autism spectrum or blind, sight and multiple impaired.
  • Their focus is on blind children and adolescents or those with a visual impairment.
  • We wish that you will also acquire knowledge in the field of multiple impairment and in swallowing or swallowing disorders (dysphobia). - We will be happy to support you in building up these skills if necessary.

Requirements - With your completed and recognized study course in Logopaedics you have adapted the equipment for this function. - With your uncomplicated and cooperative way you contribute to a good team climate.

We offer

  • Our culture is characterized by appreciation and openness.
  • In your work you take a lot of responsibility and get the appropriate scope for design.
  • You work in a motivated and well-trained team and the pay is aligned according to the cantonal teacher employment law.
  • The Logopedics room is located in the school house.
