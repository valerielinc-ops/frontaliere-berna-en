Full description

SPITEX Grauholz is responsible for the high-quality, reliable and needs-based care of 11 municipalities in the area of outpatient care and housekeeping services. As a progressive organization, we continue to develop and count on our 170 competent, open-minded and motivated employees. They work in one of the three bases Jegenstorf, Münchenbuchsee, Urtenen-Schönbühl or in the housekeeping, psychiatry and investigation team. To strengthen our teams, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement: Dipl. Nurse HF/FH, AKP, DNII 60-100 % You want to

  • a new challenge or the re-entry into the versatile field of work of ambulatory care of people from the age of majority to old age.
  • Be on the road independently in different tasks and different settings – and have a strong, appreciative team at your side.
  • a challenging care and care situation and appreciates the varied work.
  • work in a field where you can care for customers according to their needs and with the involvement of their resources and visibly improve their quality of life and that of their loved ones.

You bring

  • have a qualification as a nurse (HF/FH) and ideally have professional experience in the field of Spitex.
  • a heart for good cooperation in the team and actively contributes to the achievement of our operational goals.
  • Enjoy the care process and take responsibility or support according to your qualifications.
  • as a re-entry: a willingness to take a refresher course to update your expertise.
  • the knowledge, experience and suitability to design and take responsibility for care processes, support employees and take on day-to-day responsibility and pikett service.
  • have the ability to perform administrative tasks efficiently and conscientiously and have a category B driving licence.

We offer you

  • an exciting field of work in a dynamic organization and a prudent familiarization.
  • Supporting a strong, motivated and stable team that is already looking forward to you today.
  • progressive employment conditions: fair wages, above-average social benefits, voluntary care allowances as a supplement to the mandatory family allowance, min. 5 weeks vacation and the possibility of unpaid vacation.
  • the opportunity to develop professionally and personally. We support you through various funded training offers (e.g. as a wound expert or in the field of palliative care, dementia care).
  • various extras such as discounted mobile subscriptions at Salt, fleet discount when buying a new car and discounts at various providers.
  • as an alternative, the possibility of working only in late service from 17.00 to 23.00.

Spitex Grauholz is an efficiently managed and well-structured organization that attaches great importance to mutual appreciation, helpfulness, a positive team spirit and cooperation on an equal footing. Our attractive working conditions, as well as information on the wide range of Spitex Grauholz can be found on our homepage.
