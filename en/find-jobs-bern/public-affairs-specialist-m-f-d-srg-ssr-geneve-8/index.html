Full description

The policy defines the framework conditions of the SSR. Dialogue with political interest groups and mutual understanding are therefore paramount. The Public Affairs Department of the SSR is responsible for the strategic management of decision-making processes at the crossroads of political, economic and societal affairs

  • It maintains permanent exchanges with political interest groups and accompanies legislative processes at the Federal Palace.

Your Mission

As a Public Affairs Specialist, you independently manage political issues, bring your political expertise to expert groups, and follow Parliament's work from end to end.

  • Responsibility for public affairs projects and representation of the Public Affairs Department in internal project and working groups
  • Research and analysis; development of public affairs scenarios, concepts and strategies (including positions and measures)
  • Development of explanatory documents, political arguments, presentations and texts for various internal and external channels
  • Monitoring of political and regulatory developments; strategic assessment of their relevance to SSR; regular reports to management
  • Support in the management of political stakeholders with, if necessary, contact and representation of interests

Your profile

  • Proven experience of several years in the public affairs of a company, association or agency
  • In-depth knowledge of political processes and institutions; good command of public affairs tools and methods
  • Political or scientific-media baggage
  • Increased understanding of media policy and public service issues
  • Mastery of German and very good knowledge of French; knowledge of other national languages would be an asset
  • Strong interest and understanding in the challenges of public service media; solution-oriented approach

What we offer you

  • You contribute to the societal added value of the public media service
  • You deal with various topics and exciting challenges in a dynamic environment and have the opportunity to work in a multilingual and multicultural context
  • You have great autonomy in your field of activity and have the opportunity to contribute to the transformation of the company
  • Your workplace is Bern; telework possible

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment, in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the universe of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the heart of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy that promotes novelty and quality.
  • Living digital: In our society, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content fits into the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
