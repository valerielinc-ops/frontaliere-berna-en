About Join the House of Fusalp

Founded in Annecy in 1952 by two master tailors, Fusalp has spent over 70 years redefining skiwear by combining technical innovation, exceptional craftsmanship and timeless elegance.

Today, Fusalp brings The Art of the Mountain to life across the world's most iconic mountain destinations and international cities. Our collections move seamlessly from the slopes to the city, guided by one conviction: performance and elegance should never be separated.

This role is designed for an experienced premium retail professional who can become operational quickly, support the Store Manager and bring confidence, structure and commercial expertise during the winter peak.

Job Description Lead by example on the sales floor and deliver an exceptional client experience.

  • Drive sales, clienteling and boutique KPIs, turning performance indicators into concrete actions.
  • Coach and support Sales Associates through daily feedback and hands-on guidance.
  • Support the Store Manager in organising daily activity and maintaining strong operational standards.
  • Take ownership of the boutique when required, including opening and closing procedures.
  • Ensure Fusalp's visual merchandising and presentation standards are consistently maintained.
  • Work alongside the team on stock, deliveries, replenishment, inventories and daily operations

Preferred Experience What will help you succeed

Here, experience matters. We are looking for someone who can bring proven premium retail expertise and support the team from day one.

This is a hands-on leadership role. You will lead from the floor, stay close to clients and the team, and step into operations whenever needed. During peak periods, the boutique requires calm, decisive and reliable leadership.

Must-have

  • Solid experience in premium or luxury retail.
  • Previous responsibility for a team, sales floor or boutique operations.
  • Strong understanding of clienteling, sales targets and retail KPIs.
  • The ability to coach others and raise team performance.
  • Confidence to make decisions and take ownership when the Store Manager is absent.
  • English is required.
  • Availability to work a flexible rota, including weekends, public holidays and peak trading periods.

Nice-to-have

  • German, French or any additional language.
  • Experience in luxury fashion, ski, outdoor or performance apparel.
  • Previous Assistant Store Manager or Supervisor experience.

What you’ll love working here

  • Seasonal 100% contract from December 2026 to 15 April 2027.
  • CHF 5,000 per month + bonus. The salary is flexible and may be adjusted based on the candidate’s profile, qualifications, and experience.
  • Leadership, product and client-experience development.
  • The opportunity to take a key role in one of Fusalp’s strategic Alpine boutiques.

Recruitment Process If your profile matches the role, you will first meet our HR team before continuing the conversation with the boutique and retail management teams.
