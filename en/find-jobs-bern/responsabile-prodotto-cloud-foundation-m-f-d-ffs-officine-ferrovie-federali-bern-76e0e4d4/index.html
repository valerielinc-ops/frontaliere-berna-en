Product Architect (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Product Architect (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. You will help shape the digital basis for traction power production and help ensure that trains are reliably supplied with energy. In the agile WATT team, you work closely with product owners and BizDevOps engineers and further develop solutions for power generation, energy trading and grid supply. You can expect tasks related to architecture, integration, cloud and modernization in a complex system landscape with a direct impact on operations. You stay close to the department, can deepen your knowledge of energy production on site if necessary and benefit from a learning-oriented team culture with exchange at eye level. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • As a Product Architect, you are responsible for the lifecycle management and stable operation of energy-specific applications such as Volue DeltaXE, Volue Flex, KYOS C/ETRM, ZRM Siloveda, NdD Tool, LMLS and BIENE-BM, so that production, trading and supply function smoothly.
  • You record requirements in a structured manner and design viable software and integration architectures in close coordination with the higher-level architecture.
  • You shape continuous development through configuration, modernization and migration of legacy systems to cloud and hybrid architectures.
  • You ensure quality through a coherent continuous delivery pipeline, clear development guidelines, test automation and code quality as well as consistent compliance with security and data protection.
  • In Agile Release Train projects, you accompany the path from analysis to implementation, initiate enablers in your area of ​​responsibility and take responsibility for architecture, business analysis, testing and documentation.
  • You advise departments and teams on processes, web technologies and interfaces, support the introduction of new IT applications, participate in tenders and digital governance and take part in SAFe ceremonies. This is what you bring with you.
  • You enjoy working in a team, communicate transparently, remain calm even in intensive phases and represent your professional perspective clearly and constructively.
  • You have several years of experience in application management and can confidently use ITIL processes in everyday life.
  • You are familiar with cloud and hybrid architectures, modern integration patterns as well as migration and modernization of legacy systems and you design continuous delivery with test automation, code quality as well as security and data protection.
  • You know agile methods such as Scrum, Kanban and SAFe and are interested in energy production and the basics of electrical engineering.
  • You have completed higher education in computer science or business informatics and have in-depth experience in software architecture and development.
  • You have mastered German and English at level C1 and are confident in communicating with different professional and technical stakeholders.
