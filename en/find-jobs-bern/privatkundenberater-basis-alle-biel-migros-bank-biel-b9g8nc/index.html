Full description

With us you can get involved and help shape the customer service of the future and the digitalization of banking. We are looking for you: A personality who thinks ahead. Someone with whom we can inspire customers together as a team. Mansioni

  • Our customers, whom you delight with a smile and your positive charisma as soon as they enter our branch.
  • With a lot of humanity and bilingually (German/French), you approach our customers with holistic, basic advice.
  • Your team, because together with them you will identify existing customer potential and further develop customer relationships.
  • Existing customers who you can advise and inspire about Migros Bank over the phone with your winning and friendly manner.
  • Requisiti
  • 1-3 years: Private customer advice
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): Bank or insurance company
  • German (business fluent) French (very good knowledge): German or French as a native language with very good knowledge of the other language.
  • Initial experience in advising private customers as well as the necessary specialist knowledge in the areas of saving, financing, investing with funds and pension provision so that you can advise our customers professionally.
  • A flair and joy in recognizing customer needs, so that it is easy for you to get customers excited about the right financial solutions.
  • A motivated, dynamic personality who enjoys developing further so that we can shape your career at Migros Bank together.
  • A responsible, communicative and resilient personality who doesn't get upset easily, because in this way you not only win over our customers, but also your team.
  • You are flexible and can ideally work on Mondays, Wednesday mornings, Thursdays and Fridays in order to optimally cover our opening times at the Biel branch.
  • Cosa offriamo
  • Targeted support: Continuous, individual exchange for professional development
  • Freedom to shape and make decisions: Optimal opportunities to contribute ideas and know-how
  • You culture: Lived team spirit with transparent communication
  • Central work location: Optimally accessible, centrally located workplace
  • Migros Bank: Accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential conditions
  • Cumulus points: Up to 5 additional points per franc of purchase value
  • Mobile phone subscription: Discounted subscriptions and registration also for family and friends
  • Leisure and cultural offers: M-Community discounts on concerts, trips, and much more.
