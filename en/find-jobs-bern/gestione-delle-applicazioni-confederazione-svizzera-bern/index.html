Put to the Point

The Swiss Accreditation Body (SAS) is the national accreditation authority of Switzerland. From product safety tests to energy management systems to material analyses in the laboratory - when it is checked whether products, systems or services comply with certain standards and legal requirements, SAS also takes a close look. In this way, we create trust in the services of others and help to make the everyday life of many people safer and to reduce technical barriers to trade. The newly founded ASRA division in March consists of a team of nine employees. Together, the team ensures that SAS employees (around 50 people) are equipped with the appropriate resources in the areas of IT, finance, procurement and administration in order to perform their core tasks in the best possible and contemporary way. Digital tools, in particular the FASAS specialist application developed especially for SAS, already play a central role, which will continue to grow in the coming months and years.
