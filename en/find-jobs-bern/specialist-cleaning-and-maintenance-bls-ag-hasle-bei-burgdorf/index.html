Full description

The first impression counts. When our passengers feel comfortable and safe, you have had a hand in it. In fact, you ensure that the stations and stops make a really good impression. Satisfied travellers – that is what matters to you.

What awaits you

  • Cleaning stations and stops
  • Proper disposal of waste
  • Carrying out minor repairs
  • Maintaining points and signalling systems
  • Maintaining ticket machines
  • Operating cleaning machines

What you bring

  • Vocational training as a building cleaner or facilities maintenance specialist
  • Professional experience in cleaning technology
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good physical fitness, including hearing and eyesight
  • German language skills at least at B1 level
  • Willingness to work irregular hours and weekends

Your benefits – what you can look forward to

  • We support the most flexible planning possible for your routes and shifts
  • Get a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare travelcard.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are additional allowances for work at night and on weekends.
  • You have the option of purchasing an additional five to ten vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external continuing education and an exciting internal learning and course program are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. More than that: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company, and we transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us in all this? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
