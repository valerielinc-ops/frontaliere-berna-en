Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking for meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

As a deputy, you support the team management in day-to-day operations and take over the personnel and organizational leadership of the team in their absence. You help to ensure a smooth process, support in personnel planning and operational coordination.

In this versatile hub function, you work actively in the secretariat and on the scheduling and coordinate the day-to-day business in Thun and in practice in Spiez together with the team, including the carrying out of the laboratory analyses. At the disposition you take over the reception of the patients: inside, the telephone triage as well as the termination of consultation hours, therapies and internal and external examinations. In addition, you work in the secretariat, write reports from Diktaphon, prepare the tumor board and do other administrative work.

Entry as of 1 November 2026 or by arrangement.
