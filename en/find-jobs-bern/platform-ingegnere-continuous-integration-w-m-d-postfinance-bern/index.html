Full description

In the dynamic team, you play a key role and support internal software developers in their projects. You design central services and tools for software development and delivery – from conception to development to operation. With your proactive nature and your team spirit, you ensure that our processes remain efficient and sustainable. So you make an important contribution to the further development of PostFinance.

You can do that.

  • You play a key role in the further development and optimization of Continuous Integration tools / platforms / assistance (e.g. GitLab, SonarQube, Artifactory) for all PostFinance developers
  • You work across teams and continuously on the improvement of the developer experience and implement demanding automations and updates with zero downtime: rollout, upgrade, bugkeeping, self-healing and integration
  • You develop innovative solutions to provide users with transparency within the platform and tools so that they can perform their tasks independently and efficiently.
  • You actively contribute to the further development of the on-demand self-service, which automatically provides all necessary resources on our platforms (e.g. tools, repositories, permissions)
  • You build our observability and make sure that problems become visible before they escalate
  • You support internal customers in using our platforms and help them find suitable solutions

You bring that with you

  • Enthusiasm for continuous problem solving, learning and knowledge sharing as well as for modern concepts of automated integration and software delivery
  • Enjoy looking after internal customers and working with them to develop solutions
  • Experience in the operation of business-critical platforms with high availability requirements
  • Very good knowledge of Linux, Shell, Observability, Infrastructure-as-Code and Docker
  • In-depth experience in software development with languages such as Go, React, TypeScript or Java
  • Good knowledge of at least two of the following tools and willingness to familiarize yourself with others: Grafana, Prometheus, Helm, Ansible and Git
  • Excellent German and English skills
