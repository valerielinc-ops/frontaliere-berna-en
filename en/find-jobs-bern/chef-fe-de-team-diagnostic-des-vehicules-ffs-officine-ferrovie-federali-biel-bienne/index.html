Team Leader Vehicle Diagnostics 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Make Switzerland move with us. Vehicle Diagnostics Team Leader Biel/Bienne / Work Smart, 01.12.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. As Team Leader Vehicle Diagnostics, you directly contribute to safe, reliable and customer-oriented mobility in Switzerland. With your team of six diagnosticians, you ensure that technical faults in passenger traffic rolling stock are quickly identified, analyzed and resolved in a sustainable manner. Interventions are carried out mobile, at the station and in your assigned sector. You combine technical know-how with modern driving and thus create the necessary conditions for high availability of our fleet. In this varied role, you take responsibility for employees, processes and technical developments. You work closely with different specialist areas and actively contribute to shaping the future of vehicle diagnostics. Our team is distinguished by a high level of technical skills, mutual support and a pragmatic, solution-oriented approach. This position offers development and further training opportunities in the areas of driving and technology. Employee Benefits: Fringe Benefits

What you can make move.

  • As a team leader, you ensure efficient vehicle diagnostics at the Biel site and directly contribute to the safe availability of vehicles and systems in strict compliance with safety regulations.
  • Supervise and develop a team of six diagnosticians and organize mobile interventions at the station and in the field.
  • Plan and manage interventions as well as the team's working time to ensure staff availability on site.
  • In the field, take charge of diagnostic and troubleshooting interventions and actively support your team when the situation requires it.
  • Ensure the proper use and proper functioning of office and site infrastructure.
  • In close collaboration with the Helpdesk, maintenance, operations and other partners, develop sustainable solutions, analyze faults and indicators and support digitalization projects to improve security, availability and reliability. What you bring.
  • With driving experience in a technical environment, you have the potential and motivation to progress in a targeted manner towards a driving role.
  • You know how to explain complex technical contexts in an understandable manner and you make well-founded decisions that you justify clearly and coherently.
  • Experience in vehicle diagnostics, maintenance or technical support for vehicles is a valued asset and you will value it in your practice.
  • Willing to work punctually during staggered hours including nights and weekends, you demonstrate operational reliability.
  • Holder of completed initial technical training, for example as a mechatronics engineer, automation engineer, electrician or comparable profession, you work with confidence in the field.
  • You have very good knowledge of German or French at minimum C1 level and you communicate with ease in the other language at B2 level.
