Role overview

For our Biel General Medical Office we are looking for one or two F/D Medical Assistants (1x100% or 2x50%-60%) This position requires versatility because you will be active in our General Medical Office and our Wald-in-Clinic which is an emergency medical office with consultations with and/or without appointments.

Main responsibilities

  • Your main responsibilities will be as follows:
  • Provide patient care, guidance and support with professionalism and kindness.
  • Manages the hotline, appointment and message processing.
  • Perform routine care such as blood tests, injections, infusions and assist doctors in specific medical actions.
  • Perform the usual diagnostic tests: laboratory, ECG, conventional radiology, 24 hour blood pressure measurement, sleep apnea screening, spirometry, etc.
  • Provide administrative follow-up of patient files and the various administrative tasks related to the proper functioning of the firm.
  • Actively participate in the quality of care and the smooth running of daily medical activities.
  • Holder of a Medical Assistant CFC.
  • Experience in medical practice, medical centre or outpatient emergency service, an asset.
  • Excellent relational skills and a developed sense of hospitality.
