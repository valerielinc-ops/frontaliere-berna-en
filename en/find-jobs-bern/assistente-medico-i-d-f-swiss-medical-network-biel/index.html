Main responsibilities

  • Your main responsibilities will be:
  • Provide patient reception, guidance and support with professionalism and kindness.
  • Manage hotline, appointment making and message processing.
  • Perform routine care such as blood taking, injections, infusions and assist physicians in specific medical actions.
  • Perform the usual diagnostic tests: laboratory, ECG, conventional radiology, 24-hour blood pressure measurement, sleep apnea screening, spirometry, etc.
  • Ensure administrative follow-up of patient files and various administrative tasks related to the proper functioning of the firm.
  • Actively participate in the quality of care and the smooth running of daily medical activities.
  • Holder of a Medical Assistant CFC.
  • Experience in medical practice, medical center or outpatient emergency service, an asset.
  • Excellent relational skills and developed sense of hospitality.
