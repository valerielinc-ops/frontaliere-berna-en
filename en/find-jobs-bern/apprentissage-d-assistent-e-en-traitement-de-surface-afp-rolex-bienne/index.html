Full description

Motivated by an assistant apprenticeship in surface treatment? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The surface treatment assistant is involved in the preparation and application of coatings on various materials. Its role is to perform preparation tasks such as degreasing, sanding and polishing, and to apply coatings in accordance with established quality standards. Quality control tests are also carried out to ensure that treated surfaces meet the required specifications.

This learning is suitable for young people who are passionate about technological environments and like manual, accurate and neat work.

Profile

  • Rigor and accuracy
  • Logical and methodical spirit
