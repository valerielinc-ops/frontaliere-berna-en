Full description

Your design space

  • You will analyze and develop a portfolio of flexibility products for BKW’s distribution network and work closely with network operations, asset management, tariffs and other interdisciplinary specialist areas.
  • You take over the product management and are responsible for the entire end-to-end process – from conception to implementation to implementation – including customer consulting, contract drafting and performance measurement.
  • Electricity storage plays a key role: You build up comprehensive expertise along the entire value chain – from grid connection to grid use and billing
  • You clarify regulatory issues, ensure compliance with legal requirements and pass on your knowledge in internal training
  • With analyses, enthusiasm and new ideas, you will develop sustainable solutions for BKW and make an important contribution to the success of the energy transition.

You bring that with you

  • You have a university degree in economics or business administration with a quantitative focus, love data, networked thinking and the analysis of incentive structures
  • Ideally, you already have professional experience in the electricity or energy industry – for example in tariff calculation, asset management or at a network operator.
  • Technical, economic and regulatory issues inspire you, interdisciplinary cooperation gives you pleasure and you approach your tasks in a structured, analytical and high-quality way.
  • Complex relationships can be brought to the point in an understandable way and provide convincing advice to different stakeholders up to management.
  • You communicate clearly and appropriately in German. Very good knowledge of French and English is beneficial
