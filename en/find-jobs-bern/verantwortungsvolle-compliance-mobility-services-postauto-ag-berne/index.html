Full description

Do you want to shape compliance where mobility, public responsibility and entrepreneurial capacity meet? In this role, you ensure that within the Mobility Services - of which CarPostal is also part - we identified risks early enough, solidly founded our decisions and remained agile. Apply now!

Make a difference

  • You are responsible for the compliance management system for the unit of the Mobility Services Group, in coordination with the Group Compliance.
  • You address the central themes of compliance that are essential to the legitimacy and sustainability of our mobility offers. These include the fight against money laundering and corruption, public procurement law, cartel law and preservation and archiving.
  • For topics such as the law of subsidies, the regime of concessions and the law on working hours, you work closely with the relevant specialists.
  • You are responsible for the compliance documentation for the Mobility Services Unit as well as the requirements, compliance reports, compliance checks and measures.
  • You assess regulatory and internal requirements, analyze risks, and support internal investigations.

What you bring

  • You have several years of experience in compliance, preferably in a politically regulated or exposed environment.
  • You know how to structure regulatory and internal requirements so as to draw comprehensible decision-making bases, clear responsibilities and achievable measures.
  • You know the risks of compliance in complex organisations and ideally have experience in relevant regulatory areas, including public transport.
  • You assume your responsibilities, set clear priorities and move the subjects forward autonomously and pragmatically.
  • You also have a very good command of a national language and good knowledge of another national language.

In order to promote linguistic and gender diversity, we are particularly pleased to receive applications from women or persons speaking French or Italian.
