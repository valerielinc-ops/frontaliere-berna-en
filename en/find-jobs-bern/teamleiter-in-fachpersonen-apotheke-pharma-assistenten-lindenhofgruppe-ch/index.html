Full description

Tasks

  • You lead a motivated team of 17 pharmaceutical assistants in all personnel and professional topics and create an appreciative and positive working atmosphere in which everyone can contribute and develop.
  • You have the overall overview and coordinate the team in hectic situations.
  • You ensure a good balance between the needs of the team and the requirements of the operation.
  • You bring in ideas, question existing processes and actively design improvements.
  • You work closely with nursing, doctors and other specialist areas and are an important contact person within the hospital.
  • You participate in projects, quality initiatives and the further development of our hospital pharmacy.

Requirements

  • You bring a completed training as a specialist Pharmacy EFZ or Pharmaceutical Assistant EFZ and have several years of professional experience in a pharmacy, ideally in the hospital environment.
  • Leadership experience is a big plus – it is important to us that you can inspire, promote and take people with you.
  • You enjoy working with people and taking responsibility.
  • Organizational talent, initiative and a solution-oriented way of thinking are central characteristics of you.
  • You are a positive, open personality with good communication skills and are interested in developing processes and helping to shape change.
  • You have a flair for IT systems (MS Office, KISIM etc.) and are open to the digitalization of processes.
