Full description

Portrait

The Bümpliz/Höhe site is one of four school sites in the Bümpliz school district in the west of the city of Bern. 800 children and young people are taught by around 100 teachers in three different school buildings.

The Höhe school building has 6 classes (grades 1–4) and four all-day school classes (foundation stage and Cycle 2).

Tasks

  • Teaching as a part-time teacher in a foundation stage class at Bümpliz all-day school
  • 11.2 lessons of teaching (D, Mu, BG, NMG) + 2 lessons of DaZ at BSa

Working days

  • Monday morning 4.4 lessons.
  • Tuesday morning 2.4 lessons.
  • Friday morning 4.4 lessons.
  • 2 lessons German as a second language (integrated) possible either on Tuesday afternoon or Thursday morning

Requirements

  • Teaching qualification for Cycle 1
  • Teamwork
  • Enjoyment of working with children from a wide range of cultures

We offer

  • fixed-term substitute employment
  • support from specialist teachers
  • spacious school facilities
  • a very dedicated and helpful team and faculty that can provide support without complications
  • A workplace close to the city with good public transport connections (tram stop in front of the school building).
