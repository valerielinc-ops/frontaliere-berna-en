Put to the Point

We design and operate SAP applications that enable livelihood-securing services for people in demanding living situations – reliable, secure and sustainable. SECO’s Labour Directorate is the Federal Government’s competence centre for labour market policy and unemployment insurance. The Payout and Financial Applications team operates and develops the IT applications for unemployment insurance claims verification, calculation and payout, supporting approximately 2,500 users of public and private unemployment funds. These applications were renewed with the ICT key project ASALfutur (SAP S/4 HANA CM with Case Management, Social Protection, BRF+ as well as PSCD, SD, FI, CO, EC-CS and SDP by Opentext). To strengthen and build our “CCSAP”, we are looking for committed and solution-oriented personalities. You work in a committed, interdisciplinary team of specialists and IT specialists who share responsibility and jointly develop and operate solutions.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
