Full description

Shape the future. Yours and ours.

Would you like to help people lead a self-determined life? And do you want to be given the creative scope to reconcile your professional and personal ambitions? Then we can offer you an exciting prospect: as part of the Swiss Life sales force, you can organize your working day independently. You will reach your targets as you see fit and with the support of a practised team.

Your area of responsibility

  • As part of a succession scheme takeover of a portfolio in the area of corporate and private customers
  • Support, maintenance and expansion of existing customer relationships in the segment 45+ with focus on pension planning, as well as in the corporate customer sector with focus on SMEs
  • Development and sale of individual, tailor-made solutions in the areas of provision, asset accumulation and asset distribution, as well as in the corporate customer sector - - Acquisition of new customers through systematic market development
  • Representation of our company in an attractive sales area
  • Mediation of business to our cooperation partners

Their strengths

  • Strong sales and consulting flair
  • Completed industry audit (insurance intermediary/in VBV, insurance and pension advisor/in IAF) or higher
  • Demonstrate many years of sales experience in the insurance and financial sector
  • High commitment, goal-oriented and performance-oriented working methods
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, resilient and sociable

Agencies near you

Your benefits We help our employees to shape their professional and personal development to their best advantage and throughout all phases of their life. Our attractive benefits, such as Cicero-certified introductory training and first-class continuing education, contribute to this. You will also receive above-average social benefits, including pension fund membership and non-contributory accident and short-term disability benefit (UVG/LTG) insurance, plus generous premium contributions to 3rd pillar products.

Your contact

If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact Human Resources Sales Force or the General Agent at your chosen General Agency. Only applications received via our application tool will be considered.
