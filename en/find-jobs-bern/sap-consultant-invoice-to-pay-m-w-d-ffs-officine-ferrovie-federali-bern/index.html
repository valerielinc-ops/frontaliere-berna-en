SAP Consultant Invoice Management (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. SAP Consultant Invoice Management (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As Digital Solution Finance (DSO FIS), we are responsible for digital solutions along the entire value chains of SBB. We develop and operate all IT applications that SBB requires to map and further develop its financial and logistical processes. In the Agile Release Train “Source-to-pay” you work in an agile, product- and process-oriented team that works together according to the SAFe methodology. In the area of accounts payable, or the Invoice-to-pay (I2P) process, you are responsible for the technical SAP ERP implementations and ensure the operation and further development. As part of the ongoing SAP S/4HANA conversion, you will find suitable solutions for the requirements of the departments and ensure their implementation. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As part of an agile team, you primarily support the further development of the core processes for company-wide invoice processing.
  • You will develop and evaluate technical solutions for the implementation of the requirements of the specialist areas.
  • Your area of activity includes system configuration, specification and documentation of the solution.
  • As part of the DevOps organization, you ensure the smooth operation of our multi-system landscape and support our support teams in the analysis and correction of system errors (3rd-level support).
  • You advise the departments as a competent partner and thus jointly design the source-to-pay system landscape in the area of invoice-to-pay.

You bring that with you.

  • You have in-depth knowledge in the design of system solutions and the technical implementation of creditor workflows (ideally in SAP Vendor Invoice Management by Opentext).
  • When it comes to customizing and implementing SAP interfaces, you’ll be familiar with them and, at best, have experience in debugging.
  • You are characterized by an independent, solution-oriented and responsible way of working as well as by strong analytical and conceptual skills.
  • With addressee-friendly communication skills, you are confident and master German fluently in word and writing (C1) and English at a good level (B2).
  • You have completed a higher technical education in computer science or business informatics (FH, ETH, Uni) or an equivalent further education.
