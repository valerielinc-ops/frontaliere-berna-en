Full description

Already have ideas for what comes after you finish school? Start your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Schweiz. Our stores are at the heart of everything we do. As a retail apprentice (specializing in creating shopping experiences), whether EBA or EFZ, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/end EBA: August 1, 2027 - July 31, 2029 Start/end EFZ: August 1, 2027 - July 31, 2030 Your tasks

  • Operating cash registers and preparing cash reports
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking goods and presenting merchandise
  • Checking incoming goods in the warehouse and supporting your team with inventory counts
  • Supporting the planning and implementation of promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining stock requirements and ordering goods in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)
  • Taking on your first shift-leadership responsibilities in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)

Your profile

  • An average to very good secondary school leaving certificate
  • A1 level in a foreign language for EBA and at least A2 for EFZ
  • Enjoyment of interacting with people
  • Ability to work independently and grasp things quickly
  • Teamwork skills, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and commitment
  • Physical stamina
  • Very good German language skills

An apprenticeship where many paths are open to you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship and if you are personally suited, we will develop you for a management position in the store. In addition, you benefit from the Lidl GAV and many attractive benefits.
