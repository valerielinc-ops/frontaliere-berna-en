Introduction of society

OMEGA is a prestigious watch brand worldwide known for its quality, precision and innovation requirements for over 170 years. Our watches are among the most beautiful in the world and are characterized by their state-of-the-art technology and timeless style.

Over the course of our long history, we have broken records of precision, redefined sporting timing and maintained our position at the cutting edge of Haute Horlogerie.

Description of post

Ensure design, development, maintenance and evolution of Anaplan models supporting Supply Chain planning processes.

You will guarantee and actively participate in the definition, design, development, testing and deployment of Anaplan application models to support business processes and business needs.

While guaranteeing users access (authorization management & roles), support (training & documentation) and optimal application operation (maintenance & support of features and data), you will be the driving force for proposing recommendations and developments of the solution and guaranteeing their implementation in accordance with the Roadmap of the defined application.

As a central player, you ensure the consistency and performance of solutions. You also contribute to the continuous improvement of Anaplan models, integrating best practices and continuously optimizing their performance to meet business needs.

  • Profile

Higher level, engineer or deemed equivalent (3 years experience).

You have a Minimum Level 3 Solution Architect Anaplan or Model Builder certification, complemented by significant experience in model design and architecture. You benefit from very good experience in the planning processes of Supply Chain.

A shift towards accompanying projects to improve our Delivery Supply processes is possible.

Very collaborative, agile, structured, you are able to evolve in a multidisciplinary environment requiring a strong long-term commitment. You also have a strong capacity for critical thinking, problem solving, and you stand out for your analytical and conceptual skills.
