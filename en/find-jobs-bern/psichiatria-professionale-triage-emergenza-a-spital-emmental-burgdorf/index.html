Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital offers demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and takes into account their own resources. In our manageable company, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. We regard individuality as enrichment. We offer jobs at the two locations Burgdorf and Langnau. We are a successful training facility of the BZ Pflege.

The Central Triage and the Emergency form the entrance gate to our psychiatric services. Here we accompany people in mental crises, advise relatives and specialists and provide a well-founded assessment for suitable and timely further treatment. Anyone who works in this area takes on a responsible and varied task at a central interface of psychiatric care. Immediately or by agreement we are looking for you as

Task area You bring calm and structure into dynamic situations, prioritize safely and act in a technically sound manner. In exchange with doctors, nurses, social services and partners in the network, you make first contacts binding and appreciative, even if it has to be done quickly.

  • Telephone and personal initial contacts as well as advice to affected persons, relatives and specialists
  • Independent implementation of initial examinations, emergency discussions and crisis interventions – if necessary also visiting
  • Triage into the offers of the psychiatric service and organization of a needs-based and sustainable further treatment
  • Active networking and cooperation with referees, authorities and other partners of the psychosocial care system in the Emmental

Profile

  • Completed master's degree in psychology or completed training as a nurse psychiatry HF/FH
  • At least one year of professional experience in psychiatry, preferably in stationary setting
  • High independence and the ability to safely prioritize in complex and dynamic situations and make responsible decisions
  • High ability to reflect, combined with the willingness to continuously develop one’s own actions and to participate constructively in the interprofessional exchange
  • Team-oriented personality with pleasure in interprofessional cooperation and an appreciative approach to patients, relatives and network partners
  • Good verbal and written communication skills as well as a safe presence in demanding conversation situations
  • Recovery-oriented attitude and interest in networked, systemic cooperation
