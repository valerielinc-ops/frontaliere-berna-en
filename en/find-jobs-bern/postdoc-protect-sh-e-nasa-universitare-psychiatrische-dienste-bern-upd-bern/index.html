Teaching and Research Directorate

The advertised position includes participation in two major research projects on the overarching topic of "Self-Harm and New Media". Part of the work is carried out within the framework of PROTECT-SH, a collaborative project with partners in seven countries funded by Horizon Europe. Over four years, PROTECT-SH investigates in a multicentre longitudinal cohort how the use of social media promotes or protects against self-harming and suicidal behavior in adolescents and young adults. Based on this, a digital intervention is developed and evaluated in two large randomized-controlled trials (prevention and clinical intervention). You take on a coordinating role in the network and support from the ethics and study documents to the implementation and evaluation of the two studies. The second project, NASA, involves the implementation and scientific evaluation of a suicide prevention app within our clinic, also in randomized-controlled design. In addition, it is possible to participate in other internal projects of our working group in the field of self-harm and suicidality as well as to evaluate and publish already existing data sets. The position is suitable for candidates in the early postdoc phase who want to actively shape a large international research project. We work closely together in a team and are interdisciplinary and internationally networked. Further information about our research department The position is initially limited to 2 years, with an option of extension within the project period and also beyond if it is particularly suitable. Remuneration is based on the guidelines of the University of Bern.

  • You collect quantitative data, write scientific publications and present results at national and international congresses

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
