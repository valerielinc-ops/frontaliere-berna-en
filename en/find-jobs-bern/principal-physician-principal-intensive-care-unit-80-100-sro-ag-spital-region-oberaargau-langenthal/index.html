Main responsibilities

  • They are jointly responsible for the medical care of the interdisciplinary patient goods of our 6 bed intensive care unit.
  • They participate in the intensive medical background service.
  • The consiliar assessments of critically ill patients in the normal wards as well as the consiliar performance of invasive activities (electroconversions, insertion of temporary pacemakers and central venous catheters, etc.) in close cooperation with other disciplines in our company, complement your work in the intensive care unit.
  • Participation in the training of assistant doctors and nurses round off their area of responsibility. Requirements
  • They have the specialist titles for intensive care medicine and anesthesia or internal medicine.
  • You are a resilient, flexible and communicative personality with a high level of social competence.
  • You are also ready to work on weekends.
  • The background services at night are carried out in accordance with the guidelines of the SGI.
  • In your function, you pass on your knowledge and skills with interest to our assistant doctors.
