Move Switzerland with us. Responsible for basic material data management (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.10.2026 or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

In this key function, you are responsible for basic material data management and transport management within the infrastructure. You lead an interdisciplinary team and actively contribute to digitization and the evolution of logistics processes. You work closely with internal and external partners and contribute decisively to the quality and transparency of logistics processes. What you can move.

  • Lead and develop an interdisciplinary team of six people in the fields of data management and transport management.
  • Ensure data governance and quality of logistics baseline data throughout the end-to-end process.
  • Develop and promote standardization of basic data processes, digital and automated solutions.
  • Ensure high quality data for planning, piloting, reporting and performance management.
  • Take responsibility for the management of road transport, including calls for tenders, monitoring performance and piloting of service providers.
  • Ensure compliance with legal requirements for external trade and customs and promote cross-cutting optimization between areas.

What you bring.

  • Ability to work in a structured and oriented way solutions, with ease in interdisciplinary environments.
  • Several-year leadership experience and solid knowledge in data management, logistics, supply chain or transportation management.
  • Experience in basic data governance, digitization and process optimization.
  • High affinity for data, analysis and digital solutions, combined with a thorough understanding of processes.
  • Excellent communication adapted to the interlocutors, with the ability to rally stakeholders to common solutions.
  • Higher education in higher specialized school, university or equivalent. Very good knowledge of German, knowledge of other national languages is appreciated.
