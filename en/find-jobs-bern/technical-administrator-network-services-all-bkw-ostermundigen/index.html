You want to take responsibility and support a reliable network operation with your work? In this versatile role, you coordinate technical services, ensure smooth processes and are an important contact person for internal and external customers.

Your field of activity

  • You coordinate services for customer facilities and ensure that orders are recorded and processed on time in the relevant systems
  • Offers, contract documents and invoices you create carefully and thus support a smooth customer support
  • Project manager supports you with administrative tasks in day-to-day business
  • Customer inquiries about contracts, invoices, services or the processing status you answer competently and solution-oriented
  • You maintain contract, project and customer data in various ICT systems and contribute to high data quality
  • Results of inspections, prepare reports and support the planning of further maintenance measures

You bring that with you

  • Contact with customers brings you pleasure and you act friendly and binding
  • You communicate stylishly in German and ideally bring your French skills with you.

  • A careful and structured working method as well as a high quality awareness distinguish you
  • Openness, honesty and collaborative cooperation are a matter of course for you and you enjoy working in a team
