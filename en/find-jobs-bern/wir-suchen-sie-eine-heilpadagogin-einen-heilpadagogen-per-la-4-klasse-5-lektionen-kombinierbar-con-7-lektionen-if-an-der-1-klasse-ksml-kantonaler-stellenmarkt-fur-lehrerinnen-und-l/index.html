Full description

Portrait The Madretsch school belongs to the school unit "Skip" – Madretsch. Children from different cultures visit our school in several locations. We place great emphasis on cooperation and live the motto "We care" actively in our everyday school life. This not only promotes the children's sense of responsibility for each other and their surroundings. Our school house is also very easy to reach – in just 5 minutes by bus or bicycle from the Biel station.

Tasks You accompany a student with status bVSA int. You create the funding planning for the child, are in close contact with the parents, communicate learning strategies especially in mathematics and German and work closely with the class team including another healing teacher. You accompany and advise the student as well as the teachers on handling ADHS.

Requirements We are looking for a dedicated curative teacher with strengths in the cooperation and interest in the social environment of the children. You are interested in the interests of the pupils, maintain an appreciative exchange with the parents and assume responsibility for your work. The class team works together on the social topics. They plan and discuss the differentiated support with the teachers. This makes profitably use of the common resources for the needs of the class.

You have a Master of Arts in Special Needs Education, are in education or bring a sound knowledge of ADHD and funding planning.

We offer a job at a lively school. You will be supported in your work by the college, other curative teachers, the school management and the school social work. Individual further education as well as interdisciplinary exchange as well as the support of the Center for Pedagogy belong with us and take place as urban events or we work together in the department of Heilpädagogik of the school unit.
