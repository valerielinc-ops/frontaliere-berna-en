Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

You know the procedures in the hospital environment, have an education in the IT sector or maybe even a medical background? Then become part of our team and actively shape the digitalization of our clinical processes. Our team is responsible for the support and further development of around 100 specialist applications. Together with the medical departments, we ensure the smooth operation and continuous optimization of our medical informatics core processes. For this area we are looking for an immediately or by agreement:

Digital Business Partner / Application Manager (80-100%)

You do

  • As a digital business partner, you are the central contact person for our medical departments in questions relating to IT and digitalization.
  • You evaluate needs, raise requirements and conduct business analyses
  • You plan and realize IT projects or coordinate sub-projects in close cooperation with internal and external partners
  • As an application manager, you are responsible for the further development and optimization of our specialist applications within the framework of product lifecycle management.
  • You ensure the 2nd-level support in the company and carry out well-founded problem analyses

This sets you apart

  • Ideally, you have professional experience in the hospital environment and are very familiar with its processes, requirements and challenges.
  • You have completed your studies (FH/HF) in (medical) computer science or a comparable education
  • You think networked, work structured and keep an overview even in complex situations
  • You communicate securely with different stakeholders and move confidently in the interface between department and IT
  • You work independently, solution-oriented and are a reliable team player
  • You speak German or French, with good knowledge of the other language
  • Readiness for pikettdienst (max. during 4 weeks per year)

You will benefit from this

  • Motivated, collegial team with an open corporate culture
  • Careful familiarization with your new work area
  • Exciting activity with scope for design in an innovative environment
  • Flexible and family-friendly working time models
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Cooperation in a bilingual and interdisciplinary environment
  • Progressive employment conditions in accordance with the collective employment contract
