Tasks

They accompany and teach a second class as deputy/deputy in the Oberdorf School House in Wimmis. Their lessons take place from Monday to Friday in the morning and on Tuesday afternoon. They teach German, NMG, mathematics, music, MGS, sports, BG and are class teachers. In 2 lessons MGS the lessons take place in the half class.

Requirements

They have a teaching diploma for the school and are a dutiful, communicative and resilient personality. You enjoy your profession and can respond to children and motivate them.

We offer

  • Pleasant working atmosphere in an established and innovative team.
  • Support from the team and school management.
  • Good cooperation with DAZ, speech therapy, IF, psychomotorik and school social work on site.
  • Good public transport connection and free parking.
