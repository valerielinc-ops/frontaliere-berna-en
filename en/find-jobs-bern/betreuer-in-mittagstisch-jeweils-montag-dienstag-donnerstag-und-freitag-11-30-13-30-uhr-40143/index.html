Full description

Portrait The Salome Brunner Foundation supports around 300 children and young people with language development disorders and learning difficulties. With our various schools, we see ourselves as an important part of the educational landscape in the canton of Bern.

The Wabern Speech Therapy School is a special elementary school for children and young people with a severe speech impediment and an intellectual disability and includes twenty-one classes from the elementary level to the vocational preparatory school year. In our day school and boarding school, children receive social-educational support. If lessons continue in the afternoon, all students eat in small groups in their lunch rooms. Here you can enjoy balanced, freshly prepared meals from our kitchen in a quiet atmosphere.
