Full description

Portrait Münsingen is a center community in the Aaretal, has two large school centers and an outdoor location. In the school centre Rebacker there are 6 kindergarten classes and 12 grades in the current school year at the lower level. We remain on the move and want to develop and strengthen the students, ourselves and the school. To this end, we also work together across centres and disciplines.

Tasks

From 12. In October 2026, we are looking for a part-time teacher for 12 lessons with 4.6 lessons on Thursday and Friday mornings and 14.6 lessons on Wednesday mornings. They teach independently and partially supported by an IF and DaZ teacher. The class has 14 students.

Requirements

You have completed the Bachelor in Arts in Primary Education or comparable training. A good classroom structure your lessons with fixed rituals and few rules. They know how to combine elements of free play with targeted pedagogical impulses and thus initiate learning processes. The SuS’ needs for self-determination and creativity are safeguarded. An appreciative exchange in the college, with the parents and the school management is important to you.

We offer

The classroom is in the Lärchenhuus in the 1. Stock with spacious premises and modern infrastructure. In the Parterre is a second kindergarten, with which they use the large outdoor area together.

We are very easy to reach with public transport. Parking (fee) is available.

This is a deputy situation (2nd quarter 26, extension possible).
