Full description

Portrait The community of Meikirch with around 3,000 inhabitants is located on the southern slope of the Frienisberg. At the three locations, almost 300 students from kindergarten to 9th grade are currently being taught in an integrative manner.

In the Gassacker schoolhouse there will be a 3rd/4th grade. Class, three 5th/6th graders classes and cycle 3. The school building is lively, well-connected and characterized by collegial cooperation. We are looking for one for cycle 2

Tasks

You teach at a 5th/6th grade school. Class German, English, NMG, mathematics and sports from Monday to Wednesday lunchtime.

The position is temporary from February 1st to July 31st, 2027. A partial takeover from January is possible to get to know the class and simplify the handover.

These include

  • Planning and implementing competency-oriented, motivating lessons
  • independent lesson preparation based on the annual planning
  • Close cooperation within the year team
  • Participation in the further development of the school location
  • Class management as part of the class teacher's office

Requirements

A committed personality who has the desire to create new things and take on responsibility.

  • Completed training as a primary teacher or in training
  • Enjoy mixed-age learning
  • Structure, clarity and pedagogical foresight
  • Teamwork, openness and humor
  • Positive attitude towards inclusive training

We offer

  • A supportive, collegial team with short decision-making processes
  • Appreciation and trust in your professionalism
  • A lively school location with a clear structure
  • Good accessibility and modern ICT infrastructure
  • enough parking spaces and a direct post bus connection from Bern to the school building
