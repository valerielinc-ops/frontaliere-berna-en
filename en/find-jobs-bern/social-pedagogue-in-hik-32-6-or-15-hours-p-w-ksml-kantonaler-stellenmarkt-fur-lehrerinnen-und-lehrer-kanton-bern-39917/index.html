Full description

Portrait Campus Muristalden (www.muristalden.ch) is a private, state-recognized and partially subsidized school based in Bern. The working group currently comprises around 500 children, adolescents and adults in the areas of elementary school, bridge offers, high school and further education.

The Healing Education Integration Classes (HIK) are a special primary school offer on behalf of the Canton of Bern for neurodivergent children and adolescents. We currently support 16 learners in two learning groups (3rd - 6th grade and 6th - 9th grade). In doing so, we work in an interdisciplinary team of teachers, curative pedagogues, social pedagogues and teaching assistants, who work together for an everyday school life in which all learners are perceived and individually supported.

Tasks

  • You support our learners at the age of about 12 - 15 years in class
  • You accompany and coach the learners in individual and group settings
  • You initiate and design preventive offers to promote

Social skills, participation and an appreciative school culture

  • You support teachers in social and challenging situations in

Everyday school life and develop sustainable solutions together

  • You work closely with teachers, parents and other professionals
  • You actively bring your experiences and ideas into the design of promotion and

Support offers

Requirements

  • Completed training as a social pedagogue*
  • Ideally, you have a CAS school social work or experience in a

comparable school setting

  • Experience with neurodivergent adolescents (e.g. ASD, ADHD)
  • High social skills, resilience and an independent working environment
  • Creativity, humor and joy of cooperative teamwork

We offer

  • A varied and meaningful activity
  • A dedicated and interdisciplinary team
  • Design room and the opportunity to use your strengths
  • An appreciative working environment with short decision paths
  • During 12 weeks per year no work commitment on site
  • Central workplace in the city of Bern with very good public transport connections

The work assignments take place at the 32.6% on Tuesday afternoon, Thursday full day and Friday morning.
