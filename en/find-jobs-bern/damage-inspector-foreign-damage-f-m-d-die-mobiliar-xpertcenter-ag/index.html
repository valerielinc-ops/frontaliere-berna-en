Full description

What awaits you

As part of XpertCenter AG, a company of the Mobiliar Group, you support and advise customers on damages in the areas of motor business, anti-abuse, regress management, case management and foreign damages.

This is what they do with us

With us, you will undergo a multi-year induction and training program with the aim of taking over the work as a damage inspector: in damage abroad after successful completion. Gradually, you deepen your knowledge of foreign damage material and develop professionally and personally into this demanding function.

Starting with medium-complex property damages in the motor vehicle liability industry, you will develop towards the independent processing of demanding personal and property damages in all liability sectors as well as claims from all property sectors such as theft, fire or water with international reference. As part of your work, you take over the final regulation of damage cases and, if necessary, visit them directly on site with our customers.

With increasing experience They have complex dossiers such as disability damages, deaths or highly complex company liability cases in purely French and participate independently in lawyer meetings. In addition, you ensure the enforcement of social and private insurance recourses abroad, support the claims services of the management and general agencies in technical matters and maintain intensive contact with our partners abroad.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic training in the insurance industry, completed federal law. Professional ID card of private insurance is a plus
  • Professional experience in the claims department and knowledge of civil liability law
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Team-oriented, flexible and negotiable personality
  • Mother tongue German or French with excellent knowledge of the other language in word and writing, other languages are advantageous

Working with us

The team Foreign Damage awaits you, consisting of clerks, specialists and claims inspectors. Together they live a committed, solution-oriented and communicative working mood. Your workload is negotiable from 80% and your place of work is in Bern, Zurich-Oerlikon or Nyon as desired. During the first 3 months, the place of work is fixed in Bern. Regular meetings between the damage inspectors will then take place in Bern.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

Our family allowances are based on the highest cantonal contributions of the cantons of Bern, Vaud and Zurich.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
