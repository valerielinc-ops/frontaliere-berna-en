Full description

This is what awaits you

In this position, you are part of the personal injury team and responsible for handling damage cases, in particular in the area of compulsory accident insurance (UVG) and day sickness allowance insurance.

This is what you do with us

The core of this position lies in the independent processing of demanding and complex claims of the compulsory accident insurance and the sickness allowance insurance. Depending on your language skills, you can take on both German and French cases.

As a competent specialist, you make a valuable contribution to claims management, make clarifications and negotiate with insured persons, employers, medical persons, other insurance institutions and lawyers. The corresponding correspondence is done in perfect language.

There is no one word for you. You are happy to support the claims services of our general agencies in technical issues.

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial apprenticeship, social security/insurance certificate of advantage
  • Professional experience in the field of UVG and medical day allowance insurance
  • Communicative and independent working methods
  • Strong social skills and negotiating skills
  • Fluent knowledge of German, good to ideally very good knowledge of French and or Italian are welcome

Working with us

A mixed, dynamic team awaits you, which can look back on a long-standing cooperation. Together you live a helpful, solution-oriented and communicative work atmosphere.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
