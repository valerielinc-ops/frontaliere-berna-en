Full description

Portrait Nestled in the gentle hilly landscape of the Gantrisch area is the Rüschegg school - a place where learning is personal, close to nature and shared. Our school combines rural tranquility with educational quality and a strong sense of togetherness. Around 141 children and young people attend our school from kindergarten to lower secondary school. They are taught by a committed, manageable team that values ​​cooperation, openness and mutual support.

We see school as a living space

Relationships are the focus, individuality is taken seriously and development is shared. Our integrative attitude shapes everyday life - all children learn together and are supported according to their needs. From school year 29/30 the school will be run as a pure primary school and the employment percentage will decrease.

Tasks

  • Pedagogical, personnel and organizational management of the school
  • Further development of school and teaching quality with a focus on AdL
  • Promoting a cooperative and appreciative team culture
  • Ensuring smooth school operations
  • Collaboration with authorities, parents and external specialist agencies
  • Design and support of school development processes

As school management, you will shape the further development of our school together with the team. You lead with clarity and empathy, create orientation and promote trusting collaboration.

You combine operational leadership with pedagogical vision and actively contribute to a positive, future-oriented school culture.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a school leader
  • Leadership experience in a school or comparable context
  • Pedagogical understanding and enjoyment of school development
  • Strong communication skills, decision-making skills and organizational skills
  • High social skills and the ability to connect people
  • Resilience, flexibility and humor

We offer

  • A manageable, familiar school with a lot of scope for design
  • A committed, supportive and warm team
  • Short decision-making processes and direct cooperation with the community
  • An appreciative school culture with a clear attitude
  • Working in a unique, natural environment
