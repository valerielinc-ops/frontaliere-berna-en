Full description

Portrait Welcome to the Belp Schools – Design They learn with At the primary school Mühlematt we accompany around 300 children in twelve standard classes and three kindergartens. We are actively developing our school – in an appreciative and reliable environment that is open to educational innovations. The annual school shows: In our community, new ideas must not only emerge, but also become reality.

With us, you create learning together with a committed team – with the pleasure of trying out and a clear view of what empowers children. Do you want to actively shape school and set new impulses? Then you are exactly right with us.

Tasks

In our 2nd class, you will create a varied and inspiring lesson that awakens the children’s curiosity and strengthens their joy of learning. They take the different needs and learning paths of the students attentively, promote them individually and accompany their development with appreciative feedback. With a clear and cordial class leadership, you create a learning-promoting cooperation and strengthen social learning. Together with the step and specialist teams, you plan the next learning steps, exchange ideas about the children’s learning development and contribute your perspectives to school conferences.

Requirements

They have completed training or are still in training and are ready to take on the class teacher function. Teamwork is important to you. With your strong relationship competence, you respond to the individual needs of the children, create clear structures and promote a positive learning atmosphere. They communicate appreciatively with children, parents and the college and bring openness to new things and the willingness to actively shape our school.

We offer

With us you can expect a helpful team that is characterized by a strong team spirit. You will find space for your own pedagogical ideas and receive support from your colleagues. Our site school management guides, promotes and always has an open ear.

You will receive permanent employment. As a manageable school, we are characterized by short paths and the support of the community. A modern IT infrastructure and the school administration platform Escola are available for your work.
