Full description

Portrait “Together for physical, motor and personal development to a self-determined life”

The Rossfeld Foundation empowers, accompanies and supports people with physical, motor and perceptual impairments of all ages in the areas of school, living, daily structure and therapy. Our clients: inside and their needs are in the center. We stand for high service quality, short decision-making paths and dynamic working methods in a changing environment. The ambulatory service Rossfeld supports children and young people with physical disabilities who attend a public school in the canton of Bern.
