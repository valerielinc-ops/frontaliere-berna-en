Resource planner: in steering customer support 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Resource planner: in steering customer support Bern, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this.

We move the train! The rail production department is responsible for ensuring that SBB passenger trains run safely and economically. We are looking for support for our team in the Traffic Control Center Mitte. Your workplace is at Bollwerk 10 in Bern. We offer you varied, challenging and exciting work that will help you advance personally and professionally. You can move that.

  • In this exciting position, you will be responsible for the day-to-day operations of the customer companions in a 24-hour shift operation.
  • Together with a team, you will ensure orderly and stable operations by managing customer support.
  • To ensure a stable process, you coordinate the tasks with various partners and third parties.
  • You work with different IT systems, especially the SOPRE planning system.

This is what you bring with you.

  • It motivates you to work in 24-hour shifts.
  • You are a team player, initiative and creative personality with a systematic way of working and very good communication skills.
  • You combine your networked, analytical thinking with your flair for solution-oriented work and various IT systems.
  • For this position you have completed vocational training combined with professional experience in the dispatching area or in the railway sector.
  • You communicate fluently in German or French (at least level C1) and have a good basic knowledge (at least level A2) of the other language. You also have basic knowledge of Italian.

Your salary and your benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information about the wage system at SBB can be viewed transparently. This position is at requirement level H, regional allowance level 1. More about wages and benefits.
