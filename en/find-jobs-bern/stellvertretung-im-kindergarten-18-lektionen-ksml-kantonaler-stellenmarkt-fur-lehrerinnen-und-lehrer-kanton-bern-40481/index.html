Full description

Portrait

  • Neuenegg, a dynamic community on the western edge of the agglomeration between Bern and Fribourg.
  • Kindergarten up to 6th grade primary level at four school locations.
  • Our 560 students are accompanied and taught by about 60 teachers on their way to school and life.
  • Good infrastructure and very good cooperation with the municipality of Neuenegg and the commission of schools of the municipality of Neuenegg.

Tasks

You teach as a teacher at the Neuenegg primary level in kindergarten with a total of 18 lessons per week. Lessons will take place on Tuesday morning and afternoon, and on Thursday and Friday mornings.

The deputy lasts from 19 November to and with 23. December 2026.

Applications received will be continuously examined.

Requirements

Ideally, you have a seminary primary teacher patent or a Bachelor for the preschool or primary level. You are a dutiful and resilient personality. We also welcome applications from students of the Pedagogical University.

We offer

  • An idyllic, rural environment.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in a well-coordinated, supportive team.
  • An exciting field of work with personal responsibility.
  • Temporary employment according to cantonal employment conditions (LAG).
  • The school house village is easy to reach by public transport.
