Property Data Clerk — Genossenschaft Migros Aare

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you fascinated by data, figures and accounting in the real estate sector? Then this job offers you an interesting overall package. In this versatile function, you are responsible for maintaining contract and master data, supporting real estate accounting, processing incidental cost statements and actively participating in professional data management.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You collect, mutate and maintain lease contracts as well as tenant master data in the management system
  • You process and record creditors’ bills in the workflow
  • You create and process incidental costs invoices on time and reliably
  • You maintain sales reports and keep master data up to date
  • You take over the debtor management and support real estate accounting
  • You are actively involved in monthly system and data management
  • You work closely with management, accounting and external partners to ensure a smooth flow of information

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in data management or willingness to acquire it
  • Completed basic or further education: as a businesswoman / businessman with experience in real estate accounting
  • You have experience in real estate data management and ideally know SAP RE-FX
  • An exact, analytical and reliable way of working distinguishes you
  • Numbers, data and systems inspire you and you like to work structured
  • With the MS Office applications you are safe and well-versed
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates of newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance funds
