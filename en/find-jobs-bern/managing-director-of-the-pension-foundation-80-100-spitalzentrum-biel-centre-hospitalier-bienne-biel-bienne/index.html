Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The Welfare Foundation of the Hospital Center Biel operates the mandatory and super-compulsory occupational provision for the employees. As Managing Director, you assume overall operational responsibility for our pension facility and, together with a small, committed team, ensure professional and customer-oriented support for the insured and affiliated companies. We search by 1.12.2026 or by agreement

Managing Director of the Pension Foundation, 80-100 %

You do.

  • Overall management and operational management of the Pension Foundation
  • Management of pension fund operations in compliance with the regulations and legal requirements
  • Professional and human leadership of a team of three employees, of which two are employees with a focus on real estate management and management
  • Assistance and advice to insured and affiliated employers
  • Responsibility for the correct handling of the day-to-day business of occupational pension provision
  • Cooperation with the Foundation Board and the Investment Commission as well as preparation and support of meetings
  • Support of the Foundation Board and the Investment Commission in the implementation of the investment strategy, in particular in the area of direct real estate investments
  • Coordination and management of cooperation with external partners and service providers
  • Monitoring legal and regulatory requirements
  • Ensuring professional communication in German and French

That sets them apart.

  • Training for/for dipl. Pension fund manager or university degree in business administration or equivalent social security education
  • Several years of practical experience in occupational pensions, ideally in a senior position
  • Responsibility and high social competence
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Leadership experience as well as strong communication skills for representation against inside and outside
  • very good oral and written knowledge in German or French with good knowledge of the other language
  • Knowledge of management of direct real estate investments

They benefit from it.

  • An exciting, responsible and versatile activity
  • Progressive conditions of employment
  • Working in a small dynamic team
  • the possibility of actively shaping the further development of the pension fund
