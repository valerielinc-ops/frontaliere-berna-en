Full description

Put to the Point

Become part of the Cyber Command and help shape the mission-critical communications of the Swiss Army and play a key role in the integration of civilian communication services into the military ICT landscape.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20748
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the added value of the agile team and maintain and prioritize the team backlog
  • Developing features in line with the vision, roadmap and needs of users for the army’s shared use of civilian services (MzD) in mobile and landline areas
  • MzD as a sub-project of the telecommunications of the army lead as well as performance, deadlines, quality and finances responsible
  • Ensure architectural and security compliance in close cooperation with system architects and military practitioners and security representatives
  • Coordinate requirements and dependencies with adjacent areas, projects and services
  • Represent MzD vis-à-vis clients, users, stakeholders, armasuisse as well as strategic partners and suppliers

This makes you unique

  • Training as (business) computer scientist, technician or engineer (UNI/ETH/FH) in the field of computer science, telecommunications or comparable
  • Several years of experience as a product owner and in managing complex ICT and telecommunications projects, ideally with SAFe
  • Good technical and operational understanding of mobile, landline and network technology
  • Ability to structure, prioritize and advance complex projects and dependencies as well as very good communication, leadership and stakeholder management skills
  • High personal responsibility and decision-making capacity as well as an understanding of information security and ICT architectures
  • Good knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, passive knowledge of a third official language

We offer this

  • We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Employing the Swiss army makes sense and brings many advantages. Convince yourself of Army.ch/advantages. Offers from recruiters are not taken into account.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
