Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a variety of activities in a family environment, you can also expect flexible leisure activities in the surrounding natural and lake region at Thun Hospital. Are you looking for meaningfulness, but also fairness? Then you have come to the right place. You work in shifts to attend to outpatient emergencies in the areas of urology, orthopedics, general and visceral surgery and ENT and carry out small emergency procedures such as nasal packing, wound care, drainage inserts (puncture/chest drains) and reductions under the supervision of senior doctors. This also includes shock room management and co-supervision of the orthopedic, surgical and urological wards in emergency events, e.g. at night under the supervision of appropriate senior doctors.
