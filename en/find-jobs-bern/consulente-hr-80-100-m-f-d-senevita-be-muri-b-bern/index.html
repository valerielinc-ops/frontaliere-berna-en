Full description

Senevita AG is a leading Swiss company in the field of care and living in old age. We focus on people – be it our team or our residents. We offer an open, appreciative and service-oriented corporate culture that is characterised by closeness to people – with head, heart and hand. Your heart burns for diverse and varied HR topics. You flourish in an appreciative, dynamic and demanding environment. You have already worked successfully in HR consulting for several years. You want to shape and develop an HR landscape with energy, perseverance and the necessary tenacity. For you, the focus is on achieving solutions and people. Then you are exactly right at Senevita AG! For our headquarters in Muri b. Bern we are looking for an HR consultant immediately or by agreement 80-100% (m/f/d)

Your tasks

  • You will advise executives at our numerous locations on challenging personnel issues and support the HR area as a whole
  • As part of Group HR, you are valued as a sparring partner, source of ideas and implementer and are in demand in all phases of the Employee Life Cycle.
  • You are actively involved in operational HR consulting and support with a wide range of issues relating to the Employee Life Cycle
  • As part of the continuous development, you are regularly involved in project work
  • At the headquarters, you work closely with payroll and HR support in operational topics

What distinguishes you

  • Completed vocational training and further training as an HR specialist are required
  • Several years of experience in an operational HR generalist function with in-depth knowledge along the entire employee lifecycle, ideally in the healthcare sector
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills in word and writing as well as stylish German, good knowledge in another national language
  • Strong resilience, fast comprehension, networked thinking and strong prioritization ability with the ability to work on a wide range of topics simultaneously and efficiently
  • Ideally, proven experience in managing small and medium-sized HR projects as well as a strong team player and hands-on mentality
  • Readiness for occasional travel within Switzerland

When you choose Senevita AG, you become part of an organization that lives its values and works for the development and well-being of its employees. With us, you have the opportunity to contribute your skills and experience and to be proud of what has been achieved.

  • Flexible working hours with occasional travel within Switzerland
  • At least 5 weeks of holiday per year (from 50 years 6 weeks, from 60 years 7 weeks)
  • Access to benefits such as a half-tax subscription, fleet discounts, cheaper fueling, supplementary insurance and fitness offers
