Full description

The Central Secretariat guarantees the coordination of the SSR Association and assists the Board of Directors in its activities.

Shipments

The SSR animates Switzerland.

As Switzerland's largest media company, SSR guarantees independent journalism, cultural diversity and public service in the four language regions. In a rapidly changing media landscape, it shapes the digital future of audiovisual public service and strengthens the social cohesion of our country. For the next administrative period we seek an integral personality, with strategic vision and strong persuasive ability, to cover the charge of

Chairman of the Board of Directors SSR (50%)

In this challenging role it will assume the management of the Board of Directors and will represent the SSR in political and economic environments, civil society and the public. In collaboration with the Board of Directors and the Management Committee, it will define the strategic development of the SSR and will accompany its transformation into a rapidly changing media context.

  • Direct the SSR Board of Directors and ensure effective and future-oriented corporate governance
  • Drive the strategic development of the SSR and ensure the lasting fulfillment of its public service mandate in the digital age
  • To support effective corporate and cultural transformation as well as the development of "A SSR", taking into account the country's cultural and linguistic diversity
  • Maintain a close dialogue with the Director-General, playing the role of "sparring partner"
  • Representing the SSR to the authorities, political decision-making bodies and other stakeholders; promoting dialogue between the company, the mantle association, politics and the public
  • Enhancing the rooting of public service in all language regions and representing SSR in political, social and media environments

Requirements

  • Multiannual management and governance experience at the highest levels, accompanied by proven experience in strategic accompaniment of complex transformation and change processes
  • Great knowledge of Switzerland's political functioning and decision-making as well as a broad network of contacts at the meeting point between Swiss companies, economics and media
  • Spicy affinity with technologies and a clear vision of how public media service should be placed in the democratic and social context in the face of the growing dominance of artificial intelligence
  • Solid knowledge of modern corporate governance principles and experience in managing a complex set of interest groups
  • Strong adherence to the public service mandate and its importance to Switzerland
  • Extensive communication skills and safety in dealing with political representatives, media and public
  • Great active knowledge of German and French; willingness to acquire knowledge of Italian and to fully live the multilingualism of Switzerland

What we offer

  • A key role in the heart of public media service, with the ability to actively contribute to shaping the future of public audiovisual service in Switzerland
  • An extraordinary management function, of great social importance and with visibility at national level
  • A close collaboration with the members of the Board of Directors, strongly motivated and committed, with an expert management committee and with different stakeholders from all language regions
  • An environment characterized by diversity, multilingualism and innovative capacity

Interested persons are invited to email SRG.SSR@egonzehnder.com . The deadline for submitting applications is 15.11.2026.

Our promise

What are the ingredients to reach the inhabitants of Switzerland with quality format? Know-how, enthusiasm and an environment rich in stimuli, in which everyone can grow. To give the best it takes a source of inspiration: Welcome to the SSR world!

  • Produce content: Quality journalism is our main activity and our passion. We enjoy editorial freedom, promote creativity and give ample space to the production of new and high-level content.
  • Give shape to digital: Quality journalism is also digital. We explore new possibilities and experience innovative forms: digital, interactive and crossmedia. A look at tomorrow for our spectators today.
  • Valorize the person: We cultivate solidarity relations, work independently and contribute together with a good working climate. Our success is also based on the personal and professional qualities of all employees.
  • Make sense: With our programs we reach and thrill Switzerland. We act with respect and sense of independence; we promote the free formation of opinions and strengthen pluralism. We are proud of the contribution we make to Switzerland.
