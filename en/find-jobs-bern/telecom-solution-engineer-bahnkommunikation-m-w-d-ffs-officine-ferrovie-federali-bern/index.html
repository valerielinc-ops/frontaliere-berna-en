Telecom Solution Engineer Railway Communication (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Telecom Solution Engineer Railway Communication (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Become part of the SBB Telecom Engineering department in Ostermundigen, Bern! At SBB Telecom Engineering, you work on communication systems that are central to safe and reliable rail operations. Together with your team, you will be responsible for the further development of mission-critical railway communication for employees on the trains, in the operations centres and for customer-relevant information systems. A major technology change is imminent in the coming years: existing systems will be further developed, integrated and gradually prepared for the next generation of mobile rail communication. You will play an important role in the further development of operational telephony, in migrations and in the preparation for FRMCS (Future Railway Mobile Communication System). You move in an exciting environment of GSM-R, IMS, operating telephony and system integration – and work on solutions that make a real difference in everyday operations. Our benefits such as child allowances, free GA and discounted travel abroad – including for your family – can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You will continue to develop central systems of railway communication and actively shape integration, migration and technical roadmaps.
  • You design operational use cases and interfaces in conjunction with IMS, GSM-R, systems of other railways and SIP-based solutions.
  • You will create technical concepts, specifications and solutions for new services and system connections. In addition, you plan and accompany laboratory, acceptance tests and live commissioning of systems and terminals.
  • Furthermore, you support with tenders from the requirement definition to the offer evaluation.
  • Together with the company and partners, you will analyze complex disruptions and work on sustainable solutions.
  • You clarify topics such as VPN, VLAN and environment connections together with network specialists: inside. You bring Security by Design into new solutions early on.

You bring that with you.

  • You have an analytical and structured way of working and enjoy working together across different disciplines.
  • With technical concepts, specifications, tests and commissioning of complex systems you have already gained experience.
  • You bring experience in the telecom, mobile, voice or system integration environment. You also bring know-how in IP-based communication, ideally with SIP and RTP
  • Knowledge of IMS, GSM-R, FRMCS, DIAMETER, SCTP, WebRTC/WebSocket or Sigtran/SS7 is advantageous.
  • For this role, we require a degree (FH/Uni/ETH), an equivalent education / further education or many years of experience in the field of telecommunications, electrical engineering, computer science or a comparable education with corresponding practical experience.
  • Your German and English skills in word and writing are very good (minor level C1).

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This job is in requirement level K, regional allowance level 1.
