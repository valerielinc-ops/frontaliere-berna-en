Telecom Solution Engineer Railway Communications (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland forward with us. Telecom Solution Engineer Railway Communications (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, starting immediately or by agreement, 60-100% Apply now

What you can look forward to. Become part of the SBB Telecom Engineering department in Ostermundigen, Bern! At SBB Telecom Engineering, you work on communication systems that are central to safe and reliable railway operations. Together with your team, you are responsible for the further development of mission-critical railway communications for employees on trains, in operations control centers and for customer-relevant information systems. A major technological transformation lies ahead in the coming years: Existing systems will be further developed, integrated and gradually prepared for the next generation of mobile railway communications. In this process, you will play an important role in the further development of operational telephony, migrations and preparations for FRMCS (Future Railway Mobile Communication System). You work in an exciting environment comprising GSM-R, IMS, operational telephony and system integration – and work on solutions that make a real difference in day-to-day railway operations. Our benefits such as child allowances, a free GA and discounted travel abroad - also for your family - can be found on our website under Benefits . What you can achieve.

  • You will further develop central railway communication systems and actively shape integration, migration and technical roadmaps.
  • You will design operational use cases and interfaces in conjunction with IMS, GSM-R, systems from other railways and SIP-based solutions.
  • You will create technical concepts, specifications and solution proposals for new services and system connections. You will also plan and support laboratory and acceptance tests as well as live commissioning of systems and terminals.
  • You will also support tenders from requirements definition through bid evaluation.
  • Together with operations and partners, you will analyze complex faults and work on sustainable solutions.
  • You will clarify topics such as VPN, VLAN and connections to surrounding systems together with network specialists. You will incorporate Security by Design into new solutions at an early stage.

What you bring.

  • You have an analytical and structured way of working and enjoy collaborating across various specialist areas.
  • You have already gained experience with technical concepts, specifications, testing and commissioning of complex systems.
  • You have experience in the telecommunications, mobile communications, voice or system integration environment. You also have know-how in IP-based communications, ideally with SIP and RTP
  • Knowledge of IMS, GSM-R, FRMCS, DIAMETER, SCTP, WebRTC/WebSocket or Sigtran/SS7 is an advantage.
  • For this role, we require a degree (FH/Uni/ETH), equivalent education or further training, or many years of experience in telecommunications, electrical engineering, computer science or comparable training with corresponding practical experience.
  • Your German and English skills, both written and spoken, are very good (at least level C1).
