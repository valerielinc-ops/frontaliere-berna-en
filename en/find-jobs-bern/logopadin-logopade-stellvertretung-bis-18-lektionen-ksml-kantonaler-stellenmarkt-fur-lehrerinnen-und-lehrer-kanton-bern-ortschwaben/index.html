Full description

Portrait Welcome to Meikirch! The Meikirch schools comprise the three school locations Ortschwaben, Meikirch and Wahlendorf. We are a manageable, rural school in the immediate vicinity of the city of Bern and attach great importance to an appreciative cooperation, short distances and a supportive cooperation.

From January 2027 until the summer holidays 2027 we are looking for a speech-pedia representative school location Ortschwaben. The course comprises a total of 18 lessons, but can also be partly taken or divided between several people.

Tasks

Speech-pediatric diagnosis, promotion and therapy of students Counselling and cooperation with teachers, parents and other specialists Professional exchange and participation in the MR team Interdisciplinary cooperation in the school environment

Requirements

Recognized degree as a speech doctor / speech doctor or currently in training as a speech doctor / speech doctor Joy of working with children in kindergarten and primary school age Independent and reliable working method Enjoy working in a dedicated, supportive team Openness, flexibility and an appreciative attitude

We offer

  • Great flexibility in the design of the timetable
  • an existing timetable as a starting point: The lessons are currently spread over the mornings from Monday to Friday, as well as Thursday and Friday afternoons
  • However, adjustments and reorganization are possible
  • an own, well-decorated speech pedia room in local Swabia
  • An open and supportive school environment
  • close cooperation with teachers, curative education, psychomotor skills, school social work and other specialists
  • short distances and uncomplicated arrangements within the school
  • a small community with a personal and family atmosphere

The main place of work is in the Ortschwaben School House. There is an own, permanently furnished speech pedia room available.

Currently, 16 of the 18 lessons take place in local Swabia and 2 lessons in the school house Airbager in Meikirch.
