Full description

You can make this contribution

  • carry out diagnoses, repairs and adjustments of engine, drive groups, brakes, steering, chassis, sub-assemblies and safety and comfort electronics
  • Check defects of vehicles not ready to march and carry out general checks for noise, engine performance, drive group function, steering and brakes
  • Restoring material readiness of non-marching systems
  • Perform revision or replacement of complete or part subassemblies
  • Provide advice, accompaniment and control of the payees (troop, third parties, etc.) on the occasion of the vehicle versions and vehicle returns regarding driving safety, accident prevention, maintenance, cleaning and vehicle inventory
  • Caring for learners

This makes you unique

  • Car mechanic, car mechatronics technician/EFZ (light/heavy motor car) or equivalent related profession
  • Further training for a diagnostician/wheeled vehicle
  • Category B, C, E driving licences
  • Good organizational skills in prioritizing orders as well as their independent and conscientious processing
  • Service-oriented working methods in contact with the force

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
