Employees: in creditor processing 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Employee:in creditor processing Ostermundigen / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, limited to 31.01.2028, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Together with a motivated team, you ensure that day-to-day credit operations run smoothly. You can always rely on the support of your team. You can also look forward to a professional introduction by an experienced team member – Step by step you will be introduced to the organization and processes. You can move that.

  • As an employee: in creditor processing, you are responsible for the operational invoice processing in SAP S/4HANA – in particular the checking, recording and booking of incoming invoices, credit notes and corrections.
  • This includes punctual clarifications with internal interfaces as well as close cooperation with purchasing, master data and the upstream and downstream offices.
  • You actively contribute to achieving the agreed performance targets – for example in the areas of processing speed, quality and productivity.
  • By identifying and introducing optimization potential in the SAP S/4 Common Core (common process standard), you actively contribute to the further development of our processes.
  • With your work, you contribute to actively living the customer orientation of SBB.

You bring that with you.

  • A reliable, responsible and integrity character distinguishes you.
  • Even in hectic times and with a high workload, you keep the overview and react calmly and calmly.
  • You are used to working independently and perform your tasks accurately and quickly.
  • We require a completed commercial education and at least one year of experience in the area of creditors.
  • You have knowledge of German at least at level C1 and ideally bring another national language at level B1.
  • Knowledge of the core SAP processes (S/4, modules FI & MM) as well as your secure handling of MS Office allow you to quickly get started in this function.

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This position is in requirement level F, Regional Allowance Level 1.
