Full description

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

As a lead frontend developer, you control the technical development of the frontend of interdiscount.ch. Together with our system architects, you define the frontend architecture. In addition, you ensure the transfer of know-how in the development team and assess new technologies. In addition, you are an active part of one of our shop teams and develop new features for our customers.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the technical development and software architecture of the frontend of interdiscount.ch
  • As a lead developer, you share your knowledge proactively and promote the professional development of the entire frontend team
  • You will actively participate in the design of our IT and software architecture, assess new technologies for their relevance and prepare architecture decisions
  • You are actively developing new features for our customers in one of our agile development teams
  • Technical excellence is a matter of course for you: you consistently implement testing, monitoring and software engineering best practices
  • You actively promote the use of AI tools in software development

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of experience in the conception and implementation of frontend solutions with Next.js or React, as well as with other web technologies
  • A completed degree (FH, Uni, ETH), ideally in computer science
  • Very good knowledge of one or more programming languages such as TypeScript, JavaScript, Java or C#
  • Visible passion for frontend engineering: You like to immerse yourself in different topics and keep your knowledge in areas such as SEO, Core Web Vitals, AI engineering, performance and user experience up-to-date
  • Strong interest in AI engineering: You bring in ideas on how we can profitably use AI technologies for our online shop
  • Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines (ideally based on GitHub Actions) is a plus
  • Stylistic German in word and writing as well as good English skills

We offer

We offer you a modern, dynamic working environment in which you can contribute your skills and personality every day and make a difference. For joint success. For a common future.
