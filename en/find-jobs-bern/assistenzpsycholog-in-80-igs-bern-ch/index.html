Their tasks

  • Diagnostic assessments in children, adolescents and young adults (with focus on psychosis or an increased risk of developing psychosis)
  • Therapeutic activity as part of the provision of modular cognitive-behavioural therapy for persons at increased risk of psychosis or psychotic first-time diseases
  • Contact person to the outpatient, semi-stationary and inpatient services of Soteria Bern, cooperation in individual group therapies and assumption of a limited number of outpatient therapies
  • Completed university degree in psychology
  • Psychotherapy training recognised by the FOPH (began or completed)
  • Great interest in psychopathology
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Previous experience, in particular with the clinical picture of psychotic disorders
  • Interest in clinical research Our offer
  • An exciting, demanding and versatile diagnostic and therapeutic activity
