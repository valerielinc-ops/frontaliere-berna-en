Full description

Portrait Difficult school situations require special measures so that a tense situation can relax, the sails can be reset and learning becomes possible again. The municipality of Köniz offers such an offer within the framework of special support with a Time Out Group (LOOP) for children and young people from the municipality of Köniz from the 5th school year onwards.

Tasks

Preparing, structuring and accompanying the work on the school material. Work on the expected changes taking into account existing resources. Activating existing resources and addressing personal deficits. Cooperation with parents, schools and specialist bodies. Engaged in the LOOP team. Working days Monday, Thursday and possibly. Wednesday.

Requirements

They have a teaching diploma at primary level and bring experience in teaching and working with children. Do you have a flair for challenging children in difficult situations and an interest in this demanding task? Are you resilient, patient and have a clear pedagogical attitude that you can implement and enforce in everyday life? You are used to working independently. Are you adept at dealing with parents, teachers, school leaders and offices? They are open to changes and further development of the offer. Imagination and humor round off your profile? Then you are the right person for our time-out group.

We offer

  • We offer temporary employment (GK 10) until 31.7.27. The concept will be fundamentally revised this school year.
  • Lessons in small groups (5 - 7 SuS). Teamteaching.
  • Adjustments in the timetable in consultation with the job partner are partially possible.
  • Good accessibility with public transport.
