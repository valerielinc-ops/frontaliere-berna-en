Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

As a versatile peripheral hospital, we are characterised by a low-threshold and family working atmosphere. Our employees also report very good development opportunities and a good skill & grade mix in the departments. Lean management is not a foreign word for us. Family-friendly conditions round off our profile. We attach particular importance to autonomous service planning, in which the team creates the service plan together. For our medical department at the Langnau location, with around 25 beds (including an integrated monitoring station with 3 beds) and a dedicated team of around 48 employees, students and learners at the Langnau location, we are looking for an immediate or by appointment.

Your tasks

  • Our patients are cared for and cared for with high personal responsibility, professionally, patient-oriented and according to the latest findings.
  • You will take over the management of care groups with health specialists, nursing assistants and students and learners.
  • You carry out the care process (including performance recording material and care) – supported by the clinic information system Clinical – and are committed to the continuous optimization of care processes
  • Learners and students you accompany in their education

Your profile

  • You have a completed education as a dipl. Nurse (AKP / DN II / HF / FH) and preferably already has professional experience in the acute field
  • The wide range of medical departments offered by a family peripheral hospital is exciting and challenging.
  • The well-being of patients is at the heart of your work and you meet people with respect and appreciation
  • You work reliably and conscientiously and like to take responsibility
  • Constantly developing is a matter of course for you
  • A stylish German rounds off your profile (provided for foreign-language diploma certificate B2)
