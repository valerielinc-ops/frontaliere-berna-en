More than 1000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: The CSD Group is a dynamic, constantly growing engineering company. Inventiveness, competence and multidisciplinarity characterize our teams, optimal solutions for our customers with a plus for the quality of life and the environment are our trademark. Do you enjoy working in a professional and agile environment and attach great importance to quality and efficiency? Become part of our success! We are looking for an intern for our area and environment in Bern for 4 to 6 months in the field of environment with a focus on flora and habitats 80-100% (m/f/d).

Your responsibilities

  • Collaboration in exciting and varied projects such as environmental impact reports, environmental construction support and soil monitoring, various expert reports (noise, soil, flora, fauna, building pollutants, contaminated sites, geology etc.)

  • Cooperation in interdisciplinary teams within the CSD network

Your profile

  • Studies in Environment or Spatial Planning, Environmental Sciences, Geography (ETH, FH or University)
  • Good knowledge of flora (400 certificate upwards) and knowledge of the habitats of Switzerland.
  • Basic knowledge of GIS (ArcGIS, Field Maps) is an advantage
  • In German you can express yourself in writing and orally
  • Ideally have You have knowledge of a second official language
  • Reliability, independence and commitment distinguish you

We offer

  • Exciting tasks in a versatile project portfolio: major projects transport (public transport and MIV), tourist infrastructures (cable cars, summer/winter uses), gravel mining and landfill projects, power grid infrastructure
  • Integration into the network of various departments
  • Professional and personal development
  • A dynamic environment and a respectful approach
  • Flexible conditions for optimal work and leisure
  • A pleasant working environment and a modern and centre-oriented workplace awaits you at our office location at Eigerplatz in Bern
