Full description

Portrait The advertised position is in the village of Schwarzenburg, 20 kilometers south of Bern. Schwarzenburg is surrounded by a diverse, beautiful nature. With the public transport you are in 35 minutes from Bern in Schwarzenburg. From Schwarzenburg station to the schoolhouse it is a five-minute walk. In the village of Schwarzenburg, 40 teachers teach at two school locations of kindergartens and primary schools.

Tasks

The main course of 11 lessons consists of sports, NMG, math, music and German. At the 1st / 2nd class, all subjects are represented except for music.

The teaching days are Do and Fr at the 3./4. Class as well as interest Mo, Di and Mi in the 1st / 2nd class.

There is scope in the distribution and number of lessons.

Requirements

You have completed education for the primary level or are studying at an educational college. Working on an age-mixed class gives them pleasure.

  • Diverse possibilities for activities in nature / teaching outside
