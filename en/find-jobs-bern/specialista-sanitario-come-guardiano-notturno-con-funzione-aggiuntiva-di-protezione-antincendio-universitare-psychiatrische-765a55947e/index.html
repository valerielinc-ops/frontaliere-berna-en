Full description

University Hospital for Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The University Psychiatric Center (UPZ) Bern offers versatile and responsible tasks in acute psychiatry at the Beyeler and Streit stations. In a dynamic environment, you accompany patients with different medical conditions. The work is based on a person-centered recovery-oriented mission statement and combines a comprehensive range of non-drug interventions to support patients’ self-efficacy at night. As an additional night watch, you support the professional care of our patients with the dipl. nurses during the night. In addition, you take over the defined tasks within the scope of the fire protection project. We are a motivated, collaborative team that supports each other, cooperates competently and values an appreciative atmosphere. Do you feel called? We look forward to meeting you.

Your tasks

  • You support patients with psychiatric conditions with their needs during the night
  • You support the sleep hygiene of patients with non-drug interventions
  • You also take on logistics tasks to support the care team
  • You will conduct cross-station control tours to ensure and maintain operations
  • You are responsible for fire prevention tasks

Your profile

  • You have completed your training as a specialist in health (EFZ) and already have your first professional experience
  • You have a strong professional, social and self-competence
  • You communicate clearly and empathetically
  • You are a reliable and responsible personality
  • You have good knowledge of German
  • You are interested in fire prevention measures

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
