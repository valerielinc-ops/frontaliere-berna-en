Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • You integrate, configure and operate backend and application services as well as supplier-specific applications and ensure their operational readiness.
  • You implement platform resources, container images, secrets, configurations and persistence according to architecture and security requirements.
  • You automate and version deployment and configuration processes and implement CI/CD and GitOps mechanisms.
  • You are responsible for technical integration, testing and delivery of releases, upgrades and patches in the intended target environments.
  • You create lifecycle and migration concepts as well as technical solution designs and document them in a comprehensible way.
  • You specify interfaces and technical dependencies to existing systems and environments and ensure the necessary integration and test proof.
  • You maintain the system and service configurations and provide up-to-date operational, installation and troubleshooting documentation.

You bring that with you

  • You have at least five years of professional experience in DevSecOps, system administration or system architecture.
  • You will have a good knowledge of Linux systems, container technologies, deployment and automation.
  • You have experience with CI/CD, GitOps and Infrastructure-as-Code as well as IT security and system hardening.
  • You are familiar with the technical integration and lifecycle management of complex system landscapes.
  • You know agile development and collaboration methods
  • Experience with SAFe is beneficial.
  • You work independently, analytically and in a structured manner and have good knowledge of German (at least B2).

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
