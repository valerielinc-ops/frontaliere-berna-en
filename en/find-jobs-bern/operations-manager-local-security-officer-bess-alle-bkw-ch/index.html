Full description

You are actively involved in the future operation of our Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). In doing so, you develop scalable operating structures, define binding standards and ensure that new plants are transferred to commercial operations safely, efficiently and on time.

Your field of activity

  • You develop and take responsibility for a scalable operating model for BESS assets and establish cross-functional governance structures, roles and responsibilities
  • The safe and timely transfer of new BESS plants into operational operation is your responsibility – from planning to take over operations
  • As a central interface, you represent operator interests and coordinate the cooperation between project organization, internal and external specialist bodies, suppliers and international partners.
  • You define binding criteria for operational readiness within the framework of the regulatory requirements, take responsibility for operational readiness checks and escalate critical deviations
  • The professional control of the internal service providers, as well as the further development of uniform operating standards are also part of your tasks
  • You ensure operational, maintenance and safety concepts, which can be certified according to ISO -27001 and KRITIS if necessary
  • As a Deputy Local Cyber Security Officer, you ensure the NIS2 and ICT minimum standards of our facilities with the OT Manager.

You bring that with you

  • You have a higher technical education or a degree, preferably in electrical engineering, energy engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable field of study
  • You will bring in-depth experience from the operation of electrotechnical plants, in commissioning or in the project management of complex technical plants; BESS knowledge is a plus
  • You have experience in the design of operating models, governance structures or cross-divisional processes
  • You quickly capture complex technical and organizational relationships and develop scalable, practical solutions from them
  • With strong interface management, secure appearance and assertiveness, you also represent interests across organizational boundaries
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, high self-responsibility and a pronounced quality awareness for the documentation of process responsibilities round off your profile. Knowledge of OT security is an advantage
