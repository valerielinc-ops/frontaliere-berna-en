Introduction For our Mechanical sector, we are looking for a Programmer (M/F) whose main mission will be to program the erosion machines for the manufacture of stamping components. Responsibilities

  • Define and apply strategies and tools for erosion manufacturing
  • Improve and deploy manufacturing processes
  • Determine programming strategies and associated tools
  • Train and support users during process transfers between industrialization (BT), internal production and external production
  • Audit manufacturing processes at our suppliers
  • Carry out the start-up, development and approval of programs on equipment
  • Participate in pre-reception and production launch new means of production Profile
  • CFC as a polymechanic, ideally with specialization in erosion
  • Additional training in manufacturing methods, control or processes, an asset
  • Confirmed technical expertise in erosion
  • Comfort in programming on different platforms
  • Knowledge and practice of erosion technologies in the field
  • Ability to define and document functional and technical specifications
  • Ability to analyze and synthesize for the resolution of complex problems
  • Technical curiosity and appetite for new technologies
  • Clear, effective communication skills and ease of working in a team
  • Rigor, organization and structured work methodology Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and first-rate social benefits.
